## 题目
The line $x = 1$ divides the circle $x^{2} + y^{2} = 4$ into two segments.

What is the area of the smaller segment?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{2\pi}{3} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{2\pi}{3} - \sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{\pi}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{\pi}{2} - 1$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \pi - \dfrac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \pi - 1$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \dfrac{4\pi}{3} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad \dfrac{4\pi}{3} - \sqrt{3}$$

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
H

## 解析
The circle has radius $2$. The chord $x = 1$ meets it where $y^2 = 4 - 1 = 3$, at $(1, \pm\sqrt{3})$.

Each endpoint makes an angle $\theta$ with the positive $x$-axis where $\cos\theta = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $\theta = \dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and the chord subtends an angle of $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$ at the centre.

The minor segment is the sector minus the triangle:

$$\frac{1}{2}r^2\left(\alpha - \sin\alpha\right) = \frac{1}{2}(4)\left(\frac{2\pi}{3} - \sin\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)$$

$$= 2\left(\frac{2\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right) = \frac{4\pi}{3} - \sqrt{3}$$

which is option H.
