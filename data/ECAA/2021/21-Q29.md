## 题目
What is the mean of $\log_{10} 27$, $\log_{10} 64$, and $\log_{10} 216$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{\log_{10} 307}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{\log_{10} 81}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{\log_{10} 6^{12}}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \log_{10} 64$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \log_{10} 72$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \log_{10} 108$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
The mean of the three logarithms is

$$\frac{\log_{10} 27 + \log_{10} 64 + \log_{10} 216}{3} = \frac{\log_{10}(27 \times 64 \times 216)}{3}$$

Now $27 \times 64 \times 216 = 373248$, and $72^3 = 373248$, so

$$\frac{\log_{10}\left(72^3\right)}{3} = \frac{3\log_{10} 72}{3} = \log_{10} 72$$

which is option E.
