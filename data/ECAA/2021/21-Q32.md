## 题目
The circles

$$x^{2} + 2x + y^{2} = 19$$

and

$$x^{2} - 2x + y^{2} - 2y = 3$$

intersect only at the point $(p, q)$.

What is the value of $p + q$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -11$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -7$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -5$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad -3$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 5$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 7$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad 11$$

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
F

## 解析
Subtracting the two equations eliminates the quadratic terms and gives the line through the intersection points:

$$\left(x^2 + 2x + y^2\right) - \left(x^2 - 2x + y^2 - 2y\right) = 19 - 3$$

$$4x + 2y = 16 \implies y = 8 - 2x$$

Substituting into the second circle, written as $(x-1)^2 + (y-1)^2 = 5$:

$$(x - 1)^2 + (7 - 2x)^2 = 5$$

$$5x^2 - 30x + 45 = 0 \implies (x - 3)^2 = 0$$

The repeated root confirms a single intersection point, at $x = 3$ and $y = 8 - 6 = 2$.

So $p + q = 5$, which is option F.
