## 题目
Find how many distinct real solutions there are to the equation

$$(x^{2} + 4x + 3)^{2} = 1$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Taking square roots gives two quadratics:

$$x^2 + 4x + 3 = 1 \qquad \text{or} \qquad x^2 + 4x + 3 = -1$$

For the first, $x^2 + 4x + 2 = 0$ has discriminant $16 - 8 = 8 > 0$, giving two distinct real roots.

For the second, $x^2 + 4x + 4 = (x+2)^2 = 0$, giving the single repeated root $x = -2$.

The two quadratics share no root, so there are $2 + 1 = 3$ distinct real solutions, which is option D.
