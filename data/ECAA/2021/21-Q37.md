## 题目
Evaluate

$$\dfrac{3}{\sqrt{27} + \sqrt{21}} + \dfrac{3}{\sqrt{24} + \sqrt{18}} + \dfrac{3}{\sqrt{21} + \sqrt{15}} + \cdots + \dfrac{3}{\sqrt{9} + \sqrt{3}}$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{3\sqrt{2}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 3\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 1 + \sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 3\left( 1 + \sqrt{2} \right)$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{3}\left( 1 + \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad \sqrt{3}\left( 1 + \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} \right)$$

## 答案
H

## 解析
Each denominator has the form $\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b}$ with $a - b = 6$, so rationalising gives

$$\frac{3}{\sqrt{a} + \sqrt{b}} = \frac{3\left(\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b}\right)}{a - b} = \frac{\sqrt{a} - \sqrt{b}}{2}$$

The terms interleave two separate telescoping chains. The first uses $27, 21, 15, 9, 3$:

$$\frac{1}{2}\left[\left(\sqrt{27} - \sqrt{21}\right) + \left(\sqrt{21} - \sqrt{15}\right) + \left(\sqrt{15} - \sqrt{9}\right) + \left(\sqrt{9} - \sqrt{3}\right)\right] = \frac{\sqrt{27} - \sqrt{3}}{2} = \frac{3\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{3}}{2} = \sqrt{3}$$

The second uses $24, 18, 12, 6$:

$$\frac{1}{2}\left[\left(\sqrt{24} - \sqrt{18}\right) + \left(\sqrt{18} - \sqrt{12}\right) + \left(\sqrt{12} - \sqrt{6}\right)\right] = \frac{\sqrt{24} - \sqrt{6}}{2} = \frac{2\sqrt{6} - \sqrt{6}}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2}$$

Adding the two chains:

$$\sqrt{3} + \frac{\sqrt{6}}{2} = \sqrt{3}\left(1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$$

which is option H.
