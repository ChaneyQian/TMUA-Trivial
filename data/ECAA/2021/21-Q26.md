## 题目
The polynomial $x^{2} - x - 1$ is a factor of the polynomial $px^{3} + qx^{2} - 1$ where $p$ and $q$ are constants.

What is the value of $q$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad -1$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad -2$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Since the divisor is quadratic and the dividend is cubic, the other factor is linear:

$$px^3 + qx^2 - 1 = (x^2 - x - 1)(px + r)$$

Expanding the right-hand side:

$$px^3 + (r - p)x^2 - (p + r)x - r$$

Comparing coefficients with $px^3 + qx^2 + 0x - 1$:

$$-r = -1 \implies r = 1$$

$$-(p + r) = 0 \implies p = -1$$

$$q = r - p = 1 - (-1) = 2$$

So $q = 2$, which is option A.
