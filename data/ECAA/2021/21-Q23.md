## 题目
A particular arithmetic series has first term $a$ and common difference $d$.

The sum of the first $k$ terms of this series is denoted by $S_k$

Which of the following is a simplification of $S_{n+1} - S_{n-1}$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad d$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 2d$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2a + d$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 2a + 2d$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 2a + nd$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 2a + 2nd$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 2a + (2n - 1)d$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad 2a + (4n - 2)d$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
G

## 解析
Subtracting the sums removes the first $n-1$ terms, leaving the $n$th and $(n+1)$th terms:

$$S_{n+1} - S_{n-1} = u_n + u_{n+1}$$

For an arithmetic series $u_k = a + (k-1)d$, so

$$u_n + u_{n+1} = \bigl(a + (n-1)d\bigr) + \bigl(a + nd\bigr) = 2a + (2n - 1)d$$

which is option G.
