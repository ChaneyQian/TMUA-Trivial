## 题目
Find the complete set of real values of $k$ for which the equation $3^{x+1} + 3^{-x} = k$ has at least one real root for $x$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad k \geq 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad k \geq 2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad k \geq 3$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad k \geq 2\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad k \geq 2 \text{ or } k \leq -2$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad k \geq 2\sqrt{3} \text{ or } k \leq -2\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad k \geq \log_{3} 2 \text{ or } k \leq -\log_{3} 2$$
