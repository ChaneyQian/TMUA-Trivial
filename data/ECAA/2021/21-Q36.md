## 题目
Find the complete set of real values of $k$ for which the equation $3^{x+1} + 3^{-x} = k$ has at least one real root for $x$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad k \geq 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad k \geq 2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad k \geq 3$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad k \geq 2\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad k \geq 2 \text{ or } k \leq -2$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad k \geq 2\sqrt{3} \text{ or } k \leq -2\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad k \geq \log_{3} 2 \text{ or } k \leq -\log_{3} 2$$

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Put $t = 3^x$, so $t > 0$ and $3^{x+1} = 3t$, $3^{-x} = \dfrac{1}{t}$. The equation becomes

$$3t + \frac{1}{t} = k$$

For $t > 0$, by AM–GM

$$3t + \frac{1}{t} \ge 2\sqrt{3t \cdot \frac{1}{t}} = 2\sqrt{3}$$

with equality at $t = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$, which is attainable since $3^x$ takes every positive value. As $t \to 0^+$ and $t \to \infty$ the expression tends to infinity, so it takes every value in $\left[2\sqrt{3}, \infty\right)$.

Hence a real root exists exactly when $k \ge 2\sqrt{3}$, which is option D.
