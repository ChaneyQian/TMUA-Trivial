## 题目
At how many distinct points do the following two curves meet?

$$y = (x - 4)(x^{2} - 2x - 8)$$

$$y = -x^{2} + 8x - 16$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 5$$

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Factorise the cubic, noting $x^2 - 2x - 8 = (x - 4)(x + 2)$:

$$y = (x - 4)^2(x + 2)$$

The second curve is $y = -(x - 4)^2$. Setting them equal:

$$(x - 4)^2(x + 2) = -(x - 4)^2$$

$$(x - 4)^2\bigl[(x + 2) + 1\bigr] = 0 \implies (x - 4)^2(x + 3) = 0$$

The roots are $x = 4$ (repeated) and $x = -3$, giving two distinct points of intersection, which is option C.
