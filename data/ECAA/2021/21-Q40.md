## 题目
Find the number of solutions to the equation

$$\Bigl\lvert \bigl\lvert \lvert x - 1 \rvert - 1 \bigr\rvert - 1 \Bigr\rvert - 1 = 0$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 5$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 6$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
The equation says the outermost modulus equals $1$:

$$\Bigl\lvert \bigl\lvert \lvert x - 1 \rvert - 1 \bigr\rvert - 1 \Bigr\rvert = 1$$

so

$$\bigl\lvert \lvert x - 1 \rvert - 1 \bigr\rvert = 2 \qquad \text{or} \qquad \bigl\lvert \lvert x - 1 \rvert - 1 \bigr\rvert = 0$$

In the first case $\lvert x - 1 \rvert = 3$ or $\lvert x - 1 \rvert = -1$; the latter is impossible, and the former gives $x = 4$ and $x = -2$.

In the second case $\lvert x - 1 \rvert = 1$, giving $x = 2$ and $x = 0$.

The four values $-2, 0, 2, 4$ are distinct, so there are $4$ solutions, which is option E.
