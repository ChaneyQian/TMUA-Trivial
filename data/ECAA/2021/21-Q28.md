## 题目
The quadratic function $f(x)$ has remainder 3 when divided by $(x - 1)$.

$f(x)$ has remainder 5 when divided by $(x + 3)$.

One solution of the equation $f(x) = 0$ is $x = 2$

What is the coefficient of $x$ in $f(x)$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -\dfrac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -\dfrac{3}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -\dfrac{5}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad -4$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad -9$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
By the remainder theorem the two remainders give $f(1) = 3$ and $f(-3) = 5$, and the given root gives $f(2) = 0$.

Writing $f(x) = ax^2 + bx + c$:

$$a + b + c = 3, \qquad 9a - 3b + c = 5, \qquad 4a + 2b + c = 0$$

Subtracting the first equation from the other two:

$$8a - 4b = 2 \implies 4a - 2b = 1$$

$$3a + b = -3 \implies b = -3 - 3a$$

Substituting the second into the first:

$$4a + 6 + 6a = 1 \implies a = -\frac{1}{2}$$

$$b = -3 - 3\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = -\frac{3}{2}$$

The coefficient of $x$ is $-\dfrac{3}{2}$, which is option B.
