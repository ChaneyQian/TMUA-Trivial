## 题目
The line $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x + c$ meets the curve $y = \dfrac{1}{8}x^{2}$ at two points, $P$ and $Q$.

The midpoint of the line segment $PQ$ has $y$-coordinate 5.

What is the value of $c$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{3}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{9}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 5$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 6$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
At the points of intersection

$$\frac{1}{8}x^2 = \frac{1}{2}x + c \implies x^2 - 4x - 8c = 0$$

The $x$-coordinates of $P$ and $Q$ are the roots of this quadratic, so their sum is $4$ and the midpoint has $x$-coordinate $2$.

The midpoint lies on the line, so its $y$-coordinate is

$$\frac{1}{2}(2) + c = 1 + c$$

Setting this equal to $5$ gives $c = 4$, which is option D.
