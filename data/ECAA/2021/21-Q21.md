## 题目
Given that

$$y = \left( 2\sqrt{x} - \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}} \right)^{2}$$

find the value of $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}$ when $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -12$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -\dfrac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{63}{16}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 5$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Expand first, so that the expression becomes a sum of powers of $x$:

$$y = \left(2\sqrt{x} - \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}\right)^2 = 4x - 2 + \frac{1}{4x}$$

Differentiate:

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = 4 - \frac{1}{4x^2}$$

At $x = \dfrac{1}{2}$ we have $4x^2 = 1$, so

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = 4 - 1 = 3$$

which is option C.
