## 题目
A geometric progression has first term $u_1 = a$ and common ratio $r$.

The sum to infinity of the geometric progression is $\dfrac{8}{5}$

The sum to infinity of the even-numbered terms $(u_2 + u_4 + u_6 + \cdots)$ is $\dfrac{3}{5}$

What is the value of $a + r$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{3}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{31}{25}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{23}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{28}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{67}{8}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
The even-numbered terms $u_2, u_4, u_6, \ldots$ themselves form a geometric progression with first term $ar$ and common ratio $r^2$, so

$$\frac{a}{1 - r} = \frac{8}{5}, \qquad \frac{ar}{1 - r^2} = \frac{3}{5}$$

Since $1 - r^2 = (1-r)(1+r)$, dividing the second equation by the first gives

$$\frac{r}{1 + r} = \frac{3}{8} \implies 8r = 3 + 3r \implies r = \frac{3}{5}$$

Then

$$a = \frac{8}{5}(1 - r) = \frac{8}{5} \times \frac{2}{5} = \frac{16}{25}$$

$$a + r = \frac{16}{25} + \frac{15}{25} = \frac{31}{25}$$

which is option B.
