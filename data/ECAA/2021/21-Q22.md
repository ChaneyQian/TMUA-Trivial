## 题目
![[Image/21-Q22-fig1.png]]

The diagram shows a circle with radius $2\,\text{cm}$, and a regular hexagon drawn so that each of its edges is tangent to the circle.

What is the area of the hexagon, in $\text{cm}^{2}$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 4\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 6\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 8\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 12\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 24\sqrt{3}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Because every edge is tangent to the circle, the circle is the inscribed circle of the hexagon, so the apothem is $2$.

A regular hexagon splits into six equilateral triangles. For side $s$, the apothem is the height of such a triangle:

$$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}s = 2 \implies s = \frac{4}{\sqrt{3}}$$

Using area $= \tfrac{1}{2} \times \text{perimeter} \times \text{apothem}$:

$$\frac{1}{2} \times 6 \times \frac{4}{\sqrt{3}} \times 2 = \frac{24}{\sqrt{3}} = 8\sqrt{3}$$

which is option C.
