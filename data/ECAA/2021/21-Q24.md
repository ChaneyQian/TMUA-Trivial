## 题目
Evaluate

$$\dfrac{\left( 2 + \sqrt{3} \right)^{4} - \left( 2 - \sqrt{3} \right)^{4}}{8}$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 16$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 18$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 22$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 2\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 8\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 14\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 17\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad 56\sqrt{3}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
F

## 解析
Write $A = 2 + \sqrt{3}$ and $B = 2 - \sqrt{3}$, and use the difference of two squares:

$$A^4 - B^4 = \left(A^2 - B^2\right)\left(A^2 + B^2\right)$$

$$A^2 = 7 + 4\sqrt{3}, \qquad B^2 = 7 - 4\sqrt{3}$$

$$A^2 - B^2 = 8\sqrt{3}, \qquad A^2 + B^2 = 14$$

So $A^4 - B^4 = 8\sqrt{3} \times 14 = 112\sqrt{3}$, and

$$\frac{112\sqrt{3}}{8} = 14\sqrt{3}$$

which is option F.
