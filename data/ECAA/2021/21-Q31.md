## 题目
Which of the following is the largest in value?

(All angles are in radians.)

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \cos 0.5$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \cos 0.75$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \cos 1$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \sin 0.5$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \sin 0.75$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \sin 1$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
All six angles lie in $\left(0, \dfrac{\pi}{2}\right)$, where $\cos$ is decreasing and $\sin$ is increasing. So the largest cosine is $\cos 0.5$ and the largest sine is $\sin 1$, and only these two need comparing.

Since $0.5 < 1 < \dfrac{\pi}{2} - 0.5 \approx 1.071$, and $\sin 1 = \cos\left(\dfrac{\pi}{2} - 1\right)$ with $\dfrac{\pi}{2} - 1 \approx 0.571 > 0.5$, the decreasing property of $\cos$ gives

$$\cos 0.5 > \cos\left(\frac{\pi}{2} - 1\right) = \sin 1$$

So $\cos 0.5$ is the largest, which is option A.
