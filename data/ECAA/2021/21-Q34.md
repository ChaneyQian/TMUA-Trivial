## 题目
What is the complete set of possible values of $k$ for which the graphs of $y = k$ and $y = x\lvert 4 - x \rvert$ have exactly two distinct points of intersection?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad k < -4,\; k = 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad k = -4,\; 0$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad k > 4$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 0 < k < 4$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad k = 0,\; 4$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad k < 4$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad k = 0,\; k > 4$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
Remove the modulus by splitting at $x = 4$:

$$y = \begin{cases} x(4 - x) & x \le 4 \\ x(x - 4) & x > 4 \end{cases}$$

On $x \le 4$ the graph is a downward parabola rising from $-\infty$ to a maximum of $4$ at $x = 2$, then falling to $0$ at $x = 4$. On $x > 4$ it increases from $0$ without bound.

Counting intersections with the horizontal line $y = k$:

$k < 0$: one, on the far left of the parabola.

$k = 0$: two, at $x = 0$ and $x = 4$.

$0 < k < 4$: three — two on the parabola and one on the right-hand branch.

$k = 4$: two — the parabola is tangent at $x = 2$, plus one on the right-hand branch at $x = 2 + 2\sqrt{2}$.

$k > 4$: one, since the parabola no longer reaches $k$.

Exactly two intersections occur only for $k = 0$ and $k = 4$, which is option E.
