## 题目
The diagram shows a partial **sketch** of the curve $y = 2x^3 - 9x^2 + 12x + p$ where $p$ is constant.

![[Image/16-Q28-fig1.png]]

The curve cuts the $x$-axis at one point only and does not touch the $x$-axis at any other point.

The $y$-coordinates of the turning points of the curve are both positive.

Find the complete range of values of $p$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad p > -5$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad p > -4$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -5 < p < -4$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 4 < p < 5$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad p > 4$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad p > 5$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Differentiate to locate the turning points.

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = 6x^2 - 18x + 12 = 6(x-1)(x-2)$$

So the turning points are at $x = 1$ (maximum) and $x = 2$ (minimum), with

$$y(1) = 2 - 9 + 12 + p = 5 + p, \qquad y(2) = 16 - 36 + 24 + p = 4 + p$$

Both $y$-coordinates are positive exactly when $4 + p > 0$, i.e. $p > -4$, since $5 + p > 4 + p$.

When the minimum lies above the $x$-axis the cubic crosses the axis once only, to the left of the maximum, and never touches it, so this single condition is also enough for the rest of the description. Hence $p > -4$, which is option B.
