## 题目
The real numbers $a$, $b$ and $c$ are non-zero and $a \le b$

Consider these three statements:

1. $\dfrac{1}{a} \ge \dfrac{1}{b}$
2. $2^a \le 2^b$
3. $ac \le bc$

Which of the above statements **must** be true?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{none}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{1 only}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{2 only}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{3 only}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{1 and 2 only}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{1 and 3 only}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \text{2 and 3 only}$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad \text{1, 2 and 3}$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Statement 1: take $a = -1$ and $b = 1$, so $a \le b$. Then $\dfrac{1}{a} = -1$ and $\dfrac{1}{b} = 1$, so $\dfrac{1}{a} \ge \dfrac{1}{b}$ fails. Not necessarily true.

Statement 2: $2^x$ is an increasing function, so $a \le b \implies 2^a \le 2^b$. Always true.

Statement 3: take $a = 1$, $b = 2$ and $c = -1$. Then $ac = -1$ and $bc = -2$, so $ac \le bc$ fails. Not necessarily true.

Only statement 2 must hold, which is option C.
