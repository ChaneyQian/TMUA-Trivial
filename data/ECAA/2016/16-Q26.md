## 题目
Each of the positive real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$, and $d$ is increased by 20%.

What is the resulting percentage increase in the value of the expression $\dfrac{abc}{2d} + \dfrac{3bcd}{a+b+c}$ ?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 20\%$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 40\%$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 44\%$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 88\%$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 92\%$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 116\%$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \text{It depends on the values of } a,\ b,\ c, \text{ and } d.$$

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Replace each of $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ by $1.2$ times itself and treat the two terms separately.

The first term has three factors on top and one underneath:

$$\frac{(1.2a)(1.2b)(1.2c)}{2(1.2d)} = 1.2^2 \cdot \frac{abc}{2d} = 1.44 \cdot \frac{abc}{2d}$$

The second term also has three factors on top, and its denominator $a+b+c$ scales by a single factor of $1.2$:

$$\frac{3(1.2b)(1.2c)(1.2d)}{1.2(a+b+c)} = 1.44 \cdot \frac{3bcd}{a+b+c}$$

Both terms are multiplied by the same factor $1.44$, so their sum is too, whatever the values of $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$. That is an increase of $44\%$, which is option C.
