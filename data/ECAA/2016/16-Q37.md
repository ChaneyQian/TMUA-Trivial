## 题目
The mean mass of a group of 20 people is $84\,\text{kg}$. When $N$ people leave the group, the mean mass is reduced by $3\,\text{kg}$.

Which one of the following is an expression, in kilograms, for the mean mass of the people who left the group?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{60}{N} - 81$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{3320}{N} - 81$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{3420}{N} - 87$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 87 - \dfrac{60}{N}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 81 + \dfrac{60}{N}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 87 + \dfrac{60}{N}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 81 + \dfrac{3320}{N}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
The total mass of the original group is

$$20 \times 84 = 1680$$

After $N$ people leave, $20 - N$ people remain with mean mass $84 - 3 = 81$, so their total mass is

$$81(20 - N) = 1620 - 81N$$

The total mass of those who left is therefore

$$1680 - (1620 - 81N) = 60 + 81N$$

Dividing by the number who left gives

$$\frac{60 + 81N}{N} = 81 + \frac{60}{N}$$

which is option E.
