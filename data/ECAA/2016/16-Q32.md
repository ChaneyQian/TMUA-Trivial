## 题目
A curve has equation $y = 3x^2 + 2$ and a line has equation $y = 5x - 6$

What is the shortest distance parallel to the $y$-axis between the curve and the line?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{5}{6}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{11}{6}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{49}{12}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{71}{12}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{73}{12}$$

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
A distance measured parallel to the $y$-axis is the difference of the two $y$-values at the same $x$:

$$d = \left(3x^2 + 2\right) - (5x - 6) = 3x^2 - 5x + 8$$

The discriminant $25 - 96 < 0$, so $d > 0$ everywhere: the line and the curve never meet, and the minimum of $d$ is the required distance. Complete the square:

$$d = 3\left(x - \frac{5}{6}\right)^2 + 8 - \frac{25}{12}$$

$$d_{\min} = \frac{96 - 25}{12} = \frac{71}{12}$$

which is option D.
