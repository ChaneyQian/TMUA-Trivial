## 题目
The diagram shows the cross section of a cylindrical roll of paper towels which are used in domestic kitchens.

[diagram not to scale]

![[Image/16-Q25-fig1.png]]

The roll consists of 64 towels, each of which is a square of side $250\,\text{mm}$, and they are tightly wrapped around an inner cardboard tube of diameter $5\,\text{cm}$. The outer diameter of the roll is $11\,\text{cm}$ and the length of the cylinder is $25\,\text{cm}$.

The thickness of an individual towel, in millimetres, is estimated from this information.

Which one of these estimates is the best?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0.01\,\text{mm}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 0.05\,\text{mm}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 0.1\,\text{mm}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 0.5\,\text{mm}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 1\,\text{mm}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 2\,\text{mm}$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Each towel is $250\,\text{mm} = 25\,\text{cm}$ square, matching the $25\,\text{cm}$ length of the cylinder, so the towels are joined end to end along the roll. The total length of paper is

$$64 \times 25 = 1600\,\text{cm}$$

The cross section of the paper is the annulus between the outer radius $5.5\,\text{cm}$ and the inner radius $2.5\,\text{cm}$:

$$\pi\left(5.5^2 - 2.5^2\right) = 24\pi \approx 75.4\,\text{cm}^2$$

Unrolled, that same cross section is a thin rectangle of length $1600\,\text{cm}$ and width equal to the thickness $t$:

$$1600\,t = 75.4 \implies t \approx 0.047\,\text{cm} \approx 0.47\,\text{mm}$$

The closest estimate is $0.5\,\text{mm}$, which is option D.
