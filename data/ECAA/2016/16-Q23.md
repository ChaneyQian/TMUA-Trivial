## 题目
The first three terms of a geometric series are equal to the first, fifth and sixth terms respectively of an arithmetic series.

Given that the terms in the geometric series are all different, find the value of the common ratio.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -1$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -\dfrac{4}{9}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -\dfrac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{4}{9}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 1$$

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Let the arithmetic series have first term $a$ and common difference $d$. Its first, fifth and sixth terms are $a$, $a+4d$ and $a+5d$.

These are consecutive terms of a geometric series, so

$$(a+4d)^2 = a(a+5d)$$

$$a^2 + 8ad + 16d^2 = a^2 + 5ad$$

$$d(3a + 16d) = 0$$

If $d = 0$ all the terms would be equal, which is excluded, so $a = -\dfrac{16}{3}d$.

$$r = \frac{a+4d}{a} = 1 + \frac{4d}{a} = 1 + \frac{4d}{-\frac{16}{3}d} = 1 - \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{4}$$

So the common ratio is $\dfrac{1}{4}$, which is option D.
