## 题目
One of the roots of the equation $2x^2 + 9x - k = 0$, where $k$ is a constant, is 4 more than the other root.

The value of $k$ is

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -\dfrac{77}{8}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -\dfrac{73}{8}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -\dfrac{65}{8}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad -\dfrac{17}{8}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{55}{8}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \dfrac{175}{8}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Let the roots be $\alpha$ and $\alpha + 4$.

The sum of the roots of $2x^2 + 9x - k = 0$ is $-\dfrac{9}{2}$:

$$2\alpha + 4 = -\frac{9}{2} \implies \alpha = -\frac{17}{4}$$

The product of the roots is $-\dfrac{k}{2}$:

$$\alpha(\alpha+4) = \left(-\frac{17}{4}\right)\left(-\frac{1}{4}\right) = \frac{17}{16}$$

$$-\frac{k}{2} = \frac{17}{16} \implies k = -\frac{17}{8}$$

which is option D.
