## 题目
A sector of a circle of radius $x\,\text{cm}$ contains an angle of $\theta$ radians.

The perimeter of the sector is $12\,\text{cm}$.

What is the maximum possible value of the area of the sector, in square centimetres, as $x$ varies?

[The area of a sector of radius $r$ containing an angle of $\theta$ radians is $\dfrac{1}{2}r^2\theta$

The length of an arc subtended by an angle $\theta$ radians is $r\theta$, where $r$ is the radius.]

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 6$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 9$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 18$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 27$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The perimeter is two radii plus the arc:

$$2x + x\theta = 12 \implies \theta = \frac{12 - 2x}{x}$$

Substitute into the area formula:

$$A = \frac{1}{2}x^2\theta = \frac{1}{2}x^2 \cdot \frac{12-2x}{x} = 6x - x^2$$

This is a quadratic in $x$ with negative $x^2$ coefficient, so it is greatest at $x = 3$:

$$A = 6(3) - 3^2 = 9$$

(Here $\theta = 2$ radians, which is admissible.) The maximum area is $9\,\text{cm}^2$, which is option C.
