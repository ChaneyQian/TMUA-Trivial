## 题目
A bag only contains $2n$ blue balls and $n$ red balls. All the balls are identical apart from colour.

One ball is randomly selected and not replaced. A second ball is then randomly selected.

What is the probability that at least one of the selected balls is red?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{n-1}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{3n-1}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{4n-2}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{4n}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{5n-1}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \dfrac{5n-5}{3(3n-1)}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Work with the complement: at least one red means not both blue.

There are $3n$ balls in total, of which $2n$ are blue.

$$P(\text{both blue}) = \frac{2n}{3n} \times \frac{2n-1}{3n-1} = \frac{2(2n-1)}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$P(\text{at least one red}) = 1 - \frac{4n-2}{3(3n-1)} = \frac{3(3n-1) - (4n-2)}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$= \frac{9n - 3 - 4n + 2}{3(3n-1)} = \frac{5n-1}{3(3n-1)}$$

which is option E.
