## 题目
Find the total area enclosed between the curve $y = x(x+a)(x-2a)$, the $x$-axis and the lines $x = -a$ and $x = a$, where $a$ is a positive constant.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{2}{3}a^4$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{5}{6}a^4$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{4}{3}a^4$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{3}{2}a^4$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{7}{2}a^4$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Expand the cubic:

$$y = x(x+a)(x-2a) = x^3 - ax^2 - 2a^2x$$

The curve meets the $x$-axis at $x = -a$, $0$, $2a$, so on $-a < x < 0$ it lies above the axis and on $0 < x < a$ it lies below. The two regions must be integrated separately.

$$\int x^3 - ax^2 - 2a^2x \ \mathrm{d}x = \frac{x^4}{4} - \frac{ax^3}{3} - a^2x^2$$

$$\int_{-a}^{0} y \ \mathrm{d}x = 0 - \left(\frac{a^4}{4} + \frac{a^4}{3} - a^4\right) = \frac{5}{12}a^4$$

$$\int_{0}^{a} y \ \mathrm{d}x = \frac{a^4}{4} - \frac{a^4}{3} - a^4 = -\frac{13}{12}a^4$$

Taking the modulus of the second, the total area is

$$\frac{5}{12}a^4 + \frac{13}{12}a^4 = \frac{18}{12}a^4 = \frac{3}{2}a^4$$

which is option D.
