## 题目
Last Saturday, Jez visited his friend in a neighbouring town.

He cycled the first $5\,\text{km}$ at an average speed of $10\,\text{km}/\text{h}$ and then immediately caught a bus which travelled the remaining $10\,\text{km}$ at an average speed of $30\,\text{km}/\text{h}$.

What was Jez's average speed for the whole journey?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 12.5\,\text{km}/\text{h}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 18\,\text{km}/\text{h}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 20\,\text{km}/\text{h}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 22\,\text{km}/\text{h}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 28\,\text{km}/\text{h}$$
