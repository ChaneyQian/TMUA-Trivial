## 题目
Which one of the following is a simplification of $\dfrac{x^2 - 4}{x^2 - 2x}$ where $x \neq 2$ and $x \neq 0$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{x - 4}{x - 2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{x - 2}{x}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{2}{x}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{x + 2}{x}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{x + 2}{x + 1}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Factorise the numerator and denominator: $\dfrac{(x-2)(x+2)}{x(x-2)}$

Cancel the $(x-2)$ factor to leave $\dfrac{x+2}{x}$
