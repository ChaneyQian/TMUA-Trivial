## 题目
The first term of a convergent geometric series is $8$

The fifth term is $2$

The sixth term is real and positive.

What is the sum to infinity of this series?

(The sum to infinity of a convergent geometric series is given by $\dfrac{a}{1-r}$, where $a$ is the first term and $r$ is the common ratio.)

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 8\left(1 + \sqrt{2}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 8\left(1 - \sqrt{2}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 8\left(2 + \sqrt{2}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 8\left(2 - \sqrt{2}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 16$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{8\sqrt[5]{4}}{\sqrt[5]{4} - 1}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \frac{8\sqrt[5]{4}}{\sqrt[5]{4} + 1}$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
The first term is $a = 8$

The fifth term is $ar^4 = 2$

Combining these two equations gives

$$r^4 = \frac{1}{4}$$

$$r^2 = \frac{1}{2}$$

So $r^2 = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$, taking positive square root because sixth term is positive.

$$\text{Sum to infinity} = \frac{a}{1-r} = \frac{8}{1 - \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}} = 16\left(1 + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) = 8\left(2 + \sqrt{2}\right)$$
