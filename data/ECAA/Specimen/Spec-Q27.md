## 题目
For any real numbers $a$, $b$ and $c$ where $a \geq b$, consider these three statements:

$$\mathbf{1} \quad -b \geq -a$$

$$\mathbf{2} \quad a^2 + b^2 \geq 2ab$$

$$\mathbf{3} \quad ac \geq bc$$

Which of the above statements must be true?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{none of them}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{1 only}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{2 only}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{3 only}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{1 and 2 only}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{1 and 3 only}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \text{2 and 3 only}$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad \text{1, 2 and 3}$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
Statement 1 subtracts $a + b$ from both sides.

Statement 2 can be written as $(a-b)^2 \geq 0$. This is always true.

Statement 3 can be false if $c$ is negative, for example $a = 2$, $b = 1$, $c = -1$
