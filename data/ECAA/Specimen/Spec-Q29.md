## 题目
The sequence $a_n$ is given by the rule:

$$a_1 = 2$$

$$a_{n+1} = a_n + (-1)^n \ \text{ for } n \geq 1$$

What is $\displaystyle\sum_{n=1}^{100} a_n$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 150$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 250$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -4750$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 5150$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4\left(1 - \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{100}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 4\left(\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{100} - 1\right)$$

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
The sequence begins $a_1 = 2$, $a_2 = 2 + (-1) = 1$, $a_3 = 2 = 1 + (-1)^2 = 2$, $a_4 = 1$, etc.

So the sum of the first 100 terms is $2 + 1 + 2 + 1 + \dots = 50 \times 2 + 50 \times 1 = 150$
