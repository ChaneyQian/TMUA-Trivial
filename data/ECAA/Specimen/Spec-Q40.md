## 题目
The triangle $PQR$ has a right angle at $R$.

The length of $PQ$ is $4\ \text{cm}$, correct to the nearest centimetre.

The length of $PR$ is $2\ \text{cm}$, correct to the nearest centimetre.

Find the minimum possible length, in centimetres, of $QR$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \sqrt{6} - \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 2\sqrt{3} - \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2\sqrt{5} - \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 2\sqrt{5}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 2\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \sqrt{6}$$

## 答案
F

## 解析
Since lengths are given to the nearest cm, the minimum possible length of $PQ$ and the maximum possible length of $PR$ are $3.5$ and $2.5$ respectively.

Use Pythagoras' Theorem:

$$QR^2 = 3.5^2 - 2.5^2 = 12.25 - 6.25 = 6$$

Take square root:

$$QR = \sqrt{6}$$
