## 题目
Which one of the following numbers is largest in value?

(All angles are given in radians.)

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \tan\left(\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \log_{10} 100$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \sin^{10}\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \log_2 10$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \left(\sqrt{2} - 1\right)^{10}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
A: $\tan\left(\dfrac{3\pi}{4}\right) = -1$

B: $\log_{10} 100 = 2$

C: $\sin^{10} x \leq 1$ for any $x$; in this case, it equals $1$

D: $\log_2 10 > \log_2 8 = 3$

E: $0 < \sqrt{2} - 1 < 1$ (as $1 < \sqrt{2} < 2$), so raising it to the power of 10 still gives a number less than $1$

So the largest of the five numbers is D: $\log_2 10$
