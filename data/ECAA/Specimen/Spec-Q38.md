## 题目
The straight line with equation $y = mx + 3$, where $m > 0$ and $m \neq 1$, is perpendicular to the line with equation $y = px + 2$

The lines cut the $x$-axis at the points $L$ and $M$ respectively. The length of $LM$ is $5$ units.

What is the value of $m + p$ given that $m > 1$?

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -\frac{5}{6}$$

## 解析
As the lines are perpendicular, the product of their gradients is $-1$

$$\text{So } mp = 1 \qquad p = -\frac{1}{m}$$

The lines intersect the $x$-axis at $L$ and $M$, when $y = 0$

$$L: \quad mx + 3 = 0 \qquad x = -\frac{3}{m}$$

$$M: \quad px + 2 = 0 \qquad x = -\frac{2}{p} = 2m$$

As the distance between these points is $5$, then

$$2m + \frac{3}{m} = 5$$

Rearranging gives:

$$2m^2 - 5m + 3 = 0$$

Factorising to:

$$(2m - 3)(m - 1) = 0 \qquad \text{giving } m = \frac{3}{2} \ \text{ or } \ 1$$

As $m \neq 1$, then $m = \dfrac{3}{2}$, giving $p = -\dfrac{2}{3}$

$$m + p = \frac{3}{2} - \frac{2}{3} = \frac{5}{6}$$
