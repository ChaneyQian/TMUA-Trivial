## 题目
The sum of the roots of the equation $2^{2x} - 8 \times 2^x + 15 = 0$ is

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 8$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2\log_{10} 2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \log_{10}\left(\frac{15}{4}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{\log_{10} 15}{\log_{10} 2}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Let $y = 2^x$, then $y^2 - 8y + 15 = 0$. Solve to give $y = 3$ or $5$

$2^x = 3$ implies that $x = \dfrac{\log_{10} 3}{\log_{10} 2}$. Similarly, the other solution is $\dfrac{\log_{10} 5}{\log_{10} 2}$

$$\text{Sum of roots} = \frac{\log_{10} 3 + \log_{10} 5}{\log_{10} 2} = \frac{\log_{10} 15}{\log_{10} 2}$$
