## 题目
For what values of the non-zero real number $a$ does the quadratic equation

$$ax^2 + (a - 2)x = 2$$

have distinct real roots?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{all values of } a$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad a = -2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad a > -2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad a \neq -2$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{no values of } a$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
For real distinct roots the discriminant condition gives:

$$(a-2)^2 > 4a(-2) \quad \text{or} \quad (a-2)^2 - 4a(-2) > 0$$

$$a^2 + 4a + 4 > 0$$

$$(a+2)^2 > 0$$

This is true for all values of $a$ except $-2$
