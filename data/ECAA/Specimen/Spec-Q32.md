## 题目
Tangents are drawn from a point $P$ to a circle of radius $10\ \text{cm}$.

The centre of the circle is $C$ and the distance $PC$ is $20\ \text{cm}$.

![[Image/Spec-Q32-fig1.png]]

Which one of the following is an expression for the shaded area in square centimetres?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{100}{3}\left(3\sqrt{3} - \pi\right)$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{100}{3}\left(3\sqrt{5} - \pi\right)$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{50}{3}\left(6\sqrt{3} - \pi\right)$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{50}{3}\left(6\sqrt{5} - \pi\right)$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{50}{3}\left(\sqrt{3} - 2\pi\right)$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{50}{3}\left(2\pi - \sqrt{3}\right)$$

## 答案
A

## 解析
![[Image/Spec-Q32-sol1.png]]

Let the tangent touch the circle at $S$ and $T$. The line $PT$ is at right angles to the radius $TC$.

Using Pythagoras' theorem:

$$PT^2 + 10^2 = PC^2$$

$$PT = \sqrt{400 - 100} = \sqrt{300} = 10\sqrt{3}$$

Angle $PCT = 60^\circ$ because $\cos PCT = \dfrac{10}{20} = \dfrac{1}{2}$

The required area $=$ (area of $PTCS$) $-$ (area of the sector $SCT$)

$$= \left(\frac{1}{2} \times 10\sqrt{3} \times 10 \times 2\right) - \left(\frac{\frac{2\pi}{3}}{2\pi} \times \pi \times 10^2\right)$$

$$= 100\sqrt{3} - \frac{100}{3}\pi = \frac{100}{3}\left(3\sqrt{3} - \pi\right)$$
