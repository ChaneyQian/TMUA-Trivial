## 题目
$R$ is the sum to infinity of the geometric progression: $1$, $\sqrt{0.3}$, $0.3$, $0.3\sqrt{0.3}$, $\ldots$

$S$ is the sum to infinity of the geometric progression: $1$, $-\sqrt{0.3}$, $0.3$, $-0.3\sqrt{0.3}$, $\ldots$

What is the value of $R-S$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -\frac{20}{7}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -\frac{2\sqrt{30}}{7}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -\frac{2\sqrt{3}}{7}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{2\sqrt{3}}{7}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{2\sqrt{30}}{7}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{20}{7}$$

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Both progressions have first term $1$; the common ratios are $\sqrt{0.3}$ and $-\sqrt{0.3}$, and $\left|\sqrt{0.3}\right| < 1$, so both sums to infinity exist.

$$R = \frac{1}{1-\sqrt{0.3}}, \qquad S = \frac{1}{1+\sqrt{0.3}}$$

Subtract over the common denominator $1 - 0.3 = 0.7$.

$$R - S = \frac{\left(1+\sqrt{0.3}\right)-\left(1-\sqrt{0.3}\right)}{0.7} = \frac{2\sqrt{0.3}}{0.7} = \frac{20\sqrt{0.3}}{7}$$

Now $\sqrt{0.3} = \sqrt{\dfrac{3}{10}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{30}}{10}$.

$$R - S = \frac{20}{7} \times \frac{\sqrt{30}}{10} = \frac{2\sqrt{30}}{7}$$

This is option E.
