## 题目
The cubic equation

$$f(x) = p^{\frac{2}{3}}x^{3} + px^{2} + p^{\frac{1}{3}}x + 3$$

where $p$ is a positive constant, has exactly one point where $f'(x) = 0$

What is the value of $p$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{3}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 6$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Differentiate:

$$f'(x) = 3p^{\frac{2}{3}}x^2 + 2px + p^{\frac{1}{3}}$$

This quadratic has exactly one root when its discriminant is zero:

$$(2p)^2 - 4 \cdot 3p^{\frac{2}{3}} \cdot p^{\frac{1}{3}} = 4p^2 - 12p = 0$$

$$4p(p - 3) = 0$$

Since $p$ is positive, $p = 3$, which is option E.
