## 题目

Shop X reduces its prices by 10%.

Shop Y reduces its prices by $\dfrac{1}{4}$

Both shops have an identical shirt in the sale for £28.80

What was the difference between the original prices of this shirt in shops X and Y?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \pounds 4.00$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \pounds 4.32$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \pounds 5.00$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \pounds 5.62$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \pounds 6.40$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \pounds 6.72$$
