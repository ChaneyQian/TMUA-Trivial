## 题目
The equation gives $y$ in terms of $x$:

$$y = 3\left(\sqrt{\left(\frac{x+1}{2}\right)}\right) - 1$$

Which one of the following is a rearrangement for $x$ in terms of $y$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad x = 2\left(\frac{y+1}{3}\right) - 1$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad x = 2\left(\frac{y+1}{3}\right) + 1$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad x = 2\left(\frac{y+1}{3}\right)^{2} - 1$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad x = 2\left(\frac{y+1}{3}\right)^{2} + 1$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad x = 3\left(\frac{y+1}{2}\right)^{2} - 1$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad x = 3\left(\frac{y+1}{2}\right)^{2} + 1$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad x = \left(\frac{2(y+1)}{3}\right)^{2} - 1$$
