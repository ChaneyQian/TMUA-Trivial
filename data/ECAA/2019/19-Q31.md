## 题目
$P$ is the area of the region bounded by the curve $y = 5x - x^{2}$ and the $x$-axis.

$Q$ is the area of the region between the curve $y = 5x - x^{2}$ and the line $y = 2x$

What is the ratio $Q:P$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 27:125$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 54:125$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 81:125$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 27:250$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 81:250$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
The curve meets the $x$-axis where $5x - x^2 = 0$, that is at $x = 0$ and $x = 5$, so

$$P = \int_0^5 (5x - x^2)\,dx = \left[\frac{5x^2}{2} - \frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^5 = \frac{125}{2} - \frac{125}{3} = \frac{125}{6}$$

The curve meets the line where $5x - x^2 = 2x$, that is $x(3 - x) = 0$, so at $x = 0$ and $x = 3$. Between these values the curve lies above the line, so

$$Q = \int_0^3 \bigl((5x - x^2) - 2x\bigr)dx = \int_0^3 (3x - x^2)\,dx = \left[\frac{3x^2}{2} - \frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^3 = \frac{27}{2} - 9 = \frac{9}{2}$$

Hence

$$Q : P = \frac{9}{2} : \frac{125}{6} = 27 : 125$$

which is option A.
