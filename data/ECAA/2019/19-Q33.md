## 题目
Find the sum of the real roots of the equation

$$49^{x} - 7^{x+1} + 12 = 0$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 7$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \log_{10} 12$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \log_{10}\left(\frac{12}{49}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{\log_{10} 12}{\log_{10} 7}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{1}{\log_{10} 7}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Write $49^x = (7^x)^2$ and $7^{x+1} = 7 \cdot 7^x$, then substitute $u = 7^x$:

$$u^2 - 7u + 12 = 0$$

$$(u - 3)(u - 4) = 0 \implies u = 3 \ \text{ or } \ u = 4$$

Both values are positive, so both give real roots $x = \log_7 3$ and $x = \log_7 4$. Their sum is

$$\log_7 3 + \log_7 4 = \log_7 12 = \frac{\log_{10} 12}{\log_{10} 7}$$

which is option E.
