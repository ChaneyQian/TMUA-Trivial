## 题目
The equation of a curve $C$ is given by

$$y = \frac{x^{2}-2}{\sqrt{x}}$$

Find the gradient of $C$ at the point $\left(2, \sqrt{2}\right)$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{5}{4}\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{7}{4}\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{7}{2}\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{9}{2}\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 8\sqrt{2}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Write the equation with fractional powers.

$$y = \frac{x^{2}-2}{\sqrt{x}} = x^{\frac{3}{2}} - 2x^{-\frac{1}{2}}$$

Differentiate.

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{3}{2}x^{\frac{1}{2}} + x^{-\frac{3}{2}}$$

Substitute $x = 2$.

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{3}{2}\sqrt{2} + \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}} = \frac{3}{2}\sqrt{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{4} = \frac{7}{4}\sqrt{2}$$

The gradient is $\dfrac{7}{4}\sqrt{2}$, which is option C.
