## 题目
The coefficient of $x^{3}$ in the expansion of $\left(2\sqrt{p}+3x\right)^{5}$ is 8640.

What is the value of $p$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 8$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 16$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 72$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 80$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
The term in $x^{3}$ of $\left(2\sqrt{p}+3x\right)^{5}$ comes from taking $3x$ three times and $2\sqrt{p}$ twice.

$$\binom{5}{3}\left(2\sqrt{p}\right)^{2}(3x)^{3} = 10 \times 4p \times 27x^{3} = 1080p\,x^{3}$$

Set the coefficient equal to $8640$.

$$1080p = 8640 \implies p = 8$$

This is option B.
