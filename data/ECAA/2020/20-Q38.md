## 题目
Find the product of the real roots of the equation

$$\left(\log_{10} x^2\right)^2 + \log_{10} x = 3$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 10^{-\frac{3}{2}}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 10^{-1}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 10^{-\frac{1}{2}}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 10^{-\frac{1}{4}}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 10^{\frac{3}{5}}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 10^{1}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The term $\log_{10} x$ requires $x > 0$, and then $\log_{10} x^2 = 2\log_{10} x$. Put $u = \log_{10} x$:

$$(2u)^2 + u = 3$$

$$4u^2 + u - 3 = 0 \implies (4u - 3)(u + 1) = 0$$

So $u = \dfrac{3}{4}$ or $u = -1$, giving the roots $x = 10^{\frac{3}{4}}$ and $x = 10^{-1}$.

Their product is

$$10^{\frac{3}{4}} \times 10^{-1} = 10^{-\frac{1}{4}}$$

which is option D.
