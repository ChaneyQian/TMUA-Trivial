## 题目
![[Image/20-Q25-fig1.png]]

A wall is used to mark out the perimeter of a rectangular field $WXYZ$.

Walls are also used to divide $WXYZ$ into four identical rectangular fields, $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$, as shown.

The total length of wall used is 260 m.

What is the length of $WZ$ that maximises the area $P$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 20\ \text{m}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 26\ \text{m}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 32.5\ \text{m}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 40\ \text{m}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 52\ \text{m}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 65\ \text{m}$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Let $WZ = w$ and $WX = \ell$. From the diagram the three internal walls are parallel to $WZ$, so each has length $w$, and they split $WX$ into four equal parts.

$$2(w + \ell) + 3w = 260 \implies 5w + 2\ell = 260 \implies \ell = \frac{260 - 5w}{2}$$

Field $P$ has dimensions $w$ by $\dfrac{\ell}{4}$, so

$$A = \frac{w\ell}{4} = \frac{w(260 - 5w)}{8} = \frac{260w - 5w^2}{8}$$

$$\frac{\mathrm{d}A}{\mathrm{d}w} = \frac{260 - 10w}{8} = 0 \implies w = 26$$

Since $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}^2A}{\mathrm{d}w^2} = -\dfrac{10}{8} < 0$ this is a maximum, so $WZ = 26\ \text{m}$, which is option B.
