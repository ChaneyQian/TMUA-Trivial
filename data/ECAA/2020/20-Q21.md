## 题目
Given that

$$\int_4^9 f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x = 3$$

and that

$$g(x) = 2f(x) + 1$$

find

$$\int_4^9 g(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 6$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 7$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 8$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 9$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 10$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 11$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
F

## 解析
Integration is linear, and the integral of the constant $1$ over $[4, 9]$ is the width of the interval.

$$\int_4^9 g(x)\,\mathrm{d}x = 2\int_4^9 f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x + \int_4^9 1\,\mathrm{d}x$$

$$= 2 \times 3 + (9 - 4) = 6 + 5 = 11$$

which is option F.
