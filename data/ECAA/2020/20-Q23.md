## 题目
![[Image/20-Q23-fig1.png]]

The area of the regular octagon is $32\sqrt{2}\ \text{cm}^2$.

What is the length, in cm, of the straight line $PT$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 8$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 16$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 4\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 8\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4\sqrt{2\sqrt{2}}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 16\sqrt{2\sqrt{2}}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Let $a$ be the distance from the centre of the octagon to each vertex. Joining the centre to the eight vertices cuts the octagon into eight isosceles triangles, each with two sides of length $a$ and included angle $\dfrac{360^\circ}{8} = 45^\circ$.

$$\text{area} = 8 \times \tfrac{1}{2}a^2 \sin 45^\circ = 4a^2 \times \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = 2\sqrt{2}\,a^2$$

$$2\sqrt{2}\,a^2 = 32\sqrt{2} \implies a^2 = 16 \implies a = 4$$

In the diagram the vertices run $P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, W$ in order, so $P$ and $T$ are opposite vertices and $PT$ passes through the centre.

$$PT = 2a = 8$$

which is option A.
