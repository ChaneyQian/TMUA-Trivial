## 题目
What is the area of the region enclosed between the curve $y = \frac{1}{2}x^2$, the line $y = -x$, and the lines $x = 1$ and $x = 3$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{1}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 6$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{25}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{28}{3}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
On $1 \leq x \leq 3$ the curve $y = \tfrac{1}{2}x^2$ lies above the line $y = -x$, so the area is the integral of the difference.

$$\int_1^3 \left(\tfrac{1}{2}x^2 - (-x)\right) \mathrm{d}x = \int_1^3 \left(\tfrac{1}{2}x^2 + x\right) \mathrm{d}x$$

$$= \left[\frac{x^3}{6} + \frac{x^2}{2}\right]_1^3 = \left(\frac{27}{6} + \frac{9}{2}\right) - \left(\frac{1}{6} + \frac{1}{2}\right)$$

$$= 9 - \frac{2}{3} = \frac{25}{3}$$

which is option E.
