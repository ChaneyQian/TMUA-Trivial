## 题目
The line L with equation $y = mx + c$, where $m > 0$ and $c \geq 0$, passes through the point $(2, 4)$.

A line is drawn through the point $(2, 4)$ perpendicular to L.

The triangle enclosed between the two lines and the $y$-axis has area 5 square units.

What is the **larger** of the two possible values of $m$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -0.5$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 0.5$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 1.25$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 5$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Since L passes through $(2, 4)$, its intercept is $c = 4 - 2m$.

The perpendicular through $(2, 4)$ has gradient $-\dfrac{1}{m}$, so its $y$-intercept is

$$c' = 4 + \frac{2}{m}$$

The triangle has vertices $(2, 4)$, $(0, c)$ and $(0, c')$. Its base lies along the $y$-axis with length

$$|c' - c| = \left|\frac{2}{m} + 2m\right| = 2\left(m + \frac{1}{m}\right) \quad (m > 0)$$

and the perpendicular height from $(2,4)$ to the $y$-axis is $2$. So

$$\frac{1}{2} \times 2\left(m + \frac{1}{m}\right) \times 2 = 5 \implies m + \frac{1}{m} = \frac{5}{2}$$

$$2m^2 - 5m + 2 = 0 \implies m = 2 \ \text{ or } \ m = \frac{1}{2}$$

Both satisfy $m > 0$, and $m = 2$ gives $c = 0$, which is allowed. The larger value is $2$, which is option D.
