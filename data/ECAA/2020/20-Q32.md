## 题目
$P$ and $Q$ are two different geometric progressions.

The $3^{\text{rd}}$ term of each geometric progression is 4.

The $5^{\text{th}}$ term of each geometric progression is 2.

What is the modulus of the difference between the sums to infinity of $P$ and $Q$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 8$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 8\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 16$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 16\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 32$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 32\sqrt{2}$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
Let the first term be $a$ and the common ratio $r$. Then

$$ar^2 = 4, \qquad ar^4 = 2$$

Dividing gives $r^2 = \dfrac{1}{2}$, so $r = \pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$, and in both cases $a = \dfrac{4}{r^2} = 8$. These two values of $r$ give the two different progressions $P$ and $Q$.

$$S_{\pm} = \frac{8}{1 \mp \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}} = \frac{8\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2} \mp 1}$$

$$S_{+} = 8\sqrt{2}\,(\sqrt{2} + 1) = 16 + 8\sqrt{2}, \qquad S_{-} = 8\sqrt{2}\,(\sqrt{2} - 1) = 16 - 8\sqrt{2}$$

The modulus of the difference is $16\sqrt{2}$, which is option E.
