## 题目
The sum of the first 20 terms of an arithmetic progression is 50.

The sum of the next 20 terms of the arithmetic progression is $-50$.

What is the sum of the first 100 terms of the arithmetic progression?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -750$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -350$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -50$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad -\frac{159}{8}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{159}{8}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 50$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 350$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad 750$$

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Let the first term be $a$ and the common difference $d$. The sum of the first 40 terms is $50 + (-50) = 0$, so

$$S_{20} = 10(2a + 19d) = 50 \implies 2a + 19d = 5$$

$$S_{40} = 20(2a + 39d) = 0 \implies 2a + 39d = 0$$

Subtracting gives $20d = -5$, so $d = -\dfrac{1}{4}$ and $2a = -39d = \dfrac{39}{4}$.

Therefore

$$S_{100} = 50(2a + 99d) = 50\left(\frac{39}{4} - \frac{99}{4}\right) = 50 \times (-15) = -750$$

which is option A.
