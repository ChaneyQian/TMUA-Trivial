## 题目
A line with non-zero gradient $m$ is reflected in the line $y = x$

What is the gradient of the reflected line?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad m$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -m$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{1}{m}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad -\frac{1}{m}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Reflection in the line $y = x$ interchanges the coordinates, sending $(x, y)$ to $(y, x)$.

If the original line is $y = mx + k$, the image is obtained by swapping $x$ and $y$:

$$x = my + k \implies y = \frac{1}{m}x - \frac{k}{m}$$

So the gradient of the reflected line is $\dfrac{1}{m}$, which is option C.
