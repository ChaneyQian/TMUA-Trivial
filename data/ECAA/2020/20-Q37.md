## 题目
It is given that

$$2^x = 3^y$$

and

$$x + y = 2$$

Which one of the following is an expression for $x$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 2\log_{10}\frac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \log_{10} 3$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{6}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{3}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{\log_{10} 9}{\log_{10} 5}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{\log_{10} 9}{\log_{10} 6}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \frac{\log_{10} 3}{\log_{10} 2}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
F

## 解析
Substituting $y = 2 - x$ into $2^x = 3^y$ and taking logarithms:

$$x\log_{10} 2 = (2 - x)\log_{10} 3$$

$$x\left(\log_{10} 2 + \log_{10} 3\right) = 2\log_{10} 3$$

$$x = \frac{2\log_{10} 3}{\log_{10} 6} = \frac{\log_{10} 9}{\log_{10} 6}$$

which is option F.
