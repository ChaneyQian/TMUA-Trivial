## 题目
A sequence is generated by

$$x_{n+1} = -\frac{12}{x_n}$$

$n$ is an integer, where $n \geq 1$.

The $50^{\text{th}}$ term of the sequence is 6.

What is the sum of the first fifteen terms of the sequence?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -2$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 6$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 10$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 22$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 26$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 34$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 58$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
Applying the rule twice:

$$x_{n+2} = -\frac{12}{x_{n+1}} = -\frac{12}{-\dfrac{12}{x_n}} = x_n$$

so the sequence has period 2 and its terms alternate between two values.

The 50th term is even-numbered and equals $6$, so every even-numbered term is $6$ and every odd-numbered term is

$$-\frac{12}{6} = -2$$

Among the first fifteen terms there are eight odd-numbered terms and seven even-numbered terms, so the sum is

$$8 \times (-2) + 7 \times 6 = -16 + 42 = 26$$

which is option E.
