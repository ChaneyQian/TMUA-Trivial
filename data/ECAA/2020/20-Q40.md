## 题目
Find the maximum value of the gradient of the curve with equation

$$y = 2 - 4x + 4x^{\frac{3}{2}} - x^2$$

where $x > 0$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -4$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -\frac{8}{9}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Differentiate to obtain the gradient function:

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = -4 + 6x^{\frac{1}{2}} - 2x$$

Substitute $u = \sqrt{x}$, so $u > 0$:

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = -2u^2 + 6u - 4 = -2\left(u - \frac{3}{2}\right)^2 + \frac{1}{2}$$

This downward parabola in $u$ is greatest at $u = \dfrac{3}{2}$, that is $x = \dfrac{9}{4}$, where the gradient equals $\dfrac{1}{2}$.

The maximum gradient is $\dfrac{1}{2}$, which is option C.
