## 题目
Find the number of solutions of the equation

$$14\cos^3 x + 10\sin^2 x \cos x = 13\cos x$$

in the range $-2\pi \leq x \leq 2\pi$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 6$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 8$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 10$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 12$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 14$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Replace $\sin^2 x$ by $1 - \cos^2 x$ and write $c = \cos x$:

$$14c^3 + 10c(1 - c^2) = 13c$$

$$4c^3 - 3c = 0 \implies c\left(4c^2 - 3\right) = 0$$

So $\cos x = 0$ or $\cos x = \pm\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$.

The interval $-2\pi \le x \le 2\pi$ has length $4\pi$, that is two full periods of $\cos x$. Each of the three values of $\cos x$ is attained twice per period, giving $2 \times 2 = 4$ solutions each.

$$3 \times 4 = 12$$

which is option E.
