## 题目

$$y = x^3 + 3\sqrt{5}\,px^2 + 3px + 13$$

has two distinct turning points.

What are all the possible values of $p$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad p < 0,\ p > 0.2$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad p \leq 0,\ p \geq 0.2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 0 < p < 0.2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 0 \leq p \leq 0.2$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad p < 0,\ p > 1.2$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad p \leq 0,\ p \geq 1.2$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 0 < p < 1.2$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad 0 \leq p \leq 1.2$$
