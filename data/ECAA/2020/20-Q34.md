## 题目
The curve

$$y = x^3 + 3\sqrt{5}\,px^2 + 3px + 13$$

has two distinct turning points.

What are all the possible values of $p$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad p < 0,\ p > 0.2$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad p \leq 0,\ p \geq 0.2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 0 < p < 0.2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 0 \leq p \leq 0.2$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad p < 0,\ p > 1.2$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad p \leq 0,\ p \geq 1.2$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 0 < p < 1.2$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad 0 \leq p \leq 1.2$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
A cubic has two distinct turning points exactly when its derivative has two distinct real roots.

$$\frac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 + 6\sqrt{5}\,px + 3p$$

Require a positive discriminant:

$$\left(6\sqrt{5}\,p\right)^2 - 4(3)(3p) > 0$$

$$180p^2 - 36p > 0 \implies 36p(5p - 1) > 0$$

The product is positive when both factors are negative or both positive, giving

$$p < 0 \quad \text{or} \quad p > \frac{1}{5} = 0.2$$

which is option A.
