## 题目
Two circles have centres $P$ and $Q$.

The radius of each circle is 1 cm.

The distance $PQ$ is 1 cm.

What is the area of overlap, in $\text{cm}^2$, of the two circles?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{2\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{2\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{4\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{4\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$
