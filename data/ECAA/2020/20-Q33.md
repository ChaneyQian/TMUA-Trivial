## 题目
Two circles have centres $P$ and $Q$.

The radius of each circle is 1 cm.

The distance $PQ$ is 1 cm.

What is the area of overlap, in $\text{cm}^2$, of the two circles?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{2\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{2\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{4\pi}{3} - \frac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{4\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The overlap is a lens bounded by two circular segments. Let the circles meet at $A$ and $B$. Since $PQ = 1$ and both radii are $1$, triangle $APQ$ is equilateral, so the half-angle at $P$ subtended by $A$ is $\dfrac{\pi}{3}$ and the full angle $APB$ is $\dfrac{2\pi}{3}$.

The circular sector of one circle cut off by $APB$ has area

$$\frac{1}{2}r^2\theta = \frac{1}{2}\cdot 1 \cdot \frac{2\pi}{3} = \frac{\pi}{3}$$

and the triangle $APB$ has area

$$\frac{1}{2}r^2\sin\theta = \frac{1}{2}\sin\frac{2\pi}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$$

So each segment has area $\dfrac{\pi}{3} - \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$, and the lens is made of two such segments:

$$2\left(\frac{\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right) = \frac{2\pi}{3} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

which is option D.
