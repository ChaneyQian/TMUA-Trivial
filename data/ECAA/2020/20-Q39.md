## 题目
Find the $y$-coordinate of the points on the curve $y = x^2$ that are closest to the point $\left(0, \frac{9}{2}\right)$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{4}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{9}{2}$$

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
A point on the curve is $(x, x^2)$, so the squared distance to $\left(0, \tfrac{9}{2}\right)$ is

$$D = x^2 + \left(x^2 - \frac{9}{2}\right)^2$$

Minimising $D$ is equivalent to minimising the distance. Writing $t = x^2 \ge 0$:

$$D = t + \left(t - \frac{9}{2}\right)^2 = t^2 - 8t + \frac{81}{4}$$

This quadratic in $t$ is least at $t = 4$, which is in the allowed range.

Since $y = x^2 = t$, the $y$-coordinate of the closest points is $4$, which is option D.
