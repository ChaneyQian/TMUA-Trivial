Two balls are removed at random without replacement.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{2}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{4}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{5}{6}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 1$$
