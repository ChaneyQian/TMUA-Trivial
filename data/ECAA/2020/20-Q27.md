## 题目
There are two red balls and two blue balls in a bag.

Two balls are removed at random without replacement.

Given that at least one of them is red, what is the probability that one of them is blue?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{2}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{4}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{5}{6}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 1$$

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Two balls are chosen from four, so there are $\binom{4}{2} = 6$ equally likely selections: one is red-red, one is blue-blue, and the remaining $2 \times 2 = 4$ are one red and one blue.

The event "at least one red" consists of the red-red selection together with the four mixed selections, so it contains $5$ outcomes.

Of these, the ones also containing a blue ball are the $4$ mixed selections. Hence

$$P(\text{one is blue} \mid \text{at least one red}) = \frac{4}{5}$$

which is option C.
