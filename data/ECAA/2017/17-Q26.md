## 题目

$$f(x) = 2x^{3} + px^{2} + qx + 6$$

where $p$ and $q$ are real constants.

When $f(x)$ is divided by $(x+1)$, the remainder is $12$

When $f(x)$ is divided by $(x-1)$, the remainder is $-6$

Find the remainder when $f(x)$ is divided by $(2x-1)$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 4.5$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 9$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 10.5$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 11$$
