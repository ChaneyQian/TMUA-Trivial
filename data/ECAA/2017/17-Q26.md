## 题目
The function $f$ is given by

$$f(x) = 2x^{3} + px^{2} + qx + 6$$

where $p$ and $q$ are real constants.

When $f(x)$ is divided by $(x+1)$, the remainder is $12$

When $f(x)$ is divided by $(x-1)$, the remainder is $-6$

Find the remainder when $f(x)$ is divided by $(2x-1)$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 4.5$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 9$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 10.5$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 11$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
By the remainder theorem, $f(-1) = 12$ and $f(1) = -6$.

$$f(-1) = -2 + p - q + 6 = 12 \implies p - q = 8$$

$$f(1) = 2 + p + q + 6 = -6 \implies p + q = -14$$

Solving gives $p = -3$ and $q = -11$.

The remainder on division by $(2x-1)$ is $f\left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)$.

$$f\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right) = \tfrac{1}{4} - \tfrac{3}{4} - \tfrac{11}{2} + 6 = 0$$

So the remainder is $0$, which is option A.
