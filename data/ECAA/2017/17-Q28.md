## 题目
The curve $C$ has equation $y = x^{3} - 7x - 6$

Find the equation of the tangent to $C$ at the point where $C$ cuts the positive $x$-axis.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad y = 4 - 4x$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad y = 2x - 6$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad y = 5x - 10$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad y = 9x - 27$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad y = 20x - 60$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Factorise to find where the curve meets the $x$-axis.

$$x^{3} - 7x - 6 = (x+1)(x+2)(x-3)$$

The only positive root is $x = 3$, so the point of contact is $(3, 0)$.

Differentiate to get the gradient there.

$$\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = 3x^{2} - 7 \implies \left.\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}\right|_{x=3} = 27 - 7 = 20$$

The tangent is therefore

$$y - 0 = 20(x - 3) \implies y = 20x - 60$$

which is option E.
