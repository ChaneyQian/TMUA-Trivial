## 题目
75 pupils in a year group study German or French, or both, or neither.

10 pupils study both languages.

The ratio of those who study both to those that study neither is $5 : 3$ respectively.

42 pupils study German.

2 pupils are chosen and each pupil is equally likely to be chosen.

What is the probability that one pupil studies French, and the other pupil studies **only** German?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{16}{75}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{128}{555}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{7}{25}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{32}{75}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{256}{555}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{14}{25}$$

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Since both : neither is $5 : 3$ and both $= 10$, the number studying neither is $6$.

So $75 - 6 = 69$ pupils study at least one language. With $42$ studying German, the number studying French is

$$69 - 42 + 10 = 37$$

and the number studying only German is $42 - 10 = 32$.

The two chosen pupils can come in either order, so

$$P = 2 \times \frac{37}{75} \times \frac{32}{74} = \frac{2 \times 37 \times 32}{75 \times 74} = \frac{32}{75}$$

which is option D.
