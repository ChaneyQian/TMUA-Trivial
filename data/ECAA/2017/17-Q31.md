## 题目
$S$ and $T$ are geometric progressions. For each, the second term is 6 and the sum to infinity is 25.

The first term of $S$ is greater than the first term of $T$.

What is the fourth term of $S$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{8}{125}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{4}{25}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{24}{25}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{162}{125}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{54}{25}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Let the first term be $a$ and the common ratio $r$. Then $ar = 6$ and $\dfrac{a}{1-r} = 25$.

Substituting $a = \dfrac{6}{r}$ into the second equation gives

$$\frac{6}{r(1-r)} = 25 \implies 25r^{2} - 25r + 6 = 0$$

$$(5r - 3)(5r - 2) = 0 \implies r = \frac{3}{5} \ \text{ or } \ r = \frac{2}{5}$$

These give $a = 10$ and $a = 15$ respectively, so $S$ is the progression with $a = 15$, $r = \dfrac{2}{5}$.

$$ar^{3} = 15 \times \frac{8}{125} = \frac{24}{25}$$

which is option C.
