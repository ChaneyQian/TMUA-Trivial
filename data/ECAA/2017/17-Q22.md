## 题目
Find

$$\int_{1}^{2} \left(3x + \frac{1}{x}\right)^{2} \, \mathrm{d}x$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 13.5$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 14.75$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 21.5$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 26.5$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 27.5$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 28.75$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Expand the integrand first.

$$\left(3x + \frac{1}{x}\right)^{2} = 9x^{2} + 6 + \frac{1}{x^{2}}$$

Integrate term by term.

$$\int \left(9x^{2} + 6 + x^{-2}\right) \mathrm{d}x = 3x^{3} + 6x - \frac{1}{x}$$

Evaluate between $1$ and $2$.

$$\left(24 + 12 - \tfrac{1}{2}\right) - \left(3 + 6 - 1\right) = 35.5 - 8 = 27.5$$

So the integral is $27.5$, which is option E.
