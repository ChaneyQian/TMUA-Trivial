## 题目
A bag contains only $n$ red balls and $2n$ green balls.

One ball is picked and its colour recorded. It is then put back in the bag, and an additional ball of the same colour is added to the bag.

A second ball is then picked.

What is the probability that the two balls picked are **not** the same colour?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{2n}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{4n}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{5n}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{5n-3}{3(3n-1)}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{2n}{3(3n+1)}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{4n}{3(3n+1)}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \frac{5n}{3(3n+1)}$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad \frac{5n+3}{3(3n+1)}$$

## 答案
F

## 解析
There are $3n$ balls at the first pick and $3n+1$ at the second. It is easier to find the probability that the colours match.

$$P(\text{both red}) = \frac{n}{3n} \times \frac{n+1}{3n+1}$$

$$P(\text{both green}) = \frac{2n}{3n} \times \frac{2n+1}{3n+1}$$

Adding them,

$$P(\text{same}) = \frac{n(n+1) + 2n(2n+1)}{3n(3n+1)} = \frac{5n^{2} + 3n}{3n(3n+1)} = \frac{5n+3}{3(3n+1)}$$

Hence

$$P(\text{not same}) = 1 - \frac{5n+3}{3(3n+1)} = \frac{9n + 3 - 5n - 3}{3(3n+1)} = \frac{4n}{3(3n+1)}$$

which is option F.
