## 题目
I make the following statements:

- Statement P: **if** a pig has horns, **then** it can breathe fire.
- Statement Q: **if** a pig can breathe fire, **then** it has wings.
- Statement R: **if** a pig has wings, **then** it has horns.

Each statement is either true or false, but I don't know which.

I then see a pig with wings breathing fire. It has no horns.

Which statements, if any, can I now conclude are definitely true or definitely false?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{none of them}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{P only}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{Q only}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{R only}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{P and Q only}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{P and R only}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \text{Q and R only}$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad \text{P, Q, and R}$$
