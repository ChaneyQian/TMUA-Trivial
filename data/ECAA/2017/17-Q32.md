## 题目
An arithmetic series has first term $a$, common difference $d$ and $n$ terms. The sum of the series is $S$.

A second arithmetic series also has common difference $d$ and $n$ terms. The first term of this series is $a + 5$.

What is the sum of the second arithmetic series?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad S + 5$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad S + 5(n-1)$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad S + 5n$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad S + (a+5)$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad S + (a+5)(n-1)$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad S + (a+5)n$$
