## 题目
Which one of the following is the area enclosed by the curves $y = -x^{2} + 5x - 4$ and $y = x^{2} - x$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{1}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{5}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{8}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 5$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Find the points of intersection.

$$-x^{2} + 5x - 4 = x^{2} - x \implies 2x^{2} - 6x + 4 = 0 \implies (x-1)(x-2) = 0$$

Between $x = 1$ and $x = 2$ the first curve is above the second, so

$$\text{Area} = \int_{1}^{2}\left[\left(-x^{2} + 5x - 4\right) - \left(x^{2} - x\right)\right] \mathrm{d}x = \int_{1}^{2}\left(-2x^{2} + 6x - 4\right) \mathrm{d}x$$

$$= \left[-\frac{2x^{3}}{3} + 3x^{2} - 4x\right]_{1}^{2} = \left(-\frac{16}{3} + 12 - 8\right) - \left(-\frac{2}{3} + 3 - 4\right) = -\frac{4}{3} + \frac{5}{3} = \frac{1}{3}$$

which is option A.
