## 题目
The equation gives $y$ in terms of $x$:

$$y = 3 + 2\left(\frac{x}{4} - 1\right)^{2}$$

Which one of the following is a rearrangement for $x$ in terms of $y$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad x = 4\left(1 \pm \sqrt{\frac{y-3}{2}}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad x = 4\left(1 \pm \sqrt{\frac{y+3}{2}}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad x = 4\left(-1 \pm \sqrt{\frac{y-3}{2}}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad x = 4\left(-1 \pm \sqrt{\frac{y+3}{2}}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad x = 2\left(1 \pm \sqrt{\frac{y-3}{2}}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad x = 2\left(-1 \pm \sqrt{\frac{y-3}{2}}\right)$$
