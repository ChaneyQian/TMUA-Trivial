## 题目
The simultaneous equations

$$3x^{2} - xy = 4$$

$$2x - y = p$$

where $p$ is a real constant, have two distinct real solutions for $x$.

What is the complete set of values that $p$ can take?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad p \text{ can take any value}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad p < -4, \; p > 4$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -4 < p < 4$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{there are no possible values of } p$$

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Substitute $y = 2x - p$ into the first equation.

$$3x^{2} - x(2x - p) = 4$$

$$x^{2} + px - 4 = 0$$

Two distinct real solutions for $x$ require a positive discriminant.

$$p^{2} - 4(1)(-4) = p^{2} + 16 > 0$$

This holds for every real $p$, so $p$ can take any value, which is option A.
