## 题目
On Monday a shirt is reduced in price by 30% of its original price.

On Tuesday the shirt is further reduced in price by 20% of its Monday reduced price.

What is the overall price reduction of the shirt as a percentage of its original price?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 6\%$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 10\%$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 25\%$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 44\%$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 50\%$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 60\%$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Work with a multiplier on the original price.

After Monday the price is $0.7$ of the original price.

Tuesday takes off 20% of that Monday price, so the price becomes

$$0.8 \times 0.7 = 0.56$$

The final price is 56% of the original, so the overall reduction is $100\% - 56\% = 44\%$, which is option D.
