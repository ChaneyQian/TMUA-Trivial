## 题目
In this question, $x$ and $y$ are non-zero real numbers.

Consider the three statements:

1. $x > y$ if $\dfrac{x}{y} > 1$
2. $\dfrac{x}{y} > 1$ **if and only if** $\dfrac{y}{x} < 1$
3. **If** $xy < 1$ **then** both $x < 1$ and $y < 1$

Which of these statements, taken independently, is/are true?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{none of them}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{1 only}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{2 only}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{3 only}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{1 and 2 only}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{1 and 3 only}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \text{2 and 3 only}$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad \text{1, 2 and 3}$$

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Each statement fails once negative values are allowed.

1: take $x = -2$ and $y = -1$. Then $\dfrac{x}{y} = 2 > 1$, but $x = -2 < -1 = y$. False.

2: take $x = -1$ and $y = 1$. Then $\dfrac{y}{x} = -1 < 1$, but $\dfrac{x}{y} = -1$ is not greater than $1$, so the "if and only if" fails. False.

3: take $x = 2$ and $y = \dfrac{1}{10}$. Then $xy = \dfrac{1}{5} < 1$, but $x > 1$. False.

So none of them, which is option A.
