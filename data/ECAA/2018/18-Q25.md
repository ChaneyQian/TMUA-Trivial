## 题目
Two fair six-sided dice are identical except for their colour.

Each of the dice has its faces numbered from 1 to 6, with one number on each face.

One of the dice is red and the other is blue.

The two dice are rolled.

The number shown on the red dice is divided by the number shown on the blue dice to give the score.

What is the probability of a score of $0.5$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{1}{36}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{1}{18}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{1}{12}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{1}{6}$$

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
A score of $0.5$ requires the red number to be half the blue number.

The ordered pairs (red, blue) that work are $(1,2)$, $(2,4)$ and $(3,6)$: three outcomes.

There are $6 \times 6 = 36$ equally likely outcomes, so the probability is

$$\dfrac{3}{36} = \dfrac{1}{12}$$

which is option D.
