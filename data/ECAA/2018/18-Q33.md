## 题目
$p$ is the greatest solution and $q$ is the least solution of the equation

$$y^4 - 15y^2 + 36 = 0$$

What is the value of $2p - q$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 3\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 6\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \sqrt{6}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 3\sqrt{6}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 6$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 21$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Treat the equation as a quadratic in $y^2$ and factorise.

$$y^4 - 15y^2 + 36 = \left(y^2 - 3\right)\left(y^2 - 12\right) = 0$$

$$y = \pm\sqrt{3} \quad \text{ or } \quad y = \pm 2\sqrt{3}$$

The greatest solution is $p = 2\sqrt{3}$ and the least is $q = -2\sqrt{3}$.

$$2p - q = 4\sqrt{3} + 2\sqrt{3} = 6\sqrt{3}$$

which is option B.
