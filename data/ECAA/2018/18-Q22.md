## 题目
The 1st term of an arithmetic progression is non-zero.

The 5th term of this arithmetic progression is the square of the 1st term.

The 33rd term of this arithmetic progression is 10 times the 3rd term.

What is the 10th term?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 31$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 34$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 39$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 248$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 496$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Let the first term be $a$ and the common difference be $d$.

The 33rd term is 10 times the 3rd term:

$$a + 32d = 10(a + 2d) \implies 12d = 9a \implies d = \dfrac{3a}{4}$$

The 5th term is the square of the 1st term:

$$a + 4d = a^2 \implies a + 3a = a^2 \implies a^2 = 4a$$

Since $a \ne 0$, $a = 4$ and $d = 3$.

$$u_{10} = a + 9d = 4 + 27 = 31$$

which is option A.
