## 题目
In the binomial expansion of $(a + 2x)^6$, where $a$ is positive, the coefficient of $x^4$ is 1200

What is the value of $a$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{\sqrt{10}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{\sqrt{15}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \sqrt{5}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 2\sqrt{10}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The term in $x^4$ of $(a + 2x)^6$ is

$$\binom{6}{4} a^2 (2x)^4 = 15 \times 16 a^2 x^4 = 240 a^2 x^4$$

So

$$240a^2 = 1200 \implies a^2 = 5$$

As $a$ is positive, $a = \sqrt{5}$, which is option D.
