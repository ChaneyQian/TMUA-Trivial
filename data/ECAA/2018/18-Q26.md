## 题目
The positive real numbers $a$ and $b$ satisfy the simultaneous equations:

$$\log_2 4a - \log_2 b = 4$$

$$\log_2 a + \log_2 2b = 3$$

What is the value of $a - 2b$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{8}{9}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{8}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 6$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Combine the logarithms in each equation.

$$\log_2 4a - \log_2 b = \log_2 \dfrac{4a}{b} = 4 \implies \dfrac{4a}{b} = 16 \implies a = 4b$$

$$\log_2 a + \log_2 2b = \log_2 2ab = 3 \implies 2ab = 8 \implies ab = 4$$

Substituting $a = 4b$ gives $4b^2 = 4$, and since $b > 0$, $b = 1$ and $a = 4$.

$$a - 2b = 4 - 2 = 2$$

which is option C.
