## 题目
Given that

$$5^{x+1} \times 25^{3x} = 125^{19}$$

what is the value of $x$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{9}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 8$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 9$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{19}{4}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Write every power with base 5.

$$5^{x+1} \times 25^{3x} = 5^{x+1} \times 5^{6x} = 5^{7x+1}$$

$$125^{19} = \left(5^3\right)^{19} = 5^{57}$$

Equating the exponents:

$$7x + 1 = 57 \implies x = 8$$

which is option C.
