## 题目
The tangents to the curve $y = x^2 - 9$ are drawn at the points where the curve meets the $x$-axis.

What is the area of the closed region bounded by the curve and the two tangents?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 9$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 18$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 54$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 72$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 90$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
The curve meets the $x$-axis at $x = -3$ and $x = 3$, and $\dfrac{dy}{dx} = 2x$, so the tangents there have gradients $-6$ and $6$:

$$y = 6(x - 3) \quad \text{ and } \quad y = -6(x + 3)$$

These meet at $(0, -18)$, so the region is symmetric about the $y$-axis. Take the right-hand half and double it.

$$\int_0^3 \left[\left(x^2 - 9\right) - (6x - 18)\right] dx = \int_0^3 (x - 3)^2 \, dx = \left[\dfrac{(x-3)^3}{3}\right]_0^3 = 0 - \left(-9\right) = 9$$

The total area is $2 \times 9 = 18$, which is option B.
