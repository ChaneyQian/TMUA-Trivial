## 题目
S is a geometric progression: $u_1$, $u_2$, $u_3$, $u_4$, $u_5$, $u_6$, ...

All of the terms in S are positive.

S is split to form two new geometric progressions, O and E.

The terms of O are: $u_1$, $u_3$, $u_5$, ...

The terms of E are: $u_2$, $u_4$, $u_6$, ...

The sum to infinity of O is $\dfrac{8}{9}$, and $u_1 = \dfrac{2}{3}$

What is the sum to infinity of E?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{4}{9}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{5}{9}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{2}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{5}{6}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{8}{9}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \dfrac{4}{3}$$
