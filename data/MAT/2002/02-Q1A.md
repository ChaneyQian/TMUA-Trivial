## 题目
The number of solutions of the equation
$$
x^{3} + ax^{2} - x - 2 = 0
$$
for which $x > 0$ is
(a) $1$
(b) $2$
(c) $3$
(d) dependent on the value of $a$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Let $f(x) = x^{3} + ax^{2} - x - 2$. We examine the signs of the coefficients of this polynomial: $1, a, -1, -2$. 
If $a \geqslant 0$, the sequence of signs is $+, +, -, -$, which has exactly one sign change. 
If $a < 0$, the sequence of signs is $+, -, -, -$, which also has exactly one sign change. 
By Descartes' Rule of Signs, since there is exactly one sign change in the coefficients for any real value of $a$, the polynomial always has exactly one positive real root. The answer is (a).
