## 题目
The absolute value $|x|$ of a real number $x$ is defined by
$$
|x| =
\begin{cases}
x & \text{if } x \geqslant 0, \\
-x & \text{if } x < 0.
\end{cases}
$$
(So, for example, $|3| = 3$ and $|-5| = 5$.)

**(a)**

Sketch the locus of points $(x, y)$ in the first quadrant ($x \geqslant 0$, $y \geqslant 0$) of the $x$-$y$ plane that satisfy the equation $x + y = 1$.

![[Image/02-Q4-fig1.jpg]]

**(b)**

Hence, sketch the set of points $(x, y)$ in the whole $x$-$y$ plane that satisfy the equation $|x| + |y| = 1$.

![[Image/02-Q4-fig2.jpg]]

**(c)**

Give the equations of the straight lines that contain the sides of the figure that you have drawn in (b).

**(d)**

If $|x| + |y| = 1$, what are the maximum and minimum values of $\sqrt{x^{2} + y^{2}}$? At which points $(x, y)$ are they attained?
