## 题目
The absolute value $|x|$ of a real number $x$ is defined by
$$
|x| =
\begin{cases}
x & \text{if } x \geqslant 0, \\
-x & \text{if } x < 0.
\end{cases}
$$
(So, for example, $|3| = 3$ and $|-5| = 5$.)

**(a)**

Sketch the locus of points $(x, y)$ in the first quadrant ($x \geqslant 0$, $y \geqslant 0$) of the $x$-$y$ plane that satisfy the equation $x + y = 1$.

![[Image/02-Q4-fig1.jpg]]

**(b)**

Hence, sketch the set of points $(x, y)$ in the whole $x$-$y$ plane that satisfy the equation $|x| + |y| = 1$.

![[Image/02-Q4-fig2.jpg]]

**(c)**

Give the equations of the straight lines that contain the sides of the figure that you have drawn in (b).

**(d)**

If $|x| + |y| = 1$, what are the maximum and minimum values of $\sqrt{x^{2} + y^{2}}$? At which points $(x, y)$ are they attained?

## 解析
**(a)** In the first quadrant $x + y = 1$ is the straight line segment joining $(1,0)$ to $(0,1)$.

**(b)** Both $|x|$ and $|y|$ are unchanged when $x$ is replaced by $-x$ or $y$ by $-y$, so the locus of $|x|+|y|=1$ is symmetric in both axes. In the first quadrant $|x| = x$ and $|y| = y$, so that part of the locus is exactly the segment drawn in (a). Reflecting it in the two axes gives the whole set: a square with vertices

$$(1,0), \quad (0,1), \quad (-1,0), \quad (0,-1),$$

that is, the original square rotated through $45^{\circ}$.

**(c)** The four sides lie on the lines

$$x+y = 1, \qquad x - y = 1, \qquad -x+y = 1, \qquad -x-y=1,$$

that is, $x + y = \pm 1$ and $x - y = \pm 1$.

**(d)** $\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$ is the distance of $(x,y)$ from the origin. Squaring the constraint,

$$1 = \left(|x|+|y|\right)^2 = x^2 + y^2 + 2|xy|,$$

so

$$x^2+y^2 = 1 - 2|xy| \leqslant 1,$$

with equality exactly when $xy = 0$, i.e. at the four vertices. Hence the **maximum** value is

$$1, \qquad \text{attained at } (\pm1, 0) \text{ and } (0,\pm1).$$

For the other bound, $\left(|x|-|y|\right)^2 \geqslant 0$ gives $2|xy| \leqslant x^2+y^2$, so from the same identity

$$1 = x^2+y^2+2|xy| \leqslant 2\left(x^2+y^2\right) \implies x^2+y^2 \geqslant \frac12,$$

with equality exactly when $|x| = |y|$, which together with $|x|+|y|=1$ forces $|x|=|y|=\tfrac12$. Hence the **minimum** value is

$$\frac{1}{\sqrt2}, \qquad \text{attained at } \left(\pm\tfrac12, \pm\tfrac12\right) \text{ (all four sign combinations),}$$

the midpoints of the four sides.
