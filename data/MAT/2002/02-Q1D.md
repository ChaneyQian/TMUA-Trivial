## 题目
Let $f(x)$ be the function $e^{e^{e^{x}}}$. The value of $f'(x)$ when $x = \ln 3$ is which of the following?
(a) $3e^{e^{3}}$
(b) $3e^{e^{3}+3}$
(c) $e^{3e+e^{3}}$
(d) $9e^{e^{3}+1}$.

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
We find the derivative of $f(x) = \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{x}}}$ by applying the chain rule repeatedly:

$$f'(x) = \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{x}}} \cdot \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x} \left(\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{x}}\right) = \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{x}}} \cdot \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{x}} \cdot \mathrm{e}^{x}$$

Substitute $x = \ln 3$, which gives $\mathrm{e}^{x} = 3$ and $\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{x}} = \mathrm{e}^{3}$:

$$f'(\ln 3) = \mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{3}} \cdot \mathrm{e}^{3} \cdot 3 = 3\mathrm{e}^{\mathrm{e}^{3} + 3}$$

The answer is (b).
