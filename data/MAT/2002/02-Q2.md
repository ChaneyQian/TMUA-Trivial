## 题目
Suppose that the equation
$$
x^{4} + Ax^{2} + B = (x^{2} + ax + b)(x^{2} - ax + b)
$$
holds for all values of $x$.

**(a)**

Find $A$ and $B$ in terms of $a$ and $b$.

**(b)**

Use this information to find a factorization of the expression
$$
x^{4} - 20x^{2} + 16
$$
as a product of two quadratics in $x$.

**(c)**

Show that the four solutions of the equation
$$
x^{4} - 20x^{2} + 16 = 0
$$
can be written as $\pm\sqrt{7} \pm \sqrt{3}$.

## 解析
**(a)** The right-hand side is a difference of two squares:

$$\left(x^2+ax+b\right)\left(x^2-ax+b\right) = \left(x^2+b\right)^2 - (ax)^2 = x^4 + \left(2b - a^2\right)x^2 + b^2.$$

Comparing coefficients,

$$A = 2b - a^2, \qquad B = b^2.$$

**(b)** For $x^4 - 20x^2 + 16$ we need $b^2 = 16$ and $2b - a^2 = -20$.

Taking $b = 4$ gives $a^2 = 2b + 20 = 28$, so $a = 2\sqrt7$ and

$$x^4 - 20x^2 + 16 = \left(x^2 + 2\sqrt7\,x + 4\right)\left(x^2 - 2\sqrt7\,x + 4\right).$$

(The choice $b = -4$ gives $a^2 = 12$ and the alternative factorisation $\left(x^2+2\sqrt3\,x-4\right)\left(x^2-2\sqrt3\,x-4\right)$; the first is the one needed below.)

**(c)** The four solutions are the roots of the two quadratics found in (b). For $x^2 \mp 2\sqrt7\,x + 4 = 0$ the quadratic formula gives

$$x = \frac{\pm 2\sqrt7 \pm \sqrt{28 - 16}}{2} = \pm\sqrt7 \pm \sqrt3,$$

where the two signs are chosen independently (the first sign selects the quadratic, the second selects the root). So the four solutions are

$$\sqrt7+\sqrt3, \quad \sqrt7-\sqrt3, \quad -\sqrt7+\sqrt3, \quad -\sqrt7-\sqrt3,$$

that is $\pm\sqrt7\pm\sqrt3$. As a check, if $x = \sqrt7+\sqrt3$ then $x^2 = 10 + 2\sqrt{21}$ and

$$x^4 - 20x^2 + 16 = \left(184 + 40\sqrt{21}\right) - \left(200 + 40\sqrt{21}\right) + 16 = 0.$$
