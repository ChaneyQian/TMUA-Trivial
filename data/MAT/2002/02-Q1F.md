## 题目
Observe that $2^{3} = 8$, $2^{5} = 32$, $3^{2} = 9$ and $3^{3} = 27$. From these facts, we can deduce that $\log_{2} 3$, the logarithm of $3$ to base $2$, is
(a) between $1\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $1\dfrac{1}{2}$
(b) between $1\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $1\dfrac{2}{3}$
(c) between $1\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $2$
(d) none of the above.

## 答案
B

## 解析
We are given that $2^{3} = 8$ and $3^{2} = 9$. Since $2^{3} < 3^{2}$, taking the base-$2$ logarithm yields $3 < 2\log_{2}3$, so $\log_{2}3 > \frac{3}{2} = 1\frac{1}{2}$. 
We are also given $3^{3} = 27$ and $2^{5} = 32$. Since $3^{3} < 2^{5}$, taking the base-$2$ logarithm yields $3\log_{2}3 < 5$, so $\log_{2}3 < \frac{5}{3} = 1\frac{2}{3}$. 
Combining these inequalities, we deduce that $\log_{2}3$ lies strictly between $1\frac{1}{2}$ and $1\frac{2}{3}$. The answer is (b).
