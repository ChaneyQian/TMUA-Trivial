## 题目
A child is presented with the following lettered tiles: M A M M A L. The number of different “words” he can make using all six tiles is
(a) $6$
(b) $30$
(c) $60$
(d) $120$.

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The word "MAMMAL" consists of $6$ letters in total, with the letter 'M' repeated $3$ times, the letter 'A' repeated $2$ times, and the letter 'L' appearing $1$ time. The number of distinguishable permutations is given by the multinomial coefficient:

$$\frac{6!}{3! \, 2! \, 1!} = \frac{720}{6 \times 2 \times 1} = \frac{720}{12} = 60$$

The answer is (c).
