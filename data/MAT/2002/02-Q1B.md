## 题目
Of the following three alleged algebraic identities, at least one is wrong.

(i) $yz(z - y) + zx(x - z) + xy(y - x) = (z - y)(x - z)(y - x)$

(ii) $yz(z - y) + zx(x - z) + xy(y - x) = (z - y)(z - x)(y - x)$

(iii) $yz(z + y) + zx(z + x) + xy(y + x) = (z + y)(z + x)(y + x)$.

Which of the following statements is correct?
(a) Only identity (i) is right
(b) Only identity (ii) is right
(c) Identities (ii) and (iii) are right
(d) All these identities are wrong.
