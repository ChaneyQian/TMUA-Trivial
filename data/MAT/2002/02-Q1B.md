## 题目
Of the following three alleged algebraic identities, at least one is wrong.

(i) $yz(z - y) + zx(x - z) + xy(y - x) = (z - y)(x - z)(y - x)$

(ii) $yz(z - y) + zx(x - z) + xy(y - x) = (z - y)(z - x)(y - x)$

(iii) $yz(z + y) + zx(z + x) + xy(y + x) = (z + y)(z + x)(y + x)$.

Which of the following statements is correct?
(a) Only identity (i) is right
(b) Only identity (ii) is right
(c) Identities (ii) and (iii) are right
(d) All these identities are wrong.

## 答案
B

## 解析
Let us expand the expression on the left-hand side:

$$yz(z - y) + zx(x - z) + xy(y - x) = yz^2 - y^2z + zx^2 - z^2x + xy^2 - x^2y$$

Now, we expand the right-hand side of identity (ii):

$$(z - y)(z - x)(y - x) = (z^2 - zx - yz + yx)(y - x)$$
$$= z^2y - z^2x - zxy + zx^2 - y^2z + xyz + y^2x - yx^2$$
$$= yz^2 - xz^2 + zx^2 - y^2z + xy^2 - x^2y$$

This precisely matches the expanded left-hand side. The right-hand side of identity (i) evaluates to the negative of this expression, making (i) incorrect. Identity (iii) evaluates to $6$ when $x=y=z=1$, while its right-hand side evaluates to $8$, so it is also incorrect. Thus, only (ii) is right. The answer is (b).
