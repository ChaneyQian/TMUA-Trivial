## 题目
The minimum value achieved by the function

$$
f (x) = 9 \cos^{4} x - 12 \cos^{2} x + 7
$$

equals

(a) $3$
(b) $4$
(c) $5$
(d) $6$
(e) $7.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
A

## 解析
Substitute $\gamma = \cos^{2} ( x )$ and solve the resulting quadratic in $\gamma$ (noting that $0 \leqslant \gamma \leqslant 1 )$ . The answer is (a).
