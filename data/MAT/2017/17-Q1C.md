## 题目
A sequence $\left( a_{n} \right)$ has the property that

$$
a_{n+1} = \frac {a_{n}}{a_{n-1}}
$$

for every $n \geqslant 2 .$ . Given that $a_{1} = 2$ and $a_{2} = 6 ,$ , what is $a_{2017} ?$

(a) $\dfrac { 1 } { 6 }$
(b) $\dfrac 13$
(c) $\dfrac { 1 } { 2 }$
(d) $2$
(e) $3.$

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
Calculating the first few terms gives the pattern $2 , 6 , 3 , \frac { 1 } { 2 } , \frac { 1 } { 6 } , \frac { 1 } { 3 } , 2 , 6 , . .$ . The sequence repeats every six terms, and 2017 divided by 6 leaves remainder 1. Hence the answer is (d).
