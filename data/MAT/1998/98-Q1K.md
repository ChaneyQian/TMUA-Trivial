## 题目
The graph of $y = |x^2 + 2x - 1|$ lies below the graph of $y = 1$ precisely when

(i) $-1 - \sqrt{3} < x < 1 + \sqrt{3}$,
(ii) $-1 - \sqrt{3} < x < -2$ or $0 < x < \sqrt{3} - 1$,
(iii) $-1 - \sqrt{3} < x < -2$ or $0 < x < 1 + \sqrt{3}$,
(iv) $-2 < x < 0$,
(v) none of these.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(ii)

## 解析
The condition $|x^2 + 2x - 1| < 1$ is equivalent to the compound inequality

$$-1 < x^2 + 2x - 1 < 1$$

The left inequality reduces to $x^2 + 2x > 0 \implies x(x + 2) > 0$, which means $x > 0$ or $x < -2$. The right inequality reduces to $x^2 + 2x - 2 < 0$. Its roots are $x = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{12}}{2} = -1 \pm \sqrt{3}$, so the solution is $-1 - \sqrt{3} < x < \sqrt{3} - 1$. Taking the intersection of these two sets gives $-1 - \sqrt{3} < x < -2$ or $0 < x < \sqrt{3} - 1$. The answer is (ii).
