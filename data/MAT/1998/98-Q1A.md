## 题目
The point lying between $P(2,3)$ and $Q(8,-3)$ which divides the line $PQ$ in the ratio $1{:}2$ has coordinates

(i) $(4,-1)$, (ii) $(6,-2)$, (iii) $\bigl(\frac{14}{3},2\bigr)$, (iv) $(4,1)$, (v) none of these.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(iv)

## 解析
Using the section formula, the coordinates of the point dividing the line segment $PQ$ in the ratio $1{:}2$ are given by

$$\left( \frac{2 \cdot 2 + 1 \cdot 8}{1 + 2}, \frac{2 \cdot 3 + 1 \cdot (-3)}{1 + 2} \right)$$

This simplifies to $\left( \frac{12}{3}, \frac{3}{3} \right) = (4, 1)$. The answer is (iv).
