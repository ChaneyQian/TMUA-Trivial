## 题目
**(a)**

Find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ for each of the functions

$y = \sin(\ln x)$,

$y = x\sin(\ln x)$,

$y = x\cos(\ln x)$.

**(b)**

Sketch the following curves using the axes provided on the next page:

(i) $y = \ln x$, for $1 \leqslant x \leqslant \mathrm{e}^{\pi}$,

(ii) $y = \sin(\ln x)$, for $1 \leqslant x \leqslant \mathrm{e}^{\pi}$.

**(c)**

Evaluate

$$
\int_{1}^{\mathrm{e}^{\pi}} \sin(\ln x)\,dx.
$$
