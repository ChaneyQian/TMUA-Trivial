## 题目
**(a)**

You are given that

$$
\frac{1}{(x-1)(x-2)} = \frac{A}{x-2} + \frac{B}{x-1},
$$

where $A$ and $B$ are constants. Find the values of $A$ and $B$.

**(b)**

Simplify

$$
\frac{1}{(x-1)^{n+1}(x-2)} - \frac{1}{(x-1)^n(x-2)}.
$$

**(c)**

You are given that

$$
\frac{1}{(x-1)^n(x-2)} = \frac{A_0}{x-2} + \sum_{i=1}^{n} \frac{A_i}{(x-1)^i},
$$

where $A_0, A_1, A_2, \ldots$ are constants. Using your answers to (a) and (b), or otherwise, find the values of these constants.
