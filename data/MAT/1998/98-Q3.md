## 题目
Let $P$ and $Q$ be the points with co-ordinates $(7, 1)$ and $(11, 2)$.

**(a)**

The *mirror image* of the point $P$ in the $x$-axis is the point $R$ with co-ordinates $(7, -1)$. Mark the points $P$, $Q$ and $R$ on the grid provided.

**(b)**

Consider paths from $P$ to $Q$ each of which consists of two straight line segments $PX$ and $XQ$ where $X$ is a point on the $x$-axis. Find the length of the shortest such path, giving clear reasoning for your answer. (You may refer to the diagram to help your explanation, if you wish.)

**(c)**

Sketch in the line $\ell$ with equation $y = x$. Find the co-ordinates of $S$, the mirror image in the line $\ell$ of the point $Q$, and mark in the point $S$.

**(d)**

Consider paths from $P$ to $Q$ each of which consists of three straight line segments $PY$, $YZ$ and $ZQ$, where $Y$ is on the $x$-axis and $Z$ is on the line $\ell$. Find the length of the shortest such path, giving clear reasoning for your answer.
