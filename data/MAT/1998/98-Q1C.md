## 题目
As the positive integer $n$ becomes very large, $\dfrac{2n^3 + 3n^2 + 1}{5n^3 + 4}$ approaches

(i) $\frac{1}{4}$, (ii) $\frac{2}{5}$, (iii) $1$, (iv) $\infty$, (v) none of these.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(ii)

## 解析
We divide the numerator and the denominator by the highest power of $n$, which is $n^3$, to get

$$\frac{2 + \frac{3}{n} + \frac{1}{n^3}}{5 + \frac{4}{n^3}}$$

As $n$ becomes very large, the terms with $n$ in the denominator approach $0$, leaving $\frac{2 + 0 + 0}{5 + 0} = \frac{2}{5}$. The answer is (ii).
