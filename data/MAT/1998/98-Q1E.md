## 题目
The area under the curve $y = 1 + x^2 + \sin 3\pi x$ and above the $x$-axis between $x = 0$ and $x = 1$ is

(i) $\frac{1}{3}(4 + \frac{2}{\pi})$, (ii) $\frac{1}{3}(4 + \frac{1}{\pi})$, (iii) $\frac{4}{3}$, (iv) $\frac{1}{3}(4 - \frac{2}{\pi})$, (v) none of these.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(i)

## 解析
The area is given by the definite integral

$$\int_{0}^{1} (1 + x^2 + \sin 3\pi x) \,\mathrm{d}x = \left[ x + \frac{x^3}{3} - \frac{\cos 3\pi x}{3\pi} \right]_{0}^{1}$$

Evaluating at the upper limit gives $1 + \frac{1}{3} - \frac{\cos 3\pi}{3\pi} = \frac{4}{3} + \frac{1}{3\pi}$. Evaluating at the lower limit gives $0 + 0 - \frac{\cos 0}{3\pi} = -\frac{1}{3\pi}$. Subtracting these yields $\frac{4}{3} + \frac{2}{3\pi} = \frac{1}{3}\left(4 + \frac{2}{\pi}\right)$. The answer is (i).
