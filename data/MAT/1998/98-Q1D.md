## 题目
The simultaneous equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x + ay &= b, \\
x + 3y &= 4,
\end{aligned}
$$

(i) when $a = 6$ and $b$ is any value, do not have a solution,
(ii) when $b \neq 8$ and $a$ is any value, have a solution,
(iii) have a solution for any values of $a$ and $b$,
(iv) when either $a \neq 6$ or $b = 8$, have a solution,
(v) satisfy none of these.

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(iv)

## 解析
Multiplying the second equation by $2$ gives the equivalent system:

$$2x + ay = b$$
$$2x + 6y = 8$$

A unique solution exists if and only if the determinant is non-zero, which requires $a \neq 6$. If $a = 6$, the left-hand sides are identical, and the system is consistent (having infinitely many solutions) if and only if the right-hand sides are also identical, meaning $b = 8$. Therefore, the equations have a solution when either $a \neq 6$ or $b = 8$. The answer is (iv).
