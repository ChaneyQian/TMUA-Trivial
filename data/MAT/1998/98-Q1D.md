## 题目
The simultaneous equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
2x + ay &= b, \\
x + 3y &= 4,
\end{aligned}
$$

(i) when $a = 6$ and $b$ is any value, do not have a solution,
(ii) when $b \neq 8$ and $a$ is any value, have a solution,
(iii) have a solution for any values of $a$ and $b$,
(iv) when either $a \neq 6$ or $b = 8$, have a solution,
(v) satisfy none of these.
