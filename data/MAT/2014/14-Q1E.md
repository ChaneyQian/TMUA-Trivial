## 题目
As x varies over the real numbers, the largest value taken by the function

$$
\left(4 \sin^{2} x + 4 \cos x + 1\right) ^{2}
$$

equals

(a) $17 + 12 \sqrt {2},$
(b) $36,$
(c) $48 \sqrt {2},$
(d) $64 - 12 \sqrt {3},$
(e) $81.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
Consider first the extremes of the expression in the bracket. If we use the identity sin $\scriptstyle 1 ^{2} x = 1 - \cos^{2}$ x then we see

$$
4 \sin^{2} x + 4 \cos x + 1 = 5 + 4 \cos x - 4 \cos^{2} x = 6 - (1 - 2 \cos x) ^{2}.
$$

Thus the expression in the bracket can take values from −3, when cos $x = - 1$ , to 6, when cos $x = 1 / 2$ Hence the greatest the square of the expression can be is $6 ^{2} = 36$ and the answer is (b).
