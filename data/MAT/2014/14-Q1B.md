## 解析
We can complete the square in the quadratic to get $( x - 1 ) ^{2} + 1$ . So substituting $x = 1$ , we know that the graph meets the x-axis at (1,0), since $\log_{10} ( 1 ) = 0$ . This eliminates graphs (a), (b), (c), and (d). The answer is (e).
