## 题目
The inequality

$$
x^{4} <   8 x^{2} + 9
$$

is satisfied precisely when

(a) $- 3 < x < 3 ;$
(b) $0 < x < 4 ;$
(c) $1 < x < 3 ;$
(d) $- 1 < x < 9 ;$
(e) $- 3 < x < - 1 .$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
A

## 解析
We can rewrite the inequality as

$$
x^{4} - 8 x^{2} - 9 <   0
$$

which rearranges to

$$
(x^{2} - 9) (x^{2} + 1) <   0.
$$

As $x^{2} + 1 > 0$ for all values of x then we can divide by this term and see that the inequality is equivalent to $x^{2} < 9$ . The answer is (a).
