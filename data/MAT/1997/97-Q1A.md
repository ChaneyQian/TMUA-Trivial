## 题目
The straight line in the $(x, y)$ plane through the points $(-1, 3)$ and $(2, 1)$ is defined by the equation
(i) $3x + 2y = 3$,
(ii) $-x + 3y = 1$,
(iii) $2x + 3y = 7$,
(iv) $x + 2y = 5$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(iii)

## 解析
The slope of the line passing through $(-1, 3)$ and $(2, 1)$ is given by

$$m = \frac{1 - 3}{2 - (-1)} = -\frac{2}{3}$$

Using the point-slope form with the point $(2, 1)$, the equation of the line is $y - 1 = -\frac{2}{3}(x - 2)$. Multiplying by $3$ and rearranging gives $3y - 3 = -2x + 4$, which simplifies to

$$2x + 3y = 7$$

The answer is (iii).
