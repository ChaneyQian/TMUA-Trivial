## 题目
Anne, Bert, Clare, Derek and Emily are planning to play a game for which they need to divide themselves into three teams. Each team must have at least one member. The number of different ways they can do this is
(i) $10$,
(ii) $15$,
(iii) $25$,
(iv) $30$.

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(iii)

## 解析
We need to partition a set of $5$ people into $3$ non-empty subsets. The possible sizes of the teams are either $3, 1, 1$ or $2, 2, 1$.
For the $3, 1, 1$ distribution, we just need to choose $3$ people to form the first team, which can be done in $\binom{5}{3} = 10$ ways. The remaining two people form the other two singleton teams.
For the $2, 2, 1$ distribution, we choose $1$ person for the singleton team in $\binom{5}{1} = 5$ ways. The remaining $4$ people are divided into two pairs in $\frac{1}{2}\binom{4}{2} = 3$ ways. Thus, there are $5 \times 3 = 15$ ways.
The total number of ways is $10 + 15 = 25$. The answer is (iii).
