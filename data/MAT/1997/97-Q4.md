## 题目
The curves $y = \frac{1}{2}\pi \cos x$ and $x = \frac{1}{2}\pi \cos y$ intersect at the three points $(0, \frac{1}{2}\pi)$, $(a, b)$, $(\frac{1}{2}\pi, 0)$, as shown in the figure below.

![[Image/97-Q4-fig1.jpg]]

**(a)**

Explain why $a = b = \frac{1}{2}\pi \cos b$.

**(b)**

Show that $\pi \sin b = \sqrt{\pi^2 - 4b^2}$.

**(c)**

Show that the area of the shaded region is
$$
\sqrt{\pi^2 - 4b^2} - \frac{\pi}{2} - b^2
$$.
