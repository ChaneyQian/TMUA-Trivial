## 题目
The power of $x$ which has the greatest coefficient in the expansion of $\left(1 + \frac{1}{2} x\right)^{10}$ is
(i) $x^2$,
(ii) $x^3$,
(iii) $x^5$,
(iv) $x^{10}$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(ii)

## 解析
The general term in the binomial expansion of $\left(1 + \frac{x}{2}\right)^{10}$ is $c_r x^r = \binom{10}{r} \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^r x^r$. We find the ratio of consecutive coefficients:

$$\frac{c_{r+1}}{c_r} = \frac{\binom{10}{r+1} (1/2)^{r+1}}{\binom{10}{r} (1/2)^r} = \frac{10 - r}{r + 1} \cdot \frac{1}{2}$$

We want to find when this ratio is greater than $1$. Setting $\frac{10-r}{2(r+1)} > 1$ yields $10 - r > 2r + 2$, or $3r < 8$. This is satisfied for $r = 0, 1, 2$. Therefore, the sequence of coefficients increases up to $c_3$ and decreases thereafter. The greatest coefficient is $c_3$, which corresponds to the power $x^3$. The answer is (ii).
