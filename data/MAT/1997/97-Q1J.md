## 题目
In a plane there are given $n$ straight lines, no two of them parallel and no three of them meeting at a point. The number of parts they divide the plane into is
(i) $n + 1$,
(ii) $n^2 - n + 2$,
(iii) $\frac{1}{2} n(n + 1) + 1$,
(iv) $2^n$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(iii)

## 解析
Let $P_n$ be the number of parts into which $n$ lines divide the plane. When we add the $n$-th line, it must intersect all $n-1$ existing lines exactly once (since no two are parallel and no three are concurrent). These $n-1$ intersection points divide the new line into $n$ segments, each of which splits an existing region into two. Thus, $P_n = P_{n-1} + n$. Starting with $P_0 = 1$, we have

$$P_n = 1 + \sum_{k=1}^{n} k = 1 + \frac{1}{2}n(n + 1)$$

The answer is (iii).
