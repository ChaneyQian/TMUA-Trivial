## 题目
The least and greatest values of $\cos(\cos x)$ in the range $0 \leq x \leq \pi$ are
(i) $0$ and $1$,
(ii) $-\cos 1$ and $1$,
(iii) $-1$ and $1$,
(iv) $\cos 1$ and $1$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(iv)

## 解析
As $x$ ranges from $0$ to $\pi$, the inner function $y = \cos x$ takes all values in the interval $[-1, 1]$. We then need to find the maximum and minimum of the outer function $\cos y$ for $y \in [-1, 1]$. 
The cosine function is even and decreases on the interval $[0, 1]$. Its maximum over $[-1, 1]$ occurs at $y = 0$, giving $\cos 0 = 1$. Its minimum occurs at the endpoints $y = 1$ and $y = -1$, giving $\cos 1$. Thus, the least and greatest values are $\cos 1$ and $1$. The answer is (iv).
