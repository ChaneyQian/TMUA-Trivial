## 答案
B

## 解析
The equation $x^{4}-y^{2}=2y+1$ rearranges to

$$
x^{4} = (y + 1) ^{2} \Longleftrightarrow x^{2} = y + 1 \text {or} - x^{2} = y + 1.
$$

So the graph consists of two parabolae $y = x^{2} - 1$ and $y = -1 - x^{2}$ which form the graph in (b).
