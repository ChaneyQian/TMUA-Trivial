## 题目
The graph of $y = \sin x$ is reflected first in the line $x = \pi$ and then in the line $y = 2$. The resulting graph has equation

(a) $y = \cos x ;$
(b) $y = 2 + \sin x ;$
(c) $y = 4 + \sin x ;$
(d) $y = 2 - \cos x .$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
Either by using sketches of the relevant graphs, or arguing algebraically as below, we see that:

- on reflection in the line $x = \pi$ the graph of $y = \sin x$ becomes

$$
y = \sin (2 \pi - x) = \sin (- x) = - \sin x.
$$

- on reflection in the line $y = 2$ the graph $y = -\sin x$ becomes

$$
y = 4 - (- \sin x) = 4 + \sin x.
$$

The answer is (c).
