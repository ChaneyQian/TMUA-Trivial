## 题目
The expression

$$
\frac {\mathrm{d} ^{2}}{\mathrm{d} x^{2}} \left[ (2 x - 1) ^{4} (1 - x) ^{5} \right] + \frac {\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} x} \left[ (2 x + 1) ^{4} (3 x^{2} - 2) ^{2} \right]
$$

is a polynomial of degree

(a) $9 ;$
(b) $8 ;$
(c) $7 ;$
(d) less than $7$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
- The leading term in $(2x - 1)^{4}(1 - x)^{5}$ is $(2x)^{4}(-x)^{5} = -16x^{9}$ which differentiates twice to

$$
- 9 \times 8 \times 16 x^{7}.
$$

- The leading term $(2x + 1)^{4}(3x^{2} - 2)^{2}$ is $(2x)^{4}(3x^{2})^{2} = 16\times 9x^{8}$ which differentiates to

$$
16 \times 9 \times 8 x^{7}.
$$

Hence the leading terms cancel and so the polynomial actually has degree less than 7. The answer is (d).
