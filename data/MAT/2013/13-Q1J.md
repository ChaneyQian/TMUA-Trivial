## 题目
For a real number $x$ we denote by $[ x ]$ the largest integer less than or equal to $x$. Let $n$ be a natural number. The integral

$$
\int_{0} ^{n} \left[ 2 ^{x} \right] \mathrm{d} x
$$

equals

(a) $\log_{2} ((2 ^{n} - 1)!);$
(b) $n 2 ^{n} - \log_{2} ((2 ^{n})!);$
(c) $n 2 ^{n};$
(d) $\log_{2} ((2 ^{n})!),$

where $k ! = 1 \times 2 \times 3 \times \cdots \times k$ for a positive integer $k$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
Note that for $0 \leqslant x < n$ that $[2^{x}]$ takes all integer values from 1 to $2^{n} - 1$ . Further for $1 \leqslant k < 2^{n}$ we see

$$
[ 2 ^{x} ] = k \quad \text { for } \quad \log_{2} k \leqslant x <   \log_{2} (k + 1).
$$

Hence we see the desired integral equals

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & (\log_{2} (2) - \log_{2} 1) + 2 (\log_{2} (3) - \log_{2} 2) + 3 (\log_{2} (4) - \log_{2} 3) + \dots + (2 ^{n} - 1) (\log_{2} 2 ^{n} - \log_{2} (2 ^{n} - 1)) \\ = & - \log_{2} 2 - \log_{2} 3 - \dots - \log_{2} (2 ^{n} - 1) + (2 ^{n} - 1) \log_{2} 2 ^{n} \\ = & 2 ^{n} \log_{2} 2 ^{n} - (\log_{2} 2 + \log_{2} 3 + \dots + \log_{2} 2 ^{n}) \\ = & n 2 ^{n} - \log_{2} ((2 ^{n})!). \end{array}
$$

The answer is (b).
