## 题目
The functions $f , g$ and $h$ are related by

$$
f^{\prime} (x) = g (x + 1), \quad g^{\prime} (x) = h (x - 1).
$$

It follows that $f^{\prime\prime} ( 2 x )$ equals

(a) $h ( 2 x + 1 ) ;$
(b) $2 h^{\prime} ( 2 x ) ;$
(c) $h ( 2 x ) ;$
(d) $4 h ( 2 x ) .$

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
If we take the two given equations

$$
f^{\prime} (x) = g (x + 1), \qquad g^{\prime} (x) = h (x - 1),
$$

and differentiate the first with respect to $x$ we have

$$
f^{\prime\prime} (x) = g^{\prime} (x + 1), \quad g^{\prime} (x) = h (x - 1).
$$

Hence

$$
f^{\prime\prime} (x) = g^{\prime} (x + 1) = h ((x + 1) - 1) = h (x).
$$

Hence $f'' = h$ (as functions) and in particular $f''(2x) = h(2x)$ . The answer is (c).
