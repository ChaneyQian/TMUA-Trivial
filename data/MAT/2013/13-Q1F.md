## 题目
Three positive numbers a, b, c satisfy

$$
\log_{b} a = 2, \quad \log_{b} (c - 3) = 3, \quad \log_{a} (c + 5) = 2.
$$

This information

(a) specifies $a$ uniquely.

(b) is satisfied by two values of $a$.

(c) is satisfied by infinitely many values of $a$.

(d) is contradictory.

## 答案
A

## 解析
If we take exponents of the three given equations we get

$$
a = b^{2}, \qquad c - 3 = b^{3}, \qquad c + 5 = a^{2}.
$$

Hence, eliminating $a$ and $c$ we get

$$
b^{3} + 3 = b^{4} - 5 \Longrightarrow b^{3} (b - 1) = 8.
$$

We are only interested in positive solutions b to this equation. Note that $b^{3}(b-1)$ is negative for 0 < b < 1 and then both $b^{3}$ and b - 1 and positive and increasing (without bound) for b > 1. So there is only one positive solution to the equation  -  it is not hard to note in fact b = 2 (so that a = 4 and c = 11). The answer is (a).
