## 题目
You are told that the infinite series
$$
1 + \dfrac{1}{2^2} + \dfrac{1}{3^2} + \dfrac{1}{4^2} + \cdots
\quad\text{and}\quad
1 + \dfrac{1}{3^2} + \dfrac{1}{5^2} + \dfrac{1}{7^2} + \cdots
$$
have sums $\dfrac{\pi^2}{6}$ and $\dfrac{\pi^2}{8}$ respectively. The infinite series
$$
1 - \dfrac{1}{2^2} + \dfrac{1}{3^2} - \dfrac{1}{4^2} + \cdots + (-1)^{n-1}\dfrac{1}{n^2} + \cdots
$$
has sum equal to
(a) $\dfrac{\pi^2}{9}$
(b) $\dfrac{\pi^2}{10}$
(c) $\dfrac{\pi^2}{12}$
(d) $\dfrac{\pi^2}{16}$.

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Let $S$ be the sum of all terms, $O$ be the sum of the odd-indexed terms, and $E$ be the sum of the even-indexed terms. We are given $S = \frac{\pi^2}{6}$ and $O = \frac{\pi^2}{8}$. 
The sum of the even-indexed terms is $E = S - O = \frac{\pi^2}{6} - \frac{\pi^2}{8} = \frac{\pi^2}{24}$. 
The required series is an alternating sum, which can be written as the odd-indexed terms minus the even-indexed terms:

$$O - E = \frac{\pi^2}{8} - \frac{\pi^2}{24} = \frac{3\pi^2 - \pi^2}{24} = \frac{2\pi^2}{24} = \frac{\pi^2}{12}$$

The answer is (c).
