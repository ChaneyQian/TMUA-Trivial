## 题目
The faces of a cube are coloured red or blue. Exactly three are red and three are blue. The number of distinguishable cubes that can be produced (allowing the cube to be turned around) is
(a) $2$
(b) $4$
(c) $6$
(d) $20$.

## 答案
A

## 解析
We can classify the colourings by considering the arrangement of the three red faces. There are two distinct geometric possibilities: either the three red faces share a single vertex (forming a corner), or two of the red faces are opposite each other, meaning the third red face is adjacent to both. Any other arrangement of three faces falls into one of these two categories, and all configurations within each category are rotationally equivalent. Thus, there are exactly $2$ distinguishable cubes. The answer is (a).
