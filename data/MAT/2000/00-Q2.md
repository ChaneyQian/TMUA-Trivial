## 题目
Let $p$ and $q$ be real numbers. Show that the graph
$$
y = x^3 + px + q
$$
has turning points if and only if $p$ is negative.

Assume now that $p$ is negative. Find the values of $y$ at the turning points and hence show that the equation
$$
x^3 + px + q = 0
$$
has three (distinct) real roots if and only if $27q^2 < -4p^3$.

[You may find it helpful to sketch the graph, clearly indicating the turning points.]
