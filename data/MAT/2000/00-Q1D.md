## 题目
The numbers $10$, $11$ and $-12$ are solutions of the cubic equation
(a) $x^3 - 11x^2 - 122x + 1320 = 0$
(b) $x^3 - 9x^2 + 122x - 1320 = 0$
(c) $x^3 - 9x^2 - 142x + 1320 = 0$
(d) $x^3 + 9x^2 - 58x - 1320 = 0$.

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
If $10$, $11$, and $-12$ are the roots of a cubic polynomial $x^3 + ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, then the sum of the roots is $-a$ and the product of the roots is $-c$.
The sum of the roots is $10 + 11 + (-12) = 9$, which means the $x^2$ coefficient must be $-9$.
The product of the roots is $10 \times 11 \times (-12) = -1320$, which means the constant term must be $-(-1320) = 1320$. 
Looking at the options, only $x^3 - 9x^2 - 142x + 1320 = 0$ has both the correct $x^2$ coefficient and the correct constant term. The answer is (c).
