## 题目
A pack of cards consists of $52$ different cards. A malicious dealer changes one of the cards for a second copy of another card in the pack and he then deals the cards to four players, giving thirteen to each. The probability that one players has two identical cards is
(a) $\dfrac{3}{13}$
(b) $\dfrac{12}{51}$
(c) $\dfrac{1}{4}$
(d) $\dfrac{13}{51}$.
