## 题目
The expression $x^2 y + xy^2 + y^2 z + yz^2 + z^2 x + zx^2 - x^3 - y^3 - z^3 - 2xyz$ factorises as
(a) $(x + y + z)(x - y + z)(-x + y - z)$
(b) $(x + y - z)(x - y - z)(-x + y + z)$
(c) $(x + y - z)(x - y + z)(-x + y + z)$
(d) $(x - y - z)(-x - y + z)(-x + y - z)$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
We can evaluate the given expression and the options for specific values of $x, y, z$ to eliminate incorrect choices. Let $x = 1, y = 1, z = 1$. The original expression evaluates to

$$1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + 1 - 1 - 1 - 1 - 2 = 1$$

Testing the options with $x = 1, y = 1, z = 1$:
(a) $(3)(1)(1) = 3$
(b) $(1)(-1)(1) = -1$
(c) $(1)(1)(1) = 1$
(d) $(-1)(1)(1) = -1$

Only option (c) evaluates to $1$. The answer is (c).
