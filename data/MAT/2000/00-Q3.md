## 题目
**(a)**

Find the coordinates of the points at which the two curves $y = 6x^2$ and $y = x^4 - 16$ intersect.

**(b)**

Give a rough sketch of the two curves (in the same diagram) for the range $-3 \leqslant x \leqslant 3$.

**(c)**

Find the area of the region enclosed by the two curves.

## 解析
**(a)** At an intersection $6x^2 = x^4 - 16$, so

$$x^4 - 6x^2 - 16 = 0 \implies \left(x^2 - 8\right)\left(x^2+2\right) = 0.$$

Since $x^2 + 2 > 0$ for real $x$, the only possibility is $x^2 = 8$, i.e. $x = \pm 2\sqrt{2}$, and then $y = 6 \times 8 = 48$. The curves meet at

$$\left(2\sqrt{2},\,48\right) \qquad \text{and} \qquad \left(-2\sqrt{2},\,48\right).$$

**(b)** Both curves are symmetric about the $y$-axis. The parabola $y = 6x^2$ has its vertex at the origin and reaches $54$ at $x = \pm3$. The quartic $y = x^4 - 16$ has its minimum $-16$ at $x=0$, crosses the $x$-axis at $x = \pm 2$, and reaches $65$ at $x = \pm 3$; it is flatter than the parabola near the origin but climbs past it after the crossing points $x = \pm 2\sqrt{2} \approx \pm 2.83$.

**(c)** Between the intersection points the parabola lies above the quartic (at $x=0$ the values are $0$ and $-16$). So the enclosed area is

$$\int_{-2\sqrt2}^{2\sqrt2}\Bigl(6x^2 - \left(x^4-16\right)\Bigr)\mathrm{d}x = \int_{-2\sqrt2}^{2\sqrt2}\left(-x^4 + 6x^2 + 16\right)\mathrm{d}x.$$

The integrand is even, so this is

$$2\left[-\frac{x^5}{5} + 2x^3 + 16x\right]_{0}^{2\sqrt2} = 2\left(-\frac{128\sqrt2}{5} + 32\sqrt2 + 32\sqrt2\right),$$

using $\left(2\sqrt2\right)^3 = 16\sqrt2$ and $\left(2\sqrt2\right)^5 = 128\sqrt2$. Hence the area is

$$2 \times \frac{-128\sqrt2 + 320\sqrt2}{5} = \frac{384\sqrt2}{5} \approx 108.6.$$
