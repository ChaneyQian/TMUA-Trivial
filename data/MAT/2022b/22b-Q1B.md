## 答案

## 解析
If we write $u = e^{-x/2}$ then the given expression is $u - u^{2}$ . This quadratic has roots at 0 and 1, and is positive in between. Now as x varies from 0 to ∞, u will decay exponentially from 1 to 0. Along the way, $u - u^{2}$ will start at 0, then vary through positive numbers, first increasing for $u > 1 / 2$ then decreasing for $u < 1 / 2$ and eventually tending towards 0. The answer is (a)
