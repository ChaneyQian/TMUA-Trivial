## 题目
A circle is inscribed in an equilateral triangle as shown in the diagram below.

![[Image/05-Q1E-fig1.jpg]]

The area of the circle, as a percentage of the triangle's, is approximately
(a) $40\%$
(b) $50\%$
(c) $60\%$
(d) $70\%$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Let the side length of the equilateral triangle be $a$. The area of the triangle is:

$$A_{\text{triangle}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^{2}$$

The inscribed circle of an equilateral triangle has a radius $r$ equal to its apothem, which is given by:

$$r = \frac{a}{2\sqrt{3}}$$

The area of the inscribed circle is:

$$A_{\text{circle}} = \pi r^{2} = \pi \left(\frac{a}{2\sqrt{3}}\right)^{2} = \frac{\pi a^{2}}{12}$$

The ratio of the area of the circle to the area of the triangle is:

$$\frac{A_{\text{circle}}}{A_{\text{triangle}}} = \frac{\frac{\pi a^{2}}{12}}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^{2}} = \frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{3}}$$

Using the approximations $\pi \approx 3.14159$ and $\sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$:

$$\frac{\pi}{3\sqrt{3}} \approx \frac{3.14159}{3 \times 1.732} \approx \frac{3.14159}{5.196} \approx 0.6046$$

Converting this to a percentage, the area is approximately $60\%$ of the triangle's area. The answer is (c).
