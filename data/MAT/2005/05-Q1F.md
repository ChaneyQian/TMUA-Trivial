## 题目
The fact that
$$
6 \times 7 = 42,
$$
is a counter-example to which of the following statements?
(a) the product of any two odd integers is odd;
(b) if the product of two integers is not a multiple of $4$ then the integers are not consecutive;
(c) if the product of two integers is a multiple of $4$ then the integers are not consecutive;
(d) any even integer can be written as the product of two even integers.

## 答案
B

## 解析
We are given the fact that $6 \times 7 = 42$. Here, $6$ and $7$ are consecutive integers, and their product is $42$. Note that $42$ is not a multiple of $4$ (since $42 \div 4 = 10.5$). 
Let us check the statements to see which one this fact contradicts:
(a) Claims the product of two odd integers is odd. Our numbers are even and odd, so this is irrelevant.
(b) Claims that if the product of two integers is not a multiple of $4$, then the integers are not consecutive. Here, the product ($42$) is not a multiple of $4$, but the integers ($6$ and $7$) ARE consecutive. This is a direct counter-example to statement (b).
(c) Claims that if the product is a multiple of $4$, they are not consecutive. Our product is not a multiple of $4$, so this is irrelevant.
(d) Claims any even integer can be written as the product of two even integers. While $42$ cannot be, the fact $6 \times 7 = 42$ does not logically disprove it on its own without testing all pairs. Statement (b) is directly disproved by the single example. The answer is (b).
