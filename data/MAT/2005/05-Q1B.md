## 题目
The equation
$$
(x^{2} + 1)^{10} = 2x - x^{2} - 2
$$
(a) has $x = 2$ as a solution;
(b) has no real solutions;
(c) has an odd number of real solutions;
(d) has twenty real solutions.

## 答案
B

## 解析
Let us analyze the minimum and maximum possible values for the left-hand and right-hand sides of the equation.
The left-hand side is $(x^{2} + 1)^{10}$. Since $x^{2} \geqslant 0$ for all real $x$, $x^{2} + 1 \geqslant 1$. Thus:

$$(x^{2} + 1)^{10} \geqslant 1^{10} = 1$$

The right-hand side is $2x - x^{2} - 2$. We can complete the square to find its maximum value:

$$2x - x^{2} - 2 = -(x^{2} - 2x + 1) - 1 = -(x - 1)^{2} - 1$$

Since $-(x - 1)^{2} \leqslant 0$ for all real $x$, we have:

$$2x - x^{2} - 2 \leqslant -1$$

Since the left-hand side is always at least $1$ and the right-hand side is always at most $-1$, they can never be equal for any real value of $x$. The equation has no real solutions. The answer is (b).
