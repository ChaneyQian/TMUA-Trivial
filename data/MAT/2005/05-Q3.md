## 题目
**(a)**

Find the co-ordinates of the turning points of
$$
f(x) = e^{x}\bigl(2x^{2} - x - 1\bigr).
$$

**(b)**

Sketch the graph of $y = f(x)$ on the axes below for the range $-4 \leqslant x \leqslant 2$.

**(c)**

Now consider
$$
g(x) =
\begin{cases}
e^{x}\bigl(2x^{2} - x - 1\bigr) & \text{if } x < 1; \\
\sin(x - 1) & \text{if } x \geqslant 1.
\end{cases}
$$
Determine, with explanations, the maximum and minimum values of $g(x)$ as $x$ varies over the real numbers.
