## 题目
The number of positive values $x$ which satisfy the equation $x = 8 ^{\log_{2} x } - 9 ^{\log_{3} x } - 4 ^{\log_{2} x } + \log_{0.5} 0 . 25$ is

(a) $0,$
(b) $1,$
(c) $2,$
(d) $3.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
Note that $\log_{0.5} 0 . 25 = 2$ and that if $x > 0$ then

$$
8 ^{\log_{2} x} = 2 ^{3\log_{2} x} = \left(2 ^{\log_{2} x}\right) ^{3} = x^{3}
$$

and likewise $9 ^{\log_{3} x } = x^{2} = 4 ^{\log_{2} x }$ . The given equation then rearranges to

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & x = x^{3} - x^{2} - x^{2} + 2 \\ \Longleftrightarrow & x^{3} - 2 x^{2} - x + 2 = 0 \\ \Longleftrightarrow & (x - 2) (x^{2} - 1) = 0 \\ \Longleftrightarrow & (x - 2) (x - 1) (x + 1) = 0. \end{array}
$$

So the positive roots are $x = 1 , 2$ and the answer is (c).
