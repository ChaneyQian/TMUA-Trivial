## 题目
The circle in the diagram has centre C. Three angles $\alpha , \beta , \gamma$ are also indicated.

![[Image/11-Q1E-fig1.jpg]]

The angles $\alpha , \beta , \gamma$ are related by the equation:

(a) $\cos \alpha = \sin \left( \beta + \gamma \right)$

(b) $\sin \beta = \sin \alpha \sin \gamma ;$

(c) $\sin \beta \left( 1 - \cos \alpha \right) = \sin \gamma$ ;

(d) $\sin ( \alpha + \beta ) = \cos \gamma \sin \alpha$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
Without any loss of generality, we can assume the radius of the circle to be 1. Let A be the vertex of the angles $\alpha$ and $\beta$ and B be the vertex of the angle $\gamma$. We see that AC has length $1 / \sin \alpha$. Applying the sine rule to the triangle ABC we find

$$
\frac {\sin \beta}{1} = \frac {\sin \gamma}{1 / \sin \alpha}
$$

and the answer is (b).
