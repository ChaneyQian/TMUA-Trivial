## 题目
For **ALL APPLICANTS**.

Suppose that x satisfies the equation

$$
x^{3} = 2 x + 1. \quad (*)
$$

(i) Show that

$$
x^{4} = x + 2 x^{2} \qquad \mathrm{and} \qquad x^{5} = 2 + 4 x + x^{2}.
$$

(ii) For every integer $k \geqslant 0$ , we can uniquely write

$$
x^{k} = A_{k} + B_{k} x + C_{k} x^{2}
$$

where $A_{k} , B_{k} , C_{k}$ are integers. So, in part (i), it was shown that

$$
A_{4} = 0, B_{4} = 1, C_{4} = 2 \quad \text { and } \quad A_{5} = 2, B_{5} = 4, C_{5} = 1.
$$

Show that

$$
A_{k+1} = C_{k}, \qquad B_{k+1} = A_{k} + 2 C_{k}, \qquad C_{k+1} = B_{k}.
$$

(iii) Let

$$
D_{k} = A_{k} + C_{k} - B_{k}.
$$

Show that $D_{k+1} = - D_{k}$ and hence that

$$
A_{k} + C_{k} = B_{k} + (- 1) ^{k}.
$$

(iv) Let $F_{k} = A_{k+1} + C_{k+1}$ . Show that

$$
F_{k} + F_{k+1} = F_{k+2}.
$$

## 解析
2. (i) [2 marks] Multiplying $x^{3} = 2 x + 1$ by x we get the first equation. Multiplying by x again we get

$$
x^{5} = x^{2} + 2 x^{3} = x^{2} + 2 (2 x + 1) = 2 + 4 x + x^{2}.
$$

(ii) [5 marks] If $x^{k} = A_{k} + B_{k} x + C_{k} x^{2}$ then

$$
\begin{array}{r c l} x^{k+1} & = & A_{k} x + B_{k} x^{2} + C_{k} x^{3} \\ & = & A_{k} x + B_{k} x^{2} + C_{k} (2 x + 1) \\ & = & C_{k} + (A_{k} + 2 C_{k}) x + B_{k} x^{2}. \end{array}
$$

Comparing the coefficients of $1 , x , x^{2}$ we then get

$$
A_{k+1} = C_{k}, \qquad B_{k+1} = A_{k} + 2 C_{k}, \qquad C_{k+1} = B_{k}.
$$

(iii) [5 marks] With $D_{k} = A_{k} + C_{k} - B_{k}$ we have

$$
\begin{array}{r c l} D_{k+1} & = & A_{k+1} + C_{k+1} - B_{k+1} \\ & = & C_{k} + B_{k} - (A_{k} + 2 C_{k}) \qquad [ \text {using the previous part} ] \\ & = & B_{k} - A_{k} - C_{k} \\ & = & - D_{k}. \end{array}
$$

As $1 = 1 + 0 x + 0 x^{2}$ then $A_{0} = 1 , B_{0} = 0 , C_{0} = 0$ and $D_{0} = 1$ . So $D_{k} = { \left( - 1 \right) } ^{k}$ and

$$
A_{k} + C_{k} = B_{k} + (- 1) ^{k}.
$$

(iv) [3 marks] Let $F_{k} = A_{k+1} + C_{k+1}$ . Then

$$
\begin{array}{r c l} F_{k} + F_{k+1} & = & (A_{k+1} + C_{k+1}) + (A_{k+2} + C_{k+2}) \\ & = & B_{k+1} + (- 1) ^{k+1} + B_{k+2} + (- 1) ^{k+2} \\ & = & B_{k+1} + B_{k+2} \\ & = & C_{k+2} + C_{k+3} \\ & = & A_{k+3} + C_{k+3} \\ & = & F_{k+2}. \end{array}
$$
