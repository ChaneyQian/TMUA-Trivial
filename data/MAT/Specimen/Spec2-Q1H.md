## 题目
The equation
$$
\left(x^{2} + 1\right)^{10} = 2x - x^{2} - 2
$$
(a) has $x = 2$ as a solution;
(b) has no real solutions;
(c) has an odd number of real solutions;
(d) has twenty real solutions.

## 答案
B

## 解析
Completing the square on the RHS of the equation gives
$$
\left(x^{2} + 1\right)^{10} = 2x - x^{2} - 2 = -1 - (x - 1)^{2}.
$$
For real $x$, the LHS is always positive and the RHS is always negative and so the equation has no real solutions. The answer is (b).
