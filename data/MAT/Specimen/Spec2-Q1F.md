## 题目
The turning point of the parabola
$$
y = x^{2} - 2ax + 1
$$
is closest to the origin when
(a) $a = 0$
(b) $a = \pm 1$
(c) $a = \pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ or $a = 0$
(d) $a = \pm\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.

## 答案
D

## 解析
As
$$
y = x^{2} - 2ax + 1 = (x - a)^{2} + \left(1 - a^{2}\right)
$$
then the parabola's turning point is at $\left(a,\, 1 - a^{2}\right)$.

The distance $D$ from the origin to this point satisfies
$$
D^{2} = a^{2} + \left(1 - a^{2}\right)^{2} = a^{4} - a^{2} + 1 = \left(a^{2} - \frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} + \frac{3}{4}.
$$
So $D^{2}$, and hence $D$, are at a minimum when $a^{2} = 1/2$. The answer is (d).
