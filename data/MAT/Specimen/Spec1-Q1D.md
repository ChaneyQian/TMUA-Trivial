## 题目
The equation $x^{3} - 30x^{2} + 108x - 104 = 0$
(a) no real roots;
(b) exactly one real root;
(c) three distinct real roots;
(d) a repeated root.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Let
$$
f(x) = x^{3} - 30x^{2} + 108x - 104.
$$
By inspection we note that
$$
f(2) = 8 - 120 + 216 - 104 = 0,
$$
so that $(x - 2)$ is a factor of $f(x)$. We see
$$
f(x) = (x - 2)\left(x^{2} - 28x + 52\right) = (x - 2)(x - 2)(x - 26).
$$
Hence $x = 2$ is a repeated root of the equation and the answer is (d).
