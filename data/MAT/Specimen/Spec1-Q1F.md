## 题目
How many values of $x$ satisfy the equation
$$
2 \cos^{2} x + 5 \sin x = 4
$$
in the range $0 \leqslant x < 2\pi$?
(a) $2$
(b) $4$
(c) $6$
(d) $8$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
We have
$$
2\cos^{2} x + 5 \sin x = 4 \implies 2 - 2\sin^{2} x + 5 \sin x = 4 \implies 2\sin^{2} x - 5 \sin x + 2 = 0 \implies (2\sin x - 1)(\sin x - 2) = 0.
$$
Hence $\sin x = 1/2$, which occurs at $\pi/6$ and $5\pi/6$ in the given range, or $\sin x = 2$, which is true for no values of $x$. Hence there are two solutions and the answer is (a).
