## 题目
Observe that $2^{3} = 8$, $2^{5} = 32$, $3^{2} = 9$ and $3^{3} = 27$. From these facts, we can deduce that $\log_{2} 3$, the logarithm of $3$ to base $2$, is
(a) between $1\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $1\dfrac{1}{2}$;
(b) between $1\dfrac{1}{2}$ and $1\dfrac{2}{3}$;
(c) between $1\dfrac{2}{3}$ and $2$;
(d) between $2$ and $3$.

## 答案
B

## 解析
We are told that
$$
2^{3} < 3^{2} < 3^{3} < 2^{5}.
$$
So, taking logarithms to base $2$ of these inequalities (remembering $\log_{2} x$ is an increasing function) then
$$
3 < 2\log_{2} 3 < 3 \log_{2} 3 < 5.
$$
Hence
$$
\frac{3}{2} < \log_{2} 3 < \frac{5}{3},
$$
and the answer is (b).
