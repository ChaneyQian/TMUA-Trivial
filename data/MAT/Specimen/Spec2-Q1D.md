## 题目
The numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy the following inequalities
$$
\begin{aligned}
2x + 3y &\leqslant 23, \\
x + 2 &\leqslant 3y, \\
3y + 1 &\leqslant 4x.
\end{aligned}
$$
The largest possible value of $x$ is
(a) $6$
(b) $7$
(c) $8$
(d) $9$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Sketching the three lines
$$
2x + 3y = 23, \qquad x + 2 = 3y, \qquad 3y + 1 = 4x,
$$
we see (as in the diagram below)

![[Image/Spec2-Q1D-sol1.png]]
that the intersection of the three given regions is the interior of the triangle with vertices $(1, 1)$, $(4, 5)$, $(7, 3)$. Hence the answer is (b).
