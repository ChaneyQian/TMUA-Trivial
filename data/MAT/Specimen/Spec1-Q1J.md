## 答案
C

## 解析
If $x^{2}y^{2}(x + y) = 1$ then it is clear that $x \neq 0$ and $y \neq 0$. This means that the curve never crosses the $x$- and $y$-axes. This eliminates (a) and (b) as options. From $x^{2}y^{2}(x + y) = 1$ it follows that
$$
x + y = \frac{1}{x^{2}y^{2}} > 0
$$
and so the curve lies entirely in the region $x + y > 0$, which eliminates (d) as a possibility. Hence the correct answer is (c).
