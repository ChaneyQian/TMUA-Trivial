## 题目
In the range $0 \leqslant x < 2\pi$ the equation
$$
\cos(\sin x) = \frac{1}{2}
$$
has
(a) no solutions;
(b) one solution;
(c) two solutions;
(d) three solutions.

## 答案
A

## 解析
The equation $\cos\theta = 1/2$ has solutions
$$
\theta = \pm\frac{\pi}{3},\ \pm\frac{5\pi}{3},\ \pm\frac{7\pi}{3},\ \pm\frac{11\pi}{3},\ \cdots
$$
So if $\cos(\sin x) = 1/2$ then
$$
\sin x = \pm\frac{\pi}{3},\ \pm\frac{5\pi}{3},\ \pm\frac{7\pi}{3},\ \pm\frac{11\pi}{3},\ \cdots
$$
Note that the smallest numbers on this list are $\pm\pi/3$; as $\pi > 3$ both of these lie outside sine's range, which is $-1 \leqslant \sin x \leqslant 1$. So $\sin x$ can never equal any of the numbers on the above list and there are no solutions. The answer is (a).
