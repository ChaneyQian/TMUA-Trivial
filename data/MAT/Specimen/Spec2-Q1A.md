## 题目
The point lying between $P(2, 3)$ and $Q(8, -3)$ which divides the line $PQ$ in the ratio $1 : 2$ has co-ordinates
(a) $(4, -1)$
(b) $(6, -2)$
(c) $\left(\dfrac{14}{3}, 2\right)$
(d) $(4, 1)$

## 答案
D

## 解析
The vector from $P(2, 3)$ to $Q(8, -3)$ is $\overrightarrow{PQ} = (6, -6)$. If the point $R$ lies on $PQ$ such that $PR : RQ = 1 : 2$ then $R$ is one third of the way along $PQ$. Hence
$$
R = P + \frac{1}{3}\overrightarrow{PQ} = (2, 3) + \frac{1}{3}(6, -6) = (4, 1)
$$
and the answer is (d).
