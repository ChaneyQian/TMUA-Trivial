## 题目
The inequalities $x^{2} + 3x + 2 > 0$ and $x^{2} + x < 2$, are met by all $x$ in the region:
(a) $x < -2$;
(b) $-1 < x < 1$;
(c) $x > -1$;
(d) $x > -2$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Note
$$
x^{2} + 3x + 2 > 0 \implies (x + 1)(x + 2) > 0 \implies x > -1 \text{ or } x < -2. \tag{1}
$$
Also
$$
x^{2} + x < 2 \implies (x - 1)(x + 2) < 0 \implies -2 < x < 1. \tag{2}
$$
The only $x$ for which (1) and (2) agree are in the range $-1 < x < 1$. Hence the answer is (b).
