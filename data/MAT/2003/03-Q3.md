## 题目
Let $O$, $P$, $P_{1}$, $P_{2}$ be the points in the $(x, y)$-plane with coordinates $(0, 0)$, $(s, 1/s)$, $(s_{1}, 1/s_{1})$, $(s_{2}, 1/s_{2})$ respectively.

**(a)**

Using the axes below, sketch the curve traced out by $P$ as $s$ varies over non-zero real values, and find an equation for the curve in the form $y = f(x)$.
![[Image/03-Q3-fig1.jpg]]

**(b)**

Write down the equation of the straight line $PP_{1}$ joining $P$ to $P_{1}$, giving your answer in the form $y = m_{1}x + c_{1}$.

**(c)**

Show that the line $PP_{1}$ is perpendicular to $PP_{2}$ if, and only if, $s_{1}s_{2} = -1/s^{2}$.

**(d)**

Let $m_{1}$, $m_{2}$, $n_{1}$, $n_{2}$ be the gradients of the lines $PP_{1}$, $PP_{2}$, $OP_{1}$, $OP_{2}$ respectively. Show that
$$
\left(\dfrac{m_{1}}{m_{2}}\right)^{2} = \dfrac{n_{1}}{n_{2}}.
$$
