## 题目
How many solutions does the equation
$$
\sin 2x = \cos x
$$
have in the range $0 \leqslant x \leqslant \pi$?
(a) one solution;
(b) two solutions;
(c) three solutions;
(d) four solutions.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Using the double angle identity $\sin 2x = 2\sin x \cos x$, we can rewrite the equation as:

$$2\sin x \cos x = \cos x \implies \cos x (2\sin x - 1) = 0$$

This gives two possibilities: $\cos x = 0$ or $\sin x = \frac{1}{2}$.
In the range $0 \leqslant x \leqslant \pi$:
- $\cos x = 0$ has one solution: $x = \frac{\pi}{2}$.
- $\sin x = \frac{1}{2}$ has two solutions: $x = \frac{\pi}{6}$ and $x = \frac{5\pi}{6}$.

Therefore, there are $3$ solutions in total. The answer is (c).
