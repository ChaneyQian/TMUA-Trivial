## 题目
You are given that $e^{3}$ is approximately $20$ and that $2^{10}$ is approximately $1000$. Using this information, a student can obtain an approximate value for $\ln 2$. Which of the following is it?
(a) $\dfrac{7}{10}$
(b) $\dfrac{9}{13}$
(c) $\dfrac{38}{55}$
(d) $\dfrac{41}{59}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
We are given $2^{10} \approx 1000 = 10^3$ and $\mathrm{e}^3 \approx 20 = 2 \times 10$.
From the second approximation, we can express $10$ as:

$$10 \approx \frac{\mathrm{e}^3}{2}$$

Substituting this into the first approximation gives:

$$2^{10} \approx \left(\frac{\mathrm{e}^3}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{\mathrm{e}^9}{2^3}$$

Multiplying both sides by $2^3$, we get $2^{13} \approx \mathrm{e}^9$. Taking the natural logarithm of both sides gives:

$$13\ln 2 \approx 9 \implies \ln 2 \approx \frac{9}{13}$$

The answer is (b).
