## 题目
Two players take turns to throw a fair six-sided die until one of them scores a six. What is the probability that the first player to throw the die is the first to score a six?
(a) $\dfrac{5}{9}$
(b) $\dfrac{3}{5}$
(c) $\dfrac{6}{11}$
(d) $\dfrac{7}{12}$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Let $p$ be the probability that the first player wins. The first player can win in two mutually exclusive ways:
1. They roll a $6$ on their very first throw (probability $\frac{1}{6}$).
2. They do not roll a $6$ (probability $\frac{5}{6}$), the second player also does not roll a $6$ (probability $\frac{5}{6}$), and then the first player goes on to win from what is essentially a new game. The probability of winning from this point is $p$.

Thus, we can write an equation for $p$:

$$p = \frac{1}{6} + \left(\frac{5}{6}\right)\left(\frac{5}{6}\right)p = \frac{1}{6} + \frac{25}{36}p$$

Solving for $p$:

$$p \left(1 - \frac{25}{36}\right) = \frac{1}{6} \implies \frac{11}{36}p = \frac{1}{6} \implies p = \frac{6}{11}$$

The answer is (c).
