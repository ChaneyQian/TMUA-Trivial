## 题目
Let $k$ and $n$ be positive integers such that $n \geqslant 2k$ and
$$
\dfrac{(n - 2)!}{(n - 2k)!} = k! \, 2^{k-1}.
$$
(Recall that, for $r \geqslant 1$, $r!$ is the product $r \cdot (r - 1) \cdot (r - 2) \cdots 2 \cdot 1$ and that $0!$ is defined to equal $1$.)

**(a)**

Suppose that $k = 1$. What are the possible values of $n$?

**(b)**

Suppose that $k = 2$. Show that $(n - 2)(n - 3) = 4$. What are the possible values of $n$?

**(c)**

Suppose that $k = 3$. Show that it is impossible that $n \geqslant 7$.

**(d)**

Suppose that $k \geqslant 4$. Show that there are no possible values of $n$.
