## 题目
The substitution $x = y + t$ transforms the equation $x^{3} + ax^{2} + bx + c = 0$ into an equation of the form $y^{3} + py + q = 0$ when

(a) $t = \dfrac{a}{3}$
(b) $t = -\dfrac{a}{3}$
(c) $t = a$
(d) $t = -a$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Substituting $x = y + t$ into the equation gives

$$(y + t)^3 + a(y + t)^2 + b(y + t) + c = 0$$

Expanding the terms, the coefficient of $y^2$ is $3t + a$. For the equation to be in the form $y^3 + py + q = 0$, the $y^2$ term must vanish. This requires $3t + a = 0$, which gives $t = -\frac{a}{3}$. The answer is (b).
