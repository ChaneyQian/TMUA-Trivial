## 题目
The maximum gradient of the curve $y = x^{4} - 4x^{3} + 4x^{2} + 2$ in the range $0 \leq x \leq 2\dfrac{1}{5}$ occur when

(a) $x = 0$
(b) $x = 1 - \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
(c) $x = 1 + \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$
(d) $x = 2\dfrac{1}{5}$.

## 答案
D

## 解析
Let the gradient be $g(x) = \frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = 4x^3 - 12x^2 + 8x$. We want to maximize $g(x)$ on the interval $[0, \frac{11}{5}]$. 
Setting $g'(x) = 12x^2 - 24x + 8 = 0$ yields $3x^2 - 6x + 2 = 0$, which has roots $x = 1 \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$. 
Evaluating $g(x)$ at the local maximum $x = 1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ gives $g(1 - \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}}) = \frac{8}{3\sqrt{3}} \approx 1.54$. 
We must also check the endpoints. At $x = 0$, $g(0) = 0$. At the right endpoint $x = \frac{11}{5}$, we have

$$g\left(\frac{11}{5}\right) = 4\left(\frac{11}{5}\right)^3 - 12\left(\frac{11}{5}\right)^2 + 8\left(\frac{11}{5}\right) = \frac{264}{125} = 2.112$$

Since $2.112 > 1.54$, the absolute maximum occurs at the endpoint $x = 2\frac{1}{5}$. The answer is (d).
