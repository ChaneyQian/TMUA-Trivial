## 题目
A pack of cards consists of $52$ different cards. A malicious dealer changes one of the cards for a second copy of another card in the pack and he then deals the cards to four players, giving thirteen to each. The probability that one player has two identical cards is

(a) $\dfrac{3}{13}$
(b) $\dfrac{12}{51}$
(c) $\dfrac{1}{4}$
(d) $\dfrac{13}{51}$.

## 答案
B

## 解析
There are $52$ cards in total, but one card has been replaced by a duplicate, so there is exactly one pair of identical cards in the deck. We can imagine the $52$ cards being dealt into $52$ distinct slots, with $13$ slots belonging to each of the four players. 
The first identical card is dealt into one of the $52$ slots. For the same player to receive the second identical card, it must be dealt into one of the remaining $12$ slots belonging to that player. Since there are $51$ remaining slots in total, the probability is

$$\frac{12}{51}$$

The answer is (b).
