## 题目
The shortest distance from the origin to the line $3x + 4y = 25$ is

(a) $3$
(b) $4$
(c) $5$
(d) $6$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The shortest distance from the origin $(0, 0)$ to a line given by the equation $Ax + By + C = 0$ is $\frac{|C|}{\sqrt{A^2 + B^2}}$. Writing the line as $3x + 4y - 25 = 0$, the distance is

$$\frac{|-25|}{\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2}} = \frac{25}{5} = 5$$

The answer is (c).
