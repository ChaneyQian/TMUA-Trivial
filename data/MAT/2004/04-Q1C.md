## 题目
The turning point of the parabola
$$
y = x^{2} - 2ax + 1
$$
is closest to the origin when
(a) $a = 0$
(b) $a = \pm 1$
(c) $a = \pm \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ or $a = 0$
(d) $a = \pm \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
By completing the square, we can find the turning point of the parabola $y = x^{2} - 2ax + 1$:

$$y = (x - a)^{2} + 1 - a^{2}$$

The turning point is $T(a, 1 - a^{2})$. We want to minimize the distance to the origin, which is equivalent to minimizing the square of the distance:

$$D = a^{2} + (1 - a^{2})^{2} = a^{2} + 1 - 2a^{2} + a^{4} = a^{4} - a^{2} + 1$$

We complete the square for this quartic expression in terms of $a^{2}$:

$$D = \left(a^{2} - \frac{1}{2}\right)^{2} + \frac{3}{4}$$

This expression achieves its minimum value when $a^{2} = \frac{1}{2}$, which gives $a = \pm\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$. The answer is (d).
