## 题目
**(a)**

For what values of the constant $k$ does the quadratic equation
$$
x^{2} - 2x - 1 = k
$$
have:
(i) no real solutions;
(ii) one real solution;
(iii) two real solutions.

**(b)**

Showing your working, express $(x^{2} - 2x - 1)^{2}$ as a polynomial of degree $4$ in $x$.

**(c)**

Show that the quartic equation
$$
x^{4} - 4x^{3} + 2x^{2} + 4x + 1 = h
$$
has exactly two real solutions if either $h = 0$ or $h > 4$.

Show that there is no value of $h$ such that the above quartic equation has just one real solution.
