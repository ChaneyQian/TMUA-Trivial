## 题目
Which of the following numbers is largest in value? (All angles are given in radians.)
(a) $\tan\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{4}\right)$
(b) $\sin^{2}\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{4}\right)$
(c) $\log_{10}\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{4}\right)$
(d) $\ln\left(\dfrac{5\pi}{4}\right)$

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
We evaluate the approximate magnitude of each expression. First, notice that the angle $\frac{5\pi}{4}$ lies in the third quadrant and its tangent is $1$, so (a) is exactly $1$.
For (b), $\sin^{2}\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right) = \left(-\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{2} = 0.5$.
For the logarithmic values, we note that $\frac{5\pi}{4} \approx \frac{5 \times 3.1415}{4} \approx 3.927$.
For (c), since $1 < 3.927 < 10$, $\log_{10}(3.927)$ is between $0$ and $1$.
For (d), since $\mathrm{e} \approx 2.718$ and $3.927 > \mathrm{e}$, taking the natural logarithm gives $\ln(3.927) > \ln(\mathrm{e}) = 1$.
Thus, $\ln\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right)$ is strictly greater than $1$, making it the largest of the four values. The answer is (d).
