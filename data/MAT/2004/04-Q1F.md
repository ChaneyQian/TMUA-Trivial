## 题目
![[Image/04-Q1F-fig1.jpg]]

In the figure above, $A$, $B$, $P$ and $Q$ are arbitrary distinct points on a circle centred at $O$. The chords $AQ$ and $BP$ meet at $R$, and the angles $\angle APB$, $\angle AQB$ and $\angle ARB$ are equal to $x$, $y$ and $z$ respectively. Which of the following statements is true?
(a) $x + y = \dfrac{\pi}{2}$;
(b) $x + y = z$;
(c) $x + y = 2x$;
(d) $x + y = \pi$.

## 答案
C

## 解析
The points $A, B, P, Q$ all lie on the circumference of a circle. The angles $\angle APB$ and $\angle AQB$ are both subtended by the same chord $AB$ and both points $P$ and $Q$ must lie on the same side of the chord $AB$ since the chords $AQ$ and $BP$ intersect at $R$ inside the circle.
According to the circle theorem (angles subtended by the same arc at the circumference are equal), we must have:

$$x = \angle APB = \angle AQB = y$$

Since $x = y$, the equation $x + y = 2x$ is always identically true. (The other relations involve $z$, the angle at the intersection point, which depends on the other arcs and does not give a constant universal relation for all possible configurations). The answer is (c).
