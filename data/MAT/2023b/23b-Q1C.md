## 题目
The sum $\sum_{k=1} ^{n} k^{3} = { \frac { n^{2} ( n + 1 ) ^{2} } { 4 } }$ . It follows that

$$
1 ^{3} + 3 ^{3} + 5 ^{3} + 7 ^{3} + \dots + 19 ^{3}
$$

is equal to

(a) $19{,}800$

(b) $19{,}900$

(c) $20{,}000$

(d) $20{,}100$

(e) $20{,}200$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
The required sum is the sum of all cubes up to $20 ^{3}$ minus the sum of the even cubes up to $20 ^{3}$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} 1 ^{3} + 3 ^{3} + 5 ^{3} + 7 ^{3} + \dots + 19 ^{3} & = \left(1 ^{3} + 2 ^{3} + 3 ^{3} + 4 ^{3} + \dots + 20 ^{3}\right) - \left(2 ^{3} + 4 ^{3} + 6 ^{3} + \dots + 20 ^{3}\right) \\ & = \left(1 ^{3} + 2 ^{3} + 3 ^{3} + 4 ^{3} + \dots + 20 ^{3}\right) - 2 ^{3} \left(1 ^{3} + 2 ^{3} + 3 ^{3} + \dots + 10 ^{3}\right) \\ & = \frac {20 ^{2} \times 21 ^{2}}{4} - 8 \times \frac {10 ^{2} \times 11 ^{2}}{4} \\ & = 10 ^{2} \times \left(21 ^{2} - 2 \times 11 ^{2}\right) \\ & = 10 ^{2} \times (441 - 2 \times 121) \\ & = 19, 900 \end{array}
$$

The answer is (b)
