$$
y = x^{2}\ln x

(a) $x\,\dfrac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x} = 2y + x^{2}$, (and only this part),

(c) $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}^{2}y}{\mathrm{d}x^{2}} \ne 0$ for all $x$, (and only this part),
(d) all of the above.
