## 题目
**For ALL APPLICANTS.**

Three equilateral triangles with areas $A$, $B$, $C$ are drawn on the sides of a right-angled triangle as in the diagram below.

![[Image/06-Q1G-fig1.jpg]]

These areas are related by the equation:
(a) $A^{1/2} + B^{1/2} = C^{1/2}$,
(b) $A + B = C$,
(c) $A^{3/2} + B^{3/2} = C^{3/2}$,
(d) $A^{2} + B^{2} = C^{2}$.

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Let the side lengths of the right-angled triangle be $a$, $b$, and $c$, with $c$ being the hypotenuse. By Pythagoras' theorem, we have:
$$a^{2} + b^{2} = c^{2}$$

The area of an equilateral triangle with side length $s$ is $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}s^{2}$. 
Thus, the areas of the three equilateral triangles are:
$A = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^{2}$, \quad $B = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}b^{2}$, \quad $C = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}c^{2}$

If we add the areas $A$ and $B$, we get:
$$A + B = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}a^{2} + \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}b^{2} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}(a^{2} + b^{2})$$
Substituting $a^{2} + b^{2} = c^{2}$ gives:
$$A + B = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}c^{2} = C$$
Thus, $A + B = C$. The answer is (b).
