## 题目
**For ALL APPLICANTS.**

Which of the following numbers is largest?
(a) $\bigl((2^{3})^{2}\bigr)^{3}$
(b) $(2^{3})^{(2^{3})}$
(c) $2^{((3^{2})^{3})}$
(d) $2^{(3^{(2^{3})})}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
We can evaluate each of the numbers by using the laws of exponents:
(a) $\bigl((2^{3})^{2}\bigr)^{3} = 2^{3 \times 2 \times 3} = 2^{18}$
(b) $(2^{3})^{(2^{3})} = (2^{3})^{8} = 2^{3 \times 8} = 2^{24}$
(c) $2^{((3^{2})^{3})} = 2^{(9^{3})} = 2^{729}$
(d) $2^{(3^{(2^{3})})} = 2^{(3^{8})} = 2^{6561}$

Comparing the exponents of $2$, we have $18 < 24 < 729 < 6561$. Therefore, (d) is by far the largest number. The answer is (d).
