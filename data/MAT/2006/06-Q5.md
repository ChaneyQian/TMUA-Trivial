## 题目
**For ALL APPLICANTS.**

For any four numbers $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$, the symbol
$$
\frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d}
$$
represents a number that depends on $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$ and has the following properties:
$$
\begin{align}
\frac{s\times a\mid s\times b}{c\mid d} &= s \times \frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d} \tag{1} \\
\frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d} &= \frac{d\mid b}{c\mid a} \tag{2} \\
\frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d} &= \frac{a\mid c}{b\mid d} \tag{3} \\
\frac{a+x\mid b+y}{c\mid d} &= \frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d} + \frac{x\mid y}{c\mid d} \tag{4}
\end{align}
$$
You may assume nothing else about $\dfrac{a\mid b}{c\mid d}$.

**(a)**

Use property (1) to show that $\dfrac{0\mid 0}{c\mid d} = 0$.

**(b)**

Hence use properties (2) and (3) to show that each of
$$
\frac{a\mid 0}{c\mid 0},\qquad \frac{0\mid b}{0\mid d},\qquad \frac{a\mid b}{0\mid 0}
$$
is also zero.

**(c)**

Show that
$$
\frac{a\mid b}{s\times c\mid s\times d} = s \times \frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d}.
$$

**(d)**

Show that
$$
\frac{a\mid b}{c+x\mid d+y} = \frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d} + \frac{a\mid b}{x\mid y}.
$$

## 解析
**(a)** Take $s = 0$ in property (1). Since $0 \times a = 0$ and $0 \times b = 0$, the left-hand side becomes $\dfrac{0 \mid 0}{c \mid d}$ and the right-hand side becomes $0 \times \dfrac{a\mid b}{c\mid d} = 0$. Hence

$$\frac{0\mid 0}{c \mid d} = 0.$$

**(b)** Take the three symbols in turn.

Applying property (2) to $\dfrac{a \mid 0}{c \mid 0}$ (so that $b = 0$ and $d = 0$) interchanges $a$ and $d$:

$$\frac{a\mid 0}{c\mid 0} = \frac{0 \mid 0}{c \mid a} = 0$$

by part (a). Applying property (3) to $\dfrac{0\mid b}{0 \mid d}$ (so that $a=0$, $c=0$) interchanges $b$ and $c$:

$$\frac{0\mid b}{0\mid d} = \frac{0\mid 0}{b \mid d} = 0,$$

again by part (a). Finally, applying property (3) to $\dfrac{a\mid b}{0\mid 0}$ gives

$$\frac{a\mid b}{0\mid 0} = \frac{a\mid 0}{b\mid 0} = 0$$

by the first of the three results just proved.

**(c)** First combine (2) and (3) to swap the top and bottom rows. Property (2) gives

$$\frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d} = \frac{d\mid b}{c\mid a},$$

and applying property (3) to the right-hand side (interchanging its "$b$" and "$c$", namely $b$ and $c$) gives

$$\frac{d\mid b}{c\mid a} = \frac{d\mid c}{b\mid a}.$$

So for all $a,b,c,d$,

$$\frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d} = \frac{d\mid c}{b\mid a}. \qquad (\ast)$$

Now apply $(\ast)$ with $c$ and $d$ replaced by $s\times c$ and $s \times d$, then use property (1) on the resulting top row, then apply $(\ast)$ again:

$$\frac{a\mid b}{s\times c\mid s\times d} = \frac{s\times d\mid s\times c}{b\mid a} = s\times \frac{d\mid c}{b\mid a} = s \times \frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d}.$$

**(d)** Use $(\ast)$ in the same way, with property (4) supplying the additivity in the top row:

$$\frac{a\mid b}{c+x\mid d+y} = \frac{d+y \mid c+x}{b\mid a} = \frac{d\mid c}{b\mid a} + \frac{y\mid x}{b\mid a}.$$

By $(\ast)$ the first of these is $\dfrac{a\mid b}{c\mid d}$, and — reading $(\ast)$ in the other direction, with $c$ replaced by $x$ and $d$ by $y$ — the second is $\dfrac{a\mid b}{x\mid y}$. Hence

$$\frac{a\mid b}{c+x\mid d+y} = \frac{a\mid b}{c\mid d} + \frac{a\mid b}{x\mid y}.$$
