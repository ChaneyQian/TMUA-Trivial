## 题目
**For APPLICANTS IN**
$\left\{\begin{array}{l}\text{MATHEMATICS}\\\text{MATHEMATICS \& STATISTICS}\\\text{MATHEMATICS \& PHILOSOPHY}\\\text{MATHEMATICS \& COMPUTER SCIENCE}\end{array}\right\}$
**ONLY.**

*Computer Science applicants should turn to page 14.*

Let
$$
f(x) = x^{3} - 3x^{2} + 2x.
$$

**(a)**

On the axes below, sketch the curve $y = f(x)$ for the range $-1 < x < 3$, carefully labelling any turning points.

![[Image/06-Q3-fig1.jpg]]

**(b)**

The equation $f(x) = k$ has exactly one positive solution and exactly one negative solution. Find $k$.

**(c)**

For $x$ in the range $0 \leqslant x \leqslant 2$ the functions $g(x)$ and $h(x)$ are defined by
$$
\begin{aligned}
g(x) &= \int_{0}^{x} f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t \\
h(x) &= \int_{0}^{x} |f(t)|\,\mathrm{d}t.
\end{aligned}
$$
Find the value $X_{1}$ of $x$ in the range $0 \leqslant x \leqslant 2$ for which $g(x)$ is greatest.

Calculate $g(X_{1})$.

**(d)**

Find the value $X_{2}$ of $x$ in the range $0 \leqslant x \leqslant 2$ for which $h(x)$ is greatest. [You are not asked to calculate $h(X_{2})$.]
