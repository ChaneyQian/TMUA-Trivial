## 题目
**For ALL APPLICANTS.**

The equation
$$
|x| + |x - 1| = 0
$$
has
(a) no solutions,
(b) one solution,
(c) two solutions,
(d) three solutions.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
The absolute value of any real number is non-negative ($|y| \geqslant 0$). Therefore, the sum of two absolute values can be zero if and only if both terms are simultaneously zero.
This gives the system of equations:
$|x| = 0 \implies x = 0$
$|x - 1| = 0 \implies x - 1 = 0 \implies x = 1$
Since $x$ cannot be both $0$ and $1$ at the same time, the system has no solutions. The answer is (a).
