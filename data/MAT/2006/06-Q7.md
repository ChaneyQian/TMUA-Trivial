## 题目
**For APPLICANTS IN COMPUTER SCIENCE ONLY.**

The game of ABC involves creating "words" formed from the letters $\mathbf{A}$, $\mathbf{B}$, $\mathbf{C}$, according to certain rules. All forms of the game start with the word $\mathbf{AB}$.

In the simple form of the game, there is a single rule:

If the current word is of the form $\mathbf{A}x$, for some word $x$, then it may be replaced with the word $\mathbf{A}xx$. \hfill (1)

For example the word $\mathbf{ABC}$ could be replaced by $\mathbf{ABCBC}$.

**(a)**

Show how to produce the word $\mathbf{ABBBB}$.

**(b)**

Describe precisely all the words that can be produced in the simple form of the game. You may want to write $\mathbf{B}^{n}$ as a shorthand for the word formed from $n$ copies of $\mathbf{B}$; e.g. $\mathbf{AB}^{4} = \mathbf{ABBBB}$.

In the intermediate form of the game, there is a second rule:

If the current word is of the form $x\mathbf{BBB}$, for some word $x$, then it may be replaced by the word $\mathbf{C}x$. \hfill (2)

**(c)**

Show how to produce the word $\mathbf{CCABB}$.

**(d)**

Describe precisely all the words that can be produced in the intermediate form of the game. Explain your answer.

In the advanced form of the game, there is a third rule:

If the current word is of the form $x\mathbf{B}$, for some word $x$, then it may be replaced by the word $x$. \hfill (3)

**(e)**

Describe precisely all the words that can be produced in the advanced form of the game. Explain your answer.
