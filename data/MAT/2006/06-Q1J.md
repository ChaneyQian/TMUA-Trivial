## 题目
**For ALL APPLICANTS.**

The two circles with equations
$$
x^{2} + y^{2} = 1, \qquad (x - a)^{2} + (y - b)^{2} = r^{2}
$$
(where $r > 0$) do **not** intersect if
(a) $\sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}} + r < 1$, (and only this part),
(b) $\sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}} + 1 < r$, (and only this part),
(c) $\sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}} - r > 1$, (and only this part),
(d) all of the above.

D

## 解析
The two circles have centers $C_{1}(0, 0)$ and $C_{2}(a, b)$, and radii $r_{1} = 1$ and $r_{2} = r$. The distance between their centers is $d = \sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}}$.
Two circles do not intersect if one of the following mutually exclusive conditions holds:
1. They are completely outside each other. This happens when the distance between their centers is strictly greater than the sum of their radii:
   $$d > r_{1} + r_{2} \implies \sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}} > 1 + r \implies \sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}} - r > 1$$ (This is condition (c)).
2. The first circle is completely inside the second circle. This happens when the distance between their centers plus the radius of the first circle is strictly less than the radius of the second:
   $$d + r_{1} < r_{2} \implies \sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}} + 1 < r$$ (This is condition (b)).
3. The second circle is completely inside the first circle. This happens when the distance between their centers plus the radius of the second circle is strictly less than the radius of the first:
   $$d + r_{2} < r_{1} \implies \sqrt{a^{2} + b^{2}} + r < 1$$ (This is condition (a)).

Since any one of these three conditions is sufficient to guarantee that the circles do not intersect, all of the statements (a), (b), and (c) correctly identify a scenario where the circles do not intersect. Therefore, all of the above are valid conditions. The answer is (d).
