## 题目
[4 marks] In this question we write $\alpha = \log_{10} 2 , \beta = \log_{10} 3$ , and $\gamma = \log_{10} 7$ . Each of the following numbers is close to an integer. Which is the closest to an integer?

(a) $2 \beta ,$
(b) $5 \alpha + \beta ,$
(c) $\alpha + 2 \gamma ,$
(d) $2 \alpha + 5 \beta ,$
(e) $2 \alpha + \beta + \gamma .$

[Hint: $2 ^{2} \times 3 ^{5} = 9 . 72 \times 10 ^{2} ]$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
Each of these can be written as $M + \log_{10} N$ with M an integer and N a number between 9 and 10. That's close to M + 1, and we should pick the one with N closest to 10.

$$
\begin{array}{r} 2 \beta = \log_{10} 9, \qquad 5 \alpha + \beta = 1 + \log_{10} 9. 6, \qquad \alpha + 2 \gamma = 1 + \log_{10} 9. 8, \\ 2 \alpha + 5 \beta = 2 + \log_{10} 9. 72, \qquad 2 \alpha + \beta + \gamma = 1 + \log_{10} 8. 4 \end{array}
$$

The answer is (c).
