## 题目
[4 marks] The coefficient of $x^{12}$ in

$$
(1 - 2 x) ^{5} (1 + 4 x^{2}) ^{5} (1 + 2 x) ^{5}
$$

is

(a) $- 2 ^{13} \times 5 ,$
(b) $- 2 ^{12} \times 5 ,$
(c) $2 ^{12} \times 5 ,$
(d) $2 ^{13} \times 5 ,$
(e) $0.$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Multiply out $( 1 - 2 x ) ( 1 + 2 x )$ first to get $( 1 - 4 x^{2} )$ , then multiply that by $( 1 + 4 x^{2} )$ to get $( 1 - 16 x^{4} )$ , then look for the $\binom { 5 } { 3 }$ coefficient to get $( \ O_{3} ^{5} ) ( - 16 x^{4} ) ^{3} = - 10 \times 2 ^{12} x^{12}$ The answer is (a).
