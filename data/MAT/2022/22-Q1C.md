## 题目
The equation $x^{2}-4kx+y^{2}-4y+8=k^{3}-k$ is the equation of a circle

(a) for all real values of $k$ .

(b) if and only if either $-4 < k < -1$ or $k > 1$ .

(c) if and only if k > 1.

(d) if and only if $k < -1$ .

(e) if and only if either $-1 < k < 0$ or $k > 1$ .

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
Complete the square for x and for $y \ ( x - 2 k ) ^{2} - 4 k^{2} + ( y - 2 ) ^{2} - 4 + 8 = k^{3} - k$ This is the equation of a circle with centre (2k, 2) and $r^{2} = k^{3} + 4 k^{2} - k - 4$ provided that expression is positive. Factorising the cubic as $( k + 4 ) ( k - 1 ) ( k + 1 )$ reveals that this happens if and only if either $- 4 < k < - 1$ or if $k > 1$
