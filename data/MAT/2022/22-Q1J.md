## 题目
The real numbers $m$ and $c$ are such that the equation

$$
x^{2} + (m x + c) ^{2} = 1
$$

has a repeated root $x$ , and also the equation

$$
(x - 3) ^{2} + (m x + c - 1) ^{2} = 1
$$

has a repeated root x (which is not necessarily the same value of x as the root of the first equation). How many possibilities are there for the line $y = mx + c$ ?

(a) $0,$
(b) $1,$
(c) $2,$
(d) $3,$
(e) $4.$

### AI备注

## 答案
E

## 解析
Write $y = m x + c .$ A repeated root becomes a point $( x , y )$ where the line $y = m x + c$ is tangent to the curve $x^{2} + y^{2} = 1$ and also tangent to the curve $( x - 3 ) ^{2} + ( y - 1 ) ^{2} = 1$ . These are circles.

![[Image/22-Q1J-sol1.jpg]]

The answer is (e).

There are four lines that are tangent to both circles.
