## 题目
Which of the following are true for all real values of $x$? All arguments are in radians.

$$
\mathbf{I} \qquad \sin \left(\frac {\pi}{2} + x\right) = \cos \left(\frac {\pi}{2} - x\right)
$$

$$
\mathbf {I I} \qquad 2 + 2 \sin (x) - \cos^{2} (x) \geqslant 0
$$

$$
\mathrm{III} \qquad \sin \left(x + \frac {3 \pi}{2}\right) = \cos (\pi - x)
$$

$$
\mathbf {I V} \qquad \sin (x) \cos (x) \leqslant \frac {1}{4}
$$

(a) I and II,
(b) I and III,
(c) II and III,
(d) III and IV,
(e) II and IV.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
I is false when $x = 0$ . II can be rearranged as $( \sin ( x ) + 1 ) ^{2} \geqslant 0$ , using $\cos^{2} ( x ) + \sin^{2} ( x ) = 1$ and hence is true. III translates the $\sin(x)$ graph by $- \frac { 3 \pi } { 2 }$ along the $x$-axis, giving $- \cos ( x )$ , and $\cos ( \pi - x )$ translates the $\cos(x)$ graph by $\pi$ and reflects in the $y$-axis, giving $- \cos ( x )$ , and hence is true. The answer is (c).
