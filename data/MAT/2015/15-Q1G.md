## 解析
Consider integer multiples of $\pi$ . When $y = n \pi , \ \cos^{2} ( n \pi ) = 1$ . Hence this is satisfied when $x = m \pi$ , so all the points $(n\pi, m\pi)$ must exist on the graph. The answer is (c).
