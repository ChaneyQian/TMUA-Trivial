## 题目
Into how many regions is the plane divided when the following equations are graphed, not considering the axes?

$$
\begin{array}{l} {y = x^{3}} \\ {y = x^{4}} \\ {y = x^{5}} \end{array}
$$

(a) $6,$
(b) $7,$
(c) $8,$
(d) $9,$
(e) $10.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
Sketching the three equations suggests the answer 7, however it is necessary to note the behaviour of the three equations when $0 \leqslant x \leqslant 1$ . All three equations pass through 0, and $x^{5} > x^{4} > x^{3}$ when $x > 1$ . When $0 \leqslant x \leqslant 1$ it should be noted that $\bar { x } ^{3} \geqslant x^{4} \geqslant x^{5}$ and that all three equations pass through (1, 1). This behaviour produces two further regions (in addition to the 7 already discussed), and as such the answer is (d).
