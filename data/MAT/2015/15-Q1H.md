## 题目
How many distinct solutions does the following equation have?

$$
\log_{x^{2} + 2} (4 - 5 x^{2} - 6 x^{3}) = 2
$$

(a) None,
(b) $1,$
(c) $2,$
(d) $4,$
(e) Infinitely many.

## 答案
C

## 解析
Recall that $\log_{a} ( b ) = c$ describes the exponential relationship between $a , b ,$ and c - namely that $a^{c} = b$ . Hence, the equation becomes

$$
4 - 5 x^{2} - 6 x^{3} = (x^{2} + 2) ^{2}.
$$

Rearranging and cancelling terms gives

$$
x^{4} + 6 x^{3} + 9 x^{2} = 0.
$$

This factorises to

$$
x^{2} (x + 3) ^{2} = 0.
$$

This has two roots and so the answer is (c).
