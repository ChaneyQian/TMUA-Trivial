## 题目
Let

$$
f (x) = (x + a) ^{n}
$$

where $a$ is a real number and $n$ is a positive whole number, and $n \geqslant 2$. If $y = f ( x )$ and $y = f^{\prime} ( x )$ are plotted on the same axes, the number of intersections between $f(x)$ and $f'(x)$ will

(a) always be odd,
(b) always be even,
(c) depend on $a$ but not $n$,
(d) depend on $n$ but not $a$,
(e) depend on both $a$ and $n$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
If $f ( x )$ is an even function then $f^{\prime} ( x )$ will be an odd function, and vice versa. $f ( x )$ and $f^{\prime} ( x )$ will cross the x-axis at $( - a , 0 )$ and will have one further intersection when y and x are greater than 0. The answer is (b).
