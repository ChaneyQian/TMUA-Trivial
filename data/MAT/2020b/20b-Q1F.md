## 题目
The following are statements about a real number x.

$$
P: \quad \frac {x^{2} - 1}{x + 2} <   0, \qquad Q: \quad \frac {1 + x}{1 - x} > 0.
$$

Then it follows that

(a) P implies Q but Q does not imply P .

(b) Q implies P but P does not imply Q.

(c) P and Q are equivalent.

(d) If P is true then Q is false.

(e) If Q is true then P is false.

## 答案
B

## 解析
Statement P is equivalent to $^{\ast} - 1 < x < 1$ or $x < - 2 ^{\mathfrak{s} }$ . Statement $Q$ is equivalent to $^{66} - 1 <$ $x < 1 ^{\mathfrak{s} }$ . So Q implies P but $P$ does not imply $Q$ The answer is (b)
