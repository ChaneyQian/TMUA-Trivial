## 题目
A sequence $a_{n}$ is defined by $a_{0} = A$ and $a_{k} = ( a_{k-1} ) ^{2}$ for $k > 0$ , where $A > 1$ . The sequence $b_{n}$ is defined by $b_{n} = \log_{2} { a_{n} }$ . The sequence $b_{n}$ is

(a) constant

(b) an arithmetic progression

(c) a geometric progression

(d) all of the above

(e) none of the above

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
$a_{n} = A^{2^{n} }$ so $b_{n} = 2 ^{n} \log_{2}$ A which is a geometric progression, but not an arithmetic progression or a constant, because $\log_{2} A > 0$
