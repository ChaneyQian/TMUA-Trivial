## 解析
There are some solutions with x and y both bigger than 1, with x slightly larger than $y .$ So not (a) or (c). $\operatorname { I f } - 1 < x < 1$ then $x^{20} < 1$ , so $x^{20} - y^{20} < 1$ , since $y^{20}$ is positive. So no solutions in that range.
