## 题目
An equilateral triangle is drawn in the xy-plane. Two of its vertices are at (0, 0) and (1000, 0). The number of points (x, y) inside the triangle, where x and y are both whole numbers, equals

(a) $866{,}025,$
(b) $866{,}026,$
(c) $866{,}027,$
(d) $432{,}512,$
(e) $432{,}513.$

[Note that ${ \sqrt { 3 } } = 1 . 7321$ to 4 decimal places.]

## 答案
D

## 解析
The other vertex of the triangle is at $( 500 , 500 { \sqrt { 3 } } )$ and the points inside are either in $x < 500$ $x > 500$ , or down the middle $x = 500$ . Now $866 < 500 \sqrt { 3 } < 867$ so there are 866 points down the middle. The total is therefore even. Imagine the parallelogram made by adding a triangle with vertices at (1000, 0) and $( 500 , 500 { \sqrt { 3 } } )$ . There are approximately 866,000 points contained in these two triangles (866 rows of about 1000), so about 433,00 in each triangle. Only one option is both even and approximately 433,000.

The answer is (d)
