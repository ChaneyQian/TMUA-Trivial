## 题目
If x is a very large positive real number, then the product

$$
2 ^{x} \times 3 ^{-x} \times 4 ^{x} \times 5 ^{-x} \times \dots \times 18 ^{x} \times 19 ^{-x} \times 20 ^{x} \times 21 ^{-x}
$$

is

(a) very close to zero,

(b) slightly larger than 1,

(c) equal to 1,

(d) very close to 2,

(e) very large.

## 答案
A

## 解析
Pair the terms for $( 2 / 3 ) ^{x} ( 4 / 5 ) ^{x} \dots ( 20 / 21 ) ^{x}$ . Each of these terms gets very small for large $x ,$ so the product gets close to zero.

The answer is (a)
