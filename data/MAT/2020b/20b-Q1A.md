## 题目
The distance between opposite corners of a cube is 2. The surface area of the cube equals

(a) $4,$
(b) $6,$
(c) $8,$
(d) $12,$
(e) $24.$

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
If the side length of the cube is $x ,$ then the distance between opposite corners is $x { \sqrt { 3 } }$ . So here $x = 2 / \sqrt { 3 }$ and the surface area is $6 x^{2} = 8$ The answer is (c)
