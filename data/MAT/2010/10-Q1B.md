## 题目
The sum of the first 2n terms of

$$
1, 1, 2, \frac {1}{2}, 4, \frac {1}{4}, 8, \frac {1}{8}, 16, \frac {1}{16}, \ldots
$$

is

(a) $2 ^{n} + 1 - 2 ^{1-n} .$
(b) $2 ^{n} + 2 ^{-n} .$
(c) $2 ^{2n} - 2 ^{3-2n} ,$
(d) $\dfrac { 2 ^{n} - 2 ^{-n} } { 3 } .$

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
A

## 解析
Taking every other term in the sequence

$$
1, \quad 1, \quad 2, \quad \frac {1}{2}, \quad 4, \quad \frac {1}{4}, \quad 8, \quad \frac {1}{8}, \quad 16, \quad \frac {1}{16}, \dots
$$

from amongst the first 2n terms gives $1 , 2 , 4 , \dotsc , 2 ^{n-1}$ and the remaining terms are their reciprocals. So, recognising these as geometric series, we need to sum

$$
\begin{array}{l l} & \left(1 + 2 + 4 + \ldots + 2 ^{n-1}\right) + \left(1 + \frac {1}{2} + \frac {1}{4} + \dots + \frac {1}{2 ^{n-1}}\right) \\ = & \frac {1 (2 ^{n} - 1)}{(2 - 1)} + \frac {1 (2 ^{-n} - 1)}{(1 / 2 - 1)} \\ = & (2 ^{n} - 1) + (2 - 2 ^{1-n}) \\ = & 2 ^{n} + 1 - 2 ^{1-n}. \end{array}
$$

The answer is (a).
