## 题目
Which is the largest of the following four numbers?

(a) $\log_{2} 3,$

(b) $\log_{4} 8,$

(c) $\log_{3} 2,$

(d) $\log_{5} 10.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
A

## 解析
Note that $\log_{2} 3 > 1 > \log_{3} 2$ . Also note

$$
\log_{4} 8 = \frac {\log_{2} 8}{\log_{2} 4} = \frac {3}{2}.
$$

We are faced with decided whether or not $\log_{2} 3$ and $\mathrm { l o g } _{5}$ 10 are bigger than $3 / 2$ . Well,

$$
\log_{5} 10 <   3 / 2 \iff 10 <   5 ^{3/2} \iff 100 <   125
$$

and

$$
\log_{2} 3 > 3 / 2 \iff 3 > 2 ^{3/2} \iff 9 > 8.
$$

So we've shown $\log_{2} 3 > \log_{4} 8 > \log_{5} 10 > \log_{3} 2$ and in particular the answer is (a).
