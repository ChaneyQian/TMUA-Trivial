## 题目
The point on the circle

$$
x^{2} + y^{2} + 6 x + 8 y = 75,
$$

which is closest to the origin, is at what distance from the origin?

(a) $3,$
(b) $4,$
(c) $5,$
(d) $10.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
Completing the square we rewrite $x^{2} + y^{2} + 6 x + 8 y = 75$ as

$$
(x + 3) ^{2} + (y + 4) ^{2} = 100.
$$

Hence the circle has centre $( - 3 , - 4 )$ and radius 10. The radius from the centre, back through the origin, meets the circle at $( 3 , 4 )$ which is at a distance 5 from the origin. The answer is (c).
