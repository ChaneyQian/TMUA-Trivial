## 题目
The smallest positive integer n such that

$$
1 - 2 + 3 - 4 + 5 - 6 + \dots + (- 1) ^{n+1} n \geqslant 100,
$$

is

(a) $99,$
(b) $101,$
(c) $199,$
(d) $300.$

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
Summing the first few terms of the series

$$
1 - 2 + 3 - 4 + 5 - 6 + \dots + (- 1) ^{n+1} n,
$$

we see that we get the sequence

$$
1, - 1, 2, - 2, 3, - 3, 4, - 4, \ldots
$$

The first time that this equals or exceeds 100 is when $n = 199$ . The answer is (c).
