## 题目
The polynomial

$$
n^{2} x^{2n+3} - 25 n x^{n+1} + 150 x^{7}
$$

has $x^{2} - 1$ as a factor

(a) for no values of $n$;

(b) for $n = 10$ only;

(c) for $n = 15$ only;

(d) for $n = 10$ and $n = 15$ only.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
In order for the polynomial

$$
p (x) = n^{2} x^{2n+3} - 25 n x^{n+1} + 150 x^{7}
$$

to have $x^{2} - 1 = \left( x - 1 \right) \left( x + 1 \right)$ as a factor, the polynomial must be zero at both $x = 1$ and $x = - 1$ Now

$$
p (1) = n^{2} - 25 n + 150 = (n - 10) (n - 15)
$$

is zero when $n = 10$ or $n = 15$ . And

$$
p \left(- 1\right) = - n^{2} + \left(- 1\right) ^{n} 25 n - 150 = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} - \left(n - 10\right) \left(n - 15\right) & n \text {is even} \\ - \left(n + 10\right) \left(n + 15\right) & n \text {is odd} \end{array} \right.
$$

is zero when n = 10 or $n = - 15$ . Only $n = 10$ meets both requirements and so the answer is (b)
