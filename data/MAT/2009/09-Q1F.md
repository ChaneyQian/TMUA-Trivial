## 题目
The equation in x

$$
3 x^{4} - 16 x^{3} + 18 x^{2} + k = 0
$$

has four real solutions

(a) when $- 27 < k < 5 ;$

(b) when $5 < k < 27 ;$

(c) when $- 27 < k < - 5 ;$

(d) when $- 5 < k < 0 .$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
Setting

$$
y = 3 x^{4} - 16 x^{3} + 18 x^{2} + k
$$

we see

$$
y^{\prime} = 12 x^{3} - 48 x^{2} + 36 x = 12 x (x - 1) (x - 3)
$$

and so y has turning points at $x = 0 , 1 , 3$ which are respectively minimum, maximum, minimum because of the shape of a quartic. There will be four real solutions when both minima are below the x-axis and the maximum is above the x-axis. Now

$$
y (0) = k, \qquad y (1) = 5 + k, \qquad y (3) = 3 ^{2} (27 - 48 + 18) + k = k - 27.
$$

Hence we need

$$
k <   0, \qquad k > - 5, \qquad k <   27
$$

each to be true. The answer is (d).
