## 题目
The smallest value of

$$
I (a) = \int_{0} ^{1} (x^{2} - a) ^{2} \mathrm{d} x,
$$

as a varies, is

(a) $\dfrac { 3 } { 20 } ,$
(b) $\dfrac { 4 } { 45 } ,$
(c) $\dfrac { 7 } { 13 } ,$
(d) $1.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
We have

$$
\begin{array}{r c l} I (a) & = & \int_{0} ^{1} (x^{2} - a) ^{2} \mathrm{d} x \\ & = & \int_{0} ^{1} (x^{4} - 2 a x^{2} + a^{2}) \mathrm{d} x \\ & = & \frac {1}{5} - \frac {2 a}{3} + a^{2} \\ & = & \left(a - \frac {1}{3}\right) ^{2} + \left(\frac {1}{5} - \frac {1}{9}\right) \\ & = & \left(a - \frac {1}{3}\right) ^{2} + \frac {4}{45}. \end{array}
$$

So the smallest value of $I \left( a \right)$ is $4 / 45$ , achieved when $a = 1 / 3$ . The answer is (b).
