## 答案

## 解析
Thinking about the graph/periodicity of sine, we know that

$$
\sin (x + 2 \pi) = \sin x
$$

and so all the lines $y = x + 2n\pi$ where $n$ is an integer will be part of the graph of $\sin y = \sin x$ . But also we have

$$
\sin (\pi - x) = \sin x
$$

and so all the lines $y = \left( 2 n + 1 \right) \pi - x$ where $n$ is an integer will also be part of $\sin y = \sin x$ . The answer is (c).
