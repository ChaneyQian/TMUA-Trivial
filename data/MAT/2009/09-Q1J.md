## 题目
The number of pairs of positive integers x, y which solve the equation

$$
x^{3} + 6 x^{2} y + 12 x y^{2} + 8 y^{3} = 2 ^{30}
$$

is

(a) $0,$
(b) $2 ^{6} .$
(c) $2 ^{9} - 1,$
(d) $2 ^{10} + 2 .$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
Factorising the given equation we see it now reads as

$$
(x + 2 y) ^{3} = 2 ^{30}
$$

and so

$$
x + 2 y = 2 ^{10},
$$

where x and y are both positive integers. The solutions $( x , y )$ are then of the form

$$
\left(2 ^{10} - 2, 1\right), \quad \left(2 ^{10} - 4, 2\right), \dots \dots . \left(4, 2 ^{9} - 2\right), \quad \left(2, 2 ^{9} - 1\right)
$$

and so the answer is (c).
