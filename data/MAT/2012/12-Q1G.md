## 题目
There are positive real numbers x and $y$ which solve the equations

$$
2 x + k y = 4,
$$

$$
x + y = k
$$

for

(a) all values of $k ;$

(b) no values of $k ;$

(c) $k = 2$ only;

(d) only $k > -2.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
Using the second equation to eliminate x we see that

$$
2 (k - y) + k y = 4 \implies (k - 2) y = (4 - 2 k) = - 2 (k - 2).
$$

If $k \neq 2$ then we can divide by $k - 2$ and we see $y = - 2$ . So positive solutions aren't possible when $k \neq 2 .$ . When $k = 2$ then the equations are

$$
2 x + 2 y = 4, \qquad x + y = 2
$$

which are both clearly satisfied by $x = y = 1$ . The answer is (c).
