## 解析
If $- 1 < c < 0$ then the horizontal and vertical sides of the shaded triangle have length $1 + c$ and hence

$$
A (c) = \frac {1}{2} \left(1 + c\right) ^{2}.
$$

This is a parabolic curve with minimum at (-1, 0) and so the answer is (a).
