## 题目
If two chords QP and RP on a circle of radius 1 meet in an angle $\theta$ at P , for example as drawn in the diagram below,

![[Image/12-Q1J-fig1.jpg]]

then the largest possible area of the shaded region RPQ is

(a) $\theta \left( 1 + \cos \left( { \dfrac { \theta } { 2 } } \right) \right) ;$
(b) $\theta + \sin \theta ;$
(c) ${ \dfrac { \pi } { 2 } } \left( 1 - \cos \theta \right) ;$
(d) $\theta .$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
The area QP R is largest when $Q$ and R are symmetrically placed about $P ,$ for if (say) P Q were longer than $P R$ then Q could be moved so as to gain more area than would be lost by the corresponding move of R. This means that the angles $Q P O$ and $R P O$ are both $\theta / 2 ;$ the angle $QOR$ is $2\theta$ as the angle subtended by $QR$ at the centre $O$ is twice that subtended at the circumference $P$.

![[Image/12-Q1J-sol1.jpg]]

We then see that the area of P QR equals

$$
\underbrace {2 \times \frac {1}{2} \times 1 ^{2} \times \sin Q O P} _{\mathrm{triangles}} + \underbrace {\frac {1}{2} \times 1 ^{2} \times 2 \theta} _{\mathrm{sector}} = \sin (\pi - \theta) + \theta = \sin \theta + \theta
$$

and the answer is (b).
