## 题目
Let $N = 2 ^{k} \times 4 ^{m} \times 8 ^{n}$ where $k , m , n$ are positive whole numbers. Then N will definitely be a square number whenever

(a) $k$ is even;

(b) $k + n$ is odd;

(c) $k$ is odd but $m + n$ is even;

(d) $k + n$ is even.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
We can rewrite N as

$$
N = 2 ^{k} \times 4 ^{m} \times 8 ^{n} = 2 ^{k+2m+3n}.
$$

Now $2 ^{r}$ is a square when r is even and is not a square when r is odd. So we need

$$
k + 2 m + 3 n = k + n + 2 (m + n)
$$

to be even , which is equivalent to needing $k + n$ to be even. The answer is (d).
