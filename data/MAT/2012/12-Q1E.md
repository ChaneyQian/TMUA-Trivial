## 题目
Which one of the following equations could possibly have the graph given below?

(a) $y = \left( 3 - x \right) ^{2} ( 3 + x ) ^{2} ( 1 - x ) \mathrm { . }$

(b) $y = - x^{2} \left( x - 9 \right) \left( x^{2} - 3 \right)$

(c) $y = ( x - 6 ) \left( x - 2 \right) ^{2} \left( x + 2 \right) ^{2}$

(d) $y = \left( x^{2} - 1 \right) ^{2} ( 3 - x )$

![[Image/12-Q1E-fig1.jpg]]

## 答案
D

## 解析
The curve is that of a quintic with a negative leading coefficient which eliminates (c). Two of its roots are repeated which eliminates (b). Finally the smallest and middle of these roots are the repeated ones which eliminates (a) which has its smallest and largest roots repeated. The answer is (d).
