## 解析
When $x = 0 , y = 0 + 0 + 0 + \cdot \cdot \cdot = 0$ . When $x = 90 ^{\circ} , y = 1 + 1 + 1 + . . .$ diverges. Alternatively, note that this is a geometric series, with sum $\sin^{2} x / ( 1 - \sin^{2} x ) = \tan^{2} x$ The answer is (d)
