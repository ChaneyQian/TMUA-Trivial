## 题目
In the interval $0 \leqslant x < 360^{\circ}$, the equation

$$
\sin^{3} x + \cos^{2} x = 0
$$

has

(a) $0,$
(b) $1,$
(c) $2,$
(d) $3,$
(e) $4$

solutions.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
Use $\cos^{2} x + \sin^{2} x = 1$ . The equation becomes si $\mathrm { n } ^{3} x + 1 - \sin^{2} x = 0$ . Write s for sin x. The cubic $s^{3} - s^{2} + 1$ is −1 at $s = - 1$ , is 1 at $s = 0$ , and is 1 at $s = 1$ . The turning points are at $s = 0$ and $s = 2 / 3$ , and the value at $2 / 3$ is $23 / 27 > 0$ . So this cubic has one (negative) root.

There are two solutions for sin $x = s$ in the given range.

The answer is (c)
