## 题目
Let $a, b, c > 0$ . The equations

$$
\log_{a} b = c, \quad \log_{b} a = c + \frac {3}{2}, \quad \log_{c} a = b,
$$

(a) specify $a, b$ and $c$ uniquely.

(b) specify $c$ uniquely but have infinitely many solutions for $a$ and $b$ .

(c) specify c and a uniquely but have infinitely many solutions for b.

(d) specify $a$ and $b$ uniquely but have infinitely many solutions for $c$ .

(e) have no solutions for $a, b$ and $c$ .

## 解析
The equations are equivalent to;

$$
b = a^{c}, \qquad a = b^{c+3/2}, \qquad a = c^{b}
$$

Raise each side of the second equation to the power of $c .$ . Then $b = b^{c^{2} + 3 c / 2 }$ , so we must have $1 = c^{2} + 3 c / 2$ (since $b > 1$ for the logarithms to make sense). This has solutions $c = - 2$ or $c = - 1 / 2$ , but we know $c > 0$ so $c = 1 / 2$ . The remaining equations read $a = b^{2}$ and $a = 2 ^{-b}$ Sketch both curves;
