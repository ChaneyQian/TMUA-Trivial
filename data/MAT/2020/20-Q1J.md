## 答案
D

## 解析
For $r < 1 , A = 0$ and $B = 4 - \pi r^{2}$ . For $r > { \sqrt { 2 } } , A = \pi r^{2} - 4$ and $B = 0$ , which eliminates options (a) and (c). Now options (b) and (e) both have $A + B = 0$ at some value of $r ,$ which would require $A = 0$ and $B = 0 .$ , that is; no area inside the square that's not also inside the circle and vice versa. This is impossible, and the only remaining option is (d). The answer is (d)
