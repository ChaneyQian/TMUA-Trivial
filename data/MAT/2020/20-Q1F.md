## 题目
Which of the following expressions is equal to $\log_{10} ( 10 \times 9 \times 8 \times \cdot \cdot \cdot \times 2 \times 1 ) ?$

(a) $1 + 5 \log_{10} 2 + 4 \log_{10} 6 ,$

(b) $1 + 4 \log_{10} 2 + 2 \log_{10} 6 + \log_{10} 7,$

(c) $2 + 2 \log_{10} 2 + 4 \log_{10} 6 + \log_{10} 7 ,$

(d) $2 + 6 \log_{10} 2 + 4 \log_{10} 6 + \log_{10} 7,$

(e) $2 + 6 \log_{10} 2 + 4 \log_{10} 6 .$

## 备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
Factorise the numbers from 1 to 10; this gives

$$
\log_{10} (2 \times 5 \times 3 ^{2} \times 2 ^{3} \times 7 \times 2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 2 ^{2} \times 3 \times 2) = \log_{10} (2 ^{8} 3 ^{4} 5 ^{2} 7).
$$

Use $\log_{10} \left( 2 ^{2} 5 ^{2} \right) = 2$ . Use $2 ^{4} 3 ^{4} = 6 ^{4}$ . This gives $2+\log_{10} (2^{2}6^{4}7) = 2+2\log_{10} 2+4\log_{10} 6+\log_{10} 7$. The answer is (c)
