## 题目
A square has centre (3, 4) and one corner at (1, 5). Another corner is at

(a) $(1, 3),$
(b) $(5, 5),$
(c) $(4, 2),$
(d) $(2, 2),$
(e) $(5, 2).$

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
The centre is at $( 3 , 4 )$ and one corner is at (1, 5), which is a displacement of $( - 2 , 1 )$ . There's another corner opposite, at $( 3 , 4 ) - ( - 2 , 1 ) = ( 5 , 3 )$ , but this is not one of the options. To find the other corners, we need to find a vector with equal magnitude, but at right-angles to (−2, 1). Rotating by $90 ^{\circ}$ gives (1, 2). The other corners are at $( 3 , 4 ) \pm ( 1 , 2 )$ , which includes (2, 2) The answer is (d)
