## 题目
The sum

$$
1 - 4 + 9 - 16 + \dots + 99 ^{2} - 100 ^{2}
$$

equals

(a) $-101$
(b) $-1000$
(c) $-1111$
(d) $-4545$
(e) $-5050.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
E

## 解析
Pair the terms to get $- 3 - 7 -- 199$ . This is the sum of an arithmetic progression and is equal to $( - 3 - 199 ) \times 50 / 2 = - 5050$ The answer is (e)
