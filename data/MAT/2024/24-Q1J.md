## 题目
[4 marks] The graph of $y = \sqrt{x}$ is first stretched parallel to the $x$-axis by a factor of $k$, then stretched parallel to the $y$-axis by a factor of $l$. The resulting graph passes through $(4,\,6)$. Find the relationship between $k$ and $l$.

(a) $k = l^2$
(b) $9k = l^2$
(c) $k^2 = 9l$
(d) $l = 9k^2$
(e) $kl = 9$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Stretching parallel to the $x$-axis by a factor of $k$ replaces $x$ by $x/k$; stretching parallel to the $y$-axis by a factor of $l$ multiplies the whole expression by $l$. The resulting curve is $y=l\sqrt{x/k}$.

$$6=l\sqrt{\frac{4}{k}}=\frac{2l}{\sqrt{k}}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad \sqrt{k}=\frac{l}{3}\qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad 9k=l^2.$$

The answer is (b).
