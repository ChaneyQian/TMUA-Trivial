## 题目
[4 marks] Given that $x^3y^{-2} = 2^6$ and $x^2y^{-4} = 2^7$, find the value of $xy$.

(a) $2^{-1/8}$
(b) $2^{1/8}$
(c) $2^{3/8}$
(d) $2^{5/8}$
(e) $2^{7/8}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Take logarithms to base $2$ and write $a=\log_2 x$, $b=\log_2 y$.

$$3a-2b=6,\qquad 2a-4b=7.$$

Doubling the first equation and subtracting the second gives $4a=5$, so $a=\tfrac54$ and then $b=-\tfrac98$.

$$\log_2(xy)=a+b=\tfrac54-\tfrac98=\tfrac18.$$

The answer is (b).
