## 题目
[4 marks] A triangle has side lengths $a = 5$, $b = 6$, $c = 7$. Find the value of $$\frac{\cos A}{2} + \cos B + \frac{\cos C}{2}.$$

(a) $\dfrac{1}{2}$
(b) $1$
(c) $\dfrac{3}{2}$
(d) $2$
(e) $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
The projection formulae $c=a\cos B+b\cos A$ and $a=b\cos C+c\cos B$ add to give

$$a+c=b\left(\cos A+\cos C\right)+(a+c)\cos B.$$

Here $a+c=12=2b$, so dividing by $2b$ leaves $\dfrac{\cos A}{2}+\cos B+\dfrac{\cos C}{2}=1$: the value depends only on the side lengths being in arithmetic progression.

Directly, the cosine rule gives $\cos A=\dfrac57$, $\cos B=\dfrac{19}{35}$, $\cos C=\dfrac15$, and $\dfrac{5}{14}+\dfrac{19}{35}+\dfrac{1}{10}=\dfrac{25+38+7}{70}=1$.

The answer is (b).
