## 题目
[4 marks] Let $y = x^3 - 3x$. Define $L(y)$ as the largest $x$ satisfying $y = x^3 - 3x$, and $S(y)$ as the smallest such $x$. Find the set of values of $y$ for which $S(y) \ne L(y)$.

(a) $(-\infty,\,-2) \cup (2,\,+\infty)$
(b) $[-2,\,2]$
(c) $(-\infty,\,-2\,] \cup [\,2,\,+\infty)$
(d) $(-2,\,2)$
(e) $\mathbb{R}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
$S(y)\neq L(y)$ says exactly that $x^3-3x=y$ has more than one solution. The cubic has a local maximum $2$ at $x=-1$ and a local minimum $-2$ at $x=1$, so a horizontal line meets it more than once precisely when $-2\le y\le 2$.

```tikz
\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=1]
  \draw[->] (-2.7,0) -- (2.7,0) node[right] {$x$};
  \draw[->] (0,-3.5) -- (0,3.5) node[above] {$y$};
  \draw[dashed] (-2.6,2) -- (2.6,2) node[right] {\small $y=2$};
  \draw[dashed] (-2.6,-2) -- (2.6,-2) node[right] {\small $y=-2$};
  \draw[very thick,domain=-2.1:2.1,samples=140,smooth] plot (\x,{\x*\x*\x-3*\x});
  \fill (-1,2) circle (2pt);
  \fill (2,2) circle (2pt);
  \fill (1,-2) circle (2pt);
  \fill (-2,-2) circle (2pt);
  \node[above left] at (-1,2) {\small $-1$};
  \node[above right] at (2,2) {\small $2$};
  \node[below right] at (1,-2) {\small $1$};
  \node[below left] at (-2,-2) {\small $-2$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

The endpoints do belong to the set, because a repeated root still leaves two *distinct* values of $x$:

$$x^3-3x-2=(x+1)^2(x-2),\qquad x^3-3x+2=(x-1)^2(x+2),$$

so $S(2)=-1\neq 2=L(2)$ and $S(-2)=-2\neq 1=L(-2)$. The set is the closed interval $[-2,2]$.

The answer is (b).
