## 题目
[4 marks] $xy = 1$, $yz = 3$, $zx = 7$. Find $x + y + z$.

(a) $\pm\dfrac{11}{\sqrt{21}}$
(b) $\pm\dfrac{23}{\sqrt{21}}$
(c) $\pm\dfrac{31}{\sqrt{21}}$
(d) $\pm\dfrac{41}{\sqrt{21}}$
(e) $\pm\dfrac{51}{\sqrt{21}}$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Multiply all three equations together to get $xyz$ up to sign.

$$(xyz)^2=(xy)(yz)(zx)=1\times3\times7=21,\qquad\text{so}\quad xyz=\pm\sqrt{21}.$$

Dividing by each equation in turn, and keeping the same sign throughout,

$$x=\frac{xyz}{yz}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{21}}{3},\qquad y=\frac{xyz}{zx}=\pm\frac{\sqrt{21}}{7},\qquad z=\frac{xyz}{xy}=\pm\sqrt{21}.$$

$$x+y+z=\pm\sqrt{21}\left(\frac13+\frac17+1\right)=\pm\sqrt{21}\cdot\frac{31}{21}=\pm\frac{31}{\sqrt{21}}.$$

The answer is (c).
