## 答案
E

## 解析
Since $\tan(x+90^\circ)=-\cot x$, the equation is $-\cot x=e^x$, that is

$$\tan x=-e^{-x}.$$

On each interval $\left(k\pi-\tfrac\pi2,\ k\pi+\tfrac\pi2\right)$ the left-hand side increases continuously from $-\infty$ to $+\infty$, while the right-hand side is a small negative number there; so there is exactly one root in each such interval, just below $x=k\pi$. Taking $k=1,2,3,\dots$ gives infinitely many roots with $x>0$.
