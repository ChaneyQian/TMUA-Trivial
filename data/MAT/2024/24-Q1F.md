## 题目
[4 marks] Let $y = x^2$, and let $A(2,\,4)$ be a point on the curve. The tangent at $A$ meets the $x$-axis at $B$, and the normal at $A$ meets the $x$-axis at $C$. Find the area of triangle $ABC$.

(a) $17$
(b) $34$
(c) $51$
(d) $68$
(e) $85$

## 答案

## 解析
Here $\frac{dy}{dx}=2x=4$ at $A$, so the tangent is $y=4x-4$, meeting the $x$-axis at $B(1,0)$, and the normal is $y=4-\tfrac14(x-2)$, meeting the $x$-axis at $C(18,0)$.

Taking $BC$ as the base, the height is the $y$-coordinate of $A$, so

$$[ABC]=\tfrac12\times(18-1)\times 4=34.$$
