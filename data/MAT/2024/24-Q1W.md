## 题目
[4 marks] A square $R$ of side length 1 is centred at the origin with its sides parallel to the axis. Another square $S$ of length $r$, where $r > 0$, is centred at the origin, and its sides at 45 degrees to the axis. $A(r)$ is the area within $R$ but not in $S$. What is the graph of $A(r)$.

(a) $A(r) = \begin{cases} 1 - r^2, & r \le \dfrac{\sqrt2}{2} \\ r^2 - 2\sqrt2\,r + 2, & \dfrac{\sqrt2}{2} < r < \sqrt2 \end{cases}$
(b) $A(r) = \begin{cases} 1 - \dfrac{r^2}{2}, & r \le \sqrt2 \\ 0, & r > \sqrt2 \end{cases}$
(c) $A(r) = \begin{cases} 1 - r^2, & r \le \sqrt2 \\ 0, & r > \sqrt2 \end{cases}$
(d) $A(r) = \begin{cases} 1 - r^2, & r \le \dfrac{\sqrt2}{2} \\ 2 - r^2, & \dfrac{\sqrt2}{2} < r < \sqrt2 \end{cases}$
(e) $A(r) = \begin{cases} 1 - r^2, & r \le \dfrac{\sqrt2}{2} \\ r^2 - 2r + 1, & \dfrac{\sqrt2}{2} < r < \sqrt2 \end{cases}$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Let $d$ be the distance from the origin to a vertex of $S$. A square of side $r$ has half-diagonal $d=\dfrac{r}{\sqrt2}$, so $S$ is the region $|x|+|y|\le d$, of area $2d^2=r^2$, while $R=\left[-\tfrac12,\tfrac12\right]^2$.

If $d\le\tfrac12$, that is $r\le\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$, then $S$ lies entirely inside $R$ and $A(r)=1-r^2$.

If $\tfrac12<d<1$, that is $\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}<r<\sqrt2$, the four vertices of $S$ poke out through the four sides of $R$, and what is left of $R$ is four right-angled isosceles triangles at the corners of $R$, each with legs $1-d$.

```tikz
  \fill[black!15] (0.5,-0.5) -- (0.5,-0.3) -- (0.3,-0.5) -- cycle;

  \node[below right] at (0.8,0) {$\frac{r}{\sqrt2}$};

```

$$A(r)=4\cdot\tfrac12(1-d)^2=2\left(1-\frac{r}{\sqrt2}\right)^2=r^2-2\sqrt2\,r+2.$$

The two branches agree at $r=\tfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$, both giving $\tfrac12$, and $A(\sqrt2)=0$, which is right because $|x|+|y|\le1$ just swallows the corners of $R$.

The answer is (a).
