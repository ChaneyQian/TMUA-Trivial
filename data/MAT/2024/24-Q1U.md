## 题目
[4 marks] For $x \in [0^\circ,\,360^\circ]$, how many solutions does the equation $$4^{\sin^2 x} + 4^{1+\cos^2 x} = 8\sqrt{2}$$ have?

(a) $0$
(b) $2$
(c) $4$
(d) $6$
(e) $8$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Since $\cos^2 x = 1-\sin^2 x$, the second term is $4^{2-\sin^2 x}$, so with $t=4^{\sin^2 x}$ the equation reads

$$t+\frac{16}{t}=8\sqrt2 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad t^2-8\sqrt2\,t+16=0 \qquad\Longrightarrow\qquad t=4\sqrt2\pm4.$$

But $\sin^2x\in[0,1]$ forces $t\in[1,4]$, and only $t=4\sqrt2-4\approx1.657$ lies there. Hence $\sin^2 x=\log_4\!\left(4\sqrt2-4\right)$, a single constant strictly between $0$ and $1$, and $\sin^2x=c$ with $0<c<1$ has four solutions in $[0^\circ,360^\circ]$ (two from $\sin x=+\sqrt{c}$, two from $\sin x=-\sqrt{c}$).

The answer is (c).
