## 题目
[4 marks] Let $f(x) = 3x^2 - 2x$, $x_n = \dfrac{n}{N}$, and $$S = f'(x_0) + f'(x_1) + \cdots + f'(x_{N-1}),$$ where $N$ is a positive integer. Find a closed-form expression for $S$.

(a) $N - 1$
(b) $N - 2$
(c) $N - 3$
(d) $N$
(e) $2N - 3$

## 答案
C

## 解析
$f'(x)=6x-2$, so the sum is an arithmetic progression in $n$.

$$S=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\left(\frac{6n}{N}-2\right)=\frac{6}{N}\cdot\frac{(N-1)N}{2}-2N=3(N-1)-2N=N-3.$$

The answer is (c).
