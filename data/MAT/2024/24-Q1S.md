## 题目
[4 marks] Two points are at $(0,\,0)$ and $(1,\,0)$. Find the area of the set of all points within distance 1 of **at least one** of these two points, restricted to $0 < x < 1$.

(a) $\dfrac{\pi}{3} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
(b) $\dfrac{2\pi}{3} + \sqrt{3}$
(c) $\pi + \sqrt{3}$
(d) $\dfrac{\pi}{2} + \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
(e) $\dfrac{3\pi}{4} + \sqrt{3}$

## 答案

## 解析
The set is the union of two unit discs. Inside the strip the taller of the two cross-sections is the one whose centre is nearer, so the region is bounded by the circle about $(0,0)$ for $x<\frac12$ and by the circle about $(1,0)$ for $x>\frac12$; the two halves are reflections of one another in $x=\frac12$.

The left half is the half-disc $x>0$ of the first circle with the segment beyond the chord $x=\frac12$ removed. That chord subtends $\frac{2\pi}{3}$, so the segment has area $\frac12\left(\frac{2\pi}{3}-\sin\frac{2\pi}{3}\right)=\frac{\pi}{3}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$, and

$$A=2\left(\frac{\pi}{2}-\left(\frac{\pi}{3}-\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)\right)=\frac{\pi}{3}+\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}.$$

The answer is (a).
