## 题目
[4 marks] Given that $f(t) + e(t)\,f(-t) = o(t)$, where $e$ is an even function and $o$ is an odd function, find $f(t)$.

(a) $f(t) = \dfrac{o(t)}{1 - e(t)}$
(b) $f(t) = \dfrac{o(t)}{1 + e(t)}$
(c) $f(t) = \dfrac{o(t)}{e(t) - 1}$
(d) $f(t) = \dfrac{o(t) - e(t)\,o(t)}{1 + e(t)}$
(e) $f(t) = \dfrac{o(t) - e(t)\,o(t)}{1 - e(t)}$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Replace $t$ by $-t$ and use $e(-t)=e(t)$, $o(-t)=-o(t)$:

$$f(-t)+e(t)f(t)=-o(t).$$

Multiplying this by $e(t)$ and subtracting it from the original equation removes $f(-t)$:

$$f(t)-e(t)^2f(t)=o(t)+e(t)o(t),$$

so $f(t)\left(1-e(t)\right)\left(1+e(t)\right)=o(t)\left(1+e(t)\right)$, and cancelling $1+e(t)$ gives $f(t)=\dfrac{o(t)}{1-e(t)}$.

The answer is (a).
