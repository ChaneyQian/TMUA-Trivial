## 答案
C

## 解析
As $x$ runs over the reals, $(3x-2)^9$ takes every real value, so the sine takes every value in $[-1,1]$ (the same is true if the ninth power is applied to the sine instead). Therefore

$$3\sin\big((3x-2)^9\big)-2\in[-5,1],$$

and squaring, the largest value is $(-5)^2=25$.

The answer is (c).
