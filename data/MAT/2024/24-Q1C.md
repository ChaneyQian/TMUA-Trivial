## 题目
[4 marks] A sequence is defined by $A_{n+1} = 4\sqrt{A_n}$ with $A_0 = 4$. As $n \to \infty$, what does $A_n$ converge to?

(a) $2$
(b) $4$
(c) $8$
(d) $16$
(e) $32$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Take logarithms to base $2$: with $B_n=\log_2 A_n$ the recurrence becomes $B_{n+1}=2+\tfrac12B_n$ and $B_0=2$. Hence

$$B_n-4=\frac{B_0-4}{2^{\,n}}=-2^{\,1-n}\longrightarrow 0,$$

so $A_n\to 2^4$. (Equivalently, a limit $L$ must satisfy $L=4\sqrt L$, so $L=16$, and $A_0=4,\ A_1=8,\ A_2=4\sqrt8,\dots$ increases towards it.)

The answer is (d).
