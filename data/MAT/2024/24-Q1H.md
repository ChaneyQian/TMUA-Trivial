## 题目
[4 marks] Let $T = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2 + z^2}$, $P = 4(x + y + z)$, and $S = 2(xy + yz + xz)$. Express $S$ in terms of $T$ and $P$.

(a) $S = \dfrac{P^2}{4} - T^2$
(b) $S = \dfrac{P^2}{8} - T^2$
(c) $S = \dfrac{P^2}{16} - T^2$
(d) $S = T^2 - \dfrac{P^2}{16}$
(e) $S = T^2 - \dfrac{P^2}{8}$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Expand the square of the sum:

$$(x+y+z)^2=x^2+y^2+z^2+2(xy+yz+zx)=T^2+S.$$

Since $x+y+z=P/4$, the left-hand side is $P^2/16$, so $S=\dfrac{P^2}{16}-T^2$.

The answer is (c).
