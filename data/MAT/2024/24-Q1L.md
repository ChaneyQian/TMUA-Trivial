## 题目
[4 marks] A bulb company has probability $\dfrac{1}{i}$ to produce the $i$th bulb working. Ask the expression for probability that $n-1$ bulbs works well among $n$ bulbs produced.

(a) $\dfrac{1}{n!}$
(b) $\dfrac{1}{2n!}$
(c) $\dfrac{1}{2(n-1)!}$
(d) $\dfrac{1}{(n-1)!}$
(e) $\dfrac{1}{n(n-1)!}$

## 解析
Exactly $n-1$ working bulbs means exactly one bulb fails; sum over which one, say the $j$th, using $\prod_{i\neq j}\frac1i=\frac{j}{n!}$.

$$\sum_{j=1}^{n}\left(1-\frac1j\right)\prod_{i\neq j}\frac1i=\sum_{j=1}^{n}\frac{j-1}{j}\cdot\frac{j}{n!}=\frac{1}{n!}\sum_{j=1}^{n}(j-1)=\frac{n(n-1)}{2\,n!}=\frac{1}{2(n-2)!}.$$

**This is not one of the five options.** Take $n=4$: the probability above is $\frac14$, while the options give $\frac1{24}$, $\frac1{48}$, $\frac1{12}$, $\frac16$, $\frac1{24}$ respectively, and none of them is $\frac14$. (Options (a), (c), (e) do agree with the correct value at $n=2$, and (d) at $n=3$, but those are coincidences of small $n$.)
