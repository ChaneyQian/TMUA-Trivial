## 题目
[4 marks] Define $$I(k) = \left(\int_k^{k+1} x\,\mathrm{d}x\right)^2 - \int_k^{k+1} x^2\,\mathrm{d}x.$$ Which of the following best describes $I(k)$ as a function of $k$?

(a) A cubic polynomial in $k$
(b) A quadratic polynomial in $k$
(c) A constant
(d) A strictly increasing function of $k$
(e) A strictly decreasing function of $k$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Work out the two pieces and watch the $k^2$ and $k$ terms cancel.

$$\int_k^{k+1}x\,\mathrm{d}x=\frac{(k+1)^2-k^2}{2}=k+\frac12,\qquad \int_k^{k+1}x^2\,\mathrm{d}x=\frac{(k+1)^3-k^3}{3}=k^2+k+\frac13.$$

Hence

$$I(k)=\left(k+\frac12\right)^2-\left(k^2+k+\frac13\right)=\frac14-\frac13=-\frac{1}{12}.$$

The answer is (c).
