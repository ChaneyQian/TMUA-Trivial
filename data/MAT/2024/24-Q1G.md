## 题目
[4 marks] How many real solutions does $4\sin^2 x - \cos^4 x = 0$ have for $0 \le x \le 2\pi$?

(a) $0$
(b) $2$
(c) $4$
(d) $6$
(e) $8$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Write $c=\cos^2x$, so $\sin^2x=1-c$ and the equation becomes

$$4(1-c)=c^2,\qquad\text{i.e.}\qquad c^2+4c-4=0,\qquad c=-2\pm2\sqrt2.$$

Only $c=2\sqrt2-2\approx0.828$ lies in $[0,1]$, so $\cos x=\pm\sqrt{2\sqrt2-2}$, a value strictly between $0$ and $1$ in modulus. Each of the two signs gives two solutions in $0\le x\le 2\pi$.

The answer is (c).
