## 题目
[4 marks] $$C_1 : x^2 + y^2 = 3,$$
$$C_2 : r_2 = 2\sqrt{3}$$
$$C_1 \cap C_2 = P$$
$$C_1 \angle O_1PO_2 = 60^\circ$$

The center of $C_1$ is $O_1$, and the center of $C_2$ is $O_2$. $O_2$ is on the $x$-axis. The equation of $C_2$:

(a) $(x-1)^2 + y^2 = 12$
(b) $(x-2)^2 + y^2 = 12$
(c) $(x-3)^2 + y^2 = 12$
(d) $(x-4)^2 + y^2 = 12$
(e) $(x-5)^2 + y^2 = 12$

## 答案
C

## 解析
$C_1$ has centre $O_1=(0,0)$ and radius $\sqrt{3}$, and $C_2$ has radius $2\sqrt{3}$, so the right-hand side of the answer is $12$ in every case. All that is unknown is $O_1O_2$, and the cosine rule in triangle $O_1PO_2$ gives it.

$$O_1O_2^{\,2}=3+12-2\cdot\sqrt{3}\cdot 2\sqrt{3}\cos 60^\circ=15-6=9,$$

so $O_1O_2=3$. As $O_2$ lies on the $x$-axis (to the right, by the options), $O_2=(3,0)$.
