[4 marks] How many 7-digit palindromic numbers contain the consecutive digit sequence “101”?

(a) $36$
(b) $72$
(c) $108$
(d) $144$
(e) $216$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Write the palindrome as $abcdcba$ with $a\neq0$. Because "101" is itself a palindrome, an occurrence in the left half is mirrored by one in the right half, so only three distinct conditions arise:

$$A:\ (a,b,c)=(1,0,1),\qquad B:\ (b,c,d)=(1,0,1),\qquad C:\ (c,d,c)=(1,0,1).$$

$A$ leaves $d$ free, giving $10$ numbers; $B$ leaves $a\in\{1,\dots,9\}$ free, giving $9$; and $C$ just forces $c=1$, $d=0$, leaving $a$ and $b$ free, giving $9\times10=90$. Conditions $A$ and $B$ clash over $b$, and $B$ and $C$ clash over $c$, so the only overlap is $A\cap C$, the single number $1010101$.

$$10+9+90-1=108.$$

The answer is (c).
