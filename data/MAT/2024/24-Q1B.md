## 题目
[4 marks] $\log(x+y) = \log x + \log y$. Which of the following conditions can satisfy this equation?

(a) $x = y$
(b) $x > 1$, $y > 1$
(c) $x < 1$, $y < 1$
(d) $xy = 1$
(e) None of the above

## 答案
E

## 解析
Both logarithms on the right need $x>0$ and $y>0$, and then the equation says $x+y=xy$, that is

$$\frac1x+\frac1y=1.$$

None of the four conditions forces this. Not (a): $x=y=1$ gives $\log 2\neq 0$. Not (b): $x=y=3$ gives $\log 6\neq\log 9$. Not (c): if $0<x<1$ and $0<y<1$ then $xy<x$ and $xy<y$, so $xy<x+y$ and the equation is impossible. Not (d): $xy=1$ would also force $x+y=1$, but then $x,y$ are roots of $t^2-t+1=0$, which has none.

(Condition (b) is forced by the equation, since $\frac1x<1$ and $\frac1y<1$, but it is not enough to guarantee it.)

The answer is (e).
