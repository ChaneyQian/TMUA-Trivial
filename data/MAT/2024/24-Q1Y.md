## 题目
[4 marks] It is given that $$(9a^3 - 1)^3 + (9a^4 - 3a)^3 = (3a^2)^6 - 1.$$ This corresponds to $x^3 + y^3 = z^3 - 1$ for $x, y, z$ depending on $a$. Given that there is exactly one integer value of $z$ with $700 < z < 800$, find $z$.

(a) $725$
(b) $729$
(c) $777$
(d) $798$
(e) $799$

## 答案
B

## 解析
$(3a^2)^6=729a^{12}=\left(9a^4\right)^3$, so $z=9a^4=\left(3a^2\right)^2$ and $z$ must be a perfect square. Of the five options only $729=27^2$ is one.

It really occurs: $3a^2=27$ gives $a=3$, and then $x=9a^3-1=242$, $y=9a^4-3a=720$, with

$$242^3+720^3=387\,420\,488=729^3-1.$$

The answer is (b).
