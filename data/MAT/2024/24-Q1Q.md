## 题目
[4 marks] Let $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ satisfy the differential equation $y''x + y' = x$. Given that $y(1) = 0$, find $$\int_0^1 x\,y\,\mathrm{d}x.$$

(a) $-\dfrac{1}{8}$
(b) $-\dfrac{1}{16}$
(c) $0$
(d) $\dfrac{1}{16}$
(e) $\dfrac{1}{8}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Substituting $y=ax^2+bx+c$ into $y''x+y'=x$ gives $2ax+(2ax+b)=x$, so $4a=1$ and $b=0$. Then $y(1)=0$ forces $c=-\frac14$, so

$$y=\frac{x^2-1}{4}.$$

Therefore

$$\int_0^1 xy\,\mathrm{d}x=\frac14\int_0^1\left(x^3-x\right)\mathrm{d}x=\frac14\left(\frac14-\frac12\right)=-\frac{1}{16}.$$

The answer is (b).
