## 题目
A sequence $\left( a_{n} \right)$ has first term $a_{1} = 1$ , and subsequent terms defined by $a_{n+1} = l a_{n}$ for $n \geqslant 1$ . What is the product of the first 15 terms of the sequence?

(a) $l^{14} ,$
(b) $15 + l^{14} ,$
(c) ${ \dfrac { 1 - l^{15} } { 1 - l } } ,$
(d) $l^{105}$
(e) $15 + l^{105} .$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
Considering the sequence, $a_{2} = l , a_{3} = l^{2} , a_{4} = l^{3}$ , each additional term multiplies the previous term by l. The product of the first 15 terms is equal to $l^{1+2+\ldots+14} = l^{\frac{14*15} { 2 } } = l^{105}$ . The answer is (d).
