## 解析
When $x = 0 , y = 1 - 1 = 0$ , so we can rule out (d) and (e). To work out the number of x-axis intersection points, consider $( x - 1 ) ^{2} = \cos ( \pi x )$ . The shape of these graphs means they cannot intersect 6 times (eliminating (b)). The answer cannot be (c), because we know there is a crossing point $x = 2$ , but that y is positive when $x = 1$ . So the answer is (a).
