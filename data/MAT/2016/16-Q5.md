## 题目
For **ALL APPLICANTS**.

This question concerns the sum $s_{n}$ defined by

$$
s_{n} = 2 + 8 + 24 + \dots + n 2 ^{n}.
$$

(i) Let $f ( n ) = ( A n + B ) 2 ^{n} + C$ for constants , and yet to be determined, and suppose $s_{n} = f ( n )$ for all $n \geqslant 1$ . By setting $n = 1 , 2 , 3$ find three equations that must be satisfied by , and .

(ii) Solve the equations from part (i) to obtain values for ,  and $C .$

(iii) Using these values, show that if $s_{k} = f ( k )$ for some $\geqslant 1$ then $s_{k+1} = f ( k + 1 )$

You may now assume that $f ( n ) = s_{n}$ for all $n \geqslant 1$

(iv) Find simplified expressions for the following sums:

$$
t_{n} = n + 2 (n - 1) + 4 (n - 2) + 8 (n - 3) + \dots + 2 ^{n-1} 1,
$$

$$
u_{n} = \frac {1}{2} + \frac {2}{4} + \frac {3}{8} + \dots + \frac {n}{2 ^{n}}.
$$

(v) Find the sum

$$
\sum_{k=1} ^{n} s_{k}.
$$

## 解析
5. (i) [3 marks] We have

$$
\begin{array}{c} {s_{1} = 2 (A + B) + C = 2} \\ {s_{2} = 4 (2 A + B) + C = 10} \\ {s_{3} = 8 (3 A + B) + C = 34} \end{array}
$$

(ii) [3 marks] Subtracting the first equation from the other two gives

$$
\begin{array}{c} 6 A + 2 B = 8 \\ 22 A + 6 B = 32, \end{array}
$$

whence $4 A = 8 , \mathrm { ~ s o ~ } A = 2 , B = - 2 , C = 2 { \mathrm { ~ a n d ~ } } f ( n ) = ( n - 1 ) 2 ^{n+1} + 2 .$

(iii) [2 marks] We have

$$
\begin{array}{r l} s_{k+1} & = f (k) + (k + 1) 2 ^{k+1} \\ & = (k - 1) 2 ^{k+1} + 2 + (k + 1) 2 ^{k+1} \\ & = k 2 ^{k+2} + 2 = f (k + 1) \end{array}
$$

as required.

(iv) [4 marks] We have

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & t_{n} = (n + 2 n + 4 n + \dots + 2 ^{n}. n) - (2 + 8 + 24 + \ldots + 2 ^{n}. n) \\ & \quad = n (2 ^{n+1} - 1) - f (n) \\ & \quad = n (2 ^{n+1} - 1) - (n - 1) 2 ^{n+1} - 2 \\ & \quad = 2 ^{n+1} - n - 2. \end{array}
$$

Now $u_{n} = t_{n} / 2 ^{n}$ , so

$$
u_{n} = 2 - \frac {n + 2}{2 ^{n}}.
$$

(v) [3 marks]

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \sum_{k=1} ^{n} s_{k} & = \sum_{k=1} ^{n} (2 k 2 ^{k} - 2 ^{k+1} + 2) \\ & = \sum_{k=1} ^{n} (k 2 ^{k+1}) - \sum_{k=1} ^{n} (2 ^{k+1}) + 2 n \\ & = 2 \sum_{k=1} ^{n} (k 2 ^{k}) - 2 ^{n+2} + 4 + 2 n \\ & = 2 f (n) - 2 ^{n+2} + 4 + 2 n \\ & = 2 ^{n+2} n - 2 ^{n+3} + 2 n + 8 \end{array}
$$
