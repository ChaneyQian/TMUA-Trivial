## 题目
Let $r$ and $s$ be integers. Then
$$
\frac{6^{r+s} \times 12^{r-s}}{8^{r} \times 9^{r+2s}}
$$
is an integer if

(a) $r + s \leqslant 0,$
(b) $s \leqslant 0,$
(c) $r \leqslant 0,$
(d) $r \geqslant s.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
Separating out the powers of 2 and 3 we have

$$
\frac {6 ^{r+s} \times 12 ^{r-s}}{8 ^{r} \times 9 ^{r+2s}} = 2 ^{(r+s+2r-2s-3r)} \times 3 ^{(r+s+r-s-2r-4s)} = 2 ^{-s} \times 3 ^{-4s}
$$

which is an integer if $s \leqslant 0$ . The answer is (b).
