## 题目
The point on the circle
$$
(x - 5)^{2} + (y - 4)^{2} = 4
$$
which is closest to the circle
$$
(x - 1)^{2} + (y - 1)^{2} = 1
$$
is

(a) $(3.4, 2.8),$
(b) $(3, 4),$
(c) $(5, 2),$
(d) $(3.8, 2.4).$

## 答案
A

## 解析
The circle with equation $\left( x - 5 \right) ^{2} + \left( y - 4 \right) ^{2} = 4$ has centre $( 5 , 4 )$ and radius 2.

The circle with equation $\left( x - 1 \right) ^{2} + \left( y - 1 \right) ^{2} = 1$ has centre (1, 1) and radius 1.

The vector from the first circle's centre to the second circle's centre is ( 4, 3) which has length ${ \sqrt { \left( - 4 \right) ^{2} + \left( - 3 \right) ^{2} } } = 5$ So the point on the first circle, closest to the second is

$$
(5, 4) + \frac {2}{5} (- 4, - 3) = (5, 4) + (- 1. 6, - 1. 2) = (3. 4, 2. 8).
$$

The answer is (a).
