## 答案
A

## 解析
If $y = 2 ^{-x} \sin^{2} \left( x^{2} \right)$ then note that $y > 0$ , which discounts (b). Also $y \left( 0 \right) = 0$ which discounts (d). Finally the points where graph (c) meets the x-axis arise regularly  -  this is not the case with $y = 2 ^{-x} \sin^{2} \left( x^{2} \right)$ where $y = 0$ at $x = \sqrt { \pi } , \sqrt { 2 \pi } , \sqrt { 3 \pi } , . .$ . The answer is (a).
