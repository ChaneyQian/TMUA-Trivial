## 题目
Given that $a$ and $b$ are positive and
$$
4 (\log_{10} a)^{2} + (\log_{10} b)^{2} = 1,
$$
then the greatest possible value of $a$ is

(a) $\dfrac{1}{10}$,
(b) $1,$
(c) $\sqrt{10}$,
(d) $10^{\sqrt{2}}$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
Note that a is largest when $\log_{10} a$ is largest. As $4 \left( \log_{10} a \right) ^{2} + \left( \log_{10} b \right) ^{2} = 1$ then $\left( \log_{10} a \right) ^{2}$ is largest when $b = 1$ and $\log_{10} b = 0$ . So

$$
4 \left(\log_{10} a\right) ^{2} = 1 \implies \log_{10} a = \frac {1}{2} \implies a = \sqrt {10}.
$$

The answer is (c).
