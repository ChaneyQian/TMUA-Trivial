## 题目
Given a function $f(x)$, you are told that
$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_{0}^{1} 3 f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x + \int_{1}^{2} 2 f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x &= 7, \\
\int_{0}^{2} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x + \int_{1}^{2} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x &= 1.
\end{aligned}
$$
It follows that $\int_{0}^{2} f(x) \, \mathrm{d}x$ equals

(a) $-1$,
(b) $0$,
(c) $\dfrac{1}{2}$,
(d) $2$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
If we set

$$
A = \int_{0} ^{1} f (x) \mathrm{d} x, \quad B = \int_{1} ^{2} f (x) \mathrm{d} x
$$

then we have the equations

$$
3 A + 2 B = 7, \quad (A + B) + B = 1.
$$

Solving these simultaneous equations we find $A = 3$ and $B = - 1$ . Hence

$$
\int_{0} ^{2} f (x) \mathrm{d} x = A + B = 3 - 1 = 2.
$$

The answer is (d).
