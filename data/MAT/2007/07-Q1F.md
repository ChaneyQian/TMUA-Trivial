## 题目
The equation
$$
8^{x} + 4 = 4^{x} + 2^{x+2}
$$
has

(a) no real solutions;
(b) one real solution;
(c) two real solutions;
(d) three real solutions.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
If we set $y = 2 ^{x}$ then the equation $8 ^{x} + 4 = 4 ^{x} + 2 ^{x+2}$ can be rewritten as

$$
\begin{array}{c} y^{3} + 4 = y^{2} + 4 y \\ \Longleftrightarrow y^{3} - y^{2} - 4 y + 4 = 0 \\ \Longleftrightarrow (y - 1) (y^{2} - 4) = 0 \end{array}
$$

So $y = 1 , 2 , - 2$ are the possible values for y. But as $y = 2 ^{x} > 0$ then only positive values for y will lead to real values for x. Hence $y = 1 , 2$ and $x = 0 , 1$ are the only possible x-values. The answer is (c).
