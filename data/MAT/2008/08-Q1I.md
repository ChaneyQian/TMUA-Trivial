## 题目
The function $S \left( n \right)$ is defined for positive integers $n$ by
$$S (n) = \text{sum of the digits of } n.$$
For example, $S \left( 723 \right) = 7 + 2 + 3 = 12 .$ = The sum
$$
S (1) + S (2) + S (3) + \dots + S (99)
$$

equals

(a) $746,$
(b) $862,$
(c) $900,$
(d) $924.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
We have

$$
S (1) + S (2) + S (3) + \dots + S (99) = S (00) + S (01) + \dots + S (99)
$$

and in the 100 two-digit numbers $00 , \ldots , 99$ there are twenty 0s, twenty $1 s , \ldots ,$ twenty 9s. So

$$
S (1) + S (2) + S (3) + \dots + S (99) = 20 \times (0 + 1 + \dots + 9) = 20 \times \frac {10}{2} (0 + 9) = 900
$$

and the answer is (c).
