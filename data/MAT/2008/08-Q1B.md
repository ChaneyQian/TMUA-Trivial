## 题目
Which is the smallest of these values?

(a) $\log_{10} \pi,$
(b) $\sqrt{\log_{10} (\pi^2)},$
(c) $\left(\dfrac{1}{\log_{10}\pi}\right)^3,$
(d) $\dfrac{1}{\log_{10}\sqrt{\pi}}.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
A

## 解析
As $\pi < 10$ then

$$
L = \log_{10} \pi <   1.
$$

So

$$
\sqrt {\log_{10} \left(\pi^{2}\right)} = \sqrt {2 L} > \sqrt {L \times L} = L; \qquad \left(\frac {1}{\log_{10} \pi}\right) ^{3} = L^{-3} > 1; \qquad \frac {1}{\log_{10} \sqrt {\pi}} = \frac {2}{L} > 2.
$$

The answer is (a).
