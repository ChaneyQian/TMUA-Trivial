## 题目
When

$$
1 + 3 x + 5 x^{2} + 7 x^{3} + \dots + 99 x^{49}
$$

is divided by $x - 1$ the remainder is

(a) $2000,$
(b) $2500,$
(c) $3000,$
(d) $3500.$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
By the remainder theorem when a polynomial $p \left( x \right)$ is divided by x 1 then the remainder is $p \left( 1 \right)$ . So the required remainder here is

$$
1 + 3 + 5 + 7 + \dots + 99 = \frac {50}{2} (1 + 99) = 2500
$$

as the series is an arithmetic progression. The answer is (b).
