## 题目
The equation

$$
9 ^{x} - 3 ^{x+1} = k
$$

has one or more real solutions precisely when

(a) $k \geqslant - 9 / 4 ,$
(b) $k > 0 ,$
(c) $k \leqslant - 1 .$
(d) $k \geqslant 5 / 8 .$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
A

## 解析
If we set $y = 3 ^{x}$ then the equation $9 ^{x} - 3 ^{x+1} = k$ now reads

$$
y^{2} - 3 y - k = 0.
$$

This has solutions

$$
y = \frac {3 \pm \sqrt {9 + 4 k}}{2}
$$

which are real when $k \geqslant - 9 / 4 . { \mathrm { ~ A s ~ } } y = 3 ^{x}$ then we further need that $y > 0$ for x to be real, but this is not a problem as the larger root is clearly positive. The answer is (a).
