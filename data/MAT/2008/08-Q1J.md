## 题目
In the range $0 \leqslant x < 2 \pi$ the equation

$$
(3 + \cos x) ^{2} = 4 - 2 \sin^{8} x
$$

has

(a) $0$ solutions,
(b) $1$ solution,
(c) $2$ solutions,
(d) $3$ solutions.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
Note that

$$
(3 + \cos x) ^{2} \geqslant (3 - 1) ^{2} = 4; \quad 4 - 2 \sin^{8} x \leqslant 4.
$$

So the equation will hold only when cos $x = - 1$ and sin $x = 0$ . In the range $0 \leqslant x < 2\pi$ this only occurs at $x = \pi$ . The answer is (b).
