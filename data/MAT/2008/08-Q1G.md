## 答案

## 解析
As $4 x - x^{2} - 5 = - \left( x - 2 \right) ^{2} - 1$ , then $y = \left( 4 x - x^{2} - 5 \right) ^{-1}$ is always negative and has a minimum value at $x = 2$ . The answer is (c).
