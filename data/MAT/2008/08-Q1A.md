## 题目
The function

$$
y = 2 x^{3} - 6 x^{2} + 5 x - 7
$$

has

(a) no stationary points;
(b) one stationary point;
(c) two stationary points;
(d) three stationary points.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
As $y = 2 x^{3} - 6 x^{2} + 5 x - 7$ then

$$
y^{\prime} = 6 x^{2} - 12 x + 5.
$$

The quadratic y has discriminant $12 ^{2} - 4 \times 6 \times 5 = 24 > 0$ and hence the equation $y^{\prime} = 0$ has two distinct real roots. The answer is (c).
