## 题目
[4 marks] Estimate $\left(\dfrac{4}{5}\right)^{25}$, given that $2^{10} \approx 1000$ and $\sqrt{10} \approx 3$.

(a) $0.03\%$
(b) $0.3\%$
(c) $3\%$
(d) $30\%$
(e) $100\%$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Turn the fraction into a power of ten:

$$\left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^{25} = \frac{2^{50}}{5^{25}} = \frac{2^{50}\cdot 2^{25}}{10^{25}} = \frac{2^{75}}{10^{25}}.$$

Now $2^{75} = \left(2^{10}\right)^{7.5} \approx \left(10^{3}\right)^{7.5} = 10^{22}\sqrt{10} \approx 3\times 10^{22}$, so the
quantity is about $3\times 10^{-3}$.

The answer is (b).
