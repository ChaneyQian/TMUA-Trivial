## 题目
[4 marks] Kevin builds a train of total length 10. It has one locomotive of length 1, followed by any number of carriages each of length 1, 2, or 3 (order matters). How many different carriage arrangements are possible?

(a) $81$
(b) $121$
(c) $149$
(d) $168$
(e) $200$

## 答案
C

## 解析
The locomotive uses length $1$, so the carriages form an ordered sequence of $1$s, $2$s and $3$s
summing to $9$. Let $T_n$ count such sequences; conditioning on the last carriage gives

$$T_n = T_{n-1} + T_{n-2} + T_{n-3}, \qquad T_0 = 1,\ T_1 = 1,\ T_2 = 2.$$

Running this on: $T_3,\dots,T_9 = 4,\ 7,\ 13,\ 24,\ 44,\ 81,\ 149$.

The answer is (c).
