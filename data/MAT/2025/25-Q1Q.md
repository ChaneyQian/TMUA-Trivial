## 题目
[4 marks] In a parallelogram, the two pairs of adjacent sides have lengths $a$ and $b$, and the two diagonals have lengths $c$ and $d$. Find the value of

$$\frac{c^2 + d^2}{a^2 + b^2}.$$

(a) $1$
(b) $2$
(c) $\sqrt{2}$
(d) $4$
(e) It depends on the specific parallelogram

## 答案
B

## 解析
Write the parallelogram as $\mathbf{0},\ \mathbf{u},\ \mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v},\ \mathbf{v}$ with $|\mathbf{u}| = a$ and $|\mathbf{v}| = b$.
The diagonals are $\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}$ and $\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}$, and the cross terms cancel:

$$c^{2} + d^{2} = |\mathbf{u}+\mathbf{v}|^{2} + |\mathbf{u}-\mathbf{v}|^{2} = 2|\mathbf{u}|^{2} + 2|\mathbf{v}|^{2} = 2\left(a^{2}+b^{2}\right).$$

The ratio is $2$ for every parallelogram, so (e) is a trap.
