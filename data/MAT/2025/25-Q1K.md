## 题目
[4 marks] Evaluate the infinite series

$$\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^k\, k}{2^k}.$$

(a) $-\dfrac{1}{3}$
(b) $-\dfrac{2}{9}$
(c) $\dfrac{1}{9}$
(d) $-\dfrac{1}{4}$
(e) $\dfrac{2}{9}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Differentiating $\sum_{k\ge 0} x^{k} = \dfrac{1}{1-x}$ and multiplying by $x$ gives
$\sum_{k\ge 1} k x^{k} = \dfrac{x}{(1-x)^{2}}$ for $|x| < 1$.

Here $x = -\dfrac{1}{2}$, so

$$\sum_{k=1}^{\infty} \frac{(-1)^{k} k}{2^{k}} \;=\; \frac{-\frac{1}{2}}{\left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^{2}} \;=\; -\frac{1/2}{9/4} \;=\; -\frac{2}{9}.$$

The answer is (b).
