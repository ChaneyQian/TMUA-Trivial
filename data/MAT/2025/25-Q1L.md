## 题目
[4 marks] An unfair coin is tossed repeatedly. The probability of getting Heads on the $n$-th toss is $\dfrac{1}{2n + 1}$. Let $p_n$ be the probability that an **odd** number of the first $n$ tosses show Heads. Find $p_6$.

(a) $\dfrac{1}{13}$
(b) $\dfrac{2}{13}$
(c) $\dfrac{6}{13}$
(d) $\dfrac{1}{2}$
(e) $\dfrac{11}{13}$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Track $q_n = 1 - 2p_n$, which is the expected value of $(-1)^{\text{(number of Heads)}}$.
Independence makes it multiplicative, and the $n$-th toss contributes a factor
$1 - \dfrac{2}{2n+1} = \dfrac{2n-1}{2n+1}$, so the product telescopes:

$$q_n = \prod_{k=1}^{n} \frac{2k-1}{2k+1} = \frac{1}{2n+1}, \qquad\text{hence}\qquad p_n = \frac{1}{2}\left(1 - \frac{1}{2n+1}\right) = \frac{n}{2n+1}.$$

Taking $n = 6$ gives $p_6 = \dfrac{6}{13}$.

The answer is (c).
