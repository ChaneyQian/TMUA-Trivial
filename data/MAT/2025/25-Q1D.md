## 题目
[4 marks] Find the area of the region enclosed by $y = x^2 - 2$, $y = x$, and the $y$-axis, for $x > 0$.

(a) $\dfrac{7}{3}$
(b) $\dfrac{8}{3}$
(c) $\dfrac{10}{3}$
(d) $\dfrac{11}{3}$
(e) $\dfrac{4}{3}$

## 答案
C

## 解析

For $x>0$ the line and the parabola meet where $x^{2}-2=x$, that is at $x=2$, and the line is above the parabola on $0<x<2$.

```tikz
  \draw[->] (-0.9,0) -- (2.9,0) node[below right] {$x$};
  \draw[->] (0,-2.9) -- (0,3.4) node[above left] {$y$};

  \node[below left] at (0,0) {$0$};
  \filldraw (0,0) circle (2.4pt);
  \filldraw (2,2) circle (2.4pt);
  \filldraw (0,-2) circle (2.4pt);
  \node[above left] at (2,2.05) {$(2,2)$};
  \node[right] at (0.1,-2.1) {$-2$};
  \node[red!85!black,right] at (2.3,2.55) {$y=x$};
  \node[blue!65!black,right] at (2.28,2.9) {};
  \node[blue!65!black,left] at (-0.88,-1.1) {\small$y=x^{2}-2$};
\end{tikzpicture}

```

$$\int_0^2\left(x-\left(x^{2}-2\right)\right)\mathrm{d}x=\left[\frac{x^{2}}{2}-\frac{x^{3}}{3}+2x\right]_0^2=2-\frac83+4=\frac{10}{3}.$$

The answer is (c).
