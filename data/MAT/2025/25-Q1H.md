## 题目
[4 marks] A circle has radius $r$. A sector has radius $\dfrac{3}{4}r$ and the same area as the circle. Find the central angle $\theta$ of the sector in radians.

(a) $\dfrac{16\pi}{9}$
(b) $\dfrac{32\pi}{9}$
(c) $\dfrac{8\pi}{9}$
(d) $2\pi$
(e) $\dfrac{4\pi}{3}$

## 答案
B

## 解析

A sector of radius $\rho$ and angle $\theta$ has area $\frac12\rho^{2}\theta$, so

$$\frac12\left(\frac{3r}{4}\right)^{2}\theta=\pi r^{2}\implies \frac{9r^{2}}{32}\theta=\pi r^{2}\implies\theta=\frac{32\pi}{9}.$$

(Note that $\frac{32\pi}{9}>2\pi$, so no such sector actually exists; the intended answer is the algebraic one.)

The answer is (b).
