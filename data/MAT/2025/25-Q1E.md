## 题目
[4 marks] The line $y = ax + 1$ is tangent to the ellipse $x^2 + 9y^2 = 1$. Find all possible values of $a$.

(a) $a = \pm 1$
(b) $a = \pm\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$
(c) $a = \pm\dfrac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$
(d) $a = \pm\dfrac{3}{2}$
(e) $a = \pm\dfrac{1}{3}$

## 答案
C

## 解析

Substitute the line into the ellipse and ask for a repeated root:

$$x^{2}+9(ax+1)^{2}=1\iff \left(1+9a^{2}\right)x^{2}+18ax+8=0.$$

$$\Delta=(18a)^{2}-32\left(1+9a^{2}\right)=36a^{2}-32=0\implies a^{2}=\frac89 .$$

The point $(0,1)$ lies outside the ellipse, so there are indeed two tangents, touching at $\left(\mp\frac{2\sqrt2}{3},\frac19\right)$.

The answer is (c).
