## 题目
[4 marks] Which of the following four numbers is the largest?

$$P = 2^{2^{100}}, \qquad Q = 4^{10^{30}}, \qquad R = 10{,}000{,}000^{10{,}000{,}000}, \qquad S = 8^{8^{50}}.$$

(a) $P$
(b) $Q$
(c) $R$
(d) $S$
(e) All four are equal

## 答案
D

## 解析

Three of these are powers of $2$, and $R$ is far too small to matter.

$$P=2^{2^{100}},\qquad Q=2^{2\times 10^{30}},\qquad S=2^{3\times 2^{150}},\qquad R=10^{7\times 10^{7}}<2^{2.4\times 10^{8}}.$$

Now compare the exponents: $2^{100}\approx 1.3\times 10^{30}$ and $2\times 10^{30}$ are of the same size, both dwarfed by $3\times 2^{150}\approx 4\times 10^{45}$, and $R$'s exponent is only about $2\times 10^{8}$.

The answer is (d).
