## 题目
[4 marks] Find the constant term in the expansion of

$$\left(x^2 + \frac{1}{x}\right)^{2025}.$$

(a) $\binom{2025}{670}$
(b) $\binom{2025}{674}$
(c) $\binom{2025}{675}$
(d) $\binom{2025}{676}$
(e) There is no constant term

## 答案
C

## 解析

The general term is

$$\binom{2025}{k}\left(x^{2}\right)^{2025-k}\left(\frac1x\right)^{k}=\binom{2025}{k}x^{4050-3k},$$

which is constant when $k=1350$. Finally $\binom{2025}{1350}=\binom{2025}{2025-1350}=\binom{2025}{675}$.

The answer is (c).
