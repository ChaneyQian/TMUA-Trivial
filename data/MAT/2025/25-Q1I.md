## 题目
[4 marks] How many real solutions does the equation

$$x^2 - 2\lfloor x \rfloor - 3 = 0$$

have, where $\lfloor x \rfloor$ denotes the floor function?

(a) $1$
(b) $2$
(c) $3$
(d) $4$
(e) Infinitely many

## 答案
C

## 解析

Put $n=\lfloor x\rfloor$. Then $x^{2}=2n+3$ with $n\le x<n+1$, so $x=\pm\sqrt{2n+3}$ and we need $n\ge-1$.

```tikz

\begin{tikzpicture}[>=stealth,x=1.0cm,y=0.28cm,line width=0.9pt]
  \draw[->] (-2.2,0) -- (4.3,0) node[below right] {$x$};
  \draw[->] (0,-2.2) -- (0,14.2) node[above left] {$y$};
  \foreach \n in {-1,0,1,2,3}{
    \draw[red!85!black,line width=1.4pt] (\n,{2*\n+3}) -- ({\n+1},{2*\n+3});
    \filldraw[red!85!black] (\n,{2*\n+3}) circle (2.2pt);
    \filldraw[red!85!black,fill=white,line width=1.0pt] ({\n+1},{2*\n+3}) circle (2.2pt);
  }
  \draw[blue!65!black,smooth,samples=120,domain=-2.05:3.72] plot ({\x},{\x*\x});
  \node[below right] at (0.05,-0.1) {\small$0$};
  \foreach \t/\l in {-1/-1,1/1,2/2,3/3}{\draw (\t,-0.35) -- (\t,0.35); \node[below] at (\t,-0.4) {\small$\l$};}
  \filldraw (-1,1) circle (2.6pt);
  \filldraw (2.64575,7) circle (2.6pt);
  \filldraw (3,9) circle (2.6pt);
  \node[blue!65!black,left] at (-2.1,5.0) {\small$y=x^{2}$};
  \node[red!85!black,right] at (3.2,10.8) {\small$y=2\lfloor x\rfloor+3$};
  \node[below right] at (2.70,6.6) {\small$\sqrt7$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

$n=-1$ gives $x=-1$, which does lie in $[-1,0)$. $n=0,1$ give $x=\sqrt3,\sqrt5$, neither in the required interval. $n=2$ gives $x=\sqrt7\in[2,3)$ and $n=3$ gives $x=3\in[3,4)$. For $n\ge4$ we have $n^{2}>2n+3$, so $\sqrt{2n+3}<n$ and nothing works.

So $x=-1$, $x=\sqrt7$, $x=3$.

The answer is (c).
