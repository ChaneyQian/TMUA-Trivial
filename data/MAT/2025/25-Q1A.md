## 题目
[4 marks] Given that

$$\log_2 x + \log_4 x + \log_8 x = 11,$$

find the value of $x$.

(a) $x = 32$
(b) $x = 48$
(c) $x = 64$
(d) $x = 128$
(e) $x = 256$

## 答案
C

## 解析

Everything is a power of $2$: $\log_4 x=\tfrac12\log_2 x$ and $\log_8 x=\tfrac13\log_2 x$.

$$\left(1+\tfrac12+\tfrac13\right)\log_2 x=\tfrac{11}{6}\log_2 x=11,$$

so $\log_2 x=6$ and $x=2^6$.

The answer is (c).
