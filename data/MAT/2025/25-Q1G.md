## 答案
C

## 解析

Write $f(x)=3x^{5}-5x^{3}$ and $g(x)=3^{x}+2$, and count sign changes of $f-g$.

Since $f'(x)=15x^{2}\left(x^{2}-1\right)$, on $x<0$ the largest value of $f$ is $f(-1)=2$, which is still below $g(-1)=\frac73$; and for $0\le x\le 1$ we have $f(x)=x^{3}\left(3x^{2}-5\right)\le 0<g(x)$. So there is no root with $x\le 1$.

$$f(1)=-2<5=g(1),\qquad f(2)=56>11=g(2),$$
$$f(12)=737856>531443=g(12),\qquad f(13)=1102894<1594325=g(13).$$

That gives a root in $(1,2)$ and a root in $(12,13)$; the quintic stays above the exponential in between, and beyond $x=13$ the exponential runs away for good.

The answer is (c).
