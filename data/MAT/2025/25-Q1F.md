(c) $13e - 10$
(d) $15e - 12$

## 答案
C

## 解析

Since $\ln\!\left(e^{2}x^{3}\right)=2+3\ln x$, the integral is

$$\int_1^e\left(4+12\ln x+9(\ln x)^{2}\right)\mathrm{d}x.$$

Integrating by parts, $\int_1^e\ln x\,\mathrm{d}x=\left[x\ln x-x\right]_1^e=1$ and $\int_1^e(\ln x)^{2}\,\mathrm{d}x=\left[x(\ln x)^{2}-2x\ln x+2x\right]_1^e=e-2$, so the value is

$$4(e-1)+12(1)+9(e-2)=13e-10.$$

The answer is (c).
