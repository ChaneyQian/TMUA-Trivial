## 题目
[4 marks] A triangle is inscribed in the unit circle $x^2 + y^2 = 1$. Two of its vertices are

$$A(1, 0) \quad \text{and} \quad B(\cos 60^\circ, \sin 60^\circ).$$

Find the **maximum** possible area of this triangle.

(a) $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$
(b) $\dfrac{2 + \sqrt{3}}{4}$
(c) $\dfrac{1 + \sqrt{3}}{4}$
(d) $\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$
(e) $\dfrac{3}{4}$

## 答案
B

## 解析

The chord $AB$ subtends $60^\circ$ at the centre, so $|AB|=2\sin 30^\circ=1$ and its distance from $O$ is $\cos 30^\circ=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$. With $AB$ fixed, the area is largest when $C$ is as far from the line $AB$ as possible, i.e. at the midpoint of the major arc, diametrically opposite the midpoint $M$ of $AB$.

```tikz

  \draw[->] (-1.45,0) -- (1.5,0) node[below right] {$x$};
  \draw[->] (0,-1.45) -- (0,1.5) node[above left] {$y$};
  \draw[blue!65!black] (0,0) circle (1);
  \coordinate (A) at (1,0);
  \coordinate (B) at (0.5,0.86603);
  \coordinate (C) at (-0.86603,-0.5);
  \draw[red!85!black,line width=1.0pt] (A) -- (B) -- (C) -- cycle;
  \draw[dashed,line width=0.6pt] (C) -- (0.75,0.43301);
  \draw[dashed,line width=0.6pt] (0,0) -- (0.75,0.43301);
  \filldraw (A) circle (1.8pt) node[right] {\small$A(1,0)$};
  \filldraw (B) circle (1.8pt) node[above right] {\small$B$};
  \filldraw (C) circle (1.8pt) node[below left] {\small$C$};
  \node[below left] at (0,0) {\small$O$};
  \node[above right] at (0.76,0.42) {\small$M$};
  \node[below] at (0,-1.5) {\small$OM=\frac{\sqrt3}{2},\quad CM=1+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

That greatest distance is $1+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$, so

$$\text{area}=\frac12\times 1\times\left(1+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right)=\frac{2+\sqrt3}{4}.$$

The answer is (b).
