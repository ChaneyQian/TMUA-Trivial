## 题目
Tangents to $y = e^{x}$ are drawn at $(p, e^{p})$ and $(q, e^{q})$ . These tangents cross the x-axis at a and b respectively. It follows that, for all p and q,

(a) $pa = qb$,

(b) $p - a < q - b$,

(c) $p - a = q - b$,

(d) $p - a > q - b$,

(e) $p + q = a + b$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
C

## 解析
The gradient at $p$ is $e^{p}$ and so the tangent is $y = e^{p} ( x - p ) + e^{p}$ . This crosses the x-axis when $e^{p} ( a - p ) + e^{p} = 0$ which happens if $a = p - 1$ . Similarly $b = q - 1$ so $p - a = q - b$ (they're both 1).

The answer is (c)
