## 题目
The area of the region bounded by the curve $y = e^{x}$ , the curve $y = 1 - e^{x}$ , and the y-axis equals

(a) $0,$
(b) $1 - \ln 2,$
(c) $\dfrac{1}{2} - \dfrac{1}{2} \ln 2,$
(d) $\ln 2 - 1,$
(e) $1 - \ln \dfrac{1}{2}.$

[Note that $\ln x$ is alternative notation for $\log_e x$ .]

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
The intersection point is at $e^{x} = 1 - e^{x}$ which happens when $2 e^{x} = 1$ , that is $x = - \ln 2 < 0$

![[Image/21-Q1D-sol1.jpg]]

The area has reflectional symmetry in the line $\begin{array} { r } { y = \frac { 1 } { 2 } } \end{array}$ so we want

$$
2 \int_{-\ln2} ^{0} e^{x} - \frac {1}{2} \mathrm{d} x = 2 \left[ e^{x} - \frac {x}{2} \right] _{-\ln2} ^{0} = 2 \left((1) - \left(\frac {1}{2} + \frac {\ln 2}{2}\right)\right) = 1 - \ln 2
$$

The answer is (b)
