## 题目
An entrance candidate is dealt three cards from a pack of fifty-two playing cards. To one significant figure the probability that he receives exactly one king is:

(i) 0.003; (ii) 0.01; (iii) 0.2; (iv) 0.05.

[There are four kings in a pack of playing cards.]

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(iii)

## 解析
The total number of ways to draw three cards from a pack of $52$ is $\binom{52}{3}$. To get exactly one king, we must choose $1$ king from the $4$ available, and $2$ non-kings from the remaining $48$ cards. The number of such combinations is

$$\binom{4}{1}\binom{48}{2} = 4 \times 1128 = 4512$$

The probability is therefore

$$\frac{4512}{\binom{52}{3}} = \frac{4512}{22100} \approx 0.204$$

To one significant figure, this is $0.2$. The answer is (iii).
