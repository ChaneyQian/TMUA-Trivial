## 题目
The inequality $2^n > n^2$ is true for:

(i) no integers $n \geq 0$; (ii) all integers $n \geq 0$;
(iii) all integers $n > 4$; (iv) all integers $n \geq 4$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(iii)

## 解析
We can check the inequality $2^n > n^2$ for small non-negative integers $n$. For $n=2, 3, 4$, we have $2^2 = 4 \not> 4$, $2^3 = 8 \not> 9$, and $2^4 = 16 \not> 16$, so the inequality is false. This eliminates options (i), (ii), and (iv). The inequality holds for $n=5$, as $32 > 25$, and continues to hold for all integers $n > 4$. The answer is (iii).
