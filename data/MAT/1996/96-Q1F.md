## 答案

## 解析
The transformation from $y = f(x)$ to $y = -f(x + 1)$ can be decomposed into two steps. First, the transformation $x \mapsto x + 1$ translates the original graph by $1$ unit to the left. Second, the overall negative sign reflects this translated graph in the $x$-axis. The answer is (i).
