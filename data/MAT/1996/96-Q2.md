## 题目
**(a)**

Factorise the expression $x^2 + x - 6$.

**(b)**

For which values of the real constant $a$ does the equation

$$
x^2 + x - a = 0
$$

have at least one real solution? Write down these solutions in terms of $a$.

**(c)**

Show that, for any value of the real constant $b$, the equation

$$
x^3 - (b + 1)x + b = 0
$$

has $x = 1$ as a solution. Find all values of $b$ for which this equation has exactly two distinct solutions.
