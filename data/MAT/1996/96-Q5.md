## 题目
A total of $12$ noughts and $4$ crosses are arranged in $4$ rows of $4$. One such arrangement is illustrated below.

$$
\begin{array}{cccc}
0 & 0 & \times & 0 \\
0 & \times & 0 & \times \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
\times & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}
$$

**(a)**

How many arrangements are there altogether?

**(b)**

How many arrangements are there in which there is a cross in every row?

**(c)**

How many arrangements are there in which there is a cross in every row and in every column?

## 解析
An arrangement is determined by choosing which of the $16$ cells hold the $4$ crosses; the noughts then fill the rest.

**(a)** The number of ways of choosing $4$ cells out of $16$ is

$$\binom{16}{4} = \frac{16 \times 15 \times 14 \times 13}{4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1} = 1820.$$

**(b)** There are $4$ rows and only $4$ crosses, so "a cross in every row" forces exactly one cross per row. Each row independently offers $4$ choices of column, giving

$$4^4 = 256.$$

**(c)** Now there is exactly one cross in each row and exactly one in each column. Reading off the column used by row $1$, row $2$, row $3$, row $4$ in turn gives a permutation of the four columns, and every permutation arises from exactly one such arrangement. Hence the number is

$$4! = 24.$$
