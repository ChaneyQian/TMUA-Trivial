## 题目
A total of $12$ noughts and $4$ crosses are arranged in $4$ rows of $4$. One such arrangement is illustrated below.

$$
\begin{array}{cccc}
0 & 0 & \times & 0 \\
0 & \times & 0 & \times \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
\times & 0 & 0 & 0
\end{array}
$$

**(a)**

How many arrangements are there altogether?

**(b)**

How many arrangements are there in which there is a cross in every row?

**(c)**

How many arrangements are there in which there is a cross in every row and in every column?
