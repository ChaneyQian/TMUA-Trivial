## 题目
![[Image/96-Q1A-fig1.jpg]]

The diagram above shows the graph of the function $y = ax^2 + bx + c$. Then:

(i) $b^2 - 4ac > 0$; (ii) $b^2 - 4ac = 0$; (iii) $b^2 - 4ac \leq 0$; (iv) $b^2 - 4ac < 0$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
(i)

## 解析
The graph of the quadratic function $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ intersects the $x$-axis at two distinct points. This means that the quadratic equation

$$ax^2 + bx + c = 0$$

has two distinct real roots. This occurs when the discriminant is strictly positive, so $b^2 - 4ac > 0$. The answer is (i).
