## 题目
The simultaneous equations

$$
\begin{aligned}
ax + by &= 1 \\
cx + dy &= 0
\end{aligned}
$$

in $x$ and $y$:

(i) have a solution whatever the values of $a, b, c, d$ may be;
(ii) have a unique solution whatever the values of $a, b, c, d$ may be;
(iii) have a solution only if $ad \neq bc$;
(iv) have a unique solution only if $ad \neq bc$.
