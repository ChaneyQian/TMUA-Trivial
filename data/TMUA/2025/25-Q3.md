## 题目
Geometric sequence $\{a_n\}$ has sum to infinity $S_\infty = 1$. All the terms are positive. What is the maximum value of the $3^{\text{rd}}$ term, $a_3$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{1}{8}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{4}{27}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{1}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{4}{9}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{2}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 1$$
