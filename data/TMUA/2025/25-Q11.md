## 题目
Given $f(x) = \bigl||x| - 3\bigr|$ and $I = \int_{\alpha}^{\alpha+2} f(x)\,dx$.

When does $I$ take its minimum?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \alpha = -4 \text{ only}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \alpha = -4 \text{ or } \alpha = 2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \alpha = -3 \text{ or } \alpha = 3$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \alpha = -1 \text{ only}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \alpha = 0 \text{ only}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \alpha = 2 \text{ only}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad I \text{ takes the same value for every } \alpha$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
**回忆答案:** $\alpha = -4$ or $\alpha = 2$

The graph of $f(x) = \bigl||x| - 3\bigr|$ is piecewise linear with every slope $\pm 1$: it decreases from $f(-3)=0$ up to a local maximum $f(0) = 3$ and back down to $f(3) = 0$, and outside $[-3,3]$ it increases without bound. In particular $f \geq 0$ everywhere, with zeros exactly at $x = \pm 3$.

Since $I(\alpha) = \int_{\alpha}^{\alpha+2} f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$ is the area under a non-negative graph over a window of fixed width $2$, we make it small by sliding the window onto the lowest part of the graph, i.e. around a zero. By the fundamental theorem of calculus,

$$I'(\alpha) = f(\alpha+2) - f(\alpha),$$

so stationary points occur where $f(\alpha + 2) = f(\alpha)$. Around the zero at $x = -3$ this needs the window centred there, $\alpha = -4$; around the zero at $x = 3$ it needs $\alpha = 2$. (The remaining solution $\alpha = -1$, centring the window on the peak $x=0$, is where $I$ is largest.)

Evaluating at $\alpha = 2$, where $f(x) = 3 - x$ on $[2,3]$ and $f(x) = x - 3$ on $[3,4]$:

$$I(2) = \int_{2}^{3}(3-x)\,\mathrm{d}x + \int_{3}^{4}(x-3)\,\mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = 1,$$

and by the evenness of $f$, $I(-4) = I(2) = 1$. For comparison $I(-3) = I(3) = 2$, $I(0) = 4$ and $I(-1) = 5$, so $1$ is indeed the minimum and it is attained at both values.

Because $f$ is even, any minimising $\alpha$ has a mirror partner $-\alpha - 2$, so the "only" options A and F cannot be right; and $f$ is not periodic, so $I$ certainly does depend on $\alpha$, ruling out G.

The answer is B.
