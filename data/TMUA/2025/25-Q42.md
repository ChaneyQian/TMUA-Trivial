## 题目
In the expansion of $\left(k + (1 + x)^{3}\right)^{4}$, the coefficient of $x^{8}$ is $135$.

What is the coefficient of $x^{9}$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -224$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -4$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 180$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 220$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 495$$

- 答案 **C 级**：无答案键，统筹者自解，另用整数多项式乘法穷举 $k \in [-200, 200]$ 独立确认 $k = -10$ 唯一、$x^{9}$ 系数为 $180$。

## 答案
C

## 解析
Write $u = (1 + x)^{3}$. By the binomial theorem
$$\left(k + u\right)^{4} = \sum_{j=0}^{4} \binom{4}{j} k^{4-j} (1 + x)^{3j}.$$
The term $(1 + x)^{3j}$ has degree $3j$, so only $j = 3$ (degree $9$) and $j = 4$ (degree $12$) can contribute to $x^{8}$ or $x^{9}$.

The coefficient of $x^{8}$ is
$$\binom{4}{3}k\binom{9}{8} + \binom{12}{8} = 36k + 495,$$
so $36k + 495 = 135$, giving $k = -10$.

The coefficient of $x^{9}$ is
$$\binom{4}{3}k\binom{9}{9} + \binom{12}{9} = 4k + 220 = -40 + 220 = 180.$$

The answer is C.
