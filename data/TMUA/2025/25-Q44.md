## 题目
The sum of the first $n$ terms of a geometric sequence is denoted by $S_{n}$. It is given that the sequence satisfies:
$$S_{10} = \mu S_{5}$$
$$S_{20} = \lambda S_{5}$$

Which of the following is a direct expression for $\lambda$ in terms of $\mu$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \lambda = \mu^{2} - \mu + 1$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \lambda = \mu^{2} + 2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \lambda = \mu^{3} - 2\mu^{2} + \mu$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \lambda = \mu^{3} + 2\mu^{2} + 2\mu$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \lambda = \mu(\mu + 1)$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \lambda = \mu^{3} - 2\mu^{2} + 2\mu$$

## 答案
F

## 解析
Let the common ratio be $r$. For $r \neq 1$,
$$S_{n} = \frac{a(1 - r^{n})}{1 - r},$$
so
$$\mu = \frac{S_{10}}{S_{5}} = \frac{1 - r^{10}}{1 - r^{5}} = 1 + r^{5}.$$

Put $t = r^{5} = \mu - 1$. Then
$$\lambda = \frac{S_{20}}{S_{5}} = \frac{1 - r^{20}}{1 - r^{5}} = 1 + t + t^{2} + t^{3} = (1 + t)(1 + t^{2}).$$

Substituting $t = \mu - 1$:
$$\lambda = \mu\left(1 + (\mu - 1)^{2}\right) = \mu\left(\mu^{2} - 2\mu + 2\right) = \mu^{3} - 2\mu^{2} + 2\mu.$$

This also covers $r = 1$, where $\mu = 2$ and $\lambda = 4$, and $8 - 8 + 4 = 4$.

The answer is F.
