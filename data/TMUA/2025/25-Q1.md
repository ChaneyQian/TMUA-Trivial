## 题目
The first, fourth, ninth term of an arithmetic sequence is the first three terms of a geometric sequence. What is the common ratio of the geometric sequence?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{3}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{5}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{25}{16}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{5}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{8}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \frac{25}{9}$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
**回忆答案:** $\dfrac{5}{3}$

Write the arithmetic sequence as $a$, $a+d$, $a+2d$, $\ldots$, so that its $n$th term is $a+(n-1)d$. The first, fourth and ninth terms are therefore

$$a, \quad a+3d, \quad a+8d.$$

These are the first three terms of a geometric sequence, so consecutive ratios are equal:

$$(a+3d)^{2} = a(a+8d).$$

Expanding,

$$a^{2} + 6ad + 9d^{2} = a^{2} + 8ad,$$

so $9d^{2} = 2ad$. If $d = 0$ the terms would all be equal and the common ratio would be $1$, which is not offered; hence $d \neq 0$ and

$$a = \frac{9d}{2}.$$

The common ratio is then

$$r = \frac{a+3d}{a} = \frac{\frac{9d}{2} + 3d}{\frac{9d}{2}} = \frac{\frac{15d}{2}}{\frac{9d}{2}} = \frac{5}{3}.$$

As a check, taking $d = 2$ gives the terms $9, 15, 25$, and $\frac{15}{9} = \frac{25}{15} = \frac{5}{3}$.

Note that $\frac{a+8d}{a} = \frac{25}{9}$ is $r^{2}$, not $r$, so answering $\frac{25}{9}$ is the common slip of comparing the first and ninth terms directly.

The answer is D.
