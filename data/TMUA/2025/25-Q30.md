## 题目
$$|1 - x| = 3x - 4 \quad (*)$$

I. $|1 - x| = 1 - x$ or $-(1 - x)$

II. $1 - x = 3x - 4 \implies x = \dfrac{5}{4}$

III. $x - 1 = 3x - 4 \implies x = \dfrac{3}{2}$

IV. Hence, $x = \dfrac{5}{4}, \dfrac{3}{2}$ are solutions to $(*)$.

Which step does the first error occur in?
