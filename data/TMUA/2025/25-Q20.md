## 题目
Quadratic function $16x^{2} + kx + 9$ can be factorised into $(ax + b)(cx + d)$, where $a, c > 0$ and $a, b, c, d \in \mathbb{Z}$. How many distinct values can $k$ take?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 8$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 16$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 18$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 30$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{Infinitely many}$$
