## 题目
A triangle $\triangle ABC$. $A = \angle BAC$, $B = \angle ABC$, $C = \angle ACB$.

Given $A < B < C$. Which of the following must be correct?

	I. $\sin A < \sin B < \sin C$
	II. $\cos A > \cos B > \cos C$
	III. $\tan A < \tan B < \tan C$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{none of them}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{I only}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{II only}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{III only}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{I and II only}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{I and III only}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \text{II and III only}$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad \text{I, II and III}$$

## 答案

## 解析
**回忆答案:** I and II only

In a triangle each angle lies in $(0^\circ, 180^\circ)$ and $A + B + C = 180^\circ$, so at most one angle is obtuse. Test each statement.

**I.** By the sine rule, $\sin A = \dfrac{a}{2R}$, $\sin B = \dfrac{b}{2R}$, $\sin C = \dfrac{c}{2R}$, where $R$ is the circumradius. Larger angles face longer sides, so $A < B < C$ gives $a < b < c$ and hence

$$\sin A < \sin B < \sin C.$$

**True.** (Note this works even though $\sin$ is not increasing on the whole of $(0^\circ,180^\circ)$ — the sine rule does the work for us.)

**II.** $\cos$ is strictly decreasing on $(0^\circ, 180^\circ)$, so $A < B < C$ immediately gives

$$\cos A > \cos B > \cos C.$$

**True.**

**III.** $\tan$ is increasing on $(0^\circ, 90^\circ)$ but is **negative** for obtuse angles, so the ordering breaks as soon as $C > 90^\circ$. For instance $A = 30^\circ$, $B = 40^\circ$, $C = 110^\circ$ gives

$$\tan A \approx 0.577, \quad \tan B \approx 0.839, \quad \tan C \approx -2.747,$$

so $\tan C < \tan A < \tan B$. **False.** (If $C = 90^\circ$, $\tan C$ is not even defined.)

So statements I and II must hold but III need not.

The answer is E.
