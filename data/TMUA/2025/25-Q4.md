## 题目
Consider the following spiral graph. $OR$ has length $1$. Each of the following line segment has $\dfrac{3}{4}$ times the length of the previous one. The graph converges at $P$. Determine the length of $OP$.

![[Image/25-Q4-fig1.png]]
$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{12}{25}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{16}{25}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{3}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{4}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{28}{25}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \frac{20}{7}$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad 4$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
**回忆答案:** $OP = \dfrac{4}{5}$

原稿演算：$x_P = 1 - \left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{2} + \left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{4} - \cdots = \dfrac{1}{1-\left(-\frac{9}{16}\right)} = \dfrac{16}{25}$，
$y_P = \dfrac{3}{4} - \left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{3} + \left(\dfrac{3}{4}\right)^{5} + \cdots = \dfrac{\frac{3}{4}}{1-\left(-\frac{9}{16}\right)} = \dfrac{12}{25}$，故 $OP = \dfrac{20}{25} = \dfrac{4}{5}$。

The key point is that the segments turn through $90^\circ$ each time, so they alternate between the two coordinate directions: the segments of length $1, \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{2}, \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{4}, \ldots$ are horizontal (pointing right, left, right, $\ldots$) and those of length $\frac{3}{4}, \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{3}, \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{5}, \ldots$ are vertical (pointing up, down, up, $\ldots$). So treat the two coordinates separately, each as its own geometric series with common ratio $-\left(\frac{3}{4}\right)^{2} = -\frac{9}{16}$.

Horizontally,

$$x_P = 1 - \left(\tfrac{3}{4}\right)^{2} + \left(\tfrac{3}{4}\right)^{4} - \cdots = \frac{1}{1 + \frac{9}{16}} = \frac{16}{25}.$$

Vertically,

$$y_P = \tfrac{3}{4} - \left(\tfrac{3}{4}\right)^{3} + \left(\tfrac{3}{4}\right)^{5} - \cdots = \frac{\frac{3}{4}}{1 + \frac{9}{16}} = \frac{3}{4} \cdot \frac{16}{25} = \frac{12}{25}.$$

Both series converge since $\left|-\frac{9}{16}\right| < 1$. By Pythagoras,

$$OP = \sqrt{\left(\frac{16}{25}\right)^{2} + \left(\frac{12}{25}\right)^{2}} = \frac{1}{25}\sqrt{256 + 144} = \frac{20}{25} = \frac{4}{5}.$$

Two traps are worth naming: dropping the alternating signs gives ratio $+\frac{9}{16}$ and the wrong value $\frac{20}{7}$; and summing the lengths of all the segments, $\frac{1}{1 - \frac{3}{4}} = 4$, gives the total path length rather than the displacement $OP$.

The answer is D.
