## 题目
A student wants to prove $x + y = 180^\circ$. His proof is as follows:

![[Image/25-Q24-fig1.png]]
I. If $O$ is on $BD$, then $BD$ is the diameter of $\odot O$.

II. Thus $x = y = 90^\circ$, $x + y = 180^\circ$.

III. If $O$ is inside $\triangle ABD$, then $\angle BOD = 2y$.

IV. As $C$ bisects the arc $BD$, ...

Which step does the first error occur in?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{The proof is correct; there is no error.}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{Step I}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{Step II}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{Step III}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{Step IV}$$
