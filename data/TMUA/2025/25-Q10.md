## 题目
A circle with radius $5$ is tangent to $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x^{2}$ at $2$ points. The circle also has tangential line $y = k$. Find the value of the bigger $k$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad k = 8$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad k = 10$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad k = 12$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad k = 13$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad k = 18$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad k = 18.5$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad k = 38$$
