## 题目
A circle with radius $5$ is tangent to $y = \dfrac{1}{2}x^{2}$ at $2$ points. The circle also has tangential line $y = k$. Find the value of the bigger $k$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad k = 8$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad k = 10$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad k = 12$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad k = 13$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad k = 18$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad k = 18.5$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad k = 38$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
**回忆答案:** $k = 18$

The parabola $y = \frac{1}{2}x^{2}$ is symmetric in the $y$-axis, and a circle touching it at two points must share that symmetry, so its centre is at $(0, c)$ for some $c$ and the circle is

$$x^{2} + (y - c)^{2} = 25.$$

Substitute $x^{2} = 2y$ from the parabola:

$$2y + (y-c)^{2} = 25 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad y^{2} + (2 - 2c)y + c^{2} - 25 = 0.$$

Tangency at two symmetric points means this quadratic in $y$ has a repeated root (one value of $y$, giving the two values $x = \pm\sqrt{2y}$). Setting the discriminant to zero,

$$(2-2c)^{2} - 4\left(c^{2} - 25\right) = 0,$$

$$4 - 8c + 4c^{2} - 4c^{2} + 100 = 0 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad 8c = 104 \quad \Longrightarrow \quad c = 13.$$

Then $y = c - 1 = 12$ and $x^{2} = 24 > 0$, so the contact points are $\left(\pm 2\sqrt{6},\, 12\right)$ — genuinely two distinct points, as required.

The circle has centre $(0, 13)$ and radius $5$, so its horizontal tangents are

$$y = 13 - 5 = 8 \quad \text{and} \quad y = 13 + 5 = 18,$$

and the larger value is $k = 18$.

(Taking $c = 5$, so that the circle sits on the vertex, gives $k = 10$ but only **one** point of contact, so it does not satisfy the question; and $13$ itself is the centre's height, not a tangent line.)

The answer is E.
