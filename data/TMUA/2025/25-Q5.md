## 解析
**回忆答案:** $5(5 - \pi)$

From the diagram the circle is centred at the origin and the line touches it at $(4, 2)$, so the radius is

$$r = \sqrt{4^{2} + 2^{2}} = \sqrt{20}, \qquad r^{2} = 20,$$

and the circle is $x^{2} + y^{2} = 20$.

The tangent at $(4,2)$ is perpendicular to the radius $O(4,2)$, whose gradient is $\frac{1}{2}$; hence the tangent has gradient $-2$:

$$y - 2 = -2(x - 4) \quad \Longrightarrow \quad 2x + y = 10.$$

(Equivalently, the tangent to $x^{2}+y^{2}=r^{2}$ at $(x_1,y_1)$ is $x_1x + y_1y = r^{2}$, i.e. $4x + 2y = 20$.)

This line meets the axes at $(5, 0)$ and $(0, 10)$, so the triangle it forms with the two axes has area

$$\frac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 10 = 25.$$

The shaded region is this triangle with the quarter of the disc lying in the first quadrant removed, and that quarter has area

$$\frac{1}{4}\pi r^{2} = \frac{1}{4}\pi (20) = 5\pi.$$

Therefore the shaded area is

$$25 - 5\pi = 5(5 - \pi) \approx 9.29.$$

Note that $5$ is the $x$-intercept of the tangent, not the radius: using $\frac{1}{4}\pi(5)^{2} = \frac{25\pi}{4}$ for the quarter-disc is the common error.

The answer is C.
