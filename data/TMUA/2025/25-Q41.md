## 题目
Find the area of the region enclosed by the curve
$$|y| + |x|^{2} = 4.$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{16}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{32}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{64}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 16$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 32$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Since $|x|^{2} = x^{2}$, the curve is $|y| = 4 - x^{2}$, which requires $-2 \leqslant x \leqslant 2$. For each such $x$ the curve consists of the two points $y = \pm(4 - x^{2})$, so the enclosed region is bounded above by $y = 4 - x^{2}$ and below by $y = -(4 - x^{2})$.

The area is therefore
$$2\int_{-2}^{2} \left(4 - x^{2}\right)\mathrm{d}x = 2\left[4x - \frac{x^{3}}{3}\right]_{-2}^{2} = 2 \times \frac{32}{3} = \frac{64}{3}.$$

The answer is C.
