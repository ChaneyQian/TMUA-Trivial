## 题目
Calculate:

$$\log_{4}(8\sqrt{2}) + \log_{3}\left(\frac{1}{3}\right) + \log_{2}\left(\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -\dfrac{9}{8}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{3}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{5}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 2$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
**回忆答案:** $\dfrac{1}{4}$

Write every argument as a power of its own base and read off the logarithm.

For the first term, $8\sqrt{2} = 2^{3} \times 2^{1/2} = 2^{7/2}$ and the base is $4 = 2^{2}$, so

$$\log_{4}\left(8\sqrt{2}\right) = \frac{\log_{2}\left(2^{7/2}\right)}{\log_{2}\left(2^{2}\right)} = \frac{7/2}{2} = \frac{7}{4} .$$

For the second term, $\dfrac{1}{3} = 3^{-1}$, so $\log_{3}\left(\dfrac{1}{3}\right) = -1$.

For the third term, $\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \dfrac{2^{1/2}}{2} = 2^{-1/2}$, so $\log_{2}\left(\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right) = -\dfrac{1}{2}$.

Adding,

$$\frac{7}{4} - 1 - \frac{1}{2} = \frac{7 - 4 - 2}{4} = \frac{1}{4} .$$

(The two negative terms are where marks are lost: both arguments are less than $1$, so both logarithms must be negative.)

The answer is D.
