## 题目
A circle has equation
$$ x^2 + y^2 - 4x + 6y - 3 = 0. $$

The circle is first translated by $5$ units in the positive $x$-direction, then reflected in the line $y = x$, then reflected in the $x$-axis, and finally enlarged by a scale factor of $3$ about the origin. The equation of the final circle is

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad (x + 9)^2 + (y + 21)^2 = 144 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad (x + 9)^2 + (y + 21)^2 = 48 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad (x + 9)^2 + (y + 21)^2 = 16 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad (x + 9)^2 + (y - 21)^2 = 144 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad (x - 9)^2 + (y + 21)^2 = 144 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad (x + 21)^2 + (y + 9)^2 = 144 $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad (x + 3)^2 + (y + 7)^2 = 144 $$
$$ \mathbf{H} \quad (x + 9)^2 + (y + 21)^2 = 90 $$

## 解析
Completing the squares:

$$ x^2 - 4x + y^2 + 6y - 3 = 0 \implies (x - 2)^2 - 4 + (y + 3)^2 - 9 - 3 = 0 \implies (x - 2)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 16. $$

So the original centre is $(2, -3)$ and the radius is $4$.

Apply the transformations to the centre (each is a rigid motion except the last). (1) Translate by $+5$ in $x$: centre becomes $(7, -3)$, radius still $4$. (2) Reflect in $y = x$ (swap coordinates): centre becomes $(-3, 7)$, radius still $4$. (3) Reflect in the $x$-axis (negate $y$): centre becomes $(-3, -7)$, radius still $4$. (4) Enlarge by scale factor $3$ about the origin (multiply each coordinate by $3$, multiply radius by $3$): centre becomes $(-9, -21)$, radius becomes $12$.
