## 题目
The curve $y = x^3 - 4x$ is shown below.

![[Image/JZMaths_SetB-Mock-P1-Q2-fig1.png]]

What is the total area enclosed between the curve, the $x$-axis and the lines $x = -3$ and $x = 3$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{9}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 8 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{25}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{41}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \frac{33}{2} $$

## 解析
Due to symmetry, we can just do:
$$
2 \times \left( \int_0^2 -y \, dx + \int_2^3 y \, dx \right)
= 2 \times \left( 4 + \frac{25}{4} \right)
= \frac{41}{2}.
$$
