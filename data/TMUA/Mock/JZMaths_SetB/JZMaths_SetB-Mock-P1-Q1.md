## 题目
The equation
$$x^{\log_2 x} = \frac{x^3}{4}$$
has exactly two solutions for $x > 0$. Find their product.
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 2 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 4 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 6 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 8 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 16 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 20 $$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Take logarithms base 2 of both sides. The left side becomes $(\log_2 x)(\log_2 x)$, and the right becomes $3\log_2 x - 2$. Writing $u = \log_2 x$,
$$u^2 = 3u - 2, \qquad \text{so} \qquad u^2 - 3u + 2 = 0,$$
giving $u = 1$ or $u = 2$, that is $x = 2$ or $x = 4$. Their product is $2^{u_1} \cdot 2^{u_2} = 2^{u_1+u_2} = 2^3 = 8$.
