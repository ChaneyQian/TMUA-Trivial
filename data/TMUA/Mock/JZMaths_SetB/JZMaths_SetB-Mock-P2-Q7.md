## 题目
The circle $C$ has equation
$$ x^2 + y^2 - 6x + 4y - 12 = 0. $$
The point $P = (11, 2)$ lies outside $C$. The two tangents from $P$ to $C$ touch the circle at $A$ and $B$.
Let $\Gamma$ be the circle passing through $P$, $A$ and $B$. What is the radius of $\Gamma$?
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \sqrt{17} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \sqrt{20} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \sqrt{21} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \sqrt{28} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \sqrt{34} $$

## 解析
Complete the square in the equation of $C$:
$$ (x - 3)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 25. $$
So the centre of $C$ is $O = (3, -2)$ and its radius is $5$.

By a circle theorem, since $OPA$ is a right-angled triangle, the circle $C_1$ that passes points $O$, $P$ and $A$, has $OP$ as a diameter.

Similarly, since $OPB$ is a right-angled triangle, the circle $C_2$ that passes points $O$, $P$ and $B$, also has $OP$ as a diameter.

Therefore $C_1$ and $C_2$ are in fact the same circles as they share the diameter $OP$, and since it passes $A$, $B$ and $P$, this circle also must be our $\Gamma$, and so
$$ OP^2 = (11 - 3)^2 + (2 - (-2))^2 = 8^2 + 4^2 = 80. $$

Therefore the radius of $\Gamma$ is
$$ \frac{OP}{2} = \frac{\sqrt{80}}{2} = \sqrt{20}. $$
