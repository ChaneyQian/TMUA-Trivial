$$ f(x) = x^{2008} - 2x^{2007} + 3x^{2006} - 4x^{2005} + \dots + 2007x^2 - 2008x + 2009 $$

$$ g(x) = \frac{2012x + 2009}{(1 + x)^2} $$
Which of the following is a solution to $g(x) = f(x)$

$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 3^{\frac{1}{2008}}
