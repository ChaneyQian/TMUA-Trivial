## 题目
Given that $\sin \theta + \cos \theta = \frac{1}{2}$, find the value of $\sin^3 \theta + \cos^3 \theta$.
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \frac{1}{4} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{5}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{7}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{7}{12} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{11}{16} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \frac{13}{18} $$

E

## 解析
Squaring the given equation implies $1 + 2 \sin \theta \cos \theta = \frac{1}{4}$, which implies

$$ \sin \theta \cos \theta = -\frac{3}{8}. $$

Then

$$ \sin^3 \theta + \cos^3 \theta = (\sin \theta + \cos \theta)^3 - 3 \sin \theta \cos \theta (\sin \theta + \cos \theta) = \frac{1}{8} - 3 \left(-\frac{3}{8}\right) \left(\frac{1}{2}\right) = \frac{1}{8} + \frac{9}{16} = \frac{11}{16}. $$
