## 题目
Three distinct numbers are chosen at random from $\{1, 2, \dots, 10\}$. Find the probability that, arranged in increasing order, they form an arithmetic progression.
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \frac{1}{4} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{1}{5} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{1}{6} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{1}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{2}{9} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \frac{2}{15} $$

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
There are $\binom{10}{3} = 120$ equally likely choices.

An increasing progression $a, a + d, a + 2d$ needs $d \ge 1$ and $a + 2d \le 10$.

Counting by common difference, $d = 1$ gives $8$ progressions, $d = 2$ gives $6$, $d = 3$ gives $4$ and $d = 4$ gives $2$, a total of $20$. The probability is $\frac{20}{120} = \frac{1}{6}$.
