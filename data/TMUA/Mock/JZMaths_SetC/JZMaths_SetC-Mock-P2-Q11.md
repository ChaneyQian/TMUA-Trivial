## 题目
In triangle $ABC$, $\angle A = 30^\circ$, $AB = 2x^2$ and $BC = 3x$, where $x > 0$. Suppose that there are exactly two non-congruent triangles satisfying these conditions. Which of the following conditions can be deduced?
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 2 < x < \frac{7}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{5}{3} < x < 5 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 1 < x < 4 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{1}{2} < x < \frac{5}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 0 < x < 2 $$

## 答案
C

## 解析
For two non-congruent triangles to be possible, the side $BC$ must lie strictly between the perpendicular height and the side $AB$.

The perpendicular height is
$$ AB \sin 30^\circ = 2x^2 \cdot \frac{1}{2} = x^2. $$

Therefore, two non-congruent triangles are possible if and only if
$$ x^2 < 3x < 2x^2. $$

Since $x > 0$, the first inequality gives
$$ x < 3, $$

and the second inequality gives
$$ x > \frac{3}{2}. $$

Hence
$$ \frac{3}{2} < x < 3. $$

Looking at all the options, the only conclusion that can be drawn is: $1 < x < 4$.
