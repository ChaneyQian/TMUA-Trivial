## 题目
Which of the following values is the **largest**?
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \frac{7}{5} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{17}{12} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{10}{7} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{11}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 2 \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right) $$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
First note that
$$ 2 \cos \left( \frac{\pi}{4} \right) = 2 \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \sqrt{2}. $$

Next, we compare fractions by cross multiply, compare against $\frac{10}{7}$ as it has a $10$ for easy multiplication.

We get $\frac{10}{7} > \frac{7}{5}$ because $10 \cdot 5 > 7 \cdot 7$.

Also $\frac{10}{7} > \frac{17}{12}$ because $10 \cdot 12 > 17 \cdot 7$.

Similarly $\frac{10}{7} > \frac{11}{8}$ because $10 \cdot 8 > 11 \cdot 7$.

Finally, since numbers are positive, we can compare squares: $\left( \frac{10}{7} \right)^2 = \frac{100}{49} > 2$. Therefore $\frac{10}{7} > \sqrt{2}$ and so the largest value is $\frac{10}{7}$.
