## 题目
Find the number of real solutions of $|x^2 - 1| = 2^x$.
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 2 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 3 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 4 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 5 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 6 $$

E

## 解析
Sketch the graphs of $y = |x^2 - 1|$ and $y = 2^x$.

![[Image/JZMaths_SetC-Mock-P1-Q2-fig1.png]]

From the sketch, we can see that $x = 0$ is a solution. There are also two solutions with $x < 0$, and **2** solutions with $x > 0$.

On the right of $0$, there are two solutions because the graph of $y = x^2 - 1$ initially catches up with $y = 2^x$, but eventually $2^x$ grows faster. This can be observed from the graph, so we do not need to find the derivatives explicitly.

Therefore, the total number of real solutions is $1 + 2 + 2 = 5$.
