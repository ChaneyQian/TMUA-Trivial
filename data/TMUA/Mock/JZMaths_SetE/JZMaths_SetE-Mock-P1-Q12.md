## 题目
Find the sum of all positive values of $k$ satisfying
$$ \int_0^k (|2x - 6| - 2) \, dx = 3. $$

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 4 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 8 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 9 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 13 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 16 $$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Sketch a graph of $y = |2x - 6| - 2$, the first triangle has area 4, the second which is under the $x$-axis has area of 2.

By inspection, it is possible to deduce the possible values of $k$ are 1, 3 and 5, so their sum is $1 + 3 + 5 = 9$.

Remark: Although the question can be solved using integration, this approach is lengthy and tedious, which is the intended trap.
