## 题目
Real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ satisfy
$$ 5^a = 2^b = 10^{-c}, \quad abc \ne 0. $$
What is the value of $\frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c}$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad -1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{1}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 2 $$

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Let the common value be $k$, so
$$ 5^a = 2^b = 10^{-c} = k. $$

Taking logarithms gives
$$ a \ln 5 = b \ln 2 = -c \ln 10 = \ln k. $$

Since $abc \ne 0$, we have $k \ne 1$, so $\ln k \ne 0$. Hence
$$ \frac{1}{a} = \frac{\ln 5}{\ln k}, \quad \frac{1}{b} = \frac{\ln 2}{\ln k}, \quad \frac{1}{c} = -\frac{\ln 10}{\ln k}. $$

Therefore,
$$ \frac{1}{a} + \frac{1}{b} + \frac{1}{c} = \frac{\ln 5 + \ln 2 - \ln 10}{\ln k} = \frac{\ln \left(\frac{5 \cdot 2}{10}\right)}{\ln k} = 0. $$
