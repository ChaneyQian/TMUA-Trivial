## 题目
Find all real values of $k$ for which
$$ kx^2 + (k+3)x + (k+3) > 0 $$
holds for every real number $x$.
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad k > 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad k < -3 \text{ or } k > 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad -3 < k < 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad k > 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad k > -3 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{There are no such values of } k $$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Case 1. If $k=0$, the expression becomes $3x+3$, which is negative when $x < -1$. Hence $k=0$ does not work.
Case 2. Suppose $k \neq 0$. For the quadratic to be strictly positive for every real $x$, its leading coefficient must be positive and its discriminant must be negative. Therefore,
$$ k > 0 \quad \text{and} \quad (k+3)^2 - 4k(k+3) < 0. $$
The discriminant condition simplifies to
$$ (k+3)(3-3k) < 0, $$
so $(k+3)(k-1) > 0$. Hence $k < -3$ or $k > 1$.
Combining this with $k > 0$ gives $k > 1$.
