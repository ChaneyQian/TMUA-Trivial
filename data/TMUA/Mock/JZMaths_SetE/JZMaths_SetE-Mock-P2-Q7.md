## 题目
Positive real numbers $x$, $y$ and $z$ are such that

$$ x, y, z $$

are consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence, while

$$ \log_2 x, \log_2 y, \log_2 z $$

are consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence.

It is also known that

$$ x + y + z = 12. $$

Find $xyz$.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 32 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 48 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 60 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 64 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 72 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 96 $$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Since $x, y, z$ are consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence,

$$ x + z = 2y. $$

Since $\log_2 x, \log_2 y, \log_2 z$ are consecutive terms of an arithmetic sequence,

$$ \log_2 x + \log_2 z = 2\log_2 y. $$

This means

$$ xz = y^2. $$

So $x, y, z$ are also consecutive terms of a geometric sequence.

A positive sequence which is **both** arithmetic and geometric must have all three terms equal.
Therefore $x = y = z = 4$, and so $xyz = 64$.
