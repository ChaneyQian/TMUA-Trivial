## 题目
Find the sum of all solutions $x$ in the interval $0 \le x < 2\pi$ to

$$ |\sin x| + |\cos x| = 1. $$

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \pi $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{3\pi}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 2\pi $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{5\pi}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 3\pi $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \frac{7\pi}{2} $$

## 解析
$$ a = |\sin x|, \qquad b = |\cos x|. $$

Then $a \ge 0$, $b \ge 0$, and

$$ a^2 + b^2 = 1. $$

We also need $a + b = 1$. Squaring gives

$$ a^2 + b^2 + 2ab = 1, $$

so $2ab = 0$. Thus either $|\sin x| = 0$ or $|\cos x| = 0$.

In $0 \le x < 2\pi$, the solutions are
$$ 0, \frac{\pi}{2}, \pi, \frac{3\pi}{2}. $$

Their sum is $3\pi$.

**Remark:** the answer can also be **seen** from a sketch of $|\sin x|$ and $|\cos x|$ on the same plot.
