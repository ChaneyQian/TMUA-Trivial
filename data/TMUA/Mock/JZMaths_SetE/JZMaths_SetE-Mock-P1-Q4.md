## 题目
For how many integer values of $c$ does the line $y = 2x + c$ meet the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 20$ at two distinct points?
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 19 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 18 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 17 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 16 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 21 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 24 $$

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Write the line as $2x - y + c = 0$. Its distance from the centre $(0,0)$ is $\frac{|c|}{\sqrt{5}}$ by similar triangles. Two distinct intersection points occur exactly when the distance is less than the radius $2\sqrt{5}$:
$$ \frac{|c|}{\sqrt{5}} < 2\sqrt{5} \iff |c| < 10. $$
The integers with $|c| < 10$ are $-9, -8, \dots, 9$, of which there are $19$.
