## 题目
In triangle $ABC$, $AB = t$, $BC = 10$ and $\angle BAC = 45^{\circ}$, where $t > 0$.

For which values of $t$ is the length of $AC$ uniquely determined?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 0 < t \le 10 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 0 < t < 10\sqrt{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 0 < t \le 10 \text{ or } t = 10\sqrt{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad t = 10\sqrt{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad t > 10 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 0 < t \le 10\sqrt{2} $$

## 答案
C

## 解析
The length $AC$ is uniquely determined if and only if the information determines a unique triangle.

There are two ways this can happen.

First, $ABC$ may be right-angled at $C$. In this case, $AB$ is the hypotenuse, so
$$ \sin 45^{\circ} = \frac{BC}{AB} = \frac{10}{t}. $$

Hence $t = 10\sqrt{2}$.

Second, we may have $AB \le BC$, so $t \le 10$. Since the larger side is opposite the larger angle, this gives $\angle ACB \le \angle BAC = 45^{\circ}$. The supplementary possibility for $\angle ACB$ would therefore be at least $135^{\circ}$, which is impossible since $\angle BAC = 45^{\circ}$. Thus there is only one possible triangle.

Therefore the required values of $t$ are given by: $0 < t \le 10 \text{ or } t = 10\sqrt{2}$.
