## 题目
Given $\tan \theta = 2, \ 90^\circ < \theta < 360^\circ$, find the value of $\sin \theta \cos \theta$.
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \frac{2}{5} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad -\frac{2}{5} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad -\frac{5}{3} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{3}{5} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad -\frac{1}{5} $$

## 答案
A

## 解析
This question can be solved directly using the definitions of the trigonometric functions, by considering a point $P$ moving around a circle of radius $r$.

Since $\tan \theta = 2 > 0$ and $90^\circ < \theta < 360^\circ$, the angle must lie in the third quadrant. By definition,

$$ \tan \theta = \frac{y}{x} = 2, $$

where $(x, y)$ are the coordinates of $P$. To keep the numbers simple, take $x = -1$ and $y = -2$, since both coordinates must be negative in the third quadrant.

Then

$$ r = \sqrt{x^2 + y^2} = \sqrt{1 + 4} = \sqrt{5}. $$

Therefore,

$$ \sin \theta \cos \theta = \frac{y}{r} \cdot \frac{x}{r} = \left(-\frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}\right)\left(-\frac{1}{\sqrt{5}}\right) = \frac{2}{5}. $$
