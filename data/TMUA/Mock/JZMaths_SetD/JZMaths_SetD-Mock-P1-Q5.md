## 题目
Find the area of the region in the $(x, y)$-plane consisting of all points satisfying both
$$ |x| + |y| \le 2 \quad \text{and} \quad |x - 1| + |y| \le 2. $$
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 3 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{9}{4} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{7}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 4 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{9}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 6 $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \frac{15}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{H} \quad 8 $$

## 解析
The inequality $|x| + |y| \le 2$ describes a diamond centred at $(0, 0)$, while $|x - 1| + |y| \le 2$ describes an identical diamond centred at $(1, 0)$.

Sketch out the two diamonds, then you will see that their common region is also a diamond. Its leftmost and rightmost vertices are $(-1, 0)$ and $(2, 0)$, so its horizontal diagonal has length $3$.

Its upper and lower vertices are
$$ \left(\frac{1}{2}, \frac{3}{2}\right) \quad \text{and} \quad \left(\frac{1}{2}, -\frac{3}{2}\right), $$
so its vertical diagonal also has length $3$.

Therefore, the area of the common region is
$$ \frac{1}{2} \cdot 3 \cdot 3 = \frac{9}{2}. $$
