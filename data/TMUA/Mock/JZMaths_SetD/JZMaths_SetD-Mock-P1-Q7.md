## 题目
Find the value of
$$ \sum_{r=1}^{100} \frac{1}{\sqrt{r} + \sqrt{r - 1}}. $$
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 9 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 10 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 11 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 20 $$

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Rationalising each term gives
$$ \frac{1}{\sqrt{r} + \sqrt{r - 1}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{r} + \sqrt{r - 1}} \times \frac{\sqrt{r} - \sqrt{r - 1}}{\sqrt{r} - \sqrt{r - 1}} = \sqrt{r} - \sqrt{r - 1}. $$

Therefore,
$$ \sum_{r=1}^{100} \frac{1}{\sqrt{r} + \sqrt{r - 1}} = \sum_{r=1}^{100} \left(\sqrt{r} - \sqrt{r - 1}\right) $$
$$ = \sqrt{100} - \sqrt{0} $$
$$ = 10. $$
