## 题目

$$ \int_{0}^{n} f(\{x\} + 1) \,dx $$
where $n$ is a positive integer.

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{425n}{13}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{1}{2}n(n + 1)
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{83n}{12}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{437n}{12}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 15n^2 + 4n + 8
$$
