## 题目
Find the sum of all real values of $z$ for which there exist positive real numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfying the simultaneous equations

$$ \log_2(x^2yz) = 3, \qquad \log_2(xz) = 1, \qquad (\log_2 y)(\log_2 z) = 2. $$

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 3 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{9}{4} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{9}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 7 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 8 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 9 $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \frac{63}{4} $$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Let $X = \log_2 x$, $Y = \log_2 y$, $Z = \log_2 z$. The three equations become

$$ 2X + Y + Z = 3, \qquad X + Z = 1, \qquad YZ = 2. $$

From the second equation, $X = 1 - Z$. Substituting into the first gives

$$ 2(1 - Z) + Y + Z = 3 \quad \text{or} \quad Y = 1 + Z. $$

Sub into the third equation: $(1 + Z)Z = 2 \Leftrightarrow (Z + 2)(Z - 1) = 0$ and $Z \in \{-2, 1\}$, these gives $z = \frac{1}{4}, 2$, and so sum of these is $\frac{9}{4}$.
