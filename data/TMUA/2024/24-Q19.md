## 题目
The recursive sequence $(a_n)$ satisfies

$$a_{n+1}=\frac{a_n+3}{2-a_n}.$$

Given $a_{101}=1$, find $a_{99}$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
$-8$

## 解析
The recurrence can be run backwards, because each term determines the previous one uniquely: clearing the fraction in $a_{n+1}(2-a_n)=a_n+3$ and collecting the $a_n$ terms gives

$$a_n=\frac{2a_{n+1}-3}{a_{n+1}+1}.$$

Applying this twice from $a_{101}=1$,

$$a_{100}=\frac{2\cdot1-3}{1+1}=-\frac12,\qquad
a_{99}=\frac{2\left(-\frac12\right)-3}{-\frac12+1}=\frac{-4}{\frac12}=-8.$$

Running the recurrence forwards confirms the chain: $\dfrac{-8+3}{2-(-8)}=-\dfrac12$ and $\dfrac{-\frac12+3}{2+\frac12}=1$.

So the answer is $-8$.
