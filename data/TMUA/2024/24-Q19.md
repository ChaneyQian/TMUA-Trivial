## 题目
The recursive sequence $(a_n)$ satisfies

$$a_{n+1}=\frac{a_n+3}{2-a_n}.$$

Given $a_{101}=1$, find $a_{99}$.

## 解析
From $1=(a_{100}+3)/(2-a_{100})$, we obtain $a_{100}=-\tfrac12$. Then

$$-\frac12=\frac{a_{99}+3}{2-a_{99}},$$

so $a_{99}=-8$.

ANSWER: $-8$
