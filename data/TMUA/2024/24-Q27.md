## 题目
Find the region satisfying both inequalities

$$
\begin{aligned}
(x+y)^2-8(x+y)+8&<0,\\
x^2+y^2-2xy+4x-4y+3&<0.
\end{aligned}
$$

## 解析
The first inequality gives

$$4-2\sqrt2<x+y<4+2\sqrt2,$$

while the second is $(x-y)^2+4(x-y)+3<0$, hence

$$-3<x-y<-1.$$

Therefore the required region is the open parallelogram bounded by the four lines

$$x+y=4\pm2\sqrt2\qquad\text{and}\qquad x-y=-3,-1.$$

ANSWER: $4-2\sqrt2<x+y<4+2\sqrt2$ and $-3<x-y<-1$
