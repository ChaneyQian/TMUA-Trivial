## 题目
Find all integer pairs $(x,y)$ satisfying

$$\log(x+y)=\log x+\log y.$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
$(2,2)$

## 解析
First fix the domain: $\log x$ and $\log y$ require $x>0$ and $y>0$ (and then $x+y>0$ automatically). Using $\log x+\log y=\log(xy)$ and the fact that $\log$ is one-to-one,

$$\log(x+y)=\log(xy)\iff x+y=xy.$$

Rearranging and factorising,

$$xy-x-y+1=1\iff(x-1)(y-1)=1.$$

For integers, the only factorisations of $1$ are $1\times1$ and $(-1)\times(-1)$. The first gives $x=y=2$; the second gives $x=y=0$, which is rejected because the logarithms are then undefined.

Checking the surviving pair: $\log(2+2)=\log4=\log 2+\log 2$, as required.

So the answer is $(x,y)=(2,2)$.
