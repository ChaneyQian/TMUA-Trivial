## 题目
Given the following equations: $y = ax^{2} + b$ and $x^{2} + y^{2} = 1$.

Let P and Q be the intersection points of the circle and the quadratic function.

If $ab = -\frac{5}{2}$, find the value of $a + b$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{5}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{1}{6}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad -\frac{3}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{9}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \pm\frac{3}{2}$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
On the circle $x^2=1-y^2$, so substituting into $y=ax^2+b$ eliminates $x$:

$$y=a(1-y^2)+b\implies ay^2+y-(a+b)=0.$$

A root $y_0$ of this quadratic with $|y_0|<1$ gives the pair of intersection points $\left(\pm\sqrt{1-y_0^2},\,y_0\right)$; the curves *touch* there rather than crossing precisely when the root is repeated. The diagram shows the parabola resting on the circle at $P$ and $Q$, so the discriminant vanishes:

$$1+4a(a+b)=0\implies 4a^2+4ab+1=0.$$

Substituting $ab=-\dfrac52$ gives $4a^2-10+1=0$, so $a^2=\dfrac94$ and $a=\pm\dfrac32$. The parabola in the diagram opens downwards, so $a=-\dfrac32$, and then $b=\dfrac{-5/2}{a}=\dfrac53$. Hence

$$a+b=-\frac32+\frac53=\frac16.$$

Check: with $y=-\frac32x^2+\frac53$ the quadratic above becomes $-\frac32y^2+y-\frac16=0$, i.e. $(3y-1)^2=0$, so the contact height is $y=\frac13$ and the contact points are $\left(\pm\frac{2\sqrt2}{3},\frac13\right)$, exactly as drawn (the vertex sits at $\frac53\approx1.67$, just above the circle).

The sign $a=+\frac32$ would give $a+b=-\frac16$, so option C is the trap for ignoring the orientation shown in the diagram.

The answer is B.
