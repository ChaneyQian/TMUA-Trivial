## 题目
Let $f(x)$ be the greatest integer less than or equal to $x$

eg. $f(4) = f(4.9) = 4$

Evaluate $\int_{0}^{50} \lfloor \sqrt{x} \rfloor \, dx$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 195$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 203$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 210$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 217$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The integrand is a step function: $\lfloor\sqrt x\rfloor=k$ exactly when $k^2\le x<(k+1)^2$, an interval of length $2k+1$. Since $7^2=49<50<64$, the last step is only partial: on $49\le x\le50$ the integrand equals $7$ over a stretch of length $1$.

Splitting the integral,

$$\int_0^{50}\lfloor\sqrt x\rfloor\,dx=\sum_{k=0}^{6}k\big((k+1)^2-k^2\big)+7\cdot(50-49)=\sum_{k=1}^{6}k(2k+1)+7 .$$

Now

$$\sum_{k=1}^{6}(2k^2+k)=2\cdot91+21=203,$$

using $1^2+\cdots+6^2=91$ and $1+\cdots+6=21$. Hence

$$\int_0^{50}\lfloor\sqrt x\rfloor\,dx=203+7=210 .$$

Stopping at $x=49$ and forgetting the final strip gives $203$ (option B). The answer is C.
