## 题目
The parabola $y=5-x^2$ meets the coordinate axes at three points. Find the equation of the circle through these three points.

## 解析
The three intercepts are $(0,5)$ and $(\pm\sqrt5,0)$. Symmetry places the centre on the $y$-axis; solving for the centre and radius gives centre $(0,2)$ and radius $3$.

ANSWER: $x^2+(y-2)^2=9$
