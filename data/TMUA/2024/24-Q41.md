## 题目
The parabola $y=5-x^2$ meets the coordinate axes at three points. Find the equation of the circle through these three points.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
$x^2+(y-2)^2=9$

## 解析
First find the three points. Setting $x=0$ gives the $y$-intercept $(0,5)$, and setting $y=0$ gives $x^2=5$, so the $x$-intercepts are $(\sqrt5,0)$ and $(-\sqrt5,0)$.

The two $x$-intercepts are symmetric about the $y$-axis, so the centre of the circle lies on the $y$-axis; write the equation as

$$x^2+(y-k)^2=r^2.$$

Substituting $(\sqrt5,0)$ gives $5+k^2=r^2$, and substituting $(0,5)$ gives $(5-k)^2=r^2$. Hence

$$25-10k+k^2=5+k^2\quad\Longrightarrow\quad k=2,$$

and then $r^2=5+4=9$.

Checking all three points: $5+(0-2)^2=9$ and $0+(5-2)^2=9$, as required.

So the answer is $x^2+(y-2)^2=9$, a circle with centre $(0,2)$ and radius $3$.
