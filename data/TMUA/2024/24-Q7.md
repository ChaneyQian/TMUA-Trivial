## 题目
A car starts from depot and moves repeatedly between 4 stations in the following pattern: 1, 2, 3, 4, 3, 2, 1, 2, 3, ...It follows the below statements:

Statement 1: If the car has stopped at Station 1 a total of 10 times, it must have stopped at Station 2 exactly 20 times.

Statement 2: If the car has stopped at Station 3 a total of 10 times, it must have stopped at Station 4 exactly 5 times.

Statement 3: If the car has stopped at Station 2 a total of 10 times, it must have stopped at Station 3 exactly 10 times.

Determine which of the following statements are correct.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{Only statement 1}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{Only statement 2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{Only statement 3}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{Statement 2 and 3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{Statement 1 and 2}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{Statement 1 and 3}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \text{All statements}$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
The route is periodic with period $6$:

$$\underbrace{1,\,2,\,3,\,4,\,3,\,2}_{\text{one cycle}},\ \underbrace{1,\,2,\,3,\,4,\,3,\,2}_{\text{one cycle}},\ 1,\dots$$

In each complete cycle the car stops once at Station $1$, twice at Station $2$, twice at Station $3$ and once at Station $4$. The subtlety is that the journey may be observed at **any** moment, not only at the end of a cycle, so a statement is true only if the stated count is forced at every such moment.

**Statement 1 is false.** The $10$th stop at Station $1$ is the $55$th stop, and the first $54$ stops form exactly $9$ complete cycles containing $9\times2=18$ stops at Station $2$. So at that moment the count at Station $2$ is $18$, not $20$; as the journey continues the count at Station $1$ stays at $10$ for the stops $55$–$60$, during which the Station $2$ count runs $18,19,19,19,19,20$. Hence "exactly $20$" is not forced.

**Statement 2 is true.** Stops at Station $3$ come in pairs, two per cycle, so the $10$th of them is the second Station‑$3$ stop of the $5$th cycle, i.e. stop $29$; the $5$th stop at Station $4$ is stop $28$, and the next is stop $34$. The Station‑$3$ count stays at $10$ only for stops $29$–$32$, and throughout that window the Station‑$4$ count is exactly $5$.

**Statement 3 is true.** Similarly the $10$th stop at Station $2$ is stop $30$, and the count stays at $10$ for stops $30$–$31$ (the next $2$ is stop $32$). The $10$th stop at Station $3$ is stop $29$ and the next is stop $33$, so at stops $30$ and $31$ the Station‑$3$ count is exactly $10$.

So statements 2 and 3 only. The answer is D.
