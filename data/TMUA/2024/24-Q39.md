## 题目
Find the number of positive integer solutions for the equation $n$ which satisfy $((n-1)!)! = (n-1)!$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 5$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 6$$

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Put $m=(n-1)!$, so the equation becomes $m!=m$ and we first solve this for the non-negative integer $m$.

- $m=0$: $0!=1\neq0$.
- $m=1$: $1!=1$, so this works.
- $m=2$: $2!=2$, so this works.
- $m\ge3$: $m!=m\cdot(m-1)!\ge2m>m$, so no solutions.

Hence $m!=m$ exactly when $m=1$ or $m=2$, i.e. $(n-1)!=1$ or $(n-1)!=2$.

$(n-1)!=1$ gives $n-1=0$ or $n-1=1$, so $n=1$ or $n=2$; $(n-1)!=2$ gives $n-1=2$, so $n=3$. All three are positive integers, and checking directly: $(0!)!=1=0!$, $(1!)!=1=1!$, $(2!)!=2=2!$.

So there are exactly $3$ positive integer solutions, $n=1,2,3$. (Forgetting $0!=1$ loses $n=1$ and leads to the answer $2$.)

The answer is D.
