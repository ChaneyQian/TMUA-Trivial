## 题目
Find the coefficient of $x^2$ in the expansion of

$$(x^2+2x-3)^5.$$

## 解析
The $x^2$ term arises either by choosing two factors of $2x$, or by choosing one factor of $x^2$:

$$\binom52(2)^2(-3)^3+\binom51(-3)^4=-1080+405=-675.$$

ANSWER: $-675$
