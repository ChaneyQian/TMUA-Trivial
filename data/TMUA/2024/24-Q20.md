## 题目
Find the coefficient of $x^2$ in the expansion of

$$(x^2+2x-3)^5.$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
$-675$

## 解析
Think of $(x^2+2x-3)^5$ as five identical brackets, from each of which we take $x^2$, $2x$ or $-3$. The chosen degrees must add up to $2$, so either one bracket contributes $x^2$ and the other four contribute $-3$, or two brackets contribute $2x$ and the other three contribute $-3$.

$$\binom51(-3)^4=5\times81=405,\qquad
\binom52(2)^2(-3)^3=10\times4\times(-27)=-1080.$$

Adding the two contributions gives $405-1080=-675$.

As a check, $x^2+2x-3=(x+3)(x-1)$, so the expression is $(x+3)^5(x-1)^5$; the low-order coefficients are $243+405x+270x^2+\cdots$ and $-1+5x-10x^2+\cdots$, and

$$243(-10)+405(5)+270(-1)=-2430+2025-270=-675.$$

So the answer is $-675$.
