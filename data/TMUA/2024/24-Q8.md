## 题目
Evaluate

$$\int_{\sqrt{2}}^{\sqrt{3}}\left(x^2+\frac{1}{x^2}\right)\,dx.$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
$\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{6}$

## 解析
Write the integrand as a sum of powers, $x^2+x^{-2}$, and integrate term by term:

$$\int\left(x^2+\frac{1}{x^2}\right)dx=\frac{x^3}{3}-\frac1x+c .$$

Evaluating between the limits, and using $\sqrt3^{\,3}=3\sqrt3$, $\sqrt2^{\,3}=2\sqrt2$ together with $\dfrac1{\sqrt3}=\dfrac{\sqrt3}{3}$ and $\dfrac1{\sqrt2}=\dfrac{\sqrt2}{2}$,

$$\begin{aligned}
\left[\frac{x^3}{3}-\frac1x\right]_{\sqrt2}^{\sqrt3}
&=\left(\frac{3\sqrt3}{3}-\frac{\sqrt3}{3}\right)-\left(\frac{2\sqrt2}{3}-\frac{\sqrt2}{2}\right)\\
&=\frac{2\sqrt3}{3}-\frac{4\sqrt2-3\sqrt2}{6}\\
&=\frac{2\sqrt3}{3}-\frac{\sqrt2}{6}.
\end{aligned}$$

Numerically this is about $1.1547-0.2357=0.919$, which agrees with the fact that the integrand is near $2.4$ over an interval of length $\sqrt3-\sqrt2\approx0.318$.

So the answer is $\dfrac{2\sqrt3}{3}-\dfrac{\sqrt2}{6}$.
