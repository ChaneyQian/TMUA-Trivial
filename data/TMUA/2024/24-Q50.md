## 题目
Find all values of $a$ for which

$$\int_a^{2a}(x+2)\,dx<4(a+4).$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
$-\dfrac83<a<4$

## 解析
Evaluate the integral as a function of $a$ and then solve the resulting quadratic inequality.

$$\int_a^{2a}(x+2)\,\mathrm{d}x=\left[\frac{x^2}{2}+2x\right]_a^{2a}=\left(2a^2+4a\right)-\left(\frac{a^2}{2}+2a\right)=\frac32a^2+2a.$$

(This is valid for every real $a$; no sign assumption is needed.) The inequality becomes

$$\frac32a^2+2a<4a+16,$$

and multiplying by $2$ and collecting terms,

$$3a^2-4a-32<0.$$

The roots of $3a^2-4a-32=0$ are

$$a=\frac{4\pm\sqrt{16+384}}{6}=\frac{4\pm20}{6}=4\ \text{ or }\ -\frac83,$$

so the quadratic factorises as $(3a+8)(a-4)<0$. As the leading coefficient is positive, the expression is negative strictly between the roots.

So the answer is $-\dfrac83<a<4$.
