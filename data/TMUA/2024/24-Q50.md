## 题目
Find all values of $a$ for which

$$\int_a^{2a}(x+2)\,dx<4(a+4).$$

## 解析
$$\frac32a^2+2a<4a+16
\iff3a^2-4a-32<0
\iff(3a+8)(a-4)<0.$$
