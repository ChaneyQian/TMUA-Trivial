## 题目
Let P be the statement "$f(x)$ is divisible by $(x+2)(x-3)$" and Q be the statement "$f(x)$ is divisible by $4x^{2} - 4x - 24$".

Determine what type of condition P is for Q.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{a sufficient condition for Q}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{a sufficient but not necessary condition for Q}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{a necessary and sufficient condition for Q}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{neither a sufficient nor a necessary condition for Q}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{Q is a necessary condition for P}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{P is a sufficient and necessary condition for Q}$$
