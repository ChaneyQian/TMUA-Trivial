## 题目
The lines $x=1$, $y=1$, and the coordinate axes enclose a square. The parabolas

$$y=x^2\qquad\text{and}\qquad y=\frac12x^2+bx-\frac12$$

divide the square into three parts. Find the range of values of $b$.

## 解析
The second parabola passes through $(0,-\tfrac12)$. For it to cross the right side of the square strictly above $(1,0)$ and no higher than $(1,1)$, we require

$$0<\frac12+b-\frac12\le1.$$

ANSWER: $0<b\le1$
