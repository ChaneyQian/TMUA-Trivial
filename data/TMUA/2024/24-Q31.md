## 题目
A square with a side length of 1 contains an inscribed equilateral triangle.

Find the area of the equilateral triangle.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{2\sqrt{3} - 3}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 2\sqrt{3} - 3$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{3 - \sqrt{3}}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 1 - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}$$
