## 题目
Let $O$ be the center of a circle, and $M$ be a point outside the circle. Two tangents are drawn from $M$ to the circle $O$, touching the circle at points $A$ and $B$. The line segment $AB$ is connected, and $MO$ intersects $AB$ at point P. It is given that $OP = y$, $OM = 10y$ and $AB = 2y + 6$.

Find the area of the $S_{OBMA}$.

## 答案

## 解析
The key is that the two tangent lengths are equal, so $OAMB$ is a kite whose axis of symmetry is $OM$. Hence the diagonals $OM$ and $AB$ are perpendicular, $P$ is the midpoint of $AB$, and

$$AP=\frac{AB}{2}=y+3.$$

A tangent is perpendicular to the radius at the point of contact, so triangle $OAM$ has a right angle at $A$ and $AP$ is the altitude from $A$ to the hypotenuse $OM$. The relation $OA^2=OP\cdot OM$ therefore gives

$$OA^2=y\cdot 10y=10y^2 .$$

By Pythagoras in triangle $OAP$,

$$AP^2=OA^2-OP^2=10y^2-y^2=9y^2,$$

so $AP=3y$. Comparing the two expressions for $AP$,

$$3y=y+3\quad\Longrightarrow\quad y=\frac32 .$$

Thus $AB=2y+6=9$ and $OM=10y=15$. (As a check, $OA^2=10y^2=22.5$, and $AP=4.5<\sqrt{22.5}$, so the configuration really exists.)

Since the diagonals of $OAMB$ are perpendicular, its area is half the product of the diagonals:

$$S_{OBMA}=\frac12\cdot OM\cdot AB=\frac12\cdot15\cdot9=\frac{135}{2}.$$

A common slip is to forget the factor $\tfrac12$, which gives $135$ (option D). The answer is E.
