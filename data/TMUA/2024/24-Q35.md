## 题目
Find the number of points $(x, y)$ where $x$ and $y$ are integers, such that $y \geq |x|$ and $y + |x| \leq 20$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 200$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 210$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 220$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 221$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 235$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 240$$

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The two conditions trap $y$ between two values depending only on $|x|$:

$$|x|\le y\le20-|x|.$$

Such a $y$ exists only if $|x|\le20-|x|$, that is $|x|\le10$, and then the number of integers $y$ in the range is

$$(20-|x|)-|x|+1=21-2|x|.$$

Summing over the integers $x=-10,-9,\dots,10$, and pairing $x$ with $-x$,

$$21+2\sum_{k=1}^{10}(21-2k)=21+2(19+17+15+\cdots+1)=21+2\times100=221,$$

using that the sum of the first ten odd numbers is $10^2=100$.

As a check, the region is the square with vertices $(0,0)$, $(10,10)$, $(0,20)$, $(-10,10)$, of area $200$; each of its four edges carries $10$ lattice segments, so there are $B=40$ boundary points, and Pick's theorem gives $I=200-\frac{40}{2}+1=181$ interior points, i.e. $181+40=221$ in total. Option C, $220$, is what one gets by losing the single point $(0,0)$ or $(0,20)$.

The answer is D.
