## 题目
Given that $f(x) = ax^{2} + bx + c$, which of the following is a sufficient condition for $f(|x|) = k$ to have 4 different real number solutions?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad b^{2} - 4ac < 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad b = 0$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad ab < 0$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad a > 0$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad b^{2} > 4ac$$

ℹ️ 2026-09-14 题干漏了 k 的量词：对固定 k，ab<0 不充分（a=1,b=−1,c=0,k=100 只有 2 个实根）；读成「存在 k 使 f(|x|)=k 有 4 个相异实根」时 ab<0 才是等价条件，逐项排除后 C 仍唯一。
## 答案
C

## 解析
Since $f(|x|)$ depends on $x$ only through $t=|x|\ge0$, substitute $t=|x|$ and study

$$at^2+bt+(c-k)=0.$$

Each root $t>0$ contributes two solutions $x=\pm t$, while $t=0$ contributes only one and $t<0$ contributes none. So $f(|x|)=k$ has four distinct solutions precisely when this quadratic in $t$ has two distinct **positive** roots, which requires

$$b^2-4a(c-k)>0,\qquad -\frac{b}{a}>0,\qquad \frac{c-k}{a}>0.$$

The middle condition is $ab<0$, and it is the one condition here that does not involve $k$: the vertex of the parabola $t\mapsto at^2+bt+c$ must lie at $t=-\tfrac{b}{2a}>0$. So $ab<0$ is necessary, and it is also enough to guarantee that a suitable $k$ exists: if, say, $a>0$ and $b<0$, the function $g(t)=at^2+bt+c$ decreases on $\left[0,-\tfrac{b}{2a}\right]$ and increases afterwards, so for every $k$ with

$$c-\frac{b^2}{4a}<k<c$$

the horizontal line $y=k$ meets the graph of $g$ twice with $t>0$, giving four values of $x$. (For example $a=1$, $b=-4$, $c=0$, $k=-3$ gives $t=1,3$ and the four solutions $x=\pm1,\pm3$.) The case $a<0$, $b>0$ is the mirror image.

Each of the other options fails because it permits $b=0$, where the quadratic in $t$ has at most one positive root and hence at most two solutions in $x$:

- **B** ($b=0$) is exactly this bad case: $at^2+c=k$ gives a single admissible $t$, so only two solutions.
- **A** ($b^2-4ac<0$), **D** ($a>0$) and **E** ($b^2>4ac$) are all satisfied by examples with $b=0$ (e.g. $a=1,b=0,c=1$ for A and D, and $a=1,b=0,c=-1$ for D and E), so none of them is sufficient.

The answer is C.
