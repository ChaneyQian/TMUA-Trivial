## 题目
Given the sequence $u_{n+1} = 3u_{n} - 6$ with $u_{n} \in \mathbb{Z}$, which of the following statements is true?

	1. If $6 \mid u_{6}$, then $3 \mid u_{3}$.
	2. If $10 \mid u_{10}$, then $30 \mid u_{30}$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{Only statement 1}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{Only statement 2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{Both statements}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{Neither statement}$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
The fixed point of the map is $3$, so subtract it: $u_{n+1}-3=3(u_n-3)$, and iterating,

$$u_m-3=3^{\,m-n}(u_n-3)\qquad(m>n).$$

**Statement 1.** Every term produced by the recurrence satisfies $u_{n+1}=3(u_n-2)$, so every term after the first is a multiple of $3$. In particular $3\mid u_3$ whatever $u_6$ does, so the implication holds (its hypothesis is not even needed). True.

**Statement 2.** Suppose $10\mid u_{10}$. Taking $m=30$, $n=10$ above,

$$u_{30}=3+3^{20}(u_{10}-3).$$

Since $3^4=81\equiv1\pmod{10}$ we have $3^{20}\equiv1\pmod{10}$, so

$$u_{30}\equiv3+(u_{10}-3)=u_{10}\equiv0\pmod{10}.$$

Also $u_{30}=3+3^{20}(u_{10}-3)$ is visibly a multiple of $3$. As $\gcd(3,10)=1$, it follows that $30\mid u_{30}$. True.

Both statements hold.

The answer is C.
