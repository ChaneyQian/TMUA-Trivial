## 题目
Point $S$ lies on the line $y = \frac{1}{2}x$ with $OS = 2\sqrt{15}$. Point T lies on the $x$-axis, with $ST = 4$, and $\angle OTS > \angle OST$. Find the area of $\triangle OST$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 6 - \sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 12 + 2\sqrt{3}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 12$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 8\sqrt{3} - 2$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 12 - 2\sqrt{3}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Put $S=\left(s,\tfrac12 s\right)$ on the line $y=\tfrac12x$. Then

$$OS^2=s^2+\frac{s^2}{4}=\frac{5s^2}{4}=60\quad\Longrightarrow\quad s^2=48,\ s=4\sqrt3$$

(taking $S$ in the first quadrant; the other choice is a reflection and gives the same areas). So $S=(4\sqrt3,\,2\sqrt3)$ and the distance from $S$ to the $x$-axis is $2\sqrt3$.

Let $T=(t,0)$. From $ST=4$,

$$(t-4\sqrt3)^2+(2\sqrt3)^2=16\quad\Longrightarrow\quad (t-4\sqrt3)^2=4\quad\Longrightarrow\quad t=4\sqrt3\pm2 .$$

Both positions give a genuine triangle, so the angle condition must decide between them. In a triangle the larger angle faces the longer side: $\angle OTS$ faces $OS$ and $\angle OST$ faces $OT$, so

$$\angle OTS>\angle OST\iff OS>OT\iff t<2\sqrt{15}\approx7.75 .$$

Since $4\sqrt3+2\approx8.93$ and $4\sqrt3-2\approx4.93$, we must take $t=4\sqrt3-2$.

Taking $OT$ as base and $2\sqrt3$ as height,

$$[OST]=\frac12\left(4\sqrt3-2\right)\left(2\sqrt3\right)=\sqrt3\left(4\sqrt3-2\right)=12-2\sqrt3 .$$

The other root gives $12+2\sqrt3$ (option B), which is the trap for ignoring the angle condition. The answer is E.
