## 题目
Find the area enclosed by the curves

$y = p|x| - p$ and $y = -\frac{1}{p}|x| + p$

when $p$ is a positive constant.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{4p^{3}}{p^{2}+1}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{2p^{3}}{p^{2}+1}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{8p^{3}}{p^{2}+1}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{4p^{3}}{p-1}$$

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Both graphs are symmetric about the $y$-axis. The first is a "V" with vertex $(0,-p)$ and arms of gradient $\pm p$; the second is an inverted "V" with vertex $(0,p)$ and arms of gradient $\mp\frac1p$. The enclosed region is therefore a kite with vertices $(0,-p)$, $(0,p)$ and the two crossing points.

For $x>0$ the arms meet where

$$px-p=-\frac xp+p\implies x\left(p+\frac1p\right)=2p\implies x=\frac{2p^2}{p^2+1},$$

and by symmetry the other crossing is at $x=-\dfrac{2p^2}{p^2+1}$, at the same height

$$y=p\left(\frac{2p^2}{p^2+1}-1\right)=\frac{p(p^2-1)}{p^2+1},$$

which lies strictly between $-p$ and $p$, so the two vertical vertices really are opposite corners of the kite.

The diagonals are the vertical segment from $(0,-p)$ to $(0,p)$, of length $2p$, and the horizontal segment joining the crossing points, of length $\dfrac{4p^2}{p^2+1}$. They are perpendicular, so

$$\text{area}=\frac12\times2p\times\frac{4p^2}{p^2+1}=\frac{4p^3}{p^2+1}.$$

(Halving this, as one does by forgetting that the kite has two halves, gives option B.)

The answer is A.
