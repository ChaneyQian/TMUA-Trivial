## 题目
A cylinder has initial volume $V_1$. Its radius is increased by $x\%$ and its height is decreased by $x\%$, where $0\le x\le100$, giving a new volume $V_2$.

Which statement is true?

$$\mathbf{A}\quad V_1=V_2$$

$$\mathbf{B}\quad V_1>V_2$$

$$\mathbf{C}\quad V_1<V_2$$

$$\mathbf{D}\quad \text{The relationship depends on }x.$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Write $t=\dfrac{x}{100}$, so $0\le t\le1$. The radius becomes $r(1+t)$ and the height $h(1-t)$, so

$$\frac{V_2}{V_1}=\frac{\pi r^2(1+t)^2\,h(1-t)}{\pi r^2h}=(1+t)^2(1-t).$$

Expanding,

$$(1+t)^2(1-t)=1+t-t^2-t^3=1+t\left(1-t-t^2\right),$$

so for $0<t<1$ the sign of $V_2-V_1$ is the sign of $1-t-t^2$. This vanishes at $t=\dfrac{\sqrt5-1}{2}\approx0.618$, is positive below it and negative above it. Hence

$$\begin{aligned}
0<x<61.8\ldots &\;\Rightarrow\; V_2>V_1 \quad(\text{e.g. }x=30:\ V_2/V_1=1.183),\\
61.8\ldots<x\le100 &\;\Rightarrow\; V_2<V_1 \quad(\text{e.g. }x=90:\ V_2/V_1=0.361),
\end{aligned}$$

with equality only at $x=0$ and $x=61.8\ldots$.

So no one ordering holds for all admissible $x$: the comparison depends on $x$. (Answering B comes from assuming the squared increase is always outweighed by the decrease, which is false for small $x$.) The answer is D.
