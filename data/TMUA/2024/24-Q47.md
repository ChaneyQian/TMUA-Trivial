## 题目
Find the number of intersection points between $y^{2} - x^{2} = 0$ and $y = x^{3} - x^{2} - 8x + 14$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 6$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 8$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
The first equation factorises, $y^2-x^2=(y-x)(y+x)=0$, so it is not a curve but the pair of lines $y=x$ and $y=-x$. Count the intersections with the cubic on each line.

**On $y=x$:**

$$x^3-x^2-8x+14=x\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x^3-x^2-9x+14=0.$$

Here $x=2$ is a root ($8-4-18+14=0$), and dividing out gives

$$(x-2)(x^2+x-7)=0.$$

The quadratic has discriminant $1+28=29>0$, so it has two further real roots $x=\tfrac{-1\pm\sqrt{29}}{2}\approx2.193,\,-3.193$, neither equal to $2$. That is $3$ distinct points.

**On $y=-x$:**

$$x^3-x^2-8x+14=-x\quad\Longrightarrow\quad x^3-x^2-7x+14=0.$$

Let $g(x)=x^3-x^2-7x+14$. Then $g'(x)=3x^2-2x-7=0$ at $x=\tfrac{1\pm\sqrt{22}}{3}$, i.e. $x\approx-1.230$ (local maximum) and $x\approx1.897$ (local minimum). Since $g(1.897)\approx3.95>0$, the local minimum lies above the $x$-axis, so $g$ crosses the axis only once, on the decreasing-to-the-left branch ($g(-3)=-1<0<g(-2.9)$, so the root is near $x\approx-2.95$). That is $1$ point.

The two lines meet only at the origin, which is not on the cubic ($g(0)=14\neq0$), so no point is counted twice. The total is $3+1=4$.

The answer is C.
