## 题目
Given that $0 \leq f(x) \leq 1$ for $0 < x < 4$, and that:

$$\int_{0}^{2} f(x)\,dx = 1 \quad \text{and} \quad \int_{1}^{3} f(x)\,dx = 1.5$$

Which of the following are possible values for $\int_{0}^{4} f(x)\,dx$?

	I: 2.4
	II: 2.8
	III: 3.2

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{Only I}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{Only II}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{Only III}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{I and II}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{I and III}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{II and III}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \text{All of them}$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Cut $[0,4]$ into unit pieces and write

$$A=\int_0^1 f,\qquad B=\int_1^2 f,\qquad C=\int_2^3 f,\qquad D=\int_3^4 f .$$

Since $0\le f\le1$, each of $A,B,C,D$ lies in $[0,1]$, and conversely any such quadruple is realised by a function that is constant on each unit interval. The two given conditions say

$$A+B=1,\qquad B+C=1.5 .$$

So $B=1-A$ and $C=0.5+A$. The constraint $C\le1$ forces $0\le A\le0.5$. The quantity asked for is

$$\int_0^4 f=A+B+C+D=1+C+D=1.5+A+D,\qquad 0\le A\le\tfrac12,\ 0\le D\le1 .$$

Hence the attainable values are exactly

$$1.5\le\int_0^4 f\le 3 .$$

- **I ($2.4$):** possible, e.g. $A=0.4,\ B=0.6,\ C=0.9,\ D=0.5$.
- **II ($2.8$):** possible, e.g. $A=0.5,\ B=0.5,\ C=1,\ D=0.8$.
- **III ($3.2$):** impossible, since the maximum is $1.5+0.5+1=3$.

So I and II only. The answer is D.
