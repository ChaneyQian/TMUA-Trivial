## 题目
For positive integers $a$, $b$ and $c$, define three least common multiples: $L = \mathrm{lcm}(a, b)$, $M = \mathrm{lcm}(b, c)$, and $N = \mathrm{lcm}(a, c)$. Which of these is not possible?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad L < M < N$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad L < M = N$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad L = M < N$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad L = M = N$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{All of them are possible}$$
