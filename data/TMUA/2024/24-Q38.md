## 题目
Which of the following is a necessary but insufficient condition for $\sin x > \frac{1}{2}$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \cos x = 0$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{1}{2}\sin x < \frac{1}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \tan\frac{x}{2} = 1$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \cos x > \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \sin^{2} x > \frac{9}{4}$$
