## 题目
In the semicircle shown below, $\angle PQR=25^\circ$ and $\angle RQT=18^\circ$. Find $\angle T$.

![[Image/24-Q32-fig1.png]]

## 答案
$29^\circ$

## 解析
In the diagram $QH$ is the diameter of the semicircle, $P$ and $R$ lie on the arc, and $T$ is the point where the line $PR$ produced meets the line $QH$ produced. The angle asked for is $\angle QTP$, so work in triangle $QPT$.

At $Q$: the ray $QT$ runs along the diameter, so

$$\angle PQT=\angle PQR+\angle RQT=25^\circ+18^\circ=43^\circ.$$

At $P$: the angle in a semicircle gives $\angle QPH=90^\circ$, and the angles subtended by the chord $RH$ at the two circumference points $P$ and $Q$ are equal, so

$$\angle HPR=\angle HQR=18^\circ.$$

Since $R$ lies on the arc between $P$ and $H$, it is on the far side of the chord $PH$ from $Q$, so these two angles add rather than subtract:

$$\angle QPT=\angle QPH+\angle HPR=90^\circ+18^\circ=108^\circ.$$

The angle sum of triangle $QPT$ now gives

$$\angle T=180^\circ-43^\circ-108^\circ=29^\circ.$$

As a check, triangle $PQR$ gives $\angle PRQ=180^\circ-25^\circ-108^\circ=47^\circ$ (consistent with $\angle PRQ$ being the inscribed angle on the arc $QP$, whose central angle is $180^\circ-2\times43^\circ=94^\circ$), so $\angle QRT=180^\circ-47^\circ=133^\circ$ and triangle $QRT$ gives $180^\circ-18^\circ-133^\circ=29^\circ$ as well.

So the answer is $29^\circ$.
