## 题目
In the semicircle shown below, $\angle PQR=25^\circ$ and $\angle RQT=18^\circ$. Find $\angle T$.

## 解析
Let $H$ be the right-hand endpoint of the diameter. Since $QH$ is a diameter, $\angle QPH=90^\circ$. The inscribed-angle theorem gives $\angle RQH=\angle RPH=18^\circ$. Therefore

$$\angle T=180^\circ-25^\circ-18^\circ-90^\circ-18^\circ=29^\circ.$$

ANSWER: $29^\circ$
