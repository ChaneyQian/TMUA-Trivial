## 题目
ABCD is a cyclic quadrilateral.

The lengths of its sides are $AB = x$, $BC = 2x + 1$, $CD = 3x + 8$, and $DA = 4x - 1$.

Angle $DAB$ is a right angle.

Find the value of $x$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 8$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 12$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 16$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 18$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 22$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 32$$

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
In a cyclic quadrilateral opposite angles are supplementary, so $\angle BCD=180^\circ-\angle DAB=90^\circ$. The diagonal $BD$ therefore subtends a right angle in both triangles $ABD$ and $CBD$, and Pythagoras can be applied twice to the same length:

$$BD^2=AB^2+DA^2=x^2+(4x-1)^2,$$

$$BD^2=BC^2+CD^2=(2x+1)^2+(3x+8)^2.$$

Equating the two expressions,

$$\begin{aligned}
17x^2-8x+1&=13x^2+52x+65\\
4x^2-60x-64&=0\\
x^2-15x-16&=0\\
(x-16)(x+1)&=0.
\end{aligned}$$

Since the sides must be positive, $x=-1$ is rejected and $x=16$. Checking: the sides are $16,33,56,63$, and indeed $16^2+63^2=4225=33^2+56^2$, with $BD=65$.

The answer is C.
