## 题目
Let R be the set of all points $(x, y)$ in the xy-plane such that:

$$\begin{cases} (x+y)^{2} - 6(x+y) + 8 \leq 0 \\ (x-y)^{2} + 4(x-y) + 3 \leq 0 \end{cases}$$

(Note: The boundaries of the region are included in R.)

Find the area of R.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 6$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 8$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Both inequalities involve only $x+y$ and $x-y$, so change variables to $u=x+y$ and $v=x-y$.

The first inequality is $u^2-6u+8\le0$, i.e. $(u-2)(u-4)\le0$, so

$$2\le x+y\le4.$$

The second is $v^2+4v+3\le0$, i.e. $(v+1)(v+3)\le0$, so

$$-3\le x-y\le-1.$$

In the $(u,v)$-plane this is a square of side $2$, hence of area $4$. To return to the $(x,y)$-plane note that $x=\tfrac{u+v}{2}$, $y=\tfrac{u-v}{2}$, so

$$\mathrm{d}x\,\mathrm{d}y=\left|\frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)}\right|\mathrm{d}u\,\mathrm{d}v=\frac12\,\mathrm{d}u\,\mathrm{d}v,$$

and the area is halved: $\tfrac12\cdot4=2$.

Equivalently, $R$ is the parallelogram bounded by the four lines $x+y=2$, $x+y=4$, $x-y=-1$ and $x-y=-3$; its vertices are $\left(\tfrac12,\tfrac32\right)$, $\left(-\tfrac12,\tfrac52\right)$, $\left(\tfrac32,\tfrac52\right)$, $\left(\tfrac12,\tfrac72\right)$. Taking the side from $\left(\tfrac12,\tfrac32\right)$ to $\left(\tfrac32,\tfrac52\right)$, of length $\sqrt2$, as base, the distance between the parallel lines $x-y=-1$ and $x-y=-3$ is $\tfrac{2}{\sqrt2}=\sqrt2$, giving area $\sqrt2\cdot\sqrt2=2$.

(The answer $4$ is what you get by forgetting the factor $\tfrac12$ from the change of variables.)

The answer is B.
