## 题目
The curve C is defined as $y = x^{3} - x + 17$. The tangent and the normal to $C$ at the point where $x = -1$ form a triangle with the y-axis. Find the area of the triangle.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{4}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{5}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{2}{3}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{3}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{3}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \frac{4}{3}$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
The triangle is cut off by the two lines through the point of contact, so we only need the two $y$-intercepts and the horizontal distance from the point to the $y$-axis.

At $x=-1$ the curve gives $y=(-1)^3-(-1)+17=17$, and since $\frac{\mathrm{d}y}{\mathrm{d}x}=3x^2-1$ the gradient there is $3-1=2$. So the point of contact is $P(-1,17)$, the tangent has gradient $2$ and the normal has gradient $-\tfrac12$.

Tangent: $y-17=2(x+1)$, so $y=2x+19$ and it cuts the $y$-axis at $(0,19)$.

Normal: $y-17=-\tfrac12(x+1)$, so $y=-\tfrac12x+\tfrac{33}{2}$ and it cuts the $y$-axis at $\left(0,\tfrac{33}{2}\right)$.

The triangle has the segment of the $y$-axis between these two intercepts as its base, of length

$$19-\frac{33}{2}=\frac52,$$

and the perpendicular height is the horizontal distance from $P$ to the $y$-axis, namely $|-1|=1$. Hence

$$\text{Area}=\frac12\cdot\frac52\cdot1=\frac54.$$

The answer is C.
