## 题目
There are $24$ men and a certain number of women. Each man shakes hands with $6$ women, and each woman shakes hands with $8$ men. How many people are there in total?

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
$42$

## 解析
Count the man–woman handshakes in two ways. Counting from the men's side, each of the $24$ men shakes $6$ hands, giving $24\times6=144$ handshakes. Counting from the women's side, if there are $w$ women then there are $8w$ handshakes. Hence

$$8w=144,\qquad w=18.$$

Such a configuration really does exist: number the women $0,1,\dots,17$ and let man $k$ (for $k=0,1,\dots,23$) shake hands with the six women $6k,6k+1,\dots,6k+5$ taken modulo $18$; every woman then shakes hands with exactly $8$ men. Hence the total number of people is

$$24+18=42.$$

So the answer is $42$.
