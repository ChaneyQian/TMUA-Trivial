## 题目
There are $24$ men and a certain number of women. Each man shakes hands with $6$ women, and each woman shakes hands with $8$ men. How many people are there in total?

## 解析
Counting handshakes from both sides, $24\cdot6=8w$, so $w=18$. Hence the total is $24+18=42$.

ANSWER: $42$
