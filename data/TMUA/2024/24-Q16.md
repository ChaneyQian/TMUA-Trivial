## 题目
Given the equations of two curves:

$$y = x^{3} - x^{2} - 7x + 19$$

and

$$y = x + 7$$

How many regions do these two curves divide the plane into?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 5$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 6$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 7$$

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
First find where the curves meet:

$$x^3-x^2-7x+19=x+7\iff x^3-x^2-8x+12=0 .$$

Since $x=2$ is a root, factorising gives

$$x^3-x^2-8x+12=(x-2)^2(x+3),$$

so there are only **two** intersection points, at $x=-3$ and $x=2$, and the one at $x=2$ is a *tangential* contact: the difference

$$g(x)=(x-2)^2(x+3)$$

changes sign at $x=-3$ but not at $x=2$, so there the line touches the cubic without crossing it.

Now classify a point $(x,y)$ by whether it lies above or below each curve. The regions "above both" and "below both" are each connected (both curves are graphs over the whole $x$-axis), giving $2$ regions. The set between the curves splits according to the sign of $g$:

- where $g(x)<0$, i.e. $x<-3$, the points with the line above the cubic form $1$ region;
- where $g(x)>0$, i.e. $x>-3$ with $x\neq2$, the points between the curves are pinched off at the point of tangency $x=2$, giving $2$ regions.

In total

$$2+1+2=5 .$$

As a check, Euler's formula on the sphere with the two curves joined at the point at infinity gives $V=3$ (the two intersections plus $\infty$), $E=6$ (three arcs on each curve) and $F=2-V+E=5$.

A tangency that genuinely crossed would give $6$; treating $x=2$ as two separate crossings is the usual trap. The answer is D.
