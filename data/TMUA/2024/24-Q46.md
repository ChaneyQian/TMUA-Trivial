## 题目
Let $S$ denote the sum $1+2+3+\cdots+2n$. Express the sum of the first $2n$ positive odd integers in terms of $S$ and $n$.

## 解析
Since $S=n(2n+1)$ and the sum of the first $2n$ odd integers is $4n^2$,

$$4n^2=2S-2n.$$

ANSWER: $2S-2n$
