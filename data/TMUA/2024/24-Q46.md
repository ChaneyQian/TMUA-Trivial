## 题目
Let $S$ denote the sum $1+2+3+\cdots+2n$. Express the sum of the first $2n$ positive odd integers in terms of $S$ and $n$.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
$2S-2n$

## 解析
Compute both sums in closed form and then eliminate $n^2$.

The given sum is an arithmetic series with $2n$ terms, so

$$S=1+2+\cdots+2n=\frac{2n(2n+1)}{2}=n(2n+1)=2n^2+n.$$

The first $2n$ positive odd integers are $1,3,5,\dots,4n-1$, an arithmetic series with $2n$ terms, first term $1$ and last term $4n-1$:

$$1+3+\cdots+(4n-1)=\frac{2n\bigl(1+(4n-1)\bigr)}{2}=4n^2.$$

From $S=2n^2+n$ we have $2n^2=S-n$, hence

$$4n^2=2(S-n)=2S-2n.$$

As a check with $n=2$: $S=1+2+3+4=10$ and $1+3+5+7=16=2(10)-4$.

So the answer is $2S-2n$.
