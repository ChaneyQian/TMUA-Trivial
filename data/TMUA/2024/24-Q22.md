## 题目
Which of the following is equal to $(\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{2})^{6} - (\sqrt{3} - \sqrt{2})^{6}$

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 396\sqrt{6}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 192\sqrt{6}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 388\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 132\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 384\sqrt{6}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Rather than expanding sixth powers, square the surds first. Put

$$u=(\sqrt3+\sqrt2)^2=5+2\sqrt6,\qquad v=(\sqrt3-\sqrt2)^2=5-2\sqrt6,$$

so that the required value is $u^3-v^3$. Here

$$u-v=4\sqrt6,\qquad u+v=10,\qquad uv=5^2-(2\sqrt6)^2=25-24=1,$$

hence $u^2+uv+v^2=(u+v)^2-uv=100-1=99$ and

$$u^3-v^3=(u-v)(u^2+uv+v^2)=4\sqrt6\times99=396\sqrt6.$$

The binomial expansion gives the same thing: in $(\sqrt3+\sqrt2)^6-(\sqrt3-\sqrt2)^6$ the terms with an even power of $\sqrt2$ cancel and those with an odd power double, leaving

$$2\left[\binom61(\sqrt3)^5\sqrt2+\binom63(\sqrt3)^3(\sqrt2)^3+\binom65\sqrt3(\sqrt2)^5\right]
=2(54+120+24)\sqrt6=396\sqrt6.$$

The answer is A.
