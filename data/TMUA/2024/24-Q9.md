## 题目
When $f(x)$ is divided by $x^2-8x+16$, the remainder is $9$.
When $f(x)$ is divided by $2x-8$, find the product of all possible remainders.

## 解析
Since $x^2-8x+16=(x-4)^2$, write

$$f(x)=g(x)(x-4)^2+9=(2x-8)\left(\frac{x-4}{2}g(x)\right)+9.$$

The remainder is always $9$.

ANSWER: $9$
