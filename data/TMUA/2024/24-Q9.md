## 题目
When $f(x)$ is divided by $x^2-8x+16$, the remainder is $9$.
When $f(x)$ is divided by $2x-8$, find the product of all possible remainders.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
$9$

## 解析
Since $2x-8$ is linear, the remainder on dividing by it is a constant, and by the remainder theorem that constant is the value of $f$ at the root of $2x-8=0$, namely $x=4$.

The first piece of information pins that value down. As $x^2-8x+16=(x-4)^2$, there is a polynomial $g$ with

$$f(x)=g(x)(x-4)^2+9 ,$$

so $f(4)=9$.

Explicitly, this can be rearranged as

$$f(x)=(2x-8)\cdot\frac{(x-4)g(x)}{2}+9 ,$$

which displays $9$ as the remainder (the quotient $\tfrac12(x-4)g(x)$ is still a polynomial). The remainder is therefore forced to be $9$ whatever $f$ is, so there is only one possible value and the product of all possible remainders is $9$.

So the answer is $9$.
