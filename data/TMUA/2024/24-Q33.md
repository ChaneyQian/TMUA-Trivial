## 题目
Given that $\theta^{2} \leq 2\pi$ and $\sin(\theta^{2}) = \frac{1}{2}$

find the product of all possible solutions for $\theta$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{\pi\sqrt{5}}{6}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{5\pi^{2}}{36}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -\frac{\pi}{6}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{7\pi^{2}}{24}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{11\pi^{4}}{1296}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{3\pi^{2}}{16}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Substitute $u=\theta^2$. Since $u\ge0$ and $u\le2\pi$, we need the solutions of $\sin u=\frac12$ in $0\le u\le2\pi$, which are

$$u=\frac{\pi}{6}\quad\text{and}\quad u=\pi-\frac{\pi}{6}=\frac{5\pi}{6}$$

(the next one, $\frac{\pi}{6}+2\pi$, exceeds $2\pi$).

Each value of $u$ gives **two** values of $\theta$, namely $\theta=\pm\sqrt{u}$, since nothing restricts $\theta$ to be positive. There are therefore four solutions,

$$\theta=\pm\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{6}},\qquad\theta=\pm\sqrt{\frac{5\pi}{6}},$$

and their product is

$$\left(-\frac{\pi}{6}\right)\left(-\frac{5\pi}{6}\right)=\frac{5\pi^2}{36},$$

because each $\pm$ pair multiplies to $-u$ and the two minus signs cancel.

Forgetting the negative roots would give the product $\sqrt{\frac{\pi}{6}\cdot\frac{5\pi}{6}}=\frac{\pi\sqrt5}{6}$, which is option A.

The answer is B.
