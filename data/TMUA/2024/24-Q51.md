## 题目
Let

$$f(x)=p\sin x+q\cos x,$$

where $p$ and $q$ are positive. Given

$$f(x)^2=16\sin^2x+k\sin x\cos x+9,$$

find $k$.

### AI备注


## 答案
（无）

## 解析
Expanding and using $\cos^2x=1-\sin^2x$ gives

$$f(x)^2=(p^2-q^2)\sin^2x+q^2+2pq\sin x\cos x.$$

Hence $q^2=9$ and $p^2-q^2=16$. Positivity gives $q=3$ and $p=5$, so

$$k=2pq=30.$$

ANSWER: $30$
