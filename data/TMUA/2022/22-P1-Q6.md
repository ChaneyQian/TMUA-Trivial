## 题目
Given that

$$
\int_{\log_2 5}^{\log_2 20} x\,\mathrm{d}x = \log_2 M
$$

what is the value of $M$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 15
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 16
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 20
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 25
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 100
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad 10000
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
F

## 解析
We calculate the integral and use the laws of logarithms; we obtain

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {\log_ {2} 5} ^ {\log_ {2} 2 0} x \mathrm{d} x & = \left[ \frac {1}{2} x ^ {2} \right] _ {\log_ {2} 5} ^ {\log_ {2} 2 0} \\ & = \frac {1}{2} (\log_ {2} 2 0) ^ {2} - \frac {1}{2} (\log_ {2} 5) ^ {2} \\ & = \frac {1}{2} \big ((\log_ {2} 2 0) ^ {2} - (\log_ {2} 5) ^ {2} \big) \\ & = \frac {1}{2} \big ((\log_ {2} 4 + \log_ {2} 5) ^ {2} - (\log_ {2} 5) ^ {2} \big) \\ & = \frac {1}{2} \big ((2 + \log_ {2} 5) ^ {2} - (\log_ {2} 5) ^ {2} \big) \\ & = \frac {1}{2} \big (4 + 4 \log_ {2} 5 + (\log_ {2} 5) ^ {2} - (\log_ {2} 5) ^ {2} \big) \\ & = \frac {1}{2} (4 + 4 \log_ {2} 5) \\ & = 2 + 2 \log_ {2} 5 \\ & = \log_ {2} 4 + \log_ {2} 2 5 \\ & = \log_ {2} 1 0 0 \end{array}
$$

so the answer is option F.
