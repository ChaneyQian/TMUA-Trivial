## 题目
![[Image/22-P2-Q11-fig1.jpg]]

The diagram shows a kite PQRS whose diagonals meet at O.

$$
\begin{array}{l} {O P = x} \\ {O Q = y} \\ {O R = x} \\ {O S = z} \end{array}
$$

Which of the following is necessary and sufficient for angle SPQ to be a right angle?

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad x = y = z
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 2x = y + z
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad x^{2} = yz
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad y = z
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad y^{2} = x^{2} + z^{2}
$$

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The angle SP Q is a right angle if and only if the triangle SP Q satisfies Pythagoras’s theorem, that is, if and only if

$$
S P ^ {2} + P Q ^ {2} = S Q ^ {2}.
$$

Since P QRS is a kite, its diagonals intersect at right angles, so both $S O P$ and P OQ are rightangled triangles. Therefore, again by Pythagoras,

$$
\begin{array}{r} S P ^ {2} = x ^ {2} + z ^ {2} \\ P Q ^ {2} = x ^ {2} + y ^ {2}. \end{array}
$$

Substituting this, along with $S Q = y + z$ , into the above equation gives the following necessary and sufficient condition for angle SP Q to be a right angle:

$$
x ^ {2} + z ^ {2} + x ^ {2} + y ^ {2} = (y + z) ^ {2}.
$$

Expanding and simplifying, this is equivalent to

$$
2 x ^ {2} = 2 y z
$$

or $x ^ { 2 } = y z$

Therefore option C gives a necessary and sufficient condition.

(One should also check that none of the others are necessary and sufficient; we could take $x = 2$ $y = 4 , z = 1$ : this satisfies option C but none of the others.)
