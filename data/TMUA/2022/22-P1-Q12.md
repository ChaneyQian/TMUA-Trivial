## 题目
A family of quadratic curves is given by

$$
y _ {k} = 2 \left(x - \frac {k}{2}\right) ^ {2} + \frac {k ^ {2}}{2} + 4 k + 3
$$

where k is any real number and $y _ { k }$ is a function of x.

All these curves are sketched, and the point with the lowest y-coordinate among all the curves $y _ { k }$ is (a, b).

Find the value of $a + b$

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad -13
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad -11
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad -9
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad -7
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad -5
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad -3
$$

$$
\mathbf {G} \quad -1
$$

$$
\mathbf {H} \quad 1
$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
The formula for $y _ { k }$ is given in completed square form. The minimum of the graph $y = a ( x - b ) ^ { 2 } + c .$ where $a > 0$ , is at $x = b$ , and the minimum value is c. Therefore the minimum of $y _ { k }$ is at $( \textstyle { \frac { k } { 2 } } , \textstyle { \frac { k ^ { 2 } } { 2 } } + 4 k + 3 )$

The minimum of the minimum y-coordinates is therefore the minimum of $\frac { k ^ { 2 } } { 2 } + 4 k + 3$ . We can find this minimum either by completing the square or by differentiating this with respect to k. Let us use the latter method here as it seems simpler. We have

$$
\frac {\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d} k} \left(\frac {k ^ {2}}{2} + 4 k + 3\right) = k + 4
$$

so the minimum occurs at $k = - 4$ , giving

$$
\left(\frac {k}{2}, \frac {k ^ {2}}{2} + 4 k + 3\right) = (- 2, - 5)
$$

so that $a + b = - 7$ , which is option D.
