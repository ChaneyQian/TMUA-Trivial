## 题目
The real numbers $x$, $y$ and $z$ are all greater than $1$, and satisfy the equations

$$
\log_x y = z \quad \text{and} \quad \log_y z = x
$$

Which one of the following equations for $\log_z x$ **must** be true?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \log_z x = y
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \log_z x = \dfrac{1}{y}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \log_z x = xy
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \log_z x = \dfrac{1}{xy}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \log_z x = xz
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \log_z x = \dfrac{1}{xz}
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad \log_z x = yz
$$

$$
\mathbf{H} \quad \log_z x = \dfrac{1}{yz}
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
F

## 解析
Since $\log _ { x } y = z$ , we have $y = x ^ { z }$ . Likewise $z = y ^ { x }$ . Combining these gives

$$
z = y ^ {x} = (x ^ {z}) ^ {x} = x ^ {x z}.
$$

Therefore raising the equation to the power of ${ \frac { 1 } { x z } } { \mathrm { ~ g i v e s ~ } } z ^ { 1 / x z } = x$ , hence $\log _ { z } x = { \frac { 1 } { x z } }$ , which is option F.
