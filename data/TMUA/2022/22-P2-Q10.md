## 题目
Which of the following statements is/are true?

I. **For all** real numbers $x$ and **for all** positive integers $n$, $x < n$

II. **For all** real numbers $x$, **there exists** a positive integer $n$ such that $x < n$

III. **There exists** a real number $x$ such that **for all** positive integers $n$, $x < n$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \text{none of them}

\mathbf{B} \quad \text{I only}

\mathbf{D} \quad \text{III only}
$$

$$

$$

\mathbf{G} \quad \text{II and III only}
$$

\mathbf{H} \quad \text{I, II and III}
$$

## 答案
G

## 解析
I We can find a counterexample to this statement: taking $x = 2$ and $n = 1$ shows that this statement is false.

II This is true; if $x \le 0 .$ , we can take $n = 1$ , and if $x > 0$ , we can take n to be the smallest integer greater than x.

III This is true; we can take $x = 0$ . (If, though, we replaced ‘positive integers’ with ‘integers’, the resulting statement would be false.)

Therefore II and III are true, and the correct option is G.
