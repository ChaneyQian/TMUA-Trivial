## 题目
The terms $x _ { n }$ of a sequence follow the rule

$$
x _ {n + 1} = \frac {x _ {n} + p}{x _ {n} + q}
$$

where $p$ and $q$ are real numbers.

Given that $x _ { 1 } = 3 , x _ { 2 } = 5$ , and $x _ { 3 } = 7$ , find the value of $x _ { 4 }$
$$
\mathbf {A} \quad -9
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad -5
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad \frac { 5 1 } { 7 }
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad \frac { 1 5 } { 2 }
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad \frac { 2 3 } { 3 }
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad 9
$$

$$
\mathbf {G} \quad 11
$$

$$
\mathbf {H} \quad 13
$$

## 答案
H

## 解析
We write out the formulae for $x _ { 2 } , x _ { 3 }$ and $x _ { 4 }$ in terms of $p$ and $q ,$ using the values $x _ { 1 } = 3 , x _ { 2 } = 5$ and $x _ { 3 } = 7 \colon$

$$
n = 1: \qquad 5 = \frac {3 + p}{3 + q}
$$

$$
n = 2: \qquad 7 = \frac {5 + p}{5 + q}
$$

$$
n = 3: \qquad x _ {4} = \frac {7 + p}{7 + q}
$$

Multiplying the first equation by $3 + q$ and the second by $5 + q$ gives

$$
\begin{array}{l} {1 5 + 5 q = 3 + p} \\ {3 5 + 7 q = 5 + p} \end{array}
$$

These are simultaneous equations for $p$ and $q ;$ subtracting gives $2 0 + 2 q = 2 \mathrm{~so~} q = - 9$ and therefore $p = - 3 3$

Substituting these into the expression for $x _ { 4 }$ gives

$$
x _ {4} = \frac {7 - 3 3}{7 - 9} = 1 3
$$

so the answer is option H.
