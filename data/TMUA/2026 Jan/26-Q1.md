## 题目
A cubic function $f(x) = ax^{3} + bx^{2} + cx + d$ has a turning point on the $y$-axis, but not at the origin. Given that the curve has exactly two distinct $x$-intercepts $p$ and $q$.

Which of the following is a correct expression for the sum $p + q$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -\frac{b}{a}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{b}{a}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad -\frac{2b}{3a}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 0$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad -\frac{b}{3a}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{It cannot be determined uniquely without the value of } d.$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
A turning point on the $y$-axis means $f'(0) = 0$, and since $f'(x) = 3ax^{2} + 2bx + c$ this gives $c = 0$. The turning point is not at the origin, so $f(0) = d \neq 0$.

A cubic with exactly two distinct real roots must have one repeated root $\alpha$ and one simple root $\beta$. At a repeated root both $f(\alpha) = 0$ and $f'(\alpha) = 0$. Now
$$f'(x) = 3ax^{2} + 2bx = x(3ax + 2b),$$
so $\alpha = 0$ or $\alpha = -\frac{2b}{3a}$. But $f(0) = d \neq 0$, so $\alpha = -\frac{2b}{3a}$ (and in particular $b \neq 0$).

The sum of the roots counted with multiplicity is $-\frac{b}{a}$, so
$$2\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a} \implies \beta = -\frac{b}{a} + \frac{4b}{3a} = \frac{b}{3a}.$$

Therefore
$$p + q = \alpha + \beta = -\frac{2b}{3a} + \frac{b}{3a} = -\frac{b}{3a}.$$

The answer is E.
