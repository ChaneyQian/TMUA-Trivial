## 题目
The sequence of functions $f_1(x)$, $f_2(x)$, $f_3(x)$, $\ldots$ is defined as follows:

$$
f_1(x) = x^{10}
$$

$$
f_{n+1}(x) = x f_n'(x) \quad \text{for } n \geq 1
$$

where $f_n'(x) = \dfrac{\mathrm{d}f_n(x)}{\mathrm{d}x}$.

Find the value of

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{20} f_n(x)
$$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{x^{10}(x^{20} - 1)}{x - 1}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{x^{10}(x^{21} - 1)}{x - 1}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \left(\frac{10^{20} - 1}{9}\right)x^{10}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \left(\frac{10^{21} - 1}{9}\right)x^{10}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \left(\frac{(10x)^{20} - 1}{10x - 1}\right)x^{10}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \left(\frac{(10x)^{21} - 1}{10x - 1}\right)x^{10}
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad x^{10} + x^{9} + x^{8} + \cdots + x + 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{H} \quad x^{10} + 10x^{9} + (10 \times 9)x^{8} + \cdots + (10 \times 9 \times \cdots \times 2)x + (10 \times 9 \times \cdots \times 2 \times 1)
$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
We work out the first few functions in the sequence to find a pattern.

$$
\mathrm{f} _{1} (x) = x^{10}
$$

$$
\mathrm{f} _{2} (x) = x (10 x^{9}) = 10 x^{10}
$$

$$
\mathrm{f} _{3} (x) = x (100 x^{9}) = 100 x^{10}
$$

$$
\mathrm{f} _{4} (x) = x (1000 x^{9}) = 10 ^{3} x^{10}
$$

$$
\mathrm{f} _{5} (x) = x (10 ^{4} x^{9}) = 10 ^{4} x^{10}
$$

and so the pattern is clear: $\mathrm { f } _{n} ( x ) = 10 ^{n-1} x^{10}$

The sum expands to

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \sum_{n=1} ^{20} \mathrm{f} _{n} (x) & = x^{10} + 10 x^{10} + 10 ^{2} x^{10} + 10 ^{3} x^{10} + \dots + 10 ^{19} x^{10} \\ & = (1 + 10 + 10 ^{2} + \dots + 10 ^{19}) x^{10} \\ & = \left(\frac {10 ^{20} - 1}{10 - 1}\right) x^{10} \end{array}
$$

where on the final line we have summed the geometric series with $a = 1 , r = 10 , n = 20$ using the formula

$$
S_{n} = \frac {a (r^{n} - 1)}{r - 1}.
$$

The answer is therefore option C.
