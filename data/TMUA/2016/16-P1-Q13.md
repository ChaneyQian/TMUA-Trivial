## 题目
How many real roots does the equation $3x^5 - 10x^3 - 120x + 30 = 0$ have?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 2
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 5
$$

C

## 解析
Commentary: This is not something that you will have learnt how to do, and in general, this is a very dificult problem indeed. But we can use the tools at our disposal to do something helpful. We don't know how to solve a quintic $( x^{5} )$ equation, but we can at least try to find the turning (stationary) points, and see where that gets us.

We attempt to sketch the graph of $y = 3 x^{5} - 10 x^{3} - 120 x + 30$ , or at least think about what a sketch might look like.

We calculate

$$
\frac {\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} x} = 15 x^{4} - 30 x^{2} - 120 = 15 (x^{4} - 2 x^{2} - 8) = 15 (x^{2} - 4) (x^{2} + 2)
$$

so there are stationary points when $\begin{array} { r } { \frac { \mathrm { d } y } { \mathrm { d } x } = 0 . } \end{array}$ , that is, when $x = 2$ and when $x = - 2$ . We can now calculate the y-coordinates: when $x = - 2 , y = - 36 + 80 + 240 + 30 > 0$ (we do not need to calculate the exact value), and when $x = 2 , y = 36 - 80 - 240 + 30 < 0$

So the graph must look something like the following:

![[Image/16-P1-Q13-sol1.jpg]]  
There are clearly 3 points of intersection of this graph with $y = 0$ , so the original equation has 3 real roots, and the answer is C.
