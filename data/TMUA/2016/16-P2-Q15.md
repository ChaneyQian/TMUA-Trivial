## 题目
Consider the quadratic $f(x) = x^2 - 2px + q$ and the statement:

		$(^{*})$ $f(x) = 0$ has two real roots whose difference is greater than 2 and less than 4.

Which one of the following statements is true if and only if $(^{*})$ is true?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad q < p^2 < q + 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \sqrt{q + 1} < p < \sqrt{q + 4}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad q - 3 \leq p^2 - 4 \leq q
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad q < p^2 - 1 < q + 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad q - 2 < p^2 - 3 < q + 2
$$
