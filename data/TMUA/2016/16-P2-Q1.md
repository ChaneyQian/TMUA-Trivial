\int_{1}^{2}\left(x^{2} - \frac{4}{x^{2}}\right)^{2}\,\mathrm{d}x

## 解析
We begin by expanding the brackets, either using the binomial theorem or by multiplying them out by hand, to get

$$
{ \begin{array}{r l} & {\int_{1} ^{2} \left(x^{2} + \frac {4}{x^{2}}\right) ^{2} \mathrm{d} x = \int_{1} ^{2} x^{4} - 8 + \frac {16}{x^{4}} \mathrm{d} x} \\ & {\qquad = \int_{1} ^{2} x^{4} - 8 + 16 x^{-4} \mathrm{d} x} \\ & {\qquad = \left[ \frac {x^{5}}{5} - 8 x + \frac {16 x^{-3}}{- 3} \right] _{1} ^{2}} \\ & {\qquad = \left(\frac {32}{5} - 8 \times 2 - \frac {16 / 8}{3}\right) - \left(\frac {1}{5} - 8 - \frac {16}{3}\right)} \\ & {\qquad = \frac {31}{5} - 8 + \frac {14}{3}} \\ & {\qquad = \frac {93 - 120 + 70}{15}} \\ & {\qquad = \frac {43}{15}} \end{array} }
$$
