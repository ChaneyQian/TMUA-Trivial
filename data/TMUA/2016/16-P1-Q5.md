## 题目
What is the total area enclosed between the curve $y = x^2 - 1$, the $x$-axis and the lines $x = -2$ and $x = 2$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{4}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{8}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{16}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 12
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 16
$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
To answer this question, it is worth drawing a sketch.

The graph is of $y = x^{2} - 1 = ( x + 1 ) ( x - 1 )$ , so the parabola intersects the x-axis at (1, 0) and (-1, 0):

![[Image/16-P1-Q5-sol1.jpg]]

To find the area enclosed, we integrate over the three separate regions, from 2 to 1, from 1 to 1, and from 1 to 2:

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_{-2} ^{-1} x^{2} - 1 \mathrm{d} x & = \left[ \frac {1}{3} x^{3} - x \right] _{-2} ^{-1} \\ & = (- \frac {1}{3} + 1) - (- \frac {8}{3} + 2) \\ & = \frac {4}{3} \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_{-1} ^{1} x^{2} - 1 \mathrm{d} x & = \left[ \frac {1}{3} x^{3} - x \right] _{-1} ^{1} \\ & = (\frac {1}{3} - 1) - (- \frac {1}{3} + 1) \\ & = - \frac {4}{3} \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\int_{1} ^{2} x^{2} - 1 \mathrm{d} x = \left[ \frac {1}{3} x^{3} - x \right] _{1} ^{2}} \\ & {\qquad = (\frac {8}{3} - 2) - (\frac {1}{3} - 1)} \\ & {\qquad = \frac {4}{3}} \end{array}
$$

Thus the three areas are each $\textstyle { \frac { 4 } { 3 } }$ , and the total area is 4, so the answer is C.
