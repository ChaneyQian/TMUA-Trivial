## 题目
$$
f(x) = \frac{(x^{2} + 5)(2x)}{\sqrt[4]{x^{3}}},\quad x > 0
$$

Which one of the following is equal to $f'(x)$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 8x^{\frac{9}{4}} + \frac{40}{3}x^{\frac{1}{4}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{9}{2}x^{\frac{5}{4}} + \frac{5}{2}x^{-\frac{3}{4}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 8x^{\frac{9}{4}} + \frac{40}{3}x^{-\frac{1}{4}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{8}{13}x^{\frac{13}{4}} + 8x^{\frac{5}{4}}
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
We begin by expanding the brackets and simplifying the powers of x:

$$
\begin{array}{r} \mathrm{f} (x) = \frac {2 x^{3} + 10 x}{x^{\frac{3}{4}}} \\ = 2 x^{\frac{9}{4}} + 10 x^{\frac{1}{4}} \end{array}
$$

so

$$
\begin{array}{c} \mathrm {f^{\prime}} (x) = 2 \times \frac {9}{4} x^{\frac{5}{4}} + 10 \times \frac {1}{4} x^{-\frac{3}{4}} \\ = \frac {9}{2} x^{\frac{5}{4}} + \frac {5}{2} x^{-\frac{3}{4}} \end{array}
$$
