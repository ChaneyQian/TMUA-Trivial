## 题目
The real roots of the equation $4^{2x} + 12 = 2^{2x+3}$ are $p$ and $q$, where $p > q$.

The value of $p - q$ can be expressed as

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{3}{4}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad -\frac{1}{2} + \log_{10}\frac{3}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{\log_{10} 3}{\log_{10} 4}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{\log_{10} 3}{\log_{10} 2}
$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
This is a quadratic in $2 ^{2x}$ , so we write $y = 2 ^{2x}$ and solve for y. The quadratic becomes

$$
y^{2} + 12 = 8 y
$$

as $4 ^{2x} = ( 2 ^{2} ) ^{2x} = 2 ^{4x} = ( 2 ^{2x} ) ^{2} { \mathrm { ~ a n d ~ } } 2 ^{2x+3} = 2 ^{2x} \times 2 ^{3}$.

This rearranges to $y^{2} - 8 y + 12 = 0$ , which factorises as $( y - 6 ) ( y - 2 ) = 0 \quad$ , so $y = 6$ or $y = 2$ Thus $2 ^{2x} = 6 { \mathrm { ~ o r ~ } } 2 ^{2x} = 2$ , with the value of $x$ being larger in the first than in the second, so $2 ^{2p} = 6$ and $2 ^{2q} = 2$ . The options are given in terms of log to base 10, so we take $\log_{10}$ of the first equation. Thus 2p $\log_{10} 2 = \log_{10} 6$ and $2 q = 1$ , and hence

$$
p = \frac {\log_{10} 6}{2 \log_{10} 2} \quad \mathrm{and} \quad q = \frac {1}{2}.
$$

Therefore

$$
\begin{array}{r} p - q = \frac {\log_{10} 6}{2 \log_{10} 2} - \frac {1}{2} \\ = \frac {\log_{10} 6 - \log_{10} 2}{2 \log_{10} 2} \\ = \frac {\log_{10} 3}{\log_{10} 4} \end{array}
$$

so the answer is option E.
