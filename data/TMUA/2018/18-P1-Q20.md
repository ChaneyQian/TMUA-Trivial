## 解析
This reminds us of adding the terms of an arithmetic series. One way to do so is to write out the series twice, once forwards and once backwards, and then add corresponding pairs of terms together. If we do that here, we obtain

$$
S = \sin^ {2} 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 1 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 2 ^ {\circ} + \dots + \sin^ {2} 8 8 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 8 9 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 9 0 ^ {\circ}
$$

$$
S = \sin^ {2} 9 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 8 9 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 8 8 ^ {\circ} + \dots + \sin^ {2} 2 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 1 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 0 ^ {\circ}
$$

so that

$$
2 S = \left(\sin^ {2} 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 9 0 ^ {\circ}\right) + \left(\sin^ {2} 1 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 8 9 ^ {\circ}\right) + \dots + \left(\sin^ {2} 9 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin^ {2} 0 ^ {\circ}\right)
$$

Now we know that $\sin ^ { 2 } x + \cos ^ { 2 } x = 1$ for all $x ,$ and since sin $1 ( 9 0 ^ { \circ } - x ) = \cos x$ (by, for example, thinking about a right-angled triangle or by looking at the graphs of the functions), we can rewrite this sum as

$$
\begin{array}{r l} 2 S & = (\sin^ {2} 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos^ {2} 0 ^ {\circ}) + (\sin^ {2} 1 ^ {\circ} + \cos^ {2} 1 ^ {\circ}) + \dots + (\sin^ {2} 9 0 ^ {\circ} + \cos^ {2} 9 0 ^ {\circ}) \\ & = 1 + 1 + \dots + 1 \\ & = 9 1 \end{array}
$$

(since there are 91 terms), therefore $S = 4 5 . 5$ , which is option E.
