## 题目
Consider the following statement:

For any positive integer N there is a

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad \text{positive integer K such that} N(Km+1)-1 \text{is not prime for any positive integer m. Which one of the following is the negation of this statement? A For any positive integer N there is a positive integer K such that there is a positive integer m for which} N(Km+1)-1 \text{is prime.}
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad \text{For any positive integer N there is a positive integer K such that there is a positive integer m for which} N(Km+1)-1 \text{is not prime.}
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad \text{For any positive integer N there is a positive integer K such that for any positive integer m,} N(Km+1)-1 \text{is not prime.}
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad \text{For any positive integer} N \text{, any positive integer} K \text{and any positive integer} m \text{,} N(Km + 1) - 1 \text{is not prime.}
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad \text{There is a positive integer N such that for any positive integer K there is a positive integer m for which} N(Km+1)-1 \text{is not prime.}
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad \text{There is a positive integer N such that for any positive integer K there is a positive integer m for which} N(Km+1)-1 \text{is prime.}
$$

$$
\mathbf {G} \quad \text{There is a positive integer N such that for any positive integer K and any positive integer m,} N(Km+1)-1 \text{is prime.}
$$

$$
\mathbf {H} \quad \text{There is a positive integer N and a positive integer K for which there is no positive integer m for which} N(Km+1)-1 \text{is prime.}
$$
