## 题目
An arithmetic progression has first term a and common difference d.

The sum of the first 5 terms is equal to the sum of the first 8 terms.

Which one of the following expresses the relationship between $a$ and $d$ ?

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad a = - \frac {3 8}{3} d
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad a = - 7 d
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad a = - 6 d
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad a = 6 d
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad a = 7 d
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad a = \frac {3 8}{3} d
$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
One approach is to use the formulae for the sum of the first 5 and the first 8 terms (that is, $S _ { n } = { \textstyle { \frac { 1 } { 2 } } n } ( 2 a + ( n - 1 ) d ) )$ .

We have

$$
\begin{array}{l} S _ {5} = \frac {1}{2} \times 5 (2 a + 4 d) = \frac {5}{2} (2 a + 4 d) \\ S _ {8} = \frac {1}{2} \times 8 (2 a + 7 d) = 4 (2 a + 7 d) \end{array}
$$

These are equal, so we have

$$
\frac {5}{2} (2 a + 4 d) = 4 (2 a + 7 d)
$$

We want to rearrange this to get a formula in the form $a = \cdots$ , so we multiply both sides by 2, expand the brackets and collect like terms:

$$
\begin{array}{r l r} & & 5 (2 a + 4 d) = 8 (2 a + 7 d) \\ \Longleftrightarrow & & 1 0 a + 2 0 d = 1 6 a + 5 6 d \\ \Longleftrightarrow & & - 6 a = 3 6 d \\ \Longleftrightarrow & & a = - 6 d \end{array}
$$

so the answer is $\mathrm { C } .$

An alternative approach is to note that the difference between the sum of the first 5 and the sum of the first 8 terms equals the sum of the 6th, 7th and 8th terms. (This is because $S _ { 5 } =$ $u _ { 1 } + u _ { 2 } + \cdot \cdot \cdot + u _ { 5 }$ while $S _ { 8 } = u _ { 1 } + u _ { 2 } + \cdot \cdot \cdot + u _ { 5 } + u _ { 6 } + u _ { 7 } + u _ { 8 }$ , where we have written the sequence as $u _ { 1 } , u _ { 2 } , . . . . )$ Since the two sums $S _ { 5 }$ and $S _ { 8 }$ are equal, the sum of the 6th, 7th and 8th terms must be zero. This gives

$$
(a + 5 d) + (a + 6 d) + (a + 7 d) = 0
$$

Expanding and collecting like terms gives $3 a + 1 8 d = 0 .$ , so $a = - 6 d ,$ , as before.
