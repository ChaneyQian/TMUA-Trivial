## 答案
D

## 解析
We write the integrand (the thing being integrated) in terms of powers of x and split up the fraction in order to be able to compute the integral:

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\int_ {1} ^ {4} \frac {3 - 2 x}{x \sqrt {x}} \mathrm{d} x = \int_ {1} ^ {4} \frac {3 - 2 x}{x ^ {\frac {3}{2}}} \mathrm{d} x} \\ & {\qquad = \int_ {1} ^ {4} 3 x ^ {- \frac {3}{2}} - 2 x ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} \mathrm{d} x} \\ & {\qquad = \left[ \frac {3 x ^ {- \frac {1}{2}}}{(- \frac {1}{2})} - \frac {2 x ^ {\frac {1}{2}}}{\frac {1}{2}} \right] _ {1} ^ {4}} \\ & {\qquad = \left[ - 6 x ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} - 4 x ^ {\frac {1}{2}} \right] _ {1} ^ {4}} \\ & {\qquad = (- 6 \times \frac {1}{2} - 4 \times 2) - (- 6 \times 1 - 4 \times 1)} \\ & {\qquad = - 1 1 - (- 1 0)} \\ & {\qquad = - 1} \end{array}
$$

so the correct answer is D.
