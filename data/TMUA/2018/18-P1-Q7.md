## 题目
The non-zero constant k is chosen so that the coefficients of $x^{6}$ in the expansions of $(1 + kx^{2})^{7}$ and $(k + x)^{10}$ are equal.

What is the value of $k$ ?

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad \frac{1}{6}
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 6
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad \frac{\sqrt{6}}{6}
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad \sqrt{6}
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad \frac{\sqrt{30}}{30}
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad \sqrt{30}
$$

## 答案
A

## 解析
We can expand these two expressions using the binomial theorem. We look just at the term involving $x ^ { 6 }$ :

$$
\begin{array}{r l} (1 + k x ^ {2}) ^ {7} & = \dots + \binom {7} {3} 1 ^ {4} (k x ^ {2}) ^ {3} + \dots \\ & = \dots + \frac {7 \times 6 \times 5}{3 \times 2 \times 1} k ^ {3} x ^ {6} + \dots \\ & = \dots + 3 5 k ^ {3} x ^ {6} + \dots \end{array}
$$

and

$$
{ \begin{array}{r l} & {(k + x) ^ {1 0} = \dots + {\binom {1 0} {6}} k ^ {4} x ^ {6} + \dots} \\ & {\qquad = \dots + {\binom {1 0} {4}} k ^ {4} x ^ {6} + \dots} \\ & {\qquad = \dots + {\frac {1 0 \times 9 \times 8 \times 7}{4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1}} k ^ {4} x ^ {6} + \dots} \\ & {\qquad = \dots + 2 1 0 k ^ {4} x ^ {6} + \dots} \end{array} }
$$

We therefore need

$$
3 5 k ^ {3} = 2 1 0 k ^ {4}
$$

so $k = { \frac { 3 5 } { 2 1 0 } } = { \frac { 5 } { 3 0 } } = { \frac { 1 } { 6 } }$

Thus the correct answer is A.
