## 题目
Find the value of the constant term in the expansion of

$$
\left(x ^ {6} - \frac {1}{x ^ {2}}\right) ^ {1 2}
$$

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad -495
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad -220
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad -66
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad 66
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad 220
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad 495
$$

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
The constant term is the one with $x ^ { 0 }$ . The expansion is

$$
(x ^ {6}) ^ {1 2} + \binom {1 2} {1} (x ^ {6}) ^ {1 1} \left(- \frac {1}{x ^ {2}}\right) + \dots + \binom {1 2} {k} (x ^ {6}) ^ {1 2 - k} \left(- \frac {1}{x ^ {2}}\right) ^ {k} + \dots + \left(- \frac {1}{x ^ {2}}\right) ^ {1 2}
$$

(This notation for binomial coefficients is much clearer than the older ${}^{n}\mathrm{C}_{r}$ notation, and is recommended.)

The kth term is therefore

$$
\binom {1 2} {k} x ^ {6 (1 2 - k)} (- 1) ^ {k} x ^ {- 2 k} = \binom {1 2} {k} (- 1) ^ {k} x ^ {6 (1 2 - k) - 2 k} = \binom {1 2} {k} (- 1) ^ {k} x ^ {7 2 - 8 k}
$$

The value of $k$ which makes the power zero is therefore $k = 9$ , so the constant term is

$$
\binom {1 2} {9} (- 1) ^ {9} = - \binom {1 2} {3} = - \frac {1 2 \times 1 1 \times 1 0}{3 \times 2 \times 1} = - 2 \times 1 1 \times 1 0 = - 2 2 0
$$

where we have used ${ \binom { n } { k } } = { \binom { n } { n - k } }$ to simplify the calculation. Thus the answer is B.

Commentary: We have used k here (beginning our counting with $k = 0 )$ . You might prefer to use another letter such as r.
