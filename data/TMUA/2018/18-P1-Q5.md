## 题目
The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = x^3 + ax^2 + bx + c$ .

$a, b$ and $c$ take the values 1, 2 and 3 with no two of them being equal and not necessarily in this order.

The remainder when $\mathrm{f}(x)$ is divided by $(x + 2)$ is $R$ .

The remainder when $\mathrm{f}(x)$ is divided by $(x + 3)$ is $S$ .

What is the largest possible value of $R - S$ ?

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad -26
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 5
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad 7
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad 17
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad 29
$$
## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
For this question, we make use of the remainder theorem: the remainder when f(x) is divided by (x − a) is f(a).

In this case, we take $a = - 2$ and $a = - 3$ , so

$$
\begin{array}{l} \mathrm{f(-2)=R} \\ \mathrm{f(-3)=S} \end{array}
$$

Substituting these into the given formula for f(x) gives

$$
\begin{array}{l} \mathrm{f(-2)=-8+4a-2b+c=R} \\ \mathrm{f(-3)=-27+9a-3b+c=S} \end{array}
$$

We want the largest possible value of $R - S _ { \mathrm { \ell } }$ , so we subtract these two equations to get

$$
1 9 - 5 a + b = R - S
$$

To get the largest possible value, we want a to be as small as possible and b to be as large as possible, so we take $a = 1 , b = 3$ (and then $c = 2 )$ , giving $R - S = 1 9 - 5 + 3 = 1 7$

Thus the correct answer is D.
