## 题目
$x$ and $y$ satisfy $|2 - x| \leq 6$ and $|y + 2| \leq 4$ .

What is the greatest possible value of $|xy|$ ?

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad 16
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 24
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad 32
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad 40
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad 48
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad \text{There is no greatest possible value.}
$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
We notice that the first inequality involves x only and the second involves y only, so we can solve each inequality separately.

For the first one, $| 2 - x | \leqslant 6$ , this means that x is at distance at most 6 from 2, so $- 4 \leqslant x \leqslant 8$ Likewise, the second inequality, $| y + 2 | \leqslant 4$ means that y is at most 4 from $- 2 \ ( \mathrm { a s } \ | y + 2 | =$ $| y - ( - 2 )$ |, so it measures the distance of y from −2). So $- 6 \leqslant y \leqslant 2$

We can now obtain the greatest possible value of $| x y | = | x | . | y |$ by taking the greatest possible value of |x|, which is 8, and the greatest possible value of |y|, which is 6, so the greatest possible value of |xy| is $8 \times 6 = 4 8$ , which is option E.
