## 题目
The function $f$ is given, for $x > 0$ , by

$$
\mathrm{f} (x) = \frac {x ^ {3} - 4 x}{2 \sqrt {x}}
$$

Find the value of $\mathrm{f}'(4)$ .

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 9
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad 9.5
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad 12
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad 39.5
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad 88
$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
We first simplify the fraction using index laws:

$$
{ \begin{array}{r l} {\operatorname{f} (x) = {\frac {x ^ {3} - 4 x}{2 {\sqrt {x}}}}} \\ & {= {\frac {x ^ {3} - 4 x}{2 x ^ {{\frac {1}{2}}}}}} \\ & {= {\frac {1}{2}} x ^ {{\frac {5}{2}}} - 2 x ^ {{\frac {1}{2}}}} \end{array} }
$$

so

$$
\mathrm{f} ^ {\prime} (x) = \frac {5}{4} x ^ {\frac {3}{2}} - x ^ {- \frac {1}{2}}
$$

Substituting $x = 4$ then gives

$$
\mathrm{f} ^ {\prime} (4) = \frac {5}{4} \times 8 - \frac {1}{2} = 1 0 - \frac {1}{2} = 9. 5
$$

and so the answer is C.
