## 题目
The area of a rectangle is measured to be $5600\,\mathrm{cm}^2$ correct to 2 significant figures.

The width of the rectangle is measured to be $80\,\mathrm{cm}$ correct to the nearest centimetre.

Which one of the following expressions gives the greatest possible height of the rectangle?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 70.5\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 75\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{5650}{85}\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{5650}{80.5}\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{5650}{75}\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \dfrac{5650}{79.5}\,\mathrm{cm}
$$

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
F

## 解析
The area of the rectangle is height times width, so

$$
\mathrm{height} = \frac {\mathrm{area}}{\mathrm{width}}.
$$

To obtain the greatest possible height, we require the greatest possible area and the least possible width. The upper bound for the area is 5650 cm, and the lower bound for the height is 79.5 cm, so the greatest possible height is $\frac { 5 6 5 0 } { 7 9 . 5 } \mathrm { c m }$ , which is option F.
