## 答案
C

## 解析
A simple way to decide between the four given options is to determine which of the points (1, 0), (−1, 0), (0, 1) and (0, −1) lie on the graph.

We start with (1, 0): this gives $1 \times 1 = 1$ , so (1, 0) lies on the graph. We now look at (0, 1): this gives $1 \times 1 = 1$ , so this also lies on the graph. On the other hand, (−1, 0) gives $- 1 \times 1 = - 1$ and (0, −1) gives the same, so neither of these points lies on the graph.

Therefore the graph must be that shown in option C.

Alternatively, we can multiply out the brackets, giving $x ^ { 3 } + y ^ { 3 } = 1$ ; therefore for large values of $x$, we must have $y ^ { 3 } \approx - x ^ { 3 }$ or $y \approx - x$, , giving A or C. Now when $0 < x < 1$ , we must have $0 < y < 1$ , and hence the correct graph is C.
