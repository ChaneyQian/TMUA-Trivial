## 题目

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad x = -\frac{1}{\log_{10}(1-y)}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad x = 1 + \log_{10} y
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad x = 1 - \log_{10} y
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad x = 1 - 10^{-y}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad x = 10^{-y} - 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad x = 10^{1-y}
$$
