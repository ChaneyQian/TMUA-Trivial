## 题目
The positive real numbers $a$, $b$, and $c$ are such that the equation

$$
x^3 + ax^2 = bx + c
$$

has three real roots, one positive and two negative.

Which one of the following correctly describes the real roots of the equation

$$
x^3 + c = ax^2 + bx?
$$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \text{It has three real roots, one positive and two negative.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \text{It has three real roots, two positive and one negative.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \text{It has three real roots, but their signs differ depending on } a, b, \text{ and } c.
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \text{It has exactly one real root, which is positive.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \text{It has exactly one real root, which is negative.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \text{It has exactly one real root, whose sign differs depending on } a, b, \text{ and } c.
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad \text{The number of real roots can be one or three, but the number of roots differs depending on } a, b, \text{ and } c.
$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
The first equation can be rewritten as

$$
\mathrm{f} (x) = x ^ {3} + a x ^ {2} - b x - c = 0\tag{1}
$$

and the second equation as

$$
\mathrm{g} (x) = x ^ {3} - a x ^ {2} - b x + c = 0.\tag{2}
$$

Comparing these two equations, we see that the coefficient of $x ^ { 2 }$ and the constant term have been negated. We can also negate equation (2) to give

$$
- \mathrm{g} (x) = - x ^ {3} + a x ^ {2} + b x - c = 0.\tag{3}
$$

This is similar to equation (1), but now the coefficients of $x ^ { 3 }$ and x have been negated. This indicates that we can relate this to equation (1) by substituting in −x. We find that

$$
\mathrm{f} (- x) = (- x) ^ {3} + a (- x) ^ {2} - b (- x) - c = - x ^ {3} + a x ^ {2} + b x - c = - \mathrm{g} (x)
$$

and so equation (3) becomes

$$
\mathrm{f} (- x) = 0.
$$

Since we are told that $\operatorname { f } ( x ) = 0$ has three real roots, one positive and two negative, and $\operatorname { f } ( - x ) = 0$ must have three real roots, one negative and two positive. (It might be helpful to note here that $y = \operatorname { f } ( - x )$ is the reflection of $y = \operatorname { f } ( x )$ in the y-axis.)

Hence the answer is B.
