## 题目
The number of solutions in the interval $0 \leq \theta \leq 4\pi$ of the equation $\sin^{2}\theta + 3\cos\theta = 3$ is

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad 0
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad 2
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad 5
$$

$$
\mathbf {G} \quad 6
$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
We rewrite $\sin ^ { 2 } \theta$ as $1 - \cos ^ { 2 } \theta$ to give

$$
1 - \cos^ {2} \theta + 3 \cos \theta = 3.
$$

This is now a quadratic in $u = \cos \theta ,$ giving

$$
1 - u ^ {2} + 3 u = 3
$$

or $u ^ { 2 } - 3 u + 2 = 0$ . This factorises as $( u - 1 ) ( u - 2 ) = 0 , \mathrm { s o } u = 1 \mathrm { o r } u = 2$ , that is, cos $\theta = 1$ or cos $\theta = 2 .$

cos $\theta = 2$ has no real solutions. The solutions of cos $\theta = 1$ in the given range are $\theta = 0 , \theta = 2 \pi$ $\theta = 4 \pi$ . So there are three solutions, and the answer is option D.
