## 题目

The angle $x$ is measured in radians and is such that $0 \leq x \leq \pi$

The total length of any intervals for which $-1 \leq \tan x \leq 1$ and $\sin 2x \geq 0.5$ is

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad \frac{\pi}{12}
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad \frac{\pi}{6}
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad \frac{\pi}{4}
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad \frac{\pi}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad \frac{5\pi}{12}
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad \frac{\pi}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf {G} \quad \frac{5\pi}{6}
$$
