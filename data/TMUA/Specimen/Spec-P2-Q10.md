## 答案
E

## 解析
We can start by finding the value of $y$ when x is close to 1. When $x = 1 . 1$ , say, $y = \log _ { 1 . 1 } 2$ which is equivalent to $1 . 1 ^ { y } = 2$ . Now $1 . 1 ^ { 2 } = 1 . 2 1$ , so $y > 2$ (it looks as though $y \approx 1 0 )$ . The only graph with this value of $y$ near $x = 1$ is E, so this must be the answer.

Alternatively, we could rearrange the equation to get $x ^ { y } \ = \ 2$ . y must always be positive, otherwise $x ^ { y }$ would be less than 1. As x gets large, y must $\mathrm { g e t }$ closer to 0. Putting these together again shows that only graph E works.
