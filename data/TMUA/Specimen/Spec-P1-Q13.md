## 题目
How many real roots does the equation $x^{4} - 4x^{3} + 4x^{2} - 10 = 0$ have?

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad 0
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad 2
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad 4
$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
We can determine the answer to this question by locating the stationary points of the function $\operatorname { f } ( x ) = x ^ { 4 } - 4 x ^ { 3 } + 4 x ^ { 2 } - 1 0$ . We have

$$
\mathrm{f} ^ {\prime} (x) = 4 x ^ {3} - 1 2 x ^ {2} + 8 x = 4 x \left(x ^ {2} - 3 x + 2\right) = 4 x (x - 1) (x - 2).
$$

There are therefore stationary points at $x = 0 , x = 1$ and $x = 2 .$ . Substituting these values into f(x) gives us the y-coordinates of these points on the graph of $y = \operatorname { f } ( x )$ : they are $( 0 , - 1 0 )$ $( 1 , - 9 )$ and $( 2 , - 1 0 )$ . There are thus only two real solutions to $\operatorname { f } ( x ) = 0$ , and the answer is option C.
