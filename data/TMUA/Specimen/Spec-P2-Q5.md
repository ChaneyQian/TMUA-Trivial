## 题目
Using the observation that $2 ^ { 5 } \approx 3 ^ { 3 }$ , it is possible to deduce that $\log _ { 3 } 2$ is approximately

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad \frac{3}{5}
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad \frac{2}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad \frac{3}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad \frac{5}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad 2
$$
## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
The target $\log _ { 3 } 2$ suggests that we want to take logarithms of both sides of the given approximation to base 3, giving

$$
\log_ {3} 2 ^ {5} \approx \log_ {3} 3 ^ {3}
$$

so that

$$
5 \log_ {3} 2 \approx 3
$$

and hence $\textstyle \log _ { 3 } 2 \approx { \frac { 3 } { 5 } }$ , which is option A.
