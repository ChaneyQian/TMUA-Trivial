## 题目
$$
\mathrm{f} (x) = \frac {1}{5 ^ {2 x} - 4 (5 ^ {x}) + 7}
$$

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad \frac{1}{7}
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad \frac{1}{4}
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad \frac{1}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad 7
$$
## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The denominator is a quadratic in $u = 5 ^ { x }$ , so we can write it as

$$
\frac {1}{u ^ {2} - 4 u + 7} = \frac {1}{(u - 2) ^ {2} + 3}
$$

The maximum value of this fraction is obtained when the denominator takes its minimum value, which is when $u = 2 .$ , giving a value of $\frac 1 3$ . We must also check that this value can be obtained; this is the case as $5 ^ { x } = 2$ has a real solution.

Therefore the correct answer is option C.
