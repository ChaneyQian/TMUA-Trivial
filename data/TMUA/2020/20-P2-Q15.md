## 题目
Which one of the following is a necessary and sufficient condition for

$$
\sum_ {k = 1} ^ {n} \sin \left(\frac {k \pi}{3}\right) = \frac {\sqrt {3}}{2}
$$

to be true?

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad n = 1
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad n \text{ is a multiple of 3}
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad n \text{ is a multiple of 6}
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad n \text{ is 1 more than a multiple of 3}
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad n \text{ is 1 more than a multiple of 6}
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad n \text{ is 1 more than a multiple of 6 or } n \text{ is 2 more than a multiple of 6}
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
It is very helpful to write out the values of the summands (the terms being added) in terms of $\sqrt { 3 }$ and similar, and then work out the first few values of the sum.

We can see that the sum repeats every 6 steps, and that it equals $\textstyle { \frac { \sqrt { 3 } } { 2 } }$ twice in every cycle of 6, specifically when $n = 1 , 4 , 7 , 1 0 , . . . .$ This sequence of n values are the numbers which are 1 more than a multiple of 3, and so the correct option is D.
