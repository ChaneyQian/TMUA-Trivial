## 题目
Which of the following is an expression for the first derivative with respect to $x$ of

$$
\frac{x^3 - 5x^2}{2x\sqrt{x}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad -\frac{\sqrt{x}}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{\sqrt{x}}{4}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{3x-5}{4\sqrt{x}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{3\sqrt{x} - 5}{4\sqrt{x}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{3\sqrt{x} - 10}{3\sqrt{x}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{3x^2 - 10x}{3\sqrt{x}}
$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
We start by writing the expression as a sum of powers of x:

$$
{\frac {x ^ {3} - 5 x ^ {2}}{2 x {\sqrt {x}}}} = {\frac {x ^ {3} - 5 x ^ {2}}{2 x ^ {\frac {3}{2}}}} = {\frac {x ^ {\frac {3}{2}} - 5 x ^ {\frac {1}{2}}}{2}} = \frac {1}{2} x ^ {\frac {3}{2}} - \frac {5}{2} x ^ {\frac {1}{2}}
$$

We can now differentiate this expression to get

$$
\frac {1}{2} \times \frac {3}{2} x ^ {\frac {1}{2}} - \frac {5}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} x ^ {- \frac {1}{2}}
$$

We now need to convert this into the form given in the options, so we write it as

$$
\frac {3}{4} \sqrt {x} - \frac {\frac {5}{4}}{\sqrt {x}} = \frac {3 \sqrt {x}}{4} - \frac {5}{4 \sqrt {x}} = \frac {3 x}{4 \sqrt {x}} - \frac {5}{4 \sqrt {x}} = \frac {3 x - 5}{4 \sqrt {x}}
$$

which is option C.
