## 解析
We can make some immediate observations about this equation:

The left hand side takes values between −3 and $+ 3 .$

The right hand side is only defined for $x \geq 0$ and is always non-negative, and when $x > 9 .$ 8 $\sqrt { x } > 3$

Therefore we need only consider the region $0 \leq x \leq 9$ . Note that $3 \pi \approx 9 . 5$ , so we have just less than 1.5 periods of the cosine function in this range, and 3 cos $9 < 0$ . We can therefore sketch the pair of graphs $y = 3$ cos x and $y = { \sqrt { x } }$ and observe how many times they intersect:

We see that there are 3 points of intersection, and hence 3 solutions to the equation, which is option D.
