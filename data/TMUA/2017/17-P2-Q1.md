## 题目
Given that $y = \frac{(1 - 3x)^{2}}{2x^{\frac{3}{2}}}$ , which one of the following is a correct expression for $\frac{dy}{dx}$ ?

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad \frac{9}{4}x^{-\frac{1}{2}} + \frac{3}{2}x^{-\frac{3}{2}} - \frac{3}{4}x^{-\frac{5}{2}}
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad \frac{9}{4}x^{-\frac{1}{2}} - \frac{3}{2}x^{-\frac{3}{2}} + \frac{3}{4}x^{-\frac{5}{2}}
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad \frac{9}{4}x^{-\frac{1}{2}} - \frac{3}{2}x^{-\frac{3}{2}} - \frac{3}{4}x^{-\frac{5}{2}}
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad -\frac{9}{4}x^{-\frac{1}{2}} + \frac{3}{2}x^{-\frac{3}{2}} + \frac{3}{4}x^{-\frac{5}{2}}
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad -\frac{9}{4}x^{-\frac{1}{2}} + \frac{3}{2}x^{-\frac{3}{2}} - \frac{3}{4}x^{-\frac{5}{2}}
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad -\frac{9}{4}x^{-\frac{1}{2}} - \frac{3}{2}x^{-\frac{3}{2}} - \frac{3}{4}x^{-\frac{5}{2}}
$$
## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
We first expand the brackets and split up the fraction:

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & y = \frac {(1 - 3 x) ^ {2}}{2 x ^ {\frac {3}{2}}} \\ & \quad = \frac {1 - 6 x + 9 x ^ {2}}{2 x ^ {\frac {3}{2}}} \\ & \quad = \frac {1}{2} x ^ {- \frac {3}{2}} - 3 x ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} + \frac {9}{2} x ^ {\frac {1}{2}} \end{array}
$$

and we can now diferentiate this to get

$$
\begin{array}{r} \frac {\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} x} = \frac {1}{2} (- \frac {3}{2}) x ^ {- \frac {5}{2}} - 3 (- \frac {1}{2}) x ^ {- \frac {3}{2}} + \frac {9}{2} \times \frac {1}{2} x ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} \\ = - \frac {3}{4} x ^ {- \frac {5}{2}} + \frac {3}{2} x ^ {- \frac {3}{2}} + \frac {9}{4} x ^ {- \frac {1}{2}} \end{array}
$$

and so the correct answer is A.
