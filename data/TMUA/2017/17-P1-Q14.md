## 题目
The solution of the simultaneous equations

$$
2^x + 3 \times 2^y = 3
$$

$$
2^{2x} - 9 \times 2^{2y} = 6
$$

is $x = p,\ y = q$.

Find the value of $p - q$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{5}{12}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{7}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \log_2 \frac{5}{12}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \log_2 \frac{7}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \log_2 9
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \log_2 15
$$

## 答案
F

## 解析
The equations involve $2 ^ { x }$ and $2 ^ { 2 x } = ( 2 ^ { x } ) ^ { 2 }$ , so we write $a = 2 ^ { x }$ ; likewise we write $b = 2 ^ { y }$ , and so our equations become:

$$
\begin{array}{c} {a + 3 b = 3} \\ {a ^ {2} - 9 b ^ {2} = 6} \end{array}
$$

The first equation gives $a = 3 - 3 b$ , which we can substitute into the second equation to obtain

$$
(3 - 3 b) ^ {2} - 9 b ^ {2} = 6
$$

which expands to $9 - 1 8 b = 6 \AA$ , and hence $\begin{array} { r } { b = \frac { 1 } { 6 } } \end{array}$ and $\textstyle a = { \frac { 5 } { 2 } }$

Another way to solve these simultaneous equations is to note that the second one can be written as $( a + 3 b ) ( a - 3 b ) = 6$ , using the identity for the difference of two squares. Since $a + 3 b = 3$ this gives $a - 3 b = 2$ , from which we can again deduce a and b.

We can now find x and $y \colon$

$$
\begin{array}{l} {x = \log_ {2} a = \log_ {2} \frac {5}{2} = p} \\ {y = \log_ {2} b = \log_ {2} \frac {1}{6} = q} \end{array}
$$

and hence

$$
p - q = \log_ {2} \frac {5}{2} - \log_ {2} \frac {1}{6} = \log_ {2} \left(\frac {5}{2} \div \frac {1}{6}\right) = \log_ {2} 1 5
$$

Thus the correct answer is $\mathrm { F } .$
