## 题目
The function $f$ is given by

$$
f(x) = \left(\frac{2}{x} - \frac{1}{2x^2}\right)^2 \quad (x \neq 0)
$$

What is the value of $f''(1)$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad -3
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad -1
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 5
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 17
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 29
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 80
$$

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
We expand the brackets, writing everything as powers of x:

$$
\mathrm{f} (x) = \left(\frac {2}{x} - \frac {1}{2 x ^ {2}}\right) ^ {2}
$$

$$
= \frac {4}{x ^ {2}} - 2 \frac {2}{x} \frac {1}{2 x ^ {2}} + \frac {1}{4 x ^ {4}}
$$

$$
= 4 x ^ {- 2} - 2 x ^ {- 3} + \frac {1}{4} x ^ {- 4}
$$

(Take care with the last simplification: writing $\textstyle { \frac { 1 } { 4 x ^ { 4 } } } = 4 x ^ { - 4 }$ is a common error!)

Then diferentiating gives

$$
\mathrm{f} ^ {\prime} (x) = - 8 x ^ {- 3} + 6 x ^ {- 4} - x ^ {- 5}
$$

and diferentiating a second time then gives

$$
\mathrm{f} ^ {\prime \prime} (x) = 2 4 x ^ {- 4} - 2 4 x ^ {- 5} + 5 x ^ {- 6}
$$

We can now subsitute $x = 1$ to give

$$
\mathrm{f} ^ {\prime \prime} (1) = 2 4 - 2 4 + 5 = 5
$$

so the correct answer is C.
