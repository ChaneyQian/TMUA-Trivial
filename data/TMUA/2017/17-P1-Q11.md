## 题目
The sequence $x_n$ is defined by the rules

$$
x_1 = 7
$$

$$
x_{n+1} = \frac{23x_n - 53}{5x_n + 1}
$$

The first three terms in the sequence are 7, 3, 1

What is the value of $x_{100}$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad -5
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 0
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 7
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
We have $x _ { 1 } = 7 , x _ { 2 } = 3 , x _ { 3 } = 1$ . It is not at all clear what the pattern here is, so we calculate the next few terms:

$$
x _ {4} = \frac {2 3 \times 1 - 5 3}{5 \times 1 + 1} = \frac {- 3 0}{6} = - 5
$$

$$
x _ {5} = \frac {2 3 \times (- 5) - 5 3}{5 \times (- 5) + 1} = \frac {- 1 6 8}{- 2 4} = 7
$$

so $x _ { 6 } = 3 , x _ { 7 } = 1 , x _ { 8 } = - 5$ and so on. Thus the terms repeat in a cycle with length 4, and as $1 0 0 = 2 5 \times 4$ , we see that $x _ { 1 0 0 } = - 5$ , which is option A.
