## 题目
Given that

$$
\frac{dy}{dx} = 3x^2 - \frac{2-3x}{x^3}, \quad x \neq 0
$$

and $y = 5$ when $x = 1$, find $y$ in terms of $x$.

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad y = \frac{1}{3}x^3 + x^{-2} - 3x^{-1} + 6\frac{2}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad y = x^3 + \frac{1}{2}x^{-2} - 3x^{-1} + 6\frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad y = x^3 + x^{-2} - 3x^{-1} + 6
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad y = x^3 + x^{-2} - x^{-1} + 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad y = x^3 + 2x^{-2} - x^{-1} + 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad y = 3x^3 + x^{-2} - x^{-1} + 2
$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
We are given the derivative of $y$, and we want to find y itself, so we need to integrate. We start by expanding the fraction:

$$
\begin{array}{r} \frac {\mathrm{d} y}{\mathrm{d} x} = 3 x ^ {2} - \frac {2 - 3 x}{x ^ {3}} \\ = 3 x ^ {2} - 2 x ^ {- 3} + 3 x ^ {- 2} \end{array}
$$

so we can now integrate term-by-term to get

$$
y = x ^ {3} + x ^ {- 2} - 3 x ^ {- 1} + c
$$

Now as $y = 5$ when $x = 1$ , we can substitute to give

$$
5 = 1 ^ {3} + 1 ^ {- 2} - 3 \times 1 ^ {- 1} + c = - 1 + c
$$

so $c = 6$ and the answer is C.

Commentary: It turns out that there is a subtlety in this question which we usually ignore at school level. The question assumes that there is a ‘simple’ formula for y in terms of $x$. Since the function is not defined at $x = 0$, , it is possible that we have different constants when $x > 0$ and when $x < 0$ , so we could have, for example,

$$
y = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} x ^ {3} + x ^ {- 2} - 3 x ^ {- 1} + 6 & \text {when} x > 0 \\ x ^ {3} + x ^ {- 2} - 3 x ^ {- 1} + 8 & \text {when} x <   0 \end{array} \right.
$$

This may seem quite strange, but it would be a valid solution to the differential equation given in the question.
