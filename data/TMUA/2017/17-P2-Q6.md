## 题目
A sequence $u_0, u_1, u_2, \ldots$ is defined as follows:

$$
u_0 = 1
$$

$$
u_n = \int_0^1 4x u_{n-1}\,dx \quad \text{for } n \geq 1
$$

What is the value of $u_{1000}$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 2^{1000}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 4^{1000}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{4}{1000!}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{4}{1001!}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{2^{1000}}{1000!}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{4^{1000}}{1000!}
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad \frac{2^{1000}}{1001!}
$$

$$
\mathbf{H} \quad \frac{4^{1000}}{1001!}
$$

## 答案
A

## 解析
We calculate the first few values of $u _ { n }$ in the hope that this will show a clear pattern.

We have

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {u _ {0} = 1} \\ & {u _ {1} = \int_ {0} ^ {1} 4 x. 1 \mathrm{d} x} \\ & {\qquad = [ 2 x ^ {2} ] _ {0} ^ {1}} \\ & {\qquad = 2} \\ & {u _ {2} = \int_ {0} ^ {1} 4 x. 2 \mathrm{d} x} \\ & {\qquad = [ 4 x ^ {2} ] _ {0} ^ {1}} \\ & {\qquad = 4} \\ & {u _ {3} = \int_ {0} ^ {1} 4 x. 4 \mathrm{d} x} \\ & {\qquad = [ 8 x ^ {2} ] _ {0} ^ {1}} \\ & {\qquad = 8} \end{array}
$$

The pattern is now apparent: $u _ { n } = 2 ^ { n }$ , and so the answer is A.

If we wanted to prove this to be true, we could use induction. The result is clearly true when $n = 0$ , so assume that it is true when $n = k$ . Then

$$
\begin{array}{r l} u _ {k + 1} & = \int_ {0} ^ {1} 4 x. 2 ^ {k} \mathrm{d} x \\ & = [ 2. 2 ^ {k} x ^ {2} ] _ {0} ^ {1} \\ & = 2. 2 ^ {k} \\ & = 2 ^ {k + 1} \end{array}
$$

and so the result holds for $n = k + 1$ . Hence by induction, $u _ { n } = 2 ^ { n }$ for all $n \geqslant 0$
