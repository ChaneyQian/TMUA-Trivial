## 题目
Find the value of

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{90} \sin(10 + 90k)^\circ
$$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 0
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \sin 10^\circ
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \sin 100^\circ
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \sin 190^\circ
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \sin 280^\circ
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 1
$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
We start by writing out the first few terms of the summation, and writing each of them in terms of sin $1 0 ^ { \circ }$ or $\sin 100^{\circ}$:

$$
\begin{array}{l l} k = 0: & \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ} \\ k = 1: & \sin 1 0 0 ^ {\circ} \\ k = 2: & \sin 1 9 0 ^ {\circ} = - \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ} \\ k = 3: & \sin 2 8 0 ^ {\circ} = - \sin 1 0 0 ^ {\circ} \\ k = 4: & \sin 3 7 0 ^ {\circ} = \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ} \\ k = 5: & \sin 4 6 0 ^ {\circ} = \sin 1 0 0 ^ {\circ} \\ k = 6: & \sin 5 5 0 ^ {\circ} = - \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ} \\ k = 7: & \sin 6 4 0 ^ {\circ} = - \sin 1 0 0 ^ {\circ} \end{array}
$$

and we see that we repeat every four terms. The first four terms sum to zero, as do the next four, and so on.

In total, there are 91 terms, and $9 1 = 2 2 \times 4 + 3$ , so the only terms which contribute to the final sum are the last three. Therefore the whole sum is that from $k = 8 8 , k = 8 9 \mathrm { a n d } k = 9 0$ , giving

$$
\sin 1 0 ^ {\circ} + \sin 1 0 0 ^ {\circ} + (- \sin 1 0 ^ {\circ}) = \sin 1 0 0 ^ {\circ}.
$$

Hence the answer is C.
