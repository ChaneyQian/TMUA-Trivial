## 题目
Find the coefficient of the $x ^ { 4 }$ term in the expansion of

$$
x ^ {2} \left(2 x + \frac {1}{x}\right) ^ {6}
$$

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad 15
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 30
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad 60
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad 120
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad 240
$$
## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
We need the coefficient of $x ^ { 2 }$ in the bracketed term to get the term in $x ^ { 4 }$ in the whole expansion. We expand this using the binomial theorem to get

$$
x ^ {2} \left(2 x + \frac {1}{x}\right) ^ {6} = x ^ {2} \left((2 x) ^ {6} + \binom {6} {1} (2 x) ^ {5} \left(\frac {1}{x}\right) + \binom {6} {2} (2 x) ^ {4} \left(\frac {1}{x}\right) ^ {2} + \dots\right).
$$

We stop here as the power of x in this last term is $x ^ { 4 - 2 } = x ^ { 2 }$ , as we require. This term is then

$$
\begin{array}{c} x ^ {2} \binom {6} {2} (2 x) ^ {4} \left(\frac {1}{x}\right) ^ {2} = 1 5 x ^ {2}. 1 6 x ^ {4}. x ^ {- 2} \\ = 2 4 0 x ^ {4} \end{array}
$$

so the answer is E.
