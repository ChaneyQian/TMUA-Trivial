## 题目
$a$, $b$ and $c$ are real numbers with $a < b < c < 0$.

Which of the following statements **must** be true?

I. $ac < ab < a^2$

II. $b(c + a) > 0$

III. $\dfrac{c}{b} > \dfrac{a}{b}$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \text{none of them}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \text{I only}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \text{II only}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \text{III only}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \text{I and II only}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \text{I and III only}
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad \text{II and III only}
$$

$$
\mathbf{H} \quad \text{I, II and III}
$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
When thinking about inequalities involving negative numbers, we must take care to reverse the sign of any inequality when multiplying or dividing by a negative number. In this case, all three of $a , b$ and c are negative.

I We start with $a < b < c$ and multiply by $a < 0$ to obtain

$$
a ^ {2} > a b > a c
$$

so this statement must be true.

II Since $a < 0$ and $c < 0 , a + c < 0$ . Since $b < 0$ , it follows that $b ( a + c ) > 0$ . So this statement must be true.

III We start with $c > a$ (since $a < c )$ . Dividing by $b < 0$ then gives

$$
\frac {c}{b} <   \frac {a}{b}
$$

and so this statement must be false.

The correct answer is E.
