## 题目
Consider the following inequality:

$$
(*): \quad a|x| + 1 \leq |x - 2|
$$

where $a$ is a real constant.

Which one of the following describes the complete set of values of $a$ such that $(*)$ is true for all real $x$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad a \leq \frac{3}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad a \leq 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad a \leq \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad a \leq 0
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad a \leq -\frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad a \leq -1
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad a \leq -\frac{3}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{H} \quad \text{There are no such values of } a.
$$

## 解析
We would do well to sketch a graph for this question! There are two functions involved, $y =$ $a | x | + 1$ and $y = \left| x - 2 \right|$ , so we sketch these for diferent values of $a$.

The graph of $y = a | x | + 1$ is the graph of $y = | x |$ first stretched by a factor of a in the y-direction, and then translated by 1 in the positive y-direction.

The graph of $y = | x - 2 |$ is the graph of $y = | x |$ translated by 2 in the positive x-direction.

This gives us the following sketch:

![[Image/19-P2-Q18-sol1.jpg]]

What we can see from this sketch is that $y = a | x | + 1$ will be below $y = | x - 2 |$ when $a$ is sufficiently negative; when $a$ is positive, it will certainly intersect the graph of $y = \left| x - 2 \right|$ |. We can find the value of $a$ at which the behaviour changes by sketching the graph at this point and then doing some algebra:

![[Image/19-P2-Q18-sol2.jpg]]

We can work out the value of $a$ by noting that the graph passes through (2, 0):

$$
a \vert 2 \vert + 1 = 0
$$

therefore $a = - { \frac { 1 } { 2 } }$ , and hence the correct answer is $a \leq - { \frac { 1 } { 2 } }$ , which is option E.
