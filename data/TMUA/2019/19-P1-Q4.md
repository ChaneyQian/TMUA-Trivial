## 题目
The sequence $x_n$ is given by:

$$
x_1 = 10
$$

$$
x_{n+1} = \sqrt{x_n} \quad \text{for } n \geq 1
$$

What is the value of $x_{100}$?

[Note that $a^{b^c}$ means $a^{(b^c)}$]

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 10^{2^{99}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 10^{2^{100}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 10^{2^{-99}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 10^{2^{-100}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 10^{-2^{99}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 10^{-2^{100}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad 10^{-2^{-99}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{H} \quad 10^{-2^{-100}}
$$

## 解析
We work out the first few terms, writing them in the form of the given options:

$$
\begin{array}{l} x _ {1} = 1 0 \\ x _ {2} = \sqrt {1 0} = 1 0 ^ {\frac {1}{2}} = 1 0 ^ {2 ^ {- 1}} \\ x _ {3} = \sqrt {1 0 ^ {2 ^ {- 1}}} = \left(1 0 ^ {2 ^ {- 1}}\right) ^ {\frac {1}{2}} = 1 0 ^ {2 ^ {- 1} \times \frac {1}{2}} = 1 0 ^ {2 ^ {- 2}} \end{array}
$$

As this pattern continues, we will end up with $x _ { 1 0 0 } = 1 0 ^ { 2 ^ { - 9 9 } }$ , which is option C.
