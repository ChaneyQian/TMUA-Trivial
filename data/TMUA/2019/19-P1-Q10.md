What is the value of

\int_ {- 1} ^ {3} | x | (1 - x) \mathrm{d} x

$$

## 答案
F

## 解析
We do not have a rule for integrating |x|, so we will split this into two separate integrals, one from $x = - 1$ to $x = 0$ , where $| x | = - x$ , and one from x = 0 to $x = 3 .$ , where $| x | = x$ . This gives

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {- 1} ^ {3} | x | (1 - x) \mathrm{d} x & = \int_ {- 1} ^ {0} | x | (1 - x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {0} ^ {3} | x | (1 - x) \mathrm{d} x \\ & = \int_ {- 1} ^ {0} (- x) (1 - x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {0} ^ {3} x (1 - x) \mathrm{d} x \\ & = \int_ {- 1} ^ {0} - x + x ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {0} ^ {3} x - x ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x \\ & = \left[ - \frac {x ^ {2}}{2} + \frac {x ^ {3}}{3} \right] _ {- 1} ^ {0} + \left[ \frac {x ^ {2}}{2} - \frac {x ^ {3}}{3} \right] _ {0} ^ {3} \\ & = - (- \frac {1}{2} - \frac {1}{3}) + (\frac {9}{2} - 9) \\ & = \frac {5}{6} - \frac {9}{2} \\ & = - \frac {2 2}{6} \\ & = - \frac {1 1}{3} \end{array}
$$

so the correct answer is F.
