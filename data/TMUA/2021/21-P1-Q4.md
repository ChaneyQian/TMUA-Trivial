## 题目
Find the minimum value of the function

$$
2^{2x} - 2^{x+3} + 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad -16
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad -12
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad -8
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 0
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 20
$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
This function is a quadratic in $u = 2 ^ { x }$ ; we can rewrite it as

$$
u ^ {2} - 2 ^ {3} u + 4
$$

or

$$
u ^ {2} - 8 u + 4.
$$

We can complete the square to express this as

$$
(u - 4) ^ {2} - 4 ^ {2} + 4 = (u - 4) ^ {2} - 1 2.
$$

The minimum therefore occurs when $u = 4 .$ , at which point the function value is 12.

We must also check that this value can actually be attained: $u = 4$ if and only if $2 ^ { x } = 4$ , which happens when $x = 2 .$ , so this minimum is attained, and the correct answer is B.

The minimum can also be found using calculus: differentiating $u ^ { 2 } - 8 u + 4$ gives $2 u - 8 ;$ this is zero when $u = 4$ , at which point the function value is $4 ^ { 2 } - 8 \times 4 + 4 = - 1 2$ ; this is a minimum as the function is a quadratic with positive $u ^ { 2 }$ coefficient.
