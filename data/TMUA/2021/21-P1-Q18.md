## 题目
The curve with equation

$$
x = y^2 - 6y + 11
$$

is rotated $90^{\circ}$ clockwise about the point $P$ to give the curve $C$.

$P$ has $x$-coordinate $-2$ and $y$-coordinate $3$.

What is the equation of $C$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad y = -x^2 - 4x - 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad y = -x^2 - 4x - 5
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad y = -x^2 - 6x - 7
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad y = -x^2 - 6x - 11
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad y = x^2 - 4x + 5
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad y = x^2 + 4x + 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad y = x^2 - 6x + 11
$$

$$
\mathbf{H} \quad y = x^2 + 6x + 7
$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
We should start by sketching the original curve. This is a quadratic in $y .$ It does not factorise, but we can complete the square:

$$
x = (y - 3) ^ {2} + 2
$$

It therefore has its vertex at $y = 3 , x = 2$ , and $x \ge 2$ for all values of $y .$ . We can then sketch it:

![[Image/21-P1-Q18-sol1.jpg]]

The vertex of the parabola is 4 units to the right of $P .$

Now rotating this by $9 0 ^ { \circ }$ clockwise about $P$ moves the vertex to 4 units below $P ,$ , that is $( - 2 , - 1 )$ , giving the following:

![[Image/21-P1-Q18-sol2.jpg]]

This curve then has equation $y = - ( x + 2 ) ^ { 2 } - 1$ , which we can expand to $y = - x ^ { 2 } - 4 x - 5$ giving option B.
