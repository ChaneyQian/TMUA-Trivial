## 题目
Two circles have the same radius.

The centre of one circle is $(-2, 1)$.

The centre of the other circle is $(3, -2)$.

The circles intersect at two distinct points.

What is the equation of the straight line through the two points at which the circles intersect?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 3x - 5y = 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 3x + 5y = -1
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 5x - 3y = -4
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 5x - 3y = -1
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 5x - 3y = 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 5x - 3y = 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad 5x + 3y = 1
$$

## 答案
F

## 解析
## Approach 1: using the equations of the circles

We can find the equation by solving the equations of the circles simultaneously.

The equations are

$$
\begin{array}{l} (x + 2) ^ {2} + (y - 1) ^ {2} = r ^ {2} \\ (x - 3) ^ {2} + (y + 2) ^ {2} = r ^ {2} \end{array}
$$

(We do not know the radius, so we have called it $r ;$ we are given that both circles have the same radius.)

We can expand the brackets to give

$$
\begin{array}{l} {x ^ {2} + 4 x + 4 + y ^ {2} - 2 y + 1 = r ^ {2}} \\ {x ^ {2} - 6 x + 9 + y ^ {2} + 4 y + 4 = r ^ {2}} \end{array}
$$

Subtracting these equations then gives an equation which must be satisfied by any points lying on both circles:

$$
1 0 x - 6 y - 8 = 0
$$

which simplifies to $5 x - 3 y = 4 ,$ , and so the answer is $\mathrm { F } .$

## Approach 2: using geometry

The circles have the same radius, so by symmetry, the straight line through the two points of intersection must be the perpendicular bisector of the line segment joining the two circle centres, as shown in this diagram:

![[Image/21-P1-Q1-sol1.jpg]]

In our case, the two centres are at $( - 2 , 1 )$ and $( 3 , - 2 )$ , so the midpoint is as $\left( { \frac { 1 } { 2 } } , - { \frac { 1 } { 2 } } \right)$ . The gradient of the line joining the centres is $\textstyle { \frac { - 2 - 1 } { 3 - ( - 2 ) } } = - { \frac { 3 } { 5 } }$ . Therefore the gradient of the perpendicular bisector is $\frac { 5 } { 3 }$ , and it has equation

$$
y - (- \frac {1}{2}) = \frac {5}{3} (x - \frac {1}{2})
$$

Multiplying by 3 gives

$$
3 y + \frac {3}{2} = 5 (x - \frac {1}{2})
$$

which rearranges to give

$$
5 x - 3 y = 4
$$

which is option F.
