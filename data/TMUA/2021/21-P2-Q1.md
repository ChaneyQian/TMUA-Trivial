## 解析
We write the integral as powers of x and then integrate:

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\int_ {1} ^ {4} \Big (3 \sqrt {x} + \frac {4}{x ^ {2}} \Big) \mathrm{d} x = \int_ {1} ^ {4} 3 x ^ {\frac {1}{2}} + 4 x ^ {- 2} \mathrm{d} x} \\ & {\qquad = \left[ \frac {3 x ^ {\frac {3}{2}}}{\left(\frac {3}{2}\right)} + \frac {4 x ^ {- 1}}{- 1} \right] _ {1} ^ {4}} \\ & {\qquad = \left[ 2 x ^ {\frac {3}{2}} - 4 x ^ {- 1} \right] _ {1} ^ {4}} \\ & {\qquad = (2 \times 8 - 4 \times \frac {1}{4}) - (2 \times 1 - 4 \times 1)} \\ & {\qquad = 1 5 - (- 2)} \\ & {\qquad = 1 7} \end{array}
$$
