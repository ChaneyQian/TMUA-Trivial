## 题目
The first seven terms of a sequence of positive integers are:

$$
\begin{align*}
u_1 &= 15 \\
u_2 &= 21 \\
u_3 &= 30 \\
u_4 &= 37 \\
u_5 &= 44 \\
u_6 &= 51 \\
u_7 &= 59
\end{align*}
$$

Consider the following statement about this sequence:

$(*)$ If $n$ is a prime number, **then** $u_n$ is a multiple of 3 **or** $u_n$ is a multiple of 5.

What is the smallest value of $n$ that provides a **counterexample** to $(*)$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 2
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 5
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 6
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad 7
$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
To be a counterexample, we need the antecedent (the ‘if’ part) to be true but the consequent (the ‘then’ part) to be false.

<table><tr><td>n</td><td>n is prime</td><td>an multiple of 3 or 5</td></tr><tr><td>1</td><td>false</td><td>-</td></tr><tr><td>2</td><td>true</td><td>true</td></tr><tr><td>3</td><td>true</td><td>true</td></tr><tr><td>4</td><td>false</td><td>-</td></tr><tr><td>5</td><td>true</td><td>false</td></tr><tr><td>6</td><td>false</td><td>-</td></tr><tr><td>7</td><td>true</td><td>false</td></tr></table>

Therefore $n = 5$ and $n = 7$ are both counterexamples, and the smallest counterexample is $n = 5$ 5 which is option E.
