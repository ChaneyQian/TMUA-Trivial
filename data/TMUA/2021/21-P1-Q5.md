## 题目
The function $\mathrm{f}$ is such that

$$
\mathrm{f}(mn) = \begin{cases} \mathrm{f}(m)\mathrm{f}(n) & \text{if } mn \text{ is a multiple of } 3 \\ mn & \text{if } mn \text{ is not a multiple of } 3 \end{cases}
$$

for all positive integers $m$ and $n$.

Given that $\mathrm{f}(9) + \mathrm{f}(16) - \mathrm{f}(24) = 0$, what is the value of $\mathrm{f}(3)$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{8}{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 2\sqrt{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{16}{5}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 3\sqrt{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 4
$$

## 答案
F

## 解析
We can rewrite f(9), f(16) and f(24) using the information given about the function:

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \mathrm{f(9)} & = \mathrm{f(3} \times 3) \\ & = \mathrm{f(3)f(3)} \qquad \text {as 9 is a multiple of 3} \\ \mathrm{f(16)} & = \mathrm{f(16} \times 1) \\ & = 1 6 \qquad \text {as 16 is not a multiple of 3} \\ \mathrm{f(24)} & = \mathrm{f(3} \times 8) \\ & = \mathrm{f(3)f(8)} \qquad \text {as 24 is a multiple of 3} \\ & = \mathrm{f(3)f(8} \times 1) \\ & = \mathrm{f(3)} \times 8 \qquad \text {as 8 is not a multiple of 3} \\ & = 8 \mathrm{f(3)} \end{array}
$$

Substituting these into the $\mathrm { g i }$ ven equation then $\mathrm { g i }$ ves

$$
\left(\mathrm{f} (3)\right) ^ {2} + 1 6 - 8 \mathrm{f} (3) = 0
$$

which is a quadratic in f(3); we can factorise it to give

$$
\left(\mathrm{f} (3) - 4\right) ^ {2} = 0
$$

which has one (repeated) solution: f(3) = 4. Hence the answer is option F.
