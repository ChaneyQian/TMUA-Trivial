## 题目
Which one of the following statements is a sufficient condition for exactly three of the other four statements?

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad x \ge 0
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad x = 1
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad x = 0 \quad \text{or} \quad x = 1
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad x \ge 0 \quad \text{or} \quad x \le 1
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad x \ge 0 \quad \text{and} \quad x \le 1
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
We work through them sequentially:

A If $x \geq 0$ , then B may be false, C may be false, D may be false and E may be false

B If $x = 1$ , then A is true, C is true, D is true and E is true

C If $x = 0$ or x = 1, then A is true, B may be false, D is true and E is true

D If $x \ge 0 \mathrm { ~ o r ~ } x \le 1$ , then A may be false (for example if $x = - 1 )$ , B may be false, C may be false and E may be false

E If $x \geq 0$ and $x \leq 1$ , then A is true, B may be false, C may be false and D is true

The correct option is therefore C, which is sufficient for exactly three of the other four statements.
