## 题目
The ceiling of $x$, written ⌈x ⌉, is defined to be the value of $x$ rounded up to the nearest integer.

For example: $\lceil \pi \rceil = 4$, $\lceil 2.1 \rceil = 3$, $\lceil 8 \rceil = 8$

What is the value of the following integral?

$$
\int_ {0} ^ {9 9} 2 ^ {\lceil x \rceil} d x
$$

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad 2 ^ { 9 9 }
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 2 ^ { 9 9 } - 1
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad 2 ^ { 9 9 } - 2
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad 2 ^ { 1 0 0 }
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad 2 ^ { 1 0 0 } - 1
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad 2 ^ { 1 0 0 } - 2
$$

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
F

## 解析
The value of $2 ^ { \lceil x \rceil }$ changes at every integer. We have

$$
2 ^ {\lceil x \rceil} = \left\{ \begin{array}{l l} 2 ^ {0} & \text {when} x = 0 \\ 2 ^ {1} & \text {when} 0 <   x \leq 1 \\ 2 ^ {2} & \text {when} 1 <   x \leq 2 \\ 2 ^ {3} & \text {when} 2 <   x \leq 3 \\ \dots \\ 2 ^ {9 9} & \text {when} 9 8 <   x \leq 9 9 \end{array} \right.
$$

We therefore break the integral up into unit intervals. The value of $2 ^ { \lceil x \rceil }$ at the endpoints does not influence the value of the integral, so we have

$$
\begin{array}{r l} & {\int_ {0} ^ {9 9} 2 ^ {\lceil x \rceil} \mathrm{d} x = \int_ {0} ^ {1} 2 ^ {\lceil x \rceil} \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {1} ^ {2} 2 ^ {\lceil x \rceil} \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {2} ^ {3} 2 ^ {\lceil x \rceil} \mathrm{d} x + \dots + \int_ {9 8} ^ {9 9} 2 ^ {\lceil x \rceil} \mathrm{d} x} \\ & {\qquad = \int_ {0} ^ {1} 2 ^ {1} \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {1} ^ {2} 2 ^ {2} \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {2} ^ {3} 2 ^ {3} \mathrm{d} x + \dots + \int_ {9 8} ^ {9 9} 2 ^ {9 9} \mathrm{d} x} \\ & {\qquad = 2 ^ {1} + 2 ^ {2} + 2 ^ {3} + \dots + 2 ^ {9 9}} \end{array}
$$

as the integral of a constant is just the constant times the interval width. We now have a geometric series with first term $a = 2$ , common ratio $r = 2$ and $n = 9 9$ terms, so the sum is

$$
{\frac {a (r ^ {n} - 1)}{r - 1}} = {\frac {2 ^ {1} (2 ^ {9 9} - 1)}{2 - 1}} = 2 ^ {1 0 0} - 2
$$

which is option F.
