## 题目
For any integer $n \geq 0$

$$
\int_ {n} ^ {n + 1} f (x) \mathrm{d} x = n + 1
$$

Evaluate

$$
\int_ {0} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {1} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {2} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {4} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {5} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x
$$

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad -2
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 0
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad 18
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad 27
$$

C

## 解析
We work out the values of each of the individual integrals by splitting them into unit intervals:

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {0} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x & = \int_ {0} ^ {1} f (x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {1} ^ {2} f (x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {2} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x \\ & = 1 + 2 + 3 \\ & = 6 \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {1} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x & = \int_ {1} ^ {2} f (x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {2} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x \\ & = 2 + 3 \\ & = 5 \end{array}
$$

$$
\int_ {2} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x = 3
$$

$$
\begin{array}{c} {\int_ {4} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x = - \int_ {3} ^ {4} f (x) \mathrm{d} x} \\ {= - 4} \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {5} ^ {3} f (x) \mathrm{d} x & = - \int_ {3} ^ {5} f (x) \mathrm{d} x \\ & = - \left(\int_ {3} ^ {4} f (x) \mathrm{d} x + \int_ {4} ^ {5} f (x) \mathrm{d} x\right) \\ & = - (4 + 5) \\ & = - 9 \end{array}
$$

where for the final two integrals, we have used the rule $\textstyle \int _ { a } ^ { b } f ( x ) \mathrm { d } x = - \int _ { b } ^ { a } f ( x ) \mathrm { d } x$

Adding these gives the required sum:

$$
6 + 5 + 3 - 4 - 9 = 1
$$

which is option C.
