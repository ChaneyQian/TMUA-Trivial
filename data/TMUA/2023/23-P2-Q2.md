## 解析
We first expand the brackets to obtain

$$
\int_ {9} ^ {1 6} \frac {1}{x} + 2 + x \mathrm{d} x - \int_ {9} ^ {1 6} \frac {1}{x} - 2 + x \mathrm{d} x.
$$

Since these integrals have the same limits, we can combine them into a single integral to get

$$
\int_ {9} ^ {1 6} \left(\frac {1}{x} + 2 + x\right) - \left(\frac {1}{x} - 2 + x\right) = \int_ {9} ^ {1 6} 4 \mathrm{d} x = 4 \times (1 6 - 9) = 2 8,
$$

which is option F.
