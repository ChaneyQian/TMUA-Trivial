## 题目
P(x) and Q(x) are defined as follows:

$$
\mathrm{P} (x) = 2 ^ {x} + 4
$$

$$
Q (x) = 2 ^ {(2 x - 2)} - 2 ^ {(x + 2)} + 1 6
$$

Find the largest value of $x$ such that P(x) and Q(x) are in the ratio 4 : 1, respectively.

$$
\mathbf {A} \quad 5
$$

$$
\mathbf {B} \quad 12
$$

$$
\mathbf {C} \quad 32
$$

$$
\mathbf {D} \quad \log_{2} 3
$$

$$
\mathbf {E} \quad \log_{2} 5
$$

$$
\mathbf {F} \quad \log_{2} 12
$$

$$
\mathbf {G} \quad \log_{2} 20
$$

## 答案
F

## 解析
Note that $\mathrm { P } ( x )$ and $\operatorname { Q } ( x )$ being in the ratio 4 : 1 means that $\mathrm { P } ( x ) = 4 \mathrm { Q } ( x )$ , so

$$
2 ^ {x} + 4 = 4 \left(2 ^ {(2 x - 2)} - 2 ^ {(x + 2)} + 1 6\right).
$$

Simplifying the powers, noting that $4 = 2 ^ { 2 }$ , gives

$$
2 ^ {x} + 4 = 2 ^ {2 x} - 2 ^ {x + 4} + 6 4.
$$

This is a quadratic in $2 ^ { x }$ , so let us write $u = 2 ^ { x }$ . The equation can then be written as

$$
u + 4 = u ^ {2} - 1 6 u + 6 4.
$$

This rearranges to $u ^ { 2 } - 1 7 u + 6 0 = 0$ , which factorises as $( u - 5 ) ( u - 1 2 ) = 0 , \mathrm { s o } u = 5 \mathrm { o r } u = 1 2$. Therefore, as $u = 2 ^ { x }$ , we have $x = \log _ { 2 } 5$ or $x = \log _ { 2 } 1 2$ , and the largest possible value of $x$ is $\log _ { 2 } 1 2$ , which is option F.
