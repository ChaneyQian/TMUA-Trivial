## 题目
Given that

$$
\int_{0}^{1} (ax+b)\,\mathrm{d}x = 1
$$

and

$$
\int_{0}^{1} x(ax+b)\,\mathrm{d}x = 1
$$

find the value of $a + b$.

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad -1
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 0
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 2
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad 5
$$

## 答案
F

## 解析
We calculate the integrals in terms of $a$ and b:

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {0} ^ {1} (a x + b) \mathrm{d} x & = \left[ \frac {1}{2} a x ^ {2} + b x \right] _ {0} ^ {1} \\ & = \frac {1}{2} a + b \\ & = 1 \end{array}
$$

$$
\begin{array}{r l} \int_ {0} ^ {1} x (a x + b) \mathrm{d} x & = \int_ {0} ^ {1} (a x ^ {2} + b x) \mathrm{d} x \\ & = \left[ \frac {1}{3} a x ^ {3} + \frac {1}{2} b x ^ {2} \right] _ {0} ^ {1} \\ & = \frac {1}{3} a + \frac {1}{2} b \\ & = 1 \end{array}
$$

Multiplying the first equation by 2 and the second by 6 gives the simultaneous equations

$$
\begin{array}{r l} a + 2 b = 2 & \quad \text {(1)} \\ 2 a + 3 b = 6 & \quad \text {(2)} \end{array}
$$

Then $2 \times ( 1 ) - ( 2 )$ gives $b = - 2$. , so $a = 6 \AA$ . Therefore $a + b = 4$ and the correct answer is option F.
