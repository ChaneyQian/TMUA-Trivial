## 题目
The graphs of $y = x^2 + 5x + 6$ and $y = mx - 3$, where $m$ is a constant, are plotted on the same set of axes.

Given that the graphs do not meet, what is the complete range of possible values of $m$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad -1 < m < 11
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad m < -1,\ m > 11
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad -\sqrt{11} < m < \sqrt{11}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad m < -\sqrt{11},\ m > \sqrt{11}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad -11 < m < 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad m < -11,\ m > 1
$$

## 答案
A

## 解析
As the graphs do not meet, the simultaneous equations $y = x ^ { 2 } + 5 x + 6$ and $y = m x - 3$ have no solutions. Therefore $x ^ { 2 } + 5 x + 6 = m x - 3$ has no solutions, so the quadratic

$$
x ^ {2} + (5 - m) x + 9 = 0
$$

has no solutions and must therefore have a negative discriminant. This gives

$$
(5 - m) ^ {2} - 4 \times 1 \times 9 = 2 5 - 1 0 m + m ^ {2} - 3 6 = m ^ {2} - 1 0 m - 1 1 <   0.
$$

Factorising this quadratic in m gives

$$
(m - 1 1) (m + 1) <   0.
$$

This quadratic has roots −1 and 11, and as the coefficient of $m ^ { 2 }$ in $m ^ { 2 } - 1 0 m - 1 1$ is positive, the quadratic in $m$ is negative between the roots. So the complete range of possible values of $m$ is $- 1 < m < 1 1$ , which is option A.
