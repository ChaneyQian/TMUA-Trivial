## 题目
200 T-shirts have been bought for a Fun Run at a cost of £400 plus VAT at $17\frac{1}{2}\%$. The cost of entry for the run is £5 per person. What is the minimum number of entries needed in order to cover the total cost of the T-shirts?

A 40 $\qquad$ B 47 $\qquad$ C 80 $\qquad$ D 84 $\qquad$ E 94

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Since $1\%$ of £400 = £4, the total VAT charged was £$4 \times 17.5 =$ £70, giving a total cost of £400 + £70 = £470. Therefore the minimum number of entries needed is 94.
