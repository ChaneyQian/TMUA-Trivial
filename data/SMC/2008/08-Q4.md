## 题目
In this subtraction, $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$ are digits. What is the value of $P + Q + R + S$?

## 解析
From the units column we see that $S = 0$. Then the tens column shows that $R = 9$, the hundreds column that $Q = 1$, and the thousands that $P = 6$. So $P + Q + R + S = 16$.
