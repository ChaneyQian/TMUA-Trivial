## 题目
How many pairs of real numbers $(x, y)$ satisfy the equation $(x + y)^2 = (x + 3)(y - 3)$ ?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C 2 $\qquad$ D 4 $\qquad$ E infinitely many

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Let $X = x + 3$ and $Y = y - 3$. Then the given equation becomes $(X + Y)^2 = XY$.

So $X^2 + XY + Y^2 = 0$. However $X^2$, $Y^2$ and $XY\,(= (X + Y)^2)$ are non-negative. Hence $X = Y = 0$; so $x = -3$ and $y = 3$ is the only solution.
