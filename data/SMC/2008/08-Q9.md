## 题目
What is the remainder when the 2008-digit number $222 \ldots 22$ is divided by 9?

A 8 $\qquad$ B 6 $\qquad$ C 4 $\qquad$ D 2 $\qquad$ E 0

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
A number is divisible by 9 if, and only if, the sum of its digits is divisible by 9. The given number is $N + 2$, where $N = 222\ldots 220$ has 2007 2s. Since $2007 = 223 \times 9$, $N$ is divisible by 9 and the required remainder is therefore 2.
