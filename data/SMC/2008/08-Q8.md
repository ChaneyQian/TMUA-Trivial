## 题目
In the figure shown, $AB = AF$ and $ABC$, $AFD$, $BFE$ and $CDE$ are all straight lines.

Which of the following expressions gives $z$ in terms of $x$ and $y$?

![[Image/08-Q8-fig1.png]]

A $\dfrac{y - x}{2}$ $\qquad$ B $y - \dfrac{x}{2}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{y - x}{3}$ $\qquad$ D $y - \dfrac{x}{3}$ $\qquad$ E $y - x$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
In triangle $ACD$, $\angle CAD = (180 - x - y)^\circ$.

As $AB = AF$, triangle $ABF$ is isosceles hence $\angle ABF = \angle AFB = \tfrac{1}{2}(x + y)^\circ$.

Thus $\angle DFE = \angle AFB = \tfrac{1}{2}(x + y)^\circ$ (vertically opposite angles). Now in triangle $DFE$, $\angle FDE = (180 - y)^\circ$. Hence $z^\circ = 180^\circ - \angle DFE - \angle FDE = \tfrac{1}{2}(y - x)^\circ$.
