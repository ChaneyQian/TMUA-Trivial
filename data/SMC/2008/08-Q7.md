## 题目
A newspaper headline read ‘Welsh tortoise recaptured 1.8 miles from home after 8 months on the run’. Assuming the tortoise travelled in a straight line, roughly how many minutes did the tortoise take on average to ‘run’ one foot?
[1 mile = 5280 feet]

A 3 $\qquad$ B 9 $\qquad$ C 16 $\qquad$ D 36 $\qquad$ E 60

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
In 1.8 miles there are $1.8 \times 5280$ feet $= 18 \times 528$ feet, while in 8 months there are roughly $8 \times 30 \times 24 \times 60$ minutes. Hence the time to 'run' one foot in minutes is roughly

$$\dfrac{10 \times 30 \times 20 \times 60}{20 \times 500} = 36\text{ minutes}.$$
