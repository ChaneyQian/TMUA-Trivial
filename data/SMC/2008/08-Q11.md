## 题目
The distance between two neighbouring dots in the dot lattice is 1 unit. What, in square units, is the area of the region where the two rectangles overlap?

![[Image/08-Q11-fig1.png]]

A 6 $\qquad$ B $6\frac{1}{4}$ $\qquad$ C $6\frac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ D 7 $\qquad$ E $7\frac{1}{2}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Let the six points where lines meet on the dot lattice be $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$, $F$ as shown and let the other two points of intersection be $P$ (where $AC$ and $BF$ meet) and $Q$ (where $CE$ and $DF$ meet).

Triangles $APB$ and $CPF$ are similar with base lengths in the ratio $3:5$. Hence triangle $CPF$ has height $\tfrac{5}{8} \times 2 = \tfrac{5}{4}$ units and base length 5 units so that its area is $\tfrac{1}{2} \times \tfrac{5}{4} \times 5$ square units. Since the same is true of triangle $CQF$, the required area is $\tfrac{5}{4} \times 5 = 6\tfrac{1}{4}$ square units.
