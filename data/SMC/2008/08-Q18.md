## 题目
The shaded square of the lattice shown has area 1. What is the area of the circle through the points $X$, $Y$ and $Z$?

![[Image/08-Q18-fig1.png]]

A $\dfrac{9\pi}{2}$ $\qquad$ B $8\pi$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{25\pi}{2}$ $\qquad$ D $25\pi$ $\qquad$ E $50\pi$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Since $XY^2 = 18$, $YZ^2 = 32$ and $XZ^2 = 50$, we have $XZ^2 = XY^2 + YZ^2$. Hence by the converse of Pythagoras' Theorem, $\angle XYZ = 90^\circ$. Since the angle in a semi-circle is $90^\circ$ the segment $XZ$ is the diameter of the specified circle. Hence the radius is $\tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{50}$ and the area of the circle is $\dfrac{50\pi}{4} = \dfrac{25\pi}{2}$.
