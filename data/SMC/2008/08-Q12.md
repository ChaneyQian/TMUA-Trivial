## 题目
Mr and Mrs Stevens were married on a Saturday in July 1948. On what day of the week did their diamond wedding anniversary fall this year?

A Monday $\qquad$ B Tuesday $\qquad$ C Thursday $\qquad$ D Friday $\qquad$ E Saturday

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
There are 365 days in a normal year and 366 in a leap year. Apart from certain exceptions (none of which occurs in this period) a leap year occurs every 4 years. Now $365 = 7 \times 52 + 1$ and $366 = 7 \times 52 + 2$. Hence each date moves on by 5 days every 4 years. So in 60 years, it moves on 75 days. Since $75 = 7 \times 10 + 5$, that means it moves on to a Thursday.
