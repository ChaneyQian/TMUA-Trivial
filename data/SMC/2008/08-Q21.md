## 题目
The fraction $\dfrac{2008}{1998}$ may be written in the form $a + \dfrac{1}{b + \dfrac{1}{c + \dfrac{1}{d}}}$ where $a$, $b$, $c$ and $d$ are positive integers. What is the value of $d$?

A 2 $\qquad$ B 4 $\qquad$ C 5 $\qquad$ D 199 $\qquad$ E 1998

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Since $2008/1998$ lies between 1 and 2, $a = 1$. Subtracting 1 and inverting gives $b + 1/(c + 1/d) = 1998/10 = 199 + 4/5$ so that $b = 199$. Then $1/(c + 1/d) = 4/5$ so that $c + 1/d = 5/4$ and this gives $c = 1$ and $d = 4$.

**Note**: This is an example of a continued fraction.
