## 题目
The numbers $x$, $y$ and $z$ satisfy the equations

$$x + y + 2z = 850, \qquad x + 2y + z = 950, \qquad 2x + y + z = 1200.$$

What is their mean?

A 250 $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{1000}{3}$ $\qquad$ C 750 $\qquad$ D 1000 $\qquad$ E More information is needed.

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Adding the three given equations gives $4(x + y + z) = 3000$. Therefore $x + y + z = 750$. So the mean is $\tfrac{750}{3} = 250$.
