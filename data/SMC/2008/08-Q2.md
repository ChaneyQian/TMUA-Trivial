## 题目
A giant thresher shark weighing 1250 pounds, believed to be the heaviest ever caught, was landed by fisherman Roger Nowell off the Cornish coast in November 2007. The fish was sold by auction at Newlyn Fish Market for £255. Roughly, what was the cost per pound?

A 5p $\qquad$ B 20p $\qquad$ C 50p $\qquad$ D £2 $\qquad$ E £5

## 中文题干
2007 年 11 月，渔民 Roger Nowell 在康沃尔海岸附近捕获了一条重达 1250 磅的巨型长尾鲨，据信是有史以来捕获的最重的一条。这条鱼在 Newlyn Fish Market 以 £255 拍卖售出。每磅的价格大约是多少？
