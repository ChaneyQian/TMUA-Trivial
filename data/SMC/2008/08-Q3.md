## 题目
What is the value of $\sqrt{\dfrac{1}{2^6} + \dfrac{1}{6^2}}$ ?

A $\dfrac{1}{10}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{1}{9}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{1}{3}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{5}{24}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{7}{24}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
$$\dfrac{1}{2^6} + \dfrac{1}{6^2} = \dfrac{3^2 + 2^4}{2^6 \times 3^2} = \dfrac{25}{2^6 \times 3^2} = \dfrac{5^2}{(2^3 \times 3)^2}.$$

Hence the answer is $\dfrac{5}{2^3 \times 3} = \dfrac{5}{24}$.
