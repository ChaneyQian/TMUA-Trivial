## 题目
Which one of the following rational numbers *cannot* be expressed as $\dfrac{1}{m} + \dfrac{1}{n}$ where $m$, $n$ are different positive integers?

A $\dfrac{3}{4}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{3}{5}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{3}{6}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{3}{7}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{3}{8}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
By inspection

$$\dfrac{3}{4} = \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{4}; \qquad \dfrac{3}{5} = \dfrac{1}{2} + \dfrac{1}{10}; \qquad \dfrac{3}{6} = \dfrac{1}{3} + \dfrac{1}{6}; \qquad \dfrac{3}{8} = \dfrac{1}{4} + \dfrac{1}{8}.$$

However $\dfrac{3}{7} \ne \dfrac{1}{m} + \dfrac{1}{n}$. [To see why, suppose that $\dfrac{3}{7} = \dfrac{1}{m} + \dfrac{1}{n}$ and note that $\dfrac{1}{m} > \dfrac{1}{n}$ or vice versa. We will suppose the former. Then $\dfrac{1}{m} \ge \dfrac{3}{14} > \dfrac{3}{15}$ and so $\dfrac{1}{m} > \dfrac{1}{5}$ and $m < 5$. Also $\dfrac{1}{m} < \dfrac{3}{7}$ and so $3m > 7$. Hence $m \ge 3$. So $m = 4$ or $m = 3$. However $\dfrac{3}{7} - \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{28}$ and $\dfrac{3}{7} - \dfrac{1}{3} = \dfrac{2}{21}$ neither of which has the form $\dfrac{1}{n}$.]
