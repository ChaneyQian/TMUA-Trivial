## 题目
Positive integers $m$ and $n$ are such that $2^m + 2^n = 1280$. What is the value of $m + n$?

A 14 $\qquad$ B 16 $\qquad$ C 18 $\qquad$ D 32 $\qquad$ E 640

## 中文题干
正整数 $m$ 和 $n$ 满足 $2^m + 2^n = 1280$。$m + n$ 的值是多少？

## 答案
C

## 解析
Since $1280 = 2^8 \times 5 = 2^8(2^0 + 2^2) = 2^8 + 2^{10}$, we may take $m = 8$ and $n = 10$ (or vice versa) to get $m + n = 8 + 10 = 18$. It is easy to check that there are no other possibilities.
