## 题目
The diagram shows a square $ABCD$ and a right-angled triangle $ABE$. The length of $BC$ is 3. The length of $BE$ is 4. What is the area of the shaded region?

## 解析
We let $F$ be the point where the lines $AE$ and $CD$ meet.

Because $ABCD$ is a square, $\angle BCF = 90^\circ$. It follows, because angles on a line have sum $180^\circ$, that $\angle FCE = 90^\circ$.

Hence, in the triangles $ABE$ and $FCE$ we have $\angle ABE = \angle FCE = 90^\circ$. Also, we see that $\angle BEA = \angle CEF$. It follows that these triangles are similar.

![[Image/16-Q6-sol1.png]]

Therefore

$$\frac{FC}{AB} = \frac{CE}{BE}. \qquad (1)$$

Because $ABCD$ is a square $AB = BC = 3$. We also have $BE = 4$. Therefore $CE = BE - BC = 4 - 3 = 1$.

It follows from equation $(1)$ that

$$\frac{FC}{3} = \frac{1}{4}.$$

Hence $FC = \dfrac{3}{4}$.

The shaded region $ABCF$ is a trapezium. The area of a trapezium is given by the formula $\frac{1}{2}h(a+b)$, where $a$ and $b$ are the lengths of the parallel sides, and $h$ is the distance between these sides. It follows that the area of the trapezium $ABCF$ is given by

$$\tfrac{1}{2}BC(AB + FC) = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(3 \times \left(3 + \tfrac{3}{4}\right)\right) = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(3 \times \tfrac{15}{4}\right) = \tfrac{45}{8} = 5\tfrac{5}{8}.$$
