## 题目
How many times does the digit 9 appear in the answer to $987654321 \times 9$?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C 5 $\qquad$ D 8 $\qquad$ E 9

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
There seems no better method here than just doing the multiplication.

$$9 \times 987\,654\,321 = 8\,888\,888\,889.$$

So the number of times that the digit $9$ occurs in the answer is $1$.
