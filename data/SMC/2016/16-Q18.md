## 题目
The circumference of a circle with radius 1 is divided into four equal arcs. Two of the arcs are ‘turned over’ as shown. What is the area of the shaded region?

![[Image/16-Q18-fig1.png|155]]

A 1 $\qquad$ B $\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ C $\frac{1}{2}\pi$ $\qquad$ D $\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ E 2

## 解析
Because the four arcs are equal, their endpoints form a square. This square is shown in the diagram.

The four segments $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$, indicated in the diagram, are congruent. The shaded region is made up of the region $T$, together with the segments $P$ and $Q$. It follows that the area of this region is equal to that of the square which is made up of $T$ and the two segments $R$ and $S$.

The diagonals of the square are diameters of the circle. Therefore they have length $2$. It follows that the square has sides of length $\sqrt{2}$. Hence the area of the square is $\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2}$, that is, $2$.

![[Image/16-Q18-sol1.png]]

Therefore the area of the shaded region is $2$.
