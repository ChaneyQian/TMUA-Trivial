## 题目
On a Monday, all prices in Isla's shop are 10% more than normal. On Friday all prices in Isla's shop are 10% less than normal. James bought a book on Monday for £5.50. What would be the price of another copy of this book on Friday?

A £5.50 $\qquad$ B £5.00 $\qquad$ C £4.95 $\qquad$ D £4.50 $\qquad$ E £4.40

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The book cost £5.50 on a Monday after prices have risen by $10\,\%$. Therefore the price before this increase was £5. It follows that on a Friday, after prices have been cut by $10\,\%$, the price of the book has fallen by 50p to £4.50.
