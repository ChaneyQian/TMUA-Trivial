## 题目
For which value of $k$ is $\sqrt{2016} + \sqrt{56}$ equal to $14^k$?

A $\dfrac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{3}{4}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{5}{4}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{3}{2}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{5}{2}$

## 中文题干
$\sqrt{2016} + \sqrt{56}$ 等于 $14^k$，$k$ 的值是多少？

## 答案
D

## 解析
We have already seen (in the solution to question 12) that $2016 = 2^5 \times 3^2 \times 7$. It follows that $2016 = (2^4 \times 3^2) \times 2 \times 7 = (2^2 \times 3)^2 \times 2 \times 7 = 12^2 \times 14$. Therefore $\sqrt{2016} = 12\sqrt{14}$.

Also, $\sqrt{56} = \sqrt{2^2 \times 14} = 2\sqrt{14}$. Hence $\sqrt{2016} + \sqrt{56} = 12\sqrt{14} + 2\sqrt{14} = 14\sqrt{14}$.

Now, $14\sqrt{14} = 14^1 \times 14^{1/2} = 14^{1+1/2} = 14^{3/2}$. Therefore $k = \dfrac{3}{2}$.
