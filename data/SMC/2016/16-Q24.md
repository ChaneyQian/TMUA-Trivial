## 题目
The diagram shows a square $PQRS$. The arc $QS$ is a quarter circle. The point $U$ is the midpoint of $QR$ and the point $T$ lies on $SR$. The line $TU$ is a tangent to the arc $QS$. What is the ratio of the length of $TR$ to the length of $UR$?

![[Image/16-Q24-fig1.png|205]]

A $3 : 2$ $\qquad$ B $4 : 3$ $\qquad$ C $5 : 4$ $\qquad$ D $7 : 6$ $\qquad$ E $9 : 8$

## 答案
B

## 解析
It is convenient, because $U$ is the midpoint of $QR$, to choose units so that the square has sides of length $2$. It follows that each of $UQ$ and $UR$ has length $1$.

Suppose that $TR$ has length $x$. Then the length of $TS$ is $2 - x$.

Let $V$ be the point where the line $TU$ is tangent to the arc.

Because the two tangents from a point to a circle have the same length, $UV = UQ = 1$ and $VT = TS = 2 - x$. It follows that $UT = UV + VT = 1 + (2 - x) = 3 - x$.

![[Image/16-Q24-sol1.png]]

In the right-angled triangle $URT$ the hypotenuse has length $3 - x$, and the other two sides have lengths $1$ and $x$. Therefore, by Pythagoras’ theorem applied to this triangle,

$$1^2 + x^2 = (3 - x)^2.$$

It follows that

$$1 + x^2 = 9 - 6x + x^2,$$

and hence

$$6x = 8.$$

We deduce that $x = \dfrac{4}{3}$.

Therefore $TR : UR = \frac{4}{3} : 1 = 4 : 3$.
