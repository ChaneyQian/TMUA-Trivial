## 题目
Points are drawn on the sides of a square, dividing each side into $n$ equal parts (so, in the example shown, $n = 4$).

![[Image/16-Q8-fig1.png|190]]

The points are joined in the manner indicated, to form several small squares (24 in the example, shown shaded) and some triangles. How many small squares are formed when $n = 7$?

A 56 $\qquad$ B 84 $\qquad$ C 140 $\qquad$ D 840 $\qquad$ E 5040

## 答案
B

## 解析
The main diagonals of the square shown in the question divide the square into four quarters each of which contains $1 + 2 + 3$ small squares. So the number of small squares when $n = 4$ is $4 \times (1+2+3) = 4 \times 6 = 24$, as the question says.

It can be seen from the diagram on the right that, similarly, when $n = 7$ the number of small squares is given by $4 \times (1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + 6) = 4 \times 21 = 84$.
