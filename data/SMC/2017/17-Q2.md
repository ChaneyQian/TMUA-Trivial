## 题目
Last year, an earthworm from Wigan named Dave wriggled into the record books as the largest found in the UK. Dave was 40 cm long and had a mass of 26 g. What was Dave's mass per unit length?

A $0.6\text{ g/cm}$ $\qquad$ B $0.65\text{ g/cm}$ $\qquad$ C $0.75\text{ g/cm}$ $\qquad$ D $1.6\text{ g/cm}$ $\qquad$ E $1.75\text{ g/cm}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Dave's mass per unit length is $\dfrac{26}{40}$ g/cm. We have

$$\frac{26}{40} = \frac{26}{10 \times 4} = \frac{2.6}{4} = 0.65.$$

Therefore Dave's mass per unit length is $0.65$ g/cm.
