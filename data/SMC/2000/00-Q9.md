## 题目
What is the value of $(61^2 - 39^2) \div (51^2 - 49^2)$?

A 10.5 $\qquad$ B 11 $\qquad$ C 12 $\qquad$ D 21 $\qquad$ E 22

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
$a^2 - b^2 = (a + b)(a - b)$. Thus $\dfrac{\left(61^2 - 39^2\right)}{\left(51^2 - 49^2\right)} = \dfrac{100 \times 22}{100 \times 2} = 11$.
