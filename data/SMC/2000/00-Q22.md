## 题目
Given that $0 < b < a$ and $a^2 + b^2 = 6ab$, what is the value of $\dfrac{a + b}{a - b}$?

A $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ $\qquad$ B $\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2} - 1}$ $\qquad$ D $2\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ E $\sqrt{6}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
$\left(\dfrac{a + b}{a - b}\right)^2 = \dfrac{a^2 + 2ab + b^2}{a^2 - 2ab + b^2} = \dfrac{6ab + 2ab}{6ab - 2ab} = 2$. As $0 < b < a$, $\left(\dfrac{a + b}{a - b}\right) > 0$ and hence $\left(\dfrac{a + b}{a - b}\right) = \sqrt{2}$.
