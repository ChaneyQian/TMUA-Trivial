## 题目
When written in decimal form, $\dfrac{1}{81} = 0.0123456790123\dots$. What is the value of $\dfrac{2}{81}$ correct to six decimal places?

A 0.02468 $\qquad$ B 0.024681 $\qquad$ C 0.024690 $\qquad$ D 0.024691 $\qquad$ E 0.0246914

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
$\dfrac{1}{81} = 0.012345 + 0.00000067\ldots$; therefore $\dfrac{2}{81} = 0.024690 + 0.0000013\ldots = 0.0246913\ldots = 0.024691$ to six decimal places.
