## 题目
An attempt by two men to sit in every one of the 72 000 seats in Cardiff's Millennium Stadium to raise money for charity ended in painful failure, despite their wearing man-made-fibre tracksuits to help them slide from seat to seat. Before the pain stopped them, they had managed 64 000 seats between them in 27 hours. On average, roughly how long did each man take per seat?

A 0.5 seconds $\qquad$ B 1 second $\qquad$ C 1.5 seconds $\qquad$ D 2 seconds $\qquad$ E 3 seconds

## 中文题干
两名男子为了给慈善募捐，尝试坐遍 Cardiff 的 Millennium Stadium 中的全部 72 000 个座位，尽管他们穿着人造纤维运动服以帮助自己在座位间滑动，但最终仍以痛苦的失败告终。在疼痛迫使他们停下之前，两人在 27 小时内一共坐了 64 000 个座位。平均而言，每人每个座位大约耗时多久？

## 答案
E

## 解析
The average time each man took per seat is approximately $(27 \times 60 \times 60 \div 32000)$ seconds $= (27 \times 9 \div 80)$ seconds $\approx 3$ seconds.
