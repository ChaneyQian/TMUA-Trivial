## 题目
Last year, a newspaper reported that the mean forecourt price of petrol was 73.3 pence per litre for unleaded petrol. The price before tax, however, was 15.2 pence per litre. What was the approximate percentage increase from the price before tax to the mean forecourt price?

A $20\%$ $\qquad$ B $40\%$ $\qquad$ C $80\%$ $\qquad$ D $400\%$ $\qquad$ E $500\%$

## 中文题干
去年，一家报纸报道称无铅汽油的加油站平均售价为每升 73.3 便士。然而，税前价格为每升 15.2 便士。从税前价格到加油站平均售价的百分比增幅大约是多少？
