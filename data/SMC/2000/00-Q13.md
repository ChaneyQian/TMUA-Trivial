## 题目
A trapezium has parallel sides of length $a$ and $b$, and height $h$. Sides $a$ and $b$ are both decreased by $10\%$ and the height $h$ is increased by $10\%$. What is the percentage change in the area of the trapezium?

![[Image/00-Q13-fig1.png]]

A $10\%$ decrease $\qquad$ B $1\%$ decrease $\qquad$ C no change $\qquad$ D $10\%$ increase $\qquad$ E $30\%$ increase

## 中文题干
一个梯形的平行边长度分别为 $a$ 和 $b$，高为 $h$。边 $a$ 和 $b$ 均减少 $10\%$，且高 $h$ 增加 $10\%$。该梯形面积的百分比变化是多少？

## 答案
B

## 解析
New area $= \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{9a}{10} + \dfrac{9b}{10}\right) \times \dfrac{11h}{10} = \dfrac{99}{100} \times \dfrac{1}{2}(a + b)h = 99\%$ of previous area of trapezium.
