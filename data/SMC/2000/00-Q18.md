## 题目
The curve $y = x^2 - 6x + 11$ is rotated through $180^\circ$ about the origin. What is the equation of the new curve?

![[Image/00-Q18-fig1.png]]

A $y = x^2 + 6x + 11$  
B $y = x^2 - 6x - 11$  
C $y = -x^2 + 6x - 11$  
D $y = -x^2 - 6x + 11$  
E $y = -x^2 - 6x - 11$

## 答案
E

## 解析
$x^2 - 6x + 11 = (x - 3)^2 + 2$. When the curve is rotated $180^\circ$ about the origin, the equation of the new curve will be $y = -(x + 3)^2 - 2$ i.e. $y = -x^2 - 6x - 9 - 2$ i.e. $y = -x^2 - 6x - 11$.

**Note**: the image of point $(a, b)$ under a $180^\circ$ rotation about the origin is the point $(-a, -b)$. An alternative method, therefore, is to replace $x$ and $y$ in the original equation by $-x$ and $-y$ respectively.
