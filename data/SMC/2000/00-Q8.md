## 题目
A van travels from Newcastle to South Shields at an average speed of $30\text{ mph}$ and returns by the same route at an average speed of $40\text{ mph}$. Which of the following is closest to the van's average speed for the whole journey?

A $33\text{ mph}$ $\qquad$ B $34\text{ mph}$ $\qquad$ C $35.5\text{ mph}$ $\qquad$ D $36\text{ mph}$ $\qquad$ E more information needed

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Let the distance between Newcastle and South Shields be $x$ miles. Then the time for the whole journey is $\left(\dfrac{x}{30} + \dfrac{x}{40}\right)$ hours $= \dfrac{7x}{120}$ hours. Thus the average speed is $\left(2x \div \dfrac{7x}{120}\right)$ mph $= \dfrac{240}{7}$ mph $= 34\frac{2}{7}$ mph.
