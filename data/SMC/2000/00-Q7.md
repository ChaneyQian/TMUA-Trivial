## 题目
The three regular hexagons are all the same size. $X$, $Y$ and $Z$ denote the values of the shaded areas in the hexagons, as shown. Which of the following statements is true?

![[Image/00-Q7-fig1.png]]

A $X$ is equal to $Y$ but not to $Z$  
B $X$ is equal to $Z$, but not to $Y$  
C $Y$ is equal to $Z$, but not to $X$  
D $X$ is equal to $Y$ and to $Z$  
E $X$, $Y$ and $Z$ are all different
