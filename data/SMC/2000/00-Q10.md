## 题目
A square piece of wood, of side $8\text{ cm}$, is painted black and fixed to a table. An equal square, painted white, is placed on the table alongside the black square and has a point $P$ marked one quarter of the way along a diagonal, as shown. Whilst keeping the same orientation on the table and always remaining in contact with the black square, the white square now slides once around the black square. Through what distance does $P$ move?

![[Image/00-Q10-fig1.png]]

A $32\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ B $48\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ C $64\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ D $72\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ E $80\text{ cm}$

## 答案
C

## 解析
The dotted line shows the locus of $P$ as the white square slides around the black square. The locus is a square of side $16\text{ cm}$ and hence $P$ moves through a distance of $64\text{ cm}$.

**Note**: It may be shown that the the total distance moved by $P$ is $64\text{ cm}$, irrespective of the position of $P$ on the white square.
