## 题目
The figure on the right shows two parallel lines, $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$. Line $\ell_1$ is a tangent to circles $C_1$ and $C_3$, line $\ell_2$ is a tangent to circles $C_2$ and $C_3$ and the three circles touch as shown. Circles $C_1$ and $C_2$ have radius $s$ and $t$ respectively. What is the radius of circle $C_3$?

## 解析
The diagram shows points $A$, $B$ and $C$, the centres of circles $C_1$, $C_2$ and $C_3$ respectively.

Let the radius of circle $C_3$ be $r$.

The positions of points $D$, $E$ and $F$ are as shown on the diagram.

In $\triangle ACE$: $AE^2 = AC^2 - EC^2 = (r + s)^2 - (r - s)^2 = 4rs$.

In $\triangle BCF$: $BF^2 = BC^2 - CF^2 = (r + t)^2 - (r - t)^2 = 4rt$.

Hence $DA = DE - AE = BF - AE = 2\sqrt{rt} - 2\sqrt{rs}$.

Also, $DB = 2r - (s + t)$.

![[Image/00-Q25-sol1.png]]

Therefore, in $\triangle ABD$:

$$(s + t)^2 = \left(2r - (s + t)\right)^2 + \left(2\sqrt{rt} - 2\sqrt{rs}\right)^2$$

i.e.

$$(s + t)^2 = 4r^2 - 4r(s + t) + (s + t)^2 + 4rt - 8\sqrt{rt}\sqrt{rs} + 4rs$$

i.e.

$$0 = 4r^2 - 8r\sqrt{st}$$

i.e. $r = 0$ (impossible) or $r = 2\sqrt{st}$.
