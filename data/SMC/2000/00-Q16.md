## 题目
A roll of adhesive tape is wound round a central cylindrical core of radius $3\text{ cm}$. The outer radius of a roll containing $20\text{ m}$ of tape is $4\text{ cm}$. Approximately, what is the outer radius of a roll containing $80\text{ m}$ of tape?

A $5\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ B $5.5\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ C $6\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ D $7\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ E $12\text{ cm}$

## 答案
C

## 解析
The cross-section of the $20\text{ m}$ tape has area $\pi\left(4^2 - 3^2\right)\text{ cm}^2 = 7\pi\text{ cm}^2$. Therefore, the $80\text{ m}$ tape should have a cross-section of area $28\pi\text{ cm}^2$. Hence, the outer radius of the $80\text{ m}$ roll will be approximately $\sqrt{37}\text{ cm}$.
