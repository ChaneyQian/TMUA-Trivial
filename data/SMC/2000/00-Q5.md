## 题目
$ABCD$ is a rectangle. $P$ is the midpoint of $AD$; the length of $BQ$ is one third of the length of $BC$. What fraction of the area of the rectangle is the area of the shaded quadrilateral $ABQP$?

![[Image/00-Q5-fig1.png]]

A $\frac{5}{12}$ $\qquad$ B $\frac{2}{5}$ $\qquad$ C $\frac{3}{8}$ $\qquad$ D $\frac{1}{3}$ $\qquad$ E $\frac{7}{16}$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Let $AB$ and $AD$ be of length $b$ and $h$ respectively. Then the area of $ABCD = bh$ and the area of $ABQP = \dfrac{1}{2}b\left(\dfrac{h}{2} + \dfrac{h}{3}\right) = \dfrac{5bh}{12}$. Thus the required ratio $= \dfrac{5}{12}$.
