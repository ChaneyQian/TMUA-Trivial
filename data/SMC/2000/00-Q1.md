## 题目
What is the remainder when the number $743589 \times 301647$ is divided by 5?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C 2 $\qquad$ D 3 $\qquad$ E 4

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The units digit of the product is 3; therefore the remainder when that number is divided by 5 is also 3.
