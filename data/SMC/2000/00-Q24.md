## 题目
How many pairs of positive integers $(x, y)$ satisfy the equation $\sqrt{x} - \sqrt{17} = \sqrt{y}$?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C 2 $\qquad$ D 17 $\qquad$ E infinitely many

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Squaring both sides of the equation gives $y = x - 2\sqrt{17x} + 17$. The right-hand side will be a positive integer if $x$ is of the form $17n^2$ for an integer $n \ge 2$, and the equation then becomes $y = 17n^2 - 34n + 17 = 17(n - 1)^2$.

As there are infinitely many $n \ge 2$, there are infinitely many pairs of positive integers $(x, y)$ which satisfy the equation e.g. $(68, 17)$, $(153, 68)$, $(272, 153)$ etc.
