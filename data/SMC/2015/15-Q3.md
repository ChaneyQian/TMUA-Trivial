## 题目
When Louise had her first car, 50 litres of petrol cost £40. When she filled up the other day, she noticed that 40 litres of petrol cost £50. By approximately what percentage has the cost of petrol increased over this time?

A 50% $\qquad$ B 56% $\qquad$ C 67% $\qquad$ D 75% $\qquad$ E 80%

## 答案
B

## 解析
We note first that £40 is $40 \times 100$ pence, that is, $4000$ pence. Therefore when Louise had her first car, the cost of petrol, in pence per litre, was
$$\frac{4000}{50} = 80.$$

Similarly, when she filled up the other day, the cost of petrol, in pence per litre, was
$$\frac{5000}{40} = 125.$$

It follows that the cost of petrol has increased by $(125 - 80)$ pence per litre, that is, by $45$ pence per litre. As a percentage of the original price this increase is
$$\frac{45}{80} \times 100 = \frac{4500}{80} = \frac{450}{8} = 56\frac{1}{4}.$$

Therefore the increase in the cost of petrol over the given period is approximately $56\%$.
