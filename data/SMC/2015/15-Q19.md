## 题目
Three squares are arranged as shown so that their bases lie on a straight line. Also, the corners $P$, $Q$ and $R$ lie on a straight line. The middle square has sides that are $8\text{ cm}$ longer than the sides of the smallest square. The largest square has sides of length $50\text{ cm}$. There are two possible values for the length (in cm) of the sides of the smallest square. Which of the following are they?

![[Image/15-Q19-fig1.png|300]]

A 2, 32 $\qquad$ B 4, 42 $\qquad$ C 4, 34 $\qquad$ D 32, 40 $\qquad$ E 34, 42

A

## 解析
We let $S$, $T$, $U$ and $V$ be the points shown in the figure.

We let the side length of the smallest square be $x\text{ cm}$.

It follows that the middle square has sides of length $(x + 8)\text{ cm}$

Since the lines $QU$ and $RV$ are both perpendicular to $UV$ they are parallel lines.

![[Image/15-Q19-sol1.png]]

Therefore, as $PQR$ is a straight line, the corresponding angles $\angle PQS$ and $\angle QRT$ are equal.

Because the figure is made up of squares, $\angle PSU = \angle QTV = 90^\circ$. Therefore, as angles on a line add up to $180^\circ$, we have $\angle PSQ = \angle QTR = 90^\circ$. It follows that the right-angled triangles $PSQ$ and $QTR$ are similar because they have the same angles. Therefore
$$\frac{QS}{PS} = \frac{RT}{QT}.$$

Now $QS$ has length $8\text{ cm}$, $PS$ has length $x\text{ cm}$, $RT$ has length $(50 - (x + 8))\text{ cm} = (42 - x)\text{ cm}$ and $QT$ has length $(x + 8)\text{ cm}$. It follows that
$$\frac{8}{x} = \frac{42 - x}{x + 8}.$$

Multiplying both sides of this equation by $x(x + 8)$, we see that this last equation is equivalent to
$$8(x + 8) = x(42 - x),$$
that is,
$$8x + 64 = 42x - x^2.$$

This last equation may be rearranged to give
$$x^2 - 34x + 64 = 0.$$

We can now factorize the quadratic in this equation to give
$$(x - 32)(x - 2) = 0.$$

Therefore
$$x = 2 \text{ or } x = 32.$$

We deduce that the possible lengths, in cm, of the sides of the smallest square are $2$ and $32$.
