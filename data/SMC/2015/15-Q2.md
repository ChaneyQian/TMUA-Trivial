## 题目
What is the sum of all the solutions of the equation $6x = \dfrac{150}{x}$?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 5 $\qquad$ C 6 $\qquad$ D 25 $\qquad$ E 156

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
We have,
$$\begin{aligned}
6x = \frac{150}{x} &\Longleftrightarrow 6x^2 = 150 \\
&\Longleftrightarrow x^2 = 25 \\
&\Longleftrightarrow x = -5 \text{ or } x = 5.
\end{aligned}$$

Therefore the equation has the two solutions $-5$ and $5$. It follows that the sum of all the solutions of the equation is $-5 + 5 = 0$.

Note that here the symbol $\Longleftrightarrow$ stands for *if, and only if*.
