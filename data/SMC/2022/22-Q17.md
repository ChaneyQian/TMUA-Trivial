## 题目
The shape shown is made by removing four equilateral triangles with side-length 1 from a regular octagon with side-length 1.

![[Image/22-Q17-fig1.png|202]]

What is the area of the shape?

A $2 - 2\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ B $2 + 2\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ C $2 + 2\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ D $3 - 2\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ E $2 - 2\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{3}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
As the diagram on the right shows, a regular octagon with side length $1$ may be obtained by cutting four triangular corners from the square.

![[Image/22-Q17-sol1.png]]

Each of these corners is an isosceles right-angled triangles with a hypotenuse of length $1$. It follows, by Pythagoras' Theorem that the other two sides of these triangles have length $1/\sqrt{2}$.

It follows that the side length of the square is given by $1/\sqrt{2} + 1 + 1/\sqrt{2} = 1 + \sqrt{2}$. Therefore the area of the square is $(1 + \sqrt{2})^2 = 1 + 2\sqrt{2} + 2 = 3 + 2\sqrt{2}$.

The four triangular corners fit together to make a square of side length $1$ and hence area $1$.

Therefore the area of the octagon is $(3 + 2\sqrt{2}) - 1 = 2 + 2\sqrt{2}$.

From this area we need to subtract the area of the four equilateral triangles that are removed from the octagon to make the shaded shape.

We let $PQR$ be an equilateral triangle with side length $1$, and let $PK$ be the perpendicular from $P$ to $QR$ as shown.

![[Image/22-Q17-sol2.png]]

It can be checked that $K$ is the midpoint of $QR$ (see Problem 17.1) and hence that $QK$ has length $\dfrac{1}{2}$. By Pythagoras' Theorem applied to the right-angled triangle $PQK$ we have $QK^2 + PK^2 = PQ^2$. Therefore

$$PK^2 = PQ^2 - QK^2 = 1 - \left(\dfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 = \dfrac{3}{4}.$$

Therefore $PK = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}$.

We can now deduce that the area of the triangle $PQR$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}(QR \times PK) = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(1 \times \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}\right) = \dfrac{1}{4}\sqrt{3}$.

It now follows that the area of the shape is

$$2 + 2\sqrt{2} - 4 \times \dfrac{1}{4}\sqrt{3} = 2 + 2\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{3}.$$
