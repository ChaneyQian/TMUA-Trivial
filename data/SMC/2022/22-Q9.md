## 题目
A light-nanosecond is the distance that a photon can travel at the speed of light in one billionth of a second. The speed of light is $3 \times 10^8\text{ ms}^{-1}$.

How far is a light-nanosecond?

A $3\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ B $30\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ C $3\text{ m}$ $\qquad$ D $30\text{ m}$ $\qquad$ E $300\text{ m}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
One billionth of a second is $\dfrac{1}{10^9}$ seconds.

Hence, in one billionth of a second light travels

$$\dfrac{1}{10^9} \times (3 \times 10^8)\text{ m} = \dfrac{3}{10}\text{ m} = 30\text{ cm}.$$

Therefore a light-nanosecond is $30\text{ cm}$.
