## 题目
Three squares $GQOP$, $HJNO$ and $RKMN$ have vertices which sit on the sides of triangle $FIL$ as shown. The squares have areas of 10, 90 and 40 respectively.

![[Image/22-Q23-fig1.png|400]]

What is the area of triangle $FIL$?

A 220.5 $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{21}{5}\sqrt{10}$ $\qquad$ C 252 $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{21}{2}\sqrt{10}$ $\qquad$ E 441

## 答案
A

## 解析
We let $S$ be the foot of the perpendicular from the point $I$ to $HJ$. We let $h$, $x$ and $y$ be the lengths of $IS$, $HS$ and $SJ$, respectively.

The square $GQOP$ has area $10$ and hence side length $\sqrt{10}$.

![[Image/22-Q23-sol1.png]]

Similarly, the side length of the square $HJNO$ is $\sqrt{90} = 3\sqrt{10}$ and the side length of the square $RKMN$ is $\sqrt{40} = 2\sqrt{10}$.

We have $GQ = \sqrt{10}$ and $HQ = HO - QO = 3\sqrt{10} - \sqrt{10} = 2\sqrt{10}$. We leave it to the reader to check that the triangles $GPF$, $HQG$ and $ISH$ are similar. It follows that $FP = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{10}$ and $h = 2x$.

We also have $JR = JN - RN = 3\sqrt{10} - 2\sqrt{10} = \sqrt{10}$ and $RK = 2\sqrt{10}$. The triangles $KML$, $JRK$ and $ISJ$ are similar. Therefore $ML = 4\sqrt{10}$ and $h = \dfrac{1}{2}y$.

Since $h = 2x$ and $h = \dfrac{1}{2}y$, it follows that $y = 4x$. Also $x + y = 3\sqrt{10}$. Therefore $5x = 3\sqrt{10}$. Hence $x = \dfrac{3}{5}\sqrt{10}$, $y = \dfrac{12}{5}\sqrt{10}$ and $h = \dfrac{6}{5}\sqrt{10}$.

We now have that the base of the triangle $FIL$ is

$$FL = FP + PO + ON + NM + ML = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{10} + \sqrt{10} + 3\sqrt{10} + 2\sqrt{10} + 4\sqrt{10} = \dfrac{21}{2}\sqrt{10}.$$

and the height of the triangle is

$$IS + HO = \dfrac{6}{5}\sqrt{10} + 3\sqrt{10} = \dfrac{21}{5}\sqrt{10}.$$

Hence the area of the triangle $FIL$ is given by

$$\dfrac{1}{2}(\text{base} \times \text{height}) = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{21}{2}\sqrt{10} \times \dfrac{21}{5}\sqrt{10}\right) = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{21^2}{10} \times 10\right) = 220.5.$$
