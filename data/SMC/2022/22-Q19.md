## 题目
How many pairs of real numbers $(x, y)$ satisfy the simultaneous equations $x^2 - y = 2022$ and $y^2 - x = 2022$?

A infinitely many $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C 2 $\qquad$ D 3 $\qquad$ E 4

## 答案
E

## 解析
By subtracting the equation $y^2 - x = 2022$ from the equation $x^2 - y = 2022$, we obtain

$$x^2 - y^2 + x - y = 0. \qquad (1)$$

The left hand side of equation (1) factorizes to give

$$(x - y)(x + y + 1) = 0. \qquad (2)$$

It follows that either $x - y = 0$ or $(x + y + 1) = 0$. That is, either $y = x$ or $y = -x - 1$.

When $y = x$ both equations of the question are equivalent to the equation $x^2 - x = 2022$. We can rewrite this equation as

$$x^2 - x - 2022 = 0. \qquad (3)$$

Equation (3) is a quadratic equation of the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, with $a = 1$, $b = -1$ and $c = -2022$. Therefore $b^2 - 4ac = (-1)^2 + 4 \times 2022$ which is greater than $0$.

It follows that equation (3) has two distinct real number solutions, say $x_1$ and $x_2$. It follows that the two pairs of real numbers $(x_1, x_1)$ and $(x_2, x_2)$ satisfy the simultaneous equations of the question.

It can be checked that when $y = -x - 1$ both equations of the question are equivalent to the equation

$$x^2 + x - 2021 = 0. \qquad (4)$$

Equation (4) is a quadratic equation of the form $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, with $a = 1$, $b = 1$ and $c = -2021$. Therefore $b^2 - 4ac = 1^2 + 4 \times 2021$ which is greater than $0$.

It follows that equation (4) has two distinct real number solutions, say $x_3$ and $x_4$. It follows that the two pairs of real numbers $(x_3, -x_3 - 1)$ and $(x_4, -x_4 - 1)$ satisfy the simultaneous equations of the question.

In these last two solutions $y \ne x$ and therefore they are different from the first two solutions.

We can therefore conclude that there are four pairs of real numbers that satisfy the simultaneous equations of the question.
