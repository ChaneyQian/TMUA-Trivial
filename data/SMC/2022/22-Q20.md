## 题目
A square is inscribed inside a quadrant of a circle. The circle has radius 10.

![[Image/22-Q20-fig1.png|188]]

What is the area of the square?

A $25\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ B 36 $\qquad$ C $12\pi$ $\qquad$ D 40 $\qquad$ E $30\sqrt{2}$

## 答案
D

## 解析
We let $O$ be the centre of the circle, and $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$ be the vertices of the square, as shown. We let $OL$ be the perpendicular from $O$ to $QR$, and $K$ be the point where this perpendicular meets $PS$.

![[Image/22-Q20-sol1.png]]

We let $s$ be the side length of the square. It may be checked (see Problem 20.1) that the triangles $OLQ$ and $OLR$ are congruent. It follows that $QL = \dfrac{1}{2}QR = \dfrac{1}{2}s = PK$.

It may be checked that $OKP$ is a right-angled isosceles triangle (see Problem 20.2). Therefore $OK = PK = \dfrac{1}{2}s$.

It follows that in the right-angled triangle $OLQ$ we have $QL = \dfrac{1}{2}s$, $OL = OK + KL = \dfrac{1}{2}s + s = \dfrac{3}{2}s$ and $OQ = 10$.

Therefore, by Pythagoras' Theorem

$$\left(\dfrac{1}{2}s\right)^2 + \left(\dfrac{3}{2}s\right)^2 = 10^2.$$

Hence

$$\dfrac{1}{4}s^2 + \dfrac{9}{4}s^2 = 100.$$

That is,

$$\dfrac{5}{2}s^2 = 100.$$

Hence

$$s^2 = \dfrac{2}{5} \times 100 = 40.$$

It follows that the area of the square is $40$.
