## 题目
When the expression $\dfrac{(2^2 - 1) \times (3^2 - 1) \times (4^2 - 1) \times (5^2 - 1)}{(2 \times 3) \times (3 \times 4) \times (4 \times 5) \times (5 \times 6)}$ is simplified, which of the following is obtained?

A $\dfrac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{1}{3}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{1}{4}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{1}{5}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{1}{6}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Using the standard factorization $n^2 - 1 = (n - 1)(n + 1)$, we have

$$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{(2^2 - 1) \times (3^2 - 1) \times (4^2 - 1) \times (5^2 - 1)}{(2 \times 3) \times (3 \times 4) \times (4 \times 5) \times (5 \times 6)} &= \dfrac{(1 \times 3) \times (2 \times 4) \times (3 \times 5) \times (4 \times 6)}{(2 \times 3) \times (3 \times 4) \times (4 \times 5) \times (5 \times 6)} \\ &= \dfrac{1 \times 2 \times 3 \times 4}{2 \times 3 \times 4 \times 5} \\ &= \dfrac{1}{5}. \end{aligned}$$
