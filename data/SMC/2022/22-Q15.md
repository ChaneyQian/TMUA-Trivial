## 题目
The hare and the tortoise had a race over $100\text{ m}$, in which both maintained constant speeds. When the hare reached the finish line, it was $75\text{ m}$ in front of the tortoise. The hare immediately turned around and ran back towards the start line.

How far from the finish line did the hare and the tortoise meet?

A 54 $\qquad$ B 60 $\qquad$ C 64 $\qquad$ D $66\frac{2}{3}$ $\qquad$ E 72

## 答案
B

## 解析
The tortoise is $75\text{ m}$ behind the hare when the hare has run the full $100\text{ m}$. Therefore the tortoise runs $25\text{ m}$ in the same time as the hare runs $100\text{ m}$. Hence the hare runs at four times the speed of the tortoise.

When the hare turns round, the hare and the tortoise are $75\text{ m}$ apart and running towards each other.

Since the hare is running at four times the speed of the tortoise, when they meet the hare has run $\dfrac{4}{5}$ths of the $75\text{ m}$ they were apart when the hare turned around. The tortoise has run $\dfrac{1}{5}$th of this distance.

Therefore they meet at a distance $\dfrac{4}{5} \times 75\text{ m}$, that is, $60\text{ m}$, from the finish line.
