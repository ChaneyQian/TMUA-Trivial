## 题目
The numbers $x$ and $y$ are such that $3^x + 3^{y+1} = 5\sqrt{3}$ and $3^{x+1} + 3^y = 3\sqrt{3}$.

What is the value of $3^x + 3^y$?

A $\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ B $2\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ C $3\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ D $4\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ E $5\sqrt{3}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Note that $3^{x+1} = 3(3^x)$ and $3^{y+1} = 3(3^y)$.

Therefore the equations given in the question may be rewritten as

$$3^x + 3(3^y) = 5\sqrt{3} \qquad (1)$$

and

$$3(3^x) + 3^y = 3\sqrt{3}. \qquad (2)$$

Adding equations (1) and (2) gives

$$4(3^x + 3^y) = 8\sqrt{3}. \qquad (3)$$

Therefore

$$3^x + 3^y = 2\sqrt{3}. \qquad (4)$$
