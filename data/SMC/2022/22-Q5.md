## 题目
What is the value of $\dfrac{1}{1.01} + \dfrac{1}{1.1} + \dfrac{1}{1} + \dfrac{1}{11} + \dfrac{1}{101}$?

A 2.9 $\qquad$ B 2.99 $\qquad$ C 3 $\qquad$ D 3.01 $\qquad$ E 3.1

## 答案
C

## 解析
**Note**

The key to an efficient solution is to regroup the terms so as to simplify the arithmetic.

We have

$$\begin{aligned} \dfrac{1}{1.01} + \dfrac{1}{1.1} + \dfrac{1}{1} + \dfrac{1}{11} + \dfrac{1}{101} &= \left(\dfrac{1}{1.01} + \dfrac{1}{101}\right) + \left(\dfrac{1}{1.1} + \dfrac{1}{11}\right) + \dfrac{1}{1} \\ &= \left(\dfrac{100}{101} + \dfrac{1}{101}\right) + \left(\dfrac{10}{11} + \dfrac{1}{11}\right) + \dfrac{1}{1} \\ &= \dfrac{101}{101} + \dfrac{11}{11} + \dfrac{1}{1} \\ &= 1 + 1 + 1 \\ &= 3. \end{aligned}$$
