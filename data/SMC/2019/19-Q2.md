## 题目
What is the value of $(2019 - (2000 - (10 - 9))) - (2000 - (10 - (9 - 2019)))$?

A 4040 $\qquad$ B 40 $\qquad$ C $-400$ $\qquad$ D $-4002$ $\qquad$ E $-4020$

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
We evaluate the expression by first evaluating the innermost brackets and then working outwards.

$$\begin{aligned}
&\big(2019 - (2000 - (10-9))\big) - \big(2000 - (10 - (9-2019))\big)\\
&= \big(2019 - (2000 - 1)\big) - \big(2000 - (10 - (-2010))\big)\\
&= \big(2019 - (2000 - 1)\big) - \big(2000 - (10 + 2010)\big)\\
&= (2019 - 1999) - (2000 - 2020)\\
&= (2019 - 1999) - (-20)\\
&= 20 + 20\\
&= 40.
\end{aligned}$$
