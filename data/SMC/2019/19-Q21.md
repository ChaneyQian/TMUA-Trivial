## 题目
In a square garden $PQRT$ of side $10\text{ m}$, a ladybird sets off from $Q$ and moves along edge $QR$ at $30\text{ cm}$ per minute. At the same time, a spider sets off from $R$ and moves along edge $RT$ at $40\text{ cm}$ per minute.

What will be the shortest distance between them, in metres?

A 5 $\qquad$ B 6 $\qquad$ C $5\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ D 8 $\qquad$ E 10

## 答案
D

## 解析
Let $L$ be the point that the ladybird reaches after $t$ minutes. Then $QL = 30t\text{ cm}$. The side length of the square is $10\text{ m}$ which is the same as $1000\text{ cm}$. Therefore the length of $LR$ is $(1000 - 30t)\text{ cm}$.

Let $S$ be the point that the spider reaches after $t$ minutes. Then $RS = 40t\text{ cm}$.

Let the length of $LS$ be $x\text{ cm}$. This is the distance between the ladybird and the spider after $t$ minutes.

![[Image/19-Q21-sol1.png|300]]

Because $PQRT$ is a square, $\angle LRS = 90^\circ$. By Pythagoras’ Theorem applied to the triangle $LRS$, we have

$$\begin{aligned}
x^2 &= (1000 - 30t)^2 + (40t)^2\\
&= 1\,000\,000 - 60\,000t + 900t^2 + 1600t^2\\
&= 2500t^2 - 60\,000t + 1\,000\,000\\
&= 2500(t^2 - 24t + 400)\\
&= 2500\big((t-12)^2 + 256\big).
\end{aligned}$$

Because $(t-12)^2 \ge 0$ for all values of $t$, it follows that $x^2 \ge 2500 \times 256$ for all values of $t$. Since $x^2 = 2500 \times 256$ when $t = 12$, it follows that the smallest value of $x^2$ is $2500 \times 256$. Therefore the smallest value taken by $x$ is $\sqrt{2500 \times 256}$, which is equal to $50 \times 16$, that is, $800$. Hence the shortest distance between the ladybird and the spider is $800\text{ cm}$ which is $8\text{ m}$.
