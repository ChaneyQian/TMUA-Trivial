## 题目
Used in measuring the width of a wire, one mil is equal to one thousandth of an inch. An inch is about $2.5\text{ cm}$.

Which of these is approximately equal to one mil?

A $\dfrac{1}{40}\text{ mm}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{1}{25}\text{ mm}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{1}{4}\text{ mm}$ $\qquad$ D $25\text{ mm}$ $\qquad$ E $40\text{ mm}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
One mil is equal to one thousandth of an inch. An inch is about $2.5\text{ cm}$, which is the same as $25\text{ mm}$. Therefore one mil is approximately $\dfrac{25}{1000}\text{ mm}$, which is the same as $\dfrac{1}{40}\text{ mm}$.
