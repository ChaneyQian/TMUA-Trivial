## 题目
Alison folds a square piece of paper in half along the dashed line shown in the diagram. After opening the paper out again, she then folds one of the corners onto the dashed line.

![[Image/19-Q18-fig1.png|140]]

What is the value of $\alpha$?

## 答案
E

## 解析
Let the vertices of the square be $P$, $Q$, $R$ and $S$, as shown. Let $T$ be the position to which $P$ is folded, and let $U$ and $V$ be the points shown in the diagram.

![[Image/19-Q18-sol1.png|280]]

Because after the fold the triangle $PSV$ coincides with the triangle $TSV$, these triangles are congruent. In particular $TS = PS$ and $\angle PSV = \angle TSV$.

Therefore $SUT$ is a triangle with a right angle at $U$ and in which $SU = \frac{1}{2}SR = \frac{1}{2}PS = \frac{1}{2}TS$. It follows that [see Exercise 12.4] $\angle TSU = 60^\circ$.

Because $\angle USP = 90^\circ$ and $\angle PSV = \angle TSV$, it follows that $\angle TSV = \frac{1}{2}(90 - 60)^\circ = 15^\circ$.

Therefore, because the sum of the angles in the triangle $TSV$ is $180^\circ$, we have

$$\alpha + 15 + 90 = 180,$$

and therefore $\alpha = 75$.
