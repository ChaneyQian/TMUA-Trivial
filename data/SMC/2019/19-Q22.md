## 题目
A function $f$ satisfies the equation $(n - 2019)f(n) - f(2019 - n) = 2019$ for every integer $n$.

What is the value of $f(2019)$?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C $2018 \times 2019$ $\qquad$ D $2019^2$ $\qquad$ E $2019 \times 2020$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Putting $n = 0$ in the equation

$$(n - 2019)f(n) - f(2019 - n) = 2019 \qquad (1)$$

gives

$$-2019f(0) - f(2019) = 2019 \qquad (2)$$

from which it follows that

$$f(2019) = -2019f(0) - 2019. \qquad (3)$$

Putting $n = 2019$ in equation (1) gives

$$-f(0) = 2019 \qquad (4)$$

and hence

$$f(0) = -2019. \qquad (5)$$

Substituting from (5) in (3) gives

$$\begin{aligned}
f(2019) &= -2019 \times -2019 - 2019\\
&= 2019 \times 2019 - 2019\\
&= 2019(2019 - 1)\\
&= 2019 \times 2018.
\end{aligned}$$

Therefore, the value of $f(2019)$ is $2018 \times 2019$.
