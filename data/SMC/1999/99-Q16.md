## 题目
When rounded to 3 significant figures, the number $x$ is written as 1000. What is the largest range of possible values of $x$?

A $999 \le x < 1001$  
B $995 \le x < 1005$  
C $990 \le x < 1010$  
D $999.5 \le x < 1005$  
E $999.5 \le x < 1000.5$

## 中文题干
当四舍五入保留 3 位有效数字时，数 $x$ 记作 1000。$x$ 的所有可能取值的最大范围是什么？

## 答案
D

## 解析
$x$ must be closer to $1000$ than it is to $999$ and also closer to $1000$ than it is to $1010$.
