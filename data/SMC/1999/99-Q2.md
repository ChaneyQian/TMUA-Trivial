## 题目
What is the largest number of Sundays that there can be in any one year?

A 50 $\qquad$ B 51 $\qquad$ C 52 $\qquad$ D 53 $\qquad$ E 54

## 中文题干
在任意一年中，最多可以有多少个星期日？

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
If January 1st (or, in a leap year, January 1st or January 2nd) falls on a Sunday, then there will be $53$ Sundays in that particular year.
