## 题目
$P$ is a vertex of a cuboid and $Q$, $R$ and $S$ are three points on the edges as shown. $PQ = 2\text{ cm}$, $PR = 2\text{ cm}$ and $PS = 1\text{ cm}$. What is the area, in $\text{cm}^2$, of triangle $QRS$?

## 答案

## 解析
By the Theorem of Pythagoras, $QR = \sqrt{8}\text{ cm} = 2\sqrt{2}\text{ cm}$, $QS = \sqrt{5}\text{ cm}$ and $RS = \sqrt{5}\text{ cm}$. If $T$ is the midpoint of $QR$, then $TS^2 = \left(\sqrt{5}\right)^2 - \left(\sqrt{2}\right)^2 = 5 - 2 = 3$ and therefore $TS = \sqrt{3}\text{ cm}$. The area of triangle $QRS = \frac{1}{2}QR \times TS = \left(\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{3}\right)\text{ cm}^2 = \sqrt{6}\text{ cm}^2$.
