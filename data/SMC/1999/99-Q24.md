## 题目
The figure shows a hexagon $AZBXCY$ made from four congruent tiles. The shape and position of the tiles are given by triangle $ABC$ and the three reflections of triangle $ABC$ in the lines determined by its sides. For example, $ABZ$ is the image of $ABC$ when reflected in the line determined by $AB$. If a polygon is made from five tiles whose shape and position are determined by a quadrilateral and the four reflections of that quadrilateral in the lines determined by its sides, what is the smallest possible number of sides of the resulting polygon?

![[Image/99-Q24-fig1.png]]

A 4 $\qquad$ B 5 $\qquad$ C 6 $\qquad$ D 7 $\qquad$ E 8

## 中文题干
如图所示，六边形 $AZBXCY$ 由四块全等的图块拼成。这些图块的形状和位置由三角形 $ABC$ 以及三角形 $ABC$ 关于由其各边所确定的直线的三个反射给出。例如，$ABZ$ 是 $ABC$ 关于由 $AB$ 确定的直线反射所得的像。如果一个多边形由五块图块拼成，它们的形状和位置由一个四边形以及该四边形关于由其各边所确定的直线的四个反射确定，那么所得多边形的边数最少可能是多少？

## 解析
If the original tile is an isosceles trapezium made from three equilateral triangles then a quadrilateral (which is also an isosceles trapezium) will result.
