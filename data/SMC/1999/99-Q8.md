## 题目
Two numbers differ by 9 and have sum 99. What is the ratio of the larger number to the smaller?

A $5:4$ $\qquad$ B $6:5$ $\qquad$ C $7:6$ $\qquad$ D $8:7$ $\qquad$ E $9:8$

## 中文题干
两个数的差为 9，和为 99。较大数与较小数的比是多少？

## 答案
B

## 解析
$x - y = 9$; $x + y = 99$. Adding gives $2x = 108$ and therefore $x = 54$ and hence $y = 45$.

The ratio $x{:}y = 54{:}45 = 6{:}5$.

**Note** As we are interested only in the ratio rather than the numbers themselves, the problem could be reduced to finding the ratio of two numbers which differ by $1$ and have sum $11$.
