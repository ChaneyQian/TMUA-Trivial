## 题目
Just one of the following is a prime number. Which one is it?

A $1000^2 + 111^2$ $\qquad$ B $555^2 + 666^2$ $\qquad$ C $2000^2 - 999^2$ $\qquad$ D $1001^2 + 1002^2$ $\qquad$ E $1001^2 + 1003^2$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
B cannot be prime since $111$ is clearly a factor of it; C cannot be prime since it equals $2999 \times 1001$ (difference of two squares); the units digit of D will be $5$ and therefore $5$ must be a factor of it whilst the units digit of E is $0$ and therefore it cannot be prime either since both $2$ and $5$ will be factors of it.
