## 题目
How many prime numbers are there less than 20?

A 6 $\qquad$ B 7 $\qquad$ C 8 $\qquad$ D 9 $\qquad$ E 10

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The prime numbers less than $20$ are $2$, $3$, $5$, $7$, $11$, $13$, $17$, and $19$.

**Note** Prime numbers are defined as those numbers which have exactly two factors and therefore $1$ is not prime since it has only one factor.
