## 题目
What is the 1999th term of the sequence $1, 2, 2, 3, 3, 3, 4, 4, 4, 4, 5, \dots$?

A 59 $\qquad$ B 60 $\qquad$ C 61 $\qquad$ D 62 $\qquad$ E 63

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
$1 + 2 + 3 + 4 + 5 + \ldots + n = \frac{1}{2}n(n + 1)$.

Therefore the terms in the sequence with positions $\frac{1}{2}(n - 1)n + 1$ to $\frac{1}{2}n(n + 1)$ inclusive will all be $n$. Now $\frac{1}{2} \times 62 \times 63 = 1953$ and $\frac{1}{2} \times 63 \times 64 = 2016$. Therefore the 1954th term to the 2016th term inclusive will all be $63$.
