## 题目
Which is the largest of the following?

A $\sqrt{1999}$ $\qquad$ B $1\sqrt{999}$ $\qquad$ C $19\sqrt{99}$ $\qquad$ D $199\sqrt{9}$ $\qquad$ E $1999\sqrt{0}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The values are, approximately, A $45$; B $1 \times 30 = 30$; C $20 \times 10 = 200$; D $200 \times 3 = 600$; E $2000 \times 0 = 0$.
