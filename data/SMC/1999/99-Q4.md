## 题目
I need to buy 12 films for my camera before my holiday. They normally cost £4.50 each, but a number of shops have "special offers". Which of these is the best deal?

A One fifth off all prices!  
B Two for the price of four!  
C Buy two – get one free!  
D 30% price cut!  
E Pay only three quarters of the normal price!

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The reductions would be A $20\%$; C $33\frac{1}{3}\%$; D $30\%$; E $25\%$. B might be a "special offer", but is not one which should be accepted!
