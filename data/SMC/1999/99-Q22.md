## 题目
The area of each large semicircle is 2. What is the difference between the black and grey shaded areas?

![[Image/99-Q22-fig1.png]]

A $0$ $\qquad$ B $1/2$ $\qquad$ C $1 + 2\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ D $5/9$ $\qquad$ E $23 - 16\sqrt{2}$

## 答案
E

## 解析
Let the radius of each large semicircle be $R$. Then $\pi R^2 = 4$. The circle in the left-hand diagram has radius $R/2$ and therefore its area is $\pi(R/2)^2 = (\pi R^2)/4 = 1$.

Let the radii of the circles in the right-hand diagram be $r$. Then $R = \sqrt{2}r + r$.

Therefore $r = \dfrac{R}{\sqrt{2} + 1} = \dfrac{R(\sqrt{2} - 1)}{(\sqrt{2} + 1)(\sqrt{2} - 1)} = R(\sqrt{2} - 1)$.

![[Image/99-Q22-sol1.png]]

The grey shaded area

$$= 2\pi r^2 = 2\pi R^2\left(\sqrt{2} - 1\right)^2 = 2 \times 4 \times \left(2 - 2\sqrt{2} + 1\right) = 24 - 16\sqrt{2}.$$

The difference in areas is therefore $23 - 16\sqrt{2}$.
