## 题目
The ratio of Jon's age to Jan's age is $3:1$. Three years ago the ratio was $4:1$. In how many years time will the ratio be $2:1$?

A 3 $\qquad$ B 6 $\qquad$ C 9 $\qquad$ D 12 $\qquad$ E 15

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Let Jon's and Jan's ages be $3x$ and $x$ respectively. Then $3x - 3 = 4(x - 3)$ which gives $x = 9$. Therefore Jon is $27$ and Jan is $9$. If the ratio will be $2:1$ in $y$ years time then $27 + y = 2(9 + y)$ which gives $y = 9$. In $9$ years time, Jon will be $36$ and Jan will be $18$.
