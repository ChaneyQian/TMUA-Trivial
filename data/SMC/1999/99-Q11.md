## 题目
In how many different ways can I circle letters in the grid shown so that there is exactly one circled letter in each row and exactly one circled letter in each column?

![[Image/99-Q11-fig1.png]]

A 15 $\qquad$ B 24 $\qquad$ C 60 $\qquad$ D 100 $\qquad$ E 120

## 答案
E

## 解析
In the first row, any one of $5$ letters could be circled. In the second row, any one of $4$ letters could be circled since one column has now been occupied. Similarly, in the third row, any one of three letters could be circled and so on. The number of different ways is therefore $5 \times 4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1 = 120$.

**Note** Notice that this is $5!$ as in 9.
