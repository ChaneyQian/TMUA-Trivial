## 题目
In 1998 a newspaper reported that "The world record for remembering the value of $\pi$ to the greatest number of decimal places is 40 000 places, which took the record holder 17 hours and 21 minutes to recite." What was the average number of decimal places recited per minute, approximately?

A 20 $\qquad$ B 40 $\qquad$ C 200 $\qquad$ D 400 $\qquad$ E 2000

## 中文题干
1998 年，一家报纸报道称：「背诵 $\pi$ 值最多小数位数的世界纪录是 40 000 位，纪录保持者用了 17 小时 21 分钟进行背诵。」平均每分钟背诵的小数位数大约是多少？

## 备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
$17$ hours and $21$ minutes $= 1041$ minutes and the average speed is therefore slightly less than $40$ decimal places per minute.
