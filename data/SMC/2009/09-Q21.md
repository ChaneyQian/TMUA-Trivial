## 题目
A frustum is the solid obtained by slicing a right-circular cone perpendicular to its axis and removing the small cone above the slice. This leaves a shape with two circular faces and a curved surface. The original cone has base radius $6\text{ cm}$ and height $8\text{ cm}$, and the curved surface area of the frustum is equal to the area of the two circles. What is the height of the frustum?

## 解析
Let $r$ be the radius of the small cone and $h$ the height.

Let $l_1$ and $l_2$ be the slant heights of the small and large cones respectively.

By Pythagoras' Theorem $l_2 = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = 10$.

Using similar triangles, $\dfrac{l_1}{r} = \dfrac{10}{6}$ so $l_1 = \dfrac{5}{3}r$ and $\dfrac{h}{8} = \dfrac{r}{6}$ giving $h = \dfrac{4}{3}r$.

Thus the area of the curved surface of the frustum is

$$\pi \times 6 \times 10 - \pi \times r \times \frac{5}{3} \times r = \pi\left(60 - \frac{5r^2}{3}\right).$$

The sum of the areas of the two circles is $\pi \times 6^2 + \pi \times r^2 = \pi(36 + r^2)$.

Hence $\pi\left(60 - \dfrac{5r^2}{3}\right) = \pi(36 + r^2)$ and so $24 = \dfrac{8r^2}{3}$ giving $r = 3$, so $h = \dfrac{4}{3} \times 3 = 4$.

Therefore, in cms, the height of the frustum is $8 - 4 = 4$.
