## 题目
The positive numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy the equations $x^4 - y^4 = 2009$ and $x^2 + y^2 = 49$. What is the value of $y$?

A 1 $\qquad$ B 2 $\qquad$ C 3 $\qquad$ D 4 $\qquad$ E more information is needed

## 答案
B

## 解析
Since $x^4 - y^4 = 2009$ it follows that $(x^2 + y^2)(x^2 - y^2) = 2009$.

But $x^2 + y^2 = 49$ hence $x^2 - y^2 = \dfrac{2009}{49} = 41$.

Subtracting gives $2y^2 = 8$ hence $y^2 = 4$. Since $y > 0$, $y = 2$.
