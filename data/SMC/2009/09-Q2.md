## 题目
Which of the following is not a multiple of 15?

A 135 $\qquad$ B 315 $\qquad$ C 555 $\qquad$ D 785 $\qquad$ E 915

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
$\dfrac{785}{15} = 52\frac{1}{3}$ hence $785$ is not a multiple of $15$. But $\dfrac{135}{15} = 9$, $\dfrac{315}{15} = 21$, $\dfrac{555}{15} = 37$, $\dfrac{915}{15} = 61$.
