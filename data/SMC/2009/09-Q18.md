## 题目
Which of the following could be part of the graph of the curve $y^2 = x(2 - x)$?

## 答案
A

## 解析
We have $y^2 = x(2 - x)$. Now $y^2 \ge 0$ for all real $y$ hence $x(2 - x) \ge 0$.

Hence $0 \le x \le 2$. In fact we can rewrite the equation as $(x - 1)^2 + y^2 = 1$; so this is a circle of radius $1$ with centre $(1, 0)$.
