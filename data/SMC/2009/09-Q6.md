## 题目
A bag contains hundreds of glass marbles, each one coloured either red, orange, green or blue. There are more than 2 marbles of each colour.

Marbles are drawn randomly from the bag, one at a time, and not replaced.

How many marbles must be drawn from the bag in order to ensure at least three marbles of the same colour are drawn?

A 4 $\qquad$ B 7 $\qquad$ C 9 $\qquad$ D 12 $\qquad$ E 13

## 中文题干
一个袋子里装有数百颗玻璃弹珠，每颗弹珠的颜色为红、橙、绿、蓝之一。每种颜色的弹珠都多于 2 颗。每次从袋中随机取出一颗弹珠且不放回。为了确保取出至少三颗相同颜色的弹珠，必须从袋中取出多少颗弹珠？

## 答案
C

## 解析
If at most two marbles of each colour are chosen, the maximum number we can choose is $8$, corresponding to $2$ of each. Therefore, if $9$ are chosen, we must have at least $3$ of one colour, but this statement is not true if $9$ is replaced by any number less than $9$.
