## 题目
Steve travelled 150 miles on a motorbike and used 10 litres of petrol. Given that 1 gallon $\approx 4.5$ litres, roughly how many miles per gallon did Steve achieve on his journey?

A 10 $\qquad$ B 20 $\qquad$ C 40 $\qquad$ D 50 $\qquad$ E 70

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Steve achieved $\dfrac{150}{10} \times 4.5$ miles per gallon which is $15 \times 4.5 = 67.5 \approx 70$.
