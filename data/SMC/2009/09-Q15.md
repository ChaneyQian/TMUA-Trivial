## 题目
For how many integers $n$ is $\dfrac{n}{100 - n}$ also an integer?

A 1 $\qquad$ B 6 $\qquad$ C 10 $\qquad$ D 18 $\qquad$ E 100

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Let $\dfrac{n}{100 - n} = x$ where $x$ is an integer. Hence $n = 100x - nx$.

Hence $n(1 + x) = 100x$ giving $n = \dfrac{100x}{1 + x}$.

Now $x$ and $1 + x$ can have no common factors. Therefore $1 + x$ must be a factor of $100$ and can be any of them.

Hence $1 + x \in \{\pm 1, \pm 2, \pm 4, \pm 5, \pm 10, \pm 20, \pm 25, \pm 50, \pm 100\}$ thus the number of possible integers $n$ is $18$.
