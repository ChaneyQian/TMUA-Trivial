## 题目
Which of the following has the greatest value?

A $\cos 50^\circ$ $\qquad$ B $\sin 50^\circ$ $\qquad$ C $\tan 50^\circ$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{1}{\sin 50^\circ}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{1}{\cos 50^\circ}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
$\cos 50^\circ < \sin 50^\circ < 1$. Hence $\dfrac{1}{\cos 50^\circ} > \dfrac{1}{\sin 50^\circ} > 1 > \sin 50^\circ > \cos 50^\circ$.

$\tan 50^\circ = \dfrac{\sin 50^\circ}{\cos 50^\circ} < \dfrac{1}{\cos 50^\circ}$ hence $\dfrac{1}{\cos 50^\circ}$ has the greatest value.
