## 题目
A square $PQRS$ has sides of length $x$. $T$ is the midpoint of $QR$ and $U$ is the foot of the perpendicular from $T$ to $QS$. What is the length of $TU$?

![[Image/09-Q9-fig1.png]]

A $\dfrac{x}{2}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{x}{3}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{x}{\sqrt{2}}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{x}{2\sqrt{2}}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{x}{4}$

## 答案
D

## 解析
As $T$ is the midpoint of $QR$ then $QT = \frac{1}{2}x$.

Since $\angle UQT = \angle SQR = 45^\circ$ and $\angle QUT = 90^\circ$, $\angle UTQ = 45^\circ$.

Thus triangle $QTU$ is isosceles with $UQ = UT$.

In triangle $QTU$, by Pythagoras' Theorem, $QT^2 = QU^2 + TU^2$.

Hence $\left(\frac{1}{2}x\right)^2 = 2TU^2$ so $TU^2 = \frac{1}{8}x^2$ giving $TU = \dfrac{x}{2\sqrt{2}}$.
