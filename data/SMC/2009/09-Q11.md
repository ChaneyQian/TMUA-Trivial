## 题目
For what value of $x$ is $\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{2} = 2^x$ true?

A $\frac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ B $1\frac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ C $2\frac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ D $3\frac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ E $4\frac{1}{2}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
$\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{2} + \sqrt{2} = 4\sqrt{2} = 2^2 \times 2^{1/2} = 2^{2\frac{1}{2}}$. Hence $x = 2\frac{1}{2}$.
