## 题目
A mini-sudoku is a 4 by 4 grid, where each row, column and 2 by 2 outlined block contains the digits 1, 2, 3 and 4 once and once only. How many different ways are there of completing the mini-sudoku shown?

![[Image/09-Q7-fig1.png]]

A 1 $\qquad$ B 2 $\qquad$ C 4 $\qquad$ D 8 $\qquad$ E 12

## 中文题干

## 答案
B

## 解析
The top left $2$ by $2$ outlined block must contain a $3$ and a $4$ and this can be done in two ways. For each choice there is only one way to complete the entire mini-sudoku.
