## 题目
Boris Biker entered the Tour de Transylvania with an unusual bicycle whose back wheel is larger than the front. The radius of the back wheel is $40\text{ cm}$, and the radius of the front wheel is $30\text{ cm}$. On the first stage of the race the smaller wheel made 120000 revolutions. How many revolutions did the larger wheel make?

![[Image/09-Q5-fig1.png]]

A $90000$ $\qquad$ B $90000\pi$ $\qquad$ C $160000$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{160000}{\pi}$ $\qquad$ E $120000$

## 答案
A

## 解析
As the ratio of the radii is $3 : 4$ then the number of revolutions made by the larger wheel is $120000 \times \frac{3}{4} = 90000$.
