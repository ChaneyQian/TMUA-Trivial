## 题目
The entries to the Senior Mathematical Challenge grew from 87400 in 2007 to 92690 in 2008. Approximately what percentage increase does this represent?

A 4% $\qquad$ B 5% $\qquad$ C 6% $\qquad$ D 7% $\qquad$ E 8%

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The increase in entries from 2007 to 2008 is $92\,690 - 87\,400 = 5290$.

Hence the percentage increase is $\dfrac{5290}{87400} \times 100\% = \dfrac{5290}{874}\% \approx \dfrac{5400}{900}\% = 6\%$.

(The exact value is $6\frac{1}{19}$.)
