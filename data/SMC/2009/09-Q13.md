## 题目
Suppose that $x - \dfrac{1}{x} = y - \dfrac{1}{y}$ and $x \neq y$. What is the value of $xy$?

A 4 $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C $-1$ $\qquad$ D $-4$ $\qquad$ E more information is needed

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
$x - \dfrac{1}{x} = y - \dfrac{1}{y}$ hence $x^2y - y = xy^2 - x$. Thus $xy(y - x) + y - x = 0$.

Therefore $(y - x)(xy + 1) = 0$. As $x \ne y$ then $y - x \ne 0$.

Hence $xy + 1 = 0$ giving $xy = -1$.
