## 题目
Little John claims he is 2m 8cm and 3mm tall. What is this height in metres?

A $2.83\text{m}$ $\qquad$ B $2.803\text{m}$ $\qquad$ C $2.083\text{m}$ $\qquad$ D $2.0803\text{m}$ $\qquad$ E $2.0083\text{m}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
One metre is $100$ centimetres. So $1\text{ cm} = 0.01\text{ m}$ and $8\text{ cm} = 0.08\text{ m}$. Similarly, one metre is $1000$ millimetres. So $1\text{ mm} = 0.001\text{ m}$ and $3\text{ mm} = 0.003\text{ m}$. Therefore Little John's height is $2\text{ m} + 0.08\text{ m} + 0.003\text{ m} = 2.083\text{ m}$.
