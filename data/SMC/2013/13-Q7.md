## 题目
In a ‘ninety nine’ shop, all items cost a number of pounds and 99 pence. Susanna spent £65.76. How many items did she buy?

A 23 $\qquad$ B 24 $\qquad$ C 65 $\qquad$ D 66 $\qquad$ E 76

## 答案
B

## 解析
Let $k$ be the number of items that Susanna bought. The cost of these is a whole number of pounds and $99k$ pence, that is, a whole number of pounds less $k$ pence. Susanna spent £65.76, that is, a whole number of pounds less $24$ pence. It follows that $k$ pence is a whole number of pounds plus $24$ pence. So $k$ is $24$ or $124$ or $224$ or …. . However, since each item costs at least $99$ pence and Susanna spent £65.76 pence, she bought at most $66$ items. So $k$ is $24$.
