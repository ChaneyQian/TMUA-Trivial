## 题目
The right-angled triangle shown has a base which is 4 times its height. Four such triangles are placed so that their hypotenuses form the boundary of a large square as shown.
What is the side-length of the shaded square in the diagram?

![[Image/13-Q8-fig1.png|190]]

A $2x$ $\qquad$ B $2\sqrt{2}x$ $\qquad$ C $3x$ $\qquad$ D $2\sqrt{3}x$ $\qquad$ E $\sqrt{15}x$

## 答案
B

## 解析
The side length of the large square is $4x$ and hence the area of this square is $16x^2$. Each triangle has base $4x$ and height $x$ and hence has area $\tfrac{1}{2}(4x \times x) = 2x^2$. So the total area of these four triangles is $8x^2$. Therefore the area of the shaded square is $16x^2 - 8x^2 = 8x^2$. Therefore the side length of the shaded square is $\sqrt{8x^2} = \sqrt{8}x = 2\sqrt{2}x$.
