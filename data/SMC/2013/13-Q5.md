## 题目
The numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy the equations $x(y + 2) = 100$ and $y(x + 2) = 60$. What is the value of $x - y$?

A 60 $\qquad$ B 50 $\qquad$ C 40 $\qquad$ D 30 $\qquad$ E 20

## 答案
E

## 解析
The two equations expand to give $xy + 2x = 100$ and $xy + 2y = 60$. It follows that $(xy + 2x) - (xy + 2y) = 100 - 60$. That is, $2x - 2y = 40$. Hence $2(x - y) = 40$ and so $x - y = 20$.
