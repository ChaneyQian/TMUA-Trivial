## 题目
According to a headline, ‘Glaciers in the French Alps have lost a quarter of their area in the past 40 years’. What is the approximate percentage reduction in the length of the side of a square when it loses one quarter of its area, thereby becoming a smaller square?

A 13% $\qquad$ B 25% $\qquad$ C 38% $\qquad$ D 50% $\qquad$ E 65%

## 答案
A

## 解析
Suppose that a square of side length $1$, and hence area $1$, has side length $x$ when it loses one quarter of its area. Then $x^2 = \dfrac{3}{4}$ and so $x = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. Now $1.7^2 = 2.89$ and so $1.7 < \sqrt{3}$. Hence $0.85 < \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$. So the length of the side of the smaller square is at least $85\%$ of its original value. Therefore the reduction in its length is less than $15\%$. So, of the given options, it must be that $13\%$ is correct.
