## 题目
The diagram shows two straight lines $PR$ and $QS$ crossing at $O$.
What is the value of $x$?

## 答案
E

## 解析
Let $\angle SOR = \theta$. Applying the Cosine Rule to the triangle $ROS$, we obtain

$$8^2 = 4^2 + 5^2 - 2 \times 4 \times 5 \times \cos\theta,$$

from which it follows that

$$\cos\theta = \frac{4^2 + 5^2 - 8^2}{2 \times 4 \times 5} = -\frac{23}{40}.$$

We also have that $\angle QOP = \angle SOR = \theta$, since they are vertically opposite angles. Hence, applying the Cosine Rule to triangle $POQ$, we get

$$\begin{aligned}
x^2 &= 4^2 + 10^2 - 2 \times 4 \times 10 \times \cos\theta \\
&= 16 + 100 - 80\left(-\frac{23}{40}\right) \\
&= 116 + 46 = 162.
\end{aligned}$$

Therefore $x = \sqrt{162} = \sqrt{81 \times 2} = 9\sqrt{2}$.
