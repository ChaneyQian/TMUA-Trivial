## 题目
Rebecca went swimming yesterday. After a while she had covered one fifth of her intended distance. After swimming six more lengths of the pool, she had covered one quarter of her intended distance. How many lengths of the pool did she intend to complete?

A 40 $\qquad$ B 72 $\qquad$ C 80 $\qquad$ D 100 $\qquad$ E 120

## 中文题干
Rebecca 昨天去游泳。过了一会儿，她游完了计划距离的五分之一。在又游了 6 个泳池长度后，她游完了计划距离的四分之一。她原计划完成多少个泳池长度？

## 答案
E

## 解析
We have $\dfrac{1}{4} - \dfrac{1}{5} = \dfrac{1}{20}$. So the six additional lengths make up $\dfrac{1}{20}$ of Rebecca's intended distance. So the number of lengths she intended to complete was $20 \times 6 = 120$.
