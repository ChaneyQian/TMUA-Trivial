## 题目
The shaded design shown in the diagram is made by drawing eight circular arcs, all with the same radius. The centres of four arcs are the vertices of the square; the centres of the four touching arcs are the midpoints of the sides of the square. The diagonals of the square have length 1.

![[Image/13-Q21-fig1.png|260]]

What is the total length of the border of the shaded design?

A $2\pi$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{5\pi}{2}$ $\qquad$ C $3\pi$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{7\pi}{2}$ $\qquad$ E $4\pi$

## 答案
B

## 解析
The arcs whose centres are the vertices of the square are semi-circular arcs, and the arcs whose centres are the midpoints of the sides of the square form three-quarter circles. Let all these arcs have radius $r$. Then the length of the border is

$$4 \times \left(\tfrac{1}{2} \times 2\pi r\right) + 4 \times \left(\tfrac{3}{4} \times 2\pi r\right) = 10\pi r.$$

Consider the line $PQ$ joining the midpoints of two adjacent sides of the square as shown. Clearly the length of $PQ$ is half of the length of the diagonal of the square. So $PQ$ has length $\dfrac{1}{2}$. $PQ$ joins the midpoints of two touching arcs and so its length is twice the radius of these arcs. Hence $r = \dfrac{1}{4}$. Therefore the length of the border of the shaded design is $10\pi \times \dfrac{1}{4} = \dfrac{5}{2}\pi$.
