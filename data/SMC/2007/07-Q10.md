## 题目
In 1954, a total of $6\,527\text{ mm}$ of rain fell at Sprinkling Tarn and this set a UK record for annual rainfall.  The tarn has a surface area of $23\,450\text{ m}^2$.  Roughly how many million litres of water fell on Sprinkling Tarn in 1954?

A 15 $\qquad$ B 150 $\qquad$ C 1 500 $\qquad$ D 15 000 $\qquad$ E 150 000

## 备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
The volume of water which fell at Sprinkling Tarn in 1954 is approximately equal to $(25\,000 \times 6)\text{ m}^3$, that is $150\,000\text{ m}^3$. Now $1\text{ m}^3 = 10^6\text{ cm}^3 = 10^6\text{ ml} = 1000$ litres. So approximately 150 million litres of water fell on Sprinkling Tarn in 1954.
