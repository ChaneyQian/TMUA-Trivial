## 题目
The largest circle which it is possible to draw inside triangle $PQR$ touches the triangle at $S$, $T$ and $U$, as shown in the diagram.

The size of $\angle STU = 55^\circ$. What is the size of $\angle PQR$?

## 解析
By the Alternate Segment Theorem $\angle QUS = 55^\circ$. Tangents to a circle from an exterior point are equal, so $QU = QS$ and hence $\angle QSU = \angle QUS = 55^\circ$. So $\angle PQR = 180^\circ - 2 \times 55^\circ = 70^\circ$.
