## 题目
The sum of the lengths of the 12 edges of a cuboid is $x$ cm. The distance from one corner of the cuboid to the furthest corner is $y$ cm. What, in $\text{cm}^2$, is the total surface area of the cuboid?

A $\dfrac{x^2 - 2y^2}{2}$  
B $x^2 + y^2$  
C $\dfrac{x^2 - 4y^2}{4}$  
D $\dfrac{xy}{6}$  
E $\dfrac{x^2 - 16y^2}{16}$

## 答案
E

## 解析
Let the lengths of the sides of the cuboid, in cm, be $a$, $b$ and $c$. So $4(a + b + c) = x$. Also, by Pythagoras' Theorem $a^2 + b^2 + c^2 = y^2$. Now the total surface area of the cuboid is
$$2ab + 2bc + 2ca = (a + b + c)^2 - \left(a^2 + b^2 + c^2\right) = \left(\dfrac{x}{4}\right)^2 - y^2 = \dfrac{x^2 - 16y^2}{16}.$$
