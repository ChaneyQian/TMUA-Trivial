## 题目
The point $O$ is the centre of both circles and the shaded area is one-sixth of the area of the outer circle.

What is the value of $x$?

![[Image/07-Q14-fig1.png]]

A 60 $\qquad$ B 64 $\qquad$ C 72 $\qquad$ D 80 $\qquad$ E 84

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
The shaded area is $\dfrac{x}{360}\left(\pi \times 4^2 - \pi \times 1^2\right) = \dfrac{15\pi x}{360} = \dfrac{\pi x}{24}$. So $\dfrac{\pi x}{24} = \dfrac{\pi \times 4^2}{6}$; thus $x = 64$.
