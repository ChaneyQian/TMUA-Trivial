## 题目
How many whole numbers between 1 and 2007 are divisible by 2 but not by 7?

A 857 $\qquad$ B 858 $\qquad$ C 859 $\qquad$ D 860 $\qquad$ E 861

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
There are 1003 whole numbers between 1 and 2007 which are divisible by 2. Those which are also divisible by 7 are the multiples of 14, namely 14, 28, 42, …, 2002. There are 143 of these, so the required number is $1003 - 143 = 860$.
