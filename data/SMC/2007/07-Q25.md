## 题目
The line with equation $y = x$ is an axis of symmetry of the curve with equation $y = \dfrac{px + q}{rx + s}$, where $p$, $q$, $r$, $s$ are all non-zero. Which of the following is necessarily true?

A $p + q = 0$ $\qquad$ B $r + s = 0$ $\qquad$ C $p + r = 0$ $\qquad$ D $p + s = 0$ $\qquad$ E $q + r = 0$

## 答案
D

## 解析
As the line $y = x$ is an axis of symmetry of the curve, if the point $(a, b)$ lies on the curve, so too does the point $(b, a)$. Hence the equation of the curve may also be written as $x = \dfrac{py + q}{ry + s}$.

Therefore, substituting for $x$ in the original equation:
$$y = \dfrac{p\left(\dfrac{py + q}{ry + s}\right) + q}{r\left(\dfrac{py + q}{ry + s}\right) + s} = \dfrac{p(py + q) + q(ry + s)}{r(py + q) + s(ry + s)}.$$

Therefore
$$y\big(r(py + q) + s(ry + s)\big) = p(py + q) + q(ry + s),$$

that is
$$y^2 r(p + s) + y\left(qr + s^2 - p^2 - qr\right) - pq - qs = 0,$$

that is
$$(p + s)\left(y^2 r + y(s - p) - q\right) = 0.$$

Since $r$ is non-zero, the expression in the second bracket is non-zero for all but at most two values of $y$. Hence $p + s = 0$.
