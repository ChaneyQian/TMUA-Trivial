## 题目
The year 1789 (when the French Revolution started) has three and no more than three adjacent digits (7, 8 and 9) which are consecutive integers in increasing order. How many years between 1000 and 9999 have this property?

A 130 $\qquad$ B 142 $\qquad$ C 151 $\qquad$ D 169 $\qquad$ E 180

## 中文题干
1789 年（法国大革命开始的年份）有三个且仅有三个相邻数字（7、8 和 9）是按升序排列的连续整数。在 1000 到 9999 之间，具有这一性质的年份有多少个？

## 答案
A

## 解析
There are 9 years of the form $123n$ as $n$ may be any digit other than 4. Similarly, there are 9 years each of the forms $234n$, $345n$, $456n$, $567n$ and $678n$, but 10 years of the form $789n$ as, in this case, $n$ may be *any* digit. There are also 9 years of the form $n012$ and 9 of the form $n123$, as in both cases $n$ may be any digit other than 0. However, there are 8 years of the form $n234$ as in this case $n$ cannot be 0 or 1. Similarly, there are 8 years each of the forms $n345$, $n456$, $n567$, $n678$ and $n789$.

So the total numbers of years is $1 \times 10 + 8 \times 9 + 6 \times 8 = 130$.
