## 题目
In triangle $PQR$, $S$ and $T$ are the midpoints of $PR$ and $PQ$ respectively; $QS$ is perpendicular to $RT$; $QS = 8$; $RT = 12$.

What is the area of triangle $PQR$?

## 答案
D

## 解析
Let $U$ be the point of intersection of $QS$ and $RT$. As $QS$ and $RT$ are medians of the triangle, they intersect at a point which divides each in the ratio 2:1, so $QU = \tfrac{2}{3} \times 8 = \dfrac{16}{3}$. Therefore the area of triangle $QTR = \tfrac{1}{2} \times RT \times QU = \tfrac{1}{2} \times 12 \times \dfrac{16}{3} = 32$.

![[Image/07-Q22-sol1.png]]

As a median of a triangle divides it into two triangles of equal area, the area of triangle $PTR$ is equal to the area of triangle $QTR$, so the area of triangle $PQR$ is 64.
