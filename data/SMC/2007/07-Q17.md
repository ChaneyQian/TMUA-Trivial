## 题目
The two triangles have equal areas and the four marked lengths are equal.

What is the value of $x$?

![[Image/07-Q17-fig1.png]]

A 30 $\qquad$ B 45 $\qquad$ C 60 $\qquad$ D 75 $\qquad$ E more information needed

## 答案
C

## 解析
Let the equal sides have length $k$. The height of the triangle on the left is $k\cos x^\circ$ and its base is $2k\sin x^\circ$, so its area is $k^2\sin x^\circ\cos x^\circ$. The height of the triangle on the right is $k\sin x^\circ$ and its base is $k$, so its area is $\tfrac{1}{2}k^2\sin x^\circ$. Hence $\cos x^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}$ and so $x = 60$.

![[Image/07-Q17-sol1.png]]

(Alternatively, the formula $\triangle = \tfrac{1}{2}ab\sin C$ can be used to show that $\sin x^\circ = \sin 2x^\circ$; hence $x + 2x = 180$.)
