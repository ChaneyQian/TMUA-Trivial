## 题目
A $4 \times 4 \times 4$ cube has three $2 \times 2 \times 4$ holes drilled symmetrically all the way through, as shown.

What is the surface area of the resulting solid?

![[Image/07-Q11-fig1.png]]

A 192 $\qquad$ B 144 $\qquad$ C 136 $\qquad$ D 120 $\qquad$ E 96

## 中文题干
如图，一个 $4 \times 4 \times 4$ 的正方体被对称地完全打穿了三个 $2 \times 2 \times 4$ 的孔。所得几何体的表面积是多少？

## 答案
D

## 解析
Each of the original faces of the cube now has area $4 \times 4 - 2 \times 2$, that is 12. In addition, the drilling of the holes has created 24 rectangles, each measuring $2 \times 1$. So the required area is $6 \times 12 + 24 \times 2 = 120$.
