## 题目
How many two-digit numbers $N$ have the property that the sum of $N$ and the number formed by reversing the digits of $N$ is a square?

A 2 $\qquad$ B 5 $\qquad$ C 6 $\qquad$ D 7 $\qquad$ E 8

## 答案
E

## 解析
Let $N$ be the two-digit number '$ab$', that is $N = 10a + b$. So the sum of $N$ and its 'reverse' is $10a + b + 10b + a = 11a + 11b = 11(a + b)$. As 11 is prime and $a$ and $b$ are both single digits, $11(a + b)$ is a square if, and only if, $a + b = 11$. So the possible values of $N$ are 29, 38, 47, 56, 65, 74, 83, 92.
