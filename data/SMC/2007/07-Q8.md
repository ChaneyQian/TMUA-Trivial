## 题目
Travelling at an average speed of $100\text{ km/hr}$, a train took 3 hours to travel to Birmingham. Unfortunately the train then waited just outside the station, which reduced the average speed for the whole journey to $90\text{ km/hr}$. For how many minutes was the train waiting?

A 1 $\qquad$ B 5 $\qquad$ C 10 $\qquad$ D 15 $\qquad$ E 20

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
The distance travelled to Birmingham by the train was 300 km. The time taken to travel this distance at an average speed of 90 km/hr is $\dfrac{300}{90}\text{ hr} = 3\tfrac{1}{3}\text{ hr} = 3\text{ hr }20\text{ min}$. So the train was waiting for 20 minutes.
