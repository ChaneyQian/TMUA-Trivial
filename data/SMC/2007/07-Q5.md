## 题目
Which of the five expressions shown has a different value from the other four?

A $2^8$ $\qquad$ B $4^4$ $\qquad$ C $8^{8/3}$ $\qquad$ D $16^2$ $\qquad$ E $32^{6/5}$

## 中文题干
所给的五个表达式中，哪一个的值与其余四个不同？

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
$4^4 = \left(2^2\right)^4 = 2^8$; $8^{8/3} = \left(2^3\right)^{8/3} = 2^8$; $16^2 = \left(2^4\right)^2 = 2^8$. However, $32^{6/5} = \left(2^5\right)^{6/5} = 2^6$.
