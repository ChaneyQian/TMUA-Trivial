## 题目
In a sale, a shopkeeper reduced the advertised selling price of a dress by 20%. This resulted in a profit of 4% over the cost price of the dress. What percentage profit would the shopkeeper have made if the dress had been sold at the original selling price?

A 16% $\qquad$ B 20% $\qquad$ C 24% $\qquad$ D 25% $\qquad$ E 30%

## 答案
E

## 解析
Let the original cost price and original selling price of the dress be $C$ and $S$ respectively. Then $0.8 \times S = 1.04 \times C$. So $S = \dfrac{1.04}{0.8} \times C = 1.3 \times C$.

Therefore the shopkeeper would have made a profit of 30% by selling the dress at its original price.
