## 题目
The diagram shows square $PQRS$ and regular hexagon $PQTUVW$.

What is the size of $\angle PSW$?

![[Image/SMC-07-Q4-fig1.png]]

A $10^\circ$ $\qquad$ B $12^\circ$ $\qquad$ C $15^\circ$ $\qquad$ D $24^\circ$ $\qquad$ E $30^\circ$

## 答案
C

## 解析
$\angle WPQ = 120^\circ$ (interior angle of a regular hexagon), so $\angle WPS = (360 - 120 - 90)^\circ = 150^\circ$. Now $PW = PQ$ (sides of a regular hexagon) and $PS = PQ$ (sides of a square) so $PW = PS$. Therefore triangle $PSW$ is isosceles and $\angle PSW = (180 - 150)^\circ \div 2 = 15^\circ$.
