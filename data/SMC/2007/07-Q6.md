## 题目
Cheryl finds a bag of coins.  There are 50 coins inside and the value of the contents is £1.81. Given that it contains only two-pence and five-pence coins, how many more five-pence coins are there inside the bag than two-pence coins?

A 4 $\qquad$ B 6 $\qquad$ C 8 $\qquad$ D 10 $\qquad$ E 12

## 中文题干
Cheryl 发现了一袋硬币。里面有 50 枚硬币，总面值为 £1.81。已知袋中只有 2 便士和 5 便士硬币，袋中 5 便士硬币比 2 便士硬币多多少枚？

## 答案
A

## 解析
Let the number of five-pence coins be $x$. Then $5x + 2(50 - x) = 181$, that is $3x = 81$, that is $x = 27$. So there are 27 five-pence coins and 23 two-pence coins.
