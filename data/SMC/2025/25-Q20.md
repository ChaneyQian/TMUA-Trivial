## 题目
When $\dfrac{1}{x} - \dfrac{1}{y} = 2025$, what is the value of $\dfrac{x + 2026xy - y}{2y - 2025xy - 2x}$?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ D 2026 $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{1}{2025}$

## 中文题干
当 $\dfrac{1}{x} - \dfrac{1}{y} = 2025$ 时，$\dfrac{x + 2026xy - y}{2y - 2025xy - 2x}$ 的值是多少？

## 答案
E

## 解析
Since $\dfrac{1}{x} - \dfrac{1}{y} = 2025$, it follows that $\dfrac{y - x}{xy} = 2025$, and hence $y - x = 2025xy$.

Therefore
$$
\begin{aligned}
\frac{x + 2026xy - y}{2y - 2025xy - 2x} &= \frac{2026xy - (y - x)}{2(y - x) - 2025xy} \\
&= \frac{2026xy - 2025xy}{2(2025xy) - 2025xy} \\
&= \frac{xy}{2025xy} \\
&= \frac{1}{2025}.
\end{aligned}
$$
