## 题目
Ayain writes down all the 3-digit numbers consisting of three different odd digits.

How many of Ayain's numbers are divisible by 3?

A 42 $\qquad$ B 36 $\qquad$ C 30 $\qquad$ D 24 $\qquad$ E 18

## 中文题干
Ayain 写下所有由三个不同的奇数数字组成的三位数。Ayain 写下的这些数中有多少个能被 3 整除？

## 答案
D

## 解析
The test for whether an integer is divisible by $3$ is that the sum of its digits is divisible by $3$. [You are asked to prove this in Problem 12.4.]

There are four sets made up of three of the odd digits $1, 3, 5, 7, 9$, whose sum is a multiple of $3$. These are $\{1, 3, 5\}$, $\{1, 5, 9\}$, $\{3, 5, 7\}$ and $\{5, 7, 9\}$.

The three digits of each set can be arranged in six ways to form six three digits numbers. Therefore $4 \times 6 = 24$ of Ayain's numbers are divisible by $3$.
