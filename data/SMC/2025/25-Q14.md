## 题目
The simultaneous equations $x + \dfrac{1}{y} = 2$ and $y + \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{9}{4}$ have two pairs of real solutions.

What is the difference between the possible values of $x$?

A $\dfrac{1}{3}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{2}{3}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{3}{4}$ $\qquad$ D 1 $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{3}{2}$

## 中文题干
方程组 $x + \dfrac{1}{y} = 2$ 和 $y + \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{9}{4}$ 有两组实数解。$x$ 的两个可能值之差是多少？

## 答案
B

## 解析
From the equation $x + \dfrac{1}{y} = 2$, we have $\dfrac{1}{y} = 2 - x$ and therefore $y = \dfrac{1}{2 - x}$.

By substituting $\dfrac{1}{2 - x}$ for $y$ in the equation $y + \dfrac{1}{x} = \dfrac{9}{4}$, we deduce that
$$\frac{1}{2 - x} + \frac{1}{x} = \frac{9}{4}.$$
This last equation may be rearranged to give
$$9x^2 - 18x + 8 = 0. \tag{1}$$
[You are asked to check this in Problem 14.1.]

The left hand side of this last equation factorizes to give
$$(3x - 4)(3x - 2) = 0.$$
Therefore either $3x - 4 = 0$ or $3x - 2 = 0$. Hence the solutions of (1) are $x = \dfrac{4}{3}$ and $x = \dfrac{2}{3}$.

Therefore the difference between the two possible values of $x$ is $\dfrac{4}{3} - \dfrac{2}{3} = \dfrac{2}{3}$.
