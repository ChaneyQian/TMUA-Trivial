## 题目
The diagram shows three circles with radii 1, 2 and 3.

![[Image/SMC-25-Q5-fig1.png]]

What is the ratio of the shaded area to the area of the largest circle?

A $1 : 3$ $\qquad$ B $1 : 2$ $\qquad$ C $\sqrt{2} : \sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ D $2 : 3$ $\qquad$ E $4 : 9$

## 中文题干
如图是三个半径分别为 1、2、3 的圆。阴影部分的面积与最大的圆的面积之比是多少？

## 答案
A

## 解析
The circles have areas $\pi(1^2)$, $\pi(2^2)$ and $\pi(3^2)$, that is, $\pi$, $4\pi$ and $9\pi$, respectively.

It follows that the shaded area is $4\pi - \pi = 3\pi$.

Therefore the ratio of the shaded area to the area of the largest circle is $3\pi : 9\pi = 1 : 3$.
