## 题目
A 'complete' football kit consists of a shirt, a pair of shorts and a pair of socks. Three pairs of shorts and one pair of socks together cost the same as two shirts. Seven pairs of shorts and four pairs of socks together cost the same as five shirts. Eden has exactly the right amount of money to buy nine shirts. How many 'complete' football kits could be bought for the same amount of money?

A 3 $\qquad$ B 4 $\qquad$ C 5 $\qquad$ D 6 $\qquad$ E 7

## 中文题干
一套「完整」的足球装备由一件球衣、一条短裤和一双球袜组成。三条短裤和一双球袜的总价与两件球衣的总价相同。七条短裤和四双球袜的总价与五件球衣的总价相同。Eden 的钱恰好够买九件球衣。同样多的钱可以买多少套「完整」的足球装备？

## 答案
C

## 解析
Let the cost of one shirt, one pair of shorts and one pair of socks be $x$, $y$ and $z$ pounds, respectively. Thus the cost of a full football kit is $x + y + z$ pounds.

From the information given in the question, we have
$$
\begin{aligned}
3y + z &= 2x \qquad (1) \\
7y + 4z &= 5x. \qquad (2)
\end{aligned}
$$
Since $5 \times 2x = 2 \times 5x$, it follows from (1) and (2) that
$$5(3y + z) = 2(7y + 4z).$$
That is,
$$15y + 5z = 14y + 8z,$$
and hence
$$y = 3z. \qquad (3)$$
It follows from (1) and (3) that $9z + z = 2x$ and hence that
$$z = \tfrac{1}{5}x.$$
Hence, by (3),
$$y = \tfrac{3}{5}x.$$
It follows that
$$
\begin{aligned}
x + y + z &= x + \tfrac{3}{5}x + \tfrac{1}{5}x \\
&= \tfrac{9}{5}x.
\end{aligned}
$$
Therefore the cost of a complete football kit is $\tfrac{9}{5}x$ pounds.

Eden has exactly enough money to buy $9$ shirts, and so has $9x$ pounds.

Therefore Eden has enough money to buy $9x \div \tfrac{9}{5}x = 5$ complete football kits.
