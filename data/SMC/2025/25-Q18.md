## 题目
The positive integer $N$ has 2025 digits. The first digit is a 3. Every two consecutive digits of $N$ form a number that is divisible by either 17 or 23. The units digit of $N$ could either be $p$ or $q$.

What is the value of $p + q$?

A 3 $\qquad$ B 6 $\qquad$ C 7 $\qquad$ D 9 $\qquad$ E 10

## 中文题干

## 答案
C

## 解析
The two-digit multiples of $17$ are $17$, $34$, $51$, $68$ and $85$.

The two-digit multiples of $23$ are $23$, $46$, $69$ and $92$.

In numerical order, these multiples are $17, 23, 34, 46, 51, 68, 69, 85, 92$.

From this we see that in the number $N$ the digit $3$ must be followed by $4$, and $4$ by $6$. The digit $6$ could be followed by either $8$ or $9$.

The digit $8$ must be followed by $5$, $5$ by $1$ and $1$ by $7$. The sequence of digits would then end as there is no two-digit multiple of either $17$ or $23$ with tens digit $7$.

The digit $9$ must be followed by $2$ and $2$ by $3$, creating a loop.

This is illustrated by the following diagram:

![[Image/SMC-25-Q18-sol1.png]]

Since $2025 \div 5 = 405$, we see that the $2025$ digits of $N$ result either from going $405$ times round the loop $3 \to 4 \to 6 \to 9 \to 2$, or from going round this loop $404$ times followed by the sequence $3 \to 4 \to 6 \to 8 \to 5$.

Therefore the final digit of $N$ is either $2$ or $5$.

Hence $p + q = 2 + 5 = 7$.
