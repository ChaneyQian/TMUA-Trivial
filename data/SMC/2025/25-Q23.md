## 题目
Jemima took a series of $n$ tests each with the same maximum mark. After $(n - 2)$ tests her average score was $m$. She scored full marks in the $(n - 1)$th test, and so raised her average score by 4 marks. In the $n$th test when she again scored full marks, she increased her average score by 3 marks.

How many tests did Jemima take?

A 6 $\qquad$ B 7 $\qquad$ C 8 $\qquad$ D 9 $\qquad$ E 10

## 中文题干
Jemima 参加了一系列共 $n$ 次测验，每次测验的满分都相同。前 $(n - 2)$ 次测验之后，她的平均分是 $m$。她在第 $(n - 1)$ 次测验中得了满分，于是平均分提高了 4 分。在第 $n$ 次测验中她又得了满分，平均分又提高了 3 分。Jemima 一共参加了多少次测验？

## 答案
C

## 解析
We let $M$ be the maximum mark for each test.

After $(n - 2)$ tests Jemima's average mark was $m$. Therefore she scored a total of $m(n - 2)$ marks in these tests.

After getting a maximum mark in the next test, Jemima has a total of $m(n - 2) + M$ marks, with an average of $(m + 4)$ for the $(n - 1)$ tests. Therefore,
$$\frac{m(n - 2) + M}{n - 1} = m + 4,$$
and hence
$$m(n - 2) + M = (m + 4)(n - 1). \qquad (1)$$
After gaining another maximum mark, Jemima has a total of $(m + 4)(n - 1) + M$ marks with an average of $(m + 4) + 3 = (m + 7)$ for the $n$ tests. Therefore,
$$\frac{(m + 4)(n - 1) + M}{n} = m + 7,$$
and hence
$$(m + 4)(n - 1) + M = (m + 7)n. \qquad (2)$$
Subtracting equation (1) from equation (2) gives
$$(m + 4)(n - 1) - m(n - 2) = (m + 7)n - (m + 4)(n - 1). \qquad (3)$$
Expanding both sides of equation (3) gives
$$mn - m + 4n - 4 - mn + 2m = mn + 7n - mn + m - 4n + 4. \qquad (4)$$
Most of the terms in equation cancel (4), and so the equation simplifies to give $n = 8$.

Therefore Jemima took $8$ tests.
