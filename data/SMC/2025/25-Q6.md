## 题目
For what value of $x$ is $\sqrt{\left(\sqrt{(\sqrt{x} + 1)} + 1\right)} + 1 = 3$?

A 4096 $\qquad$ B 64 $\qquad$ C 8 $\qquad$ D 3 $\qquad$ E 0

## 中文题干
当 $x$ 取何值时，$\sqrt{\left(\sqrt{(\sqrt{x} + 1)} + 1\right)} + 1 = 3$？

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注
- 嵌套根式的覆盖范围按原卷根号横线的横向坐标逐根核定：最外层根号只盖到 $(\sqrt{(\sqrt{x}+1)}+1)$，末尾的 $+1$ 在根号外（否则 $x=2304$、不在选项里）。

## 答案
B

## 解析
From the given equation, we have
$$\sqrt{\left(\sqrt{(\sqrt{x} + 1)} + 1\right)} = 2.$$
Therefore, squaring both sides,
$$\sqrt{(\sqrt{x} + 1)} + 1 = 4.$$
Hence
$$\sqrt{(\sqrt{x} + 1)} = 3.$$
Therefore, squaring again,
$$\sqrt{x} + 1 = 9.$$
Hence
$$\sqrt{x} = 8.$$
Finally, by squaring again, we deduce that $x = 64$. [In Problem 6.2 you are asked to check that squaring three times has not introduced a spurious solution.]
