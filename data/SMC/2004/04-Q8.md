## 题目
The diagram shows three right-angled triangles. What is the value of $x$?

![[Image/04-Q8-fig1.png]]

A 14 $\qquad$ B $10\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ C 15 $\qquad$ D $12\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ E $10\sqrt{3}$

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
$\angle ACB + \angle ACE + \angle DCE = 180^\circ$ (angles on a straight line). So $\angle ACB = 90^\circ - \angle DCE$.

Also, $\angle CDE + \angle CED + \angle DCE = 180^\circ$ (angle sum of a triangle). So $\angle CED = 90^\circ - \angle DCE$.

Therefore $\angle ACB = \angle CED$ and we can deduce that triangles $ABC$ and $CDE$ are congruent since they have two pairs of equal angles and one pair of equal sides ($AC = CE$). So $BC$ has length $9\text{ cm}$ and we use Pythagoras' Theorem to see that $x^2 = 12^2 + 9^2 = 225$. Hence $x = 15$.
