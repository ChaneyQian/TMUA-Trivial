## 题目
Sam can mow a lawn in 3 hours. Mel takes 4 hours to mow the same lawn, and Chris takes 6 hours to do the same. If they work with a lawn mower each, and do not get in the way of each other, how long would they take to mow the lawn together?

A 1 hour 20 minutes $\qquad$ B 1 hour 30 minutes $\qquad$ C 3 hours $\qquad$ D 4 hours 20 minutes $\qquad$ E 13 hours

## 中文题干
Sam 修剪一片草坪需要 3 小时。Mel 修剪同一片草坪需要 4 小时，Chris 做同样的工作需要 6 小时。如果他们每人使用一台割草机且互不干扰，他们一起修剪这片草坪需要多长时间？

## 答案
A

## 解析
In one hour the fraction of lawn which has been mowed is $\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{4} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{3}{4}$. So the time taken to mow the lawn is $\frac{4}{3}$ hours, that is 1 hour 20 minutes.
