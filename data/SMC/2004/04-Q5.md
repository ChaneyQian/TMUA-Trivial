## 题目
The first two scales shown are perfectly balanced. How many squares will be needed on the right of the third scales so that the scales balance?

![[Image/04-Q5-fig1.png]]

A 12 $\qquad$ B 10 $\qquad$ C 9 $\qquad$ D 8 $\qquad$ E 7

## 答案
B

## 解析
Let the weights of squares, triangles and circles be $s$, $t$, $c$ respectively. Then $c + 3t = 6s$; and $4c + 2t = 8s$, so $2c + t = 4s$. Hence $(c + 3t) + (2c + t) = 10s$.
