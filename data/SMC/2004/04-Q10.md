## 题目
The diagram shows seven circles of equal radius which fit snugly in the larger circle. What is the ratio of the unshaded area to the shaded area?

![[Image/04-Q10-fig1.png]]

A 7 : 1 $\qquad$ B 7 : 2 $\qquad$ C $2\sqrt{3} : 1$ $\qquad$ D 9 : 2 $\qquad$ E 1 : 1

## 答案
B

## 解析
Let the small circles have radius $r$. Then the large circle has radius $3r$. The unshaded area is $7\pi r^2$, while the shaded area is $\pi(3r)^2 - 7\pi r^2 = 2\pi r^2$. So the required ratio is 7:2.
