## 题目
Three different positive integers add up to 12. How large could the largest of the three numbers be?

A 6 $\qquad$ B 7 $\qquad$ C 8 $\qquad$ D 9 $\qquad$ E 10

## 中文题干
三个不同的正整数之和为 12。这三个数中最大的数最大可以是多少？

## 答案
D

## 解析
For the largest of the numbers to be as large as possible, the other two numbers must be as small as possible, that is 1 and 2.
