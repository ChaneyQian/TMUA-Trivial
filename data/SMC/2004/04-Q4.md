## 题目
A pieman sells pies at 4p for 5, or 1p each. If Simon simply buys 2004 pies, what is the least amount he could pay?

A £4.04 $\qquad$ B £8.04 $\qquad$ C £12.04 $\qquad$ D £16.04 $\qquad$ E £20.04

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Simon needs to buy as many batches of 5 pies as possible. He buys 400 batches of 5, giving him 2000 pies at a cost of £16. The other 4 pies cost 1p each, making a total of £16.04.
