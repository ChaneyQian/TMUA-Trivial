## 题目
In the trapezium shown (not to scale), $XY$ is parallel to two sides and passes through the point of intersection of the diagonals. What is the length $XY$?

![[Image/04-Q22-fig1.png]]

A 8 cm $\qquad$ B 9 cm $\qquad$ C 10 cm $\qquad$ D 11 cm $\qquad$ E 12 cm

## 答案
B

## 解析
Let the vertices of the trapezium be $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ and let $AC$ meet $BD$ at $E$, as shown. Triangles $ABE$ and $CDE$ are similar since $\angle ABE = \angle CDE$ (alternate angles) and $\angle AEB = \angle CED$ (vertically opposite angles). So $\dfrac{CE}{AE} = \dfrac{CD}{AB} = \dfrac{18}{6} = 3$.

Hence $AC = 4AE$ and, by a similar argument, $BD = 4BE$. Triangles $AXE$ and $ADC$ are similar since $\angle XAE$ is the same angle as $\angle DAC$ and $\angle AXE = \angle ADC$ (corresponding angles). So $\dfrac{XE}{DC} = \dfrac{AE}{AC} = \dfrac{1}{4}$; hence $XE = 4.5\text{ cm}$. Applying a similar argument to triangles $BYE$ and $BCD$, we find that $YE = 4.5\text{ cm}$ also. So the length $XY$ is $9\text{ cm}$.
