## 题目
In triangle $PQR$, angle $P = 90^\circ, PR = 15\text{ cm}$ and $QR = 17\text{ cm}$. Circular arcs are drawn with centres at $P, Q$ and $R$, and each arc touches the other two arcs. What is the radius of the arc with centre $R$?

![[Image/04-Q16-fig1.png]]

A 10 cm $\qquad$ B 10.5 cm $\qquad$ C 11 cm $\qquad$ D 11.5 cm $\qquad$ E 12 cm

## 答案
E

## 解析
Let the radius of the arc with centre $R$ be $r\text{ cm}$. Then $QT = (17 - r)\text{ cm}$ and $PU = (15 - r)\text{ cm}$. Now $QS = QT$ (radii of arc $ST$) and $PS = PU$ (radii of arc $SU$), so $QP = (17 - r + 15 - r)\text{ cm} = (32 - 2r)\text{ cm}$. But, by Pythagoras' Theorem: $QP = \sqrt{17^2 - 15^2}\text{ cm} = \sqrt{(17 + 15)(17 - 15)}\text{ cm} = 8\text{ cm}$. So $32 - 2r = 8$, that is $r = 12$.
