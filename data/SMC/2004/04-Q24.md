## 题目
The diagram (not to scale) shows two circles of radius 105 which are tangent to each other and to a circle of radius 14. What is the radius of the largest circle which can be placed in the shaded region?

![[Image/04-Q24-fig1.png]]

## 解析
Let $A$ be the centre of one of the large circles, $B$ the point where the two large circles touch, $C$ the centre of the small circle and $D$ the centre of the largest circle which can be placed in the shaded region, i.e. the circle which touches all three of the given circles. Let the radius of this circle be $r$.

Then $AB = 105$; $AD = 105 + r$, $AC = 105 + 14 = 119$, $CD = 14 + r$.

Applying Pythagoras' Theorem to triangle $ABC$ we find that $BC = \sqrt{119^2 - 105^2} = \sqrt{224 \times 14} = 56$. So $BD = 42 - r$.

Similarly, $(105 + r)^2 = 105^2 + (42 - r)^2$. This leads to the equation $294r = 42^2$, that is $r = 6$.
