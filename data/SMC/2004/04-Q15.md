## 题目
The trunk of a monkey-puzzle tree has diameter 40 cm. As a protection from fire, the trunk of the tree has a bark which makes up 19% of its volume. On average, roughly how thick is the bark of the trunk?

A 0.4 cm $\qquad$ B 1.2 cm $\qquad$ C 2 cm $\qquad$ D 2.8 cm $\qquad$ E 4 cm

## 中文题干
一棵智利南洋杉（monkey-puzzle tree）的树干直径为 40 cm。为了防火，树干有一层占其体积 19% 的树皮。平均而言，树干的树皮大约有多厚？

## 答案
C

## 解析
The core of the trunk occupies 81% of the volume of the trunk. Assuming that the trunk is cylindrical, this means that 81% of the cross-sectional area of the trunk is occupied by the core. Now $\sqrt{0.81} = 0.9$, so the diameter of the core is 90% of the diameter of the trunk, that is $36\text{ cm}$. Hence the thickness of the bark is $4\text{ cm} \div 2$, that is $2\text{ cm}$.
