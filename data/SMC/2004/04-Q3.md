## 题目
Milly and Molly are each given a 100g ice-lolly. They start eating their lollies at the same time, but Milly eats hers twice as fast as Molly. When Molly has three times as much of her lolly left as has Milly, what fraction of her lolly has Milly eaten?

A $\frac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ B $\frac{2}{3}$ $\qquad$ C $\frac{3}{4}$ $\qquad$ D $\frac{4}{5}$ $\qquad$ E $\frac{5}{6}$

## 中文题干
Milly 和 Molly 每人分得一根 100g 的冰棒。她们同时开始吃各自的冰棒，但 Milly 吃冰棒的速度是 Molly 的两倍。当 Molly 剩下的冰棒是 Milly 剩下的三倍时，Milly 已经吃掉了自己冰棒的几分之几？

## 答案
D

## 解析
Let the fraction of her lolly that Milly has eaten be $x$. Then Molly has eaten $\frac{1}{2}x$ of her lolly. This means that the fractions they have left are $(1 - x)$ and $\left(1 - \frac{1}{2}x\right)$ respectively. So $1 - \frac{1}{2}x = 3(1 - x)$, that is $5x = 4$, giving $x = \frac{4}{5}$.
