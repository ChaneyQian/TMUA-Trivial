## 题目
Triangle $PQR$ has a right angle at $Q$ and $PQ = QR$. The line through $Q$ which divides the angle $PQR$ in the ratio 1:2 meets $PR$ at $S$. What is the ratio $RS:SP$?

![[Image/04-Q21-fig1.png]]

A $\sqrt{2}:1$ $\qquad$ B $\sqrt{3}:1$ $\qquad$ C $2:1$ $\qquad$ D $\sqrt{5}:1$ $\qquad$ E $3:1$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Note, from the information given, that $\angle PRQ = \angle RPQ = 45^\circ$; $\angle RQS = 60^\circ$; $\angle PQS = 30^\circ$.

Applying the Sine Rule to $\triangle SRQ$: $\dfrac{RS}{\sin 60^\circ} = \dfrac{SQ}{\sin 45^\circ}$ and to $\triangle SPQ$: $\dfrac{SP}{\sin 30^\circ} = \dfrac{SQ}{\sin 45^\circ}$.

Hence $\dfrac{RS}{\sin 60^\circ} = \dfrac{SP}{\sin 30^\circ}$, so $RS : SP = \sin 60^\circ : \sin 30^\circ = \sqrt{3} : 1$.
