## 题目
A dictionary defines one billion to be either one million million or one thousand million. What is the difference between these two numbers?

A 1000 $\qquad$ B 1 000 000 $\qquad$ C 999 000 $\qquad$ D 999 000 000 $\qquad$ E 999 000 000 000

## 中文题干
一本词典将「one billion」定义为一百万个一百万（one million million）或一千个一百万（one thousand million）。这两个数之间的差是多少？

## 答案
E

## 解析
One million minus one thousand $= 1\,000\,000 - 1000 = 999\,000$. So one million million minus one thousand million $= 999\,000$ million $= 999\,000\,000\,000$.
