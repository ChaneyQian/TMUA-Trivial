## 题目
The graph of $y = |f(x)|$ is shown. Given that the graph of $y = f(x)$ is a continuous curve, how many different possibilities are there for the graph of $y = f(x)$?

![[Image/04-Q17-fig1.png]]

A 16 $\qquad$ B 12 $\qquad$ C 8 $\qquad$ D 4 $\qquad$ E 2

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Consider the four portions $AB$, $BO$, $OC$ and $CD$ of the graph shown. The graph of $y = f(x)$ contains either portion $AB$ as shown, or the reflection of this portion in the $x$-axis. The same is true for the other three portions, so the number of different graphs of $y = f(x)$ which would give this graph of $y = \left|f(x)\right|$ is $2 \times 2 \times 2 \times 2$ that is 16.
