## 题目
How many numbers from 12 to 12345 inclusive have digits which are consecutive and in increasing order, reading from left to right?

A 10 $\qquad$ B 13 $\qquad$ C 18 $\qquad$ D 22 $\qquad$ E 25

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
There are eight 2-digit numbers which satisfy the required condition (12, 23, …, 89), seven 3-digit numbers (123, 234, …, 789), six 4-digit numbers (1234, 2345, …, 6789) and one 5-digit number (12345).
