## 题目
Karen has three times the number of cherries that Lionel has, and twice the number of cherries that Michael has. Michael has seven more cherries than Lionel. How many cherries do Karen, Lionel and Michael have altogether?

A 12 $\qquad$ B 42 $\qquad$ C 60 $\qquad$ D 77 $\qquad$ E 84

## 答案
D

## 解析
Suppose that Karen has $x$ cherries. Then Lionel has $\frac{1}{3}x$ cherries and Michael has $\frac{1}{2}x$ cherries. Michael has seven more cherries than Lionel and so $\frac{1}{2}x - \frac{1}{3}x = 7$. Therefore $\left(\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{3}\right)x = 7$, that is, $\left(\frac{3-2}{6}\right)x = 7$, and hence $\frac{1}{6}x = 7$. Therefore $x = 42$. It follows that Karen has $42$ cherries, Lionel has $14$ cherries and Michael has $21$ cherries. So they have $42 + 14 + 21 = 77$ cherries between them.
