## 题目
Each of the five nets P, Q, R, S and T is made from six squares. Both sides of each square have the same colour. Net P is folded to form a cube.

![[Image/14-Q13-fig1.png|560]]

How many of the nets Q, R, S and T can be folded to produce a cube that looks the same as that produced by P?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C 2 $\qquad$ D 3 $\qquad$ E 4

## 答案
E

## 解析
Each net folds to make a cube with three white and three grey faces. There are two possibilities for such a cube. Either the three grey faces meet at a common vertex, or they do not have a vertex in common.

If the three grey faces meet at a common vertex, the cube looks like the one on the left below, where the top, back and right-hand faces are grey.

If the grey faces do not have a vertex in common then the cube looks like the one on the right below, where the top, left-hand and right-hand faces are grey.

![[Image/14-Q13-sol1.png]]

It the nets are folded, the nets P, Q, R, S and T make cubes where the three grey faces do not have a vertex in common. So they all form a cube which looks like the cube on the right above. Hence all four of Q, R, S and T fold to make a cube that looks the same as that produced by P.
