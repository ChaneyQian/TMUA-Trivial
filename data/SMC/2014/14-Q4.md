## 题目
After I had spent $\frac{1}{5}$ of my money and then spent $\frac{1}{4}$ of what was left, I had £15 remaining. How much did I start with?

A £25 $\qquad$ B £75 $\qquad$ C £100 $\qquad$ D £135 $\qquad$ E £300

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Suppose that I started with £$x$. After spending $\frac{1}{5}$ of my money, I was left with $\frac{4}{5}$ of what I started with. After spending $\frac{1}{4}$ of what was left, there remained $\frac{3}{4} \times \frac{4}{5} = \frac{3}{5}$ of what I started with. Since £15 remained, $\frac{3}{5}x = 15$. Therefore $x = \frac{5}{3} \times 15 = 25$. Therefore, I started with £25.
