## 题目
According to recent research, global sea levels could rise $36.8\text{ cm}$ by the year 2100 as a result of melting ice. Roughly how many millimetres is that per year?

A 10 $\qquad$ B 4 $\qquad$ C 1 $\qquad$ D 0.4 $\qquad$ E 0.1

## 答案
B

## 解析
The year 2100 is $2100 - 2015 = 85$ years away, and $36.8\text{ cm}$ is $368$ millimetres. Now $\dfrac{368}{85}$ is approximately $\dfrac{360}{90}$, that is, $4$.

**Remark**

The key idea here is to replace the numbers $368$ and $85$ by approximations, say, $a$ and $b$, so that the fraction $\dfrac{a}{b}$ is easy to evaluate and is sufficiently close to $\dfrac{368}{85}$. Other choices are possible.
