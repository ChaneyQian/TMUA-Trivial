## 题目
A square is inscribed in a circle of radius 1. An isosceles triangle is inscribed in the square as shown.

![[Image/18-Q15-fig1.png|200]]

What is the ratio of the area of this triangle to the area of the shaded region?

A $\pi : \sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ B $\pi : 1$ $\qquad$ C $1 : 4$ $\qquad$ D $1 : \pi - 2$ $\qquad$ E $2 : \pi$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Let the sides of the square have length $s$. We see from the diagram that, by Pythagoras' theorem, $s^2 = 1^2 + 1^2$ and hence $s = \sqrt{2}$.

![[Image/18-Q15-sol1.png]]

The area of the circle is $\pi \times 1^2$, which equals $\pi$. The area of the square is $\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2}$, which equals $2$. Hence the area of the shaded region is $\pi - 2$.

The base of the isosceles triangle is one of the sides of the square and so has length $\sqrt{2}$. The perpendicular height of the isosceles triangle is also $\sqrt{2}$. The area of the triangle is therefore $\dfrac{1}{2}(\sqrt{2} \times \sqrt{2})$ which equals $1$.

Hence the ratio of the area of the triangle to the area of the shaded region is $1 : \pi - 2$.
