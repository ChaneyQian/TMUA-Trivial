## 题目
When the following are evaluated, how many of the answers are odd numbers?

$$1^2, \quad 2^3, \quad 3^4, \quad 4^5, \quad 5^6$$

A 1 $\qquad$ B 2 $\qquad$ C 3 $\qquad$ D 4 $\qquad$ E 5

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
When two odd numbers are multiplied, the result is another odd number. Therefore an odd integer raised to a positive power results in an odd number. Likewise, an even integer raised to a positive power results in an even number.

It follows that, of the given numbers, $1^2$, $3^4$ and $5^6$ are odd numbers, while $2^3$ and $4^5$ are even.

Therefore $3$ of the given expressions evaluate to an odd number.
