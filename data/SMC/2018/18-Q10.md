## 题目
On a training ride, Laura averages speeds of $12\text{ km/h}$ for 5 minutes, then $15\text{ km/h}$ for 10 minutes and finally $18\text{ km/h}$ for 15 minutes.

What was her average speed over the whole ride?

A $13\text{ km/h}$ $\qquad$ B $14\text{ km/h}$ $\qquad$ C $15\text{ km/h}$ $\qquad$ D $16\text{ km/h}$ $\qquad$ E $17\text{ km/h}$

## 答案
D

## 解析
To determine Laura's average speed over the whole ride we calculate the total distance she travels, and the total time that she takes.

Because $5$ minutes is $\dfrac{1}{12}$ of an hour, Laura travels $\dfrac{1}{12} \times 12\text{ km} = 1\text{ km}$ when she travels for $5$ minutes at $12\text{ km/h}$.

Because $10$ minutes is $\dfrac{1}{6}$ of an hour, Laura travels $\dfrac{1}{6} \times 15\text{ km} = 2.5\text{ km}$ when she travels for $10$ minutes at $15\text{ km/h}$.

Because $15$ minutes is $\dfrac{1}{4}$ of an hour, Laura travels $\dfrac{1}{4} \times 18\text{ km} = 4.5\text{ km}$ when she travels for $15$ minutes at $18\text{ km/h}$.

Therefore Laura travels at total of $1\text{ km} + 2.5\text{ km} + 4.5\text{ km} = 8\text{ km}$ in $5 + 10 + 15$ minutes, that is, in $30$ minutes, which is half an hour.

Because Laura travels $8\text{ km}$ in half an hour, her average speed is $16\text{ km/h}$.
