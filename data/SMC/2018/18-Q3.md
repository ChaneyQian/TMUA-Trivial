## 解析
Because $135^\circ = 90^\circ + 45^\circ$, a rotation through $135^\circ$ clockwise is equivalent to a rotation clockwise through $90^\circ$, which is a quarter turn, followed by a rotation clockwise through $45^\circ$, which is one-eighth of a complete turn.

![[Image/18-Q3-sol1.png]]

Therefore, as the diagram shows, a rotation through $135^\circ$ clockwise, results in the configuration given as option D.
