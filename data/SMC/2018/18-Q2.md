## 题目
The positive integer 2018 is the product of two primes.

What is the sum of these two primes?

A 1001 $\qquad$ B 1010 $\qquad$ C 1011 $\qquad$ D 1100 $\qquad$ E 1101

## 答案
C

## 解析
The prime factorization of $2018$ is given by $2018 = 2 \times 1009$. The sum of the two prime factors is $2 + 1009 = 1011$.

**Note**

There is no very quick way to check that $1009$ is prime, as this would involve checking that no prime $\le 29$ is a divisor. [Why is this enough?] Fortunately, the question tells us that $2018$ is the product of two primes. We can conclude from this that the second factor $1009$ is prime.
