## 题目
The diagram shows a rectangle $AEFJ$ inside a regular decagon $ABCDEFGHIJ$.

![[Image/18-Q9-fig1.png|195]]

What is the ratio of the area of the rectangle to the area of the decagon?

A $2 : 5$ $\qquad$ B $1 : 4$ $\qquad$ C $3 : 5$ $\qquad$ D $3 : 10$ $\qquad$ E $3 : 20$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Let $O$ be the centre of the regular decagon. The decagon is divided into ten congruent isosceles triangles by the radii joining $O$ to each of the vertices of the decagon. The triangles $AOJ$ and $EOF$ are two of these ten congruent triangles.

![[Image/18-Q9-sol1.png]]

Because $O$ is the centre of the rectangle $AEFJ$ and the diagonals of a rectangle split its area into four equal areas, the triangles $EOA$ and $FOJ$ each have the same area as triangles $AOJ$ and $EOF$.

![[Image/18-Q9-sol2.png]]

Therefore the area of $AEFJ$ is equal to the area of $4$ of the $10$ congruent triangles that make up the decagon.

Hence the ratio of the area of $AEFJ$ to the area of the decagon $ABCDEFGHI$ is $4 : 10$ which simplifies to $2 : 5$.
