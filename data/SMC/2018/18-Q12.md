## 题目
A regular tetrahedron is a polyhedron with four faces, each of which is an equilateral triangle, as shown. A solid regular tetrahedron is cut into two pieces by a single plane cut.

![[Image/18-Q12-fig1.png|200]]

Which of the following could *not* be the shape of the section formed by the cut?

A a pentagon  
B a square  
C a rectangle that is not a square  
D a trapezium  
E a triangle that is not equilateral

## 答案
A

## 解析
When the regular tetrahedron is cut by a single plane cut, each of its four faces is cut at most once. The place where each face is cut becomes the edge of the newly formed section. Therefore a pentagon, with five edges, cannot be formed.

However, each of the other four options is possible, as the following diagrams show.
