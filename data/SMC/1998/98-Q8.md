## 题目
When the following five numbers are arranged in numerical order, which one is in the middle?

A $4\sqrt{15}$ $\qquad$ B $5\sqrt{10}$ $\qquad$ C $7\sqrt{5}$ $\qquad$ D $9\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ E $11\sqrt{2}$

## 中文题干
将下列五个数按大小顺序排列时，哪一个排在中间？

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Squaring each number gives $240$, $250$, $245$, $243$ and $242$. In terms of size, the middle of these is $243$.
