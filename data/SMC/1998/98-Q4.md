## 题目
The smaller circle touches the larger circle, and goes through the centre of the larger circle. What fraction of the area of the larger circle is outside the smaller circle?

![[Image/98-Q4-fig1.png]]

A $\frac{2}{3}$ $\qquad$ B $\frac{3}{4}$ $\qquad$ C $\frac{4}{5}$ $\qquad$ D $\frac{5}{6}$ $\qquad$ E $\frac{7}{8}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
The diameter of the smaller circle and the radius of the larger circle are equal. Letting the radius of the smaller circle be $r$, the areas are $\pi r^2$ and $\pi(2r)^2 = 4\pi r^2$ so the smaller circle is $\frac{1}{4}$ of the larger.

**Note**: this is an example of the property that in an enlargement the area scale factor is the square of the linear factor.
