## 题目
Observe that $18 = 4^2 + 1^2 + 1^2 + 0^2$. How many of the first fifteen positive integers can be written as the sum of the squares of four integers?

A 11 $\qquad$ B 12 $\qquad$ C 13 $\qquad$ D 14 $\qquad$ E 15

## 答案
E

## 解析
The question really asks how many of the first $15$ positive integers can be expressed as the sum of $4$ or fewer perfect squares. These can be listed as follows:−

$1 = 1^2$; $2 = 1^2 + 1^2$; $3 = 1^2 + 1^2 + 1^2$; $4 = 2^2$. Expressions for $5$, $6$, $7$ and $8$ can be obtained by adding $2^2$ to each of the ones already given. $9 = 3^2$ and expressions for $10$, $11$, $12$, $13$, $14$, and $15$ can be obtained by adding $3^2$ to the ones for $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $5$ and $6$.

**Note**: In fact, the French mathematician Lagrange proved in $1770$ that every positive integer can be expressed as the sum of four squares.
