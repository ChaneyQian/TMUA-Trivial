## 题目
A cube is inscribed in a sphere of diameter $1\text{m}$. What is the surface area of the cube?

A $2\text{ m}^2$ $\qquad$ B $3\text{ m}^2$ $\qquad$ C $4\text{ m}^2$ $\qquad$ D $5\text{ m}^2$ $\qquad$ E $6\text{ m}^2$

## 中文题干
一个正方体内接于一个直径为 $1\text{m}$ 的球体。该正方体的表面积是多少？

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Space diagonal of the cube is $1$ metre.

So, using $x$ as the side of the cube

$$3x^2 = 1$$

$$x^2 = \frac{1}{3} = \text{area of one face}$$

$\therefore$ total area of all six faces is $2\text{ m}^2$.
