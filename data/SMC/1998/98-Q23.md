## 题目
Circles with radii $r$ and $R$ (where $r < R$) touch each other and also touch two perpendicular lines as shown. What is the value of $R/r$?

![[Image/98-Q23-fig1.png]]

A $5 + \sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ B $5.75$ $\qquad$ C $\sqrt{40}$ $\qquad$ D $6$ $\qquad$ E $3 + 2\sqrt{2}$

## 中文题干
如图，半径分别为 $r$ 和 $R$（其中 $r < R$）的两个圆互相外切，且均与两条互相垂直的直线相切。$R/r$ 的值是多少？

## 答案
E

## 解析
The small circle can be enlarged to the size of the other circle by using a scale factor of $R/r$. So if $A$ and $B$ are the centres of the circles

$$\frac{R}{r} = \frac{OB}{OA} = \frac{r\sqrt{2} + r + R}{r\sqrt{2}}$$

$$\sqrt{2}R = r\sqrt{2} + r + R$$

$$\frac{R}{r} = \frac{\sqrt{2} + 1}{\sqrt{2} - 1} = \frac{(\sqrt{2} + 1)^2}{(\sqrt{2} - 1)(\sqrt{2} + 1)} = 3 + 2\sqrt{2}.$$
