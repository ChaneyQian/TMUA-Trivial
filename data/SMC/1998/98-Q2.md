## 题目
In a class of thirty pupils, there are twenty-two pupils who are right-handed and there are fourteen girls. What is the smallest number of girls who could be right-handed?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 2 $\qquad$ C 4 $\qquad$ D 6 $\qquad$ E 8

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Of the thirty pupils, $8$ are left-handed. In the extreme case, these will all be girls. So the smallest number of girls who could be right-handed is $6$.
