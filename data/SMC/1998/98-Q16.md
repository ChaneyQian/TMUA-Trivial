## 题目
The probability of a single ticket winning the jackpot in the National Lottery is
$$\frac{6}{49} \times \frac{5}{48} \times \frac{4}{47} \times \frac{3}{46} \times \frac{2}{45} \times \frac{1}{44}$$
If I buy one ticket every week, approximately how often might I expect to win the jackpot?

A once every hundred years  
B once every twenty thousand years  
C once every hundred thousand years  
D once every quarter of a million years  
E once every million years

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
$$\text{Number of years} = \frac{49}{6} \times \frac{48}{5} \times \frac{47}{4} \times \frac{46}{3} \times \frac{45}{2} \times \frac{44}{1} \div 52$$

$$= 49 \times 1 \times 47 \times 46 \times 3 \times 44 \div 52$$

$$\approx 50^3 \times 3 = 375\,000.$$

**Note**: $375000$ is clearly an overestimate since $49 \times 1 \times 47 \times 46$ is less than $50^3$ and $3 \times 44 \div 52$ is less than $3$.
