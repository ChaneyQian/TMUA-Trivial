## 题目
A square has the same perimeter as a $4\text{ cm}$ by $2\text{ cm}$ rectangle. What is the area of the square (in $\text{cm}^2$)?

A 4 $\qquad$ B 8 $\qquad$ C 9 $\qquad$ D 10 $\qquad$ E 12

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The perimeter of the rectangle is $12\text{ cm}$. So each side of the square is $3\text{ cm}$.
