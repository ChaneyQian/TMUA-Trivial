## 题目
If $x = (1/4)^{1/2}$, what is the value of $x^{-x}$?

A $1/4$ $\qquad$ B $1/2$ $\qquad$ C $1/\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ D $\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ E 2

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
$$x = \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{\frac{1}{4}} = \frac{1}{2}$$

$$\therefore\ x^{-x} = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}} = \left(2^{-1}\right)^{-\frac{1}{2}} = 2^{\frac{1}{2}} = \sqrt{2}.$$
