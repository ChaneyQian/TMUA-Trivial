## 题目
Mary's height increased by $30\%$ between her 5th birthday and her 10th birthday. It increased by $20\%$ between her 10th birthday and her 15th birthday. By how much did her height increase between her 5th birthday and her 15th birthday?

A $50\%$ $\qquad$ B $52\%$ $\qquad$ C $54\%$ $\qquad$ D $56\%$ $\qquad$ E $60\%$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Let Mary's height at age $5$ be $h$. So at age $10$ her height is $1.3h$ and by age $15$ this will be $1.2 \times 1.3h = 1.56h$.
