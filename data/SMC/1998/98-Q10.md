## 题目
Roughly how many seconds are there in a day?

A $10^3$ $\qquad$ B $10^4$ $\qquad$ C $10^5$ $\qquad$ D $10^6$ $\qquad$ E $10^7$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
There are $60 \times 60$ seconds in an hour. In a day there are $24 \times 60 \times 60 = 24 \times 4 \times 15 \times 60 = 96 \times 900$ which is roughly $10^2 \times 10^3 = 10^5$.
