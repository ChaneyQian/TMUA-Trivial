## 题目
The *integer part* of a positive number is the part before the decimal point; the *decimal part* is the part after the decimal point. For example, the integer part of 3.72 is '3' and the decimal part is '0.72'. Which of the following numbers has decimal part equal to exactly one eighth of the integer part?

A 0.05 $\qquad$ B 1.15 $\qquad$ C 2.25 $\qquad$ D 3.35 $\qquad$ E 4.45

## 中文题干
正数的*整数部分*是小数点前面的部分；*小数部分*是小数点后面的部分。例如，3.72 的整数部分是「3」，小数部分是「0.72」。下列哪一个数的小数部分恰好等于其整数部分的八分之一？

## 答案
C

## 解析
Multiplying the decimal part of each option by $8$ gives $0.40$, $1.20$, $2$, $2.8$ and $3.6$.
