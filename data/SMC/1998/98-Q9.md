## 题目
Pascal, Newton, Galileo and Fermat all took the same test. The average score of all four candidates was 16; Pascal and Newton had an average of 16, Pascal and Fermat had an average of 13, while Newton and Fermat had an average of 18. What was Galileo's score?

A 14 $\qquad$ B 15 $\qquad$ C 16 $\qquad$ D 17 $\qquad$ E 18

## 答案
D

## 解析
Using their initial to denote a candidate's mark gives:−

$P + N = 32$; $P + F = 26$; $N + F = 36$. Adding these three equations gives $2P + 2N + 2F = 94$, hence $P + N + F = 47$. Thus $47 + G = 4 \times 16$ giving $G = 17$.

**Biographical note**: although the lives of Pascal and Fermat overlapped with the lives of the other two mathematicians, Galileo died in $1642$, the year in which Newton was born (on Christmas Day).
