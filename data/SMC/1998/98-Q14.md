## 题目
The smaller circle has radius 10 units; $AB$ is a diameter. The larger circle has centre $A$, radius 12 units and cuts the smaller circle at $C$. What is the length of the chord $CB$?

![[Image/98-Q14-fig1.png]]

A 8 $\qquad$ B 10 $\qquad$ C 12 $\qquad$ D $10\sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ E 16

## 中文题干
较小圆的半径为 10 个单位，$AB$ 为其一条直径。较大圆的圆心为 $A$，半径为 12 个单位，且与较小圆相交于点 $C$。弦 $CB$ 的长度是多少？

## 答案
E

## 解析
$AB$ is a diameter so $\angle ACB = 90^\circ$.

$$\therefore CB^2 = AB^2 - AC^2 = 400 - 144 = 256$$

$$CB = 16$$
