## 题目
If $\cos\theta = 1/2$, which of these cannot equal $\sin 2\theta$?

A $\sin\theta$ $\qquad$ B $1/2$ $\qquad$ C $-\sqrt{3}/2$ $\qquad$ D $\sqrt{3}/2$ $\qquad$ E $2\cos\theta\sin\theta$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
$\cos\theta = \frac{1}{2}$; $\sin^2\theta = \frac{3}{4}$; $\sin\theta = \pm\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}$

$\sin 2\theta = 2\cos\theta\sin\theta = 2 \times \frac{1}{2} \times \left(\pm\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}\right) = \pm\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}$

**Note**: $\sin 2\theta$ is equal to $2\cos\theta\sin\theta$ for all values of $\theta$ and since in this case $\cos\theta = \frac{1}{2}$, $\sin 2\theta$ will also equal $\sin\theta$.
