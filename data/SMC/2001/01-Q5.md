## 题目
Goldbach's conjecture, which has not been proved, states that every even number greater than two is the sum of two primes. However, the same is not true for every odd number. Which of the following odd numbers is *not* the sum of two primes?

A 13 $\qquad$ B 33 $\qquad$ C 43 $\qquad$ D 53 $\qquad$ E 73

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Since the numbers offered are all odd, they need to be the sum of an even prime and an odd prime. But $2$ is the only even prime so we need a number which is not two more than a prime. Since $13 = 2 + 11$; $33 = 2 + 31$; $43 = 2 + 41$ and $73 = 2 + 71$, we have $53 = 2 + 3 \times 17$ as the answer.
