## 题目
The diagram shows a square and two equilateral triangles. All the sides have length 1. What is the length of $XY$?

![[Image/01-Q14-fig1.png]]

A $\sqrt{3} - 1$ $\qquad$ B $\frac{2}{3}$ $\qquad$ C $\frac{3}{4}$ $\qquad$ D $\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ $\qquad$ E $\frac{2 + \sqrt{3}}{4}$

## 答案
A

## 解析
The altitude of an equilateral triangle of side $1$ is $\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}$. Hence

$$XY = 2 \times \tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{3} - 1 = \sqrt{3} - 1.$$
