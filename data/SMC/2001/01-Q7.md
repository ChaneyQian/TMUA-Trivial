## 题目
A gardener has a field in the shape of an isosceles triangle. He decided to plant it with rose bushes with the aid of his apprentice. He planted the first row with 101 bushes along the base, then his apprentice planted the next row with 100 bushes. They continued in this way, planting alternate rows, until the whole field was planted. The gardener planted the last row, which contained 1 bush. How many more bushes did the gardener plant than the apprentice?

![[Image/01-Q7-fig1.png]]

A 100 $\qquad$ B 101 $\qquad$ C 49 $\qquad$ D 50 $\qquad$ E 51

## 答案
E

## 解析
For each row that the apprentice plants, the gardener has planted one more in his previous row. The apprentice plants $50$ rows and in addition the gardener plants the last one.
