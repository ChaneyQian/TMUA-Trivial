## 题目
$ABCDEF$ is a regular hexagon of area 60. What is the area of the kite-shaped figure $ABCE$?

![[Image/01-Q8-fig1.png]]

A $20\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ B 40 $\qquad$ C 49 $\qquad$ D 50 $\qquad$ E 51

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Joining $A$ to $C$ and each of $A$, $C$ and $E$ to the centre of the hexagon divides it into six congruent triangles four of which make up $ABCE$. So the area of $ABCE$ is $\dfrac{2}{3}$ of $60$.
