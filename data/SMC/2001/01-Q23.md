## 题目
An equilateral triangle is inscribed in a circle, as shown. Another equilateral triangle is drawn in one of the segments so that the final diagram has a line of symmetry. What is the ratio of the length of the side of the bigger triangle to the length of the side of the smaller triangle?

![[Image/01-Q23-fig1.png]]

A $(\sqrt{5} + 1):1$ $\qquad$ B $(2\sqrt{5} + 1):1$ $\qquad$ C $(\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{5}):1$ $\qquad$ D $2\sqrt{5}:1$ $\qquad$ E $3:1$

## 解析
Draw in the lines and attach labels as shown and let the circle have radius $r$. ($O$ is the centre of the circle.) In $\triangle OPB$: $OP = OB \sin 30^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}r$. Then, looking at $\triangle OPQ$, $\angle OPQ = 150^\circ$ so we can apply the Cosine Rule to obtain $PQ$.

$$OQ^2 = PQ^2 + OP^2 - 2PQ.OP \cos 150^\circ$$

Writing $PQ = ry$, this gives

$$r^2 = r^2y^2 + \tfrac{1}{4}r^2 - 2 \times ry \times \tfrac{1}{2}r\left(-\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)$$

i.e.

$$1 = y^2 + \tfrac{1}{4} + \tfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{3}\,y$$

$$\left(y + \tfrac{1}{4}\sqrt{3}\right)^2 = \tfrac{3}{4} + \left(\tfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)^2 = \tfrac{15}{16}$$

$$\therefore \quad y = \frac{\sqrt{15}}{4} - \frac{\sqrt{3}}{4} \qquad \text{(ignoring the negative root)}.$$

Hence the ratio

$$\frac{AB}{PQ} = \frac{\sqrt{3}r}{\left(\frac{\sqrt{15}-\sqrt{3}}{4}\right)r} = \frac{4}{(\sqrt{5}-1)} \times \frac{\sqrt{5}+1}{\sqrt{5}+1} = \sqrt{5} + 1.$$
