## 题目
In the Hickey Hockey league, a team scores 5 points for a win, 2 for a draw and 0 if it loses. The Hickey Hockey Club has played 20 games in this league and has scored 21 points. What is the smallest possible number of games it could have lost?

A 11 $\qquad$ B 12 $\qquad$ C 13 $\qquad$ D 14 $\qquad$ E 15

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
To have lost the fewest games, there need to be as many draws as possible. However, since $21$ is odd, there needs to be at least one win. That is possible with $8$ draws, leaving $11$ games lost.
