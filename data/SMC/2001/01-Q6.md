## 题目
The mean of seven consecutive odd numbers is 21. What is the sum of the first, third, fifth and seventh of these numbers?

A 16 $\qquad$ B 21 $\qquad$ C 84 $\qquad$ D 147 $\qquad$ E more information needed

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Since the four numbers are symmetrical about the mean of the group, they must have the same mean, $21$, giving their total as $84$.

Alternatively, it is possible to state that the original numbers must be $15$, $17$, $19$, $21$, $23$, $25$, $27$ (since the numbers are given as consecutive and odd). Hence the required sum is $15 + 19 + 23 + 27 = 84$.
