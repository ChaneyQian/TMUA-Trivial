## 题目
Sam correctly calculates the value of $5^8 \times 8^5$. How many digits does her answer contain?

A 11 $\qquad$ B 12 $\qquad$ C 13 $\qquad$ D 14 $\qquad$ E 15

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
$5^8 \times 8^5 = 5^8 \times 2^{15} = (5 \times 2)^8 \times 2^7 = 128 \times 100\,000\,000 = 12\,800\,000\,000$ which has $11$ digits.
