## 题目
Given that $x = 1/y$, where $x$ and $y$ are unequal and non-zero, which of the following is always equal to $(x + 1/x)(y - 1/y)$?

A $y^2 - x^2$ $\qquad$ B $x^2 - y^2$ $\qquad$ C $2y$ $\qquad$ D $2x$ $\qquad$ E 0

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Since $x = \dfrac{1}{y}$, it follows that $y = \dfrac{1}{x}$. Hence $\left(x + \dfrac{1}{x}\right)\left(y - \dfrac{1}{y}\right) = (x + y)(y - x) = (y^2 - x^2)$.
