## 题目
A candle will burn for 100 hours. If I light it at midday on Sunday, on which day will it burn out?

A Tuesday $\qquad$ B Wednesday $\qquad$ C Thursday $\qquad$ D Friday $\qquad$ E Saturday

## 中文题干
一根蜡烛可燃烧 100 小时。如果我在星期日中午点燃它，它会在星期几燃尽？

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
$100$ hours equal four days and four hours so the candle will burn out at 4pm on Thursday.
