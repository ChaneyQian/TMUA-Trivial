## 题目
Which of the following numbers $n$ gives a counter-example for the statement: 'If $n$ is a prime number then $n^2 + 2$ is also a prime number'?

A 3 $\qquad$ B 5 $\qquad$ C 6 $\qquad$ D 9 $\qquad$ E none of them

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

**同背景题组 · n²±2 的质性**
同一素材：判断 n 为质数是否蕴含 n²±2 为质数，均以反例作答。

相关题：[[SMC/2021/21-Q11|21-Q11]] · [[TMUA/2022/22-P2-Q3|22-P2-Q3]]

## 答案
B

## 解析
Since $6$ and $9$ are not prime, C and D cannot provide counter-examples. For $3$, both parts are true (since $11$ is prime) but since $5^2 + 2 = 27$ is not prime, B does provide a counter-example.
