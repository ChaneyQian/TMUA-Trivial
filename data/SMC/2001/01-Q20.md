## 题目
In the diagram, $AB$, $CB$ and $XY$ are tangents to the circle with centre $O$ and $\angle ABC = 48^\circ$. What is the size of $\angle XOY$?

![[Image/01-Q20-fig1.png]]

A $42^\circ$ $\qquad$ B $69^\circ$ $\qquad$ C $66^\circ$ $\qquad$ D $48^\circ$ $\qquad$ E $84^\circ$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Let the points of contacts of the tangents be $P$, $Q$ and $R$ as shown and let $\angle XOQ = x$ and $\angle QOY = y$. Then since $OX$ is the axis of symmetry of the tangent kite $OPXQ$, it bisects $\angle POQ$ so $\angle XOP = x$. Similarly $\angle ROY = y$. Thus, in the quadrilateral $OPBR$ we have

$$2x + 2y + 2 \times 90^\circ + 48^\circ = 360^\circ$$

i.e.

$$2(x + y) = 132^\circ$$

i.e.

$$x + y = 66^\circ$$
