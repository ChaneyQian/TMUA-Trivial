## 题目
Rosie the road-runner recently ran in two road races. The second race was $20\%$ further in distance than the first race and Rosie's average speed was $20\%$ slower in the second race. By what percentage was her time for the second race greater than that for the first?

A $33\frac{1}{3}\%$ $\qquad$ B $40\%$ $\qquad$ C $44\%$ $\qquad$ D $50\%$ $\qquad$ E $66\frac{2}{3}\%$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Let the length of the first race be $d$ and Rosie's average speed in this race be $v$ (in appropriate units). Thus her time in this race was $\dfrac{d}{v}$.

The length of the second race was $d \times \dfrac{6}{5}$ and her average speed was $v \times \dfrac{4}{5}$. Hence her time for the second race was $\left(d \times \dfrac{6}{5}\right) \div \left(v \times \dfrac{4}{5}\right) = \dfrac{6}{5} \times \dfrac{5}{4} \times \dfrac{d}{v} = \dfrac{3}{2} \times \dfrac{d}{v}$. She took $50\%$ longer.
