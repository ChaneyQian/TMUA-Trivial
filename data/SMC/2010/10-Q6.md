## 题目
Dean runs up a mountain road at $8\text{ km per hour}$. It takes him one hour to get to the top. He runs down the same road at $12\text{ km per hour}$. How many minutes does it take him to run down the mountain?

A 30 $\qquad$ B 40 $\qquad$ C 45 $\qquad$ D 50 $\qquad$ E 90

## 答案
B

## 解析
Since it takes 1 hour at 8 km per hour to get to the top, the length of the mountain road is 8 km. Hence, running at 12 km per hour it takes $\frac{8}{12}$ th of an hour, that is, 40 minutes, to run down the road.

*Note:* It isn’t really necessary to work out that the length of the road is 8 km. We can argue directly that running at 12 km per hour takes $\frac{8}{12}$ th of the time taken to run the same distance at 8 km per hour. So the time taken to run down the road is $\frac{8}{12}$ th of the time taken to run up it.
