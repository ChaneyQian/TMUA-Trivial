## 题目
What is the digit $x$ in this cross-number?

![[Image/SMC-10-Q1-fig1.png|147]]

* **Across**
  1. A cube
  2. A cube
* **Down**
  1. One less than a cube

A 2 $\qquad$ B 3 $\qquad$ C 4 $\qquad$ D 5 $\qquad$ E 6

## 答案
C

## 解析
The only 2-digit cubes are $3^3 = 27$ and $4^3 = 64$. So the first column is either $\begin{matrix}2\\2\end{matrix}$, $\begin{matrix}2\\6\end{matrix}$, $\begin{matrix}6\\2\end{matrix}$ or $\begin{matrix}6\\6\end{matrix}$.

Of the possibilities for 1 down, only 26 is 1 less than a cube. So 3 across is 64 (and 1 across is 27). Hence, $x = 4$.
