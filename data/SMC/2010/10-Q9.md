## 题目
The symbol $\diamondsuit$ is defined by $x \diamondsuit y = x^y - y^x$. What is the value of $(2 \diamondsuit 3) \diamondsuit 4$?

A $-3$ $\qquad$ B $-\dfrac{3}{4}$ $\qquad$ C $0$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{3}{4}$ $\qquad$ E $3$

## 中文题干
符号 $\diamondsuit$ 定义为 $x \diamondsuit y = x^y - y^x$。$(2 \diamondsuit 3) \diamondsuit 4$ 的值是多少？

## 答案
D

## 解析
We have that $2 \lozenge 3 = 2^3 - 3^2 = 8 - 9 = -1$. Hence

$$(2 \lozenge 3) \lozenge 4 = (-1) \lozenge 4 = (-1)^4 - 4^{-1} = 1 - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{3}{4}.$$
