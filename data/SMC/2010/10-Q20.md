## 题目
There are 10 girls in a mixed class. If two pupils from the class are selected at random to represent the class on the School Council, then the probability that both are girls is $0.15$. How many boys are in the class?

A 10 $\qquad$ B 12 $\qquad$ C 15 $\qquad$ D 18 $\qquad$ E 20

## 答案
C

## 解析
Suppose there are $x$ boys in the class. Then there are $x + 10$ pupils altogether, of whom 10 are girls. So the probability that the first pupil chosen is a girl is $\dfrac{10}{x + 10}$. If a girl is chosen, there remain $x + 9$ pupils, of whom 9 are girls, so that the probability that the second pupil chosen is also a girl is $\dfrac{9}{x + 9}$.

Hence the probability that the two chosen pupils are both girls is $\dfrac{10}{10 + x} \times \dfrac{9}{9 + x} = \dfrac{90}{(10 + x)(9 + x)}$.

Therefore, we need to solve the equation $\dfrac{90}{(x + 10)(x + 9)} = 0.15$. In the context of the SMC you could just try the suggested alternatives in turn. A mathematically better method is to solve this equation algebraically. We have that

$$\frac{90}{(10 + x)(9 + x)} = 0.15 \Longleftrightarrow 90 = \tfrac{3}{20}(10 + x)(9 + x)$$

$$\Longleftrightarrow 600 = x^2 + 19x + 90 \Longleftrightarrow x^2 + 19x - 510 = 0 \Longleftrightarrow (x + 34)(x - 15) = 0.$$

This gives $x = 15$ or $-34$. Since you shouldn’t have negative pupils (and certainly you can’t have a negative number of them), the solution is $x = 15$.
