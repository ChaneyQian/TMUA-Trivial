## 题目
There are 120 different arrangements of the five letters in the word ANGLE. If all 120 are listed in alphabetical order starting with AEGLN and finishing with NLGEA, which position in the list does ANGLE occupy?

A 18th $\qquad$ B 20th $\qquad$ C 22nd $\qquad$ D 24th $\qquad$ E 26th

## 答案
C

## 解析
There are $4! = 24$ arrangements of the 4 letters NGLE. In alphabetical order these run from EGLN to NLGE. So the first 24 arrangements of the letters in ANGLE run from AEGLN to ANLGE. Since there are $3! = 6$ arrangements of 3 letters, the first 6 of these 24 arrangements begin AE, the next 6 with AG, the next 6 with AL, and the final 6 with AN. So the 19th to 24th arrangements are ANEGL, ANELG, ANGEL, ANGLE, ANLEG, ANLGE. Thus ANGLE is the 22nd arrangement in the list.
