## 题目
When forest trees are planted 1 metre apart in a particular repeating pattern, covering a large area of ground, the density of trees is about 10 000 per hectare.

If, instead, the trees were planted 2 metres apart in the same pattern, approximately how many trees per hectare would there be?

A 2 500 $\qquad$ B 5 000 $\qquad$ C 10 000 $\qquad$ D 20 000 $\qquad$ E 40 000

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
If the trees are planted twice as far apart then the number of trees per hectare will be a quarter of its previous value.
