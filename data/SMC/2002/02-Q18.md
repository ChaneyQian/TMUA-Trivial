## 题目
When $n = 81$, what is the value of $\dfrac{n^{20}}{3^n}$ ?

A less than $\dfrac{1}{100}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{1}{3}$ $\qquad$ C 1 $\qquad$ D 3 $\qquad$ E more than 100

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Note that $81 = 3^4$. Therefore $\dfrac{81^{20}}{3^{81}} = \dfrac{3^{80}}{3^{81}} = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
