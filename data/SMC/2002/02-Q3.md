## 题目
Peri the winkle starts at the point $(1, 1)$. Each day Peri crawls from point $(x, y)$ to point $(y, x + y)$, so that at the end of the first day Peri has reached $(1, 2)$.

Where is Peri at the end of the sixth day?

A $(6, 7)$ $\qquad$ B $(6, 12)$ $\qquad$ C $(13, 21)$ $\qquad$ D $(21, 34)$ $\qquad$ E $(144, 233)$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The points which Peri reaches from the second day onwards are $(2, 3)$, $(3, 5)$, $(5, 8)$, $(8, 13)$ and $(13, 21)$ respectively.
