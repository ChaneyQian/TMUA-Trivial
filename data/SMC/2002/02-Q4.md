## 题目
What is the remainder when $123456789 \times 987654321$ is divided by 6 ?

A 1 $\qquad$ B 2 $\qquad$ C 3 $\qquad$ D 4 $\qquad$ E 5

## 中文题干
$123456789 \times 987654321$ 除以 6 的余数是多少？

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The sum of the digits of both numbers is 45, so both are multiples of 3. Also, both numbers are odd so their product is an odd multiple of 3. Hence there is a remainder of 3 when the product is divided by 6.
