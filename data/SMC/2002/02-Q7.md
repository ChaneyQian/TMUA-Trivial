## 题目
This crossnumber must be completed so that there is a different non-zero digit in each square. Which digit goes in the square marked $X$ ?

![[Image/02-Q7-fig1.png]]

**Clues**

*Across*: 1. Cube, 3. Sum of two squares  
*Down*: 1. Square, 2. Prime

A 1 $\qquad$ B 3 $\qquad$ C 5 $\qquad$ D 7 $\qquad$ E 9

## 答案
B

## 解析
The only two-digit cubes are 27 and 64. If 1 across is 64, then 1 down must also be 64 (the only square between 61 and 69 inclusive), but there must be a different digit in each square so this is impossible. Hence 1 across is 27 and 1 down is 25. The possibilities for 2 down are now 71, 73 and 79 giving 51, 53 and 59 as possibilities for 3 across. Of these, only $53 = 2^2 + 7^2$ can be written as the sum of two squares, so 2 down is 73 and 3 across 53.
