## 题目
A function $f$ has the property that $f(n + 3) = \dfrac{f(n) - 1}{f(n) + 1}$ for all positive integers $n$.

Given that $f(2002)$ is non-zero, what is the value of $f(2002) \times f(2008)$ ?

A 1 $\qquad$ B $-1$ $\qquad$ C 2 $\qquad$ D $-2$ $\qquad$ E more information needed

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
$$f(2008) = \dfrac{f(2005) - 1}{f(2005) + 1} = \dfrac{\dfrac{f(2002) - 1}{f(2002) + 1} - 1}{\dfrac{f(2002) - 1}{f(2002) + 1} + 1} = \dfrac{f(2002) - 1 - (f(2002) + 1)}{f(2002) - 1 + f(2002) + 1}$$

$$= \dfrac{-2}{2 \times f(2002)} = \dfrac{-1}{f(2002)}$$

Hence $f(2002) \times f(2008) = -1$ provided that $f(2002) \ne 0$.
