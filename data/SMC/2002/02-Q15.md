## 题目
For how many integer values of $n$ does the equation $x^2 + nx - 16 = 0$ have integer solutions?

A 2 $\qquad$ B 3 $\qquad$ C 4 $\qquad$ D 5 $\qquad$ E 6

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
For the equation to have integer solutions, it must be possible to write $x^2 + nx - 16$ in the form $(x - \alpha)(x - \beta)$, where $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are integers.

Therefore $x^2 + nx - 16 = x^2 - (\alpha + \beta)x + \alpha\beta$ and we require that $\alpha\beta = -16$.

The possible integer values of $\alpha$, $\beta$ are $1$, $-16$; $-1$, $16$; $2$, $-8$; $-2$, $8$; $4$, $-4$ (we do not count $-16$, $1$ as being distinct from $1$, $-16$, for instance).

As $n = -(\alpha + \beta)$, the possible values of $n$ are $15$, $-15$, $6$, $-6$ and $0$.
