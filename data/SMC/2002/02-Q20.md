## 题目
Which of the following could be the graph of $y = \sin(x^2)$ ?

## 解析
We note that the graph of $y = \sin\left(x^2\right)$ passes through the origin and also is symmetrical about the $y$-axis, since $(-x)^2 = x^2$. For $-\sqrt{\pi} \le x \le \sqrt{\pi}$, $\sin\left(x^2\right) \ge 0$ and the only one of the graphs to satisfy all of these conditions is A.

[Note that the graph of $y = \sin\left(x^2\right)$ has range $-1 \le y \le 1$ and crosses the $x$-axis when $x = \pm\sqrt{n\pi}$ for all natural numbers $n$.]
