## 题目
A furlong is 220 yards long and a yard is 36 inches. A chain is 44 cubits long and a cubit is 54 barleycorns. There are 10 chains in a furlong. How many barleycorns are there in one inch?

A 6 $\qquad$ B 5 $\qquad$ C 4 $\qquad$ D 3 $\qquad$ E 2

## 中文题干
1 弗隆长 220 码，1 码为 36 英寸。1 链长 44 腕尺，1 腕尺为 54 大麦粒。1 弗隆等于 10 链。1 英寸是多少大麦粒？

## 答案
D

## 解析
The number of inches in a furlong is $220 \times 36$. The number of barleycorns in a furlong is $10 \times 44 \times 54$. So the number of barleycorns in one inch is $\dfrac{10 \times 44 \times 54}{220 \times 36}$, i.e. 3.
