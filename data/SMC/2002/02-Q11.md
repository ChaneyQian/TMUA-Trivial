## 题目
The diagram shows two concentric circles of radii $r$ and $2r$ respectively. What is the ratio of the total shaded area to the total unshaded area?

![[Image/02-Q11-fig1.png]]

A 5:7 $\qquad$ B 7:5 $\qquad$ C 1:1 $\qquad$ D 2:3 $\qquad$ E 3:2

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
The shaded area $= \dfrac{2}{3}\pi r^2 + \dfrac{1}{3}\left(4\pi r^2 - \pi r^2\right) = \dfrac{5}{3}\pi r^2$.

The unshaded area $= 4\pi r^2 - \dfrac{5}{3}\pi r^2 = \dfrac{7}{3}\pi r^2$. Hence the required ratio is $5 : 7$.
