## 题目
The cards in a set of 36 are numbered 1 to 36. The cards are shuffled and four cards are dealt. What are the chances of them being dealt in descending order?

A 1 in 2 $\qquad$ B 1 in 8 $\qquad$ C 1 in 16 $\qquad$ D 1 in 24 $\qquad$ E 1 in 36

## 中文题干
一副 36 张的卡片分别编号为 1 到 36。将卡片洗匀后发出 4 张，它们按降序发出的概率是多少？

## 答案
D

## 解析
The number of different arrangements of the four cards which are dealt is $4 \times 3 \times 2 \times 1 = 24$. In only one of these will the four cards be in descending order.
