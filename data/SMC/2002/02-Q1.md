## 题目
How many squares of area $1\text{ cm}^2$ have a total perimeter length which is equal to the perimeter of a single square of area $4\text{ cm}^2$ ?

A 2 $\qquad$ B 4 $\qquad$ C 8 $\qquad$ D 16 $\qquad$ E 32

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
A square of area $4\text{ cm}^2$ has side $2\text{ cm}$ and therefore its perimeter is $8\text{ cm}$. A square of area $1\text{ cm}^2$ has side $1\text{ cm}$, so its perimeter is $4\text{ cm}$.
