## 题目
A sculpture consists of a row of 2 metre rods each placed with one end resting on horizontal ground and the other end resting against a vertical wall. The diagram shows how the rods $BT$, $CU$, $DV$, … look from above. The bases of the rods $B$, $C$, $D$, … lie on a straight line on the ground at $45^\circ$ to the wall. The top ends of the rods $T$, $U$, $V$… lie on part of a curve on the wall. What curve is it?

A a straight line $\qquad$ B a parabola $\qquad$ C a circle $\qquad$ D a sine curve $\qquad$ E a quartic curve

## 中文题干
一座雕塑由一排长 2 米的杆组成，每根杆的一端置于水平地面上，另一端靠在竖直墙面上。如图展示了杆 $BT$、$CU$、$DV$、……从上方俯视的样子。杆的底端 $B$、$C$、$D$、……位于地面上一条与墙面成 $45^\circ$ 的直线上。杆的顶端 $T$、$U$、$V$、……位于墙面上的某条曲线的一部分上。这条曲线是什么曲线？

## 答案
C

## 解析
Let the point on the ground vertically below $T$ be $T'$, let $O$ be the point where line $DB$ meets the wall and let $OT' = x$.

Then, since $\angle T'OB = \angle T'BO = 45^\circ$, $T'B = x$.

As $TB = 2$, $TT' = \sqrt{4 - x^2}$.

Hence, with respect to axes shown in the diagram, the equation of the curve on which $T$, $U$, $V$, … lie is

$$y = \sqrt{4 - x^2}, \text{ i.e. } x^2 + y^2 = 4,\ x \ge 0,\ y \ge 0$$

which is the equation of part of a circle of radius 2.
