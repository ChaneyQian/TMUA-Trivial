## 题目
Triangle $PQU$ has a right angle at $U$. The points $R, S$ and $T$ divide the side $QU$ into quarters. Which of the following statements about the areas of the triangles $PQR, PRS, PST, PTU$ is true?

![[Image/03-Q2-fig1.png]]

A All have the same area $\qquad$ B $\triangle PQR$ is biggest $\qquad$ C $\triangle PRS$ is biggest $\qquad$ D $\triangle PST$ is biggest $\qquad$ E $\triangle PTU$ is biggest

## 中文题干
三角形 $PQU$ 在 $U$ 处为直角。点 $R, S$ 和 $T$ 将边 $QU$ 四等分。下列关于三角形 $PQR, PRS, PST, PTU$ 面积的说法中，哪一个是正确的？

## 答案
A

## 解析
The area of a triangle $= \dfrac{1}{2}$ base $\times$ perpendicular height. The four triangles have bases of equal length, since $QR = RS = ST = TU$, and the same perpendicular height, $PU$.
