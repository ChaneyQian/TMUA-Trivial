## 题目
The outer equilateral triangle has area 1. The points $A, B, C$ are a quarter of the way along the sides as shown. What is the area of the equilateral triangle $ABC$?

![[Image/03-Q21-fig1.png]]

A $\frac{3}{8}$ $\qquad$ B $\frac{7}{16}$ $\qquad$ C $\frac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ D $\frac{9}{16}$ $\qquad$ E $\frac{5}{8}$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Triangles $BCQ$, $CAP$ and $ABR$ are congruent since each has sides of $x$ and $3x$ and an included angle of $60^\circ$ (SAS). Consider triangle $BCQ$: its base, $BQ$, and height are $\dfrac{3}{4}$ and $\dfrac{1}{4}$ respectively of the base, $QR$, and height of triangle $PQR$. So area of triangle $BCQ = \dfrac{3}{16} \times$ area of triangle $PQR$ and the area of triangle $ABC = \left(1 - 3 \times \dfrac{3}{16}\right) \times$ area of triangle $PQR = \dfrac{7}{16} \times 1 = \dfrac{7}{16}$.
