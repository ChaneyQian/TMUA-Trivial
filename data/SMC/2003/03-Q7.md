## 题目
Climbers use ropes of different diameters. A 50m rope which is 9mm in diameter weighs about 2.7kg. Roughly what would a 50m rope of the same material, but of diameter 11mm, weigh?

A 2.7kg $\qquad$ B 3.3kg $\qquad$ C 4kg $\qquad$ D 4.9kg $\qquad$ E 6kg

## 中文题干
登山者使用不同直径的绳索。一根直径为 9mm 的 50m 绳索重约 2.7kg。一根相同材质但直径为 11mm 的 50m 绳索大约重多少？

## 答案
C

## 解析
For ropes of the same material and same length, the weight is directly proportional to the cross-sectional area and hence to the square of the diameter of the rope. So the weight, in kg, of the second rope

$$\approx 2.7 \times \dfrac{11^2}{9^2} = 2.7 \times \dfrac{121}{81} = \dfrac{121}{30} \approx 4.$$
