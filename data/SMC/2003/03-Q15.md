## 题目
The number of this year, 2003, is prime. How many square numbers are factors of $2003^{2003}$?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C 44 $\qquad$ D 1002 $\qquad$ E 2003

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The only square numbers which are factors of $2003^{2003}$ have the form $2003^{2n}$ for a non-negative integer $n$. But $2n \le 2003$ so $n = 0,\ 1,\ \ldots,\ 1001$.
