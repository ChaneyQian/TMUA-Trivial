## 题目
One of the oldest sporting events in the world is the Kiplingcotes Derby, a horse race which has been held in the East Yorkshire Wolds almost every year since 1519. Each rider pays a fee of £4.25 to enter the race. The first prize in the race is the sum of £50, but the second prize is the total of the entry fees minus an administration cost of 25p per rider. In 2000, 18 riders competed in the Kiplingcotes Derby. How much greater than the first prize was the second prize?

A £22 $\qquad$ B £26.50 $\qquad$ C £46 $\qquad$ D £72 $\qquad$ E £76.50

## 中文题干
世界上最古老的体育赛事之一是 Kiplingcotes Derby，这是一项自 1519 年以来几乎每年都在 East Yorkshire Wolds 举行的赛马比赛。每位骑手参赛需支付 £4.25 的费用。比赛的头等奖金额为 £50，但二等奖是参赛费总额减去每位骑手 25p 的管理费。在 2000 年，有 18 位骑手参加了 Kiplingcotes Derby。二等奖比头等奖多多少？

## 答案

## 解析
Second prize was $18 \times £4.25 - 18 \times 25\text{p} = 18 \times £4 = £72$. So in 2000, second prize was £22 more than first prize!
