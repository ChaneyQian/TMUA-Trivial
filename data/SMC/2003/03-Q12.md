## 题目
From the information given in the diagram shown alongside, what is the value of $a + b$?

![[Image/03-Q12-fig1.png]]

A 3 $\qquad$ B 4 $\qquad$ C 5 $\qquad$ D 6 $\qquad$ E 7

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Using similar triangles: $\dfrac{b}{3} = \dfrac{a}{7} = \dfrac{5}{10}$ so $a = 3\frac{1}{2}$ and $b = 1\frac{1}{2}$. Hence $a + b = 5$.

![[Image/03-Q12-sol1.png]]

Alternatively, if a copy of the large triangle is rotated through $180^\circ$ about the midpoint of its hypotenuse, then the two triangles form a rectangle and it is clear that $a + b = 5$.
