## 题目
The difference between two numbers is one quarter of their sum. What is the ratio of the smaller number to the larger number?

A 3 : 8 $\qquad$ B 1 : 2 $\qquad$ C 5 : 7 $\qquad$ D 1 : 4 $\qquad$ E 3 : 5

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Let the smaller number be $x$ and the larger number $y$.
Then $y - x = \dfrac{1}{4}(y + x)$; $4y - 4x = y + x$; $3y = 5x$; hence $x : y = 3 : 5$.
