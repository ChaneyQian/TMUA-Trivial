## 题目
$AOB$ is an isosceles right-angled triangle drawn in a quadrant of a circle of radius 1 unit. The largest possible circle is drawn in the minor segment cut off by the line $AB$. This circle has radius $r$. The radius of the inscribed circle of the triangle $AOB$ is $R$. What is the value of $\frac{R}{r}$?

![[Image/03-Q24-fig1.png]]

A 2 $\qquad$ B $2\sqrt{2}-1$ $\qquad$ C $\sqrt{2}+1$ $\qquad$ D $\frac{9}{5}$ $\qquad$ E $\sqrt{3}$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Tangents to a circle from an exterior point are equal in length so $BT = BY = \dfrac{1}{2}BA = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}$.

Radius $R = OT = OB - BT = 1 - \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}$.

Triangle $OBY$ is isosceles with $OY = BY = \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}$.

Radius $OZ = OY + YZ = 1$ so $\dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2} + 2r = 1$ and hence $r = \dfrac{1}{2}\left(1 - \dfrac{1}{2}\sqrt{2}\right) = \dfrac{1}{2}R$.
