## 题目
Mary received a 10% pay rise, and Margaret received a 5% pay rise. This gave them both salaries of £23 100 per year. How much more, per year, did Margaret earn than Mary before they received these pay rises?

A £1155 $\qquad$ B £1000 $\qquad$ C £850 $\qquad$ D £760 $\qquad$ E £550

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Mary's and Margaret's salaries before the pay rises were $£23\,100 \times \dfrac{100}{110}$ and $£23\,100 \times \dfrac{100}{105}$ respectively, i.e. £21 000 and £22 000 respectively. So the difference was £1000.
