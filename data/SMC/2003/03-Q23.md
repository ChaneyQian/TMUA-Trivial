## 题目
The cube, $XYZTABCD$, is cut into four pieces by cutting along two planes, $BCTX$ and $BDTY$. What fraction of the volume of the cube is occupied by the piece containing corner $A$?

![[Image/03-Q23-fig1.png]]

A $\frac{3}{8}$ $\qquad$ B $\frac{1}{3}$ $\qquad$ C $\frac{3}{10}$ $\qquad$ D $\frac{5}{18}$ $\qquad$ E $\frac{1}{4}$

## 中文题干
正方体 $XYZTABCD$ 沿两个平面 $BCTX$ 和 $BDTY$ 被切成四块。包含顶点 $A$ 的那一块占正方体体积的几分之几？

## 答案
B

## 解析
The piece containing corner $A$ is a pyramid. Its base is the square $ADTX$ and its vertex is $B$. Let the length of the side of the cube be 1 unit: then the volume of the pyramid $= \dfrac{1}{3}$ base area $\times$ height $= \dfrac{1}{3} \times 1 \times 1 = \dfrac{1}{3}$.
So the volume of the piece containing corner $A$ is one third of the volume of the cube.
