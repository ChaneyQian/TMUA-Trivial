## 题目
All six vertices of hexagon $UVWXYZ$ lie on the circumference of a circle; $\angle ZUV = 88^\circ$ and $\angle XYZ = 158^\circ$. What is the size of $\angle VWX$?

A $92^\circ$ $\qquad$ B $114^\circ$ $\qquad$ C $120^\circ$ $\qquad$ D $132^\circ$ $\qquad$ E it is impossible to determine

## 答案
B

## 解析
The opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral add up to $180^\circ$ so $\angle XWZ = 180^\circ - 158^\circ = 22^\circ$; $\angle VWZ = 180^\circ - 88^\circ = 92^\circ$; hence $\angle VWX = 92^\circ + 22^\circ = 114^\circ$.
