## 题目
On 2 July 2002, Steve Fossett completed the first solo balloon circumnavigation of the world after $13\frac{1}{2}$ days. Assuming the balloon travelled along a circle of diameter 12 750 km, roughly what was the average speed of the balloon in km/h?

A 12 $\qquad$ B 40 $\qquad$ C 75 $\qquad$ D 120 $\qquad$ E 300

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The average speed of the balloon, in km/h,

$$\approx \dfrac{\pi \times 12750}{13\frac{1}{2} \times 24} \approx \dfrac{\pi \times 1000}{24} \approx \pi \times 40 \approx 120.$$
