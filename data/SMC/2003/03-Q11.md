## 题目
In a fit of madness, the bee Zerk left the hive and flew 1m due North, then 1m due East, then 1m vertically up. She then made a beeline for the hive, flying directly home in a straight line. How far, in m, did she fly altogether?

A 4 $\qquad$ B $3 + \sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ C $3 + \sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ D $5\frac{1}{4}$ $\qquad$ E 6

## 中文题干
蜜蜂 Zerk 一时发狂离开蜂巢，向正北飞了 1m，又向正东飞了 1m，然后垂直向上飞了 1m。随后她径直朝蜂巢沿直线直接飞回家。她总共飞了多远（单位为 m）？

## 答案
C

## 解析
When Zerk made a beeline back to the hive, the distance she travelled is equal to the length of a space diagonal of a cube of side $1\text{m}$, i.e. $\sqrt{1^2 + 1^2 + 1^2}\text{ m} = \sqrt{3}\text{ m}$. So the total distance she flew $= (3 + \sqrt{3})\text{m}$.
