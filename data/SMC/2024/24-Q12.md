## 题目
$p$, $q$, $r$ and $s$ are two-digit primes which between them use all the non-zero digits except 5.

What is the value of $p + q + r + s$?

A 220 $\qquad$ B 210 $\qquad$ C 200 $\qquad$ D 190 $\qquad$ E more information needed

## 答案
A

## 解析
Of the digits $1$, $2$, $3$, $4$, $6$, $7$, $8$ and $9$, only $1$, $3$, $7$ and $9$ can be the units digit of a two-digit prime. This leaves $2$, $4$, $6$ and $8$ as the tens digits of the primes.

Therefore the sum of the units digits of the four primes is $1 + 3 + 7 + 9$, which is equal to $20$, and the sum of the tens digits is $2 + 4 + 6 + 8$, which is also $20$.

Hence the sum of the four primes is $20 \times 10 + 20 \times 1 = 200 + 20 = 220$.

> This answer has been obtained without finding the primes $p$, $q$, $r$ and $s$, or indeed even showing that four primes which use all the non-zero digits except $5$, exist. You are asked to check in Problem 12.1 that a solution does exist.
