## 题目
The diagram shows two concentric circles divided by radial lines into 14 pieces of equal area. The radius of the smaller circle is 1.

What is the length, $x$, of an outer radial line?

![[Image/24-Q18-fig1.png]]

A $\sqrt{14} - 1$ $\qquad$ B $\sqrt{14} - 2$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{\sqrt{14}}{2} - 1$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{\sqrt{14}}{2} - 2$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{\sqrt{14} - 1}{2}$

## 答案
C

## 解析
The outer circle has radius $1 + x$. Hence its area is $\pi(1 + x)^2$.

The inner circle has radius $1$, and hence has area $\pi(1^2) = \pi$. Hence each of the four inner pieces has area $\dfrac{\pi}{4}$.

Because all the $14$ pieces have the same area, the area of the outer circle is $14$ times the area of one of the inner pieces. Therefore

$$\pi(1 + x)^2 = 14\left(\dfrac{\pi}{4}\right).$$

This simplifies to give

$$(1 + x)^2 = \dfrac{14}{4}.$$

Hence, as $1 + x > 0$,

$$1 + x = \sqrt{\dfrac{14}{4}} = \dfrac{\sqrt{14}}{2}.$$

We conclude that

$$x = \dfrac{\sqrt{14}}{2} - 1.$$
