## 题目
What is two-fifths of the recurring decimal $0.\dot{2}\dot{5}$?

A $0.\dot{1}$ $\qquad$ B $0.0\dot{1}$ $\qquad$ C $0.\dot{0}\dot{1}$ $\qquad$ D $0.10$ $\qquad$ E $0.\dot{1}\dot{0}$

## 答案
E

## 解析
**Method 1**

One-fifth of $0.\dot{2}\dot{5}$ is $0.0\dot{5}$. Hence two-fifths of $0.\dot{2}\dot{5}$ is $0.\dot{1}\dot{0}$.

**Method 2**

Since $0.\dot{2}\dot{5} = \dfrac{25}{99}$, it follows that that two-fifths of $0.\dot{2}\dot{5} = \dfrac{2}{5} \times \dfrac{25}{99} = \dfrac{10}{99} = 0.\dot{1}\dot{0}$.
