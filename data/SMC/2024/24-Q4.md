## 题目
The sizes of the three angles in a triangle, in degrees, are $x$, $7x$ and $x^2$.

What is the size of the largest angle?

A $10^\circ$ $\qquad$ B $18^\circ$ $\qquad$ C $100^\circ$ $\qquad$ D $120^\circ$ $\qquad$ E $121^\circ$

C

## 解析
The sum of the angles in a triangle is $180^\circ$. Therefore $x + 7x + x^2 = 180$. Hence $x^2 + 8x - 180 = 0$.

By factorizing the left-hand side of this equation, we deduce that $(x + 18)(x - 10) = 0$.

This implies that either $x = 10$ or $x = -18$. Because $x^\circ$ is one of the angles of the triangle, $x > 0$. We conclude that $x = 10$.

It follows that the angles of the triangle are $10^\circ$, $70^\circ$ and $100^\circ$. Hence the largest angle is $100^\circ$.
