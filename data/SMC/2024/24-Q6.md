## 题目
One face of a solid polyhedron is an octagon.

What is the smallest possible number of edges the solid could have?

A 9 $\qquad$ B 10 $\qquad$ C 12 $\qquad$ D 16 $\qquad$ E 24

## 答案
D

## 解析
The octagonal face of the polyhedron has $8$ edges.

Each vertex of this face must be joined by an edge to a vertex not in this face. So there must be at least $8$ more edges.

Hence the polyhedron has at least $8 + 8 = 16$ edges.

If all the vertices of the octagonal face are joined by an edge to the same vertex, we obtain a pyramid with an octagonal base that has $16$ edges.

Therefore the smallest number of edges that the solid could have is $16$.
