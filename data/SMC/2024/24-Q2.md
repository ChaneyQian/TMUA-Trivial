## 题目
A *twip* is a very short unit of length, derived from imperial units, and is equal to approximately $0.000018\text{ metres}$. A *league* is a long unit of length which is equal to approximately $4800\text{ metres}$.

Roughly how many twips are there in a league?

A $270\,000\,000$ $\qquad$ B $27\,000\,000$ $\qquad$ C $2\,700\,000$ $\qquad$ D $270\,000$ $\qquad$ E $27\,000$

## 中文题干
*缇*（twip）是一个很短的长度单位，源自英制单位，约等于 $0.000018$ 米。*里格*（league）是一个长的长度单位，约等于 $4800$ 米。一里格中大约有多少缇？

## 答案
A

## 解析
The number of twips in a league is $\dfrac{4800}{0.000018}$. Now

$$\dfrac{4800}{0.000018} = \dfrac{4\,800\,000\,000}{18} = \dfrac{800\,000\,000}{3} \approx \dfrac{810\,000\,000}{3} = 270\,000\,000.$$
