## 题目
The diagram shows two squares, $JKLM$ and $NKPO$.
The length of $NL$ is $10\text{ cm}$. The shaded region has area $62\text{ cm}^2$.

What is the length of $KN$ in cm?

## 解析
Suppose that the side length of the square $JKLM$ is $x\text{ cm}$ and the side length of the square $NKPO$ is $y\text{ cm}$.

The area of the shaded region is the area of $JKLM$ minus the area of $NKPO$. Therefore

$$x^2 - y^2 = 62. \qquad (1)$$

Also, by Pythagoras' Theorem $KL^2 + NK^2 = NL^2$. That is,

$$x^2 + y^2 = 10^2. \qquad (2)$$

By subtracting equation (1) from equation (2), we obtain

$$2y^2 = 10^2 - 62 = 100 - 62 = 38.$$

Therefore $y^2 = 19$. Hence $y = \sqrt{19}$.
