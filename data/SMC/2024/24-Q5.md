## 题目
When $4^5 \times 5^4$ is correctly calculated, how many digits are there in the answer?

A 4 $\qquad$ B 6 $\qquad$ C 10 $\qquad$ D 16 $\qquad$ E 20

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
We have

$$4^5 \times 5^4 = (2^2)^5 \times 5^4 = 2^{10} \times 5^4 = 2^6 \times (2^4 \times 5^4) = 2^6 \times 10^4 = 64 \times 10\,000 = 640\,000.$$

Therefore there are $6$ digits in the correct answer.
