## 题目
Which is the largest prime factor of $3^8 - 1$?

A 41 $\qquad$ B 37 $\qquad$ C 31 $\qquad$ D 29 $\qquad$ E 23

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Using the difference of two squares factorization $x^2 - y^2 = (x - y)(x + y)$, we have

$$\begin{aligned}
3^8 - 1 &= (3^4 - 1)(3^4 + 1) \\
&= (3^2 - 1)(3^2 + 1)(3^4 + 1) \\
&= (3 - 1)(3 + 1)(9 + 1)(81 + 1) \\
&= 2 \times 4 \times 10 \times 82 \\
&= 2 \times 2^2 \times (2 \times 5) \times (2 \times 41) \\
&= 2^5 \times 5 \times 41.
\end{aligned}$$

Because $41$ is a prime, we deduce that the largest prime factor of $3^8 - 1$ is $41$.
