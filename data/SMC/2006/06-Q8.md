## 题目
Matt black paint absorbs 97% of light, the remainder being reflected. Scientists have developed a new superblack coating, “10 times blacker” than matt black paint, meaning that it reflects $\frac{1}{10}$ of the light reflected by matt black paint. What percentage of light does the new coating absorb?

A 9.7 $\qquad$ B 90.3 $\qquad$ C 99.7 $\qquad$ D 99.9 $\qquad$ E 970

## 答案
C

## 解析
Matt black paint reflects 3% of light, so the superblack paint reflects only 0.3% of light. Hence it absorbs 99.7% of light.
