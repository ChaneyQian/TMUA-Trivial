## 题目
Which symbol should replace $\oplus$ to make the following equation true?

$$1 \times 2 \times (3 \oplus 4 + 5) \times (6 \times 7 + 8 + 9) = 2006.$$

A $+$ $\qquad$ B $-$ $\qquad$ C $\div$ $\qquad$ D $\times$ $\qquad$ E none of these

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
$1 \times 2 \times (3 \oplus 4 + 5) \times (6 \times 7 + 8 + 9) = 2006$, that is $2 \times (3 \oplus 4 + 5) \times (42 + 8 + 9) = 2006$, that is $(3 \oplus 4 + 5) \times 59 = 1003$, that is $3 \oplus 4 + 5 = 17$, that is $3 \oplus 4 = 12$. So $\oplus$ should be replaced by $\times$.
