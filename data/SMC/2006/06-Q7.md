## 题目
The base of a pyramid has $n$ edges. What is the difference between the number of edges the pyramid has and the number of faces the pyramid has?

A $n - 2$ $\qquad$ B $n - 1$ $\qquad$ C $n$ $\qquad$ D $n + 1$ $\qquad$ E $n + 2$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
The pyramid has $2n$ edges and $n + 1$ faces, so the required difference is $2n - (n + 1)$, that is $n - 1$.
