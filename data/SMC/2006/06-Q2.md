## 题目
If $6x - y = 21$ and $6y - x = 14$, what is the value of $x - y$?

A 1 $\qquad$ B 2 $\qquad$ C 3 $\qquad$ D 4 $\qquad$ E 5

## 中文题干
如果 $6x - y = 21$ 且 $6y - x = 14$，那么 $x - y$ 的值是多少？

## 答案
A

## 解析
Subtracting the second equation from the first: $6x - y - (6y - x) = 21 - 14$. So $7x - 7y = 7$, that is $x - y = 1$.

**Note** *The equations may be solved to give $x = 4$, $y = 3$, but it is not necessary to do this in order to find the value of $x - y$.*
