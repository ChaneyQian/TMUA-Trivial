## 题目
Which positive integer $n$ satisfies the equation

$$\dfrac{3}{n^3} + \dfrac{4}{n^3} + \dfrac{5}{n^3} + \dots + \dfrac{n^3 - 5}{n^3} + \dfrac{n^3 - 4}{n^3} + \dfrac{n^3 - 3}{n^3} = 60\ ?$$

A 5 $\qquad$ B 11 $\qquad$ C 31 $\qquad$ D 60 $\qquad$ E 2006

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
The terms on the left-hand side of the equation form an arithmetic progression which has $n^3 - 5$ terms. So the sum of these terms is
$$\frac{n^3 - 5}{2}\left(\frac{3}{n^3} + \frac{n^3 - 3}{n^3}\right) = \frac{n^3 - 5}{2}.$$
Hence $n^3 - 5 = 120$, so $n = 5$.
