## 题目
A solid red plastic cube, volume $1\text{ cm}^3$, is painted white on its outside. The cube is cut by a plane passing through the mid-points of various edges, as shown.

What, in $\text{cm}^2$, is the *total* red area exposed by the cut?

![[Image/06-Q24-fig1.png]]

A $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}$ $\qquad$ B 2 $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{9\sqrt{2}}{5}$ $\qquad$ D 3 $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{3(\sqrt{3} + \sqrt{2})}{4}$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Let $O$ be the centre of the cube. Consider triangle $ABO$: from Pythagoras' Theorem, $OA = AB = BO = \sqrt{\left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^2}\text{ cm} = \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\text{ cm}$. So triangle $OAB$ is equilateral.

A similar argument may be applied to triangles $OBC$, $OCD$ etc. The area of each of these equilateral triangles is $\dfrac{1}{2} \times \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \times \dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \times \sin 60^\circ\text{ cm}^2$, that is $\dfrac{1}{8}\sqrt{3}\text{ cm}^2$. So the area of hexagon $ABCDEF$ is $6 \times \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{8}\text{ cm}^2$. However, the total red area exposed by the cut is twice the area of this hexagon, that is $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}}{2}\text{ cm}^2$.
