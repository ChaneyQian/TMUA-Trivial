## 题目
In the diagram, the circle and the two semicircles have radius 1. What is the perimeter of the square?

![[Image/06-Q23-fig1.png]]

A $6 + 4\sqrt{2}$  
B $2 + 4\sqrt{2} + 2\sqrt{3}$  
C $3\sqrt{2} + 4\sqrt{3}$  
D $4 + 2\sqrt{2} + 2\sqrt{6}$  
E $12$

D

## 解析
Let the vertices of the square be $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$ and the centres of the circle and the two semicircles be $P$, $Q$, $R$, as shown. The midpoint of $QR$ is $S$. By symmetry, $P$ and $S$ both lie on diagonal $BD$ of square $ABCD$ and the whole figure is symmetrical about $BD$.

As $P$ is distance 1 from both $AD$ and $DC$, the length of $DP$ is $\sqrt{2}$.

As the circles and semicircles are mutually tangent, $PQR$ is an equilateral triangle of side 2, so the length of $PS$ is $\sqrt{3}$. As angles $QBS$ and $BSQ$ are $45^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ respectively, triangle $SBQ$ is isosceles, so $SB = SQ = 1$. Hence the length of $BD$ is $\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} + 1$. Now the length of the side of the square is $BD \div \sqrt{2}$ so the perimeter of the square is $4 \times (BD \div \sqrt{2})$, that is $2\sqrt{2} \times BD$.

So the perimeter is $2\sqrt{2}\left(\sqrt{2} + \sqrt{3} + 1\right)$, that is $4 + 2\sqrt{6} + 2\sqrt{2}$.
