## 题目
Given that January 1st, 2006 fell on a Sunday, which day of the week will occur most frequently in 2007?

A Monday $\qquad$ B Tuesday $\qquad$ C Wednesday $\qquad$ D Thursday $\qquad$ E Friday

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
As 2006 is not a leap year, January 1st, 2007 will fall one day later in the week than January 1st, 2006, that is on a Monday. So there will be 53 Mondays in 2007 and 52 of each of the other days of the week.
