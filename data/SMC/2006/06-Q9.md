## 题目
The 80 spokes of the giant wheel *The London Eye* are made from 4 miles of cable. Roughly what is the circumference of the wheel in metres?

A 50 $\qquad$ B 100 $\qquad$ C 500 $\qquad$ D 750 $\qquad$ E 900

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Each spoke of the London Eye is about $\dfrac{1}{20}$ mile long. As 1 mile is approximately $1600\text{ m}$, this means that the radius of the giant wheel is roughly $80\text{ m}$. So the circumference is approximately $500\text{ m}$.
