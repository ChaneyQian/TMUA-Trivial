## 题目
The number 2005 is the sum of a sequence of five consecutive positive integers. Which of the following numbers occurs in this sequence?

A 395 $\qquad$ B 400 $\qquad$ C 405 $\qquad$ D 410 $\qquad$ E 415

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Let the five consecutive positive integers be $x - 2$, $x - 1$, $x$, $x + 1$, $x + 2$. Their sum is $5x$, so $5x = 2005$, that is $x = 401$. The five numbers are 399, 400, 401, 402, 403.
