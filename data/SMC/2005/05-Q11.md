## 题目
A sculpture is made up of 12 wooden cylinders, each of height $2\text{ cm}$. They are glued together as shown. The diameter of the top cylinder is $2\text{ cm}$ and each of the other cylinders has a diameter $2\text{ cm}$ more than the one immediately above it. The exhibit stands with its base on a marble table. What, in $\text{cm}^2$, is the total surface area of the sculpture, excluding the base?

![[Image/05-Q11-fig1.png]]

A $456\pi$ $\qquad$ B $356\pi$ $\qquad$ C $256\pi$ $\qquad$ D $156\pi$ $\qquad$ E $144\pi$

## 答案
A

## 解析
The diameter of the largest cylinder is $24\text{ cm}$, so the sum of the areas of the horizontal parts of the sculpture, excluding its base, is that of a circle of diameter $24\text{ cm}$, that is $144\pi\text{ cm}^2$. The sum of the areas of the vertical parts of the sculpture is $(2\pi \times 1 \times 2 + 2\pi \times 2 \times 2 + 2\pi \times 3 \times 2 + \ldots + 2\pi \times 12 \times 2)\text{ cm}^2$, that is $312\pi\text{ cm}^2$. So, excluding the base, the total surface area of the sculpture is $456\pi\text{ cm}^2$.
