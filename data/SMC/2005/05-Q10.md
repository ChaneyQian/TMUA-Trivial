## 题目
Sam and Pat were counting their money. They discovered that if Sam gave Pat £5, then Pat would have 5 times as much as Sam, but if Pat gave Sam £5, then Sam would have 5 times as much as Pat. How much did they have altogether?

A £10 $\qquad$ B £15 $\qquad$ C £20 $\qquad$ D £25 $\qquad$ E £30

## 中文题干
Sam 和 Pat 在数他们的钱。他们发现，如果 Sam 给 Pat £5，那么 Pat 拥有的钱将是 Sam 的 5 倍；但如果 Pat 给 Sam £5，那么 Sam 拥有的钱将是 Pat 的 5 倍。他们两人一共有多少钱？

## 答案
B

## 解析
Let Sam and Pat have £$x$ and £$y$ respectively.

Then $y + 5 = 5(x - 5)$, that is $y = 5x - 30$. Also, $x + 5 = 5(y - 5)$, that is $x = 5y - 30$. Solving these simultaneous equations gives $x = 7.5$ and $y = 7.5$ so the friends have £15 altogether.

**Note**: from the information given, we may deduce that Sam and Pat have the same amount of money and this leads to a shorter method: $x + 5 = 5(x - 5)$, that is $x = 5x - 30$, that is $x = 7.5$.
