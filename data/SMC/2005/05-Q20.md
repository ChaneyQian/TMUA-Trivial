## 题目
It takes two weeks to clean the 3312 panes of glass in the $6000\text{ m}^2$ glass roof of the British Museum, a task performed once every two years. Assuming that all the panes are equilateral triangles of the same size, roughly how long is the side of each pane?

A $50\text{ cm}$ $\qquad$ B $1\text{ m}$ $\qquad$ C $2\text{ m}$ $\qquad$ D $3\text{ m}$ $\qquad$ E $4\text{ m}$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Let the length in metres of the side of a pane be $x$. Then the area of one pane $= \dfrac{1}{2} \times x \times x \times \sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{4}x^2$. So

$$\frac{\sqrt{3}}{4}x^2 \approx \frac{6000}{3300}, \text{ that is } x^2 \approx \frac{4 \times 6000}{\sqrt{3} \times 3300}.$$

We conclude that $x^2 \approx \dfrac{7}{\sqrt{3}} \approx 4$.
