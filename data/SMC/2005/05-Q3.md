## 题目
What is the mean of the five numbers $1^5$, $2^4$, $3^3$, $4^2$ and $5^1$?

A 6.2 $\qquad$ B 11.4 $\qquad$ C 12.2 $\qquad$ D 13 $\qquad$ E 13.8

## 解析
The numbers are 1, 16, 27, 16 and 5 respectively. Their sum is 65, so their mean is 13.
