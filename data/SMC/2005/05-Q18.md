## 题目
A cube exactly fits inside a sphere and another sphere exactly fits inside this cube. What is the ratio of the volume of the smaller sphere to the volume of the larger sphere?

A $1 : 3\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ B $1 : 4$ $\qquad$ C $1 : 3$ $\qquad$ D $2 : 3$ $\qquad$ E $1 : 2$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Let the radii of the two spheres be $r_1$ and $r_2$, as shown.

Applying Pythagoras' Theorem: $r_2^2 = r_1^2 + r_1^2 + r_1^2$, so $r_2 = \sqrt{3}r_1$.

The ratio of the volumes of the spheres $= r_1^3 : r_2^3 = 1 : (\sqrt{3})^3$, that is $1 : 3\sqrt{3}$.
