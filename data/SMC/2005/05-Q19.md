## 答案
A

## 解析
When $x \ge 0$, $|x| = x$, so $x|x| = x^2$; when $x < 0$, $|x| = -x$, so $x|x| = -x^2$.

Only graph A has the same shape as the graph of $y = x^2$ for $x \ge 0$ and the same shape as the graph of $y = -x^2$ for $x < 0$.
