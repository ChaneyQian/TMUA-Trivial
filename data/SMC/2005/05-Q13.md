## 题目
In the figure shown, what is the sum of the interior angles at $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$?

D

## 解析
Let $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$, $e$, $x$ and $y$ represent the sizes in degrees of certain angles in the figure, as shown and let the points of intersection of $AD$ with $EB$ and $EC$ be $X$ and $Y$ respectively. Angle $EXY$ is an exterior angle of triangle $XBD$ so $x = b + d$. Similarly, angle $EYX$ is an exterior angle of triangle $YAC$ so $y = a + c$. In triangle $EXY$, $e + x + y = 180$, so $a + b + c + d + e = 180$.
