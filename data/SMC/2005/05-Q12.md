## 题目
The positive integer $x$ is a multiple of 7, and $\sqrt{x}$ is between 15 and 16. What is the number of possible values of $x$?

A 1 $\qquad$ B 2 $\qquad$ C 3 $\qquad$ D 4 $\qquad$ E 5

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
As $\sqrt{x}$ lies between 15 and 16, $x$ lies between 225 and 256. The multiples of 7 in this interval are 231, 238, 245 and 252.
