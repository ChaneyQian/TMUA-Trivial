## 题目
What is the value of the expression: $\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{2}\right)\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{3}\right)\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{4}\right) \dots \left(1 + \dfrac{1}{2004}\right)\left(1 + \dfrac{1}{2005}\right)$?

A 1001 $\qquad$ B 1002 $\qquad$ C 1003 $\qquad$ D 1004 $\qquad$ E 1005

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The product is $\dfrac{3}{2} \times \dfrac{4}{3} \times \dfrac{5}{4} \times \dfrac{6}{5} \times \ldots \times \dfrac{2005}{2004} \times \dfrac{2006}{2005} = \dfrac{2006}{2} = 1003$.
