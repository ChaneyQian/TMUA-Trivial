## 题目
An examination paper is made by taking 5 large sheets of paper, folding the pile in half and stapling it. The pages are then numbered in order from 1 to 20. What is the sum of the three page numbers that are on the same sheet of paper as page number 5?

A 13 $\qquad$ B 21 $\qquad$ C 33 $\qquad$ D 37 $\qquad$ E 41

## 中文题干
一份试卷是将 5 张大纸叠在一起对折并装订制成的。然后各页按顺序从 1 到 20 编上页码。与第 5 页在同一张大纸上的另外三个页码之和是多少？

## 答案
D

## 解析
The first large sheet of paper will hold pages 1, 2, 19 and 20; the second will hold pages 3, 4, 17 and 18; the third will hold pages 5, 6, 15 and 16.
