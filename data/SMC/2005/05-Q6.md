## 题目
How many differently shaped triangles exist in which no two sides are the same length, each side is of integral unit length and the perimeter of the triangle is less than 13 units?

A 2 $\qquad$ B 3 $\qquad$ C 4 $\qquad$ D 5 $\qquad$ E 6

## 中文题干
有多少个形状不同的三角形，满足没有两条边长度相等、每条边的长度均为整数单位，且周长小于 13 个单位？

## 答案
B

## 解析
The longest side of any triangle is shorter than the sum of the lengths of the other two sides. This condition means that the only possible triangles having different sides of integral unit length, and having perimeters less than 13 units, have sides of length 2, 3, 4 or 2, 4, 5 or 3, 4, 5.
