## 题目
The diagram shows an equilateral triangle, a square and a regular pentagon which all share a common vertex. What is the value of $\theta$?

## 答案
B

## 解析
The interior angles of an equilateral triangle, a square and a regular pentagon are $60^\circ$, $90^\circ$ and $108^\circ$, respectively. Hence $\theta = 360 - (60 + 90 + 108) = 360 - 258 = 102$.
