## 题目
Tom and Geri have a competition. Initially, each player has one attempt at hitting a target. If one player hits the target and the other does not then the successful player wins. If both players hit the target, or if both players miss the target, then each has another attempt, with the same rules applying. If the probability of Tom hitting the target is always $\frac{4}{5}$ and the probability of Geri hitting the target is always $\frac{2}{3}$, what is the probability that Tom wins the competition?

A $\dfrac{4}{15}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{8}{15}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{2}{3}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{4}{5}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{13}{15}$

## 中文题干
Tom 和 Geri 进行一场比赛。最初，每位选手各有一次射击目标的机会。如果一名选手击中目标而另一名没有击中，则击中的选手获胜。如果两名选手都击中目标，或者都未击中目标，则各自再射击一次，并适用相同的规则。如果 Tom 击中目标的概率始终为 $\frac{4}{5}$，而 Geri 击中目标的概率始终为 $\frac{2}{3}$，那么 Tom 赢得比赛的概率是多少？

## 答案
C

## 解析
Let the probability that Tom wins the competition be $p$. The probability that initially Tom hits and Geri misses is $\tfrac{4}{5} \times \tfrac{1}{3} = \tfrac{4}{15}$. The probability that initially they both hit is $\tfrac{4}{5} \times \tfrac{2}{3} = \tfrac{8}{15}$ and that they both miss is $\tfrac{1}{5} \times \tfrac{1}{3} = \tfrac{1}{15}$. So the probability that either they both hit or both miss is $\tfrac{8}{15} + \tfrac{1}{15} = \tfrac{9}{15} = \tfrac{3}{5}$. If they both hit or both miss the competition is in the same position as it was initially. So Tom's probability of winning is then $p$. Therefore, $p = \tfrac{4}{15} + \tfrac{3}{5}p$. So $\tfrac{2}{5}p = \tfrac{4}{15}$ and hence $p = \tfrac{2}{3}$.
