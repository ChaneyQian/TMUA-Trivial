## 题目
Given that $x + y + z = 1$, $x + y - z = 2$ and $x - y - z = 3$, what is the value of $xyz$?

A $-2$ $\qquad$ B $-\dfrac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ C $0$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ E $2$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Adding the equations, $x + y + z = 1$ and $x - y - z = 3$, gives $2x = 4$, from which it follows that $x = 2$. Adding the equations $x + y + z = 1$ and $x + y - z = 2$, gives $2(x + y) = 3$. Hence $x + y = \dfrac{3}{2}$. Therefore, as $x = 2$, it follows that $y = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. Since $x + y + z = 1$, it now follows that $z = 1 - (x + y) = 1 - \dfrac{3}{2} = -\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore, $xyz = 2 \times -\dfrac{1}{2} \times -\dfrac{1}{2} = \dfrac{1}{2}$.
