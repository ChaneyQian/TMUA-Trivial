## 题目
Professor Rosseforp runs to work every day. On Thursday he ran 10% faster than his usual average speed. As a result, his journey time was reduced by $x$ minutes. How many minutes did the journey take on Wednesday?

A $11x$ $\qquad$ B $10x$ $\qquad$ C $9x$ $\qquad$ D $8x$ $\qquad$ E $5x$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Suppose that the distance that Professor Rosseforp runs is $d$ and his usual average speed (in terms of the units used for distance and minutes) is $s$. So, on Wednesday his journey takes him $\dfrac{d}{s}$ minutes. On Thursday his average speed was $\tfrac{110}{100}s = \tfrac{11}{10}s$ and so the journey takes him $\dfrac{d}{\tfrac{11}{10}s} = \dfrac{10d}{11s}$ minutes. We therefore have $\dfrac{d}{s} - \dfrac{10d}{11s} = x$. This gives $\dfrac{d}{11s} = x$ and hence $\dfrac{d}{s} = 11x$. So on Wednesday his journey took him $11x$ minutes.
