## 题目
The diagram shows a pattern found on a floor tile in the cathedral in Spoleto, Umbria. A circle of radius 1 surrounds four quarter circles, also of radius 1, which enclose a square. The pattern has four axes of symmetry. What is the side length of the square?

![[Image/12-Q17-fig1.png|125]]

A $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ $\qquad$ B $2 - \sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{3}}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ E $\sqrt{2} - 1$

B

## 解析
Let $O$ be the centre of the circle, let $P$ be one of the points where two of the quarter circles meet, let $Q$ be the centre of one of these quarter circles, and let $R$ be the vertex of the square that lies on $OQ$, as shown.

![[Image/12-Q17-sol1.png]]

Then in the triangle $OPQ$ there is a right angle at $P$ and $OP = QP = 1$, and therefore, by Pythagoras' Theorem, $OQ = \sqrt{2}$. Since $QR = 1$, it follows that $OR = \sqrt{2} - 1$. It follows that the diagonal of the square has length $2(\sqrt{2} - 1)$. Using, Pythagoras' Theorem again, it follows that the side length of the square is $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\big(2(\sqrt{2} - 1)\big) = 2 - \sqrt{2}$.
