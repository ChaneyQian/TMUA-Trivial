## 题目
The diagram shows the ellipse whose equation is $x^2 + y^2 - xy + x - 4y = 12$. The curve cuts the $y$-axis at points $A$ and $C$ and cuts the $x$-axis at points $B$ and $D$. What is the area of the inscribed quadrilateral $ABCD$?

## 解析
The graph cuts the $y$-axis at points where $x = 0$, and hence where $y^2 - 4y = 12$, that is, where $y^2 - 4y - 12 = 0$, that is, $(y + 2)(y - 6) = 0$, giving $y = -2$ and $y = 6$. Hence $A$ is the point $(0,6)$, $C$ is the point $(0,-2)$ and so $AC$ has length 8. Similarly, $B$ and $D$ are points where $y = 0$ and hence $x^2 + x - 12 = 0$, that is, $(x + 4)(x - 3) = 0$, giving $x = -4$ and $x = 3$. So $B$ is the point $(3,0)$, $D$ is the point $(-4,0)$, and so $BD$ has length 7. The area of the inscribed quadrilateral $ABCD$ is therefore $\tfrac{1}{2}AC.BD = \tfrac{1}{2}(8 \times 7) = 28$.
