## 题目
According to one astronomer, there are one hundred thousand million galaxies in the universe, each containing one hundred thousand million stars. How many stars is that altogether?

A $10^{13}$ $\qquad$ B $10^{22}$ $\qquad$ C $10^{100}$ $\qquad$ D $10^{120}$ $\qquad$ E $10^{121}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
One hundred thousand is $10^5$ and one million is $10^6$. So one hundred thousand million is $10^5 \times 10^6 = 10^{5+6} = 10^{11}$. Therefore the total number of stars is $10^{11} \times 10^{11} = 10^{11+11} = 10^{22}$.
