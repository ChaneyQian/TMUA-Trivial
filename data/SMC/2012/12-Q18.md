## 题目
The diagram shows two squares, with sides of length $\frac{1}{2}$, inclined at an angle $2\alpha$ to one another. What is the value of $x$?

![[Image/12-Q18-fig1.png|162]]

## 答案
A

## 解析
Consider the triangle at the bottom of the diagram. We have labelled the points $P$, $Q$ and $R$, as shown. We let $N$ be the point where the perpendicular from $P$ to $QR$ meets $QR$.

![[Image/12-Q18-sol1.png]]

Now, $\angle QPR = 360 - 90 - 2\alpha - 90 = 180 - 2\alpha$. In the triangle $PQR$ we have $PQ = PR = \tfrac{1}{2}$ as they are sides of the squares. It follows that the triangle $PQR$ is isosceles and hence $\angle PQN = \angle PRN = \alpha$. So the triangles $PQN$ and $PRN$ are congruent. Hence $QN = NR = \tfrac{1}{2}x$. Therefore, from the triangle $PQN$ we have $\cos\alpha = \dfrac{QN}{QP} = \dfrac{\tfrac{1}{2}x}{\tfrac{1}{2}} = x$. That is, $x = \cos\alpha$.
