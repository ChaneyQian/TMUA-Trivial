## 题目
What is the remainder when $1234 \times 5678$ is divided by 5?

A 0 $\qquad$ B 1 $\qquad$ C 2 $\qquad$ D 3 $\qquad$ E 4

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The units digit of $1234\times 5678$ is the same as the units digit of $4\times 8$, and hence is $2$. Therefore the remainder when $1234\times 5678$ is divided by $5$ is $2$.
