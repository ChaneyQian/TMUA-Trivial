## 题目
A shape is made from five unit cubes, as shown.

![[Image/SMC-20-Q3-fig1.png|230]]

What is the surface area of the shape?

A 22 $\qquad$ B 24 $\qquad$ C 26 $\qquad$ D 28 $\qquad$ E 30

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
The surface of the shape is made up of two faces of the central cube, and five faces of each of the four other cubes.

Therefore the surface is made up of $2+4\times 5=22$ square faces each of size $1\times 1$.

Hence the surface area of the shape is $22$.
