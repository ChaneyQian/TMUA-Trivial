## 题目
The positive integers $m$, $n$ and $p$ satisfy the equation $3m + \dfrac{3}{n + \dfrac{1}{p}} = 17$.

What is the value of $p$?

A 2 $\qquad$ B 3 $\qquad$ C 4 $\qquad$ D 6 $\qquad$ E 9

## 答案
A

## 解析
Because $\dfrac{3}{n+\frac1p}=17-3m$, where $m$ is an integer, it follows that $\dfrac{3}{n+\frac1p}$ is an integer.

Because $n$ and $p$ are positive integers, $1<n+\dfrac1p$ and therefore $0<\dfrac{3}{n+\frac1p}<3$. Therefore $\dfrac{3}{n+\frac1p}$ is either $1$ or $2$.

If $\dfrac{3}{n+\frac1p}=1$, then $3m=17-\dfrac{3}{n+\frac1p}=17-1=16$. This implies that $m=\dfrac{16}{3}$ contradicting the fact that $m$ is an integer. Hence $\dfrac{3}{n+\frac1p}=2$. Therefore $n+\dfrac1p=\dfrac32=1+\dfrac12$. Hence $n=1$ and $p=2$.

Also $3m=17-\dfrac{3}{1+\frac12}=17-2=15$ and so $m=5$. We therefore see that $m=5$, $n=1$ and $p=2$.
