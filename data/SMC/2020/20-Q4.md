## 题目
The numbers $p$, $q$, $r$ and $s$ satisfy the equations $p = 2$, $p \times q = 20$, $p \times q \times r = 202$ and $p \times q \times r \times s = 2020$.

What is the value of $p + q + r + s$?

A 32 $\qquad$ B 32.1 $\qquad$ C 33 $\qquad$ D 33.1 $\qquad$ E 34

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
We have

$$p=2,$$

$$q=\frac{p\times q}{p}=\frac{20}{2}=10,$$

$$r=\frac{p\times q\times r}{p\times q}=\frac{202}{20}=10.1,$$

and

$$s=\frac{p\times q\times r\times s}{p\times q\times r}=\frac{2020}{202}=10.$$

Therefore $p+q+r+s=2+10+10.1+10=32.1$.
