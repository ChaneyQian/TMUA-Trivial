## 题目
A function $f$ satisfies $y^3 f(x) = x^3 f(y)$ and $f(3) \neq 0$. What is the value of $\dfrac{f(20) - f(2)}{f(3)}$?

A 6 $\qquad$ B 20 $\qquad$ C 216 $\qquad$ D 296 $\qquad$ E 2023

## 答案
D

## 解析
By putting $x=20$ and $y=3$ in the equation $y^3f(x)=x^3f(y)$, we have $27f(20)=8000f(3)$. Hence

$$f(20)=\frac{8000}{27}f(3).$$

By putting $x=2$ and $y=3$ in the same equation, we have $27f(2)=8f(3)$. Hence

$$f(2)=\frac{8}{27}f(3).$$

Therefore,

$$\begin{aligned}f(20)-f(2)&=\frac{8000}{27}f(3)-\frac{8}{27}f(3)\\&=\left(\frac{8000}{27}-\frac{8}{27}\right)f(3)\\&=\frac{7992}{27}f(3)\\&=296f(3).\end{aligned}$$

Therefore, because $f(3)\ne 0$, it follows that

$$\frac{f(20)-f(2)}{f(3)}=296.$$
