## 题目
In 2018, a racing driver was allowed to use the Drag Reduction System provided that the car was within 1 second of the car ahead. Suppose that two cars were 1 second apart, each travelling at $180\text{ km/h}$ (in the same direction!).

How many metres apart were they?

A 100 $\qquad$ B 50 $\qquad$ C 10 $\qquad$ D 5 $\qquad$ E 1

## 答案
B

## 解析
The distance apart of the cars was the distance that a car travelling at $180\text{ km/h}$ travels in $1$ second.

There are $1000$ metres in one kilometre. Hence there are $180\times 1000$ metres in $180\text{ km}$.

There are $60\times 60$ seconds in each hour.

It follows that at $180\text{ km/h}$ a car travels $180\times 1000$ metres in $60\times 60$ seconds.

Therefore the number of metres that it travels in $1$ second is

$$\frac{180\times 1000}{60\times 60}=\frac{3\times 1000}{60}=\frac{1000}{20}=50.$$

Therefore when the cars are $1$ second apart, they are $50$ metres apart.
