## 题目
The real numbers $x$ and $y$ satisfy the equations $4^y = \dfrac{1}{8(\sqrt{2})^{x+2}}$ and $9^x \times 3^y = 3\sqrt{3}$.

What is the value of $5^{x+y}$?

A $5\sqrt{5}$ $\qquad$ B $5$ $\qquad$ C $\sqrt{5}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{1}{5}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{5}}$

## 解析
We have $4^y=(2^2)^y=2^{2y}$, and $\dfrac{1}{8(\sqrt{2})^{x+2}}=\dfrac{1}{2^3(2^{\frac12})^{x+2}}=2^{-(3+\frac12(x+2))}$.

Therefore from the equation $4^y=\dfrac{1}{8(\sqrt{2})^{x+2}}$ we deduce that $2y=-(3+\tfrac12(x+2))$.

Therefore

$$y=-\tfrac14 x-2.\qquad(1)$$

Also, $9^x\times 3^y=(3^2)^x\times 3^y=3^{2x+y}$ and $3\sqrt{3}=(3^1)(3^{\frac12})=3^{1+\frac12}=3^{\frac32}$.

Therefore, from the equation $9^x\times 3^y=3\sqrt{3}$ we deduce that $2x+y=\tfrac32$.

Therefore

$$y=-2x+\tfrac32.\qquad(2)$$

From equations (1) and (2)

$$-\tfrac14 x-2=-2x+\tfrac32.$$

This last equation may be rearranged to give

$$\tfrac74 x=\tfrac72.$$

Hence $x=2$.

Therefore, from equation (1), $y=-\tfrac52$.

It follows that $x+y=2+(-\tfrac52)=2-\tfrac52=-\tfrac12$.

Therefore $5^{x+y}=5^{-\frac12}=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{5}}$.
