## 题目
Two circles $C_1$ and $C_2$ have their centres at the point $(3,4)$ and touch a third circle, $C_3$. The centre of $C_3$ is at the point $(0,0)$ and its radius is 2.

What is the sum of the radii of the two circles $C_1$ and $C_2$?

A 6 $\qquad$ B 7 $\qquad$ C 8 $\qquad$ D 9 $\qquad$ E 10

## 中文题干
两个圆 $C_1$ 和 $C_2$ 的圆心都在点 $(3,4)$，并且都与第三个圆 $C_3$ 相切。$C_3$ 的圆心在点 $(0,0)$，半径是 2。$C_1$ 和 $C_2$ 这两个圆的半径之和是多少？

## 答案

## 解析
Let $P$ be the centre of the circles $C_1$ and $C_2$, $O$ be the centre of the circle $C_3$, and $R$ and $S$ be the points where $C_1$ and $C_2$, respectively, touch $C_3$. We let the radius of $C_1$ be $r$.

![[Image/20-Q18-sol1.png]]

Note that the points $O$, $P$, $R$ and $S$ lie on a straight line.

By Pythagoras' Theorem, the distance between the point $P$ with coordinates $(3,4)$ and the point $O$ with coordinates $(0,0)$ is $\sqrt{3^2+4^2}=\sqrt{25}=5$.

Therefore, $r+2=5$ and hence $r=3$.

The radius of the circle $C_2$ is $r+2+2=3+2+2=7$.

Therefore the sum of the radii of $C_1$ and $C_2$ is $3+7=10$.
