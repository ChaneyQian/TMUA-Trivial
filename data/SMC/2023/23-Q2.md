## 题目
What is the difference between one-third and $0.333$?

A $0$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{3}{1000}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{1}{3000}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{3}{10000}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{1}{30000}$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Expressed as fractions, one-third is $\dfrac{1}{3}$ and $0.333$ is $\dfrac{333}{1000}$. Therefore, the difference between one-third and $0.333$ is

$$\dfrac{1}{3} - \dfrac{333}{1000} = \dfrac{1000}{3000} - \dfrac{999}{3000} = \dfrac{1}{3000}.$$
