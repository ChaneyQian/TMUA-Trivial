## 题目
The points $P\ (d, -d)$ and $Q\ (12 - d, 2d - 6)$ both lie on the circumference of the same circle whose centre is the origin.

What is the sum of the two possible values of $d$?

A $-16$ $\qquad$ B $-4$ $\qquad$ C 4 $\qquad$ D 8 $\qquad$ E 16

E

## 解析
Let $O\,(0, 0)$ be the origin.

Because the points $P$ and $Q$ lie on the same circle with centre $O$, we have $OP^2 = OQ^2$. That is,

$$d^2 + (-d)^2 = (12 - d)^2 + (2d - 6)^2.$$

Expanding both sides of this equation, we obtain

$$d^2 + d^2 = (144 - 24d + d^2) + (4d^2 - 24d + 36).$$

We can rearrange this equation to obtain

$$3d^2 - 48d + 180 = 0.$$

By dividing both sides of this last equation by $3$, it follows that

$$d^2 - 16d + 60 = 0.$$

We can now use the fact that the sum of the roots of the quadratic equation $x^2 + px + q = 0$ is $-p$ to deduce that the sum of the two possible values of $d$ is $-(-16)$, that is, $16$.
