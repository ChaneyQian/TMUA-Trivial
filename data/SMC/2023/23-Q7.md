## 题目
A sequence begins $2023, 2022, 1, \dots$. After the first two terms, each term is the positive difference between the previous two terms.

What is the value of the 25th term?

A 2010 $\qquad$ B 2009 $\qquad$ C 2008 $\qquad$ D 2007 $\qquad$ E 2006

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The sequence begins

$$2023,\ 2022,\ 1,\ 2021,\ 2020,\ 1,\ 2019,\ 2018,\ 1,\ \ldots.$$

From this it seems that, in general, for each non-negative integer $k$, the terms in positions $3k + 1$, $3k + 2$ and $3k + 3$ are $2023 - 2k$, $2023 - 2k - 1$ and $1$. [In fact, this holds only provided $2023 - 2k - 1 \ge 0$, that is, only for $k \le 1011$. See Problems 7.1 and 7.4.]

Now $25 = 3 \times 8 + 1$. Therefore, by putting $k = 8$, we deduce that the $25$ th term is $2023 - 2 \times 8 = 2007$.
