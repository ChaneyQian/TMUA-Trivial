## 题目
What is the area of the part of the $xy$-plane within which $x^3y^2 - x^2y^2 - xy^4 + xy^3 \geq 0$ and $0 \leq x \leq y$?

A $\dfrac{1}{4}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{1}{2}$ $\qquad$ C 1 $\qquad$ D 2 $\qquad$ E 4

## 答案
A

## 解析
We have

$$\begin{aligned} x^3y^2 - x^2y^2 - xy^4 + xy^3 &= xy^2(x^2 - x - y^2 + y) \\ &= xy^2\left((x^2 - y^2) - (x - y)\right) \\ &= xy^2\left((x - y)(x + y) - (x - y)\right) \\ &= xy^2(x - y)(x + y - 1). \end{aligned}$$

In the region where $0 \le x \le y$, we have $xy^2 \ge 0$ and $x - y \le 0$. Therefore in this region $xy^2(x - y)(x + y - 1) \ge 0 \Longleftrightarrow (x + y - 1) \le 0$.

Therefore the region of the $xy$-plane within which we have $x^3y^2 - x^2y^2 - xy^4 + xy^3 \ge 0$ and $0 \le x \le y$ is the region where $x + y - 1 \le 0$ and $0 \le x \le y$.

The line with equation $x = 0$ (the $y$-axis) divides the plane into the region where $x > 0$ and the region where $x < 0$. In the diagram the symbol $+$ is used to indicate the side of the line where $x > 0$ and $-$ to indicate the side where $x < 0$.

In a similar way the line with equation $y = x$ divides the plane into the region where $y > x$ and the region where $y < x$. Again, we use $+$ and $-$ to mark these two regions.

![[Image/23-Q25-sol1.png]]

Also, the line with the equation $x + y - 1 = 0$ (or, equivalently, $y = 1 - x$) divides the plane into the region where $x + y - 1 > 0$ and the region where $x + y - 1 < 0$.

Therefore the region whose area we need to find is the triangle $OPQ$ which is bounded by the lines with equations $x = 0$, $x = y$ and $y = 1 - x$, as shown in the diagram.

The point $O$ is the origin where the lines $x = 0$ and $y = x$ meet. The point $P$ is the point where the lines $x = 0$ and $y = 1 - x$ meet. The co-ordinates of $P$ are $(0, 1)$. The point $Q$ is the point where the lines $y = x$ and $y = 1 - x$ meet. The co-ordinates of $Q$ are $\left(\tfrac{1}{2}, \tfrac{1}{2}\right)$.

The triangle $OPQ$ has a base $OP$ which has length $1$. The height of the triangle with this base is the distance of $Q$ from the $y$-axis which is $\dfrac{1}{2}$. Therefore, the area of this triangle is

$$\dfrac{1}{2}\left(1 \times \dfrac{1}{2}\right) = \dfrac{1}{4}.$$
