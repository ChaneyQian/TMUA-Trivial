## 题目
What is the value of $99(0.\dot{4}\dot{9} - 0.\dot{4})$?

A 5 $\qquad$ B 4 $\qquad$ C 3 $\qquad$ D 2 $\qquad$ E 1

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
The standard method for converting a recurrent decimal to a fraction shows that

$$0.\dot{4}\dot{9} = \dfrac{49}{99} \quad \text{and} \quad 0.\dot{4} = \dfrac{4}{9}.$$

[Problem 8.1 asks you to check this.]

Therefore

$$\begin{aligned} 99\left(0.\dot{4}\dot{9} - 0.\dot{4}\right) &= 99\left(\dfrac{49}{99} - \dfrac{4}{9}\right) \\ &= 99\left(\dfrac{49}{99} - \dfrac{44}{99}\right) \\ &= 99\left(\dfrac{5}{99}\right) \\ &= 5. \end{aligned}$$
