## 题目
Triangle $LMN$ represents a right-angled field with $LM = r$, $LX = p$ and $XN = q$. Jenny and Vicky walk at the same speed in opposite directions along the edge of the field, starting at $X$ at the same time. Their first meeting is at $M$.

![[Image/23-Q20-fig1.png|260]]

Which of these expressions gives $q$ in terms of $p$ and $r$?

A $\dfrac{p}{2} + r$ $\qquad$ B $\sqrt{p^2 + r^2} + \dfrac{p}{2}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{pr}{2p + r}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{p}{2}$ $\qquad$ E $1$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Because Jenny and Vicky meet after walking at the same speed, they walk the same distance. Therefore

$$XL + LM = XN + NM. \qquad (1)$$

The question tells us that $XL = p$, $LM = r$ and $XN = q$, To find $NM$, we apply Pythagoras' Theorem to the right-angled triangle $LMN$. This gives

$$NM^2 = LN^2 + LM^2 = (p + q)^2 + r^2.$$

It follows that $NM = \sqrt{(p + q)^2 + r^2}$. Substituting these values in equation (1) gives

$$p + r = q + \sqrt{(p + q)^2 + r^2}.$$

Hence

$$p + r - q = \sqrt{(p + q)^2 + r^2}. \qquad (2)$$

By squaring both sides of equation (2) we obtain

$$(p + r - q)^2 = (p + q)^2 + r^2.$$

It follows that

$$p^2 + r^2 + q^2 + 2pr - 2pq - 2rq = p^2 + 2pq + q^2 + r^2. \qquad (3)$$

Equation (3) may be rearranged to give

$$4pq + 2rq = 2pr$$

from which it follows that

$$q(2p + r) = pr.$$

Therefore

$$q = \dfrac{pr}{2p + r}.$$
