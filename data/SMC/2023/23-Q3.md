## 题目
The base of a triangle is increased by $20\%$ and its height is decreased by $15\%$.

What happens to its area?

A It decreases by $3\%$  
B It remains the same  
C It decreases by $2\%$  
D It increases by $2\%$  
E It increases by $5\%$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Suppose the original triangle has base $b$ and height $h$. The area of this triangle is $X$, where $X = \tfrac{1}{2}(b \times h)$.

When the base of the triangle is increased by $20\%$, its base becomes $b' = \dfrac{6}{5}b$. When its height is decreased by $15\%$, its height becomes $h' = \dfrac{17}{20}h$.

Therefore the area of the changed triangle is $X'$, where $X' = \tfrac{1}{2}(b' \times h') = \tfrac{1}{2}\left(\dfrac{6}{5}b \times \dfrac{17}{20}h\right) = \left(\dfrac{6}{5} \times \dfrac{17}{20}\right)\left(\tfrac{1}{2}(b \times h)\right) = \dfrac{102}{100}X$.

Therefore the effect of the changes is to increase the area of the triangle by $2\%$.
