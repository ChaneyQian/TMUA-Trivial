## 题目
Triangle $PQR$ is equilateral. A semicircle with centre $O$ is drawn with its diameter on $PR$ so that one end is at $P$ and the curved edge touches $QR$ at $X$. The radius of the semicircle is $\sqrt{3}$.

![[Image/23-Q21-fig1.png|240]]

What is the length of $QX$?

A $\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ B $2 - \sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ C $2\sqrt{3} - 1$ $\qquad$ D $1 + \sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ E $2\sqrt{3}$

## 答案

## 解析
Because $OP$ and $OX$ are radii of the semicircle, we have $OP = OX = \sqrt{3}$.

Because the semicircle touches $QR$ at $X$, the line $QR$ is a tangent to the semicircle at $X$ and therefore the radius $OX$ is perpendicular to $QR$. Therefore $OXR$ is a right-angled triangle.

Because the triangle $PQR$ is equilateral, $\angle ORX = 60^\circ$.

![[Image/23-Q21-sol1.png]]

Hence

$$\dfrac{OX}{OR} = \sin 60^\circ = \dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \quad \text{and} \quad \dfrac{XR}{OR} = \cos 60^\circ = \dfrac{1}{2}.$$

It follows that

$$OR = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \times OX = \dfrac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \times \sqrt{3} = 2,$$

and, therefore,

$$XR = \dfrac{1}{2} \times OR = \dfrac{1}{2} \times 2 = 1.$$

We can now deduce that

$$QR = PR = OR + OP = 2 + \sqrt{3}.$$

Therefore

$$QX = QR - XR = (2 + \sqrt{3}) - 1 = 1 + \sqrt{3}.$$
