## 题目
In 2016, the world record for completing a 5000m three-legged race was 19 minutes and 6 seconds. It was set by Damian Thacker and Luke Symonds in Sheffield.

What was their approximate average speed in $\text{km/h}$?

A 10 $\qquad$ B 12 $\qquad$ C 15 $\qquad$ D 18 $\qquad$ E 25

## 中文题干
2016 年，完成 5000m 两人三足赛跑的世界纪录是 19 分 6 秒，由 Damian Thacker 和 Luke Symonds 在谢菲尔德创造。他们的平均速度约为多少 $\text{km/h}$？

## 答案
C

## 解析
The average speed running $5000\text{ m}$ in $19$ minutes and $6$ seconds is approximately the same as running this distance in $20$ minutes. So their average speed was approximately $\dfrac{5000}{20}$ metres per minute, that is $250$ metres per minute.

A speed of $250$ metres per minute is the same as $60 \times 250$ metres per hour, that is, $15\,000$ metres per hour. This is the same as $15\text{ km/h}$.
