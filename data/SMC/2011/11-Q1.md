## 题目
Which of the numbers below is not a whole number?

A $\dfrac{2011 + 0}{1}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{2011 + 1}{2}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{2011 + 2}{3}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{2011 + 3}{4}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{2011 + 4}{5}$

## 答案
D

## 解析
It is straightforward to check that $\dfrac{2011+3}{4} = \dfrac{2014}{4} = \dfrac{1007}{2}$ is not a whole number, but that all the other fractions given above are whole numbers. A more general approach to the question is as follows. We have that $\dfrac{2011+(n-1)}{n} = \dfrac{2010+n}{n} = \dfrac{2010}{n} + 1$, and therefore, $\dfrac{2011+(n-1)}{n}$ is a whole number if and only if $\dfrac{2010}{n}$ is a whole number, that is, if and only if $n$ is a factor of 2010. Since 2010 has the prime factorization $2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 67$, its distinct factors are 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 10, 15, 30, 67, 134, 201, 335, 402, 670, 1005 and 2010. These are the only positive whole number values of $n$ for which $\dfrac{2011+(n-1)}{n}$ is also a whole number.
