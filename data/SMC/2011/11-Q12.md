## 题目
The *primorial* of a number is the product of all of the prime numbers less than or equal to that number. For example, the primorial of 6 is $2 \times 3 \times 5 = 30$. How many different whole numbers have a primorial of 210?

A 1 $\qquad$ B 2 $\qquad$ C 3 $\qquad$ D 4 $\qquad$ E 5

## 中文题干
一个数的*质数阶乘*是指小于或等于该数的所有质数的乘积。例如，6 的质数阶乘是 $2 \times 3 \times 5 = 30$。有多少个不同的整数，其质数阶乘为 210？

## 答案
D

## 解析
Since $210 = 2 \times 3 \times 5 \times 7$, the primorial of a positive integer $n$ is 210 if and only if the primes less than or equal to $n$ are 2, 3, 5 and 7. This holds if and only if $7 \le n < 11$, as 11 is the next largest prime after 7. So the primorial of $n$ is 210 just for the four values $n = 7$, 8, 9 and 10.
