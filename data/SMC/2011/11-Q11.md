## 题目
$PQRSTU$ is a regular hexagon and $V$ is the midpoint of $PQ$.

What fraction of the area of $PQRSTU$ is the area of triangle $STV$?

![[Image/11-Q11-fig1.png|230]]

A $\dfrac{1}{4}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{2}{15}$ $\qquad$ C $\dfrac{1}{3}$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{2}{5}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{5}{12}$

## 中文题干
$PQRSTU$ 是一个正六边形，$V$ 是 $PQ$ 的中点。三角形 $STV$ 的面积占 $PQRSTU$ 面积的几分之几？

## 答案
C

## 解析
Let $O$ be the centre of the hexagon. The triangle $STO$ has the same base as triangle $STV$ and half its height. So the area of triangle $STV$ is twice that of triangle $STO$. The area of triangle $STO$ is $\frac{1}{6}$ of the area of the hexagon. Hence, the area of triangle $STV$ is $\frac{1}{3}$ of the area of the hexagon.
