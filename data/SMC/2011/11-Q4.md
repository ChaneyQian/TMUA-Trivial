## 题目
What is the last digit of $3^{2011}$?

A 1 $\qquad$ B 3 $\qquad$ C 5 $\qquad$ D 7 $\qquad$ E 9

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Since $3^4 = 81$, the last digit of $3^{2008} = (3^4)^{502}$ is 1. Since $3^{2011} = 3^{2008+3} = 3^{2008} \times 3^3 = 3^{2008} \times 27$, the last digit of $3^{2011}$ is the same as the last digit of 27, that is, 7.

[The last digit of a number is its remainder when we divide by 10. So here we are really working with congruences (mod 10). In this language we can write the above calculation as $3^{2011} \equiv 3^{4 \times 502 + 3} \equiv (3^4)^{502} \times 3^3 \equiv 1^{502} \times 7 \equiv 7 \pmod{10}$.]
