## 题目
A solid sculpture consists of a $4 \times 4 \times 4$ cube with a $3 \times 3 \times 3$ cube sticking out, as shown. Three vertices of the smaller cube lie on edges of the larger cube, the same distance along each.

What is the total volume of the sculpture?

## 解析
The volume of the sculpture is the sum of the volumes of the two cubes minus the volume of their intersection. This intersection consists of two tetrahedra which are both congruent to the tetrahedron $PQRS$, shown, where $P$, $Q$ and $R$ are three vertices of the smaller cube. In one of these tetrahedra the fourth vertex $S$ corresponds to the vertex of the smaller cube which is inside the larger cube. In the other, $S$ corresponds to the vertex of the larger cube which is inside the smaller cube.

![[Image/11-Q25-sol1.png]]

So $PQRS$ is a tetrahedron whose base has area $b = \frac{1}{2}3^2 = \frac{9}{2}$ and with height $h = 3$. So the volume of $PQRS$ is $\frac{1}{3}bh = \frac{9}{2}$. So the volume of the intersection which is made up of two of these tetrahedra is $2 \times \frac{9}{2} = 9$.

It follows that the volume of the sculpture is $4^3 + 3^3 - 9 = 64 + 27 - 9 = 82$.
