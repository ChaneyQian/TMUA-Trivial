## 题目
What is the area of the region inside the quadrilateral $PQRS$?

## 解析
By Pythagoras' Theorem applied to the right-angled triangle $PSR$, we have $PR^2 = 3^2 + 4^2 = 9 + 16 = 25$. Therefore $PR = 5$.

![[Image/21-Q8-sol1.png]]

It follows that in the triangle $PRQ$ we have

$$PQ^2 = 13^2 = 169 = 25 + 144 = 5^2 + 12^2 = PR^2 + RQ^2.$$

Therefore, by the converse of Pythagoras' Theorem, $\angle PRQ = 90^\circ$.

Because $\angle PRQ = 90^\circ$, the area of the triangle $PRQ$ is $\frac{1}{2}(RQ \times RP) = \frac{1}{2}(12 \times 5) = 30$. Similarly, the area of the triangle $PSR$ is $\frac{1}{2}(SP \times SR) = \frac{1}{2}(3 \times 4) = 6$.

The area of the quadrilateral $PQRS$ is the area of the triangle $PRQ$ minus the area of the triangle $PSR$. Hence the area of $PQRS$ is $30 - 6 = 24$.
