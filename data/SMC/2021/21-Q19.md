## 题目
The diagram shows two overlapping triangles: triangle $FGH$ with interior angles $60^\circ$, $30^\circ$ and $90^\circ$ and triangle $EGH$ which is a right-angled isosceles triangle.

![[Image/21-Q19-fig1.png|230]]

What is the ratio of the area of triangle $IFG$ to the area of triangle $IEH$?

A $1 : 1$ $\qquad$ B $1 : \sqrt{2}$ $\qquad$ C $1 : \sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ D $1 : 2$ $\qquad$ E $1 : 3$

## 答案
D

## 解析
We suppose that we have chosen units so that the length of $GH$ is $1$.

![[Image/21-Q19-sol1.png]]

Because $FGH$ is a $60^\circ$, $30^\circ$, $90^\circ$ triangle, it follows that $FG$ has length $\frac{1}{2}$.

Because $EGH$ is a right-angled isosceles triangle it also follows that $EH$ has length $\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.

In the triangles $IFG$ and $IEH$ we have

$$\angle GFI = \angle HEI = 90^\circ$$

and

$$\angle GIF = \angle HIE \text{ (vertically opposite angles)}.$$

Because the sum of the angles in both these triangles is $180^\circ$, it follows that

$$\angle FGI = \angle EHI.$$

Therefore the triangles $IFG$ and $IEH$ are similar.

The ratio of the areas of similar triangles equals the ratio of the squares of the lengths of corresponding sides. Therefore

$$\begin{aligned}
\text{area of } IFG : \text{area of } IEH &= FG^2 : EH^2\\
&= \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right)^2 : \left(\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\right)^2\\
&= \tfrac{1}{4} : \tfrac{1}{2}\\
&= 1 : 2.
\end{aligned}$$
