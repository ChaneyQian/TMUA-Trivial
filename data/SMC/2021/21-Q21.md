## 题目
The numbers $m$ and $k$ satisfy the equations $2^m + 2^k = p$ and $2^m - 2^k = q$.

What is the value of $2^{m+k}$ in terms of $p$ and $q$?

A $\dfrac{p^2 - q^2}{4}$ $\qquad$ B $\dfrac{pq}{2}$ $\qquad$ C $p + q$ $\qquad$ D $\dfrac{(p - q)^2}{4}$ $\qquad$ E $\dfrac{p + q}{p - q}$

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
We have

$$2^m + 2^k = p \qquad (1)$$

and

$$2^m - 2^k = q. \qquad (2)$$

Adding equations (1) and (2), we obtain

$$2(2^m) = p + q$$

and hence

$$2^m = \frac{p + q}{2}. \qquad (3)$$

Subtracting equations (2) from equation (1), we obtain

$$2(2^k) = p - q$$

and hence

$$2^k = \frac{p - q}{2}. \qquad (4)$$

Therefore, by (3) and (4)

$$\begin{aligned}
2^{m+k} &= 2^m \times 2^k\\
&= \left(\frac{p + q}{2}\right) \times \left(\frac{p - q}{2}\right)\\
&= \frac{(p + q)(p - q)}{4}\\
&= \frac{p^2 - q^2}{4}.
\end{aligned}$$
