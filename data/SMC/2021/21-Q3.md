## 题目
The diagram shows two congruent regular pentagons and a triangle. The angles marked $x^\circ$ are equal.

![[Image/21-Q3-fig1.png|240]]

What is the value of $x$?

## 答案
C

## 解析
Let $P$, $Q$ and $R$ be the points shown in the diagram.

![[Image/21-Q3-sol1.png]]

The interior angles of a regular pentagon are all $108^\circ$.

Because the two pentagons are congruent, $PR = PQ$. Therefore $\angle PRQ = \angle PQR = x^\circ$.

Because the sum of the angles in a triangle is $180^\circ$, from the triangle $PQR$, we have $x^\circ + x^\circ + \angle QPR = 180^\circ$. Therefore $\angle QPR = 180^\circ - 2x^\circ$.

The sum of the angles at a point is $360^\circ$. Therefore from the angles at the point $P$, we have

$$(180^\circ - 2x^\circ) + 108^\circ + x^\circ + 108^\circ = 360^\circ.$$

That is

$$396^\circ - x^\circ = 360^\circ.$$

It follows that

$$x^\circ = 396^\circ - 360^\circ = 36^\circ.$$
