## 题目
The number 840 can be written as $\dfrac{p!}{q!}$, where $p$ and $q$ are positive integers less than 10.

What is the value of $p + q$?

Note that, $n! = 1 \times 2 \times 3 \times \cdots \times (n - 1) \times n$.

A 8 $\qquad$ B 9 $\qquad$ C 10 $\qquad$ D 12 $\qquad$ E 15

## 解析
We note first that, as $\dfrac{p!}{q!} = 840$, it follows that $p! > q!$ and hence $p > q$. Therefore

$$\frac{p!}{q!} = \frac{1 \times 2 \times \cdots \times q \times (q + 1) \times \cdots \times p}{1 \times 2 \times \cdots \times q} = (q + 1) \times (q + 2) \times \cdots \times (p - 1) \times p.$$

Thus $840 = \dfrac{p!}{q!}$ is the product of the consecutive integers $q + 1$, $q + 2$, …, $p - 1$, $p$, where $p \le 9$.

Since $840$ is not a multiple of $9$, $p \ne 9$. Since $840$ is a multiple of $7$, $p \ge 7$.

Now $5 \times 6 \times 7 \times 8 = 1680 > 840$, while $6 \times 7 \times 8 = 336 < 840$. Hence $840$ is not the product of consecutive integers of which the largest is $8$.

We deduce that $p = 7$. It is now straightforward to check that

$$840 = 4 \times 5 \times 6 \times 7 = \frac{7!}{3!}.$$

Therefore $p = 7$ and $q = 3$. Hence $p + q = 7 + 3 = 10$.
