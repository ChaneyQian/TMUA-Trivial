## 题目
Let $x$ be a real number. What is the minimum value of $\left(x^2 - 4x + 3\right)\left(x^2 + 4x + 3\right)$?

A $-16$ $\qquad$ B $-9$ $\qquad$ C $0$ $\qquad$ D $9$ $\qquad$ E $16$

## 答案
A

## 解析
We have

$$\begin{aligned}
\left(x^2 - 4x + 3\right)\left(x^2 + 4x + 3\right) &= \big((x^2 + 3) - 4x\big)\big((x^2 + 3) + 4x\big)\\
&= (x^2 + 3)^2 - (4x)^2\\
&= x^4 + 6x^2 + 9 - 16x^2\\
&= x^4 - 10x^2 + 9\\
&= (x^2 - 5)^2 - 16.
\end{aligned}$$

For every real number $x$, $(x^2 - 5)^2 \ge 0$ and therefore $(x^2 - 5)^2 - 16 \ge -16$.

Now, when $x = \sqrt{5}$, $(x^2 - 5)^2 - 16 = 0^2 - 16 = -16$.

It follows that the minimum value of $\left(x^2 - 4x + 3\right)\left(x^2 + 4x + 3\right)$ is $-16$.
