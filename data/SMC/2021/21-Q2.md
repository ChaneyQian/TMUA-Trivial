## 题目
The sequence, formed from the sequence of primes by rounding each to the nearest ten, begins 0, 0, 10, 10, 10, 10, 20, 20, 20, 30, ... .

When continued, how many terms in this sequence are equal to 40?

A 1 $\qquad$ B 2 $\qquad$ C 3 $\qquad$ D 4 $\qquad$ E 5

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The integers that round to $40$ are those in the range from $35$ to $44$.

The primes in this range are $37$, $41$ and $43$.

Therefore there are $3$ primes that round to $40$.
