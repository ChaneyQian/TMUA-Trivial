## 题目
In a survey, people were asked to name their favourite fruit pie. The pie chart shows the outcome. The angles shown are exact with no rounding.

![[Image/21-Q10-fig1.png|250]]

What is the smallest number of people who could have been surveyed?

A 45 $\qquad$ B 60 $\qquad$ C 80 $\qquad$ D 90 $\qquad$ E 180

## 答案
D

## 解析
Suppose that $p$ people were surveyed.

The total of the angles is $360^\circ$. Therefore the proportion of the people who said that their favourite is apple pie is $\dfrac{140}{360} = \dfrac{7}{18}$. Hence the number who chose apple pie was $\dfrac{7}{18}p$. This is an integer. Therefore $p$ is a multiple of $18$.

Similarly, as the proportion who said their favourite is cherry pie is $\dfrac{108}{360} = \dfrac{3}{10}$, we deduce that $p$ is a multiple of $10$.

Likewise, as $\dfrac{72}{360} = \dfrac{1}{5}$ and $\dfrac{40}{360} = \dfrac{1}{9}$, we know that $p$ is also a multiple of $5$ and of $9$.

Therefore the smallest possible value of $p$ is the least common multiple of $18$, $10$, $5$ and $9$, which is $90$. Hence the smallest number of people who could have been surveyed is $90$.
