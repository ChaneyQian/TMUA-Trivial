## 题目
The sum of four consecutive primes is itself prime.

What is the largest of the four primes?

A 37 $\qquad$ B 29 $\qquad$ C 19 $\qquad$ D 13 $\qquad$ E 7

## 中文题干

## 答案
E

## 解析
The sum of four odd primes is an even number greater than $2$, and therefore is not a prime. Therefore the four consecutive primes whose sum is prime includes the only even prime $2$.

It follows that the four consecutive primes are $2$, $3$, $5$ and $7$. Their sum is $17$ which is a prime. The largest of these four consecutive primes is $7$.
