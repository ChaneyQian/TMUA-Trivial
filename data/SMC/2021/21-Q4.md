## 题目
The positive integer $k$ is a solution of the equation $(k \div 12) \div (15 \div k) = 20$.

What is the sum of the digits of $k$?

A 15 $\qquad$ B 12 $\qquad$ C 9 $\qquad$ D 6 $\qquad$ E 3

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
We have

$$\begin{aligned}
(k \div 12) \div (15 \div k) &= \dfrac{k}{12} \div \dfrac{15}{k}\\
&= \dfrac{k}{12} \times \dfrac{k}{15}\\
&= \dfrac{k \times k}{12 \times 15}\\
&= \dfrac{k^2}{180}.
\end{aligned}$$

It follows that

$$\begin{aligned}
(k \div 12) \div (15 \div k) = 20 &\Longleftrightarrow \dfrac{k^2}{180} = 20\\
&\Longleftrightarrow k^2 = 3600\\
&\Longleftrightarrow k = 60, \text{ as } k > 0.
\end{aligned}$$

The sum of the digits of $60$ is $6 + 0 = 6$.
