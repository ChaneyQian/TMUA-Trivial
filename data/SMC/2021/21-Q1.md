## 题目
Cicely had her 21st birthday in 1939.

When did she have her 100th birthday?

A 2020 $\qquad$ B 2019 $\qquad$ C 2018 $\qquad$ D 2010 $\qquad$ E 2008

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Cicely had her 21st birthday in 1939. Since $1939 - 21 = 1918$, it follows that she was born in 1918.

Now, $1918 + 100 = 2018$.

Therefore Cicely's 100th birthday was in 2018.
