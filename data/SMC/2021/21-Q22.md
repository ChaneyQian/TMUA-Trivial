## 题目
A triangle with interior angles $60^\circ$, $45^\circ$ and $75^\circ$ is inscribed in a circle of radius 2.

What is the area of the triangle?

A $2\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ B $4$ $\qquad$ C $6 + \sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ D $6\sqrt{3}$ $\qquad$ E $3 + \sqrt{3}$

## 解析
Let $P$, $Q$ and $R$ be the vertices of the triangle, let $O$ be the centre of the circle, as shown in the diagram.

![[Image/21-Q22-sol1.png]]

The angle subtended by an arc at the centre of a circle is twice the angle subtended at the circumference. Therefore

$$\angle QOR = 120^\circ,$$

$$\angle ROP = 90^\circ$$

and

$$\angle POQ = 150^\circ.$$

We use the notation $[XYZ]$ for the area of a triangle $XYZ$.

Using the formula $\frac{1}{2}ab \sin \theta$ for the area of a triangle with sides of lengths $a$ and $b$ with included angle $\theta$, we have

$$[QOR] = \frac{1}{2}(OQ \times OR) \sin 120^\circ = \frac{1}{2}(2 \times 2)\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} = \sqrt{3},$$

$$[ROP] = \frac{1}{2}(OR \times OP) \sin 90^\circ = \frac{1}{2}(2 \times 2) = 2$$

and

$$[POQ] = \frac{1}{2}(OP \times OQ) \sin 150^\circ = \frac{1}{2}(2 \times 2)\frac{1}{2} = 1.$$

Therefore

$$[PQR] = [QOR] + [ROP] + [POQ] = \sqrt{3} + 2 + 1 = 3 + \sqrt{3}.$$
