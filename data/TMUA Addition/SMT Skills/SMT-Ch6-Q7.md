## 题目
If $f(x) = 4x^2 - 12x + 18$, what are the coordinates of the minimum point of $y = \sqrt{f(x - 4)}$?

(a) $\left(\frac{3}{2}, \frac{3}{2}\right)$
(b) $\left(\frac{11}{2}, 3\right)$
(c) $(4, 3)$
(d) $(6, 3)$
(e) $\left(\frac{11}{2}, \frac{3}{2}\right)$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
If The co-ordinates of the minimum turning point on the graph of $y = f(x) = 4x^{2} - 12x + 18 \equiv 4\left( x - \frac{3}{2} \right)^{2} + 9$ are $\left( \frac{3}{2},\ 9 \right)$. Then the co-ordinates of the minimum point on $y = f(x - 4)$ must be $\left( \frac{3}{2} + 4,\ 9 \right) = \left( \frac{11}{2},\ 9 \right)$ and the co-ordinates of the minimum point on $y = \sqrt{f(x - 4)}$ must be $\left( \frac{11}{2},\ \sqrt{9} \right) = \left( \frac{11}{2},\ 3 \right)$. The answer is b).
