## 题目
When $2x^2 + 3x - 15$ is multiplied by $(ax - 4)$ and the resulting product is divided by $x + 2$, the remainder is $182$.
What is the value of $a$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -9$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad -5$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 5$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 9$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{207}{2}$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Left $f(x) = (ax - 4)\left( 2x^{2} + 3x - 15 \right)$

$$
f( - 2) = 182
$$

$$
( - 2x - 4)(8 - 6 - 15) = 182
$$

$$
26(a + 2) = 182
$$

$$
a + 2 = 7
$$

$$
a = 5
$$

The correct answer is C.
