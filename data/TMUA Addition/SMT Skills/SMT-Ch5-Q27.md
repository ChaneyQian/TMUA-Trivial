## 题目
Let $p, q$ and $r$ be positive real numbers and $p, q \neq 1$. The equation
$$ \log_p \left((px^2)^2\right) + \log_q r^x + \log_2 4^x + \log_q r = 0 $$
has a repeated root when

(a) $q^2 = 4r$
(b) $p = \frac{1}{r}$
(c) $qr = 1$
(d) $r = q^2$
(e) $p = q$

## 答案
D

## 解析
The expression can be written

$\log_{p}p^{2x^{2}} + x\log_{q}r + x\log_{2}4 + \log_{q}r = 0$

and from this

$2x^{2} + \left( 2 + \log_{q}r \right)x + \log_{q}r = 0$

For this to have a repeated root, the discriminant must be equal to $0$.

$\left( 2 + \log_{q}r \right)^{2} - 8\log_{q}r = 0$

Let $a = \log_{q}r$

$(2 + a)^{2} - 8a = 0$

$a^{2} + 4a + 4 - 8a = 0$

$a^{2} - 4a + 4 = 0$

$(a - 2)^{2} = 0$

$a = 2\ \overset{\Leftrightarrow}{\ }\ \ \log_{q}r = 2\ \ \overset{\Leftrightarrow}{\ }\ \ r = q^{2}$

The correct answer is (d)
