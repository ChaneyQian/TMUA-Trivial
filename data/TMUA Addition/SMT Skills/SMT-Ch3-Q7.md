## 题目
Find the coefficient of the $x^7$ term in the expansion of $x^3 \left(x + \frac{2}{x}\right)^{10}$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 960$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 320$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 120$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 15$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 15 360$$

## 答案
A

## 解析
First search for the $x^{4}$ term in the expansion of $\left( x + \frac{2}{x} \right)^{10}$. The highest power of $x$ in this expansion is $x^{10}$, and from there the powers descend by two each time (from both losing an $x$ and gaining a $\frac{2}{x}$. It follows that the $x^{4}$ term is formed as follows: $\begin{pmatrix}
10 \
3
\end{pmatrix} \times x^{7} \times \left( \frac{2}{x} \right)^{3} = \frac{10 \times 9 \times 8}{3!} \times 8x^{4} = 10 \times 3 \times 4 \times 8x^{4} = 960x^{4}$. Therefore, the $x^{7}$ term in the expansion of $x^{3}\left( x + \frac{2}{x} \right)^{10}$ is $x^{3} \times 960x^{4} = 960x^{7}$.

The answer is A.
