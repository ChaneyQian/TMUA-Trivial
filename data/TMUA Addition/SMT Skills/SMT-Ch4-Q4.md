## 题目
Find the value(s) of $a$ such that the turning point of the parabola $y = x^2 + 2ax + 1$ is closest to the origin.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad a = \sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad a = \pm \sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad a = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad a = \pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad a = 0$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
$x^{2} + 2ax + 1 = (x + a)^{2} + 1 - a^{2}$ so the turning point has coordinates $\left( - a,1 - a^{2} \right).$ Letting $d$ denote the distance from the turning point to the origin gives $d^{2} = a^{2} + \left( 1 - a^{2} \right)^{2} = a^{4} - a^{2} + 1 = \left( a^{2} - \frac{1}{2} \right)^{2} + \frac{3}{4}$. So the required values of $a$ are $\pm \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$. The correct answer is D.
