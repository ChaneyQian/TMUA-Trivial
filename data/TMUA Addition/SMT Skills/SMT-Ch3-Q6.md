## 题目
In the expansion of $(a + bx)^8$, the coefficient of $x^6$ is $4$ times the coefficient of $x^3$. What is the value of $\frac{b}{a}$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 3$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 4$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \sqrt[3]{2}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \sqrt[3]{3}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad \sqrt[3]{4}$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
The $x^{6}$ term in the expansion of $(a + bx)^{8}$ is $\begin{pmatrix}
8 \
6
\end{pmatrix} \times a^{2} \times (bx)^{6}$, so the coefficient is $\frac{8 \times 7}{2!}a^{2}b^{2} = 28a^{2}b^{6}$. The $x^{3}$ term is $\begin{pmatrix}
8 \
3
\end{pmatrix} \times a^{5} \times (bx)^{3}$, so the coefficient is $\frac{8 \times 7 \times 6}{3!}a^{5}b^{3} = 56a^{5}b^{3}$.
Therefore, $28a^{2}b^{6} = 4 \times 56a^{5}b^{3} \Leftrightarrow a^{2}b^{6} = 8a^{5}b^{3}$. Dividing both sides of the equation by $a^{5}b^{3}$ gives $a^{- 3}b^{3} = \left( \frac{b}{a} \right)^{3} = 8 \Leftrightarrow \frac{b}{a} = 2$.

The answer is B.
