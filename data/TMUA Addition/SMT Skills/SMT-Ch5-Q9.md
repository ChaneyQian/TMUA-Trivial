## 题目
You are given
$$ \log_a (xy^2z) = 6 $$
$$ \log_a (x^2yz^4) = 9 $$
$$ \log_a (x^5y^7z^5) = 25 $$
where $x, y$ and $z$ are real numbers greater than $1$.
What is the value of $a$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 1$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad x$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad y$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad z$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{There is not enough information to determine if } a \text{ is any of the other options.}$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
$\log_{a}{\left( xy^{2}z \right) = 6}$ A

$\log_{a}{\left( x^{2}yz^{4} \right) = 9}$ B

$\log_{a}{\left( x^{5}y^{7}z^{5} \right) = 25}$ C

3A + B:

$3\log_{a}\left( xy^{2}z \right) + \log_{a}\left( x^{2}yz^{4} \right) = 3 \times 6 + 9$

$\log_{a}\left( x^{3}y^{6}z^{3} \right) + \log_{a}\left( x^{2}yz^{4} \right) = 27$

$\log_{a}\left( x^{5}y^{7}z^{7} \right) = 27$ D

D – C:

$\log_{a}\left( x^{5}y^{7}z^{7} \right) - \log_{a}\left( x^{5}y^{7}z^{5} \right) = 27 - 25$

$\log_{a}\left( \frac{x^{5}y^{7}z^{7}}{x^{5}y^{7}z^{5}} \right) = 2$

$\log_{a}z^{2} = 2$

$2\log_{a}z = 2$

$\log_{a}z = 1$

$a^{1} = z$

The answer is D
