## 题目
The inequality
$$ (n + 2) + (2n + 4) + (3n + 6) + (4n + 8) + \dots + (100n + 200) > k $$
is true for all $n \ge 0$. It follows that

(a) $k < 5050$
(b) $k < 10100$
(c) $k \ge 2101$
(d) $k^2 > 100$
(e) $k < 6060$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
$(n + 2) + (2n + 4) + (3n + 6) + (4n + 8) + \cdots + (100n + 200) \equiv \frac{100 \times 101}{2}n + 2\left( \frac{100 \times 101}{2} \right) \equiv 5050n + 10100 \geq 10100$ if $n \geq 0$. It follows that the inequality in the question is valid precisely when $k < 10100$. The answer is (b).
(Note that $k < 5050$ would imply that the inequality in the question is correct, but the converse of this, which is what you want, is not true.)
