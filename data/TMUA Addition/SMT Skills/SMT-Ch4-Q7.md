## 题目
The line $y = 2x + c$ is such that it intersects the circle $x^2 + y^2 = 9$ at two points $A$ and $B$. $M$ is the midpoint of the chord $AB$ of the circle. The equation of the locus of $M$ as $c$ varies between $-3\sqrt{5}$ and $3\sqrt{5}$ is

$$\mathbf{A} \quad x^2 + y^2 = 2$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad y = -\frac{1}{2}x$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad y = \frac{1}{2}x$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad y = x^2$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad y = x$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad y = -x$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
The line $y = 2x + c$ and the circle $x^{2} + y^{2} = 9$ intersect when $x^{2} + (2x + c)^{2} = 9$ giving $5x^{2} + 4cx + \left( c^{2} - 9 \right) = 0$, so $x = \frac{- 4c \pm \sqrt{180 - 4c^{2}}}{10}$ (note that there are 2 points of intersection provided that $180 - 4c^{2} > 0$, and hence $- 4\sqrt{5} < c < 4\sqrt{5}$). By considering the means of the coordinates of $A$ and $B$ you obtain $M( - \frac{2c}{5},\ \frac{c}{5})$ (note that when computing the mean of the $x$-coordinates of $A$ and $B$ the discriminant cancels, and the mean of the $y$-coordinates of $A$ and $B$ is given by $2x + c$ with $x = - \frac{2c}{5}$). By inspection, as $c$ varies, the coordinates of $M$ satisfy $y = - \frac{x}{2}$. The correct answer is B.
