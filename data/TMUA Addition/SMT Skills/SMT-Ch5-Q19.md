## 题目
The function $S(n)$ is defined for positive integers $n$ by $S(n) =$ the sum of the digits of $n$.
For example, $S(723) = 7 + 2 + 3 = 12$.
The value of $S(1) + S(2) + S(3) + \dots + S(99)$ is

(a) $855$
(b) $900$
(c) $945$
(d) $990$
(e) $1035$

## 备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
The digits from $1$ to $9$ inclusive will sum to $45$

The digits from $10$ to $19$ will be the sum of ten $1$’s and the digits $1$ to $9$ i.e. $10 + 45$

For $20$ to $29$ this will be $20 + 45$, for $30$ to $39$ this will be $30 + 45$ and so on

$S(1) + S(2) + S(3) + S(4) + \ldots + S(99) = (10 + 20 + \ldots + 90) + 10 \times 45$

$= 10(1 + 2 + \ldots + 9) + 450 = 450 + 450 = 900$

The correct answer is (b)
