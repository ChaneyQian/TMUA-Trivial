## 题目
The solution of the simultaneous equations
$$ 5^{2x} - 16 \times 5^{2y} + 5 = 0 $$
$$ 5^x + 4 \times 5^y = 5 $$
is $x = a$ and $y = b$. Find the value of $a + b$.

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \log_5 6$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \log_5 \left(\frac{3}{4}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 1 - \log_5 2$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \log_5 \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 2\log_5 3$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
Let $p = 5^{x}$ and $q = 5^{y}$

$p^{2} - 16q^{2} + 5 = 0$ A

$p + 4q = 5\ \overset{\Leftrightarrow}{\ }\ p = 5 - 4q$ B

Substitute into A: $(5 - 4q)^{2} - 16q^{2} + 5 = 0$

$$
25 - 40q + 16q^{2} - 16q^{2} + 5 = 0
$$

$40q = 30\ \overset{\Leftrightarrow}{\ }\ q = \frac{3}{4}$

In B: $p = 5 - 3 = 2$

$$
5^{x} = 2\ \overset{\Leftrightarrow}{\ }\ a = x = \log_{5}2
$$

$5^{y} = \frac{3}{4}\ \overset{\Leftrightarrow}{\ }\ b = y = \log_{5}\frac{3}{4}$

$a + b = \log_{5}2 + \log_{5}\frac{3}{4} = \log_{5}\frac{3}{2}$

The correct answer is E
