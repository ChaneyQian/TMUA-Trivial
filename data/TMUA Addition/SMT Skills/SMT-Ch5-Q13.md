## 题目
The maximum value achieved by the function
$f(x) = 10\sin^4 2x + 13\sin^2 2x - 3$ equals

(a) $-3$
(b) $2058$
(c) $2916$
(d) $20$
(e) $2401$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注
- 来源：《STEP, MAT, TMUA: Skills for Success in University Admissions Tests for Mathematics》第 5 章 Functions 章末 MAT style 第 13 题；解析为书后官方 worked solution。

## 答案
D

## 解析
Completing the square for $\sin^{2}{2x}$ gives

$$
\left( 10\left( \sin^{4}{2x} + \frac{13}{10}\sin^{2}{2x} \right) - 3 \right) = 10\left( \sin^{2}{2x} + \frac{13}{20} \right)^{2} - \frac{289}{40}
$$

This is maximum when $\sin^{2}{2x} = 1$

$$
10\left( 1 + \frac{13}{20} \right)^{2} - \frac{289}{40} = \frac{10 \times 33^{2}}{400} - \frac{289}{40} = \frac{1089 - 289}{40} = \frac{800}{40} = 20
$$

The correct answer is (d)
