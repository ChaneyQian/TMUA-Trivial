## 题目
An A4 sheet of paper has side lengths in the ratio $1 : \sqrt{2}$. The paper is folded as shown in this diagram.

![[Image/SMT-Ch4-Q10-fig1.png]]

If the shorter side is said to have length $1$, what is the perimeter of the trapezium that is created?

(a) $\frac{3 - 2\sqrt{2}}{2}$
(b) $\frac{3(1 + \sqrt{2})}{2}$
(c) $\frac{3(1 - \sqrt{2})}{2}$
(d) $\frac{3 + 2\sqrt{2}}{2}$
(e) $2$

## 答案
B

## 解析
If the paper is unfolded, the following fold lines can be seen. The perimeter of the trapezium $AXDE$ is required.

![[Image/SMT-sol-image10.png]]

There are several $45{^\circ}$ isosceles right-angled triangles that can be seen. The length $AE = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ since it is the hypotenuse of a right-angled triangle with both of the other sides equal to $\frac{1}{2}$. The length $DE = \sqrt{2} - \frac{1}{2}$. The remaining lengths $AX$ and $DX$ require the side lengths of triangle $CXB$ to be known. This is a $45{^\circ}$ isosceles triangle with a right angle at $X$. Let $BC = x$. Using the $\sqrt{2}$ side that includes $x$, $1 - x + \frac{1}{2} = \sqrt{2}$ giving $x = \frac{3}{2} - \sqrt{2} = \frac{3 - 2\sqrt{2}}{2}$. $BX = CX = x\sin{45{^\circ}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\left( \frac{3 - 2\sqrt{2}}{2} \right) = \frac{3\sqrt{2} - 4}{4}$. From this $AX = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} - \frac{3\sqrt{2} - 4}{4} = \frac{4 - \sqrt{2}}{4}$. $DX = \sqrt{2} - \frac{3\sqrt{2} - 4}{4} = \frac{4 + \sqrt{2}}{4}$. The perimeter of the trapezium $= \frac{4 + \sqrt{2}}{4} + \frac{4 - \sqrt{2}}{4} + \sqrt{2} - \frac{1}{2} + \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{3}{2} + \frac{3\sqrt{2}}{2} = \frac{3\left( 1 + \sqrt{2} \right)}{2}$. The correct answer is (b)
