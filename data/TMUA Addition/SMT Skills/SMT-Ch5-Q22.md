## 题目
Let $n$ be a positive integer.
$x^2 + 2$ is a factor of
$$ (x^4 - 5)^n - (x^2 + 1)^{n+1} + (x^2 + 4)^n (3 + x^2)^n \text{ for} $$

(a) odd $n$
(b) even $n$
(c) no $n$
(d) all $n$
(e) $n \ge 5$

### 我的备注

## 答案
A

## 解析
Let $x^{2} = - 2$

By the factor theorem:

$(4 - 5)^{n} - ( - 2 + 1)^{n + 1} + ( - 2 + 4)(3 - 2)^{n} = 0$

$( - 1)^{n} - ( - 1)^{n + 1} + 2 \times 1^{n} = 0$

$2 = ( - 1)^{n + 1} - ( - 1)^{n}$

$2 = ( - 1)^{n}( - 1 - 1)$

$2 = - 2( - 1)^{n}$

This is true if $n$ is odd.

The correct answer is (a)
