## 题目
A circle with equation $x^2 + y^2 - 14x - 2y + 34 = 0$ has a regular octagon drawn inside it so that every vertex of the octagon touches the circle. What (in square units) is the area of the octagon?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 8$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 8\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 16$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 16\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 32$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 32\sqrt{2}$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 48$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad 48\sqrt{2}$$

## 答案
E

## 解析
The centre-radius form of the equation of the circle is $(x - 7)^{2} + (y - 1)^{2} = 16$, so the circle has radius 4. The regular octagon consists of 8 isosceles triangles each formed by joining adjacent vertices to the centre of the circle. The area of each triangle is $\frac{1}{2} \times 4 \times 4 \times \sin 45 = 4\sqrt{2}$, so the area of the octagon is $32\sqrt{2}$. The correct answer is E.
