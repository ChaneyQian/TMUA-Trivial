## 题目
The circles with equations $(x - r)^2 + (y - 2)^2 = r^2$ and $(x + r)^2 + (y + r)^2 = 4r^2$ touch precisely once when

$$\mathbf{A} \quad r = \sqrt{3} - 1$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad r = \frac{8}{5}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad r = \frac{2}{1 + \sqrt{5}}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad r = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad r = \frac{1 + \sqrt{3}}{2}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
Neither circle contains the centre of the other circle and so the circles only touch when the centres are distance $3r$ apart (the sum of their radii). It simplifies the algebra to work with the square of the distance between the centres $(r,2)$ and $( - r, - r)$. Solving $(2r)^{2} + (2 + r)^{2} = 9r^{2}$ gives $r = \frac{1 \pm \sqrt{5}}{2}$ . Since $r > 0$, the correct answer is D.
