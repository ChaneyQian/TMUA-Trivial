## 题目
$x$ and $y$ satisfy $y + |x - 2| \le 1 + |x|$ and $2|x| \le y + 4$. What is the greatest possible value of $|xy|$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad 21$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad 10.5$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 6$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad 2$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad 1.5$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
$y + |x - 2| \leq 1 + |x|$ can be rewritten as $y \leq 1 + |x| - |x - 2|$

This can be sketched using

$y = - 1$ for $x < 0$

$y = 2x - 1$ for $0 \leq x < 2$

$y = 3$ for $x \geq 2$

$2|x| \leq y + 4$ can be rewritten as $y \geq 2|x| - 4$

This can be sketched using

$y = - 2x - 4$ for $x < 0$

$y = 2x - 4$ for $x \geq 0$

![[Image/SMT-sol-image18.png]]Let $|xy| = k$ so either $xy = k$ or $xy = - k$

Consider the graphs of $y = \frac{k}{x}$ and $y = - \frac{k}{x}$ as $k$ increases

The maximum value of $|xy|$ occurs when the line $y = 3$ crosses the line $y = 2x - 4$

At that point $x = \frac{7}{2}$

The maximum value of $|xy|$ is $\frac{21}{2}$

The correct answer is B
