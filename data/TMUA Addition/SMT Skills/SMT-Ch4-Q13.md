## 题目
Given that $f(x) = x^2 - 3x + 9$, what are the coordinates of the turning point of the curve $y = 3 - 2f(x + 3)$?

(a) $\left(3, -\frac{21}{2}\right)$
(b) $\left(\frac{3}{2}, -\frac{21}{2}\right)$
(c) $\left(3, -\frac{27}{2}\right)$
(d) $\left(-\frac{3}{2}, -\frac{21}{2}\right)$
(e) $\left(-\frac{3}{2}, -\frac{27}{2}\right)$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
D

## 解析
$x^{2} - 3x + 9 = \left( x - \frac{3}{2} \right)^{2} - \left( \frac{3}{2} \right)^{2} + 9$ so the turning point of $y = f(x)$ is $(\frac{3}{2},\  - \frac{27}{4})$. Hence the turning point of $y = f(x + 3)$ is $( - \frac{3}{2},\ \frac{27}{4})$, of $y = - 2f(x + 3)$ is $( - \frac{3}{2},\  - \frac{27}{2})$, and of $y = x - 2f(x + 3)$ is $( - \frac{3}{2},\  - \frac{21}{2})$. The correct answer is (d).
