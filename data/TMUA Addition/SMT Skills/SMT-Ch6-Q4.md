## 题目
The trapezium rule approximation for
$\int_0^6 |x(x - 3)(x - 6)| \, \text{d}x$ using four trapezia is equal to

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{35}{2^3}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^4$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{35}{4}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{3^3}{2^5}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^5$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad 30$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注
- 来源：《STEP, MAT, TMUA: Skills for Success in University Admissions Tests for Mathematics》第 6 章 Calculus 章末 TMUA style 第 4 题；解析为书后官方 worked solution。

## 答案
A

## 解析
Let $y = \left| x(x - 3)(x - 6) \right|$.
When $x = 0$, $y = 0$.
When $x = \frac{3}{2}$, $y = \left| \frac{3}{2} \times - \frac{3}{2} \times - \frac{9}{2} \right| = \frac{81}{8}$.
When $x = 3$, $y = 0$.
When $x = \frac{9}{2}$, $y = \left| \frac{9}{2} \times \frac{3}{2} \times - \frac{3}{2} \right| = \frac{81}{8}$.
When $x = 6$, $y = 0$.
Applying the Trapezium Rule to $\int_{0}^{6}\left| x(x - 3)(x - 6) \right|\ dx$, with four trapezia of equal width, gives:
$\frac{1}{2} \times \frac{3}{2}\left( 0 + 2 \times \frac{81}{8} + 2 \times 0 + 2 \times \frac{81}{8} + 0 \right) = \frac{3}{4} \times \frac{81}{2} = \frac{3^{5}}{2^{3}}$.
The answer is A.
