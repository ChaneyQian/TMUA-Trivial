## 题目
Alice and Marcus Praeter are twins who make life very difficult for their parents. Alice tells the truth on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Sunday but lies on every other day of the week. Marcus lies on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday but tells the truth on all other days. Their mother asks both of them if they were lying yesterday. ‘I was!’ says Alice. ‘So was I!’ says Marcus.
What day of the week is it?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad \text{Monday}$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad \text{Tuesday}$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad \text{Wednesday}$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad \text{Thursday}$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad \text{Friday}$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{Saturday}$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Both twins say 'I was lying yesterday', so deal with that claim once and for all. If a twin is truthful today, the claim must be true, so they lied yesterday. If a twin is lying today,
the claim must be false, so they told the truth yesterday. Either way:

> a twin can say 'I was lying yesterday' only on a day when their truthfulness has *changed* since the day before.

Now list the changeover days. Alice tells the truth on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Sunday, and lies on Thursday, Friday and Saturday, so her status changes on **Thursday** (truthful
Wednesday, lying Thursday) and on **Sunday** (lying Saturday, truthful Sunday).

Marcus lies on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday and tells the truth on Thursday to Sunday, so his status changes on **Thursday** (lying Wednesday, truthful Thursday) and on **Monday** (truthful Sunday, lying Monday).

The only day on both lists is Thursday. Checking it directly: on Thursday Alice lies, and her claim that she lied on Wednesday is indeed false because she was truthful on Wednesday; on
Thursday Marcus is truthful, and his claim that he lied on Wednesday is indeed true.

The correct answer is (D).

Sunday is the day on which Alice alone could make the claim, and Monday is the day on which Marcus alone could; they are there to catch anyone who only checks one twin.
