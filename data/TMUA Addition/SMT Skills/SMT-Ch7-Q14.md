## 答案
A

## 解析
$y = \log_{10}\left(x^{4} - 16\right)$ exists only where $x^{4} > 16$, that is $|x| > 2$, and it is an even function of $x$. So the graph consists
of two branches, symmetric in the $y$ axis, with vertical asymptotes $x = -2$ and $x = 2$, and $y \to -\infty$ at each asymptote because the
logarithm's argument tends to $0^{+}$. It crosses the $x$ axis where $x^{4} - 16 = 1$, that is $x = \pm\sqrt[4]{17} \approx \pm 2.03$, and increases without bound as $|x|$ grows.
Graph (b) has only one branch; graph (c) goes to $+\infty$ at the asymptotes, so it is the reflection $y = -\log_{10}\left(x^{4} - 16\right)$;
graphs (d) and (e) have asymptotes at $x = 0, 4$ and at $x = -4, 0$, so they are horizontal translations and are not symmetric in the $y$ axis.
The correct answer is (a).
