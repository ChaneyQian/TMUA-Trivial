## 题目
The function $F(n)$ is defined for all positive integers, $n$ by
$F(1) = 1$ and for $n \ge 2$
$F(n) = 1 + 2F(n - 1)$ if 2 is a factor of $n$ but 3 is **not** a factor of $n$.
$F(n) = 2 + 3F(n - 1)$ if 3 is a factor of $n$ but 2 is **not** a factor of $n$.
$F(n) = 2F(n - 1) - 3$ if both 2 and 3 are factors of $n$.
$F(n) = F(n - 1)$ if neither 2 nor 3 is a factor of $n$.
The value of $F(15)$ equals

(a) $527$
(b) $1051$
(c) $2103$
(d) $6311$
(e) $12623$
