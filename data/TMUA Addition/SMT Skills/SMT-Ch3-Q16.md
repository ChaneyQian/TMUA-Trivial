## 题目
The highest power of $x$ in
$$ \left\{ \left[ (2x^6 + 5)^4 + (2x^5 - 7)^3 \right]^5 + \left[ (3x^7 - 2x^2)^3 + (5x^3 - 6)^4 \right]^6 \right\}^3 $$
is

(a) $75$
(b) $120$
(c) $126$
(d) $360$
(e) $378$

## 备注

### 我的备注

## 答案
E

## 解析
The highest power of $x$ in $\left( 2x^{6} + 5 \right)^{4}$ is $\left( x^{6} \right)^{4} \equiv x^{24}$ and the highest power of $x$ in $\left( 2x^{5} - 7 \right)^{3}$ is $\left( x^{5} \right)^{3} \equiv x^{15}$, so the highest power of $x$ in $\left\lbrack \left( 2x^{6} + 5 \right)^{4} + \left( 2x^{5} - 7 \right)^{3} \right\rbrack^{5}$ is $\left( x^{24} \right)^{5} \equiv x^{120}$.
The highest power of $x$ in $\left( 3x^{7} - 2x^{2} \right)^{3}$ is $\left( x^{7} \right)^{3} \equiv x^{21}$ and the highest power of $x$ in $\left( 5x^{3} - 6 \right)^{4}$ is $\left( x^{3} \right)^{4} \equiv x^{12}$, so the highest power of $x$ in $\left\lbrack \left( 3x^{7} - 2x^{2} \right)^{3} + \left( 5x^{3} - 6 \right)^{4} \right\rbrack^{6}$ is $\left( x^{21} \right)^{6} \equiv x^{126}$.
It follows that the highest power of $x$ in $\left\{ \left\lbrack \left( 2x^{6} + 5 \right)^{4} + \left( 2x^{5} - 7 \right)^{3} \right\rbrack^{5} + \left\lbrack \left( 3x^{7} - 2x^{2} \right)^{3} + \left( 5x^{3} - 6 \right)^{4} \right\rbrack^{6} \right\}^{3}$ is $\left( x^{126} \right)^{3} \equiv x^{378}$.

The answer is (e).
