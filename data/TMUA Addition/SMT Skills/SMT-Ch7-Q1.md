## 题目
$f(x)$ is a quadratic function in $x$.
Given that the graph of $y = f(x)$ passes through the point $(-1, -3)$ and has a vertex at $(1, 5)$, which of the following is an expression for $f(x)$?

$$\mathbf{A} \quad -x^2 + 4x + 3$$

$$\mathbf{B} \quad x^2 - 4x + 3$$

$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2x^2 - 4x + 3$$

$$\mathbf{D} \quad -2x^2 - 4x + 3$$

$$\mathbf{E} \quad -x^2 - 4x + 3$$

$$\mathbf{F} \quad -2x^2 + 4x + 3$$

$$\mathbf{G} \quad 4x - 2x^2$$

$$\mathbf{H} \quad 4x - x^2$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注
- 来源：《STEP, MAT, TMUA: Skills for Success in University Admissions Tests for Mathematics》第 7 章 Graphs 章末 TMUA style 第 1 题；解析为书后官方 worked solution。

## 答案
F

## 解析
Use the vertex form. A quadratic with vertex $(1, 5)$ is $f(x) = a(x - 1)^{2} + 5$, and the point $(-1, -3)$ gives
$$4a + 5 = -3 \quad\Rightarrow\quad a = -2$$
so $f(x) = -2(x - 1)^{2} + 5 = -2x^{2} + 4x + 3$.
The correct answer is (F).
Option D is the same quadratic reflected: $-2x^{2} - 4x + 3 = -2(x + 1)^{2} + 5$ has its vertex at $(-1, 5)$, not $(1, 5)$.
