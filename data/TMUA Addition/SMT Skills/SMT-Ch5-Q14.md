## 题目
As $x$ varies over the real numbers, the least value taken by the function $f$ defined by $f(x) = \left(3\sin^2 x - 2\cos^2 x - 20\sin x + 19\right)^2$ equals

(a) $2$
(b) $4$
(c) $17$
(d) $289$
(e) $1764$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
B

## 解析
Using $\sin^{2}{x + \cos^{2}x} = 1$

$$
\left( 3\sin^{2}x - 2\left( 1 - \sin^{2}x \right) - 20\sin x + 19 \right)^{2}
$$

$$
= \left( 5\sin^{2}x - 20\sin x + 17 \right)^{2}
$$

Completing the square for $\sin x$

$$
= \left( 5{(\sin x - 2)}^{2} - 3 \right)^{2}
$$

This gives its **least** value when $\sin x = 1$

$$
= (5 - 3)^{2} = 4
$$

The correct answer is (b)
