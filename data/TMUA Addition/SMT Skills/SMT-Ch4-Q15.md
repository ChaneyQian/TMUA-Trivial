## 题目
$ABCD$ is a square with vertex $A$ on the circumference of a circle of radius $4$ units. The side lengths of the square are equal to the diameter of the circle. The sides $AB$ and $AD$ of the square intersect the circle as shown in this diagram.

![[Image/SMT-Ch4-Q15-fig1.png]]

Let $X$ be the area of the intersection between the square and the circle.
What is the maximum value of $X$?

(a) $8(1 + \pi)$
(b) $2(4 + \pi)$
(c) $16(1 + \pi)$
(d) $8(2 + \pi)$
(e) $4(2 + \pi)$

## 答案
D

## 解析
The chord formed by joining the points of intersection of the square with the circle is a diameter (as it subtends a right-angle). Hence $X$ is equal to the area of the semi-circle ($8\pi$) plus the area of the triangle formed by the diameter with the point $A$. This is 4 times the perpendicular height of the triangle which varies from 0 (when one side of the square is a tangent) to 4 (when the radius at $A$ is perpendicular to the diameter). Hence the maximum value for $X$ is $8\pi + 16$. The correct answer is (d).
