## 题目
If $22 - |y + 14| = 20$, what is the sum of all possible values of $y$ ?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad -28
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad -16
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad -12
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad -4
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 4
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
A

## 解析
**Official Solution:**

$22 - |y + 14| = 20$;

$|y + 14| = 2$;

$y + 14 = 2$ or $y + 14 = -2$;

$y = -12$ or $y = -16$;

Therefore, the sum of all possible values of $y$ is $-12 + (-16) = -28.$

Answer: A
