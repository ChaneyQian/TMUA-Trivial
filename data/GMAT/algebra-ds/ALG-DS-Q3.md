## 题目
Is $|x + y| < |x| + |y|$ ?

(1) $xy < 0$
(2) $x < y$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \text{Statement (1) ALONE is sufficient but statement (2) ALONE is not sufficient.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \text{Statement (2) ALONE is sufficient but statement (1) ALONE is not sufficient.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \text{BOTH statements TOGETHER are sufficient, but NEITHER statement ALONE is sufficient.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \text{EACH statement ALONE is sufficient.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \text{Statements (1) and (2) TOGETHER are not sufficient.}
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注

## 答案
A

## 解析
**Inofficial Solution:**

Recall that $|x+y|\leq|x|+|y|$. Equality holds when $x$ and $y$ have the same sign, while the strict inequality holds when they have opposite signs.

**Statement (1):**

Since $xy<0$, $x$ and $y$ have opposite signs. Therefore, the strict inequality must hold. Sufficient.

**Statement (2):**

The condition $x<y$ does not determine their signs. For example, $x=-2,y=3$ gives opposite signs, whereas $x=2,y=3$ gives the same sign. Not sufficient.

Answer: A
