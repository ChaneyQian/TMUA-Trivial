## 题目
Two players, A and B, play a series of games. Each game is independent, and player A has a probability $p=\frac23$ of winning any single game. The match ends as soon as player A wins two games in a row. Find the probability that player A wins the match within the first $n$ games, where $n$ is a positive integer.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 1-\frac43\left(\frac23\right)^n+\frac13\left(-\frac13\right)^n $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 1-\left(1-\frac49\right)^n $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 1-\left(\frac13\right)^n $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac49 $$
