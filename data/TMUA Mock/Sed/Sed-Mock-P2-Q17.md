## 题目
The function $$f(x)=A\sin x+B\sin^3x+1$$ has unknown constants $A,B$. A student wishes to deduce the values of $A$ and $B$, thereby deducing $f(x)$. For any chosen value of $x$, they are given the value of $|f(x)|$. The student is given the values of $|f(x)|$ only after choosing all desired inputs for $x$. How many pairs of values $(x,|f(x)|)$ are necessary to **guarantee** that the student can deduce $f(x)$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 2 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 3 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 4 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 5 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 6 $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \text{no number of such pairs guarantees the deduction of} f(x) \text{.} $$
