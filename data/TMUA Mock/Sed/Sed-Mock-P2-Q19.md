## 题目

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{both statements **must** be true} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{one statement **must** be true, one statement **may** be true} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{both statements **may** be true} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{one statement **must** be true, one statement is **never** true} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{one statement **may** be true, one statement is **never** true} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{both statements are **never** true} $$
