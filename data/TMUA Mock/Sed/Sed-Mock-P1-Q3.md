## 题目
A regular hexagon $H$ has centre $O$. Let $L$ be a line through $O$ which rotates clockwise continuously. Denote $\theta$ as the total clockwise angle rotated by $L$. Denote $P,Q$ as the two points at which $L$ intersects $H$. It follows that the graph of the length of $PQ$ against $\theta$ has period:

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 15^\circ $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 30^\circ $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 60^\circ $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 120^\circ $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 180^\circ $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 360^\circ $$
