## 题目
Let $P,Q$ be two *if-then* statements. If $P$ is **sufficient** for $Q$, then:

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{the contrapositive of} Q \text{is sufficient for the contrapositive of} P $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{the negation of} P \text{is insufficient for the negation of} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{the contrapositive of} P \text{is necessary for the negation of} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{if} Q \text{is sufficient for} P \text{, then the negation of} P \text{is necessary and sufficient for the negation of} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{if} P \text{is unnecessary for} Q \text{, then} Q \text{is sufficient for} P $$
