## 题目
You are given that $$\int_0^\pi\sin^5x\,\mathrm{d}x=\frac{16}{15}.$$ Define the total area bounded between the curves $$\begin{cases}y=x\\y=2\sin^5x+x\end{cases}$$ for $0\le x\le a$ as $F(a)$. It follows that $F(a)=16$ when $a$ equals:

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \frac{16\pi}{15} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{8\pi}{15} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 8\pi $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 16\pi $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 15\pi $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \frac{15\pi}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \text{none of the values above.} $$
