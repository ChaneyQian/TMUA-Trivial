## 题目

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad (2,3) $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \left(\frac{41}{22},\frac{19}{11}\right) $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \left(\frac{17}{4},\frac32\right) $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \left(-\frac78,-\frac{15}{4}\right) $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \left(\frac{19}{8},\frac{11}{4}\right) $$
