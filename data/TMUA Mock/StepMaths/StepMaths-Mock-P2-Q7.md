## 题目
Use some or all of the lines below to prove the inequality $$(a+b)\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{4}{b}\right)\ge 9$$ holds if $a>0$ and $b>0$. 1. $(a+b)\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{4}{b}\right)\ge 9\iff \frac{b}{a}+\frac{4a}{b}+5\ge 9$ 2. $\frac{b}{a}+\frac{4a}{b}+5\ge 9\iff \frac{b}{a}+\frac{4a}{b}-4\ge 0$ 3. $\frac{b}{a}+\frac{4a}{b}-4\ge 0\iff \frac{4a^2-4ab+b^2}{ab}\ge 0$ 4. $\frac{b}{a}+\frac{4a}{b}-4\ge 0\iff \frac{4a^2-4ab+b^2}{ab}\ge 0$ if and only if $ab>0$ 5. $\frac{4a^2-4ab+b^2}{ab}\ge 0\iff \frac{(2a-b)^2}{ab}\ge 0$ 6. We have $\frac{(2a-b)^2}{ab}\ge 0$ if and only if $a>0$ and $b>0$ 7. We have $\frac{(2a-b)^2}{ab}\ge 0$ if $a>0$ and $b>0$ 8. Therefore, $(a+b)\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{4}{b}\right)\ge 9$ if $a>0$ and $b>0$ 9. Therefore, since $a>0$ and $b>0$, $(a+b)\left(\frac{1}{a}+\frac{4}{b}\right)\ge 9$
