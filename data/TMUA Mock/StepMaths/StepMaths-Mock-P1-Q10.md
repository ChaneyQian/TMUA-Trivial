## 题目
The equation $\sin(x)=kx$ has $5$ solutions, where $k>0$. What is the simplest complete set of conditions that $k$ satisfies when this is true? (You may use the fact that $\frac{d}{dx}\sin x=\cos x$.)

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 2\pi<\frac{\sqrt{1-k^2}}{k}<\frac{5\pi}{2} \text{and} \tan(\arccos(k))=\arccos(k) $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 2\pi<\frac{\sqrt{1-k^2}}{k}<\frac{5\pi}{2} \text{and} \tan\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{1-k^2}}{k}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{1-k^2}}{k} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 2\pi<\frac{\sqrt{1-k^2}}{k}<\frac{5\pi}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \tan(\arccos(k))=\arccos(k) $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \tan\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{1-k^2}}{k}\right)=\frac{\sqrt{1-k^2}}{k} $$
