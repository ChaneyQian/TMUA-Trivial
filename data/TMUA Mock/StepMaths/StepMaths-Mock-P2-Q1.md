## 题目
A letter L is printed somewhere on the $x$-$y$ plane. It then undergoes three transformations: it is rotated anti-clockwise about the origin by $\frac{\pi}{4}$ radians, then it is reflected in the $x$-axis, and finally it is rotated clockwise about the origin by $\frac{\pi}{4}$ radians. Which of the following transformations should be used to undo the change to the L?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{Rotate clockwise about the origin by} \frac{\pi}{2} \text{radians} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{Rotate clockwise about the origin by} \frac{\pi}{4} \text{radians, then reflect in the} x \text{-axis, then rotate anti-clockwise about the origin by} \frac{\pi}{4} \text{radians} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{Reflect in the line} y=x $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{Reflect in the line} y=-x $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{Reflect in the} x \text{-axis} $$
