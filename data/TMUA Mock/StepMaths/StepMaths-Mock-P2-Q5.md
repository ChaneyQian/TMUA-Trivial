## 题目
Claim: For any real function $f$ and real number $x$, if $f(f(x))=x$, then $f(x)=f^{-1}(x)$. Which of the following is a counter example to the above claim?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad f(x)=x\ \forall x\in\mathbb{R},\quad x=-1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad f(x)=\frac{1}{x}\ \forall x>0,\quad x=1 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad f(x)=\frac{1}{x}\ \forall x>0,\quad x=-1 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad f(x)=1\ \forall x\in\mathbb{R},\quad x=-1 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad f(x)=1\ \forall x\in\mathbb{R},\quad x=1 $$
