## 题目
A quadrilateral has side lengths $1$, $5$, $7$, and $x$. Which of the following values of $x$ is not possible?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 12 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 7 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 2 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 0.5 $$
