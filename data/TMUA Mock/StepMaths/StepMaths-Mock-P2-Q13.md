## 题目
Consider the false claim $\frac{d(x^2)}{dx}=x$. Below is an attempt at the proof. 1. We can write $x^2$ as $x+x+x+\ldots$ ($x$ times) for any $x$ 2. Given (1), we have $\frac{d(x^2)}{dx}=\frac{d}{dx}(x+x+x+x+\ldots)$ ($x$ times) 3. And $\frac{d(x)}{dx}+\frac{d(x)}{dx}+\frac{d(x)}{dx}\ldots$ ($x$ times) simplifies to $1+1+1+1\ldots$ ($x$ times) 5. $\ldots$ which is equal to $x$ Which of the above steps is/are wrong?
