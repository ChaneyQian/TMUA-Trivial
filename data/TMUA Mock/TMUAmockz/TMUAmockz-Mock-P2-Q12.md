## 题目
A student claims that $\log_3(x-4)+\log_3(22-x)<4$ holds for all integers $x$ with $4<x<22$. Is the student correct? If not, state the integer values for which the inequality fails and find the total number of integers with $4<x<22$ for which it does hold.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{The student is correct; all 17 integers satisfy the inequality} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{The student is incorrect; the inequality fails for} x=4 \text{and} x=22 \text{only} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{The student is incorrect; the inequality fails for 2 integers} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{The student is incorrect; the inequality fails for exactly 1 integer, giving 16 valid values} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{The student is incorrect; the inequality fails for 3 integers} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{The student is incorrect; the inequality fails for 4 integers} $$
