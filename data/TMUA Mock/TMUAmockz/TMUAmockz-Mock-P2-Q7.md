## 题目

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad m=20 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad m^2-40m+36=0 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad m^2-40m+144=0 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad m^2-20m+36=0 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad m=40 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad m^2-20m+144=0 $$
