## 题目
The equation $ax^2+bx+c=0$, where $a,b,c$ are real and $a\ne 0$, is said to have property R if it has two distinct real roots. Consider the following four statements. I. $b^2-4ac>0$ is necessary and sufficient for property R. II. $a$ and $c$ having opposite signs is a sufficient condition for property R. III. $a$ and $c$ having opposite signs is a necessary condition for property R. IV. $b\ne 0$ is a necessary condition for property R.

$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{I, II and III only} $$
