## 题目
Let $p$ and $q$ be distinct positive real numbers, and define $I=\int_0^1 x^p\,\mathrm{d}x$ and $J=\int_0^1 x^{1/q}\,\mathrm{d}x$. Which of the following is a necessary and sufficient condition for $I>J$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad p<q $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad p+q<2 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad pq<1 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad p<1 \text{and} q>1 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad pq>1 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad p<q<1 $$
