## 题目
Let $x$ be an integer. Consider the expression $$\frac{5x}{12}+\frac{x}{8}.$$ Which of the following statements about $x$ are true whenever this expression is an integer? I $x$ being a multiple of $24$ is both necessary and sufficient for the expression to be an integer. II $x$ being a multiple of $12$ is sufficient for the expression to be an integer. III $x$ must be a multiple of $8$. IV $x^2$ must be a multiple of $576$.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{I and III only} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{I and IV only} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{I, III and IV only} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{I, II, III and IV} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{II, III and IV only} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{III and IV only} $$
