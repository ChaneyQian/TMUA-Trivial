## 题目
An ant sits on the surface of a regular tetrahedron with edges of length $1$. It crawls along the surface from the midpoint of one edge to the midpoint of the opposite edge by the shortest possible route. (Two edges are opposite if they share no common vertex.) What is the length of the shortest such route?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \frac{\sqrt3}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \sqrt2 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{3}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \sqrt3 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 2 $$
