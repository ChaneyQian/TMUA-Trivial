## 题目
The distinct points $P$ and $Q$ with coordinates $(p^2,2p)$ and $(q^2,2q)$ respectively lie on the curve $C$ with equation $y^2=4x$. Consider the following two statements: S: The chord $PQ$ passes through the point $(-1,0)$ T: $p^2q^2=1$ Which of the following correctly describes the relationship between S and T?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{S is not necessary and not sufficient for T} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{S is necessary but not sufficient for T} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{S is sufficient but not necessary for T} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{S is necessary and sufficient for T} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{T is sufficient but not necessary for S} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{S is necessary and sufficient for T, but only when} p,q>0 $$
