## 题目
Three circles with radii $p$, $q$ and $r$, where $p<q<r$, are mutually externally tangent. Let $P$, $Q$, $R$ denote their respective centres. Which of the following is a necessary and sufficient condition for the triangle $PQR$ to have a right angle at $P$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad p+q=2r $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad q^2=pr $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad (p+q)(p+r)=2qr $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad p(q+r)=2qr $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad (p+q+r)^2=2qr $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad p(p+q+r)=2qr $$
