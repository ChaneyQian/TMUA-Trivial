## 题目
Consider the statement $(*)$ about a real number $x$: There exists a real number $y$ such that $|x+y|<y$. Which one of the following describes all real values of $x$ for which $(*)$ is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{no values of} x $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{exactly one value of} x $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{all real} x \text{with} x<0 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{all real} x \text{with} x>0 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{all real values of} x $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{all real} x \text{except exactly one value} $$
