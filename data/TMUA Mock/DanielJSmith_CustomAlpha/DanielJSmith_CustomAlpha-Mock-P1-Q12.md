## 题目
Fix distinct real numbers $a\ne b$ with $a<b$. Consider the equation $$|x-a|+|x-b|=|a-b|$$ Which of the following describes the complete solution set for $x$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad x=\frac{a+b}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad x=a \text{**or**} x=b $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{all real} x \text{with} a\le x\le b $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{all real} x $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{no real solutions} $$
