## 题目
Let $n>1$ be an integer. Consider the property $P(n)$: For all integers $a,b$, **if** $n\mid ab$ **then** $n\mid a$ **or** $n\mid b$. Which of the following is correct?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad P(n) \text{holds **if and only if**} n \text{is prime} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad P(n) \text{holds **if and only if**} n \text{is squarefree} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad P(n) \text{is **sufficient** but **not necessary** for} n \text{to be prime} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad P(n) \text{holds **if and only if**} n \text{is a prime power} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad P(n) \text{holds **if and only if**} n \text{has at most two distinct prime factors} $$
