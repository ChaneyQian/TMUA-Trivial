## 题目
Let $f(x)$, $g(x)$, $h(x)$ and $k(x)$ be real functions and consider the statement $(*)$ If $0<x<1$ and $f(x)>g(x)$, then $h(x)=0$ or $k(x)\ge 5$. Which of the following statements is the *contrapositive* of $(*)$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{If} h(x)\ne 0 \text{or} k(x)<5 \text{then} x\le 0 \text{or} x\ge 1 \text{or} f(x)\le g(x) \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{If} h(x)\ne 0 \text{and} k(x)<5 \text{then} x\le 0 \text{or} x\ge 1 \text{and} f(x)\le g(x) \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{If} h(x)\ne 0 \text{and} k(x)<5 \text{then} x\le 0 \text{or} x\ge 1 \text{or} f(x)\le g(x) \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{If} x\le 0 \text{or} x\ge 1 \text{or} f(x)\le g(x) \text{then} h(x)\ne 0 \text{and} k(x)<5 \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{If} h(x)=0 \text{or} k(x)\ge 5 \text{then} 0<x<1 \text{and} f(x)>g(x) \text{.} $$
