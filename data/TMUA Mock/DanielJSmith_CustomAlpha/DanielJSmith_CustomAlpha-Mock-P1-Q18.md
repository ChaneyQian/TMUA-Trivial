## 题目
Let $f(x)$, $g(x)$ and $h(x)$ be real functions and consider the statement $(*)$ If $x>0$ and $f(x)\le 0$ then $g(x)\ne 1$ or $h(x)\ge -1$. Which of the following statements is the *contrapositive* of $(*)$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{If} g(x)=1 \text{and} h(x)<-1 \text{then} x\le 0 \text{or} f(x)>0 \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{If} g(x)=1 \text{or} h(x)<-1 \text{then} x\le 0 \text{or} f(x)>0 \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{If} x\le 0 \text{or} f(x)>0 \text{then} g(x)=1 \text{and} h(x)<-1 \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{If} g(x)\ne 1 \text{or} h(x)\ge -1 \text{then} x>0 \text{and} f(x)\le 0 \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{If} x>0 \text{and} f(x)\le 0 \text{then} g(x)=1 \text{and} h(x)<-1 \text{.} $$
