## 题目
Let $x$ and $y$ be real numbers. Which of the following is a necessary and sufficient condition for $|x-y|=|x|+|y|$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad x\ge0 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad x+y\ge0 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad x+y+|x-y|\ge0 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad xy\ge0 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad x^2+y^2-2xy\ge0 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad x-y\ge0 $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad |x|\ge|y| $$
$$ \mathbf{H} \quad xy\le0 $$
