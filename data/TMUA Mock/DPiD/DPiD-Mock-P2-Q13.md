## 题目
Let $n$ be a positive integer and define $I_n=\int_0^1 x^n e^x\,\mathrm{d}x$. Which of the following statements is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad I_1=1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad I_1=e-1 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{The sequence} (I_n) \text{is increasing.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad I_n\to e \text{as} n\to\infty \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{e}{n+1}\le I_n\le\frac{1}{n+1} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad I_1=e $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \text{The sequence} (I_n) \text{does not tend to 0.} $$
$$ \mathbf{H} \quad I_n\ge1 \text{for all} n \text{.} $$
