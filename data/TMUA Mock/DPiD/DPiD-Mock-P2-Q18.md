## 题目
Newton has a wardrobe containing 7 hats of 3 different types: 3 Einstein hats, 2 Euler hats, and 2 Newton hats. He repeatedly takes out 2 hats at a time from the wardrobe at random, and stops if the two hats are of the same type. However, Newton is “Einstein-Euler type-blind”, so he also stops if he takes out one Einstein hat and one Euler hat. What is the probability that he stops with 2 hats of the same type? Assume he returns both hats to the wardrobe after each step.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \frac{3}{7} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{5}{11} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{5}{21} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{6}{11} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{1}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \frac{5}{16} $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \frac{7}{16} $$
$$ \mathbf{H} \quad \frac{11}{21} $$
