## 题目
Let $A$ and $B$ be subsets of the $xy$-plane, and consider the following statements. $P$: For all real numbers $x$ and $y$, $(x,y)$ is in $A$ if and only if $(x,y)$ is in $B$. $Q$: There exists a point $(x,y)$ that lies in both $A$ and $B$. Which of the following statements is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad P \text{is necessary and sufficient for} Q \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad P \text{is necessary but not sufficient for} Q \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad P \text{is sufficient but not necessary for} Q \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad P \text{is not necessary and not sufficient for} Q \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad Q \text{is sufficient but not necessary for} P \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad P \text{and} Q \text{are logically equivalent.} $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad Q \text{implies} P \text{whenever} A \text{is non-empty.} $$
$$ \mathbf{H} \quad \text{Both} P \text{and} Q \text{are always true.} $$
