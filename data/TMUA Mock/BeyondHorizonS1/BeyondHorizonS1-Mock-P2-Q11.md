## 题目
Mr. Earl E. Bird leaves home every day at 8:00 AM to go to work. If he drives at an average speed of 40 miles per hour, he will be late by 3 minutes. If he drives at an average speed of 60 miles per hour, he will be early by 3 minutes. How many miles per hour does Mr. Bird need to drive to get to work exactly on time?
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 45$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 48$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 50$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 55$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 58$$

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Let $d$ be the distance in miles and $t$ the on-time journey duration in hours, and note that three minutes is $\tfrac{1}{20}$ of an hour. The two scenarios give $\frac{d}{40}=t+\frac{1}{20}$ and $\frac{d}{60}=t-\frac{1}{20}$, and subtracting eliminates $t$ to give $d\left(\frac{1}{40}-\frac{1}{60}\right)=\frac{1}{10}$, that is $\frac{d}{120}=\frac{1}{10}$ and so $d=12$. Substituting back gives $t=\frac{12}{60}+\frac{1}{20}=\frac{1}{4}$ hour, so the required speed is $\frac{12}{1/4}=48$ miles per hour. The answer is B.
