## 题目
What is the sum of the coefficients in $\left( \frac{2}{x} + \frac{3}{x^2} + 5x \right)^3$?
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 1000$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 750$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 125$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 343$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 100$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad 729$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Expanding produces a sum of terms of the form $c_k x^{n_k}$, and the sum of all the coefficients $c_k$ is obtained simply by setting $x = 1$, since every power of $x$ then becomes $1$. Substituting gives
$$\left( \frac{2}{1} + \frac{3}{1} + 5 \right)^3 = 10^3 = 1000$$
The answer is A. Option C is the trap of cubing only the $5x$ term, and option F is $9^3$, obtained by mis-adding the three coefficients to $9$ instead of $10$.
