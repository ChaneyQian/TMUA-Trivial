## 题目
The mean, median, unique mode, and range of a collection of eight integers are all equal to 8. The largest integer that can be an element of this collection is:
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 11$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 12$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 13$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 14$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 15$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Write the collection in increasing order as $a_1\leq\cdots\leq a_8$, so that the mean condition gives $\sum a_i=64$, the median condition gives $a_4+a_5=16$, and the range condition gives $a_8-a_1=8$. The value $14$ is attainable: the collection $6,6,6,8,8,8,8,14$ has sum $64$, median $\tfrac{8+8}{2}=8$, range $14-6=8$, and $8$ occurs four times against three occurrences of $6$, so $8$ is the unique mode. To see that $15$ is impossible, note that it would force $a_1=7$ and hence every entry to be at least $7$, giving a minimum total of $56$; the single entry $15$ already consumes all $64-56=8$ units of surplus, so every other entry would have to equal $7$, making $a_4+a_5=14$ rather than $16$. The answer is D.
