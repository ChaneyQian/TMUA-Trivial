## 题目
The complete set of values of $x$ for which $(x^2-1)(x-2)>0$ is:
$$\mathbf{A} \quad x<-1,\ 1<x<2$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad x<-1,\ x>2$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad -1<x<2$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad x<1,\ x>2$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad -1<x<1,\ x>2$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Factorising gives $(x-1)(x+1)(x-2)>0$, so the critical values are $x=-1$, $x=1$ and $x=2$, and the sign of the product changes at each of them since each factor is linear. For $x>2$ all three factors are positive, so the product is positive. Moving left across $x=2$ the product becomes negative on $1<x<2$, then positive again on $-1<x<1$, then negative on $x<-1$. Hence the product is strictly positive exactly on $-1<x<1$ together with $x>2$; a quick check at $x=0$ gives $(-1)(-2)=2>0$ and at $x=3$ gives $(8)(1)=8>0$, confirming both pieces. The answer is E.
