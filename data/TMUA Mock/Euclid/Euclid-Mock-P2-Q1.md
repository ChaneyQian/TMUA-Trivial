## 题目
Let $x, y > 0$ and $$x + y = 1.$$ The following statements may be used to prove $$\frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} \ge 4.$$ $$P: \quad (x - y)^2 \ge 0,$$ $$Q: \quad x^2 + y^2 \ge 2xy,$$ $$R: \quad (x + y)^2 \ge 4xy,$$ $$S: \quad 1 \ge 4xy,$$ $$T: \quad \frac{1}{xy} \ge 4,$$ $$U: \quad \frac{1}{x} + \frac{1}{y} = \frac{x + y}{xy} = \frac{1}{xy}.$$ Which ordering gives a valid proof?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad P, Q, R, S, T, U $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad P, R, Q, S, U, T $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad Q, P, S, R, T, U $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad R, P, Q, T, S, U $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad U, T, S, R, Q, P $$
