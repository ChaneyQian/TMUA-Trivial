## 题目
The trapezium rule, using any positive number of equal-width strips, is applied to $$\int_0^2 \left(x^4 - 4x^3 + 6x^2\right)\,\mathrm{d}x.$$ Which statement is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{It always gives an overestimate.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{It always gives an underestimate.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{It is exact only when the number of strips is even.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{It is exact for every number of strips.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{It gives an overestimate for an odd number of strips and an underestimate for an even number.} $$
