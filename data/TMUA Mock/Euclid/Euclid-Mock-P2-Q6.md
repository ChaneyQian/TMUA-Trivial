## 题目
A student calculates $$n^2 + n + 11$$ for $n = 0, 1, 2, 3$ and obtains four prime numbers. The student conjectures that the expression is prime for every integer $n \ge 0$. Which statements are true? I. $n = 10$ is a counterexample. II. Every positive integer $n$ that leaves remainder $0$ or $10$ when divided by $11$ is a counterexample. III. Every counterexample must leave remainder $0$ or $10$ when divided by $11$.
