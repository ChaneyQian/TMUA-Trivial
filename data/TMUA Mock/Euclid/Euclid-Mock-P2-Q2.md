## 题目
A sequence $(a_n)$ satisfies $$a_{n+2} = a_{n+1} + a_n$$ for every positive integer $n$. A student claims: If $a_1$ is even, then every term of the sequence is even. The student writes: 1. $a_1$ is even. 2. Assume $a_k$ is even. 3. Then $a_{k+1} = a_k + a_{k-1}$, which is the sum of two even numbers. 4. Therefore $a_{k+1}$ is even, completing the induction. What is the main flaw?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{The recurrence is valid only when} n \text{is even.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{The value of} a_2 \text{has not been shown to be even.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{An even number plus an even number can be odd.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{Mathematical induction cannot be applied to sequences.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{There is no flaw.} $$
