## 题目
A quartic polynomial $P$ has leading coefficient $4$ and satisfies $$P(x) \ge 0$$ for every real $x$. It has exactly two distinct real roots and also satisfies $$P(1) = P(-1) \qquad \text{and} \qquad P(2) - P(-2) = 192.$$ The three stationary points of the curve $y = P(x)$ form a triangle. What is the area of this triangle?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 8 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 8\sqrt{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 16 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 16\sqrt{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 24 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 24\sqrt{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad 32 $$
