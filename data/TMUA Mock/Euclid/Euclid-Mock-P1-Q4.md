## 题目
For each positive integer $n$, define $$a_n = \sqrt{n}\left(\sqrt{n+1} - \sqrt{n}\right).$$ Which of the following is true for every positive integer $n$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 0 < a_n \le \frac{2}{5} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{2}{5} < a_n < \frac{1}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{1}{2} < a_n < \frac{3}{5} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{3}{5} < a_n < 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad a_n > 1 $$
