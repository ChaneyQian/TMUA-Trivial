## 题目
The following lines can be arranged to prove that there are no positive integers $a$ and $b$ satisfying $$a^2 = 2b^2.$$ **P.** Suppose that such pairs exist, and choose one for which $a$ is as small as possible. **Q.** Since $a^2$ is even, $a$ is even; write $a = 2c$. **R.** Substitution gives $b^2 = 2c^2$. **S.** Hence $b$ is even; write $b = 2d$. **T.** Substitution now gives $c^2 = 2d^2$. **U.** But $c = a/2 < a$, so $(c, d)$ is a smaller positive-integer solution.
