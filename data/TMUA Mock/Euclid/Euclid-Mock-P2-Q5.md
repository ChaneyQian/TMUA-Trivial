## 题目

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad P \text{is necessary and sufficient for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad P \text{is sufficient but not necessary for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad P \text{is necessary but not sufficient for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad P \text{is neither necessary nor sufficient for} Q $$
