## 题目
For a real parameter $p$, consider the two curves $$C_p : \quad (|x|-1)^2 + y^2 = (p+1)^2$$ and $$D_p : \quad x^2 + (|y|-1)^2 = (p+1)^2.$$ The curves intersect at eight distinct points. The four intersection points nearest the origin are the vertices of a square $S$, while the other four are the vertices of a square $T$. It is given that $$\mathrm{Area}(T) = 9\,\mathrm{Area}(S).$$ Find the value of $p^2 + 2p$.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad -\frac{7}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad -\frac{5}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad -\frac{3}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad -\frac{1}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{1}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \frac{3}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \frac{5}{8} $$
$$ \mathbf{H} \quad \frac{7}{8} $$
