## 题目
Which of these statements is true?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \sqrt{361} \text{ is irrational}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \sqrt{x^2} = x \text{ for all real } x
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad f(x) = 3x^2 - x + 5x^{\frac{1}{2}} + 1 \text{ is a polynomial function}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{195}{13} \text{ is an integer}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 2 + 2 = 5
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \ln(-1) = \pi
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
Check each statement in turn.

$\sqrt{361} = 19$, an integer, so A is false. $\sqrt{x^{2}} = |x|$, which equals $-x$ rather than $x$ whenever $x < 0$, so B is false. A polynomial may only have non-negative integer powers of $x$, and $5x^{1/2}$ breaks that, so C is false. Plainly $2 + 2 = 4$, so E is false. And $\ln$ is undefined at negative arguments over the reals, so F is false.

That leaves D, and indeed $\frac{195}{13} = 15$. The answer is D.
