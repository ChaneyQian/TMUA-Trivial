## 题目
Which of these values is the smallest?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \sin(1)
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \cos(\frac{1}{2})
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 0.88
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{1}{2}\tan(\frac{\pi}{3})
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{2}{\sqrt{5}}
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
All five values are close to $0.88$, so compare them carefully. Note $1$ radian is about $57.3^{\circ}$.

$\sin 1 \approx 0.8415$; $\cos\frac12 \approx 1 - \frac{(1/2)^{2}}{2} = 0.875$ (more precisely $0.8776$); $\frac{1}{2}\tan\frac{\pi}{3} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \approx 0.8660$; and $\frac{2}{\sqrt5} = \frac{2\sqrt5}{5} \approx 0.8944$.

So the ordering is $\sin 1 < \frac{\sqrt3}{2} < \cos\frac12 < 0.88 < \frac{2}{\sqrt5}$, and the smallest is $\sin 1$.

A calculator-free check that $\sin 1 < \frac{\sqrt3}{2}$: $\frac{\sqrt3}{2} = \sin\frac{\pi}{3}$ and $\sin$ increases on $\bigl[0, \frac{\pi}{2}\bigr]$, so it is enough that $1 < \frac{\pi}{3} \approx 1.047$. The answer is A.
