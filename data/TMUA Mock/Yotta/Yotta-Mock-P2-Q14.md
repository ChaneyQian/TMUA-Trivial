## 题目
Which of these numbers is the smallest?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \ln(9)
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \pi^{\log_3(2)}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{1337}{668}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad -2\cos(3.14)
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \sqrt[3]{5}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 3^{\sin(\frac{25\pi}{6})}
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Estimate each. $\ln 9 \approx 2.20$; $\pi^{\log_{3} 2} \approx \pi^{0.63} \approx 2.06$; $\frac{1337}{668} \approx 2.0015$; and $3.14$ is just under $\pi$, so $\cos(3.14) \approx -1$ and $-2\cos(3.14) \approx 2.0$. Those four all sit at or above $2$.

The two small ones are $\sqrt[3]{5} \approx 1.71$ and, since $\frac{25\pi}{6} = 4\pi + \frac{\pi}{6}$,

$$ 3^{\sin(25\pi/6)} = 3^{\sin(\pi/6)} = 3^{1/2} = \sqrt{3} \approx 1.73 $$

To separate these two without a calculator, raise both to the sixth power:

$$ \left(\sqrt[3]{5}\right)^{6} = 5^{2} = 25 < 27 = 3^{3} = \left(\sqrt{3}\right)^{6} $$

so $\sqrt[3]{5} < \sqrt{3}$. The answer is E.
