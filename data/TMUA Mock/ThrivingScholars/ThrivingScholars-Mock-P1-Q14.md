## 题目
The function $f$ is defined by $f(x) = \frac{x}{x + 2}$. What points $(x,y)$ on the graph of $f$ have the property that the line tangent to $f$ at $(x,y)$ has slope $\frac{1}{2}$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad (0,0) \text{ only}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad (\frac{1}{2}, \frac{1}{5}) \text{ only}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad (0,0) \text{ and } (-4, 2)
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad (0,0) \text{ and } (4, \frac{2}{3})
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \text{There are no such points.}
$$
