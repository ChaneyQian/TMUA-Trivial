## 题目
Let $g$ be a function with first derivative given by $g'(x) = \int_{0}^{x} e^{-t^2} \,dt$. Which of the following must be true on the interval $0 < x < 2$?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad g \text{ is increasing, and the graph of } g \text{ is concave up.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad g \text{ is increasing, and the graph of } g \text{ is concave down.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad g \text{ is decreasing, and the graph of } g \text{ is concave up.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad g \text{ is decreasing, and the graph of } g \text{ is concave down.}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad g \text{ is decreasing, and the graph of } g \text{ has a point of inflection on } 0 < x < 2 \text{.}
$$
