## 题目
Consider the following statement about the polynomial $p(x)$ where $a$ and $b$ are real numbers with $a<b$: (*) There exists a number $c$ with $a<c<b$ such that $p(c)=0$. Which of the following is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{The condition} p(a)p(b)<0 \text{is necessary and sufficient for (*)} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{The condition} p(a)p(b)<0 \text{is not necessary and not sufficient for (*)} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{The condition} p(a)p(b)<0 \text{is necessary but not sufficient for (*)} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{The condition} p(a)p(b)<0 \text{is sufficient but not necessary for (*)} $$
