## 题目
Let $P_1(x)$, $P_2(x)$ and $P_3(x)$ be three quadratic polynomials with real coefficients. The polynomial $Q(x)=P_1(x)P_2(x)P_3(x)$ has exactly 5 distinct real roots. Let $\Delta_i$ be the discriminant of $P_i(x)$ for $i=1,2,3$. Which of the following statements must be true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \Delta_1\Delta_2\Delta_3<0 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \Delta_1\Delta_2\Delta_3=0 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \Delta_1\Delta_2\Delta_3>0 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \Delta_1\Delta_2\Delta_3\ge 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{There is insufficient information to determine a correct answer.} $$
