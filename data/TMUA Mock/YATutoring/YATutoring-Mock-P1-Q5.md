## 题目
A sequence is defined by $u_n=2^{3n-1}$ for integers $n\ge 1$. Which of the following statements correctly describes the sequence defined by $v_n=\log_4(u_n)$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{It is an arithmetic sequence with a common difference of 3.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{It is an arithmetic sequence with a common difference of 1.5.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{It is a geometric sequence with a common ratio of 1.5.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{It is a geometric sequence with a common ratio of 3.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{It is neither an arithmetic sequence nor a geometric sequence.} $$
