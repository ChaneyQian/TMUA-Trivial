## 题目
Let $x$ be a real number. Which one of the following statements is a sufficient condition for exactly three of the other four statements?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad x\geq 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad x=1 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad x=0 \text{or} x=1 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad x\geq 0 \text{or} x\leq 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad x\geq 0 \text{and} x\leq 1 $$
