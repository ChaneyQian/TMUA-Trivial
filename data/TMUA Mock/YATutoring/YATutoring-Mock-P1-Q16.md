## 题目
A function $f$ is defined for all real numbers and has the property that $f(x+y)=f(x)f(y)$ for all real numbers $x$ and $y$. It is given that there exists at least one real number $a$ such that $f(a)\ne 0$. If $f(4)=16$ which of the following is the only possible value for $f(-2)$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad -4 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad -\frac14 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac1{16} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac14 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 4 $$
