## 题目
A list consists of $k$ integers, and the mean of these is calculated to be $m$. When an integer $a$ is added to this list, the mean decreases by 1. When a further integer $b$ is added to the new list, the mean decreases again by another 1. Which one of the following statements is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad m=k+4 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad k<m $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad a+b=2(m-k) $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad a(m-1)=b(m-2) $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad a-b=2 $$
