## 题目
A locked box has two levers, A and B, which can be positioned either left or right at any particular time. It is known that if lever A is left or lever B is right, then the box is unlocked. Which of the following statements must be true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{If the box is unlocked then lever A is left or lever B is right} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{If the box is locked then lever A is left and lever B is right} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{If the box is unlocked then lever A is left and lever B is right} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{If the box is locked then lever A is right or lever B is left} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{If the box is unlocked then lever A is right or lever B is left} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{If the box is locked then lever A is right and lever B is left} $$
