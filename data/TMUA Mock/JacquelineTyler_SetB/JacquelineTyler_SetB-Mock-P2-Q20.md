$$ \mathbf{D} \quad a\ge b \text{if} f(a)\ge f(b) $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad a<b \text{only if} f(a)\ge f(b) $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad a<b \text{only if} f(a)<f(b) $$
