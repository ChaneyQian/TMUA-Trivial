## 题目
The curve $C$ is defined by
$$ x = y + \sqrt{y} + 1. $$
Find the equation of the tangent to $C$ at the point where $x = 3$.
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad y = \frac{2}{3}x - 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad y = \frac{3}{2}x - \frac{7}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad y = \frac{1}{2}x - \frac{1}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad y = \frac{3}{4}x - \frac{5}{4} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad y = -\frac{2}{3}x + 3 $$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Just interchange $x$ and $y$, it becomes a standard question, just remember to change it back at the end!

Interchange $x$ and $y$. The resulting curve is
$$ y = x + \sqrt{x} + 1. $$
The point on the original curve with $x = 3$ satisfies
$$ 3 = y + \sqrt{y} + 1. $$
Hence $y + \sqrt{y} = 2$, so $y = 1$. Therefore, after interchanging $x$ and $y$, the corresponding point is $(1, 3)$.

For the swapped curve,
$$ \frac{dy}{dx} = 1 + \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}. $$
At $x = 1$,
$$ \frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{3}{2}. $$
Therefore, the tangent to the swapped curve is
$$ y - 3 = \frac{3}{2}(x - 1). $$
Interchanging $x$ and $y$ back again gives
$$ x - 3 = \frac{3}{2}(y - 1). $$
Making $y$ the subject,
$$ y - 1 = \frac{2}{3}(x - 3), $$
so
$$ y = \frac{2}{3}x - 1. $$
