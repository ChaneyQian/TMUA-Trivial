## 题目
An arithmetic progression has $(2n + 1)$ terms, where $n$ is a positive integer. Let $S_o$ denote the sum of the terms in odd-numbered positions (the 1st, 3rd, 5th, ... terms) and let $S_e$ denote the sum of the terms in even-numbered positions (the 2nd, 4th, 6th, ... terms).

It is given that
$$ S_o + S_e = 505 \quad \text{and} \quad S_o - S_e = 5. $$

What is the value of $n$?
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 40 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 50 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 51 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 55 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 56 $$

## 答案
B

## 解析
Since there are an odd number $2n + 1$ terms, there must be a middle term, call it $M$, then $S_o = (n + 1)M$ and $S_e = nM$. Therefore
$$ S_o + S_e = (n + 1)M + nM = (2n + 1)M, $$
$$ S_o - S_e = (n + 1)M - nM = M. $$

From $S_o - S_e = 5$ we get $M = 5$. Substituting into $S_o + S_e = 505$ gives $(2n + 1) \cdot 5 = 505$, so $2n + 1 = 101$ and hence $n = 50$.
