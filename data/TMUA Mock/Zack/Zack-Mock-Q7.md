## 题目
Alvin and Simon can create a song in 5 days. Alvin and Theodore can create a song in 8 days. Simon and Theodore can create a song in 10 days. Approximately how many days does it take Alvin, Simon and Theodore to make a song together?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 3.2
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 3.5
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 3.7
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 4.7
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
Work in rates (songs per day). If $A$, $S$, $T$ are the three individual rates, the given information is

$$ A+S = \frac15, \qquad A+T = \frac18, \qquad S+T = \frac1{10} $$

Adding all three counts each person twice:

$$ 2(A+S+T) = \frac15+\frac18+\frac1{10} = \frac{8+5+4}{40} = \frac{17}{40} $$

so $A+S+T = \dfrac{17}{80}$ songs per day, and the time for all three together is the reciprocal:

$$ \frac{80}{17} \approx 4.7 \text{ days} $$

The answer is E. It is worth noticing that three people together are *slower* than Alvin and Simon alone — which is only possible because Theodore's individual rate comes out negative, a quirk of the invented numbers rather than a mistake in the working.
