## 题目
Two identical ice sculptures are left to melt outside. Sculpture A melts in 6 hours. Sculpture B melts in 12 hours. Assuming each sculpture melts uniformly, after how long will Sculpture B have twice as much ice remaining as Sculpture A?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \text{3 hours}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \text{3.5 hours}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \text{4 hours}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \text{4.5 hours}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \text{5 hours}
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Melting uniformly means the remaining fraction falls linearly to zero. After $t$ hours sculpture A has $1-\dfrac{t}{6}$ of its ice left and sculpture B has $1-\dfrac{t}{12}$.

Setting B's remainder to twice A's:

$$ 1-\frac{t}{12} = 2\left(1-\frac{t}{6}\right) = 2-\frac{t}{3} $$

$$ \frac{t}{3}-\frac{t}{12} = 1 \implies \frac{4t-t}{12} = 1 \implies \frac{t}{4} = 1 \implies t = 4 $$

Check: after $4$ hours A has $\tfrac13$ left and B has $\tfrac23$ — twice as much.

The answer is C.
