## 题目
Let $n$ be a positive integer, and consider the statements $$P: n^2 - 1 \text{ is divisible by } 24. \qquad Q: \gcd(n, 6) = 1.$$ Which one of the following is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad P \text{is sufficient but not necessary for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad P \text{is necessary but not sufficient for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad P \text{is necessary and sufficient for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad P \text{is neither sufficient nor necessary for} Q $$
