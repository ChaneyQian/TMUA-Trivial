## 题目
A sequence is defined by $a_1 = k$, for some real number $k$, and $$a_{n+1} = a_n^2 - 2 \quad (n \ge 1).$$ $P$: The sequence $(a_n)$ is bounded. $\qquad$ $Q$: $-2 \le k \le 2$. Which one of the following is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad P \text{is neither necessary nor sufficient for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad P \text{is sufficient but not necessary for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad P \text{is necessary and sufficient for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad P \text{is necessary but not sufficient for} Q $$
