## 题目
Consider the statement: "For every real number $x$, there exists a real number $y$ such that $x + y > 5$ and $xy < 1$." Which of the following is the correct negation of this statement?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{There exists a real number} x \text{such that, for every real number} y \text{,} x + y \le 5 \text{and} xy \ge 1 \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{For every real number} x \text{, there exists a real number} y \text{such that} x + y \le 5 \text{or} xy \ge 1 \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{There exists a real number} x \text{such that, for every real number} y \text{,} x + y \le 5 \text{or} xy \ge 1 \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{There exists a real number} x \text{such that, for every real number} y \text{,} x + y \le 5 \text{or} xy \ge 1 \text{, and there exists such a} y \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{For every real number} x \text{, for every real number} y \text{,} x + y \le 5 \text{or} xy \ge 1 \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{There exists a real number} x \text{such that, for every real number} y \text{,} x + y < 5 \text{or} xy > 1 \text{.} $$
