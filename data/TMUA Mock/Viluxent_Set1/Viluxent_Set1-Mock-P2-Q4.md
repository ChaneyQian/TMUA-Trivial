## 题目
Let $b$ and $c$ be real numbers with $c \ne 0$. $P$: The equation $x^2 + bx + c = 0$ has two distinct positive real roots. $Q$: The equation $cx^2 + bx + 1 = 0$ has two distinct positive real roots. Which one of the following is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad P \text{is necessary but not sufficient for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad P \text{is sufficient but not necessary for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad P \text{is neither necessary nor sufficient for} Q $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad P \text{is necessary and sufficient for} Q $$
