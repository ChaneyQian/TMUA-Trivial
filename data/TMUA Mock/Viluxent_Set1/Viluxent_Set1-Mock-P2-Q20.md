## 题目
Let $f$ be differentiable on $\mathbb{R}$ with $f'(x) > 0$ for all real $x$. Define $g(x) = \int_0^x f(t) dt$. $$P: g \text{ is strictly increasing on } \mathbb{R}.$$ $$Q: g \text{ is convex on } \mathbb{R} \text{ (that is, } g''(x) \ge 0 \text{ for all } x).$$ $$R: g(x) > 0 \text{ for all } x > 0.$$ Which of the following is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad P \text{only} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad Q \text{only} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad R \text{only} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad P \text{and} Q \text{only} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad Q \text{and} R \text{only} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{all of them} $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \text{none of them} $$
