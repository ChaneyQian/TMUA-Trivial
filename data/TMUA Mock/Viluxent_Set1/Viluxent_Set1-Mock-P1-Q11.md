## 题目
Let $g_0(x) = x$ for $x \in [0, 1)$, and for $n \ge 0$ define $$g_{n+1}(x) = 2g_n(x) - \lfloor 2g_n(x) \rfloor$$ (that is, $g_{n+1}(x)$ is the fractional part of $2g_n(x)$). For each $n \ge 1$, let $$u_n = \int_0^1 g_n(x) dx.$$ Find the value of $\sum_{n=1}^{100} u_n$.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 25 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 50 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 75 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 100 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 5050 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \frac{1}{2} $$
