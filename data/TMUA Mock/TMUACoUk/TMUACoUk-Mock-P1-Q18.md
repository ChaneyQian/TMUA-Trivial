## 题目
Let $\theta$ be a real number. Define two infinite series: $$S_1(\theta) = \sin^2\theta + \sin^4\theta + \sin^6\theta + \cdots$$ $$S_2(\theta) = \cos^2\theta + \cos^4\theta + \cos^6\theta + \cdots$$ Both series converge whenever $|\sin\theta| < 1$ and $|\cos\theta| < 1$. Suppose that $$S_1(\theta) = \frac{2}{3} \quad\text{and}\quad S_2(\theta) = \frac{3}{2}$$ How many distinct real values of $\theta$ are there in the range $-2\pi \le \theta \le 2\pi$ which satisfy both equations?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 2 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 4 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 6 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 8 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 10 $$
