## 题目
When setting a TMUA question, I write $n$ statements, where none of them, some of them, or all of them are true. I then ask which of the statements is/are true. What is the minimum number of answer options I must set such that they cover every possible combination (disregarding order) of correct statements? Note: See Question 1 for an example.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}\binom nr $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \displaystyle\sum_{r=1}^{n}\binom nr $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 1+\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}\binom nr $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 2^{n-1} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 2^{n+1} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 1+\displaystyle\sum_{r=1}^{n}\binom nr $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad 2+\displaystyle\sum_{r=0}^{n}\binom nr $$
$$ \mathbf{H} \quad 2+\displaystyle\sum_{r=1}^{n}\binom nr $$
