## 题目
The 5 Backstreet Boys are asked if they want it that way. Their responses are as follows: Backstreet Boy A: "Exactly one of us wants it that way." Backstreet Boy B: "Exactly two of us wants it that way." Backstreet Boy C: "Exactly three of us wants it that way." Backstreet Boy D: "Exactly four of us wants it that way." Backstreet Boy E: "Exactly five of us wants it that way." If a Backstreet Boy does want it that way, they will tell the truth, but if a Backstreet Boy does not want it that way, they will lie. Which of these statements must be false?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{It is not necessary that at least one Backstreet Boy wants it that way.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{It is only possible that Backstreet Boy A wants it that way.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{It is not possible that two or more of the Backstreet Boys want it that way.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{It may be possible that Backstreet Boy B wants it that way.} $$
