## 题目
Consider the claim that $\frac{x+y}{2}>\sqrt{xy}$ for all positive $x,y$. Here is an attempted proof of this claim: $$\begin{array}{ll}(x-y)^2>0 & (1)\\ x^2+y^2-2xy>0 & (2)\\ x^2+y^2+2xy>4xy & (3)\\ \frac{(x+y)^2}{4}>xy & (4)\\ \text{Taking the square root of both sides, } \frac{x+y}{2}>\sqrt{xy} & (5)\end{array}$$ Which option best describes this attempt to prove the claim?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{The claim is correct and the proof is correct} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{The claim is correct but the proof is incorrect} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{The claim & proof are incorrect and the first error is on line (1)} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{The claim & proof are incorrect and the first error is on line (2)} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{The claim & proof are incorrect and the first error is on line (3)} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{The claim & proof are incorrect and the first error is on line (4)} $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \text{The claim & proof are incorrect and the first error is on line (5)} $$
