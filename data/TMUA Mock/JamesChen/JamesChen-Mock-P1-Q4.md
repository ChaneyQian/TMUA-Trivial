## 题目

$$ \mathbf{B} \quad r>0 \text{is a necessary and sufficient condition for} P<A $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 0<r<2 \text{is a sufficient but not necessary condition for} P>A $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad r>2 \text{is a necessary and sufficient condition for} P>A $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 0<r<2 \text{is a necessary and sufficient condition for} P<A $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad r>2 \text{is a sufficient but not necessary condition for} P<A $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad 0<r<2 \text{is a necessary and sufficient condition for} P>A $$
