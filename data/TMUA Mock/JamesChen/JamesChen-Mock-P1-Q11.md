## 题目
Which function $f(x)$ satisfies the equation $\sqrt[4]{abcd}=f^{-1}\left(\frac{f(a)+f(b)+f(c)+f(d)}{4}\right)$, where $f^{-1}(x)$ is the inverse function of $f(x)$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad f(x)=x $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad f(x)=x^2 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad f(x)=\log_{2025}(x) $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad f(x)=(\ln(x))^2 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad f(x)=\ln(x^2) $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad f(x)=e^x $$
