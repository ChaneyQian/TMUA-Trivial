## 题目
The real numbers $a,b$ are such that $a>b$. You are given that the function $f(x)$ is defined over positive real $x$ and is either always increasing or always decreasing. Which of these conditions is sufficient but not necessary for $f(a)<f(b)$ to be true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad f'(x)<0 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad f(x) \text{is a decreasing function} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad f(x)=\log_c x,0<c<1 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad f(x)=\log_c x,c>1 $$
