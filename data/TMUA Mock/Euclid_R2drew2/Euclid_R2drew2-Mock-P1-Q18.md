## 题目
For a real number $a$, consider the graphs $$y=|x^2-1|$$ and $$y=|(x-a)^2-1|.$$ The graphs intersect at exactly three distinct points. Which of the following describes all the possible values of $a$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad a=0 \text{only} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad -2<a<0 \text{or} 0<a<2 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad -2\le a\le2 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad a<-2 \text{or} a>2 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad a\ne0 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad a=\pm2 \text{only} $$
