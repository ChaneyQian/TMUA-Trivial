## 题目
For real numbers $a$ and $b$, consider the statement $S(a,b)$: **if** $ab<0$ **then** $a^2+b^2>0$ Which of the following descriptions is correct?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad S(a,b) \text{is false for some real} a,b \text{because} a^2+b^2 \text{can be negative} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad S(a,b) \text{is false for some real} a,b \text{because} a^2+b^2=0 \text{can occur when} ab<0 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad S(a,b) \text{is false for some real} a,b \text{because} ab<0 \text{forces} a^2+b^2=0 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad S(a,b) \text{is true for all real} a,b \text{, and the condition} ab<0 \text{is **necessary** for} a^2+b^2>0 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad S(a,b) \text{is true for all real} a,b \text{, and the condition} ab<0 \text{is **sufficient and necessary** for} a^2+b^2>0 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad S(a,b) \text{is true for all real} a,b \text{, and the condition} ab<0 \text{is **sufficient** but **not necessary** for} a^2+b^2>0 $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad S(a,b) \text{is true for all real} a,b \text{, and the condition} ab<0 \text{is neither **necessary** nor **sufficient** for} a^2+b^2>0 $$
