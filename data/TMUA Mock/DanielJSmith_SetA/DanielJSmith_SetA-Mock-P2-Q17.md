## 题目
Consider the claim: For all integers $a,b,c$, **if** $ab=ac$ **then** $b=c$ A student's argument is as follows: I. Suppose $ab=ac$ II. Then $ab-ac=0$ III. So $a(b-c)=0$ IV. Therefore $b-c=0$ V. Hence $b=c$ Which of the following best describes this argument?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{The argument is completely correct.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{The argument is incorrect, and the first error is on line I} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{The argument is incorrect, and the first error is on line II} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{The argument is incorrect, and the first error is on line III} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{The argument is incorrect, and the first error is on line IV} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{The argument is incorrect, and the first error is on line V} $$
