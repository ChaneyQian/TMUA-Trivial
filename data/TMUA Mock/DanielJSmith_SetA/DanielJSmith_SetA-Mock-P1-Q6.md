## 题目
Let $f$ be a polynomial defined for all real $x$, and define $$J=\int_{-2}^{3}\bigl(f(x)-f(|x|)\bigr)\,\mathrm{d}x$$ Which of the following expressions is equal to $J$ for all such polynomials $f$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad -\int_0^2 f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \int_0^2 f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \int_{-2}^0 f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t-\int_0^2 f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad f(0) $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad f(2)-f(0) $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \int_{-2}^0 f(t)\,\mathrm{d}t $$
