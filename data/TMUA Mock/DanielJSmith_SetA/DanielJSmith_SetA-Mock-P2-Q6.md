## 题目
A statement $S$ is given: For all real numbers $x$, **if** $x^2\ge 1$ **then** $x^4\ge x^2$ A student wishes to prove $S$ by contradiction and writes the following argument: Assume that there exists a real number $x$ such that $x^2\ge 1$ **and** $x^4<x^2$ I. Then $x^4-x^2<0$ II. So $x^2(x^2-1)<0$ III. So $x^2-1<0$ IV. So $x^2<1$, which contradicts $x^2\ge 1$ Therefore the original statement $S$ is concluded to be true. Which of the following best describes this argument?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{It is completely correct.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{It is incorrect, but it would be correct if written in the reverse order.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{It is incorrect, and the student has actually shown that} S \text{is false.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{It is incorrect because line II does not follow from line I.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{It is incorrect because line III does not follow from line II.} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{It is incorrect because the student has **not** really used proof by contradiction.} $$
