## 题目
Define functions $g_1,g_2,g_3,g_4$ on the real numbers by $$\begin{aligned}g_1(x)&=\sin x\\ g_2(x)&=\sin(\sin x)\\ g_3(x)&=\sin(\sin(\sin x))\\ g_4(x)&=\sin(\sin(\sin(\sin x)))\end{aligned}$$ Let $M_k$ be the maximum value of $g_k$ for $k=1,2,3,4$ Which one of the following statements is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad M_1=M_2=M_3=M_4=1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 1>M_1>M_2>M_3>M_4>0 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 1>M_1=M_2=M_3=M_4>0 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad M_1=1 \text{and} 1>M_2>M_3>M_4>0 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad M_2=M_4 \text{and} M_1=M_3 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{None of the above} $$
