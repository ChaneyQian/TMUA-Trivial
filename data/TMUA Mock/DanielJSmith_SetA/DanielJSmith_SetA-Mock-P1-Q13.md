## 题目
Let $c$ be a real constant. Consider the equation $$|\sin x|+|\cos x|=c$$ for $0\le x<2\pi$ Which of the following gives the number of distinct solutions as a function of $c$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{If} 0<c<1 \text{, there are 8 solutions; if} 1<c<\sqrt{2} \text{there are 4 solutions; if} c=1 \text{**or**} c=\sqrt{2} \text{there are 2 solutions; otherwise there are 0 solutions} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{If} 0<c<1 \text{, there are 0 solutions; if} c=1 \text{there are 4 solutions; if} 1<c<\sqrt{2} \text{there are 8 solutions; if} c=\sqrt{2} \text{there are 4 solutions; otherwise there are 0 solutions} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{If} 0<c<1 \text{, there are 0 solutions; if} c=1 \text{there are 2 solutions; if} 1<c<\sqrt{2} \text{there are 4 solutions; if} c=\sqrt{2} \text{there are 8 solutions; otherwise there are 0 solutions} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{If} 0<c<1 \text{, there are 4 solutions; if} c=1 \text{there are 8 solutions; if} 1<c<\sqrt{2} \text{there are 4 solutions; if} c=\sqrt{2} \text{there are 2 solutions; otherwise there are 0 solutions} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{If} 0<c<1 \text{, there are 0 solutions; if} 1\le c\le\sqrt{2} \text{there are 8 solutions; otherwise there are 0 solutions} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{If} 0<c<\sqrt{2} \text{there are 8 solutions; if} c=\sqrt{2} \text{there are 4 solutions; if} c>\sqrt{2} \text{there are 0 solutions} $$
