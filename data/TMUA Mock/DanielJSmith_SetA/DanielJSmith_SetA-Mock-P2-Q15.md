## 题目
A non-empty finite set $S$ of whole numbers is called *linked* **if and only if** for every $a$ in $S$, there exists $b$ in $S$ with $b\ne a$ such that $\gcd(a,b)>1$ where $\gcd(a,b)$ denotes the greatest common divisor (also known as the highest common factor) of integers $a$ and $b$ Which of the following is true **if and only if** $S$ is **not** linked?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{For every} a \text{in} S \text{, there exists} b \text{in} S \text{with} b\ne a \text{such that} \gcd(a,b)=1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{For every} a \text{in} S \text{, there exists a prime factor of} a \text{that divides every element of} S $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{There exists} a \text{in} S \text{such that for every} b \text{in} S \text{with} b\ne a \text{we have} \gcd(a,b)>1 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{There exists} a \text{in} S \text{such that for every} b \text{in} S \text{with} b\ne a \text{we have} \gcd(a,b)=1 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{There exists a prime} p \text{such that} p \text{divides exactly one element of} S $$
