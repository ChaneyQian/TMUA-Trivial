## 题目
Let $a$ and $b$ be positive integers and define a sequence $v_n$ by $$v_1=a,\quad v_2=b,\quad v_{n+2}=2v_{n+1}-v_n\quad\text{for } n\ge 1$$ Suppose the highest common factor of $a$ and $b$ is $d$ Which of the following statements must be true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{The highest common factor of} v_{10} \text{and} v_{11} \text{is} 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad v_n \text{is divisible by} d \text{only for even} n $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{The highest common factor of} v_n \text{and} v_{n+1} \text{is} d \text{for all} n\ge 1 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{For all} n\ge 1 \text{,} v_n \text{is divisible by} 2d $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{If} a\ne b \text{then} v_n \text{is never divisible by} d $$
