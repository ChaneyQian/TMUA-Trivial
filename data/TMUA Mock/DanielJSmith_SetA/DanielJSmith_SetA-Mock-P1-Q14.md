## 题目
Let $a$, $b$, $c$ be real constants with $a>0$ and $c\ne 0$. Suppose the inequality $ax^2+bx+c>0$ has solution set $$x<r\quad\text{or}\quad x>s$$ where $r<s$ In terms of $r$, $s$ and $c$, what is the solution set of $$a\bigl(cx\bigr)^2+b\bigl(cx\bigr)+c>0\quad?$$

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad x<cr \text{**or**} x>cs $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad x<\frac{r}{c} \text{**or**} x>\frac{s}{c} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad x<cs \text{**or**} x>cr $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad x<\frac{s}{c} \text{**or**} x>\frac{r}{c} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{If} c>0 \text{:} x<\frac{r}{c} \text{**or**} x>\frac{s}{c} \text{; if} c<0 \text{:} x<\frac{s}{c} \text{**or**} x>\frac{r}{c} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{If} c>0 \text{:} \frac{r}{c}<x<\frac{s}{c} \text{; if} c<0 \text{:} \frac{s}{c}<x<\frac{r}{c} $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \text{If} c>0 \text{:} x<\frac{r}{c} \text{**or**} x>\frac{s}{c} \text{; if} c<0 \text{:} x<\frac{r}{c} \text{**or**} x>\frac{s}{c} $$
