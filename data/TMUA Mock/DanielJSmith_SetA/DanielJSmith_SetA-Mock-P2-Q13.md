## 题目
Let $P$ and $Q$ be statements Suppose exactly one of (**if** $P$ **then** $Q$) and (**if** $Q$ **then** $P$) is true Suppose further that ($P$ **or** $Q$) is true Which statement must be true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{(} P \text{**and**} Q \text{) is true} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{Exactly one of} P \text{and} Q \text{is true} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{Both} P \text{and} Q \text{are false} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{(**if**} \neg P \text{**then**} \neg Q \text{) is true} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{(} P \text{**if and only if**} Q \text{) is true} $$
