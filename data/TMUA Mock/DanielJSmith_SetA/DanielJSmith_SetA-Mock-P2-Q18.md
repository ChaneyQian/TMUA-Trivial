## 题目

$$ \mathbf{B} \quad f(-2)=f(4)=0 $$

$$ \mathbf{E} \quad f(x+2)=-f(2-x) \text{for all real} x $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad f(x-1)=-f(1-x) \text{for all real} x $$
