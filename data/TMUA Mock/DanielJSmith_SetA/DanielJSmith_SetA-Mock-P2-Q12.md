## 题目
A sequence $(a_n)$ is defined by $a_1=1$ and, for $n\ge 1$, $$a_{n+1}=\frac{a_n+4}{\sqrt{a_n+1}}$$ Which of the following statements is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad (a_n) \text{is strictly decreasing for all} n $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad (a_n) \text{is bounded above but **not** bounded below} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad (a_n) \text{is bounded below by} 1 \text{and bounded above by} 4 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad (a_n) \text{is bounded below by} 2 \text{and bounded above by} 4 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad (a_n) \text{is bounded below by} 1 \text{but **not** bounded above} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad a_n>4 \text{for all} n\ge 2 $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \text{The sequence} (a_n) \text{converges to} 4 $$
