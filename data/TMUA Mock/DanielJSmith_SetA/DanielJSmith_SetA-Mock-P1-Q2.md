## 题目
For non-zero real numbers $a$ and $b$, consider the equation $$\log_3(x-a)=\log_3 x+b$$ The number of real solutions $x$ depends on $a$ and $b$. Which of the following is correct?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{Exactly one real solution if} a(1-3^b)>0 \text{**and** no real solutions if} a(1-3^b)<0 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{Exactly one real solution if} a(1-3^b)<0 \text{**and** no real solutions if} a(1-3^b)>0 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{Exactly two real solutions if} a(1-3^b)>0 \text{**and** none otherwise} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{No real solutions if} ab>0 \text{**and** exactly one real solution if} ab<0 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{The equation always has exactly one real solution for all non-zero} a \text{and} b $$
