## 题目
Consider the statement: For every positive integer $n$ there exists a positive integer $k$ such that for all positive integers $m$, the number $n(m+k)$ is **not** a square Which one of the following is the **negation** of this statement?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{For every positive integer} n \text{there exists a positive integer} k \text{such that for every positive integer} m \text{, the number} n(m+k) \text{is a square.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{There exists a positive integer} n \text{such that for every positive integer} k \text{**and** every positive integer} m \text{, the number} n(m+k) \text{is **not** a square.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{There exists a positive integer} n \text{such that there exists a positive integer} k \text{for which} n(m+k) \text{is a square for all positive integers} m \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{For every positive integer} n \text{, for every positive integer} k \text{, there exists a positive integer} m \text{such that} n(m+k) \text{is **not** a square.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{There exists a positive integer} n \text{such that for every positive integer} k \text{there exists a positive integer} m \text{such that} n(m+k) \text{is a square.} $$
