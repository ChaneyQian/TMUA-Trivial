## 题目
Let $S$ be the statement about integers $n$: $(*)$ For every integer $n$ there exists an integer $m$ such that $m>n$ **and** $m$ is even Which of the following statements is **logically equivalent** to the **negation** of $(*)$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{For some integer} n \text{there exists an integer} m \text{such that} m>n \text{**and**} m \text{is odd.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{For some integer} n \text{, **if** an integer} m \text{is greater than} n \text{**then**} m \text{is even.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{For every integer} n \text{, **if**} n \text{is even **then** there is no integer} m>n \text{that is even.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{It is **not** the case that there exists an integer} m \text{which is greater than every integer} n \text{**and** is even.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{For every integer} m \text{there exists an integer} n<m \text{such that} m \text{is odd.} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{There exists an integer} n \text{such that there is exactly one even integer greater than} n \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \text{There exists an integer} n \text{such that for all integers} m \text{, **if**} m>n \text{**then**} m \text{is **not** even.} $$
