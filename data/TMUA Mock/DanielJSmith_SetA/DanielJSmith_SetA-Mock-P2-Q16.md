## 题目
Consider the statement: **if** $0\le f(x)\le 1$ for all real $x$ with $0\le x\le 1$, **then** $$(*)\qquad \int_0^1 f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x\le\int_0^1 (f(x))^2\,\mathrm{d}x$$ Which of the following functions is a *counterexample* to $(*)$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad f(x)=2x $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad f(x)=x^2+\frac{1}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad f(x)=\frac{1}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad f(x)=1+x $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad f(x)=0 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad f(x)=5x(1-x) $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad f(x)=\sqrt{x}+\frac{1}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{H} \quad f(x)=1 $$
