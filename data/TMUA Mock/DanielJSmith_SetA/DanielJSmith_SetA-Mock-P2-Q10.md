## 题目
Let $C$ be the circle $$x^2+y^2=9$$ Let $P$ be the point $(a,0)$ where $a>3$ The two tangents from $P$ to $C$ touch the circle at $T_1$ and $T_2$ What is the area of triangle $PT_1T_2$ in terms of $a$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 3\sqrt{a^2-9} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{3(a^2-9)^{3/2}}{a^2} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{9}{a}\sqrt{a^2-9} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{a}{3}\sqrt{a^2-9} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{a^2-9}{3} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \frac{a^2-9}{a} $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \frac{9}{\sqrt{a^2-9}} $$
