## 题目
A sequence $(u_n)$ is defined by $u_1>0$ and $$u_{n+1}=\frac{u_n}{1+u_n}$$ for all integers $n\ge 1$ Which of the following is equal to $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}u_n$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad u_1 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{u_1}{1-u_1} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{1}{u_1} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 1+u_1 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{1}{1+u_1} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{The series does not converge for any} u_1>0 $$
