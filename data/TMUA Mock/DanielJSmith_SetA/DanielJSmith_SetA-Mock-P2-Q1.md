## 题目
For each positive integer $n$, define $$E_n=n^4+4$$ For which one of the following statements is $n=3$ a counterexample?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{For all positive integers} n \text{,} E_n \text{is divisible by} n^2-2n+2 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{For all positive integers} n \text{,} E_n \text{is divisible by} n^2+2n+2 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{For all positive integers} n \text{,} E_n \text{is **not** divisible by} 17 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{For all positive integers} n \text{,} E_n \text{is **not** divisible by} 3 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{For all positive integers} n \text{,} E_n \text{is divisible by} 5 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{For all positive integers} n \text{,} E_n \text{has remainder} 1 \text{when divided by} 4 $$
