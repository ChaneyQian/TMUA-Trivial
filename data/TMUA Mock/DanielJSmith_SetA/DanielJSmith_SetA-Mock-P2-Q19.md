## 题目
Consider the two statements about a real-valued function $f$: (A) For all real numbers $x$ there exists a real number $y$ such that $y>x$ **and** $f(y)<f(x)$ (B) There exists a real number $y$ such that for all real numbers $x$ with $x<y$, $f(y)<f(x)$ Which of the following is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{Both (A) **and** (B) are always true for any function} f \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{For some functions} f \text{, (A) is true **and** (B) is true, but **not** for all} f \text{.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{There is a function} f \text{for which (A) is true **and** (B) is false, but **not** the other way round.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{There is a function} f \text{for which (B) is true **and** (A) is false, but **not** the other way round.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{For every function} f \text{, (A) **and** (B) are either both true or both false.} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{There is no function} f \text{for which (A) is true.} $$
