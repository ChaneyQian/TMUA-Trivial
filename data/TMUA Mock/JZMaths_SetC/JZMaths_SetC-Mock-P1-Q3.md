## 题目
The real numbers $a$, $b$, $c$ satisfy
$$ 4 - |a - 8| \ge 0, \qquad |2b - 10| \le 6, \qquad |c - 8| \le 7. $$

What is the least possible value of $|abc|$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 15 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 12 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 10 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 32 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 8 $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad 0 $$

## 解析
Solve each:
$$ 4 - |a - 8| \ge 0 \iff |a - 8| \le 4 \iff 4 \le a \le 12, $$
$$ |2b - 10| \le 6 \iff -6 \le 2b - 10 \le 6 \iff 2 \le b \le 8, $$
$$ |c - 8| \le 7 \iff 1 \le c \le 15. $$

Since $|abc| = |a||b||c|$, $|abc|$ is minimised by minimising each factor separately. Hence the least possible value of $|abc|$ is $4 \times 2 \times 1 = 8$.
