## 题目
Given that $a \neq d \neq 0$ and that
$$ \frac{a^2 - 2a + d^2}{2d - 2ad - 1} = 1 $$
What is the value of $a + d$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 2
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 3
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 4
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \sqrt{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{1}{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad -1
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Clear the denominator and gather everything on one side:

$$ a^{2}-2a+d^{2} = 2d-2ad-1 \implies a^{2}+2ad+d^{2}-2a-2d+1 = 0 $$

The first three terms are $(a+d)^{2}$ and the next two are $-2(a+d)$, so this is

$$ (a+d)^{2}-2(a+d)+1 = 0 \implies \bigl((a+d)-1\bigr)^{2} = 0 $$

Hence $a+d = 1$.

The answer is A. The conditions $a \neq d \neq 0$ only serve to keep the original fraction well defined; they do not change the value.
