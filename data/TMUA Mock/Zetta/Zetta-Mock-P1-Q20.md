## 题目
Given that:
$$ f(x) = x^{2008} - 2x^{2007} + 3x^{2006} - 4x^{2005} + \dots + 2007x^2 - 2008x + 2009 $$
and that:
$$ g(x) = \frac{2012x + 2009}{(1 + x)^2} $$
Which of the following is a solution to $g(x) = f(x)$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 2^{\frac{1}{2009}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 3^{\frac{1}{2009}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 4^{\frac{1}{2009}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 2^{\frac{1}{2008}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{F} \quad 3^{\frac{1}{2008}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{G} \quad 4^{\frac{1}{2008}}
$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
The coefficients of $f$ are $1,-2,3,-4,\dots$, which is the pattern generated by $\dfrac{1}{(1+x)^{2}}$. That suggests multiplying $f$ by $(1+x)^{2}$ and watching almost everything collapse.

Writing $f(x) = \sum_{j=0}^{2008}(-1)^{j}(2009-j)x^{j}$, the coefficient of $x^{n}$ in $(1+2x+x^{2})f(x)$ is, for $2 \leq n \leq 2008$,

$$ (-1)^{n}\bigl[(2009-n)-2(2010-n)+(2011-n)\bigr] = 0 $$

Only the ends survive, giving

$$ (1+x)^{2}f(x) = 2009+2010x+x^{2010} $$

So $g(x)=f(x)$ becomes $2012x+2009 = 2009+2010x+x^{2010}$, that is

$$ 2x = x^{2010} \implies x\left(x^{2009}-2\right) = 0 $$

The non-zero solution is $x = 2^{1/2009}$.

The answer is B. ($x=0$ also satisfies the equation but is not offered as an option.)
