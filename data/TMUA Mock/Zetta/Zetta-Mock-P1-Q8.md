## 题目
Which of the following expressions is closest to the value of
$$ \sqrt{100001} - \sqrt{100000} $$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{1}{\sqrt{100000}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{1}{2\sqrt{100000}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{1}{10\sqrt{100000}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{1}{100\sqrt{100000}}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{1}{100000\sqrt{100000}}
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Rationalise, which turns a difference of nearly equal numbers into something well behaved:

$$ \sqrt{100001}-\sqrt{100000} = \frac{100001-100000}{\sqrt{100001}+\sqrt{100000}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{100001}+\sqrt{100000}} $$

The two square roots differ by a negligible amount, so the denominator is very close to $2\sqrt{100000}$ and the expression is approximately $\dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{100000}}$.

The answer is B. This is the discrete version of $\dfrac{d}{dx}\sqrt{x} = \dfrac{1}{2\sqrt{x}}$.
