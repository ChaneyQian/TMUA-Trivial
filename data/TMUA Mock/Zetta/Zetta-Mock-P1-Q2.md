## 题目
Given that the points $(-2, 0)$, $(-\frac{1}{2}, \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2})$ and $(-\frac{1}{2}, -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2})$ all lie on the circle $C$ with radius $r$ and center $(a, 0)$
Find $r - a$

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 1
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 1 - \sqrt{2}
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 2
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 3 + \sqrt{3}
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 3
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
The centre is $(a,0)$, so every listed point is at distance $r$ from it:

$$ (-2-a)^{2} = r^{2}, \qquad \left(-\tfrac12-a\right)^{2}+\tfrac34 = r^{2} $$

(the two points with $y=\pm\frac{\sqrt3}{2}$ give the same equation, which is why the circle is determined). Subtracting,

$$ (a+2)^{2}-\left(a+\tfrac12\right)^{2}-\tfrac34 = 0 \implies 3a+3 = 0 \implies a = -1 $$

Then $r^{2}=(-2+1)^{2}=1$, so $r=1$ and $r-a = 1-(-1) = 2$.

The answer is C. (These three points are the cube roots of unity scaled and shifted — they sit on the unit circle centred at $(-1,0)$.)
