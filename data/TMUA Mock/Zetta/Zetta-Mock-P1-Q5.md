## 题目
After Euclid High School's last basketball game, it was determined that $\frac{1}{4}$ of the team's points were scored by Alexa and $\frac{2}{7}$ were scored by Brittany. Chelsea scored 15 points. None of the other 7 team members scored more than 2 points. What was the total number of points scored by the other 7 team members?

$$
\mathbf{A} \quad 10
$$

$$
\mathbf{B} \quad 11
$$

$$
\mathbf{C} \quad 12
$$

$$
\mathbf{D} \quad 13
$$

$$
\mathbf{E} \quad 14
$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Let $T$ be the team total. Alexa scored $\tfrac{T}{4}$ and Brittany $\tfrac{2T}{7}$, so $T$ must be a multiple of $28$. The other seven players scored

$$ T-\frac{T}{4}-\frac{2T}{7}-15 = \frac{28T-7T-8T}{28}-15 = \frac{13T}{28}-15 $$

Each of those seven scored at most $2$, so this total lies between $0$ and $14$:

$$ 0 \leq \frac{13T}{28}-15 \leq 14 \implies 15 \leq \frac{13T}{28} \leq 29 \implies 32.3 \leq T \leq 62.5 $$

The only multiple of $28$ in that range is $T=56$, giving $\dfrac{13(56)}{28}-15 = 26-15 = 11$.

The answer is B.
