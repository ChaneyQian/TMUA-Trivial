## 答案
C

## 解析
Look for the surd in the form $(\sqrt{p}-\sqrt{q})^{2} = p+q-2\sqrt{pq}$. Matching against $3-2\sqrt{2}$ needs $p+q=3$ and $pq=2$, so $\{p,q\}=\{2,1\}$ and

$$ 3-2\sqrt{2} = \left(\sqrt2-1\right)^{2} $$

Therefore $\sqrt{3-2\sqrt2} = \left|\sqrt2-1\right| = \sqrt2-1$, taking the positive root since $\sqrt2>1$.

The answer is C. Option E is the same square root with the sign wrong — a square root is never negative.
