## 题目
A certain bus arrives at the stop near Duru's house at exactly 09:02 with probability $\dfrac{7}{10}$, and at exactly 09:03 with probability $\dfrac{3}{10}$. Duru leaves her house at exactly 09:00 to catch this bus; the time it takes her to reach the stop is $100$ seconds with probability $\dfrac{1}{2}$, $150$ seconds with probability $\dfrac{3}{10}$, and $250$ seconds with probability $\dfrac{1}{5}$. What is the probability that Duru is at the stop at the moment the bus arrives?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \dfrac{55}{100} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \dfrac{59}{100} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \dfrac{63}{100} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \dfrac{67}{100} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \dfrac{71}{100} $$
