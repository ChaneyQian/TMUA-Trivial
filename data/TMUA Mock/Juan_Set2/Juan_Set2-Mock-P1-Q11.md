## 题目
Melisa is going to stack, on a table, $10$ circular pieces of cardboard whose radii are $1$ cm, $2$ cm, $3$ cm, $\ldots$, $10$ cm (each a different natural number of centimetres), one on top of another with their centres aligned. In how many different ways can Melisa arrange this stack so that, viewed from above, exactly one of the circles is completely hidden from view?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 36 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 40 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 45 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 48 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 54 $$
