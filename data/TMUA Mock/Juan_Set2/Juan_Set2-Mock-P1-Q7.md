## 题目
Let $a$ and $b$ be positive real numbers such that for each of the equations $$ax^2 - 2x + b = 0, \qquad bx^2 - 3bx + a = 0$$ the sum of the roots is $1$ more than the product of the roots. Which of the following could be the second-degree equation whose roots are $a$ and $b$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 9x^2 + 8x + 18 = 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 9x^2 - 14x + 8 = 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 9x^2 - 18x + 14 = 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 9x^2 - 8x + 14 = 0 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 9x^2 - 18x + 8 = 0 $$
