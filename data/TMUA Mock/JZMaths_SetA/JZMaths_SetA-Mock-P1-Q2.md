## 题目
The equation $2x^2 + px + 24 = 0$ has two real roots in the ratio $1:3$. Find the value of $p^2$.
$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 256 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 128 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 512 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 64 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 192 $$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Let the two roots be $k$ and $3k$.
$$ 2x^2 + px + 24 = 0 $$
$$ x^2 + \frac{p}{2}x + 12 = 0 $$
$$ (x - k)(x - 3k) = 0 \qquad (\text{roots are } k \text{ and } 3k) $$
$$ x^2 - 4kx + 3k^2 = 0 $$

Comparing coefficients:
$$ 3k^2 = 12 \quad \Leftrightarrow \quad k = \pm 2, $$
and
$$ \frac{p}{2} = -4k \quad \Rightarrow \quad p^2 = 64k^2 = 64 \cdot 4 = 256. $$
