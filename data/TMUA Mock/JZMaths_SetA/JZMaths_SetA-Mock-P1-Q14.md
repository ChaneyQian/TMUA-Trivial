## 题目
The following sequence of transformations is applied, in the order listed, to the curve $y = 2x^2 - 3x + 1$.

(1) translation by $\binom{-1}{4}$;

(2) reflection in the line $y = 2$;

(3) stretch parallel to the $x$-axis with scale factor $\frac{1}{2}$.

What is the equation of the resulting curve?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad y = -8x^2 - 2x $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad y = -8x^2 + 2x $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad y = -2x^2 - x $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad y = -\frac{1}{2}x^2 - x $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad y = -\frac{1}{2}x^2 + x $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad y = -8x^2 - 4x $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad y = 8x^2 + 2x $$

## 答案
A

## 解析
Let $f(x) = 2x^2 - 3x + 1$.

(1) Translation by $\binom{-1}{4}$, in terms of transformations, is $f(x) \longrightarrow f(x + 1) + 4$. So

$$ y = 2(x + 1)^2 - 3(x + 1) + 1 + 4 = 2x^2 + x + 4. $$

(2) Reflection in the line $y = 2$, in terms of transformations, is $f(x) \longrightarrow 4 - f(x)$. See my **remark** at the end for more details. We get

$$ y = 4 - (2x^2 + x + 4) = -2x^2 - x. $$

For (3), a stretch parallel to the $x$-axis with scale factor $\frac12$ sends $f(x)$ to $f(2x)$. Therefore

$$ y=-2(2x)^2-2x=-8x^2-2x. $$

**Remark:** Reflections in lines of the form $x=a$ and $y=b$ are not covered in much depth at AS Mathematics, but students have already encountered examples of them. Notably, $\sin x=\sin(180^\circ-x)$ comes from symmetry about $x=90^\circ$.
