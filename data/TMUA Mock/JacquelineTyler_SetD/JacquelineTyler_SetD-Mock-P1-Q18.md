$$ \mathbf{A} \quad k=\dfrac{c+2}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad k=\dfrac{2}{c-2} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad k=\dfrac{2}{c} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad k=\dfrac{c-4}{4} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad k=\dfrac{c}{4} $$
