## 题目
A set of cards has a single letter printed on the front and a single number printed on the back. Five of these cards are laid on the table so that only one side of each card is visible. The cards show the following:\qquad 5\qquad E\qquad 7\qquad F\qquad 9 Lara states that all cards with a vowel on the front have a prime number on the back. Which cards do you need to turn over in order to check if this statement is true?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{all of the cards} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{cards E, 5 and 7} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{card E only} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{cards F and 9} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{cards E and 9} $$
