## 题目
A rectangular box has 12 edges whose combined length is $L$ centimetres. The space diagonal (the longest distance between any two vertices) measures $d$ centimetres. Express the total surface area of the box in terms of $L$ and $d$.
$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{L^2 - 4d^2}{4}$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad L^2 - d^2$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{L^2 - 16d^2}{16}$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{Ld}{12}$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{L^2 - 12d^2}{12}$$

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Let the edge lengths be $x$, $y$, $z$. The box has four edges of each length, so $4(x+y+z) = L$, that is $x+y+z = \frac{L}{4}$, and the space diagonal satisfies $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = d^2$. Squaring the first relation,
$$\left(\frac{L}{4}\right)^2 = x^2+y^2+z^2 + 2(xy+yz+zx),$$
and the total surface area is exactly $S = 2(xy+yz+zx)$. Therefore $S = \frac{L^2}{16} - d^2 = \frac{L^2 - 16d^2}{16}$. The answer is C.
