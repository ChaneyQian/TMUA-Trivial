## 题目
Find the value of the digit at the ones place of the following expression:
$$116 \times 142 \times 154 \times 177 \times 208 - 111 \times 127 \times 134 \times 166 \times 198$$
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 1$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 3$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 7$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 9$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad 5$$

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
E

## 解析
The units digit of a product depends only on the units digits of the factors, so we may work modulo 10 throughout. For the first product the units digits are $6, 2, 4, 7, 8$, and multiplying them in turn gives $6 \times 2 = 12$, then $2 \times 4 = 8$, then $8 \times 7 = 56$, then $6 \times 8 = 48$, so that product ends in 8. For the second product the units digits are $1, 7, 4, 6, 8$, and the same running calculation gives $7$, then $28$, then $48$, then $64$, so that product ends in 4. The first product is plainly the larger of the two, so the difference is positive and its units digit is $8 - 4 = 4$. The answer is E.
