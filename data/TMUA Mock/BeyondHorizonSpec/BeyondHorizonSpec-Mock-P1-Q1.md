## 题目
Let $x$ be a positive integer multiple of 12 such that $\sqrt{x}$ lies strictly between 20 and 21. How many possible values are there for $x$?
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 1$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 2$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 3$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 4$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 5$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad 6$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Everything in sight is positive, so the condition $20 < \sqrt{x} < 21$ squares to $400 < x < 441$, with both inequalities strict. The multiples of 12 in that window are $12 \times 34 = 408$, $12 \times 35 = 420$ and $12 \times 36 = 432$; the neighbouring multiples $12 \times 33 = 396$ and $12 \times 37 = 444$ both fall outside. Hence exactly three integers qualify. The answer is C.
