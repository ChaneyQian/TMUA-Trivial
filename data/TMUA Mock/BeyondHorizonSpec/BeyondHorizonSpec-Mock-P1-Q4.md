## 题目
The following sequence of transformations is applied to the curve $y = 3x^2$:

1. Translation by $\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$
2. Reflection in the $y$-axis
3. Stretch parallel to the $x$-axis with scale factor 5

What is the equation of the resulting curve?
$$\mathbf{A} \quad y = \frac{3}{25}x^2 + \frac{18}{5}x + 26$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad y = \frac{3}{25}x^2 + \frac{18}{5}x + 24$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad y = \frac{3}{5}x^2 + \frac{18}{5}x + 12$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad y = 75x^2 - 90x + 28$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad y = 15x^2 + 90x + 134$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad y = \frac{3}{25}x^2 - \frac{18}{25}x + \frac{24}{25}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
A

## 解析
Apply the transformations one at a time to the equation. The translation by $\begin{pmatrix} 3 \\ -1 \end{pmatrix}$ replaces $x$ by $x-3$ and subtracts 1, giving $y = 3(x-3)^2 - 1$. Reflecting in the $y$-axis replaces $x$ by $-x$, and since $(-x-3)^2 = (x+3)^2$ this becomes $y = 3(x+3)^2 - 1$. The horizontal stretch of scale factor 5 replaces $x$ by $\frac{x}{5}$, giving
$$y = 3\left(\frac{x}{5} + 3\right)^2 - 1.$$
Expanding, $3\left(\frac{x^2}{25} + \frac{6x}{5} + 9\right) - 1 = \frac{3}{25}x^2 + \frac{18}{5}x + 27 - 1$, so the curve is $y = \frac{3}{25}x^2 + \frac{18}{5}x + 26$. The answer is A.
