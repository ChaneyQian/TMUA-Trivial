## 题目
Big M, Round T, Lucky L and King K all race around a 1 kilometer track. It turns out that Round T is first, Big M is second, Lucky L is third, and King K is fourth. Round T finishes his lap 200 meters ahead of Big M. Furthermore, Big M finishes his lap 200 meters ahead of Lucky L, and Lucky L finishes 200m ahead of King K. Assuming that each person moves with a constant speed, at the moment when Round T finishes his lap, how many meters is King K away from finishing his lap?
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 800$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 640$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 488$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 360$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 512$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad 600$$

## 答案
C

## 解析
Because the speeds are constant, distances covered in equal times are in the same ratio as the speeds. When Round T completes his 1000 m, Big M has covered 800 m, so $\frac{v_M}{v_T} = \frac{4}{5}$. The same statement about Big M and Lucky L gives $\frac{v_L}{v_M} = \frac{4}{5}$, and the one about Lucky L and King K gives $\frac{v_K}{v_L} = \frac{4}{5}$. Multiplying, $\frac{v_K}{v_T} = \left(\frac{4}{5}\right)^3 = \frac{64}{125} = 0.512$. So in the time Round T runs 1000 m, King K runs 512 m and is therefore $1000 - 512 = 488$ m short. The answer is C.
