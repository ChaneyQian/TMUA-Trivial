## 题目
Which of the following numbers is the largest in value?
$$\mathbf{A} \quad \tan\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right)$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad \sin^2\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right)$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad \log_{10}\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right)$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad \log_{2}\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right)$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad \log_{3}\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right)$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad \tan\left(\frac{3\pi}{4}\right)$$

## 答案
D

## 解析
Evaluate each one. Since $\frac{5\pi}{4}$ is $\pi + \frac{\pi}{4}$, the tangent repeats with period $\pi$ and $\tan\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right) = 1$, while $\sin\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right) = -\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$ so its square is $\frac{1}{2}$, and $\tan\left(\frac{3\pi}{4}\right) = -1$. For the logarithms the argument is the same number $\frac{5\pi}{4} \approx 3.927$, so they are all positive and the one with the smallest base is the largest: $\log_2 3.927 \approx 1.97$, $\log_3 3.927 \approx 1.25$ and $\log_{10} 3.927 \approx 0.59$. The largest value in the list is therefore $\log_2\left(\frac{5\pi}{4}\right)$, which beats $1$ because $\frac{5\pi}{4} > 2$. The answer is D. Option A is the trap for anyone who stops at the tangents without noticing that a base-$2$ logarithm of a number close to $4$ is nearly $2$.
