## 题目
Which of these numbers is the largest?
$$\mathbf{A} \quad \frac{2+\sqrt{5}}{5\sqrt{2}}$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad \frac{\sqrt{9!}}{6!}$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad \frac{\log_3 26}{\log_2 33}$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{\sqrt{7}}{5}$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{\pi}{5}$$

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Every option is a small positive number, so it is enough to estimate each to two decimal places. For A, $\sqrt{5} \approx 2.236$, so the numerator is about $4.236$ while the denominator is $5\sqrt{2} \approx 7.071$, giving roughly $0.599$. For B, $9! = 362880$ and $\sqrt{362880} \approx 602.4$, while $6! = 720$, so B is about $0.837$. For C, change both logarithms to natural logarithms: $\log_3 26 = \ln 26/\ln 3 \approx 3.258/1.099 \approx 2.966$ and $\log_2 33 = \ln 33/\ln 2 \approx 3.497/0.693 \approx 5.045$, so C is about $0.588$. For D, $\sqrt{7} \approx 2.646$ so D is about $0.529$, and E is $\pi/5 \approx 0.628$.

A cleaner way to see that B wins without a calculator is to note that $\sqrt{9!} = \sqrt{9 \cdot 8 \cdot 7 \cdot 6!}$, so
$$\frac{\sqrt{9!}}{6!} = \sqrt{\frac{504}{720}} = \sqrt{0.7} \approx 0.837,$$
whereas every other option is visibly below $0.63$: A is less than $(2+2.24)/7.07 < 0.6$, C is a ratio of a number just under $3$ to a number just over $5$, D is $\sqrt{7}/5 < 0.53$, and E is $\pi/5 < 0.63$. Hence B is the largest. The answer is B.
