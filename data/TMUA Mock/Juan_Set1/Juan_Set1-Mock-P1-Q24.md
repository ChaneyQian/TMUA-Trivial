Which of the following could be the radius of this circle, in units?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 11 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 13 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 15 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 17 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 19 $$
