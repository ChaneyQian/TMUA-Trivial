## 题目
Let $a$ be a nonzero real number and $b$, $c$ be real numbers. For the function $f$ defined on the set of real numbers by $f(x) = ax + b$, and its inverse function $f^{-1}$, it is given that $$\lim_{x \to b} \frac{f(x)}{f^{-1}(x)} = c, \qquad f(1) = 3$$ What is the sum of the distinct possible values that $c$ can take?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad 6 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad 7 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad 10 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 11 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 12 $$
