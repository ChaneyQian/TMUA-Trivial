## 题目
Let $x$, $y$, $z$ be distinct elements, one each, of the set $\left\{ \dfrac{\pi}{4}, \dfrac{3\pi}{4}, \dfrac{5\pi}{4} \right\}$, such that $$\sin x < \tan y < \sec z$$ Which of the following is the correct ordering of $x$, $y$, and $z$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad x < y < z $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad y < x < z $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad y < z < x $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad z < x < y $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad z < y < x $$
