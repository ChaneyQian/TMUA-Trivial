## 题目

Let $u=\frac1x$ and $\frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}x}=1$. 2. Then $\frac{\mathrm{d}u}{\mathrm{d}x}=-\frac1{x^2}$ and $v=x$. 3. So $\int\frac1x\,\mathrm{d}x=\frac1x\cdot x-\int x\left(-\frac1{x^2}\right)\mathrm{d}x=1+\int\frac1x\,\mathrm{d}x$. 4. Subtracting $\int\frac1x\,\mathrm{d}x$ from both sides gives $0=1$. The proof is incorrect. Where is the first error?
