## 题目
Consider the statement: "for every function $f$ defined on $0\le x\le1$, the value $\int_0^1 f(x)\,\mathrm{d}x$ equals the area enclosed by $y=f(x)$, the $x$-axis, $x=0$ and $x=1$." Which of the following is a counterexample?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad f(x)=(x-2)^2 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad f(x)=x^2+1 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad f(x)=2x-1 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad f(x)=\cos x $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad f(x)=e^x $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad f(x)=1+\cos x $$
$$ \mathbf{G} \quad \text{the statement is correct, so there is no counterexample} $$
