## 题目
A function $f$ is defined on all of $\mathbb{R}$, but is not assumed to be continuous. Let J be "$f(1)=2$ and $f(4)=-3$" and let K be "$f(x)=0$ for some $x$ with $1<x<4$". Which option is correct?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{J is sufficient for K} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{J is necessary for K} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{J is necessary and sufficient for K} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{J is neither necessary nor sufficient for K} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{K is sufficient for J} $$
$$ \mathbf{F} \quad \text{K is necessary for J} $$
