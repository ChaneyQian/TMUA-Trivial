## 题目
Three boxes are labelled "Apples", "Oranges" and "Mixed". You are told every label is wrong. You may draw one fruit from a single box of your choice and look at it. From which box should you draw to guarantee you can deduce the contents of all three boxes?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{the box labelled "Apples"} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{the box labelled "Oranges"} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{the box labelled "Mixed"} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{any single box will work} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{it cannot be done with a single draw} $$
