## 题目
You wish to prove the statement "if $n^2$ is even then $n$ is even". Which one of the following arguments actually proves it?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{Assume} n \text{is even and show} n^2 \text{is even.} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \text{Assume} n \text{is odd and show} n^2 \text{is odd.} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{Check that} n=2,4,6,8 \text{all have} n^2 \text{even.} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{Assume} n^2 \text{is odd and show that} n \text{is even.} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \text{Assume} n^2 \text{is even and show that} n^2 \text{is a multiple of 4.} $$
