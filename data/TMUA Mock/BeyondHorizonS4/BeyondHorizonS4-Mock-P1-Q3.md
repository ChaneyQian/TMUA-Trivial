## 题目
If $\alpha, \beta$ are the roots of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$, then the equation whose roots are $\alpha^2, \beta^2$ is
$$\mathbf{A} \quad a^2x^2 + (b^2 - 2ac)x + c^2 = 0$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad a^2x^2 - (b^2 + 2ac)x + c^2 = 0$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad a^2x^2 - (b^2 - 2ac)x + c^2 = 0$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad a^2x^2 - (a^2 - 2bc)x + c^2 = 0$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad b^2x^2 - (b^2 - 2ac)x + c^2 = 0$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad \text{none of the above equations}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
By Vieta's formulae $\alpha + \beta = -\frac{b}{a}$ and $\alpha\beta = \frac{c}{a}$. Hence the new roots satisfy $\alpha^2 + \beta^2 = (\alpha + \beta)^2 - 2\alpha\beta = \frac{b^2}{a^2} - \frac{2c}{a} = \frac{b^2 - 2ac}{a^2}$ and $\alpha^2\beta^2 = \frac{c^2}{a^2}$. The monic quadratic with those roots is $x^2 - \frac{b^2 - 2ac}{a^2}x + \frac{c^2}{a^2} = 0$, and multiplying through by $a^2$ gives
$$a^2x^2 - (b^2 - 2ac)x + c^2 = 0.$$
The answer is C. Option A is the same expression with the sign of the middle term flipped, which is what you get if you forget that the coefficient of $x$ is minus the sum of the roots.
