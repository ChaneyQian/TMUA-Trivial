## 题目
The largest volume of a cube that can be enclosed in a sphere of diameter $2$ cm, is, in $\mathrm{cm}^3$,
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 1$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 2\sqrt{2}$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad \pi$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad \frac{8}{3\sqrt{3}}$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad \frac{8}{9}$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad \frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The largest cube inside a sphere is the inscribed one, whose space diagonal is a diameter. If the side is $s$ then the space diagonal is $s\sqrt{3}$, so $s\sqrt{3} = 2$ and $s = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$. The volume is therefore
$$s^3 = \frac{8}{3\sqrt{3}}.$$
The answer is D. Option B, $2\sqrt{2}$, is the trap for using the face diagonal instead of the space diagonal: $s\sqrt{2} = 2$ would give $s = \sqrt{2}$ and $s^3 = 2\sqrt{2}$.
