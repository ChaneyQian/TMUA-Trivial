## 题目
In a parallelogram $PQRS$, $P$ is the point $(-1, -1)$, $Q$ is $(8, 0)$ and $R$ is $(7, 5)$. Then $S$ is the point
$$\mathbf{A} \quad (-1, 4)$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad (-2, 4)$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad \left(-2, \frac{7}{2}\right)$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad \left(-\frac{3}{2}, 4\right)$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad (-2, 5)$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad (-1, 5)$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
The labelling $PQRS$ lists the vertices in cyclic order, so $PQ$ and $SR$ are the pair of opposite sides and $\vec{PQ} = \vec{SR}$. That gives $S = P + R - Q$, so
$$S = (-1 + 7 - 8,\; -1 + 5 - 0) = (-2, 4).$$
Equivalently, the diagonals bisect each other: the midpoint of $PR$ is $(3, 2)$, so $S = 2(3, 2) - (8, 0) = (-2, 4)$. The answer is B. Option C, $\left(-2, \frac{7}{2}\right)$, is the trap for averaging coordinates instead of using the midpoint condition on the correct diagonal.
