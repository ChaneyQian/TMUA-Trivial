## 题目
What is the area of the polygon formed by all the points $(x, y)$ in the plane satisfying the inequality
$$|x - 2| + |y - 2| \leq 8?$$
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 32$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 64$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 96$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 128$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 160$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
D

## 解析
The condition describes a square tilted through $45^\circ$, centred at $(2, 2)$, whose vertices lie eight units from the centre along the horizontal and vertical directions, namely at $(10, 2)$, $(-6, 2)$, $(2, 10)$ and $(2, -6)$. Its two diagonals are perpendicular and both of length $16$, so the area is
$$\tfrac{1}{2} \times 16 \times 16 = 128.$$
The answer is D. Option B, $64$, is what you get by reading the $8$ as the side length of the square rather than as half a diagonal.
