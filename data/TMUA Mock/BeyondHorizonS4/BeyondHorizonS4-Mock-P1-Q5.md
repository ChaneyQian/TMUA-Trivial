## 题目
The number of distinct points common to the curves $x^2 + 4y^2 = 1$ and $4x^2 + y^2 = 4$ is
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 0$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 1$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 2$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 3$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad 5$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
C

## 解析
Both equations involve only $x^2$ and $y^2$, so set $u = x^2$ and $v = y^2$, giving the linear pair $u + 4v = 1$ and $4u + v = 4$. Substituting $u = 1 - 4v$ into the second gives $4 - 16v + v = 4$, so $-15v = 0$ and $v = 0$, whence $u = 1$. Therefore $y = 0$ and $x = \pm 1$, and both $(1, 0)$ and $(-1, 0)$ do satisfy the two original equations, so there are
$$2 \text{ common points}.$$
The answer is C. Option E, $4$, is the count you would expect for two ellipses in general position, but these two are tangent to each other on the $x$-axis rather than crossing transversally.
