## 题目
What is the digit in the unit position of the integer
$$1! + 2! + 3! + \cdots + 99!$$
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 3$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 0$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 1$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 7$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 5$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad 9$$

## 答案
A

## 解析
For every $n \geq 5$ the factorial $n!$ contains both a factor $2$ and a factor $5$, hence is a multiple of $10$ and contributes nothing to the units digit of the sum. Only the first four terms matter, and
$$1! + 2! + 3! + 4! = 1 + 2 + 6 + 24 = 33,$$
whose units digit is $3$. The answer is A.
