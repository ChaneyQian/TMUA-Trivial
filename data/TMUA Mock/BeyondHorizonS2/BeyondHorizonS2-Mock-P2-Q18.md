## 题目
Mr. Earl E. Bird leaves home every day at 8:00 AM to go to work. If he drives at an average speed of 40 miles per hour, he will be late by 3 minutes. If he drives at an average speed of 60 miles per hour, he will be early by 3 minutes. How many miles per hour does Mr. Bird need to drive to get to work exactly on time?
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 45$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 48$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 50$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 55$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 58$$

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Let $d$ be the distance in miles and $t$ the on-time travel time in hours. Three minutes is $\frac{1}{20}$ of an hour, so the two scenarios give $\frac{d}{40} = t + \frac{1}{20}$ and $\frac{d}{60} = t - \frac{1}{20}$. Subtracting eliminates $t$:
$$\frac{d}{40} - \frac{d}{60} = \frac{d}{120} = \frac{1}{10},$$
so $d = 12$ miles. Then $t = \frac{12}{40} - \frac{1}{20} = \frac{1}{4}$ of an hour, and the required speed is $12 \div \frac{1}{4} = 48$ miles per hour. The answer is B.
