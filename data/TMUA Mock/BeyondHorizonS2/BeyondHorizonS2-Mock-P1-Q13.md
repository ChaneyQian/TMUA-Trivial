## 题目
Consider a rectangular cardboard box of height 3, breadth 4, and length 10 units. There is a lizard in one corner $A$ of the box and an insect in the corner $B$, which is farthest from $A$. The length of the shortest path between the lizard and the insect along the surface of the box is
$$\mathbf{A} \quad \sqrt{5^2 + 10^2} \text{ units}$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad \sqrt{7^2 + 10^2} \text{ units}$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 4 + \sqrt{3^2 + 10^2} \text{ units}$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 3 + \sqrt{10^2 + 4^2} \text{ units}$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 5 + \sqrt{10^2 + 7^2} \text{ units}$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad 6 + \sqrt{10^2 + 5^2} \text{ units}$$

## 答案
B

## 解析
A shortest surface path between opposite corners becomes a straight line once the two faces it crosses are unfolded flat. Each unfolding produces a right triangle whose legs are one dimension of the box and the sum of the other two, so the three candidate lengths are
$$\sqrt{(3 + 4)^2 + 10^2} = \sqrt{149}, \qquad \sqrt{(3 + 10)^2 + 4^2} = \sqrt{185}, \qquad \sqrt{(4 + 10)^2 + 3^2} = \sqrt{205}.$$
The smallest is $\sqrt{149} = \sqrt{7^2 + 10^2} \approx 12.21$, obtained by adding the two smaller dimensions and keeping the longest edge as the single leg. The answer is B. Options C to F all describe routes that run along an edge and then across a single face; any such bent path is longer than the straight line across two unfolded faces.
