## 题目
$3x^4 - 6x^3 + kx^2 - 8x - 12$ is divisible by $x - 3$, so it is also divisible by
$$\mathbf{A} \quad 3x^2 - 4$$
$$\mathbf{B} \quad 3x^2 + 4$$
$$\mathbf{C} \quad 3x^2 + x$$
$$\mathbf{D} \quad 3x^2 - x$$
$$\mathbf{E} \quad 4x^2 + x$$
$$\mathbf{F} \quad 4x^2 - x$$

## 备注

### 我的备注

### AI备注


## 答案
B

## 解析
Because $x - 3$ is a factor, the value of the quartic at $x = 3$ must vanish, so $3(81) - 6(27) + 9k - 24 - 12 = 0$, that is $45 + 9k = 0$ and therefore $k = -5$. Dividing $3x^4 - 6x^3 - 5x^2 - 8x - 12$ by $x - 3$ gives the quotient $3x^3 + 3x^2 + 4x + 4$, and grouping this as $3x^2(x + 1) + 4(x + 1)$ shows it equals $(x + 1)(3x^2 + 4)$. The full factorisation is therefore
$$3x^4 - 6x^3 - 5x^2 - 8x - 12 = (x - 3)(x + 1)(3x^2 + 4),$$
so the polynomial is also divisible by $3x^2 + 4$. The answer is B.
