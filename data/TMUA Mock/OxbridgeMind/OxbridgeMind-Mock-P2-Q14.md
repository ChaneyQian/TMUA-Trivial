## 题目
The cross section of a triangular prism is an isosceles triangle with the 2 longer sides having length $2x$ cm and shorter side with length $x$ cm. The length of the prism $d$ cm. Let the total volume of the prism be $\frac12 T\text{ cm}^3$, and the total prism surface area be $T\text{ cm}^2$, which one of the following is an expression for $d$ in terms of $x$?

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \frac{\sqrt{3x}}{\sqrt{30}x-10} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{2\sqrt{15}x}{2\sqrt{15}x-5} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{\sqrt{30}x}{\sqrt{30}x-15} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{\sqrt{15}x}{\sqrt{15}x-10} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{\sqrt{15}x}{\sqrt{15}x-5} $$
