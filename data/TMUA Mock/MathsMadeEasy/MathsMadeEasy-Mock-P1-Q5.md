## 题目
A circle of radius $r$ has its centre on the positive $y$-axis and is tangent to both the parabola $y=x^2$ and the straight line $y=x+1$. Find $r$.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \frac{\sqrt5-\sqrt2}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac{\sqrt3-1}{2} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \sqrt5-\sqrt2 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac{\sqrt6-\sqrt3}{3} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac{\sqrt{10}-\sqrt5}{4} $$
