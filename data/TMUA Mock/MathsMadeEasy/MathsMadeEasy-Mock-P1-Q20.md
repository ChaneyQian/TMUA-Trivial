## 题目
Two chords are chosen independently and uniformly at random within a unit circle. The probability that a single random chord in a unit circle has length greater than $\sqrt3$ is $P$. Given that the two chords intersect inside the circle, find the probability that both chords have a length exceeding $\sqrt3$.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \frac19 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad \frac1{12} $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \frac{\pi-2}{4\pi} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \frac1{18} $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad \frac16 $$
