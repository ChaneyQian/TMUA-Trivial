## 题目
The polynomial $f(x) = x^3 + cx^2 + dx - 6$ satisfies both of the following: - $(x+2)$ is a factor of $f(x)$; - $f(x)$ leaves a remainder of $3$ when divided by $(x-1)$. Once $c$ and $d$ are determined, $f(x)$ can be written as $(x+2)q(x)$ for some quadratic $q(x)$. Find the product of the roots of $q(x) = 0$.

$$ \mathbf{A} \quad -6 $$
$$ \mathbf{B} \quad -3 $$
$$ \mathbf{C} \quad -2 $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad 3 $$
$$ \mathbf{E} \quad 6 $$
