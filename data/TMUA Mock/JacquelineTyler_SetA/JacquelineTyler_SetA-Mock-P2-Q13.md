$$ \mathbf{A} \quad \text{P is **necessary** and **sufficient** for Q} $$

$$ \mathbf{C} \quad \text{P is **necessary** but **not sufficient** for Q} $$
$$ \mathbf{D} \quad \text{P is **sufficient** but **not necessary** for Q} $$
